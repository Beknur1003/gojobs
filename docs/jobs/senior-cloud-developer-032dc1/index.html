Job Description: HPE is seeking a Cloud Developer to lead the design, development, and deployment of innovative cloud solutions that support mission-critical applications. This role impacts HPE’s Cloud Services portfolio and involves collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure efficient delivery and integration of cloud-based systems. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in cloud-native architectures, microservices, and containerized environments, with strong programming skills and leadership capabilities. The position requires a deep understanding of cloud technology, excellent communication skills, and the ability to mentor team members to drive technological innovation and high-quality results. Key Responsibilities: • Lead the design and development of complex cloud products and platforms, including architecture, coding, testing, and integration. • Evaluate and review design, test plans, and code to ensure compliance with cloud standards and best practices. • Provide feedback to improve product quality and mitigate risks. • Represent the engineering team in technical forums; mentor and guide less-experienced team members. • Drive the adoption of new technologies and innovative solutions within projects. • Analyze data processing and system issues to develop effective solutions. • Collaborate with project managers and stakeholders to align technical strategies with business goals. Basic Qualifications: • Bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, engineering, or related field. • Typically, 6+ years of experience in cloud development, software engineering, or related disciplines. Knowledge and Skills: • Strong programming skills in languages such as Python, Java, Golang, or JavaScript. • Experience or familiarity with microservice architecture, containerization, and Kubernetes. • Knowledge of multiple platform integrations and cloud-native design. • Experience in testing methodologies, debugging, and quality assurance. • Excellent communication and collaboration skills. • Ability to lead technical teams and drive innovation. • Soft skills include leadership, teamwork, and effective communication. • Technical expertise in cloud architecture, microservices, container orchestration (Kubernetes), and programming languages mentioned. • Familiarity with cloud platforms and enterprise software development practices.
