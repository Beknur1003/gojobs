Spendbase is a global fintech company with a startup mindset, helping businesses optimize their spending on SaaS, cloud services, and corporate cards. We’re building and scaling fintech products used by businesses worldwide, and we’re looking for a Senior Golang Engineer to join our Banking team. You’ll work on backend systems powering financial products, contribute to technical decisions, and help strengthen security across our systems. What you’ll do • Develop and maintain backend services using Go. • Design and integrate APIs using REST and gRPC. • Build reliable and scalable financial systems, ensuring their performance, stability, and security. • Identify potential security vulnerabilities and implement solutions to protect financial data and transactions. • Work with PostgreSQL, Redis, and Kafka, Temporal to support data-intensive applications and asynchronous workflows. • Contribute to architectural decisions, focusing on scalability, maintainability, and security. • Collaborate with engineers, QA, and product teams to deliver high-quality features. • Take ownership of technical solutions from design to implementation and production. What we’re looking for • 4+ years of experience in software development, with strong backend engineering expertise. • Hands-on experience with Golang in production environments. • Previous experience in fintech, banking, crypto, or trading products. • Solid understanding of application security, common vulnerabilities, and potential attack vectors. • Experience building and integrating REST APIs; familiarity with gRPC. • Experience working with PostgreSQL and designing reliable, scalable systems. • Understanding of distributed systems and asynchronous processing. • Strong problem-solving skills, technical ownership, and the ability to make sound engineering decisions. • English level B2 or higher. Nice to have • Experience with high-load and distributed systems. • Experience with cloud infrastructure and container orchestration. • Experience with other backend languages, such as Java or C# or Rust • Previous experience in a fast-growing product company or startup. What we offer • Competitive salary • Fast-paced, ownership-driven environment • High level of autonomy and impact • Paid vacation, holidays, and sick leave • Online medical consultation services Join the growth!
