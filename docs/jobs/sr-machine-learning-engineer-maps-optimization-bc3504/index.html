About the Role The Maps Optimization team builds and maintains the foundational stack powering the full user journey on the Uber map, including location search, ETA calculation and routing. Our technology leverages advanced machine learning models and high-throughput scalable infrastructure capable of serving millions of QPS. The work of the team influences Uber’s core business performance in a meaningful way and is highly visible to the executive leadership team. We are looking for a highly motivated, entrepreneurial machine learning engineer to join the Maps Optimization team. As a senior machine learning engineer, you will design, build, and optimize large-scale ML systems and models to improve efficiency and travel experience across millions of daily trips worldwide.
