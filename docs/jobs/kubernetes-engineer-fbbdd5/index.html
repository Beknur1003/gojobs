We are seeking a Senior Platform Engineer to help evolve and scale a modern Kubernetes-based infrastructure platform supporting mission-critical applications and engineering teams. This role is focused on platform reliability, automation, infrastructure engineering, and improving developer productivity through cloud-native technologies and DevOps best practices. The ideal candidate has strong Kubernetes expertise, hands-on Golang development experience, and a deep understanding of distributed systems, infrastructure automation, observability, and production operations. This is a remote-friendly role with flexible hours, provided there is overlap with Eastern Time business hours. Responsibilities • Design, maintain, and improve highly available Kubernetes-based infrastructure platforms • Partner with development, SRE, networking, and infrastructure teams to gather requirements and deliver scalable platform solutions • Build and enhance automation tooling, CI/CD pipelines, and GitOps workflows • Customize and extend open-source platform components written in Golang • Contribute fixes, enhancements, and integrations to internally maintained platform tooling • Support zero-downtime deployments, upgrades, and platform migrations • Improve cluster observability, monitoring, logging, and alerting capabilities • Automate issue detection, remediation, and operational workflows • Assist application teams with onboarding workloads into Kubernetes environments • Help modernize legacy applications for containerized deployment architectures • Evaluate and test emerging platform technologies and infrastructure tooling Required Qualifications • Strong production experience operating Kubernetes environments • Hands-on Golang development experience • Deep understanding of Kubernetes architecture, APIs, control plane, and node operations • Experience implementing GitOps workflows using tools such as Argo CD or Flux • Experience building automation and infrastructure tooling • Experience with Helm and Kubernetes application packaging • Experience with CI/CD pipeline design and implementation • Strong Linux systems and networking knowledge • Experience with ingress, reverse proxies, or service networking technologies • Experience implementing monitoring and observability using Prometheus or similar tooling • Experience with centralized logging and operational visibility platforms • Strong troubleshooting and production incident response skills Preferred Qualifications • Experience contributing to or modifying open-source infrastructure tooling • Experience with Kubernetes networking and traffic management concepts • Familiarity with cloud-native infrastructure patterns and distributed systems • Experience with highly available infrastructure deployments • Experience with persistent storage orchestration in containerized environments • Familiarity with cloud providers or hybrid infrastructure environments • Experience supporting developer enablement and platform engineering initiatives Additional Information •
