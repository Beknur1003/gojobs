Job Description: About the Role We are looking for a Technical Cloud Architect to serve as a trusted technical advisor to our largest strategic customers. You will bring deep, hands-on expertise in KVM-based virtualization and the surrounding ecosystem — automation, observability, software-defined networking (SDN), and hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI) storage — to help customers design, validate, and adopt modern private cloud architectures powered with HPE CloudOps Software This is a customer-facing, high-impact role that sits at the intersection of pre-sales engineering, solutions architecture, and product strategy. You'll spend your time in technical whiteboard sessions, hands-on proof of concepts, and writing the design guidance that shapes how customers deploy at scale — while also being a direct voice back into our engineering organization. What You'll Do • Engage with large strategic customers on technical and architectural discussions, acting as a subject-matter expert on KVM virtualization and adjacent cloud infrastructure technologies • Design and execute proof of concepts (PoCs) that demonstrate solution value and validate architecture decisions in customer environments • Author design guides and reference architectures that document best practices for deployment, scaling, and operations • Provide structured product feedback to engineering, representing the voice of the customer and helping shape the roadmap • Partner with sales, product management, and customer success teams to support the technical sales cycle from discovery through deployment What You'll Bring • Hands-on production experience with KVM-based hypervisor platforms and their surrounding ecosystem, including: • Automation (e.g., Ansible, Terraform, or similar IaC tooling) • Observability (monitoring, logging, and telemetry stacks for virtualized infrastructure) • Software-Defined Networking (SDN) • Hyperconverged Infrastructure (HCI) storage • Deep hands-on experience at VMware, Red Hat, or Nutanix, or Hyperscalers • Strong ability to communicate complex technical concepts to both engineering and executive audiences • Experience running customer-facing proofs of concept and producing technical documentation or design guides • Comfort working cross-functionally with engineering, product, and sales teams • Excellent written and verbal communication skills Nice to Have • Experience with public cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP) and hybrid cloud architectures • Relevant certifications (RHCA, VCAP/VCDX, Nutanix NCA/NCX, or equivalent) • Prior experience in a solutions architect, technical field engineer, or
