Our Global Structure Deliveroo is now part of DoorDash, bringing together teams with even greater reach, scale, and ambition. Depending on your role, you may collaborate with teammates, systems, and leaders across DoorDash and Wolt. Together, we’re unlocking new possibilities as one global team. Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack - EMEA Pricing and Affordability You’ll join EMEA Pricing & Affordability, the team that owns how an order is priced for consumers across EMEA: service and delivery fees, distance-based pricing and surcharges, fee discounts and the benefits that subscribers get. Our work sits right at the cart and checkout, on the new Pedregal platform shared across Deliveroo and Wolt. Price is the most visible part of every order. Getting it right means customers can see what they’ll pay and trust it, and can afford to order again. That drives conversion, order frequency and retention, and it is one of the biggest levers on both consumer growth and marketplace economics. Our roadmap covers subscriber pricing, service fee optimisation, distance-based pricing, long distance surcharges and priority delivery, so the work reaches millions of orders in many markets. As a full stack engineer on this team you’ll take pricing features from idea to production: designing fee and discount logic on the backend, building the checkout and basket experience that shows it, and measuring the result through experiments. You’ll be expected to be comfortable with Go and React, with correctness and testing for money-critical code, and with working closely with Product, Data and the teams across Pedregal. Your changes will be seen by consumers within weeks of shipping, and you’ll help decide what we build next. We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer - Full Stack to join our London team. In this role, you’ll help us build and scale seamless, high-performance features that span our entire stack, ensuring a world-class experience for our customers, partners, and riders. Get to know our Engineering team — what drives us, how we work, and what you can expect. What You’ll Be Doing This role is based in our London office with a hybrid setup, typically 3 days in the office. Here’s what your day-to-day might look like: • Drive Technical Excellence: Improve code structure and architecture across your team’s remit, developing, releasing and running large-scale web applications whilst transitioning our monolithic codebase to a microservice-based architecture • Lead Project Planning: Foresee dependencies and risks, partnering closely with Product and Design to ensure smooth delivery of complex features, whilst defining the backend architecture and tech stack for a product area • Optimise Operations:Improve performance, reliability, scalability and security for our backend systems • Collaborate & Influence: Build relationships across global engineering teams to identify collaboration opportunities and influence the wider technical roadmap. • Mentor & Hire: Support the personal growth of your colleagues through technical guidance and take an active role in conducting engineering interviews to scale our team. What You’ll Need to Thrive Our ideal candidate will bring strong expertise in some of these areas and curiosity to grow in others: • Proficiency in using AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) in the full software development lifecycle, including designing, generating code, testing, monitoring and releasing software • • Strong Engineering Principles: Deep pride in writing readable, well-designed, and well-tested software. • Backend Expertise: Significant experience building web-based applications (we use Go, Ruby/Rails, Python, Scala, and Rust, React). • Data Proficiency: Practical experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and "NoSQL" backends like Redis, DynamoDB, or ElasticSearch. • Systems at Scale: Proven ability to manage web architecture at scale (handling 20krpm and above). • Technical Leadership: Experience breaking down large pieces of work and guiding implementation choices for a team of engineers.
