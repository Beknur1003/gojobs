Microservices Engineer

This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Microservices Engineer based in Netherlands. This role focuses on building scalable, resilient, and high-quality back-end services using Go and Python. You will contribute to APIs and microservices that support millions of clients and critical infrastructure across a global technology ecosystem. The position offers broad exposure to API design, distributed systems, databases, DevOps, security, performance, and multi-region service delivery. You will help evolve engineering practices around scalability, reliability, observability, and continuous delivery. Working within a globally distributed engineering team, you will collaborate across disciplines and contribute to both internal and external technical communities. This is a remote-first opportunity with international collaboration and regular in-person team events. Accountabilities • Design, develop, and maintain high-quality, robust software and microservices that operate reliably at significant scale. • Design APIs and software architectures for peer and technical review, with a focus on maintainability and long-term evolution. • Build comprehensive automated tests that validate software behavior and service reliability. • Develop and maintain fault-tolerant applications and distributed services. • Contribute to API design, documentation, governance, and engineering standards. • Improve scalability, performance, resilience, and availability across web services and distributed systems. • Work with relational and non-relational databases, considering performance, reliability, and architectural trade-offs. • Apply modern DevOps practices across continuous integration, continuous delivery, deployment, monitoring, tracing, and profiling. • Diagnose complex technical issues, identify root causes, and implement high-quality solutions. • Contribute to proactive security practices, including fuzzing and other security analysis techniques. • Collaborate proactively with globally distributed engineering teams and contribute to cross-team technical initiatives. • Provide technical leadership through design discussions, documentation, mentoring, and engagement with external technical communities. • Contribute to technical documentation and help establish clear, reusable engineering knowledge. • Work remotely while participating in international company events approximately twice per year. Requirements: • Strong software engineering experience with a track record of building modern, maintainable, and scalable software. • Proficiency in Python and/or Go. • Experience designing and operating scalable web services, microservices, or systems software. • Solid understanding of REST APIs, API architecture, documentation, and service design principles. • Experience working with relational and non-relational database systems. • Experience with continuous integration and continuous delivery practices. • Experience with Linux, preferably Debian or Ubuntu. • Strong understanding of distributed systems, reliability, scalability, and fault-tolerant architecture. • Professional written and spoken English. • Strong analytical and debugging abilities, with a practical approach to solving complex engineering problems. • Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across technical teams. • Curiosity, flexibility, accountability, and a strong sense of ownership. • Self-motivated and results-oriented, with a consistent focus on delivering high-quality outcomes. • A degree in Computer Science, STEM, or a compelling alternative professional background. • Additional experience with LXD, Docker, Kubernetes, or other container technologies is valuable. • Familiarity with build systems, toolchains, or cross-compilation is a plus. • Experience with hyperscale public or private cloud environments is beneficial. • Experience designing, building, and operating distributed systems is advantageous. • Background in performance engineering or application security is a plus. • Willingness and ability to travel approximately twice a year for company events lasting up to two weeks. Benefits: • Geographically adjusted compensation based on location, experience, and performance. • Performance-driven annual bonus. • Annual compensation review. • Fully distributed work environment with twice-yearly in-person team sprints. • Personal learning and development budget of USD 2,000 per year. • Recognition rewards. • Annual holiday leave. • Maternity and paternity leave. • Employee Assistance Programme. • Opportunities to travel and meet colleagues in different locations around the world. • Priority Pass and travel upgrades for long-haul company events. • Opportunity to work on large-scale microservices supporting millions of clients and critical infrastructure. • Exposure to modern cloud, distributed systems, DevOps, security, performance, and observability practices. • Collaboration with a globally distributed engineering community across multiple time zones.
