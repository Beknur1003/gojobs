The weaponization of AI is driving a paradigm shift in the cyber security industry from proactive to preemptive security: clients must now invest more intelligence and effort in understanding their vulnerabilities before their adversaries do (Open AI). The only way to achieve this is to simulate future attack scenarios at scale, and to run them in high fidelity digital replicas of production environments (Gartner/Forrester). SimSpace serves as the AI Proving Grounds to generate hypersynthetic data which pressure tests controls, tools, teams, and agentic capabilities in full kill chain attacks hidden in realistic environmental traffic and patterns of life. Founded in 2015 by experts from U.S. Cyber Command and MIT Lincoln Laboratory, the platform was initially conceived to assist in Offensive Cyber Operations (OCO), to quickly replicate target environments to conduct mission rehearsal of human operators and their tools to deny (disrupt, degrade, destroy) or deceive (manipulate or exfiltrate data) adversaries. The company has since expanded to serve over a dozen allied governments in OCO/DCO as well as critical infrastructure enterprises around the world. The company is actively investing in the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe and the Middle East through direct sales and channel partners. SimSpace has launched the AI Proving Grounds Consortium, leading the revolution by creating an open platform for the InfoSec ecosystem to train, validate and operationalize their agentic capabilities before committing to production. Job Summary You will lead the software engineering for evaluation and scoring in AI Proving Grounds (AIPG). Customers need to see what an agent attempted, what happened and whether the evidence supports the assessment. AI-native development is central to this role. Use coding agents as a primary tool for design, implementation, testing and diagnosis, and build repeatable workflows for the team. You will contribute critical code and guide design across TPA features. The Engineering Manager owns staffing, people and delivery priorities. Evaluation specialists review methods and scoring criteria; you own implementation and evidence integrity. What will you be doing at SimSpace? • Lead technical design across TPA's evaluation workflow and implement critical paths from execution coordination and evidence capture through scoring and results. Agree shared infrastructure and frontend responsibilities with the owning teams. • Establish reusable agent instructions, repository context and spec-driven workflows. Build automated checks, review generated changes and coach engineers to use the workflow effectively within approved data and access boundaries. • Define versioned APIs and data contracts that preserve the identity, environment, method, access permissions and source evidence behind each result, including known uncertainty. • Build asynchronous services with explicit state, duplicate protection, bounded retries, cancellation and recovery from interrupted work. • Work with Data Scientists on reference cases and reproducible scoring. Show missing, invalid or inconclusive evidence explicitly so incomplete input cannot produce an unsupported passing result. • Establish integration tests, diagnostics and operating documentation that distinguish software, environment and evaluation-method failures through rollout and support. • Guide other engineers through implementation and review. Keep designs coherent across features, document decisions and coordinate shared-interface changes with their owners. What are the qualifications to apply? • Experience leading technical design across related features, contributing production code and helping other engineers carry the design through rollout and operation. • Evidence of shipping and maintaining software with AI coding agents and improving the workflow for others, including task decomposition, useful repository context, independent verification and correction of generated errors. • Strong API and data-contract design skills, including access controls, validation, compatibility and interface migration across components. • Experience building distributed or asynchronous systems that tolerate failures and diagnosing partial failures across service boundaries. • Experience integrating analytical, data-processing or model-based capabilities into maintained software services. • Ability to design tests and observability that explain evidence provenance, incorrect results and recovery behavior. • Technical leadership through mentoring, design reviews, written decisions and coordination with engineers and specialists outside your immediate team. Who you are • You test whether a conclusion is supported and make the evidence and its limits visible in interfaces and results. • You explain the cost of technical trade-offs and choose designs that support useful releases and future work. • You improve how the team works with agents, judging progress through reliable delivery, review effort and rework. Nice to have but not required • Experience with agent evaluation frameworks, experimental platforms, or evidence and audit systems. • Production experience with Go and Python, including integration between services and analytical workers. • Experience with cybersecurity, simulation or secure handling of evaluation artifacts. Our Tech Stack Includes The team works across Go and Python services, application and browser integrations, distributed execution, containers and automated qualification. The development workflow centers on AI coding agents, written requirements, automated checks and code review.
