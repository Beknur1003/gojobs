At Saily, we're removing the hassle of staying connected while traveling — no roaming fees, no plastic SIM cards, just lightning-fast, secure mobile data in 200+ destinations. Millions of paying customers trust us, and hundreds of thousands of travelers browse Saily at any given moment. We're not looking for someone to close tickets. We're looking for a builder — someone who has shipped products real people use, felt the pain of a 2 AM incident on something they care about, and gets a kick out of seeing a metric move because of something they built. Our Culture: AI-native Product Engineering • The Engineer is the builder — responsible for solving customer problems end-to-end, from idea to production • AI Agents are the enablers — they write most of the code and handle repeatable tasks, so you maximize your impact • Everyone makes Saily more effective — you suggest what to delegate to agents, then implement the changes • Curiosity, learning, and sharing are everyone's job • The customer — a traveler on a comfortable, connected, hassle-free journey — never leaves our focus We value growth over comfort, contribution over hierarchy, and impact over process. We give sharp people context, autonomy, and AI as their multiplier — and expect their best work in return. Main Responsibilities • Own product features end-to-end: from shaping the idea with Product and Design to shipping, measuring, and iterating • Build features supporting multi-dimensional personalization, A/B experimentation, and analytics • Make technical decisions with a product lens — you'll know the "why" behind every "what" • Develop and maintain a high-quality, high-performance codebase in Go • Mentor colleagues and raise the bar on engineering best practices • Influence your team's vision and long-range goals — your opinion on what we build matters as much as how • Run R&D spikes when the path forward isn't obvious • Collaborate across engineering, product, and design in a cross-functional team Requirements • 5+ years of backend development experience, with strong skills in Python, PHP, Ruby, or similar — and readiness to work in Go daily • Product company experience, ideally startup or scale-up — you've worked where speed, ambiguity, and ownership are the norm, not the exception • B2C experience strongly preferred — you understand what it means to build for millions of users you'll never meet, where small UX details and performance directly hit revenue and retention • Solid system design skills: you know which patterns to use, when, and why • Experience with SQL (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and NoSQL (Redis, Cassandra) solutions • Experience with Kafka or similar event-driven systems • Passion for building APIs that are easy to use and simple to understand • A collaborative mindset — you love discussing, rethinking, and redesigning ideas toward shared success Bonus Points • You've built your own product — a side project, a startup (successful or not), an indie app, anything you took from zero to real users. We find that people who've carried a product themselves think differently: they obsess over the user, they ship, they own outcomes. If that's you, tell us about it — it matters more to us than logos on your CV • Experience with K8s and microservice architecture • A track record of experiments shipped, hypotheses tested, and impact measured (not just features delivered) • Comfort working AI-native: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor — you see AI as a multiplier, not a threat Don’t quite match every requirement? Our technical bar is high, but we’d rather help you reach it than lose a great person over it. If your mindset and technical potential make an impression, but your Go skills aren’t quite there yet, we may invite you to the GO Masterclass: fully funded access to the Three Dots Labs’ Golang training platform. Level up, come back, and interview again — we’d love to see you grow into this role!
