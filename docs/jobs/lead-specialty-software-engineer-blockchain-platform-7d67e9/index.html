About this role: Wells Fargo is seeking a Lead Speciality Software Engineer. In this role, you will: • Lead complex initiatives on selected domains • Ensure systems are monitored to increase operational efficiency and managed to mitigate risk • Define opportunities to maximize resource utilization and improve processes while reducing cost • Lead, design, develop, test and implement applications and system components, tools and utilities, models, simulation, and analytics to manage complex business functions using sophisticated technologies • Resolve coding, testing and escalated platform issues of a technically challenging nature • Lead team to ensure compliance and risk management requirements for supported area are met and work with other stakeholders to implement key risk initiatives • Mentor less experienced software engineers • Collaborate and influence all levels of professionals including managers • Lead team to achieve objectives • Partner with production support and platform engineering teams effectively Required Qualifications: • 5+ years of Specialty Software Engineering experience, or equivalent demonstrated through one or a combination of the following: work experience, training, military experience, education. Desired Qualifications: • Experience with programming languages Golang and Rust. • Hands-on experience with DevOps tools including Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines. • Experience with Kanban Agile methodology. • Prior SRE (Site Reliability Engineering) experience. • Experience in Systems Engineering and Technology Architecture, or equivalent background gained through work experience, training, military experience, or education. • 5+ years of hands-on experience with Blockchain platforms, including Cosmos SDK, CometBFT, and Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) Protocol. • Strong expertise across multiple blockchain technologies such as Ethereum, Hyperledger Fabric, Corda, and Solana. • Practical experience in smart contract development using Go and Rust. • Deep understanding of Distributed Systems, Cryptographic principles, Consensus algorithms, Token standards, and API design & REST integrations. • 5+ years of experience working with relational and NoSQL databases including Oracle, MS SQL, and MongoDB. • 5+ years of experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, UrbanCode Deploy, and/or Harness. • 5+ years of experience with application performance and monitoring tools including Grafana, Splunk, or AppDynamics. • Hands-on experience in private cloud environments (PCF/OCP) and/or public cloud environments (GCP). • Working knowledge of Agile methodologies. • Strong analytical capability to perform independent ticket analysis and resolution. • Good understanding of Infrastructure Service Management processes (Incident / Change / Problem Management) with hands-on experience using ServiceNow. Job Expectations: • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to follow established processes. • Ability to assess and recommend permanent fixes to enhance application stability and resiliency. • Capability to perform Root Cause Analysis, prepare accurate recaps of issues, and communicate effectively with stakeholders and cross-functional teams. • Demonstrated experience preparing complex reports and dashboards for leadership review. • High attention to detail with the ability to make quick, accurate decisions and escalate critical issues appropriately. • Must be a strong team player with the ability to lead teams through technical challenges.
