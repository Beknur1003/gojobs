About Different AI: Different AI is a new kind of marketing platform built around the belief that modern B2B marketing is broken. The company is rethinking customer engagement by building an AI-driven B2B SaaS platform focused on transforming how marketing is done. Different AI is building its products from the ground up using the latest advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, with a focus on creating intuitive and impactful solutions for marketing teams. The company is currently building its core team in Bangalore. Role Overview: As a Data Platform Engineer, you will contribute to building the technology and data foundation for Different AI's products. The role involves working in an early-stage environment where engineers have significant ownership and the opportunity to contribute to building products from the ground up. Key Responsibilities: - Work directly with the founders and contribute to shaping the product and technology. - Build core systems and products from scratch rather than working on an existing legacy stack. - Take significant ownership across architecture, engineering, product decisions, and execution. - Work deeply with AI/ML and modern engineering technologies to solve meaningful B2B problems. - Contribute to defining engineering culture, practices, and the technical foundation of the company. - Work as an early engineering hire with substantial impact on the company's direction and growth. - Collaborate closely with experienced founders in a high-ownership environment. Tech Stack: Machine Learning, Java, Python, Golang, Node.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, Redux, HTML, CSS, Tailwind CSS, Git, Playwright, Selenium, Jest, Cypress.
