About this Position We are looking for an experienced Engineering Manager to lead our Buyer Platform engineering team, consisting of two squads responsible for critical customer-facing capabilities, including Promotions, Campaigns, Cart, Browse, and Search & Discovery. These capabilities are at the heart of Astro's e-grocery experience and directly influence customer engagement, conversion, and business growth. As an Engineering Manager, you will combine strong technical judgment, people leadership, and business understanding to build scalable, reliable, and high-performing systems while fostering an engineering culture focused on ownership, continuous improvement, and AI-driven productivity. Key Responsibilities Engineering Leadership & People Development • Lead, coach, and develop engineers and technical leads across two engineering squads. • Build a high-performing team through clear accountability, regular feedback, performance management, and career development. • Foster a culture of engineering excellence, ownership, collaboration, and continuous learning. • Identify technical and leadership potential within the team and develop future engineering leaders. Technical Excellence & System Reliability • Own the technical health, scalability, performance, and reliability of Buyer Platform services. • Drive architectural decisions and technical improvements to support business growth and increasing transaction volumes. • Establish engineering best practices covering system design, code quality, observability, security, and incident management. • Proactively identify technical risks, eliminate bottlenecks, and balance technical debt with business priorities. • Drive infrastructure and operational efficiency through cost-conscious engineering decisions. Product Delivery & Business Impact • Partner closely with Product Managers, business stakeholders, and cross-functional engineering teams to translate business objectives into technical execution. • Own engineering delivery, resource planning, prioritization, and execution predictability across both squads. • Drive continuous improvements in customer experience, including conversion funnel performance, cart responsiveness, and product discovery. • Use data and engineering metrics to evaluate delivery effectiveness and identify opportunities for improvement. • Balance speed, quality, reliability, and engineering investment to deliver sustainable business outcomes. AI-Driven Engineering Productivity • Champion practical adoption of AI-assisted software engineering across the development lifecycle. • Improve engineering productivity through automation, AI coding assistants, and agentic development workflows. • Establish measurable productivity improvements while maintaining engineering quality, security, and maintainability. • Promote experimentation and continuous optimization of engineering workflows. Requirements Must Have • 7+ years professional software engineering experience, including at least 2 years in Engineering Manager or equivalent engineering leadership role. • Strong technical background in backend engineering, distributed systems, API design, database technologies. • Proven experience decomposing a monolithic service into independent services — database and schema separation, zero-downtime data migrations. Java or comparable enterprise stack. • Track record improving stability and availability of transactional systems: owning SLOs, raising end-to-end transaction success rates, resolving data-consistency issues across service boundaries. • Demonstrated ability to make sound architectural decisions balancing technical trade-offs against business priorities. • Experience leading 2+ engineering teams through supervisors or tech leads — translating domain vision into quarterly goals and actionable execution plans. • Experience managing engineering performance — coaching engineers, strong analytical problem-solving skills, translating engineering metrics into actionable improvements, developing technical leadership capabilities. • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, cross-functional collaboration skills. • Hands-on experience adopting AI-assisted development tools improving engineering workflows. Nice to Have • Experience building high-scale e-commerce, retail, marketplace, or transaction-intensive platforms. • Order-to-payment flows: order lifecycle, payment orchestration, idempotency, failure recovery — plus promotions/pricing mechanics: valuation, stacking, budget control. • Search relevance design and practices on Elasticsearch: query understanding, ranking, indexing pipelines feeding search from source-of-truth systems. • Strong proficiency in Go (Golang), event-driven architectures, GCP Kubernetes. • Familiarity with business metrics such as conversion rate, average order value, customer retention, and gross merchandise value. • Experience introducing AI-native development practices, coding agents, and engineering automation – adopted by team-level. What Success Looks Like As an Engineering Manager at Astro, success is measured not only by delivering features but also by creating sustainable engineering and business outcomes: • Business Impact: Consistently deliver platform improvements that support customer experience, conversion, and business growth. • Engineering Excellence: Maintain reliable, performant, and scalable systems while continuously improving operational efficiency. • Delivery Effectiveness: Improve engineering throughput, predictability, and time-to-market without compromising quality. • People Development: Build a self-sufficient, high-performing team with strong ownership and growing technical leadership. • AI & Productivity: Demonstrate measurable improvements in engineering effectiveness through responsible AI adoption and automation. Why Join Astro? At Astro, engineering plays a direct role in shaping how customers shop and how our business scales. You'll have the opportunity to lead engineering teams responsible for core revenue-driving capabilities, solve challenging technical problems at scale, and help shape the next generation of AI-powered engineering practices. We value engineers and leaders who take ownership, challenge existing approaches, and focus on delivering meaningful outcomes rather than simply completing tasks.
