Job Title: Technical Lead Location: Shah Alam (In-person) Salary: RM 9,000.00 RM 15,000.00 per month About the Opportunity Our client is Malaysia's leading PropTech (Property Technology) enterprise platform, a dominant FinTech-enabled company that simplifies property transactions for developers, agents, and buyers. They are seeking an experienced Technical Lead to guide a development team in building and enhancing their custom, large-scale solutions. This is a critical leadership role combining deep technical expertise with team management. You will be responsible for the architecture and technical design, managing the full project lifecycle, and mentoring a team of developers to deliver high-quality software that bridges the gap between complex business requirements and technical implementation. Key Responsibilities • Technical Leadership & Architecture: • Take full responsibility for the architecture and technical design of new features and solutions. • Review all implementation plans to ensure they adhere to best practices for performance, scalability, and governance. • Provide technical guidance, conduct code reviews, and lead/develop your team members, fostering their career growth. • Project & Risk Management: • Define and manage resource plans in collaboration with Technical and Product Managers. • Plan and drive the project's technical execution to achieve the committed scope and timeline. • Identify, communicate, and manage technology risks, developing clear mitigation plans for all stakeholders. • Monitor and report on technical execution progress, issues, and risks in a timely manner. • Stakeholder & Team Collaboration: • Participate in the full software development lifecycle (design, implementation, testing, and support) for new features, integrations, and production defect fixes. • Interact with Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) and product managers to ensure a clear understanding of business requirements. • Be accountable for ensuring there are no gaps between business requirements and technical implementation. • Assist in estimating efforts and timescales for tasks delegated to the team. Required Qualifications & Skills • Education: Degree in Computer Sciences, Software Engineering, or Information Technology. • Experience: • A minimum of 5 years of experience in technical software development. • A minimum of 2 years of proven experience as a Technical Lead. • Technical Stack: • Proficiency in React. • Strong programming skills in Golang and/or JavaScript. • Well-versed in AWS cloud services. • Experience with RDBMS (specifically PostgreSQL is a strong plus). • Familiarity with CI/CD deployment processes. • Domain Knowledge (Bonus): Experience in the Property Industry, CRM, or Accounting domains is a significant advantage. • Skills: • Proven ability to lead a technical team, assign tasks, and manage delivery schedules. • Understanding of design documentation standards (e.g., UML). • Excellent communication (written and verbal English), analytical, troubleshooting, and consultative skills. • Ability to cope with tight deadlines and a strong commitment to delivery.
