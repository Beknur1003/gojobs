Job Description – Lead DevOps Engineer / Lead Platform Engineer Experience: 10–14 Years About the Role We are looking for an experienced and hands-on Lead DevOps Engineer to lead the design, development, implementation and operations of enterprise-grade infrastructure and platform engineering solutions. The candidate will be responsible for building and managing scalable Kubernetes platforms, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, networking, storage, security infrastructure and observability solutions. The ideal candidate should have strong experience in large-scale on-premises infrastructure automation, container orchestration, platform engineering and distributed systems. The candidate will work closely with architecture, development, security and operations teams to drive platform reliability, scalability, automation and engineering excellence. Key Responsibilities 1. Kubernetes & Platform Engineering • Lead the architecture, deployment and administration of enterprise Kubernetes platforms. • Manage production-grade Kubernetes clusters, including cluster lifecycle management, upgrades, high availability and troubleshooting. • Strong understanding of Kubernetes internals, control plane components, scheduling, networking and storage. • Work with Kubernetes operators, CRDs, controllers and custom automation using Golang or Python. • Implement and manage container virtualization platforms such as KubeVirt. • Manage multi-cluster environments and enterprise platform services. • Implement platform engineering practices, self-service infrastructure and developer enablement. 2. Networking & Storage • Strong understanding of Kubernetes networking and CNI implementations such as Calico, Cilium or equivalent. • Experience with BGP, VXLAN, IP-in-IP, VLANs, routing, load balancing and network security. • Experience with Multus, SR-IOV, OVS and advanced Kubernetes networking is highly desirable. • Understanding of CSI drivers, persistent volumes and enterprise storage solutions. • Hands-on experience with distributed storage platforms such as Ceph, Rook or equivalent. • Experience troubleshooting complex network and storage issues in production environments. 3. CI/CD & Automation • Design and implement enterprise CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins, GitLab CI or equivalent. • Experience with GitOps practices and tools. • Strong experience with Terraform, Ansible, Helm and automation frameworks. • Build reusable automation frameworks for infrastructure provisioning and application deployment. • Develop automation tools and platform services using Golang or Python. • Implement automated deployment, configuration management and infrastructure lifecycle management. 5. Security & Secrets Management • Design and implement enterprise secrets management solutions using HashiCorp Vault. • Experience with Vault Enterprise, Vault PKI, Transit Secrets Engine and authentication methods. • Understanding of PKI, TLS, certificate lifecycle management, HSM and encryption. • Experience with RBAC, Kubernetes service accounts and workload identity. • Implement security best practices across infrastructure, Kubernetes and cloud environments. • Experience with cloud KMS and enterprise key management solutions is desirable. 6. Observability & Monitoring • Architect and manage enterprise observability platforms. • Hands-on experience with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry and distributed tracing. • Experience with Jaeger, OpenSearch, ClickHouse or similar telemetry backends. • Design centralized logging, metrics and tracing solutions. • Develop monitoring dashboards, alerting rules and incident detection mechanisms. • Optimize telemetry pipelines for high-volume, distributed infrastructure environments. 7. Service Mesh & Microservices Infrastructure • Experience implementing and managing Istio or other service mesh solutions. • Understanding of mTLS, traffic management, service discovery and ingress/egress architecture. • Troubleshoot distributed application communication and networking issues. • Design secure communication patterns across microservices and Kubernetes clusters. 8. Technical Leadership & Team Management • Lead a team of DevOps and Platform Engineers. • Drive technical design discussions, architecture reviews and implementation strategies. • Define engineering standards, best practices and operational procedures. • Mentor engineers and drive technical capability development. • Own technical delivery, prioritization, resource planning and execution. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve complex production issues. • Drive RCA, incident management, problem management and continuous improvement. • Take ownership of platform availability, reliability, scalability and operational excellence. Required Technical Skills Technology AreaRequired Skills such as Kubernetes, Helm, Operators, CRDs, KubeVirt, Terraform, Ansible, Python or Golang, Jenkins, Calico, BGP, Multus, Load Balancing, StorageCeph, CSI, Rook, Enterprise StorageSecurity, HashiCorp Vault, PKI, TLS, KMS, HSM, Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Logging & Tracing,OpenSearch, Jaeger, ClickHouse, Service Mesh Istio, mTLS, Docker, CRI-O Mandatory Qualifications • 8+ years of experience in DevOps, Infrastructure Engineering, SRE or Platform Engineering. • At least 5+ years of hands-on experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration. • Strong experience designing and managing production-grade infrastructure. • Strong hands-on expertise in Terraform and Ansible. • Strong Linux administration and troubleshooting skills. • Experience with scripting or programming using Python or Golang. • Experience leading technical teams and delivering complex infrastructure projects. • Excellent troubleshooting, communication and stakeholder management skills. • Ceph and distributed storage architecture. • Advanced networking using BGP, OVS, SR-IOV and Multus. • Vault Enterprise, PKI and HSM integrations. • OpenTelemetry and large-scale observability architecture. • Experience with FedRAMP, enterprise security or regulated environments. • Experience building internal developer platforms (IDP). What Success Looks Like • Reliable, scalable and highly available Kubernetes platforms. • Standardized and automated infrastructure provisioning and deployment. • Improved platform observability and proactive incident detection. • Secure secrets, certificate and identity management. • Reduced manual operational effort through automation. • Improved engineering productivity and platform reliability. • Effective technical leadership and mentoring of the engineering team.
