To be successful as a Migration Engineer (BCP – Red Hat OpenShift), you should have experience with (Mandatory Skillsets required): • Experience configuring, using or maintaining Kubernetes (Openshift or EKS or AKS). • Experience in developing and coding software using Python or Golang . • Experience with Docker, Containers and Cloud-Native utilities and software. • A Migration Engineer (BCP – Red Hat OpenShift) plans, builds, and executes application + platform migrations to OpenShift with a strong focus on business continuity, high availability, disaster recovery, and operational readiness. • This role bridges app teams, platform/SRE, network, security, and DR/BCP stakeholders to ensure migrated workloads meet RTO/RPO, resiliency, compliance, and performance targets.
