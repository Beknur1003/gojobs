Learn more at flexera.com We are looking for a Senior QA Engineer / SDET with strong hands-on experience in test automation, API/UI testing, and Python. The ideal candidate will work across functional, automation, data, and end-to-end testing for cloud-based, data-intensive applications. This is a hands-on QA engineer who can own quality end-to-end, build and maintain automation independently, troubleshoot failures, validate complex data workflows, and work closely with engineering teams to continuously improve product quality. Key Responsibilities: • Design and implement comprehensive test strategies, test plans, and test cases for functional and end-to-end workflows. • Develop and maintain automated tests using Playwright and Python. • Perform API, UI, integration, regression, and end-to-end testing. • Validate backend data and analytics outputs using SQL. • Test data-intensive applications, including data pipelines and ETL workflows. • Identify test scenarios suitable for automation based on risk, business impact, and coverage. • Integrate automated tests with CI/CD pipelines such as Jenkins. • Investigate test failures, identify root causes, and work closely with developers to resolve issues. • Identify, document, track, and verify defects through resolution using Jira or similar tools. • Perform manual testing for scenarios that cannot be effectively automated. • Support validation and root-cause analysis of production issues. • Collaborate closely with Engineering, Product, and Analytics teams to understand requirements and ensure product quality. • Participate in code reviews and provide feedback to improve testability and overall software quality. Required Skills: • 6+ years of experience in QA automation / SDET roles. • Strong hands-on experience with Playwright. • Strong programming skills in Python. • Strong experience with API and UI testing. • Experience testing data-heavy, analytics, or SaaS applications. • Strong SQL skills for backend/data validation. • Experience with Git and collaborative development workflows. • Practical experience integrating automated tests with CI/CD pipelines. • Strong understanding of SDLC, Agile, and continuous integration practices. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently. • Excellent communication and documentation skills. Nice to Have: • Experience with pytest. • Experience with Ginkgo / Go and BDD-style testing. • Experience with Golang. • Experience with ETL/data pipeline testing. • Experience with AWS, Terraform, and VPC. • Experience with Jenkins or similar CI tools. • Experience with Jira or similar defect management tools.
