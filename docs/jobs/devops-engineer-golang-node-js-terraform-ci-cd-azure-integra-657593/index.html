Job Summary Synechron is seeking a DevOps Engineer with 6+ years of experience in DevOps tooling and CI/CD pipelines, along with strong hands-on development expertise in Golang and Node.js. The role will support the modernization of entitlement services by migrating OMES and UPMS from direct Salesforce account and contact dependencies to a backward-compatible API and integration layer. The successful candidate will design and build integration components, manage Infrastructure as Code and deployment pipelines, support system cutovers, validate entitlement and permission integrity, and contribute to end-to-end quality assurance and user acceptance testing. The role will help maintain backward compatibility, reduce permissioning risks, and support reliable delivery across interconnected enterprise systems.
