The Software Engineer III: Go Services designs, develops, tests, and supports cloud-based applications using Golang (Go), including APIs and web user experiences integrated with data pipelines from on-premises core systems. This role contributes throughout the software development lifecycle—from technical design and prototyping through implementation, testing, release, and production support. The position works cross-functionally to deliver secure, reliable, and maintainable solutions that meet business and customer needs. This is a remote position, and candidates must live within approximately a 70-mile radius of one of our office locations: Allen, TX; Overland Park, KS; Springfield, MO; Monett, MO; Birmingham, AL; Charlotte, NC; or Louisville, KY. This position is ineligible for immigration sponsorship and support. Please do not apply if at any time you will need immigration support now or in the future (i.e., H-1B, PERM). All positions, regardless of location, may require an onsite interview or in-person onboarding requirement to verify your identity. What you’ll be responsible for: • Develop and maintain services using Golang. • Design, prototype, develop, and troubleshoot software solutions. • Develops and tests applications based on business requirements and industry best practices. • Uses best practices and standard guidance to create and modify code. Considers how code changes will affect end users and internal teams. • Collaborates with QA team to ensure timely delivery of high-quality products. • Adheres to documented departmental and corporate standards. Participates in the definition and documentation of standards. • Plans unit tests and integration tests to ensure the reliability, security, and performance of developed software. • Provide occasional on-call support and participate in incident response to diagnose and resolve production issues. • May perform other job duties as assigned. What you’ll need to have: • A minimum of 4 years of experience in software development in Golang. • Experience writing API services deployed to Kubernetes. What would be nice for you to have: • Experience developing software products for banks, credit unions, or other financial institutions. • Experience with front-end or full-stack development, including building web-based user experiences. • Experience developing and deploying applications on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Experience integrating cloud services with on-premises systems and data pipelines. • Experience with event-driven architectures, messaging, or data-streaming technologies. • Ability to demonstrate strong skills in the following areas: • Software design and implementation • Analysis and design at multiple-project level • Research, analysis and problem resolution • Communication and interpersonal skills • Strong organization and time management skills If you got this far, we hope you're feeling excited about this opportunity. Even if you don't feel you meet every single requirement on this posting, we still encourage you to apply.
