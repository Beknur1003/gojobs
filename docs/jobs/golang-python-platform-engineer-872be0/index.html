Role Summary We are looking for a highly skilled DevOps Engineer with expertise in designing, building, and operating large-scale Kubernetes platforms on AWS EKS and/or Google GKE. This role will focus on platform reliability, automation, developer experience, infrastructure scalability, security, and operational excellence. You will be responsible for building cloud-native platform capabilities, implementing Infrastructure as Code (IaC), enhancing CI/CD processes, driving platform observability, and enabling modern developer platforms through tools such as Backstage. This is a Hybrid position located at our Bangalore, India development center. You will be required to be on-site 2 to 3 days per week. When you are not working on-site, you will be working from your home office. We are only considering candidates within a commutable distance to our Bangalore office and are not offering relocation assistance at this time. Position Details About The Role • As a Platform Engineer, you will partner closely with the broader DevOps and engineering teams to design, build, and maintain cloud-native platforms and infrastructure solutions. • You will develop robust applications, APIs, and automation frameworks using Go and Python to improve operational efficiency and platform reliability. • Working within AWS and/or GCP environments, you will leverage Kubernetes and microservices architectures to deliver scalable and secure solutions. • You will contribute to infrastructure automation initiatives and support the continuous evolution of engineering best practices. • The role encourages the use of AI-assisted development tools to accelerate productivity and innovation. • You will have the opportunity to learn new technologies, experiment with emerging tools, and play a key role in delivering high-impact platform capabilities. About You • You should have 5 to 7 years of programming expertise in Golang and/or Python and a proven track record of delivering production-ready solutions. • You have hands-on experience working with cloud environments such as AWS or GCP and are comfortable building and supporting applications running on Kubernetes. • You understand APIs, microservices architecture, and automation principles, and you enjoy creating efficient, scalable systems. • You are naturally curious, adaptable, and eager to learn new technologies, frameworks, and platforms as business needs evolve. • Experience with Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, observability tools, or emerging AI and GenAI technologies is a plus, but not required. Most importantly, you are a collaborative problem solver who embraces innovation, leverages AI-assisted development effectively, and thrives in a fast-paced engineering environment.
