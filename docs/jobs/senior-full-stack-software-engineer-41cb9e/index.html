Senior Full Stack Software Engineer Radiance Technologies is an employee-owned company with benefits that are unmatched by most companies in the Dayton OH area. Employee ownership, generous 401K, full health/dental/life/vision insurance benefits, paid time off, educational reimbursement, competitive salaries, interesting assignments and a pleasant work environment combine to make Radiance Technologies a great place to work and succeed. The Senior Full Stack Software Engineer will support the National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC) by performing software activities focused on assisting the Air Force move forward with digital transformation. Successful candidates will have strong problem solving and critical thinking skills. Job Responsibilities: • Design and implement new capabilities and custom software solutions to solve unique government customer problems • Follow best practices to write well-tested, high performing code • Identify and resolve issues found in existing code • Integrate capabilities with external services and tools • Participate in code reviews and mentor team members • Communicate status and technical details with customers Required Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in computer science or relevant STEM field • U.S Citizenship with the ability to obtain a TS/SCI Security Clearance Required Experience • 12 + years experience writing, maintaining, debugging, and testing code • Experience with RESTful web services • Experience with server-side languages, preference toward Golang, NodeJS, and/or Python • Experience with frontend JavaScript frameworks, preference toward Angular and Vue • Experience with version control software (e.g., Git) • Experience with bug/issue tracking software such as JIRA Required Skills • Ability to communicate technical details of work • Ability to work in a dynamic environment and take ownership for segments of a project • Strong oral and written communication and organizational skills • Open, team-oriented, with a strong desire to learn Desired Experience • Experience with containerized deployment such as Docker • Experience utilizing document store databases, such as Elasticsearch, OpenSearch and MongoDB • Experience with Kubernetes and Helm • Experience with CI/CD pipelines utilizing Argo CD and Jenkins Radiance Technologies is an Equal Opportunity Employer. All qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, or protected veteran status.
