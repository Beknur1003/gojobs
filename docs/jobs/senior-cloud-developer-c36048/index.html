Senior Cloud Developer

Montek Tech Services Pvt Ltd Bangalore, Karnataka опубликовано 27 октября 2026

Senior
AWSGCPCI/CDPython
We are seeking a Senior Cloud Developer with expertise in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Python development. The ideal candidate will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying scalable cloud-based applications. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to understand requirements and translate them into technical specifications. Your role will involve leveraging your knowledge of cloud technologies to optimize application performance and ensure high availability. Key responsibilities include developing cloud-native applications, implementing CI/CD pipelines, and maintaining cloud infrastructure. You will also be expected to troubleshoot and resolve issues in a timely manner, ensuring minimal downtime. The successful candidate will have a strong background in Python and experience with frameworks such as Django. Familiarity with Golang and AWS is a plus. Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced environment are essential. If you are passionate about cloud technologies and looking to take your career to the next level, we encourage you to apply.
