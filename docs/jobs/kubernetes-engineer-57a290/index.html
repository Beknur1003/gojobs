Kubernetes Engineer

Overview Detailed skillset: • The technical expectations, emphasizing expertise in Terraform, Argo CD, and AWS EKS • Total Experience: 7+ years, with relevant experience levels of 5 years for EKS, 2-3 years for Terraform, and 1-2 years for Argo CD. • Location preferences: Chennai and Hyderabad as primary sites and Bangalore as a fallback, • The project's urgency requires candidates to join within 15 days. Responsibilities Role overview: • The requirements for a Senior Engineer role specializing in Kubernetes, specifically AWS EKS. The candidate is expected to handle operations, engineering automation, and development activities related to the EKS platform. • This role is critical for maintaining and enhancing the enterprise-grade Kubernetes platform's robustness and stability. • The candidate must have strong expertise in Terraform and Argo CD as key technologies. • The role includes managing cluster lifecycle operations such as provisioning, patching, and undeployment. • Argo CD expertise is required for application lifecycle management and GitOps operations. Requirements Responsibilities • Lead design, architecture, and migration of 200 applications and 850 EKS clusters to the Northstar framework. • Program and orchestrate the deployment of feature sets into the Kubernetes CaaS platform, and build Docker containers through a fully automated CI/CD pipeline (Jenkins, GitLab, ArgoCD, JIRA, AWS, Ansible, Python, Shell). • Act as a full-stack container administrator across logging, monitoring, security, and SRE practices. • Design AI workload platforms (Kubeflow, Ray) on EKS, and apply distributed-systems design to handle exponential scaling and custom AI workload architectures. • Provide technical guidance to product engineering teams on architecture, design, development, and end-user support. • Research and run proofs of concept (POCs) on new services and technologies for cloud use. • Lead root-cause analysis and resolution of production failures against platform SLOs. Required Qualifications • Expert-level experience (10+ years) in container platforms, with a bachelor’s degree. Relevant Kubernetes platform automation experience of 5+ years. • 5–6 years minimum as an architect. Development-to-architect domain expertise is ideal. • Deep expertise in AWS and EKS. Hands-on experience with one or more of EKS (preferred), GKE, or OKE. Red Hat OpenShift is also acceptable. • Strong experience with infrastructure components: Karpenter, VPC CNI, KEDA, ArgoCD (or Flux as a second choice), cert-manager, Cluster Autoscaler, Over-provisioning, CoreDNS, metrics-server. • Very strong development and engineering expertise in Terraform (including writing modules and GitOps-based K8s lifecycle management), Helm, Jenkins, Ansible, GitLab (VCS, pipelines, runners), and Artifactory. • Hands-on GitOps CI/CD workflows (ArgoCD preferred; Flux acceptable). • Demonstrated expertise with the Kubernetes ecosystem: inspecting cluster resources, determining cluster health, and identifying application issues. • Expertise creating and modifying RBAC, Pod Security Standards, Quotas, LimitRanges, and OPA/Gatekeeper/Kyverno policies. • Solid understanding of Kubernetes and microservices networking, with strong troubleshooting skills. • Strong proficiency with observability tools (New Relic, Instana, Prometheus/Grafana, Fluentd/Fluent Bit/Elastic/Splunk/OTEL/ADOT), including building dashboards. • Strong Bash scripting for automation. • Excellent verbal and written communication. • Certification (mandatory): CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator), or an equivalent lower-level Kubernetes certification such as CKAD. Preferred / Good to Have • Development and lifecycle-management experience with Istio service mesh, including configuring and troubleshooting applications on the mesh. • Kubernetes Operator development (Operator SDK), custom controllers, and admission webhooks. • Familiarity with cloud cost optimization (CloudHealth, Apptio) and network-trace analysis (Wireshark, tshark). • Security tooling experience (Sysdig, CrowdStrike, Black Duck, Xray) and K8s security practices (SCC, network policies, CVE remediation, CIS benchmarks). • Additional certifications: CKS, CKAD, Terraform, Red Hat Certified OpenShift Administrator. • Backend development experience in Golang (preferred), Spring Boot, or Python.
