Job Title : Principal Technology Architect Location : Mumbai (Andheri) Role Overview : We are looking for a Principal Technology Architect to lead the architecture, development, and delivery of our cybersecurity awareness and phishing simulation platform (Aware) at EC-Council. This role will be responsible for designing scalable systems, leading engineering teams, ensuring high-performance delivery, and aligning technology with business goals. Key Responsibilities : 1. Architecture & System Design : - Design and evolve scalable, high-performance backend systems - Define architecture for multi-tenant SaaS and on-prem deployments - Ensure system reliability, scalability, and security best practices - Drive microservices adoption and system modernization 2. Engineering Leadership : - Lead and mentor a team of developers (backend + frontend) - Drive code quality, design standards, and best practices - Review architecture, code, and technical decisions - Collaborate with product, QA, and DevOps teams 3. Product & Delivery Ownership : - Own end-to-end delivery of features and releases - Translate business requirements into technical solutions - Handle client-specific customizations and enterprise deployments - Ensure timely and high-quality releases 4. Performance & Scalability : - Optimize systems for high concurrency (100K-200K users) - Improve performance using caching, queues, and async processing - Conduct load testing and capacity planning 5. DevOps & Infrastructure : - Work with cloud (AWS/Azure) and on-prem infrastructure - Oversee CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation - Ensure monitoring, logging, and alerting systems are in place 6. Security & Compliance : - Implement secure coding practices (OWASP) - Ensure compliance with standards like ISO 27001 - Handle VAPT fixes and security audits 7. Stakeholder Management & Solutioning : - Communicate effectively with internal stakeholders, product teams, and clients - Understand business and client requirements and translate them into technical solutions - Design optimized, scalable, and cost-effective architectures based on use cases - Participate in client discussions, technical reviews, and solution presentations Required Technical Skills : 1. Backend : - Strong expertise in Node.js (Express.js) - Experience with MongoDB / SQL databases - API design (REST, JWT authentication) 2. Frontend : - Experience with React.js - Experience with Mithril.js - Strong understanding of SPA architecture 3. Infrastructure : - Experience with AWS (EC2, IAM, CloudWatch) - Docker & containerization - Nginx / reverse proxy - Messaging systems (Kafka / Redis queues) - Strong understanding of networking and security components, including firewalls and network security rules, email gateways (e.g., spam filters, secure email gateways), and VPNs and secure remote access configurations. Preferred Skills : - Experience in cybersecurity products or LMS platforms - Knowledge of email delivery systems (SMTP, SPF/DKIM) - Exposure to AI/LLM or RAG-based systems - Experience with on-prem enterprise deployments - Familiarity with Golang and Python Soft Skills : - Strong ownership and decision-making ability - Excellent problem-solving and debugging skills - Ability to handle client communication and escalations - Leadership and mentoring mindset Key Expectations : - Ensure platform stability during high-scale campaigns - Improve system performance and reduce downtime - Drive architectural improvements and innovation - Maintain high engineering standards and delivery velocity Nice to Have : - Experience in phishing simulation or awareness platforms - Exposure to SIEM integrations and security logging - Experience handling large enterprise clients
