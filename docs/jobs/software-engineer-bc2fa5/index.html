Software Engineer Level: Software Engineer, Data & Technology Solutions Location: Copenhagen Reporting to: Lead Engineer About Open Intelligence We are the Activation arm of WPP Open Intelligence. Our team builds the foundational data infrastructure and high-performance edge services that power WPP’s position in the market. With approximately one-third of all global media spend flowing through WPP, our platform operates at the core of this massive network, deeply integrated and adopted by the largest supply-side partners in the AdTech industry. Operating at our scale presents unique engineering challenges. Our edge services currently handle over 60,000 classification requests per second, while our contextual classification pipelines process 10,000 requests per second. With operations spanning the US, UK, and ongoing expansion into EMEA and APAC, our infrastructure continuously interacts with up to 98% of the population in our active markets. We are an engineering-led team focused on building robust, scalable, and highly reliable systems. WHO WE ARE LOOKING FOR We are looking for a Software Engineer who can build and own reliable backend services and API integrations in production, shipping features while contributing to strong design and operational standards. You prioritise strong software engineering fundamentals, including clean design, testable code, and solid operational habits, over allegiance to any specific framework. You are comfortable taking solutions from design through delivery: partner API clients, internal APIs, workflow steps, and the glue between systems. You harden integrations with authentication, retries, idempotency, rate limiting, and clear error handling, and back that up with unit, integration, and contract tests, plus CI/CD and safe releases. You take ownership of technical debt and reliability in the areas you work on, including incidents and systemic fixes. You are highly proficient in a backend language such as Python, Go, or Java/Kotlin, with Python preferred, alongside experience with cloud platforms and deploying with containers and Kubernetes. Experience with async or event-driven systems, familiarity with infrastructure as code such as Terraform and production observability, and comfort using AI coding assistants without lowering the quality bar are strong fits. Beyond the technical craft, you communicate trade-offs clearly to engineers, Product, and sibling teams, escalate blockers early with options, and contribute constructively through design and code reviews. You maintain a strong end-to-end view of the services and integrations you work on and are motivated by making activation and orchestration systems more reliable and scalable over time. WHY ARE WE HIRING We are at a critical inflection point in our growth and need to scale our engineering team to tackle an expanding set of complex challenges. As the digital ecosystem evolves, we are actively developing and releasing a new catalogue of privacy-preserving data products. These solutions are designed to scale advanced targeting capabilities while remaining uncompromising on our promise to protect user privacy. To support this product evolution, our infrastructure is undergoing massive global expansion. By the end of this year, we will be fully operational across the rest of the EMEA and APAC regions. Concurrently, we are deepening our integrations to support even more of the largest supply-side partners in the AdTech industry. We are hiring because we need strong engineering talent to help us architect for this next order of magnitude in scale, ensuring our systems remain resilient, performant, and compliant as our global footprint and feature set rapidly expand. WHAT YOU WILL DO • Contribute to technical solutions for backend services and API integrations; develop and review code to a high standard and contribute to strong design quality across the team. • Own and evolve orchestration and gateway components, including workflow steps, partner API clients, internal service APIs, and the glue between OIMS and activation systems, with clear responsibility for reliability and operability. • Partner with Product, lead engineers, and sibling teams across audience, data, and performance to turn requirements into durable, production-level services and well-defined interfaces, rather than one-off glue. • Deliver API integrations covering authentication, rate limiting, idempotency, retries, partial-update patterns, and runbooks as partner APIs evolve. • Contribute to production standards for the areas you own, including CI/CD, testing, contract tests at API boundaries, observability, auditability, and safe releases. • Maintain a strong end-to-end view of activation flows you touch, understanding what is live, what is in progress, and where dependencies or technical debt block the next phase; propose sequenced fixes. • Contribute hands-on to critical paths and foundations, such as new gateways, tool registry, workflow runtime, and guardrail hooks, and collaborate with others through design reviews and pairing. • Identify and address technical debt deliberately, balancing speed with reliability for systems that support live campaigns. • Support other engineers through reviews, design feedback, knowledge sharing, stand-ups, and cross-team forums. • Stay curious about agentic orchestration, durable workflows, and AI coding assistants, adopting what improves how the team designs and ships without lowering the production bar. WHAT YOU WILL NEED • Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain trade-offs clearly, align stakeholders on interfaces, and escalate blockers early with options. • 3+ years of experience in software engineering, ideally in backend, platform, or API-focused environments, including experience working with meaningful production systems. • Proven delivery of backend and API-centric systems in production, including services, integrations, or platforms at meaningful scale. • Solid experience building and operating REST/HTTP APIs and event- or workflow-driven systems such as async jobs, queues, durable state, or orchestration. • Comfort working with third-party and internal integrations, including authentication, errors, versioning, operational monitoring, and partner-driven change. • Strong proficiency in at least one backend language such as Python, Go, or Java/Kotlin, with Python preferred, and sound software design practices across patterns, testing, and code review. • Experience with CI/CD, Git, and testing best practices, including unit, integration, and boundary/contract tests where APIs matter. • Experience with cloud platforms such as GCP, AWS, or Azure, and deploying services with containers and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with infrastructure as code such as Terraform and production observability across metrics, logs, and tracing. • Experience contributing to the reliability of production systems, including incident resolution and systemic fixes, not only feature delivery. • Interest in leveraging AI coding assistants and prompt engineering to improve personal and team productivity without compromising quality. NICE TO HAVE • Experience delivering orchestration, workflow, or agentic systems, including tool registries, policy/guardrails, or human-in-the-loop flows. • Full-stack exposure, including internal UIs or operations tools backed by the APIs you build. • Familiarity with AdTech / MarTech, including SSPs, DSPs, deals, audiences, or campaign activation terminology. • Experience in a monorepo or large shared codebase with many integration surfaces. • Awareness of data privacy and safe handling of sensitive data in distributed systems. • Prior work on audit trails or decision logs for high-trust or automated systems. • Experience supporting other engineers through code reviews, design discussions, pairing, or knowledge sharing.
