THE PROJECT📝 Form3 is looking for several Senior Software Engineer contractors to work alongside our US Software Engineering team to deliver new products within our US Payments proposition. This is an opportunity to work on critical payments infrastructure in a highly technical environment, contributing to the design, build, testing and launch of new Zelle and Fedwire capabilities. You’ll be working in Go on Form3’s cloud-native platform, applying proven engineering patterns from our existing payments services while solving the specific challenges that come with high-volume, resilient, regulated payment systems. • Design and implement new US payments gateway functionality in Go, aligned to product requirements and agreed technical architecture. This includes payment flows, scheme-specific behaviours, error handling, validation and operational requirements, as well as comprehensive automated integration tests covering this functionality. • Work collaboratively with Product, Engineering and other functions to refine requirements and validate implementation choices. • Contribute to technical design activity, including design proposals, implementation plans and problem-solving within Form3 platform architecture. • Support non-functional requirements related to resilience, performance, disaster recovery, failure-mode testing and operational readiness. Establish and improve monitoring, alerting, telemetry and observability to support operational visibility from development through to production. • Support production-readiness activity, including release preparation, final validation, production deployment and post-launch issue resolution. • Produce and maintain technical documentation, including design notes, testing documentation, deployment guidance, operational procedures and runbooks. • Ensure all work aligns with Form3’s engineering standards, security expectations and the standards required for critical payments infrastructure. WE’RE LOOKING FOR 🔍 • Deep understanding of designing and building distributed systems • Enjoys working on complex challenges within critical software where high performance and robustness are essential • Familiarity with cloud and containerisation technologies, test automation tools and CI/CD pipelines • Interest in owning projects end-to-end and supporting them as they go live in production • Appreciation of clean code and software engineering best-practice • Experience with Go, along with a “right tool for the job” mentality • Great communication skills who enjoy sharing knowledge and collaborating with others TECH STACK ⚙️ Here are a few of the tools and methodologies we utilise: • Infrastructure: AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes • Platform: CockroachDB, NATS JetStream, Elasticsearch, Vault, Cilium • Tools: Terraform, Flux, GitHub, Prometheus, Pact.io, Tilt • Code: Go (and a little Java), containerised microservices, CQRS, open source • Ways of working: TDD/BDD, Pair Programming, 100% remote, SecDevOps INTERVIEW PROCESS ✍️ Stage 1: Screening Call with Talent Team Stage 2: Take home test Stage 3: Technical interview (may be separated into two parts) We always aim to stick to the above process, however there may be occasions when an additional interview stage is needed for us to be sure we’re hiring the right person!
