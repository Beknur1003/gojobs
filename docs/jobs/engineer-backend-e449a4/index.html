Backend Engineer (Mid–Senior) About the role We're seeking a mid-to-senior backend engineer to join our team and help build our cloud security platform. In this role, you won't just write code — you'll also wear an entrepreneurial hat, collaborating closely with our leaders outside engineering, along with customers, partners, and investors, as we take our product from 0 → 1 → N. We're looking for someone who has already shipped and owned production systems and is ready to bring that experience to a fast-moving startup environment. Bedrock Security is the latest investment of Greylock Partners. Greylock has backed a number of successful startups, including Palo Alto Networks, Sumo Logic, and Abnormal Security, along with non-security companies such as Airbnb, Figma, and Roblox. What you'll do Design and implement backend logic • Architect and build robust, scalable services from the ground up. • Own service security, performance, and scalability end to end. • Work across our technology stack, including Golang, Python, Node, AWS infrastructure, Neo4j, and more. Move the needle at a scrappy startup • Partner closely with other engineers, product management, marketing, and customers. • Drive architectural decisions and system design, weighing in on trade-offs with limited precedent to draw on. Drive innovation • Shape the future of data security as part of the core creative process. • Identify complex challenges and propose and implement novel solutions, with minimal oversight. Mentor and raise the bar • Provide technical guidance and code review feedback to other engineers. • Help establish and improve engineering practices, standards, and tooling as the team scales. Continuous learning and adaptation • Stay current with industry trends and emerging technologies. • Adapt quickly to changing requirements and business needs. Compensation Bedrock hires across a range of experience levels for this role. The estimated annual base salary range for this position is $150,000 to $230,000 USD, depending on experience, scope, and leveling determined during the interview process. In addition to base salary, this role may be eligible for equity compensation and benefits. Qualifications • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related field. • 4+ years of professional experience as a backend engineer, ideally including some time in a security-related domain. • Demonstrated track record of independently designing, building, and owning production services from concept through deployment. • Proficiency with backend technologies and databases relevant to our stack (e.g., Golang, Python, Node, SQL, Neo4j). • Experience contributing to architectural and system design decisions, not just implementation. • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently with minimal guidance. • Entrepreneurial mindset, comfort with ambiguity, and a willingness to take ownership beyond your immediate scope. • Bonus: experience mentoring other engineers or leading small technical initiatives.
