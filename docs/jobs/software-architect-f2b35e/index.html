Software Architect

Rapsodo İzmir, İzmir, Turkey опубликовано 7 августа 2026

Rapsodo is a global sports technology company with offices in Singapore, the United States, Türkiye, Japan, and Malaysia. We develop data-driven sports analytics products that combine computer vision, AI, hardware, and cloud technologies to help athletes measure, understand, and improve their performance. Our products deliver accurate, real-time performance insights in demanding environments, supporting professional athletes, coaches, and sports organizations around the world. At Rapsodo, engineers take ownership from concept to production, working across multidisciplinary teams to build innovative products that transform complex motion into actionable insights at global scale. About the Role As a Software Architect, you will play a key role in shaping the technical direction of Rapsodo's products. Working closely with cross-functional engineering teams, you will design scalable software architectures, establish engineering standards, and contribute hands-on to building high-quality cloud-native solutions that power innovative sports technology products used worldwide. What You'll Do • Design and evolve scalable, secure, and maintainable software architectures for new and existing products. • Define architectural standards, technical requirements, and engineering best practices while maintaining key design artifacts and API specifications. • Collaborate with engineering teams and stakeholders to translate business needs into scalable technical solutions. • Lead architecture reviews and code reviews, mentor engineers, and ensure high software quality across development teams. • Evaluate and adopt modern technologies, frameworks, cloud services, and AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering productivity. • Contribute directly to the codebase through architecture validation, refactoring, optimization, and hands-on software development. • Drive continuous improvement by promoting clean architecture, modern engineering practices, and technical innovation. What We're Looking For • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related field. • 7+ years of experience as a Software Architect or in a similar technical leadership role. • Strong knowledge of software architecture, distributed systems, design patterns, and system integration. • Professional experience with Go (Golang) and at least one object-oriented programming language such as Java, C#, or Kotlin. Experience with Python is a plus. • Hands-on experience with microservices architecture and at least one major cloud platform (Google Cloud, AWS, or Azure). • Experience using AI-assisted development tools throughout the software development lifecycle. • Strong communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills. Why Join Rapsodo? We are a global leader in sports vision technology, powered by a team that believes in failing fast, learning faster, and winning as a team. At Rapsodo, you'll thrive in a culture driven by customer focus, achieving more with less, and redefining what's possible. If you are eager to work independently while contributing to a dynamic, innovative, and passionate team, we encourage you to submit your application.
