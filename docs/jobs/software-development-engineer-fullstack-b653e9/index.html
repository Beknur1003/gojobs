Software Development Engineer - Fullstack

MySQLMongoDBRedisKafkaElasticsearchKubernetesDockerAWSGCPPrometheusGrafanaOpenTelemetryMicroservicesNode.jsAI/LLM
We aren't looking for feature factories; we are looking for owners of a resilient ecosystem. At Purplle, your success is defined by your mindset and the tangible impact. Responsibilities: • AI-Native Velocity: Lead the 70% AI-assisted PR mandate by mastering Cursor, Copilot, and Claude to accelerate delivery of Node.js (NestJS) and Golang microservices while keeping human intelligence focused on high-level system design and architecture decisions. • Unbreakable Backend Architecture: Architect high-throughput, event-driven microservices using Kafka and Redis, embedding resilience patterns like circuit breakers, health checks, and fallback mechanisms to handle massive traffic spikes without manual intervention. • Data and Performance Ownership: Own the unit economics of the data layer, optimize MySQL and MongoDB queries, design efficient schemas, and ensure sub-second p99 latencies with predictable infrastructure costs on GCP. • End-to-End Golden Path Ownership: Drive features from design docs to Kubernetes deployment, ensuring high reliability through strong engineering practices and maintaining low change failure rates and minimal Sev-1 incidents. • API and Service Design Excellence: Build clean, versioned, and backward-compatible APIs and modular microservices with a strong focus on maintainability, scalability, and clear service contracts. • Cloud-Native Operations and Observability: Design and operate services on Kubernetes with robust logging, monitoring, and alerting; proactively debug production issues and ensure high availability. • Scalability and Cost Efficiency: Continuously optimize system throughput, fault tolerance, and infrastructure utilization to support high growth while maintaining strict control over cloud costs. • Technical Leadership and Collaboration: Lead design decisions, write clear RFCs, mentor engineers, and collaborate closely with Product, Frontend, Data, and DevOps teams to drive high-quality outcomes. • Core Engineering Excellence: Apply strong fundamentals in distributed systems, concurrency, and data structures, combined with sharp problem-solving, debugging, and communication skills. Candidate Profile: • The Systems Thinker (Quality and Resilience): You hate fixing the same bug twice. You'd rather build a self-healing mechanism than perform a manual fix. Your goal is to maintain a change failure rate of < 1.5% and help us reach a state of fewer than 2 Sev-1 incidents per quarter. • The AI-Fluent Architect (High Velocity): You are already using AI tools to 10x your own productivity. You leverage these tools to drive a cycle time of < 4 hours and maintain a high frequency of daily deployments, moving code from commit to production with total confidence. • The Data-Informed Engineer (Economic Discipline): You care about the why behind the code. You understand that latency and cloud spend are engineering disciplines, and you optimize your infrastructure so that our cost-per-order remains predictable as we scale. • The Proactive Collaborator (Security and Governance): You thrive in a cross-functional environment (Eng, Data, DevOps, Security). You take ownership of the Golden Path, ensuring 100% of your repositories are gated and that zero critical vulnerabilities ever reach our production environment. Requirements: • Languages and Frameworks: Node.js (NestJS / Express), Golang. • Data and Messaging: MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, Kafka, Aerospike, ElasticSearch. • Infrastructure: Kubernetes, GCP / AWS, Docker. • Observability: Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry. • AI Productivity: Cursor, Claude, GitHub Copilot.
