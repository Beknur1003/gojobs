Abnormal Security Hybrid - Bangalore, India опубликовано 11 ноября 2026

Гибрид Junior
AWSPythonAI/LLM
About the Role This position exists to build and improve the configuration platform owned by the Config Platform team, within the Multi-Product Platform (MPP) division. The role plays a critical part in helping the team deliver on its mission: making configuration easier to access, reuse, and operate across Abnormal, so product teams can spend more time on customer-facing capabilities and less on infrastructure plumbing. You will contribute directly to the backend systems that control how Abnormal products are configured. What you will do Abnormal AI is looking for a driven and growth-oriented Backend Software Engineer to join the Config Platform team. The team builds and maintains the shared systems behind product settings, feature toggles, and tenant configuration. As a member of the team, you will build and scale backend services, help make platform capabilities more reliable, scalable, and observable, and work with engineers across product teams to turn their configuration needs into maintainable software. You’ll work across APIs, backend services, and data workflows, and take part in code reviews, testing, releases, and operational learning as you grow toward owning well-scoped components end to end. We’re looking for candidates who are eager to learn, excited to work collaboratively, and motivated by building foundations that many engineering teams rely on. Must Haves • 1+ years of experience as a software engineer with a strong foundation in coding and problem-solving. • Hands-on experience in backend development using Python or Golang, with exposure to cloud-native environments. • Solid understanding of databases, REST APIs, and AWS or any other cloud service provider. • Familiarity with LLMs and AI development tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, or Claude. • Experience owning a feature or well-defined problem end to end. • Experience working with distributed teams, proficient in asynchronous and written communication. • Experience building features and components and operating them, balancing feature development with operational tasks. Nice to Haves • Familiarity with AWS services and deployment practices. • Exposure to configuration management, feature flags, or multi-tenant systems. 🚀 Ready to be part of AI transformation at Abnormal AI?
