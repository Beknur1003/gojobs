Senior Engineering Manager [Voice & AI Platforms]

Tanla Platforms Limited Hyderabad, Telangana, India опубликовано 19 ноября 2026

Go — основной язык Senior
PostgreSQLRedisKubernetesLinuxAI/LLM
Role Overview: We are looking for a hands-on Senior Engineering Manager to lead the architecture, development and delivery of a scalable, carrier-grade Programmable Voice and AI Communication Platform supporting SIP, WebRTC, WebSockets and AI voice-agent integrations. You’ll be Responsible For? • Design and build a highly scalable, multi-tenant Programmable Voice/CPaaS platform. • Lead development of SIP signaling, media processing, dynamic call routing and programmable voice APIs. • Implement inbound/outbound calling, call recording, IVR, DTMF, conferencing and call transfers. • Enable real-time AI voice integrations through SIP, WebRTC and bidirectional WebSocket audio streaming. • Ensure high availability, low latency, security and scalability for 10,000+ concurrent calls. • Lead engineering teams, drive architecture decisions and own end-to-end delivery. What You’d have? • 12+ years of experience in building the high scalable systems with Voice & AI Platform Architecture. • Strong experience with SIP, SDP, RTP/SRTP, RTCP and VoIP protocols. • Hands-on expertise in OpenSIPS/Kamailio, RTPengine, Livekit and FreeSWITCH. • Strong backend development experience in Golang. • Knowledge of WebRTC, WebSockets, audio codecs, transcoding and media gateways. • Experience with PostgreSQL, Redis, Linux and Kubernetes/Nomad. • Understanding of SIP trunking, carrier integrations, load balancing and quality-based routing. Preferred Experience • A hands-on engineering leader with strong VoIP/SIP architecture expertise and experience building scalable communication platforms from the ground up. • Experience building CPaaS, VoIP or programmable voice platforms similar to Twilio, Plivo, Exotel or Vobiz. • Exposure to AI voice-agent integrations using LiveKit, Vapi or similar platforms. • Proven experience designing high-concurrency, carrier-grade communication systems. Why join us? • Impactful Work: Play a key role in strengthening HR operations and delivering a seamless employee experience across the organisation. • Tremendous Growth Opportunities: Be part of a rapidly growing company with opportunities to build and improve HR processes and practices. • Innovative Environment: Work alongside a collaborative team in a dynamic environment where ownership, accountability and innovation are valued. Tanla is an equal opportunity employer. We champion diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment for all employees. www.tanla.com
