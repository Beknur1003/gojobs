Role Overview We are seeking a Software Architect to design, build, and improve key parts of Intelerad's build and delivery infrastructure. In this role, you will turn DevOps and cloud-native best practices into reliable pipelines, automation, and tooling that help R&D teams ship high-quality software in a regulated healthcare environment. You will work closely with senior architects and development teams, owning the technical outcomes of your components and building toward broader platform ownership. Key Responsibilities • Design and deliver platform components: Own the design, development, and improvement of defined components of Intelerad's build system and delivery platform. Ensure each one meets performance, reliability, security, and compliance standards. • Enable development teams: Guide development teams in adopting CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and Kubernetes-based container orchestration. Resolve technical issues that block delivery. • Build product features to architectural standards: Design and implement features across Intelerad's products using established design patterns, balancing quality, functionality, and delivery timelines. • Contribute to technical direction: Partner with senior architects and R&D leadership on platform roadmaps, toolset evaluations, and architectural decisions. Document standards and share knowledge to raise team capability. • Plan and report on delivery: Scope work, gather requirements, and estimate effort for assigned initiatives. Give leadership accurate progress updates while balancing project work and ongoing platform maintenance. Qualifications & Experience • 3+ years of professional experience developing commercial software, including: • Deploying and operating containerized applications using Kubernetes • Building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines and release processes • Programming in Golang or Python Job • Working in Linux/Unix environments with shell scripting • Hands-on use of AWS services (e.g., EC2, S3, RDS, IAM, CloudWatch) • Working knowledge of software design patterns and architectural principles • Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams Preferred Qualifications & Special Requirements • Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent • Experience with infrastructure as code tools (e.g., Terraform, Ansible, or equivalent) • Experience developing Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) or Platform as a Service (PaaS) solutions • Familiarity with healthcare IT software or regulated software development environments • Experience with web development or full-stack technologies This job description is not a comprehensive list of tasks or requirements and Intelerad reserves the right to change it at any time.
