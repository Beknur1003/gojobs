About the Position: As a Staff Developer, you will play a key leadership role within a software development team, balancing deep technical expertise with mentorship, project leadership, and delivery accountability. This position requires expert-level programming and system design capabilities, ownership of end-to-end project delivery, and a commitment to software development best practices. You will lead technical initiatives, guide and mentor team members, contribute to architectural decisions, and help shape engineering standards while working across cross-functional teams. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a highly collaborative environment and enjoys solving complex technical challenges while helping others grow. Responsibilities • Lead the delivery of large-scale engineering projects from design through implementation and deployment. • Provide technical leadership and mentorship to developers across the team. • Create and review high-level and low-level design documentation. • Drive software quality through coding best practices, unit testing, and test automation. • Participate in Agile development activities including daily standups, retrospectives, and sprint planning. • Ensure adherence to architectural standards, Definition of Ready, Definition of Done, and engineering best practices. • Take ownership of production issues and identify long-term solutions to prevent recurrence. • Develop reusable design patterns and scalable software solutions. • Collaborate closely with Product Management, UX, and Engineering teams to deliver business outcomes. • Influence technical direction across teams and help drive alignment toward target architecture. • Coach and mentor team members through code reviews, pairing sessions, and knowledge-sharing initiatives. • Lead incident response reviews and effectively communicate findings and recommendations to stakeholders. • Support team delivery by helping prioritize work, unblock teammates, and ensure commitments are met with quality and timeliness. About You • Extensive experience designing, building, and delivering complex software systems. • Strong expertise in software engineering, system design, and modern development practices. • Experience with Golang, Python, and related development ecosystems. • Proven ability to lead technical projects and influence outcomes across teams. • Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to navigate ambiguity and develop effective solutions. • Deep understanding of software development best practices, including testing, automation, code quality, and architecture. • Experience mentoring and developing engineers at various levels. • Ability to effectively communicate technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences. • Strong collaboration and stakeholder management skills. • Demonstrated ability to align technical decisions with business objectives and organizational goals. • A growth mindset with the ability to learn new technologies, frameworks, and domains independently.
