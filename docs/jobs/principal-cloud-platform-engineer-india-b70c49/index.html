All roles at JumpCloud® are Remote unless otherwise specified in the Job Description. About JumpCloud® JumpCloud® is the AI-powered unified IT management platform designed to secure the modern workforce. By consolidating identity, device, and access management, JumpCloud provides intelligent, secure IT that scales from human users to autonomous AI agents. We help organizations around the globe eliminate complexity and turn AI risk into an optimized advantage, ensuring the right people and agents have secure access to the right resources at all times. JumpCloud is Intelligent, Secure IT. About the Role We're hiring a Principal Cloud Platform Engineer to join the Cloud Platform organization, which owns JumpCloud's cloud infrastructure, developer experience, and edge and traffic engineering. This is a hands-on principal role. You'll write production code and you'll run what you build. You'll set the technical direction for the platform that keeps JumpCloud available at 99.99% across regions, and you'll raise the engineering bar for everyone around you. We want someone who is as comfortable designing and shipping a platform service as they are leading a major incident at 2 a.m. What You'll Be Doing : • Set the technical direction and multi-year architecture for JumpCloud's cloud platform across AWS and GCP, covering compute, networking, Kubernetes, data stores, and edge. • Write production-grade Go or Python as a regular part of the job: platform services, Kubernetes controllers and operators, internal APIs, CLIs, and automation that removes manual work. • Build and evolve our internal developer platform, including golden paths, self-service provisioning, and CI/CD and GitOps pipelines (Argo CD), so product teams can ship safely without filing tickets. • Own the design of multi-region architecture, failover automation, and disaster recovery, with RTO and RPO targets we actually test. • Operate what you build. Take part in on-call, act as the senior escalation point during major incidents, and turn blameless post-incident reviews into lasting fixes. • Define the reliability standards for the platform: SLIs, SLOs, error budgets, production readiness reviews, and capacity planning that hold a 99.99% SLA as we grow. • Find and remove scalability bottlenecks across services, databases, queues, and network paths before customers feel them. • Shape edge and traffic engineering, including ingress, load balancing, DNS, CDN, WAF, and service mesh. • Lead our Infrastructure-as-Code architecture in Terraform across multi-account, multi-region environments, with policy-as-code guardrails for security and compliance. • Treat cost as a design input. Partner on FinOps, unit economics, and right-sizing so we scale efficiently. • Bring AI-assisted engineering (Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) into platform work to speed up tooling, runbooks, and incident triage. • Write RFCs and architecture decision records, review designs across engineering, and mentor senior and staff engineers. We're Looking For : • 12+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 5 years in platform, infrastructure, SRE, or DevOps roles running 24/7 SaaS systems at scale. • Real development depth: You've designed, built, and shipped production services in Go, Python, or Java, not just scripts. You're strong in distributed systems design, APIs, concurrency, and testing. • Real operations depth: You've carried a pager for business-critical systems, led incident response, and debugged hard problems across the application, OS, network, and cloud layers. • Deep AWS expertise (GCP a plus): IAM, VPC design, Transit Gateway, ALB/NLB, Route 53, EKS, and managed data stores. • Production Kubernetes at scale: cluster lifecycle, networking, autoscaling, RBAC, operators and controllers, and GitOps with Argo CD. • Terraform mastery: module design, state management and refactoring, and multi-account patterns. • Observability and reliability: hands-on with Datadog, Prometheus, or OpenTelemetry; you've defined SLOs and cut MTTD and MTTR in a measurable way. • Principal-level influence: You've set direction across multiple teams without direct authority, written design docs that got adopted, and led large migrations to completion. • Clear writing and speaking, with engineers and with leadership. You can explain trade-offs in cost, risk, and time. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience. • A strong team player who lives our core values: building connections, thinking big, and getting 1% better every day. Preferred Qualifications : • You've built an internal developer platform or developer portal (Backstage or similar) that teams chose to use. • Experience with service mesh (Istio, Linkerd) and proxies (Envoy, HAProxy, NGINX), plus CDN, WAF, and DDoS protection. • Experience scaling data layers such as PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, or Kafka, including multi-region or active-active designs. • Chaos engineering or regular game days in staging and production. • Security depth: secrets management (Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, External Secrets Operator), DevSecOps, zero-trust networking, and compliance frameworks such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. • Background in identity, IAM, directory services, or security-focused SaaS. • Open-source contributions, published writing, or conference talks on platform or reliability topics. #LI-PD1 Where you’ll be working/Location: JumpCloud is committed to being Remote First, meaning that you are able to work remotely within the country noted in the Job Description. You must be located in and authorized to work in the country noted in the job description to be considered for this role. Please note: There is an expectation that our engineers participate in on-call shifts.
