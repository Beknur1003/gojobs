This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Software Engineer, Privileged Access Management (PAM) / Password Manager (PWM) based in India. This role offers the opportunity to shape the architecture, security, and engineering practices of a modern cloud platform. You will design scalable, highly available microservices while contributing to enterprise-grade privileged access and identity management solutions. The position combines hands-on software engineering with technical leadership, DevOps, security, testing, and operational excellence. You will work with technologies including C#, .NET, Go, TypeScript, gRPC, AWS, Kubernetes, and automated testing frameworks. A key part of the role is advancing Zero Trust and least-privilege security principles across complex enterprise environments. You will also help establish AI-assisted engineering practices, mentor engineers, and influence technical standards across agile teams. The role is fully remote within India and offers the opportunity to solve challenging problems in a fast-paced SaaS environment. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, and maintain scalable, highly available microservices using C#, .NET Core, Go, and TypeScript, leveraging gRPC, REST APIs, and relevant application frameworks. • Architect and implement enterprise Privileged Access Management (PAM) and Identity and Access Management (IDAM) capabilities using technologies such as CyberArk, BeyondTrust, or HashiCorp Vault. • Apply Zero Trust and least-privilege principles to strengthen access controls and security across enterprise systems. • Develop and integrate SCIM-based identity and provisioning workflows where required. • Own and improve continuous delivery processes using CI/CD technologies and internal delivery platforms. • Build comprehensive automated testing strategies, including unit, functional, Playwright end-to-end, performance, scalability, and reliability testing. • Develop cross-platform services and native desktop applications supporting enterprise workflows across macOS, Windows, and Linux. • Establish effective observability through telemetry, logging, and distributed tracing to monitor system health, performance, and service-level objectives. • Participate in incident management, troubleshoot production issues, contribute to post-incident reviews, and drive timely resolution of critical problems. • Partner with Product, Design, Infrastructure, and engineering teams to manage technical debt, modernize legacy components, and establish consistent engineering practices. • Promote AI-assisted software development workflows and governance using tools such as Cursor, Claude Code, and GitHub Copilot. • Mentor engineers, contribute to technical and architectural reviews, and develop shared engineering knowledge and standards. Requirements: • 8+ years of hands-on software engineering experience, including significant technical leadership experience as a Senior Staff Engineer, Lead Engineer, Architect, or similar role. • Deep expertise in C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, and/or ABP Framework, with strong programming experience in Go and familiarity with TypeScript. • Strong knowledge of gRPC and RESTful API development and modern microservice architectures. • Advanced experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL and SQL Server, as well as NoSQL technologies such as DocumentDB. • Hands-on experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and custom CI/CD automation pipelines. • Strong understanding of software testing and automation, including Playwright for end-to-end testing and tools for load, stress, performance, and reliability testing. • Experience designing or implementing PAM/IDAM solutions, preferably with platforms such as CyberArk, BeyondTrust, or HashiCorp Vault. • Understanding of Zero Trust security models, least-privilege access, identity management, and SCIM integrations. • Experience establishing or applying structured, AI-assisted software development workflows within production engineering teams. • Strong understanding of observability, distributed systems, reliability engineering, and operational excellence. • Excellent problem-solving, communication, collaboration, and technical leadership skills. • Ability to work effectively in an agile, remote-first environment and collaborate with globally distributed teams. • Fluency in written and spoken English is required. • Must be located in and authorized to work in India. • Willingness and availability to participate in an on-call rotation and respond to assigned production alerts. Benefits: • Fully remote work opportunity within India. • Remote-first working environment with flexibility to work from home. • Opportunity to work on large-scale cloud, identity, security, and access-management technologies. • Exposure to modern engineering practices, microservices, Kubernetes, AWS, CI/CD, and AI-assisted development tools. • Opportunity to influence architecture, engineering standards, security practices, and technical strategy. • Collaboration with globally distributed engineering and product teams. • Opportunities for mentoring, knowledge sharing, and continued technical development. • Work on complex SaaS infrastructure and enterprise security challenges with broad technical scope. • Employee-focused environment supporting professional growth, collaboration, and innovation. • Healthcare, financial, wellness, and other benefits may vary according to the applicable employment package and local policies.
