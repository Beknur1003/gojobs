Staff Software Engineer (Golang)

опубликовано 5 октября 2026

Go — основной язык Lead
PostgreSQLKubernetesPythonJavaAI/LLM
We are looking for a Staff Software Engineer to take technical ownership of a new component of an enterprise network security platform operating across complex IT, OT and IoT environments. You will join at the beginning of a new product component and engineering team, designing the architecture and building the solution from the ground up. This is a hands-on technical leadership role, not people management. You’ll work closely with Product, QA and other engineering teams while remaining deeply involved in architecture and coding. What You’ll Do • Design and build a new platform component from scratch. • Own architecture and key technical decisions through production implementation. • Develop core backend and infrastructure components primarily in Go. • Work with the platform’s data plane, distributed systems and data flows. • Build scalable, reliable services using Kubernetes and cloud-native infrastructure. • Work with PostgreSQL, APIs and service-to-service communication. • Provide technical direction as the new engineering team grows. • Use modern development approaches, including AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude Code. What We’re Looking For • Experience as a Staff / Principal / Lead Engineer, Software Architect, or equivalent senior hands-on role. • Excellent Go / Golang skills — core requirement. • Strong software architecture and system design experience. • Proven experience designing and building complex distributed systems. • Strong backend and infrastructure engineering background. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and cloud-native environments. • Experience with PostgreSQL and production data architecture. • Good understanding of data plane architecture, networking, APIs, concurrency, performance, scalability and reliability. • Ability to turn ambiguous requirements into architecture and working production systems. • High degree of technical ownership and independence. • Comfortable working in a fast-moving startup environment with a strong delivery focus. Nice to Have • Python and/or Java development experience. • Experience with networking, cybersecurity or infrastructure platforms. • Experience with microsegmentation, policy enforcement or similar network security systems. • Experience with Claude Code or other AI coding agents. • Public technical leadership, such as conference speaking or engineering community contributions. Other details: Location & Working Model • CET timezone. • Kraków is preferred, with weekly office collaboration. • Strong candidates elsewhere within the required time zone can be considered. • B2B contract with a US entity. • 120-140k annual rate • Output-oriented working model. • Company laptop provided. Interview Process • 30-minute discussion with engineering leadership. • Technical deep dive with live coding and architecture discussion. • Final technical/culture discussion if needed.
