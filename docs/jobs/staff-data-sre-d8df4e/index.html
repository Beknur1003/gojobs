About the role Please note, this team is hiring across all levels and candidates are individually assessed and appropriately leveled based upon their skills and experience. The Data Production Engineering team is an SRE (Site Reliability Engineering) team responsible for the overall ownership of 3rd party data services at Netskope. Including relational databases, noSQL datastores, data caching and streaming services, etc… The team designs, implements, automates, maintains, monitors, and manages these platforms. The team consists of Data Systems Architects and Data SREs. What’s in it for you As a Lead Data SRE on the DPE team, you will participate in strategic architectural direction and lead the implementation, operation, performance tuning, and scaling of Netskope’s production data services. In addition, you will provide overarching technical leadership for the team, and mentor senior and junior team members. Essential Functions • Serving as a Subject Matter Expert (SME) and technical lead for designated DPE platforms, storage systems, and infrastructure automation tools. • Working closely with DPE architects and Product Engineering leads to architect, scale, and improve the overall reliability, efficiency, and performance of DPE platforms. • Designing, building, and maintaining software tools and automation using a variety of programming languages including Python, Go, and Bash at a senior/staff level. • Establishing and driving organization-wide adoption of SOPs, engineering standards, and procedural documentation. • Leading cross-functional technical evaluations, POCs, and integration of next-generation data platforms and tooling. • Applying a deep understanding of non-functional requirements (scalability, disaster recovery, high availability, latency, throughput) to shape platform architecture and ensure business requirements are met. • Embracing our corporate culture by fostering collaboration without boundaries, and encouraging teamwork and communication that is clear, open, and honest. Required skills and experience • Please note that due to the specific requirements of working within a FedRAMP High certified environment, all candidates for this position must be U.S. citizens currently residing within the United States. • 5+ years of general Site Reliability Engineering, Systems Engineering, or Infrastructure Engineering experience. • 8+ years of experience with large-scale, high-throughput, distributed clustered data system environments. • 8+ years of experience with deep troubleshooting, kernel/system performance tuning, and configuration of Linux systems. • Hands-on experience building tools and automating repetitive tasks with Python and Bash • Proven ability to provide technical leadership, influence architectural decisions, and collaborate effectively with cross-functional remote teams. • Participate in an on-call rotation to provide tier-3 technical escalation support for critical systems in emergency situations. • Demonstrated ability to track industry trends, evaluate emerging data technologies, and provide data-driven technical roadmaps and strategic recommendations. • Actively mentor senior and junior team members, foster technical growth, and share best practices across the engineering organization. Desired Experience • Demonstrated staff-level depth of knowledge in one or more Database platforms listed: • Relational DB: MariaDB, PostgreSQL, YugabyteDB, ClickhouseDB • NoSQL: MongoDB, ScyllaDB • Messaging: Pulsar, Kafka • Caching: Redis, ValKey • Deep operational experience with telemetry, monitoring, capacity planning, and automation tooling such as: Jenkins, Salt, Terraform, Ansible, Prometheus, Grafana, Zabbix, Jira, Confluence. • Proficiency in a variety of programming languages including Python, Go, C, and C++. • Extensive knowledge of cloud and container infrastructure technologies (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, KVM, OpenStack, or orchestration platforms).
