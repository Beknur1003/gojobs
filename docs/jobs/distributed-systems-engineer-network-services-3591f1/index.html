About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a "normalized" problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Locations: Austin TX, Champaign IL, Lisbon Portugal, London England, Atlanta GA, Washington DC, Location (all hybrid): Austin, TX; Champaign, IL; Seattle, WA; Atlanta, GA; Washington, DC; London, UK; Lisbon, PT About the team Cloudflare is redefining the way networks are run at scale, and some of the largest networks in the world now run their traffic through us. Companies connect their data centers, offices, and clouds to Cloudflare, and our network carries, routes, and protects that traffic from hundreds of cities around the world. Our teams build the systems that make those connections work: the APIs customers use to describe their networks, the control planes that turn that into configuration on every server we run, the health checks that move traffic away from failing paths, and the BGP sessions we run with customer networks (the systems behind products like Magic Transit and Cloudflare WAN). We are a team of engineers and product managers who obsess over quickly building products that solve real customer problems, using techniques that take advantage of Cloudflare's unique architecture and global network footprint. About the role We're looking for engineers who like hard distributed systems problems: keeping state consistent across hundreds of data centers, changing configuration safely while traffic is flowing, and deciding what to do when parts of the system fail. You don't need to be a networking expert; we'll help you learn the rest. Some of the things you'd work on: • The APIs and control plane services that take a customer's network configuration (tunnels, routes, health checks) and push it to every server in our network, quickly and safely • Tunnel health checks that run from every data center, and the logic that reroutes customer traffic when a path degrades • An anycast BGP implementation where customer sessions survive being moved between servers and data centers, built partly on Cloudflare's own developer platform • Experimental tooling that catch routing flaps and failures before customers do • Getting logs and analytics back to customers so they can see what their networks are doing You'll build, deploy, and operate these systems on top of one of the largest edge networks in the world. You'll partner with some of the best engineers and product managers, on our teams and across Cloudflare, to solve big problems that have a meaningful impact in the lives of our customers and users. We're looking for curious, empathetic engineers who are excited to join us in changing the way networking is done! Examples of desirable skills, knowledge, and experience • Experience designing, building, and operating distributed systems: consistency, replication, failure handling, and safe rollouts • Strong programming experience in Go or Rust, or a similar language and an interest in Go or Rust • Designing and running APIs and backend services, including databases (e.g., PostgreSQL) and asynchronous job processing • Experience with configuration distribution, service discovery, or other systems that push state to many machines • Debugging production systems across many services and machines • Networking fundamentals (IP, routing, BGP, tunnels) are a plus, but not required • Operating what you build: on-call, incident response, and root cause analysis • Reading unfamiliar code and safely evolving systems that have been in production for years • Owning work end to end, from design through deployment, and working across teams to land shared solutions • Clear written and verbal communication; we design in written specs and review each other's work Bonus points • BGP or routing protocol implementations (e.g., GoBGP, BIRD, FRR) • Serverless or edge platforms (e.g., Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects) • Data pipelines (e.g., Kafka, ClickHouse) for logs and analytics • Experience at a cloud provider, ISP, or SD-WAN company Compensation • Compensation may be adjusted depending on work location. • For Champaign IL based hires: Estimated annual salary of $168,000 - $210,000. • For Washington, DC based hires: Estimated annual salary of $185,000 - $230,000. • For Seattle, WA based hires: Estimated annual salary of $185,000 - $230,000. • For Portugal based hires: Estimated annual salary is between €66,000 - 83,000.
