Senior Software Engineer (Go + Automation)

Our client is a fast-growing, US-based accounting firm from Charleston, South Carolina, built around an AI-enabled bookkeeping model. Its team of US-based, credentialed bookkeepers handles bookkeeping, monthly close, payroll, AR/AP and tax-ready reporting for 100+ small and mid-sized businesses across the United States. Its proprietary platform takes on the repetitive work of reconciliations, transaction categorization, anomaly detection and vendor spend review, so the accountants can focus on judgment and advisory work. The company is now opening a new technology hub in Krakow and is looking for its first engineer to help build it. You will report to the founder and own the product end to end: backend services in Go, the PostgreSQL data layer, a Vue.js/TypeScript frontend, and integrations with banks, accounting platforms, payroll and AP systems. You will also build AI and LLM-powered automations that change how accounting work gets done. This is a founding role. The plan is to grow the Krakow team to 10–15 engineers over the next year and to 30+ after that. You will set the technical foundations, help hire and onboard the next engineers, and have a clear path to a Tech Lead role within 12–18 months. Key takeaways: Role & Domain: Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack, Founding Engineer, Fintech, Accounting / Bookkeeping Automation, AI Methodology & Tools: Go (Golang), Vue.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, AWS, REST APIs, third-party integrations, LLMs / AI automation Working model: Remote at the start, then hybrid. Once the first 2–3 team members are hired, the company will open a co-working space in Krakow and the role will move to a hybrid, in-person model. Location: Krakow Recruitment process: • A call with Motife recruiter - 30 min • A call with the hiring manager - 1h • Technical online interview - 1h Responsibilities: • Design, build and maintain backend services and APIs in Go. • Develop and own frontend features in Vue.js and TypeScript. • Design and evolve the PostgreSQL data model. • Integrate third-party APIs: banks, accounting platforms, payroll, AP and payment providers. • Build automations, including LLM-powered features, that reduce manual work in bookkeeping, reconciliation and month-end close. • Own features end to end, from idea and architecture through deployment and maintenance on AWS. • Work directly with the founder and the US-based accounting team to turn real operational problems into product solutions. • Set engineering standards, tooling and best practices for the new Krakow hub. • Help recruit, onboard and mentor the next engineers as the team grows. Requirements: • 5+ years of experience as a full stack engineer. • Strong Go skills for backend services and APIs. • Production experience with Vue.js and TypeScript. • Ability to own backend, database (PostgreSQL) and frontend work on your own. • Experience integrating third-party APIs. • Strong ownership and leadership skills: you are comfortable being the first engineer and want to help build and lead a growing team. • Fluent English. • Must be eligible to work in Poland. Nice to have: • Experience building LLM-powered features or AI automations. • AWS cloud experience. • Background in fintech or accounting software. • Interest in growing into a Tech Lead role within 12–18 months. What do we offer: • UoP Contract.
