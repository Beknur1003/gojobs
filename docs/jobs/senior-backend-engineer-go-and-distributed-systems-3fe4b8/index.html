Company: GraphSight Location: Lugano, Switzerland Employment Type: Full-time (Preferred) / Part-time (Optional) Department: Engineering & Technology Experience: 5+ years Primary Programming Language: Go (Golang) Specialization: Backend Engineering, Distributed Systems, Ethereum Infrastructure About GraphSight GraphSight is a technology company based in Lugano, Switzerland, focused on developing advanced artificial intelligence solutions for renewable energy forecasting. We leverage Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and proprietary deep-learning models to improve renewable energy production forecasting accuracy through advanced data reconstruction, spatial and temporal modelling, and enhanced predictive capabilities. Our technology helps energy traders, renewable energy operators, portfolio managers, and aggregators make better-informed decisions through accurate and adaptable solar production forecasts. GraphSight develops forecasting solutions across individual renewable energy assets, portfolios, and European electricity bidding zones. We aim to combine scientific innovation, advanced AI, and scalable software infrastructure to support a more sustainable, cleaner, and energy-efficient future. About the Role We are looking for an experienced Senior Go Backend Engineer to join our engineering team and contribute to the development of scalable, reliable, and high-performance backend infrastructure. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining backend services, distributed systems, data processing infrastructure, and blockchain-related integrations using Go (Golang). You will work closely with our AI and machine learning engineers to build reliable backend systems that support GraphSight's forecasting platform, data pipelines, external integrations, and real-time information processing. The role will also involve developing and integrating Ethereum-based blockchain infrastructure, exploring decentralized technologies, and building secure and reliable blockchain-enabled backend services where relevant to our technology roadmap. We are looking for a strong Go engineer with experience in backend architecture, distributed systems, API development, and blockchain technologies, who is interested in contributing to innovative AI and renewable energy solutions. Key Responsibilities 1. Go Backend Engineering - Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend applications and services using Go (Golang). - Build high-performance REST APIs, gRPC services, and microservice architectures. - Develop asynchronous and concurrent processing systems using Go routines, channels, and synchronization mechanisms. - Design reliable backend architectures for data-intensive applications. - Integrate backend services with databases, external APIs, and internal systems. - Develop efficient data ingestion, transformation, and processing services. - Optimize application performance, scalability, reliability, and resource utilization. - Write clean, maintainable, testable, and production-ready Go code. 2. Distributed Systems & Data Infrastructure - Design and implement distributed backend services and communication mechanisms. - Develop reliable data processing and event-driven architectures. - Build systems capable of handling large volumes of structured and time-series data. - Implement messaging, asynchronous jobs, and background processing. - Work with database architecture, caching, and data storage optimization. - Develop monitoring, logging, error handling, and system observability. - Ensure backend services remain reliable, secure, and scalable as the platform grows. 3. AI & Renewable Energy Platform Integration - Collaborate with AI and machine learning engineers to integrate forecasting models into production backend systems. - Develop backend services that support GraphSight's solar production forecasting platform. - Build APIs and data interfaces for weather information, solar production forecasts, and operational signals. - Integrate data from external weather providers, forecasting systems, and renewable energy assets. - Develop data ingestion and processing services for asset-level, portfolio-level, and zonal forecasting. - Support the delivery and distribution of forecasting results through REST APIs and other data exchange mechanisms. - Help improve the performance, reliability, and scalability of the infrastructure supporting AI-powered forecasting services. 4. Ethereum & Blockchain Infrastructure - Develop and integrate Ethereum-based backend services using Go. - Work with Ethereum nodes and execution clients, particularly Geth (Go Ethereum) and/or Erigon. - Develop services that interact with Ethereum networks through JSON-RPC and WebSocket interfaces. - Implement blockchain transaction monitoring, processing, and event indexing. - Develop blockchain data integration and synchronization services. - Implement secure transaction signing, verification, and cryptographic operations. - Explore blockchain-based solutions for data integrity, traceability, transparency, and decentralized infrastructure. - Integrate blockchain services with existing backend systems and external applications. - Evaluate opportunities to apply distributed ledger technology to renewable energy and energy-market use cases. Required Technical Skills - Strong professional experience with Go (Golang). - Advanced knowledge of Go concurrency, goroutines, channels, and synchronization. - Experience designing and developing scalable backend systems and microservices. - Strong understanding of distributed systems and backend architecture. - Experience developing REST APIs, gRPC services, and WebSocket communication. - Knowledge of HTTP, TCP/IP, networking protocols, and asynchronous processing. - Experience working with PostgreSQL and other database technologies. - Knowledge of Redis, caching, and data storage optimization. - Experience with Docker, Linux, Git, and CI/CD pipelines. - Strong debugging, testing, and performance optimization skills. - Understanding of software security and cryptographic fundamentals. Ethereum & Blockchain Experience - Practical experience integrating with Ethereum blockchain networks. - Familiarity with Geth and/or Erigon. - Understanding of Ethereum architecture and transaction processing. - Experience with Ethereum JSON-RPC APIs and WebSocket subscriptions. - Knowledge of blockchain nodes, blocks, transactions, and event monitoring. - Understanding of smart contract interactions and EVM fundamentals. - Knowledge of cryptographic hashing and digital signatures. Preferred Qualifications - Experience developing production-grade Go backend infrastructure. - Experience working with Ethereum clients or blockchain infrastructure. - Experience with blockchain indexing and event-driven systems. - Familiarity with AI/ML platform integration and data-intensive applications. - Experience with time-series databases and large-scale data processing. - Knowledge of cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes. - Experience with message brokers and distributed task processing. - Familiarity with renewable energy, electricity markets, or energy trading technologies. - Experience working in an AI, machine learning, or scientific software environment. - Contributions to open-source Go or Ethereum projects. Technical Stack Programming Language - Go (Golang) Backend Development - REST APIs - gRPC - JSON-RPC - WebSockets - Microservices - Event-driven architecture Blockchain - Ethereum - Geth - Erigon - EVM - Ethereum JSON-RPC Databases & Data Infrastructure - PostgreSQL - Redis - Time-series databases - Data pipelines - Event processing Infrastructure & DevOps - Docker - Kubernetes - Linux - Git - CI/CD Distributed Systems - Concurrent programming - P2P networking - Asynchronous processing - Distributed data systems What We Offer - The opportunity to work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, renewable energy, and advanced software engineering. - The opportunity to contribute to innovative technologies supporting the global energy transition. - Direct collaboration with AI and machine learning engineers. - Exposure to advanced forecasting platforms, distributed systems, and blockchain technologies. - A technically challenging environment where engineering innovation and scientific research meet practical applications. - The opportunity to contribute to the development of scalable technology products within a growing technology company. Ideal Candidate The ideal candidate is a highly motivated Go Backend Engineer with strong software engineering fundamentals and experience developing scalable backend systems. You should be comfortable working with distributed architectures, data-intensive applications, external integrations, and blockchain infrastructure. You should also be interested in applying advanced software engineering technologies to real-world challenges in artificial intelligence and renewable energy. We value engineers who take ownership of their work, think critically about architecture, and enjoy solving complex technical problems.
