At Dragos, the mission is personal. The systems we protect deliver the water you drink, power your home, and keep the hospitals your community depends on running. Those critical infrastructure systems that power our civilization around the world are under attack every day by adversaries. When those systems fail, people are immediately at risk. We are the global leader in xOT cybersecurity, combining technology, threat intelligence, and expert services. The people here chose this work because they understand what is at stake. Here, you will find a remote-first mission-driven team across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and APAC built on authenticity, transparency, and trust. If safeguarding the systems that protect your family, friends, and community is the kind of work that matters to you, you are in the right place. About the Role: We are seeking a Staff Software Engineer to raise the bar on how our Device Engineering team designs and builds software. You'll own the architecture and production quality of the systems that identify, fingerprint, and automate control of the network-connected devices that power critical industrial environments. The device domain is deep and no two devices are alike - but your core strength is software: system design, production rigor, and lifting the engineers around you. You will own complex technical work end-to-end, set the technical direction others build on, and partner with product, threat research, and reverse engineering peers to deliver capabilities that defend our customers' operations. Responsibilities: • Architect and evolve our device interaction application, owning its hardest components end-to-end and setting the design standards the team builds on • Extend and improve a large, long-lived codebase on its own terms - understanding why it is the way it is before changing it • Write efficient, well-documented production code, and design the automated test cases and frameworks that keep our device coverage reliable as it grows • Set technical direction on complex device issues and bugs, including hard-to-reproduce and protocol-level problems • Reverse engineer and automate control of network-connected devices, taking on the most complex devices and protocols • Drive identification and fingerprinting of network-connected devices across embedded, IoT, and OT/ICS endpoints • Leverage AI tools and coding assistants as a core part of your day-to-day engineering workflow - including reverse engineering research, code generation, debugging, test creation, and documentation • Build and improve internal tooling and automation that accelerates the Device Engineering team • Mentor engineers, lead code reviews, and grow the technical depth of the team through pairing, design reviews, and knowledge sharing • Collaborate cross-functionally with product, reverse engineering, threat research, and customer-facing teams to translate field needs into engineering outcomes Qualifications: • Track record operating at senior/staff scope - owning system design and influencing technical direction beyond your own code - typically 8+ years building and shipping production systems • Strong backend/systems engineering in a modern typed language; our stack is Node.js/TypeScript and Go, and we expect a strong engineer to ramp into it quickly • Depth in production software practice: testing strategy, CI/CD, observability, and operating what you build • Proficiency with relational databases, including schema design and query optimization for large datasets • Strong command of Unix/Linux environments, including systems-level debugging and performance analysis • Working proficiency with Docker and containerized development and deployment workflows • Solid understanding of network transport and application layer protocols (TCP/IP, TLS, HTTP, and common embedded/industrial protocols) • Aptitude for reverse engineering - network protocol analysis, traffic-capture interpretation, and device behavior reconstruction - or a strong track record of picking up unfamiliar, under-documented systems • Demonstrated ability to mentor engineers, lead technical design discussions, and influence direction across teams • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, evolving, remote-first environment Strong Plus: • Experience with ICS/OT environments, embedded systems, or xIoT device security • Go experience specifically • Security background Compensation: • Salary: $192,000 • Competitive Equity Package • Comprehensive Benefits Plan #LI-LF1 #LI-REMOTE Dragos is an Equal Opportunity Employer and considers applicants for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, orientation, national origin, age, disability, genetics, or any other basis forbidden under federal, state, or local laws.
