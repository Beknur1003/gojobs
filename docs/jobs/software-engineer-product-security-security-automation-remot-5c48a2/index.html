Ready to join a mission that matters? The future of cybersecurity starts with you. About the Role: Help us protect the Security Cloud from the most advanced threats! As a Software Engineer in Product Security, you will be part of a team responsible for developing and deploying security tooling and automation across our Product Security organization. Your team will ensure that our data platforms, operational tools, and security harnesses scale with CrowdStrike’s massive growth and success. You will develop a platform of applications, APIs, and frameworks that allows engineers across Product Security to safeguard our global, multi-cloud production environment! What You’ll Do: • Be a technical contributor on a global software engineering team in Product Security, building and operating robust and scalable systems that drive our security mission • Design and implement software & automation tools for security use cases such as software supply chain security, AI-powered vulnerability discovery, and visibility of a global multi-cloud platform • Partner closely with teams throughout Product Security to develop security tooling strategy, through risk assessments and architectural deep dives • Own SRE excellence, CICD, and datastore operations for mission-critical security services, implementing performance monitoring and response processes to ensure flawless performance during critical security responses What You’ll Need: • Experience in a Software Engineering role (event-driven systems, data ingestion pipelines, internal automation tools, internal monitoring tools) • Experience developing in Golang, Python, React, Angular, and/or Typescript. • In-depth understanding of CI/CD pipelines, containers, software cryptographic signing, and OSS dependencies • Experience with data engineering and datastore/queue operations (OpenSearch, Valkey, Postgres, Kafka, SQS, etc.), including technology selection and owning the health, capacity, and maintenance of those systems in production • Experience managing end-to-end SDLC, including design, implementation, and delivery, with a focus on stakeholder engagement and mission outcomes • Experience with Generative AI, both as a productivity tool and creating services that leverage LLMs Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes. • Experience with configuration management & orchestration software such as Ansible, Chef, Salt, Puppet, etc. • Experience in a Product Security role (product platforms, customer facing applications).
