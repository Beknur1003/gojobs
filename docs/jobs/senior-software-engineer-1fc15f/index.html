Build the Future with Us — EquipmentShare is Hiring a Senior Software Engineer At EquipmentShare, we’re not just filling a role — we’re assembling the best team on the planet to build something that’s never been built before. We’re on a mission to transform an industry that’s been stuck in the past by empowering contractors and communities through innovative technology, real-time support, and a team that truly cares. We’re hiring a Senior Software Engineer at our Corporate Headquarters in Columbia, MO, and we’re looking for someone who’s ready to grow with us, bring energy and drive to their work, and help us build the future of construction. This position is fully remote. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to help us continue to build the next evolution of our data and AI platform in a scalable, performant and customer-centric oriented architecture. Our main tech stack includes : AWS, Kubernetes, Python, Kafka, Flink, Snowflake, OpenSearch, PostgreSQL, TypeScript, React, CDKTF/Terraform. If you have production scale experience in a different stack (Go, Java, Scala, C#, etc.) and are interested in moving to a new stack, we should chat. Primary Responsibilities • Design, build and maintain our data and AI platform to enable automation and self-service for data scientists, analytics engineers and analysts. • Collaborate with Product Managers, Designers, Engineers, Data Scientists and Data Analysts to take ideas from concept to production at scale. • Exert your passion and use deep and wide thinking to shape the technical direction of the platform. • Collaborate with Analysts, Data Scientists, Product Managers, Designers and Engineers to take ideas from concept to delivery. Balancing the desire to ship code with the responsibility to get it right. • Contribute to our culture improving how we deliver as a team. Helping us to leave things better than we find them and making it easier for us to get stuff done. • Support, coach and mentor Engineers within our team by sharing your insight, knowledge and experience as we learn and grow together. Why EquipmentShare? Because we do things differently — and we think you’ll feel it from day one. We’re a people-first company powered by cutting-edge technology. That means our proprietary T3 platform doesn’t just run our business — it also makes your job easier, safer, and more connected. Whether you’re behind the wheel, under the hood, leading a branch, or closing deals — tech supports you, and you drive us forward. We’re a team of problem-solvers, go-getters, and builders. And we’re looking for teammates who take pride in doing meaningful work and want to be part of building something special.
