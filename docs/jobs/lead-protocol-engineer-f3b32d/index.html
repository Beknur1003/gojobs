Job#: 3060032 Job Description: Job Title: Lead Protocol Engineer Location: Concord, CA (Hybrid) Contract Duration: 12 Months Note: We are not able to consider C2C or third-party submissions Role Overview Our organization is seeking a Lead Protocol Engineer to contribute to an institutional Digital Asset Platform designed to ensure regulatory alignment while preserving transaction confidentiality. The professional in this position must architect the core settlement engine at the intersection of distributed systems and applied cryptography. The responsibilities require translating complex mathematical proofs into production-grade Go code to support critical financial infrastructure. Key Responsibilities • Architect and implement a custom blockchain application utilizing Go, Cosmos SDK, and CometBFT. • Implement and optimize privacy-preserving primitives, including Pedersen Commitments and Bulletproofs, to ensure operations proceed without a trusted setup. • Engineer custom ABCI++ extensions to deploy an on-chain Multi-Party Computation mixing network. • Design a custom KVStore state machine that manages homomorphic encrypted balances rather than plaintext integers. • Optimize cryptographic operations for low latency and high throughput within a distributed architecture. • Construct secure off-chain query layers and permissioned regulatory auditing mechanisms. • Consult on complex initiatives and contribute to engineering deliverables across specialty software disciplines. Required Qualifications Education: High school diploma, associate degree, bachelor's degree, or equivalent demonstrated through training, work experience, or military service. Experience: A minimum of five years of specialty software engineering experience, including at least three years of backend or systems programming and three years of experience with Yugabyte distributed database architecture. Technical Skills: Professional proficiency in Go (Golang), at least one year of experience with BFT consensus algorithms, peer-to-peer networking, and state replication, and at least six months of experience with Elliptic Curve Cryptography. Preferred Qualifications • Advanced experience with Cosmos SDK and CometBFT, specifically in developing custom modules and modifying consensus logic. • Demonstrated ability to translate academic preprints and mathematical specifications into functional codebases. • Experience implementing Multi-Party Computation or Verifiable Secret Sharing schemes. • Familiarity with the Ristretto255 curve, Postgres, Liquibase, or cryptographic libraries such as gnark-crypto. • Professional background in financial technology, payment networks, or high-frequency transaction systems. • Understanding of Account Model versus UTXO Model trade-offs in privacy-preserving distributed ledgers. Company Overview & Culture Our organization addresses privacy and compliance challenges within institutional finance. Technical autonomy is supported, providing engineering professionals direct input into architectural decisions, design patterns, and cryptographic library selection. The engineering environment emphasizes rigorous technical standards, collaboration, and high-impact systems design.
