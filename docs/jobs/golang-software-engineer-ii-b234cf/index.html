This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Golang Software Engineer II based in India. We are looking for a skilled Golang Software Engineer to join a global Security and Reliability Engineering (SRE) team focused on infrastructure automation and operational excellence. In this role, you will design and develop scalable backend services, automation tools, and integrations that strengthen network security operations and infrastructure reliability. You will work on solutions that automate network and server provisioning, configuration, monitoring, and troubleshooting across global environments. Collaborating with SRE, Installation, and Sustainment teams, you will identify opportunities to eliminate manual processes and improve operational efficiency. You will also contribute to cloud-native deployments, CI/CD pipelines, and automated incident remediation workflows. This is an exciting opportunity to build impactful engineering solutions using modern technologies while supporting large-scale, distributed infrastructure environments. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable backend services and automation solutions using Golang. • Build and maintain RESTful APIs and integrations with infrastructure management platforms and enterprise systems. • Develop automation workflows for network and server provisioning, configuration management, monitoring, and troubleshooting. • Create and maintain integrations with platforms such as Cisco NSO, NetBox, ServiceNow, and other infrastructure management tools. • Develop automated health checks, diagnostic capabilities, and remediation workflows to improve infrastructure reliability. • Support the development, maintenance, and optimization of CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment processes. • Build and support containerized applications and cloud-native solutions across distributed infrastructure environments. • Collaborate with Installation, Sustainment, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) teams to identify operational challenges and automation opportunities. • Improve system reliability, observability, scalability, and overall operational performance. • Implement reusable and scalable automation workflows that reduce manual operational effort and improve consistency. • Contribute to infrastructure monitoring and incident management improvements, helping accelerate troubleshooting and resolution. • Support network installation, provisioning, and operational handovers through effective automation solutions. • Identify performance bottlenecks, investigate technical issues, and implement improvements to ensure reliable services across global infrastructure environments. Requirements: • Strong hands-on experience in Golang (Go) software development, including concurrency, goroutines, and REST API development. • Practical experience developing backend services, automation tools, and integrations for infrastructure environments. • Experience with Python or Bash scripting for automation, diagnostics, and operational tasks. • Good understanding of Linux operating systems and system administration fundamentals. • Solid knowledge of networking concepts, including TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, and HTTPS. • Understanding of distributed systems, infrastructure architecture, and service reliability principles. • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Git, and CI/CD pipelines. • Familiarity with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), particularly Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE). • Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus and Grafana. • Strong debugging, troubleshooting, and analytical problem-solving abilities. • Ability to design reliable, maintainable, and scalable software solutions. • Good collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross-functional engineering and infrastructure teams. • Ability to manage technical challenges, prioritize tasks, and deliver solutions in a dynamic engineering environment. Preferred qualifications: • Experience with Cisco NSO, NetBox, or other network automation platforms. • Knowledge of network automation protocols and technologies such as NETCONF, RESTCONF, gRPC, or gNMI. • Experience developing infrastructure automation solutions and self-healing workflows. • Familiarity with enterprise networking and physical security infrastructure. • Previous experience working in global Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), infrastructure operations, or managed services environments. • Understanding of large-scale infrastructure deployments and operational support across geographically distributed locations. Benefits: • Full-time employment opportunity. • Fully remote working arrangement within India. • Opportunity to work on large-scale infrastructure automation and global SRE initiatives. • Exposure to modern technologies, including Golang, Kubernetes, Docker, GCP, and cloud-native architectures. • Hands-on experience with enterprise networking platforms and infrastructure automation tools. • Opportunity to develop scalable backend services and innovative automation solutions. • Collaboration with international engineering, infrastructure, and SRE teams. • Opportunities for continuous technical learning and professional development. • Opportunity to contribute to improving infrastructure security, reliability, and operational efficiency across global environments.
