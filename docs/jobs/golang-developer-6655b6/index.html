About the role • We're looking for a Backend Engineer who specialises in Golang to help build and scale the core services that power Hyperlayer's orchestration engine. You'll be working on high-throughput, mission-critical systems that sit at the heart of how our financial institution clients design, launch and manage Smart Accounts — so performance, reliability and correctness really matter. • You'll work closely with product, platform and other engineering teams to design APIs, evolve our service architecture, and ship features that our clients depend on in production. This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys writing clean, well-tested Go and cares about the operational health of what they build, not just shipping code. What you'll do • Key Responsibilities: • Contribute to the ongoing development of Hyperlayer's flagship platform, delivering the backend services at the heart of our programmable banking product. Design & development • Design, build and maintain high-performance backend services and APIs in Go, as part of Hyperlayer's core orchestration platform. • Contribute to the architecture of new features and services, balancing pragmatism, scalability and long-term maintainability. • Write clean, well-tested, idiomatic Go code, and participate actively in code review to help maintain a high engineering bar. Systems & reliability • Work with event-driven and asynchronous patterns (message queues, event streaming) to build resilient, decoupled services. • Design and evolve data models and queries against relational databases, with an eye on performance and correctness at scale. • Support the operational health of services in production, including monitoring, alerting and incident response for systems you own. Collaboration & delivery • Partner with Product and other Engineering teams to translate requirements into well-scoped technical designs. • Collaborate with DevOps/Platform on deployment, containerisation and infrastructure for the services you build. • Contribute to technical decisions, documentation and best practices across the wider Engineering team. Qualifications • Strong professional experience building backend services in Go (Golang), including a solid grasp of concurrency and Go idioms. • •Experience designing and building RESTful APIs and/or microservices in a production environment. • Working knowledge of relational databases (e.g. PostgreSQL), including schema design and writing efficient queries. • Experience with containerisation and orchestration tools such as Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP or Azure) and modern CI/CD practices. • Experience with message queuing or event streaming technologies (e.g. Kafka) is a strong plus. • Prior experience in fintech, financial services, or another regulated, high-reliability environment is desirable but not essential. • Strong communication skills and comfort collaborating closely with cross-functional teams.
