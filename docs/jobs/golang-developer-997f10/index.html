About the Role We are seeking a Senior Golang Developer to join a cross-functional engineering team responsible for building and evolving large-scale backend systems. This position plays a key role in delivering high-performance services that support significant transaction volumes and business-critical operations. The successful candidate will contribute to technical direction, service design, system scalability, and engineering best practices while working closely with product owners, QA engineers, and fellow software engineers. The role combines hands-on development, technical leadership, production support, and continuous improvement across modern cloud-native environments. Responsibilities • Contribute to technical planning, solution design, backlog refinement, and engineering standards within a self-managed team. • Design, develop, and enhance high-traffic, low-latency services using Go, taking ownership of complex functionality and technical decisions. • Participate in architecture discussions to ensure services remain scalable, maintainable, and performant. • Use benchmarking, profiling, and controlled experiments to guide technical decisions and validate approaches with measurable results. • Deliver production changes safely through feature flags, staged deployments, rollback strategies, and operational monitoring. • Investigate and resolve production issues including race conditions, goroutine leaks, memory consumption challenges, and latency problems. • Write secure, maintainable, and well-structured code aligned with established design principles and development standards. • Collaborate with QA teams to strengthen automated testing practices and improve release quality. • Support and mentor other engineers through code reviews, knowledge sharing, and day-to-day technical collaboration. • Contribute to the modernization of legacy systems and advocate for improvements backed by evidence and measurable outcomes. • Produce clear technical documentation, including specifications, recommendations, and architectural decisions. Requirements • At least 5 years of backend software development experience, including 3 or more years working with Go in production environments. • Strong understanding of Go concurrency, including goroutines, channels, context management, mutexes, wait groups, and atomic operations. • Demonstrated ability to identify and prevent race conditions, deadlocks, and goroutine leaks. • Experience designing and optimizing REST and/or gRPC APIs for performance-sensitive systems. • Good knowledge of distributed systems, microservices, service communication patterns, retries, timeouts, and fault handling. • Hands-on experience with Go performance testing and diagnostics using tools such as pprof, benchmarks, and the race detector. • Experience operating production workloads through CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes. • Knowledge of observability practices using metrics, logging, and tracing platforms such as Grafana, Datadog, or OpenTelemetry. • Practical use of AI-assisted software development tools. • Strong written and verbal English communication skills. • Comfort working collaboratively in self-organized teams and contributing across multiple technologies when required. • Willingness to take ownership throughout the full software lifecycle, from development through production support.
