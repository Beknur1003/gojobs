Position Overview Qube Health is seeking Software Engineers to join the team and help us build world-class user experiences across all of Qube Health's products. You will be a key contributor to the development of our payments, credit and data platforms, and ensure that it allows us to scale with the team as we introduce new products alongside. As a Software Engineer, you are a team player, comfortable with both front-end and back-end technologies, development frameworks and third-party libraries. You will collaborate closely with the technical and non-technical teams in order to develop and deploy high-quality products and features. Your work will allow customers to achieve their goals with our software and our teams to operate more efficiently as our business grows in both size and scope. At Qube Health, Software Engineers own the components involved throughout the product life cycle, from idea generation, design, prototyping, execution and shipping. Key Responsibilities • Own Software Components/Systems of the Product (Design, Create, Deploy, Maintain). • Maintain high-quality, responsive designs and components. • Continuously improve Product components. • Work and communicate effectively with teams to provide solutions. Technical Requirements • Strong understanding of programming fundamentals, Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), and Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). • Proficiency in at least one programming language: Golang, Java, or Python. • Basic understanding of REST APIs, HTTP methods, status codes, and JSON. • Familiarity with SQL and relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL. • Understanding of database concepts, including joins, indexes, primary keys, and foreign keys. Good to Have • Knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, XML, jQuery. • Knowledge of Node.js and Flutter. • Understanding of Apache Web Server and NGINX. • Familiarity with MongoDB and UI/UX design basics. • Willingness to learn and improve on Server and client-side technologies. • Keen to work in the Mobile Programming space. Experience Trainee Software Engineer, less than 0.5 years of experience at the time of applying. At least one or two short internship experiences preferred.
