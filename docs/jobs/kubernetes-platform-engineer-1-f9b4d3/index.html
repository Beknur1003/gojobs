Kubernetes Platform Engineer / 1

inetum2 Warsaw, Masovian Voivodeship, pl опубликовано 12 октября 2026

Гибрид
KubernetesTerraformAzurePrometheusLinuxCI/CDPython
We are seeking a Kubernetes Platform Engineer to join our Cloud Services team. The successful candidate will be responsible for developing and operating a large-scale Kubernetes environment in a Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) model. This environment includes several bare-metal, multi-tenant clusters running over 18,000 pods in over 1,300 distinct namespaces on over 130 hardware servers. Main tasks: • Ensure optimal maintenance of the platform through automation, monitoring, and performance measurements. • Participate in no-downtime physical migration of clusters and servers to a new data center. • Further develop the platform’s self-service capabilities. • Support our internal users in containerizing their existing applications. • Actively participate in the design and development of our platform. Must have requirements: • Experience with on-premise Kubernetes • Proficiency in Prometheus • Strong understanding of networking • Expertise in Linux Nice to have requirements: • Experience with Istio • Proficiency in Golang • Familiarity with Kyverno • Experience with Gitlab CI • Knowledge of Python and Bash scripting • Experience with Terraform and Ansible • Familiarity with Calico • Experience with Azure Hybrid work model: 3 days from the office, 2 days of home office. Office location: Warsaw, Poznań or Lublin. Thank you for your interest in our offer. We will contact selected candidates whose experience best matches the requirements of the role.
