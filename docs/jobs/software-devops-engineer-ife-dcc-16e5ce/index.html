From quantum applications and artificial intelligence to cybersecurity and 6G innovation, our solutions empower critical decisions rooted in human intelligence. In Singapore, Thales has been a trusted partner since 1973, originally focused on aerospace activities in the Asia-Pacific region. With 2,000 employees across three local sites, we deliver cutting-edge solutions across aerospace (including air traffic management), defence and security, and digital identity and cybersecurity sectors. Together, we’re shaping the future by enabling customers to make pivotal decisions that safeguard communities and power progress. In-Flyt Experience (IFE) In its business shift, Thales In-Flyt Experience Business Line is massively digitalizing its offers with comprehensive digital in-flight entertainment solutions. Over the past years, while evolving our solution, we have engaged with premier airlines across the globe to ensure we are thoroughly addressing their pain points. In parallel, Thales IFE has engaged with recognized leaders in the consumer world to enrich IFE solutions on proven hardware technologies and software development methodologies from some of the largest digital players. Thales IFE implements a technological shift, by bridging the gap with the consumer world into end-to-end platforms, spanning both on the ground and onboard the aircraft, with the onboard systems are now considered as a simple extension of the ground infrastructure. Digital Competence Center (DCC) Thales IFE has decided to create a leading technology center in Singapore for its IFE Digital Engineering. It will leverage on unique digital skillset from Singapore and neighbouring countries on Cloud engineering. Thanks to a multi-year strategic plan, Thales is locating at WeWork@Suntec, a center that will be the Cloud Center of Excellence for Thales IFE Business Line. Initiatives towards public cloud will be lead from Singapore, it covers Site Reliability expertise, Security analysts, and push-to-cloud projects. High-end cloud engineers are expected to be part of such project and rely on Agile and modern leadership practices. Responsibilities: • Design, develop and deploy IFE digital solutions with focus on high availability and scalability. • Drive collaboration within Agile teams of Engineers and Product Owners and UI Designers to build new product features • Collaborate closely with squad engineers and become a valued member of an autonomous, cross- functional squad. • Collaboration with Cloud CoE team to improve the tooling, develop a safe, secure framework for fast deployment of Inflyt Experience digital solutions. • Be hands-on and deliver on frontend applications and backend platform services. • Build and operate infrastructure, toolset and CI/CD pipelines. • Develop individually to continue to grow and provide training to individuals and other functional groups as needed • Continuously improve IFE digital solutions by innovating on the services offered. Requirements: • Bachelor's Degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science or any relevant field • Minimum 2 years of software development experience • Experience in web/mobile application development, networking or systems software development. • Experience with cloud platforms, Docker, CI tools, and configuration management tools. • Experience working with virtualization and clustered orchestration (Kubernetes). • Experience with infrastructure provisioning tools (Terraform). • Experience in Java, C/C++, C#, Python, JavaScript, NodeJs or Golang. • Good knowledge of public APIs for web (e.g. REST APIs and/or API Gateways) • Experienced in Agile software development and a culture of continuous improvement.
