ClickHouse is the open-source, real-time analytical database behind a lot of today's AI. More than 5,000 companies build on ClickHouse, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Tesla and Meta. As a Forward-Deployed Engineer ("FDE") on our team, you work directly with ClickHouse Enterprise and strategic customers to research and implement new prototypes and features based on ClickHouse Cloud and Product solutions. You're the software engineer who adapts ClickHouse for some of our largest customers. When an enterprise needs ClickHouse to run in a way the product doesn't support yet, you work with their engineering and security teams to define what's needed, then build it with our engineering teams so it ships as part of the product. As more large companies move onto ClickHouse, understanding our customers' needs and meeting their requirements by integrating and developing our products and solutions is key to success in this role. You will partner closely with ClickHouse Engineering and Development teams to ensure feature development and fixes are aligned with the ClickHouse roadmap and product strategy, while helping shape product direction into new market opportunities in close collaboration with our users, customers, prospects, and partners. What you'll do as an FDE • Embed with customers and prospects to identify requirements and then design and implement product solutions for these use cases • Collaborate with engineers, developers, product managers, TAM Architects, and Sales to align priorities and delivery • Turn recurring customer requirements and deployment constraints into reusable product capabilities rather than one-off solutions • Be the customer's technical voice inside engineering, and keep the customer clear on what's being built and when • Write the documentation and runbooks customers and our support teams rely on What you'll do in this role • Work with a customer's platform, security, and networking teams, on-site and remotely, to research, design, and build backend services and platform capabilities for ClickHouse BYOC and Private, combining reusable Cloud components with purpose-built implementations • Adapt and integrate existing ClickHouse components for Kubernetes-based deployments in customer-controlled cloud and self-hosted environments • Be the customer-facing interface to help build and maintain what customers actually implement, in collaboration with our ClickHouse Data Plane / Infrastructure team and other engineering teams: Helm charts, Kubernetes operators, and deployment tooling • Design for customer-controlled AWS, GCP and Azure environments, including VPCs, private connectivity and network-restricted deployments • Work through security requirements in detail: identity and access, encryption, certificates and PKI, TDE, authentication, data protection What you'll bring • 5+ years building and shipping production software, including infrastructure or platform systems • Strong Kubernetes development experience. You've built or extended operators or controllers, not only run workloads on Kubernetes • You write production code. Software development experience in Golang, C/C++, Java, or another OOP language • Deep hands-on experience with AWS, GCP and/or Azure, including VPC design, private networking, load balancers and firewalls • Security depth in cloud infrastructure: access controls, encryption, PKI and compliance requirements such as SOC 2 • You've worked directly with external customers' engineering or security teams, whatever your title was • You explain technical trade-offs clearly, to engineers, security teams, and executives • Fluent English Nice to have • Experience shipping software into environments you don't operate: customer sites, on-premises, self-hosted, or air-gapped • Experience with regulated customers, such as financial services or the public sector • Terraform or another infrastructure-as-code tool • Experience with ClickHouse, or with other databases Good to know • Remote (United States), with regular travel to customer sites, up to 35% of your time depending on the project • You'll work between our Support Services team (Consulting, Support, and Learning) and our Product Management and Engineering teams • This role is part of an on-call team. As a global Cloud company, all technical staff at ClickHouse take on some on-call responsibilities • You'll need existing work authorisation in the United States. Some customers also require background checks, in addition to ClickHouse's own. Perks • Flexible work environment - ClickHouse is a globally distributed company and remote-friendly. We currently operate in over 25 countries. • Healthcare - Employer contributions towards your healthcare. • Equity in the company - Every new eligible team member who joins our company receives equity. • Time off - Flexible time off in the US, generous entitlement in other countries. • A USD$500 Home office setup if you’re a remote employee. • Global Gatherings – We believe in the power of in-person connection and offer opportunities to engage with colleagues at company-wide offsites. Culture - We All Shape It As part of a rapidly scaling start-up, you will be instrumental in shaping our culture. Are you interested in finding out more about our culture?
