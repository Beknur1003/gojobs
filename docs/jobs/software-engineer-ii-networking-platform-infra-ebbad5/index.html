Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI. What is The Role In this role, you'll be deeply involved in shaping the future of application networking solutions within the Elastic Cloud, focusing on key areas such as Ingress, security controls, private connectivity, and DNS management. You will play a vital role in ensuring that every interaction with Elasticsearch, Kibana, and the broader Elastic stack is proficient and secure. You will work with various engineering teams. You will face challenges that require careful problem-solving and scalability. Your contributions will not only enhance the performance of our platform but also help lay the groundwork for future innovations in our infrastructure. What You Will Be Doing • Design and implement scalable and robust application networking features to enhance performance. Integrate security controls into networking solutions, ensuring safe interactions. Refactor existing code to improve functionality and maintainability while leveraging AI tools to boost productivity for you and your team. • Analyze performance metrics to pinpoint bottlenecks in application networking. Conduct load testing to assess scalability of networking features and optimize code to enhance response times and resource efficiency. Implement performance monitoring tools while collaborating with cross-functional teams to meet their application networking needs. • Identify and tackle application networking issues to enhance performance. Conduct root cause analysis for network-related incidents and document the troubleshooting processes for future reference. Develop tools aimed at tracing and fixing errors associated with application networking. • Collaborate with Engineering teams across Elastic to discuss and fulfill their Application Networking requirements. What You Bring • Hands-on experience developing software in Golang with an emphasis on networking components that can scale. • Experience working with Kubernetes (Cilium, external-dns, Envoy Gateway & Crossplane) are also great • Knowledge with at least one major cloud provider (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and their networking ecosystem • Proficient in using AI tools to enhance code functionality and maintainability • Facilitated knowledge-sharing initiatives that promoted best practices in application networking across teams • Care about the quality of work shipped — testing, observability, and operability • Curiosity about distributed systems and how services communicate at scale • A collaborative approach: welcoming feedback, sharing context, learning from teammates Compensation for this role is in the form of base salary.
