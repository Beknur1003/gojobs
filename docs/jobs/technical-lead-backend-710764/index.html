About Us: Netcore.ai is a leading AI-powered B2B SaaS Customer Experience and Marketing Technology platform, helping 6,500+ brands across 20+ countries deliver personalized customer experiences at scale. Our engineering teams build highly scalable, cloud-native products that process billions of customer interactions every day. Team: Engineering | Location: Thane, MH (WFO). Role Summary: As a Technical Lead - Backend, you will design, build, and lead the development of scalable backend systems that power Netcore's high-volume customer engagement platform. You will be hands-on with architecture, development, debugging, and technical problem-solving while leading a small team of engineers. This is a role for someone who enjoys solving complex backend problems, building highly available and fault-tolerant systems, and working across engineering and product teams to translate business requirements into reliable technical solutions. What we look for: - Bachelor's/Master's degree in Computer Science/IT, or equivalent practical experience. - 9 - 14 years of experience in backend software development, with strong hands-on experience in Java and/or Python or Golang. - Strong understanding of backend engineering principles, object-oriented programming, data structures, distributed systems, multithreading, concurrency, caching, fault tolerance, and API design. - Strong experience building scalable, highly available, and performance-oriented backend systems. - Hands-on experience with NoSQL databases such as MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis or similar technologies. - Strong understanding of messaging and streaming systems such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar queue-based technologies. - Experience designing application modules and services that can handle high-volume data and traffic reliably. - Strong debugging and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to identify and resolve complex production issues. - Good understanding of software engineering best practices, version control, CI/CD, and agile development methodologies. - Experience working with cloud environments and distributed systems. - Ability to work closely with Product, Engineering, and other cross-functional teams to deliver scalable solutions. - Prior experience leading a small team of engineers, including technical mentoring, task ownership, code/design reviews, and delivery. - Comfortable being hands-on while also driving technical direction and helping the team solve complex engineering challenges. Good to Have: - Exposure to high-throughput systems, real-time data processing, or distributed systems. - Experience with additional backend languages such as Golang. - Experience working in a product/ B2B SaaS environment. - Experience with AI-native products/technology. Why Join Us?: - Build products used by 6,500+ global brands. - Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI/ML projects impacting millions of users. - Be part of a collaborative, innovation-driven team in a fast-growing MarTech company. - Competitive compensation and growth opportunities in a fast-paced environment. - Thrive in a culture of ownership, innovation, and continuous learning. - We'd love to hear how your background can contribute to building the next generation of intelligent marketing solutions.
