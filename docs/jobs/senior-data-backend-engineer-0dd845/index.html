Senior Data Backend Engineer Location: Santa Clara, California, United States Industry: Business Consulting and Services Work Setting: Remote Are you passionate about building robust backend platforms that transform large-scale data into meaningful insights and power next-generation AI solutions? We are seeking an experienced Senior Data Backend Engineer to develop and enhance high-performance data processing systems, scalable services, and intelligent data platforms that support advanced analytics and machine learning initiatives. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, and optimize scalable backend services and data pipelines for processing high-volume, complex datasets. • Build efficient data processing frameworks and workflows to support analytics, machine learning, and AI-driven applications. • Develop and maintain distributed systems that ensure reliability, scalability, and high performance. • Create solutions that streamline data ingestion, transformation, storage, and retrieval processes. • Partner with cross-functional teams to enable seamless deployment and operationalization of machine learning models. • Improve platform efficiency through performance tuning, automation, and best engineering practices. • Contribute to the architecture and evolution of cloud-native data platforms and services. Required Qualifications • 6-8+ years of experience in software engineering, backend development, data engineering, or a related technical field. • Strong programming expertise in Python and/or Golang. • Hands-on experience with distributed computing and large-scale data processing technologies. • Proficiency in SQL and data-intensive application development. • Experience working with technologies such as Kubernetes, Helm, Hive, and Parquet. • Familiarity with vector databases and modern data storage architectures. • Strong understanding of microservices architecture, scalable system design, and cloud-based environments. • Excellent analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Preferred Qualifications • Experience supporting machine learning and AI workflows in production environments. • Knowledge of modern big data ecosystems and data platform optimization techniques. • Exposure to highly scalable data infrastructure, automation frameworks, and performance-focused engineering practices.
