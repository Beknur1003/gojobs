What we’re looking for... ScienceLogic is looking for a Senior Systems Engineer to drive the development, automation, and optimization of our hybrid cloud monitoring solutions. The ideal candidate will have a deep understanding of system architecture, cloud infrastructure, and automation tools to ensure our platform operates with high availability, scalability, and efficiency. As a Senior Systems Engineer, you will play a crucial role in designing, implementing, and maintaining our AI/ML-powered hybrid cloud monitoring platform, SkylarOne. What you’ll be doing... As a Senior Systems Engineer at ScienceLogic, you will work alongside elite engineers to design, deploy, and maintain our state-of-the-art monitoring solutions. Your role will focus on platform modernization, upgrading platform with latest software, and system reliability in a hybrid cloud environment. • Design and implement scalable and resilient system update software to support the SkylarOne hybrid cloud monitoring platform run latest software. • Drive SkylarOne platform modernization initiatives such as OS migrations and technology stack upgrades. • Troubleshoot complex system issues across distributed environments, identifying root causes and implementing effective solutions. • Optimize system performance, reliability, and scalability by analyzing key metrics and implementing best practices. • Participate in Agile Scrum teams to drive continuous improvements in system operations. Qualities you possess... • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. • Strong experience with AI driven software development practices. • 5+ years of experience in systems engineering. • Strong expertise in Linux administration, system and network troubleshooting. • Proficiency in automation and rpm package management tools (dnf, yum etc). • Strong scripting skills in Python and Bash. • Experience with Golang is desired but not mandatory. • Experience with SQL Databases. • Understanding of networking protocols, security best practices, and monitoring tools. • Ability to fluidly change context and priorities based on business needs. · A passion for problem-solving, attention to detail, and a drive for innovation. · Demonstrate strong teamwork skills by actively contributing to group efforts, supporting colleagues, and fostering a positive and cooperative work environment.
