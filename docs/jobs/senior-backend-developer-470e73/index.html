About Us Aonic is an end-to-end drone solutions company. We began by providing drone services and has since evolved into a comprehensive solutions provider - creating our own drones, supplying major brands like DJI, and now expanding into distributing smart home products. With expertise across B2B and B2C segments, we deliver end-to-end solutions through six integrated verticals: agriculture, industrial, services, retail, academy and lifestyle. At Aonic, our mission is to empower businesses and end users with innovative ecosystems that drive efficient living through smart technology. About the Role We are looking for a Backend Developer with strong expertise in NestJS and Golang to design, build, and optimize scalable backend systems. You will be responsible for architecting robust APIs, designing efficient database schemas (PostgreSQL), and ensuring high performance, security, and reliability. The ideal candidate is a problem-solver, a team player, and has a deep understanding of backend best practices, ORM tools, and distributed systems. Key Responsibilities • Design and develop high-performance backend services using NestJS (Node.js) and Golang. • Architect scalable RESTful/gRPC APIs and microservices. • Optimize PostgreSQL databases (schema design, indexing, query optimization). • Implement efficient data access layers using ORMs (TypeORM, Prisma, GORM, etc.). • Troubleshoot and debug complex backend issues (latency, concurrency, memory leaks). • Collaborate with frontend, DevOps, and product teams to deliver high-quality solutions. • Ensure code quality, security, and scalability through best practices (logging, monitoring,testing). • Mentor junior developers and participate in code reviews. About You Education & Experience: • Bachelor’s/Master’s in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience). • Proven track record of designing and deploying scalable backend systems. Must-Have Skills: • 5+ years of backend development experience. • Strong proficiency in NestJS (Node.js), Golang and Bun. • Deep knowledge of PostgreSQL (schema design, query optimization, transactions). • Experience with ORM tools (TypeORM, Prisma, GORM, etc.). • Familiarity with API security (JWT, OAuth2, rate limiting, encryption). • Strong understanding of microservices & distributed systems. • Experience with caching (Redis, Memcached) and message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ). • Proficient in Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure). • Excellent debugging and performance-tuning skills. Nice-to-Have Skills: • API Management tool such as Postman and Insomnia. • Experience with gRPC and Protocol Buffers. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI). • Understanding of event-driven architecture. • Contributions to open-source projects (GitHub/GitLab).
