Responsibilities We are looking for highly motivated individuals who want to work in a startup-like environment and enjoy tough challenges. You will partner with key Azure platform teams to create products that enable customers to adopt Azure to host their network infrastructure and operations. Our market opportunity is huge, and our team is expanding. As a Senior SWE you will contribute to the Azure Local product vision and be responsible for architecting solutions ground-up. Ability to lead complex projects with strong record of end to end delivery of high-quality products is a huge plus. You are willing to learn from our customers and your peers. You excel at customer and stakeholder management.
