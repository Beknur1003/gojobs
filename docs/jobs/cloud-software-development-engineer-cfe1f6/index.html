The Role and Impact As a Cloud Software Development Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in designing, developing, validating, and optimizing software to enable Intel hardware features and enhance cloud deployment models. Key Responsibilities - Develop, optimize, and debug software to enable Intel hardware features and enhance cloud/hybrid cloud/on-prem deployment models. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand partner software stacks and optimize full-stack solutions (firmware, drivers, OS, middleware, SDKs, frameworks, algorithms, and applications). - Drive the development and validation of reference platform software to improve customer designs and ensure maximum utilization of Intel products within the cloud. - Contribute to the creation and optimization of software for specific technology domains such as data services (ingestion, database, analytics), AI/ML, and open-source development projects. - Identify and resolve technical challenges to ensure high-quality, scalable, and performance-driven solutions. - Feature enablement, end-to-end software validation, and debug for next-generation HW-SW systems - Triage complex software, networking, and platform issues across validation, deployment, and production environments. Minimum Qualifications: - Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or related field, with 3+ years of hands-on experience in software development and optimization OR a Master's degree with 2+ years of hands-on experience. - 3+ years of experience working in a software product development team - 3+ years of working experience developing system software for Linux OS - Strong programming skills in C/C++. - Hands on Git and GitHub experience. - Familiarity with cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) and their associated development tools. - Understanding of software optimization techniques and debugging tools. Preferred Qualifications: - Golang/Python/Bash/Shell scripting experience is a plus. - Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams. - Experience with container orchestration tools like Kubernetes or Docker. - Knowledge of AI/ML frameworks and data analytics technologies. - Eagerness to learn, adapt, and grow in a fast-paced, innovative environment. - An understanding of graphics fundamentals, experience with Linux, or Windows based operating systems and a strong knowledge of system architecture is vital to success in this role.
