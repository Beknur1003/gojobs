A quick note about the role: Since 2026, Xplicity has been part of the Present Connection group, bringing our teams and technology communities closer together and creating more opportunities for talented professionals across both companies. Although this opportunity is shared through the Xplicity community, the position is with Present Connection, and the successful candidate will join the Present Connection team. Present Connection is an international technology partner with offices in Lithuania, the Netherlands, and Spain. For more than 14 years, we have worked with companies across Europe, combining software engineering expertise with a strong understanding of business and technology. We bring together experienced technology specialists with a drive to experiment, explore new possibilities, and find better ways forward. We believe technology is more than something businesses use. It can change how they work, grow, and compete. That's why we work alongside our clients to challenge existing ways of working and build solutions that move their business forward.﻿﻿ The Opportunity We are looking for a Mid engineer with a strong inclination towards Site Reliability Engineering to join one of the largest internet service providers in Europe. Your main focus will be on monitoring services, servers and devices – you will join the SMSA (Service Monitoring, Service Assurance) squad, working on managing, enhancing, and implementing monitoring solutions, both third-party industrial-standard software and in-house developed ones. The top priority for the team is to support mission-critical systems in an operational state and perform maintenance of monitoring solutions and services. The position involves working within one dedicated team of engineers across several sub-projects within the client's organization, including managing both passive and proactive monitoring platforms, supporting pipelines, and reacting on incidents. The work scope is not limited to support and maintenance. Due to the vast nature of the existing networks and multiple solutions, it is essential to pursue a policy of unification – the team has the power to affect the solutions' landscape and push improvements that bring a positive impact on the stability and efficiency of network operations. This is a flexible role rather than a single fixed project – you'll work against an evolving backlog of monitoring and service assurance initiatives, and your exact focus areas will be shaped around your strengths as you settle into the team. What We Expect • Solid scripting skills (Bash, PowerShell) • Good operating systems knowledge, with a focus on Linux • Excellent knowledge of networking • Experience with Logstash, Filebeat, Metricbeat, Elasticsearch, Kibana • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes • Experience with Ansible and Git • Experience with passive monitoring suites (Nagios, Icinga, Cacti) • Understanding of active monitoring suites (Selenium, Playwright) • Result-oriented engineering mindset, comfortable working in a standalone mode • Strong English communication skills • Nice to have: Programming experience (Python, Golang, or similar) What We Offer • Gain knowledge in a company with more than ten years of experience in IT sector working with high – performing development teams • You decide work model – hybrid work and flexible work hours • Yearly training budget – get access to world's largest education site and attend internal trainings, workshops • Most flexible health insurance policy with compensation for sports activities, recreation, etc. • Regular performance reviews and transparent remuneration system • Performance evaluation system with clear skill evaluation • Referral program • Mentoring program for your knowledge expansion and experience • Events with guest speakers and meetups for learning and know-how sharing • Quarterly team building events • Community that inspires – participate in our sports challenges – running, walking, cycling, table tennis • Regular events and parties • Gifts on birthdays, weddings or childbirth occasions • Office with great location and free parking, Friday lunches, healthy snacks and celebrations The gross salary in this position is ﻿3100-4600 EUR/month.
