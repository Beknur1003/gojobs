Software Architect / Lead Developer Location: Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia Work Mode: Hybrid Employment type: Permanent Our client is a global technology services and digital engineering organization with multi-billion-dollar revenue and operations across numerous countries worldwide. With a workforce of over 20,000 professionals, they deliver innovative solutions in cloud, data, AI, and enterprise modernization to leading enterprises across industries. We are seeking an experienced Software Architect / Lead Developer to provide technical leadership, drive architecture design, and guide system modernisation for complex enterprise applications. Responsibilities • Define the target architecture and modernisation roadmap for the core C# platform. • Lead system design reviews and provide technical oversight across multiple development teams. • Design scalable, maintainable, and testable platform architectures for enterprise-scale applications. • Own the architecture for decoders, including façade patterns, message parsing, and protocol handling. • Design assignment orchestration systems, including state machines and execution schedulers. • Guide development teams on coding standards, design patterns, and refactoring strategies. • Lead proof-of-concept initiatives and technology evaluations. • Troubleshoot complex system-level issues involving both hardware and software integration. • Mentor senior engineers and development teams, promoting knowledge sharing and best practices. • Drive technical debt reduction and platform optimisation initiatives. Requirements Must-have: • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or an equivalent field. • More than 10 years of software development experience. • Strong expertise in C#/.NET and C/C++ development. • Experience with large-scale enterprise applications and codebases exceeding 100 MB. • Deep understanding of Windows desktop application development using WPF and WinForms. • Experience designing high-performance systems and refactoring legacy architectures. • Proficiency in SQL Server and T-SQL. • Experience with TypeScript for map and UI components. • Strong technical communication skills, with the ability to document architectural decisions and present them to stakeholders. Nice-to-have: • Experience with telecommunications protocol decoding, including LTE, NR, GSM, and WCDMA. • Experience with Kotlin and Android development. • Experience with geospatial and mapping systems, including tile servers and offline maps. • Experience with Golang for analytics and cloud integration. • Experience with hardware–software integration platforms. Education: • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or an equivalent field. Why Join Us • Join a collaborative, forward-thinking team where your technical leadership will shape the future of enterprise software platforms. • Opportunity to work on complex, impactful projects, mentor talented engineers, and drive technology modernisation initiatives. • Be part of an environment that values innovation, professional growth, and cross-regional teamwork.
