Do you believe your designs and code could change the world? If YES, read on! Mavericks is a group of skilled IT professionals who love their craft. We solve complex business problems with simple working solutions by applying Agile software development practices. We provide consultancy advice, develop prototypes to validate ideas quickly, and develop systems that WOW people. We love to write code and build systems that delight and bring smiles to our users. We share and have fun on our continuous learning journey. Join us and multiply the fun and awesomeness of our workplace! *For your application to be considered, resume MUST be submitted in PDF format.* Your Role and Responsibilities • Empathising with stakeholders to develop appropriate, actionable and holistic working solutions that delivers business value • Championing best Agile and software engineering practices (e.g. Test-Driven Development (TDD), Continuous Integration/ Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), etc.) to develop high-quality products and solutions • Collaborating with amazing people (we only work with the best!) to solve challenging and complex problems • Be positive, proactive and help each other to learn and grow together • Provide feedback for improvement and run the company like you own it! The Qualifications To Be a Maverick 1. Minimum Qualifications • At least 5 years of experience in developing various applications • Worked in high performance and cross-functional Agile software engineering teams that used practices like Kanban/Scrum/Lean/XP, Test Driven Development (TDD), Continuous Integration/ Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), etc. • Experienced in using clean code practices, test automation and writing readable, maintainable code • Good understanding of end-to-end application architecture • Delivered at least one large-scale project to production • Experienced in backend and frontend development (e.g. Web, Mobile, Cloud, etc.) • Deep understanding of at least one programming language and at least one programming paradigm (OOP, FP) • Experience working with various techstacks (e.g. Typescript, ReactJS, NodeJS, AngularJS, Spring Boot, Ruby on Rails, Javascript, Java, GoLang, etc.) 2. Desirable Qualifications • Experience with microservices architecture • Experience with DevOps practices and Automation tools More about the job 1.
