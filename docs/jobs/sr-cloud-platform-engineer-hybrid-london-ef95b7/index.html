Ready to join a mission that matters? The future of cybersecurity starts with you. About the Role: We are seeking a Cloud Platform Engineer to join the AISOAR platform team at CrowdStrike. AISOAR is the CrowdStrike platform on which the modern SOC is established, encompassing numerous platform service areas across the platform engineering team. This position is critical to providing discoverability and search capabilities for CrowdStrike content to our customers, ensuring the seamless integration of cybersecurity solutions for our global customer base. The successful candidate will be responsible for both the technical implementation and the operational excellence of our marketplace platform, working with cutting-edge security technologies and cloud-native solutions. Over time, the scope of this role will expand to include contributions to various other AISOAR platform-related services. Location: London (Hybrid) Position Level: Senior Engineer I About the Team: You'll be part of a dynamic platform engineering organization that drives innovation across multiple platform features. Working directly with Cloud Platform Engineers to enhance our discoverability solutions for the AISOAR platform to provide the content platform for Agents and workflows. Our team culture emphasizes innovation, knowledge sharing, and technical excellence while maintaining a strong focus on security best practices.
