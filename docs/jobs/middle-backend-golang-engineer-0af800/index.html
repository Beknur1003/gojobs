We are looking for a Middle Backend Engineer (Golang) to develop reliable, scalable, and secure backend systems. The role involves building backend services, developing APIs, working with event-driven architectures, and contributing to system reliability, maintainability, and performance. While the primary focus is backend development, occasional contributions to React-based frontend functionality may be required. Responsibilities • Develop, maintain, and enhance backend services in Golang, including RESTful APIs and data contracts. • Implement and support asynchronous, event-driven communication, ensuring message reliability, error handling, and backward compatibility. • Contribute to the development of distributed systems and microservices. • Support application performance, reliability, and resource utilization improvements. • Participate in technical discussions and contribute to implementation activities. • Collaborate with team members through code reviews and knowledge sharing activities. • Write and maintain unit and integration tests for APIs, business logic, and integrations. • Develop secure APIs and service integrations, following authentication and authorization best practices. • Build integrations with enterprise platforms such as Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, ServiceNow, Genesys Cloud, and other third-party systems. • Support and maintain integrations between internal services and external enterprise platforms. • 3+ years of experience as a Software Engineer. • Experience building backend applications and services using Golang. • Good understanding of concurrency in Golang and asynchronous workflows. • Experience with event-driven architectures and message handling (e.g., Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ, or similar technologies). • Experience with AWS and cloud-native environments. • Experience developing RESTful APIs and service integrations. • Understanding of authentication, authorization, and secure API development principles. • Experience with relational and/or NoSQL databases. • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes. • Experience with automated testing, including unit and integration testing. • Nice to Have • Experience with React, TypeScript, Protobuf, Azure or GCP. • Experience with Keycloak, Auth0, Okta, or similar IAM solutions. • Experience with monitoring and observability tools (e.g., Grafana, Prometheus).
