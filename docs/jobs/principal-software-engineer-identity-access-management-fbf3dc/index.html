About the Role Cloudflare runs one of the world's largest networks, and every API call that configures it, from any customer, integration, or internal service, starts by proving who is calling and what they're allowed to do. The IAM team owns the authentication and authorization systems behind that: the identity layer of the Control Plane operating at global scale. Our surface spans things like login and SSO, OAuth and API tokens, account membership, roles and permissions, and SCIM provisioning. We’re hiring a hands-on Principal Engineer to shape the technical direction of Cloudflare’s IAM platform and lead complex initiatives across teams. You’ll work from architecture through production code and rollout, evolving services under live traffic to deliver new customer capabilities, greater scale, edge-native execution, and support for data residency requirements. You’ll help teams make sound tradeoffs between security, correctness, latency, and availability. Responsibilities • Architecture & strategy: Set the technical direction and delivery strategy for IAM, connecting customer needs with architectural decisions and practical, incremental investments. • Technical leadership & delivery: Lead complex initiatives across engineering teams, resolve ambiguity, build agreement on tradeoffs, and carry decisions through implementation and production rollout. • Hands-on engineering: Design, build, and debug critical authentication and authorization services, including safe migrations and backward-compatible API changes. • Security & reliability: Partner with Security to strengthen threat models and tenant isolation. Guide reliability improvements through observability, incident learning, and resilient system design. • Mentorship & engineering judgment: Develop senior and staff engineers through design collaboration, code reviews, and clear technical decisions. • Engineering effectiveness & automation: Identify and lead investments in automation and AI that improve how teams build, test, and operate IAM services. Prioritize measurable improvements in delivery speed, reliability, and engineering capacity while maintaining security and correctness. Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience • Extensive experience: A sustained record of building and operating large-scale production systems, setting technical direction across teams, and delivering complex initiatives with measurable customer or operational impact. • Identity expertise: Deep experience building production authentication or authorization systems, with sound judgment around identity protocols, permission models, tenant isolation, and credential and session lifecycles. • Distributed systems mastery: Proven track record in designing, architecting, and debugging large-scale distributed systems that handle global traffic. • Languages & storage: Strong backend engineering skills and willingness to work extensively in Go, with practical depth in relational data modeling, transactions, caching, and distributed consistency. Experience with Go and PostgreSQL is particularly relevant. • API stewardship: Significant experience designing and versioning public-facing APIs that are used by millions of developers. Bonus Points • Cloudflare ecosystem: Experience building on Cloudflare Workers or similar serverless/edge computing environments. • Observability advocate: A passion for building "observable" systems using Prometheus, Grafana, and Kibana to proactively identify failures. • Infrastructure as code: Experience with modern containerized deployment (Docker & Kubernetes), Terraform, and infrastructure automation. • Security mindset: A background in security engineering or formal verification of protocol implementations.
