At Bosch, we are driving a global digital transformation to empower thousands of employees with modern, secure, and seamless workplace technologies. We are looking for a Mac Developer who is passionate about building high-quality applications and tools that enhance the daily experience of macOS users across our global organization. In this role, you will design, develop, and maintain macOS applications used by researchers, engineers, and business teams worldwide. Working closely with colleagues in Poland and internationally, you will help transform user needs into reliable and scalable software solutions that improve productivity and deliver real business value. If you enjoy solving technical challenges, creating outstanding user experiences, and collaborating with teams across different countries and cultures, this is your opportunity to make a global impact. Your Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and enhance applications and developer tools for macOS users across Bosch. • Transform business and user requirements into reliable, secure, and maintainable software solutions. • Create modern and intuitive user interfaces using Apple technologies and frameworks. • Develop integrations with enterprise services and APIs. • Build automation solutions and scripts that improve efficiency and user experience. • Collaborate with globally distributed teams to deliver high-quality applications and features. • Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve complex technical issues. • Ensure application quality through testing, code reviews, and continuous improvement initiatives. • Contribute to CI/CD processes and software delivery pipelines. • Support integration with enterprise environments, authentication systems, and managed devices. • Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to the continuous improvement of development processes. We are looking for you if you have: ✅ Strong experience in macOS application development. ✅ Good knowledge of: Swift, SwiftUI, AppKit, Objective-C, macOS application lifecycle, Packaging and application distribution, User interface development ✅ Experience with scripting and automation, including: Go (Golang), Bash, AppleScript, Command-line tools and workflow automation ✅ Understanding of core macOS technologies and Apple ecosystem best practices. ✅ Experience developing and integrating with: REST APIs, JSON/XML data formats, Authentication and authorization flows, Enterprise services and integrations, Error handling and recovery mechanisms ✅ Knowledge of software quality and testing practices, including: Unit testing, Integration testing, Debugging, Logging, Crash analysis, Performance optimization and profiling ✅ Experience with: Git, Version control workflows, CI/CD pipelines, Collaborative software development ✅ Understanding of enterprise networking concepts, including: TCP/IP, DNS, Proxy configurations, SSH, Enterprise authentication, Certificate-based access ✅ Familiarity with device management solutions and managed enterprise environments. ✅ Experience working in Agile development environments. ✅ Very good communication skills in English. ❗To help us better understand your coding experience and development approach, we kindly ask you to include a link to your GitHub profile or any alternative materials such as a portfolio, GitLab or Bitbucket repository, personal website.
