Senior Engineer, Live Streaming Video Specialist

flosports Waterloo, Ontario опубликовано 7 июля 2026

FloSports is a world-class sports media company strategically positioned to be the essential destination for passionate sports fans, delighting them with live event coverage, breaking news, highlights, stats, rankings, and team and player profiles. We are growing Our Sports every day by continuing to invest in our ever-expanding ecosystem, which consists of over a dozen sport verticals and hundreds of streaming partners. FloSports is creating the home for college conferences/leagues and sports like grappling, hockey, track & field, racing, cheer, wrestling, and more, and we are looking for innovative and passionate people like you to help us! THE ROLE: You will play a key role in designing, building, and operating the live streaming platform at FloSports, delivering tens of thousands of live events every year to fans around the world. You'll own the backend services and APIs behind that pipeline end-to-end, and you'll be accountable for keeping the full live signal chain reliable under real-world broadcast conditions, including redundancy and graceful failover. Prior live-video experience is a plus rather than a prerequisite. If you're a strong backend engineer, we'll bring you up to speed on the broadcast pipeline. As a senior member of the team, you will also participate in an on-call rotation to help ensure the reliability of our live streaming platform. RESPONSIBILITIES: • Build and maintain backend microservices using Node.js/NestJS and/or Go, and design data models and queries for SQL databases. • Write clean, maintainable, well-tested code and follow team best practices for code quality. • Participate in code reviews and contribute to a culture of continuous learning and improvement. • Collaborate closely with team members, product managers and other stakeholders to define and deliver new features. • Help operate, automate, and improve our live video pipeline, the systems that take a broadcast signal in and turn it into a stream people watch (ingest, transcoding, packaging, and delivery). • Work at scale, across tens of thousands of events a year, with a focus on performance, scalability, and reliability. • Build tooling and alerting to monitor stream health and viewer quality of experience (QoE), catching issues before they reach audiences. • Diagnose and resolve live broadcast and streaming pipeline issues in real time, including during live events, and drive lasting improvements to redundancy and failover. KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES: • Proficiency in TypeScript, Node.js and/or Go, including a solid grasp of language features and design patterns. • Experience building and maintaining RESTful APIs, and familiarity with NestJS or a similar backend framework. • Comfort using AI tools (e.g. Copilot, Claude Code, ChatGPT) to multiply your impact as an engineer, accelerating development, debugging, and learning unfamiliar domains rather than relying on them as a crutch. • Experience working with relational databases. • Familiarity with event-driven architectures. • Familiarity with cloud infrastructure, preferably AWS (EC2, Lambda, S3, etc.). • Strong debugging, problem-solving, and analytical skills. BONUS POINTS: • Aptitude for, or hands-on exposure to, live broadcast video at scale, including multi-event, multi-channel contribution, transcoding, packaging, and delivery. • Working knowledge of the live broadcast signal chain, including redundancy, failover, and graceful degradation under real-world conditions. • Familiarity with live streaming transport protocols (SRT, RTMP, Zixi, WebRTC) and how they integrate with media pipelines. • Experience with tools like AWS Elemental and/or video encoding tools such as OBS, and vMix. • Experience with NoSQL solutions like Firebase/Firestore. • Experience with messaging solutions such as SNS/SQS, MQTT, Kafka, and Pubsub. • Hands-on experience writing and executing unit and integration tests using Jest and Vitest. • Hands-on experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes). • Knowledge of security best practices for API development and data handling. • Experience working in Agile development environments. • Previous experience with end-to-end (E2E) testing frameworks. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and integrating tests into deployment workflows. OUR COMMITMENT TO DIVERSITY: FloSports exists to elevate the communities, athletes, and sports that have too often been overlooked. That mission starts with our own team. We believe that a diverse, inclusive workplace—one where different perspectives are sought out, heard, and valued—is essential to building a company that can truly serve the full spectrum of sports fans. We are committed to creating a fair and equitable environment where people from all backgrounds can thrive. To help mitigate bias and expand opportunity, FloSports uses a blind recruiting process that supports more objective, inclusive hiring decisions. We’re intentional in our practices because we believe the best ideas and innovations come from teams that reflect the diversity of the world around us. OUR BENEFITS: • Recognized three years in a row as a Top Workplace by the Austin-American Statesman • Annual equity awards for all top performers • Competitive and comprehensive medical, dental and vision plans • Peace of mind through company-paid short-term disability, long-term disability and life insurance • Generous 401(K) company match vested immediately • Progressive parental leave policies • Flexible paid time off • Hack-a-thons and a full calendar of team-building and social events • Company donation to youth teams and leagues that our employees coach • Stocked snack bar, catered lunch and breakfast tacos every week
