About the Position: Staff Developer The Staff Developer will architect and build scalable, production-grade AI agent platforms and backend services within Arctic Wolf Labs. They will drive the technical roadmap for agentic AI capabilities, enabling intelligent automation across Security Research, Threat Intelligence, and Detection Engineering. We are looking for a technical leader with strong backend engineering expertise, hands-on experience building AI agents, and a solid understanding of cybersecurity to deliver innovative AI-powered security products and services. Arctic Wolf Labs is the research-focused division at Arctic Wolf focused on advancing innovation in the field of security operations. The mission of Arctic Wolf Labs is to develop cutting-edge technology and tools that are designed to enhance the company’s core mission to end cyber risk, while also bringing comprehensive security intelligence to Arctic Wolf’s customer base and the security community-at-large. Leveraging the more than ten trillion security events the Arctic Wolf Security Operations Cloud ingests, parses, enriches, and analyses each week, Arctic Wolf Labs is responsible for performing threat research on new and emerging adversaries, developing advanced threat detection models, and driving improvement in the speed, scale, and detection abilities of Arctic Wolf’s solution offerings. The Arctic Wolf Labs team comprises security and threat intelligence researchers, data scientists, security development engineers with deep domain knowledge in artificial intelligence (AI), security R&D, as well as advanced threat offensive and defensive methods and technologies. Security Platform partners with these groups to understand requirements, design & implement scalable, fault-tolerant solutions, and build the next generation of security capabilities for Arctic Wolf. Our mission is simple: End Cyber Risk. agent platforms and distributed microservices using Python, Golang, and cloud-native technologies. • Architect production-grade AI agent systems using LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, and other agentic stores. • Build and operate cloud-native services on AWS and Kubernetes (EKS), ensuring high availability, scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency. • Develop event-driven and real-time processing systems using Kafka, AWS Kinesis, SQS/SNS, and asynchronous processing architectures. • Build robust CI/CD and infrastructure automation using Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Terraform, and AWS CloudFormation, following DevOps and MLOps best practices. • Collaborate closely with Security Researchers, Threat Intelligence, and Detection Engineering teams to translate cybersecurity requirements into intelligent AI-powered products and services. • Drive cybersecurity-focused AI solutions, including threat intelligence analysis, automated threat investigation, detection engineering, security data enrichment, and security workflow automation. • Provide technical leadership and mentorship, drive architecture decisions, establish engineering best practices, and guide the team in delivering production-grade AI agent solutions. We’re Looking For Someone Who has: • 8+ years of software engineering experience, with strong expertise in backend engineering, distributed systems, and microservices, including hands-on experience designing, building, and deploying production-grade AI agents and agentic workflows. • Prior experience in cybersecurity, threat intelligence, security analytics, detection engineering, or security automation is highly desirable. • Experience leading technical initiatives, driving architecture decisions, mentoring engineers, and collaborating with cross-functional teams. • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, with a proactive approach to identifying technical challenges, evaluating trade-offs, and driving solutions. • Ability to take ownership, work independently, adapt to evolving technologies, and translate complex business and security requirements into scalable engineering solutions.
