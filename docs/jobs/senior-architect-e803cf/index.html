This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Architect based in Germany. This is a senior-level architecture opportunity for an experienced software engineering leader supporting multiple technology-driven businesses. You will operate as a fractional CTO, helping founders translate business priorities into realistic technical strategies and high-quality products. The role combines architecture, hands-on technical guidance, developer coaching, code reviews, and product-oriented problem solving. You will work across several projects, partnering closely with founders, product owners, engineers, and multidisciplinary delivery teams. Beyond client-facing work, you will contribute to improving engineering practices, recruitment, training, and internal technology capabilities. The environment is highly autonomous and collaborative, with flexibility to work full-time or begin on a part-time basis. This role is ideal for a technically strong architect who enjoys variety, strategic influence, and helping teams build scalable solutions in a startup-focused environment. Accountabilities • Act as a fractional CTO for approximately 2–3 technology businesses, providing technical leadership, architecture guidance, and strategic direction. • Work directly with founders and product owners to understand business priorities, challenge assumptions when needed, and translate requirements into realistic and actionable technical solutions. • Review and evolve software architecture and system designs to ensure scalability, maintainability, security, and alignment with product goals. • Protect engineering teams from unnecessary complexity while helping prioritize technical work according to business needs. • Conduct frequent code reviews, provide technical mentorship, and coach developers toward stronger engineering practices. • Perform technical code audits for potential investments, clients, or new projects, identifying risks, opportunities, and areas for improvement. • Provide hands-on support for complex technical challenges, including software development, DevOps, infrastructure, and troubleshooting when required. • Contribute to the continuous improvement of engineering teams through recruitment support, training initiatives, knowledge sharing, and technical standards. • Participate in candidate assessments, technical interviews, and occasional research into emerging frameworks and technologies to strengthen training and development programs. • Support occasional in-person founder or investment meetings and provide specialized technical consulting across other projects when relevant. Requirements • At least 15 years of professional experience in software development, with extensive experience progressing into senior technical leadership or architecture roles. • Expert-level proficiency in Microsoft C#/.NET or Node.js. • Mastery of React, with experience in Angular or Vue considered an advantage. • Extensive experience with relational databases such as MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server, MariaDB, or PostgreSQL. • Strong experience with both Microsoft Azure and AWS, with solid DevOps knowledge considered a plus. • Experience with Ionic and React Native is advantageous. • Familiarity with additional technologies such as Python, Java, Kotlin, iOS/Swift, or Golang is a plus, particularly for supporting diverse technical environments. • Strong understanding of software architecture, system design, development practices, cloud technologies, and modern engineering workflows. • Proven ability to communicate effectively with founders, business stakeholders, product teams, and engineers, translating between business objectives and technical execution. • Strong coaching, mentoring, analytical, and problem-solving abilities, with the confidence to challenge decisions constructively and guide teams toward pragmatic solutions. • Comfortable working autonomously across multiple projects and adapting quickly to different products, technologies, and organizational contexts. • Willingness to participate in occasional in-person client or founder meetings when needed. Benefits • Fully remote working environment within Europe. • Flexible working schedule, with freedom to choose working hours within reasonable business requirements. • Possibility to work full-time or start on a part-time/hourly basis, depending on availability and mutual fit. • Opportunity to operate in a fractional CTO capacity across multiple technology businesses. • Diverse technical challenges and exposure to startups, founders, products, and emerging technologies. • Opportunity to contribute to engineering strategy, recruitment, training, and internal technology initiatives. • Annual company trip to visit colleagues in the Philippines. • Flat management structure with an open-door culture and minimal micromanagement. • Strong emphasis on employee well-being, autonomy, trust, and meaningful contribution. • Opportunities to work on projects selected for their potential impact and technical interest. • Occasional travel for client, founder, or investment-related meetings.
