Role Description We are seeking a talented Senior Software Engineer to design, develop, and operate scalable backend services and automated workflows for our Data Platform team. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in Java or Golang, RESTful services, CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms. Experience with Apache Kafka is a strong plus. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate internal systems, improve deployment efficiency, and ensure system reliability, availability, and performance. This is a full-time role based in China, preferably Shanghai. English and Mandarin communication skills and 1–2 hours of working-time overlap with US Pacific Time are required. Requirements Key Responsibilities • Design and develop critical workflows that coordinate and integrate with various internal systems to improve operational efficiency and quality of service. • Design and develop automation for CI/CD pipelines built on Gradle, Jenkins, Spinnaker, and GitHub. • Deploy, manage, and monitor applications in Kubernetes clusters. • Collaborate with development teams to improve deployment efficiency and system reliability. • Troubleshoot and resolve system issues, ensuring high availability and performance. Qualifications • Strong proficiency in Java or Golang, including backend development with frameworks like Netty and ServiceTalk. • Experience with Apache Kafka, including broker internals, producers/consumers, and ecosystem tooling, is a strong plus. • Experience building RESTful services, working with concurrent systems, and designing resilient, high-performance backend components. • Experience designing and implementing CI/CD pipelines using Gradle, Jenkins, Spinnaker, and GitHub. • Solid working knowledge of cloud services such as AliCloud, Tencent Cloud, AWS, and GCP, including deploying and managing applications in the cloud. • Proficiency with Kubernetes for container orchestration and application monitoring. • Experience building systems for high availability, scalability, and performance.
