Director of Engineering

About the company Nuitée is the API infrastructure powering travel, inside banks, AI assistants, airlines, loyalty programs and hotel brands. Nuitée Connect gives businesses one API for 2.9M properties, 600+ airlines and 200K+ experiences, built for apps and AI agents alike. Nuitée Cloud lets hotels reach new demand and control pricing, payments and operations in one platform. Founded in 2017, Nuitée has 300 colleagues across 25 countries and a mission to make travel distribution simpler, faster and fairer for everyone in travel. We raised a $48M Series A led by Accel and work with companies such as Revolut, Grab, and Sabre. About the role Remote in Europe, or hybrid in Barcelona, Dublin, London, or Palma. A live product making its mark in travel tech, a team ready to grow, and the engineering direction yours to set. You’ll lead engineering for Nuitée Cloud: the platform hotels use to reach new demand and control pricing, payments, and operations in one place. It’s well past MVP, and 400+ hotel chains trust Nuitée to distribute their inventory. There’s still a lot to build from scratch. Cloud has two engineering teams, and you’ll lead both: Connectivity, which builds the backend in Go, and Product, a full-stack team with real product ownership. You’ll start with how the team works: how Cloud codes, designs, and tests. From there, you’ll set the technical direction with the senior ICs and grow the team. The challenge underneath is speed. Cloud has a lot of R&D, and a single product change can move metrics fast. How do you keep that pace while making the system more reliable and secure? What makes it interesting for you • You set the standard. You’ll define how a whole product area writes code, designs systems, and tests, and shape those practices around how Nuitée works. • The architecture is still open. Cloud has to stay fast and also become more reliable and secure, and you’ll help set the technical direction to get there. • You build the team around you. You’ll own Cloud’s hiring strategy, career paths, and engineering culture. • You work closely with product. You’ll partner day to day with the Head of Product, Cloud, on what the team builds and why. Nuitée raised a $48M Series A led by Accel and works with companies such as Revolut, Grab, and Sabre. The kind of problems you’ll work on • How do we build one engineering culture across a distributed team that ships much faster at higher quality? • How should Cloud’s architecture change so a product built for speed and R&D also becomes more reliable and secure? • How do we give every engineer clear work, a clear reason to be on the team, and a real path to grow at Nuitée? This role fits if you • Have led at least 10 engineers as a Director, Head of Engineering, or Senior Engineering Manager at a well-known product scale-up with a high engineering bar. • Have built large-scale, challenging web products, including the front end, and can work out how to architect and scale a product for high volume. • Have put engineering practices in place (code, architecture, QA) and built a shared culture that raised the team’s level. • Lead as a manager who sets direction and hands out work, and still get into the code when something needs to ship. • Have built career paths, hired, and kept engineers motivated. • Communicate clearly and drive things forward without waiting to be asked. You’re strong in at least one object-oriented language. Our backend runs on Go, so experience with it would be ideal. Nuitée moves quickly, has little process, and gives people a lot of room to make decisions. You’ll do well here if ambiguity does not slow you down, you like going deep into difficult problems, and you want to be accountable for what your team actually delivers. If this sounds like your kind of engineering leadership role, we’d love to hear from you. You can apply with your LinkedIn profile or CV. You’ll meet our talent team, the Head of Product, Cloud, members of the engineering team, and our leadership. Why Nuitée? Nuitée is building the infrastructure to power travel. • Real scale: Nuitée Connect gives businesses one API for 2.9M properties, 600+ airlines and 200K+ experiences, and processes 1bn searches a day. • Strong backing: We raised a $48M Series A led by Accel. • Startup agility: We combine operational discipline with the speed and innovation of a scaling startup. • Global impact: Our infrastructure supports customers such as Revolut, Grab, and Sabre, alongside banks, airlines, loyalty programs and hotel brands. • Built for AI: Nuitée Connect is built for apps and AI agents alike, so teams can launch intelligent travel experiences quickly. By joining Nuitée, you’ll be part of a team building a category-defining platform. You’ll work at global scale, innovate alongside industry leaders, and help drive the next phase of travel technology. If you’re excited to push boundaries and grow with us, Nuitée is the place for you.
