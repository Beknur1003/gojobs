The weaponization of AI is driving a paradigm shift in the cyber security industry from proactive to preemptive security: clients must now invest more intelligence and effort in understanding their vulnerabilities before their adversaries do (Open AI). The only way to achieve this is to simulate future attack scenarios at scale, and to run them in high fidelity digital replicas of production environments (Gartner/Forrester). SimSpace serves as the AI Proving Grounds to generate hypersynthetic data which pressure tests controls, tools, teams, and agentic capabilities in full kill chain attacks hidden in realistic environmental traffic and patterns of life. Founded in 2015 by experts from U.S. Cyber Command and MIT Lincoln Laboratory, the platform was initially conceived to assist in Offensive Cyber Operations (OCO), to quickly replicate target environments to conduct mission rehearsal of human operators and their tools to deny (disrupt, degrade, destroy) or deceive (manipulate or exfiltrate data) adversaries. The company has since expanded to serve over a dozen allied governments in OCO/DCO as well as critical infrastructure enterprises around the world. The company is actively investing in the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe and the Middle East through direct sales and channel partners. SimSpace has launched the AI Proving Grounds Consortium, leading the revolution by creating an open platform for the InfoSec ecosystem to train, validate and operationalize their agentic capabilities before committing to production. We value candid, constructive feedback and create opportunities for personal, professional, and financial growth. Job Summary You will build and lead a fullstack team, with an emphasis on backend engineering, for AI Proving Grounds (AIPG). The team builds user and attack emulation workflows that customers can configure, observe and repeat across applications and execution environments. You will lead as a hands-on player-coach, regularly implementing and debugging software, tests and automation with the team. You also own hiring, development, capacity and workflow delivery. The Staff Engineer leads design and integration across features; you coach engineers to take ownership. MCO delivers the integrated workflow. AIPG DEN provides user-emulation capabilities, Scenario Development provides adversary content, and range and platform teams retain their shared services. For example, MCO might connect a simulated employee's application actions to verified results and recovery. Agree responsibilities and partner capacity before committing to work that spans teams. What will you be doing at SimSpace? • Agree with the Director which roles to hire first, how to onboard them and where interim technical support will come from. Build the new team around fullstack engineers with strong backend skills. • Define a useful workflow with Product and partner teams. State what MCO will deliver, what it excludes, which interfaces it uses, how success will be verified and who receives the result. • Lead work from configuration through execution, verified effects, diagnosis and recovery. Agree changes to shared components with the teams that own them. • Review architecture and code with the Staff Engineer. Write focused changes and investigate difficult failures while helping engineers take ownership of features. • Establish integration tests that verify identities, intended effects, compatibility and cleanup. Improve repeatability with tested automation and reviewed AI-assisted development. • Coach engineers through feedback and career development. Include support, maintenance and dependencies in the plan, and explain which work moves when priorities or capacity change. What Success Looks Like The Director and Product will agree initial priorities and timing with you, based on staffing and partner commitments. • A workflow plan states MCO's deliverable, partner commitments, technical boundaries, hiring priorities and interim coverage. • A user or attack emulation workflow runs from configuration to verified effects, with diagnosis and recovery when execution fails. • The team can repeat delivery using automated tests, useful diagnostics and clear release and support responsibilities. What are the qualifications to apply? • Experience managing and developing software engineers through hiring, onboarding, coaching, feedback and delivery accountability. • Evidence of building or expanding an engineering team or capability, with technical ownership shared across the team. • Production fullstack experience with strong backend engineering, recent personal implementation of software, tests or automation, and diagnosis across service boundaries. • Experience delivering workflows that connect applications, APIs and execution systems, working with frontend and environment specialists. • Practical understanding of asynchronous state, permissions, compatibility, automated testing, observability and recovery from partial failures. • Experience turning unclear requests into agreed scope, coordinating work across independently owned systems and preparing the result for operational handover. Who you are • You delegate meaningful decisions, give direct feedback and help engineers work independently. • You resolve ownership and priority conflicts openly and judge progress by usable workflows and reliable operation. Nice to have but not required • Experience with cybersecurity, simulation, user automation or agent workflows. • Experience with Windows or Linux, browser or desktop integration, or virtualized execution. • Experience with Go, Python or modern frontend development. You can develop adjacent runtime and infrastructure skills with specialists. Our Tech Stack Includes The work spans Go and Python services, application APIs, browser and execution integrations, containers and automated tests. Fullstack experience and strong backend engineering are central to the role.
