SDE 3 at GreyLabs means owning technical bets end-to-end: writing the design doc, making the architecture call, and shipping it. You'll work across Voice AI Agents (our flagship autonomous voice platform) and Voice Analytics (conversation intelligence for CXOs). Responsibilities: • Own complex engineering problems end-to-end from scoping and design through implementation, testing, and production operations, including personally driving resolution of production incidents. • Write technical design documents, lead design reviews, and make architecture calls. • Identify and resolve cross-cutting technical challenges: latency, reliability, security, and scalability across the platform. • Work with product teams from problem definition to translate requirements into clean technical solutions and push back where the trade-offs warrant it. • Mentor SDE1 and SDE2 engineers through code reviews, design feedback, and structured pairing. • Evolve engineering standards API design, observability, testing, and incident response. Requirements: • 5-8 years of engineering experience; track record of owning and shipping complex systems. • Deep backend expertise: designed and operated distributed systems in production; familiar with failure modes at scale. • Strong Python (Django) and Golang for production systems' idiomatic, performant code. • Solid experience with React.js and modern frontend development. • Working knowledge of AWS (EC2 S3 Lambda, RDS, or equivalents). • Strong fundamentals in relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred), data modelling, and query optimisation. • Led technical design: wrote design docs, ran design reviews, and owned architecture decisions cross-functionally. • An appetite for learning and figuring things out at a growing startup in an emerging category, where the problems are often new and the playbook is still being written. Strong Signals: • Voice AI stack in production: Deepgram, Whisper, AWS Transcribe, ElevenLabs, Google TTS, or equivalent ASR/TTS tools. • LLM orchestration (LangChain, LlamaIndex, or custom) in a production voice or conversational AI system. • Streaming or event-driven architectures. • BFSI-adjacent domain experience compliance monitoring, audit trails, and data sensitivity requirements in practice. • Containerisation and CI/CD Docker, Kubernetes, and pipeline tooling (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or equivalent).
