Platform Engineer About the Role License Corp is hiring Platform Engineers to build the shared components our engineering teams depend on. This is a platform role in the true sense: you will be building the tooling, pipelines and services that other engineers build on top of. The platform charter covers common microservice infrastructure, including our observability pipeline, service discovery, internationalization and localization tooling, and the shared libraries and standards that keep our services consistent as the organization grows. The architecture for the first phase of work is already defined, so your immediate focus will be building it out and running it in production. As the platform matures, you will also have bandwidth to contribute to Java microservice development alongside the product teams. What You'll Do Design, build and maintain common platform components and shared libraries used across our microservice estate. Build and operate our observability pipeline — instrumentation standards, collection, and the metrics, logging and tracing paths that sit behind it. Develop internationalization and localization tooling so that product teams can ship into new markets without reinventing the plumbing each time. Build and tune data pipeline and event pipelines, with attention to throughput, ordering guarantees, failure handling and replay. Support the engineers who consume the platform — onboarding, documentation, troubleshooting and making the right path the easy path. Contribute to Java microservice development as capacity allows once the core platform work is established. What We're Looking For 8+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a substantial portion spent building platform, infrastructure or developer-facing components. 6+ years of hands-on Java, and fluency with the common Java ecosystem — Spring Boot, Spring Framework, Maven or Gradle, and the testing tools that go with them. Practical experience with OpenTelemetry and observability more broadly: instrumenting services, propagating context across boundaries, and turning telemetry into something people actually use. Experience building data pipelines in production, including topic and partition design, consumer group behavior, serialization, and handling the failure cases, experience in common data pipeline like Kafka, spark, flint etc. A platform mindset. You think about the engineers who will use what you build, and you measure success by their productivity rather than by lines shipped. Nice to Have Familiarity with containers and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines. Experience with observability backends such as Prometheus, Grafana, Jaeger, Datadog or similar. Cloud platform experience (AWS, GCP or Azure) and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform). Experience with other programming languages like Python, GoLang or Scala.
