About Us Visa is a world leader in payments technology, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories, dedicated to uplifting everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid. At Visa, you'll have the opportunity to create impact at scale — tackling meaningful challenges, growing your skills and seeing your contributions impact lives around the world. Join Visa and do work that matters – to you, to your community, and to the world. Progress starts with you. Job Description Visa's Corporate IT (CIT) organization is on a transformational journey to redefine how enterprise technology enables global business scale, resilience, and employee productivity. We are moving beyond traditional infrastructure models toward software-defined, automated, cloud-integrated platforms that deliver security, reliability, and exceptional user experience at global scale. As part of this journey, the candidate will join a platform engineering team as a backend and systems-focused engineer, designing, developing, and implementing cross-platform client software and large-scale back-end microservices. The ideal candidate will bring extensive experience building solutions for functionally complex, large-volume, high-throughput, data-centric distributed systems, with strong hands-on depth in Go and Java. The successful candidate is expected to be a design, coding, and configuration expert, and will help advance the engineering craft of the team related to architecture, distributed-systems design, code quality, scalability, resiliency, and CI/CD. The candidate should enjoy working on diverse technologies and functionally complex, data-centric distributed applications, should be excited to take initiatives to solve complex systems problems, and get the job done while taking on new challenges. You should thrive in team-oriented, fast-paced environments where each team member is vital to the overall success of the projects. Key responsibilities: Primary: • Design, build, and maintain a cross-platform agent/plugin in Go (Golang). • Design and build back-end microservices and APIs in Java / Spring Boot with a high level of scalability, resiliency, and monitoring. • Build and operate an Apache Kafka–based streaming pipeline (producers/consumers), including partitioning, consumer groups, and delivery/lag management, across an Active/Active multi–data-center topology. • Containerize and deploy services using Docker and Kubernetes, and help define deployment, scaling, and operational patterns. • Work with data stores — ClickHouse (or an equivalent columnar/analytical store), PostgreSQL, and Redis — including schema/data-model design and building efficient queries. • Design and build GenAI-powered features and integrations — e.g., natural-language interfaces, LLM-assisted workflows, and agentic automation — integrating with LLM APIs and tooling. • Contribute to the React front end as needed (secondary to back-end/agent work) and collaborate with UI-focused engineers on API contracts. • Manage complex, multi-service dependencies across environments and data centers. • Work with business partners, TPMs, and Product Managers to drive user-story grooming, functional/technical specifications, workflow designs, and use cases. • Contribute to and help shape engineering best practices for the team. • Stay abreast of emerging technologies, conduct POCs to prove concepts, and select the best solution approach. • Work with limited direction within a complex distributed environment to drive delivery of solutions and meet service levels. • Must be self-motivated with the ability to work in a fast-paced agile delivery model, collaborating with effective communication, quality, integrity, and reliable delivery. This is a hybrid position. Expectation of days in the office will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.
