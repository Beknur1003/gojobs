This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Software Engineer based in India. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will contribute to the development and evolution of large-scale distributed systems powering advanced web security and network infrastructure solutions. You will design and implement secure, reliable, and maintainable software that helps improve internet performance, connectivity, and protection for users worldwide. Working within a highly specialized engineering team, you will tackle complex network challenges and develop innovative features for fixed and mobile network service providers. You will collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to shape technical decisions and drive product development from conception through deployment. Beyond your technical contributions, you will serve as a subject matter expert, mentoring colleagues and helping raise engineering standards across the team. Accountabilities You will play a key role in designing, developing, and maintaining scalable software solutions while contributing to the reliability and security of large distributed network systems. Your responsibilities will combine hands-on software engineering, technical leadership, problem-solving, and collaboration. • Collaborate with software engineering teams to influence technical architecture, shape design decisions, and contribute to product development strategies. • Design, develop, and enhance software features and functionalities, covering the entire development lifecycle from initial conception and implementation to testing and deployment. • Write secure, reliable, efficient, and maintainable code for large-scale distributed systems. • Act as a technical subject matter expert, providing guidance and mentoring to other engineers to strengthen team capabilities and productivity. • Identify, investigate, and troubleshoot complex network and software issues affecting customer environments. • Work closely with internal teams and external partners to develop solutions addressing challenging network infrastructure requirements. • Contribute to improving internet traffic management, network performance, reliability, and security. • Stay up to date with emerging technologies and engineering practices to continuously improve software quality and technical capabilities. Requirements: The ideal candidate has solid software development experience, a strong understanding of internet technologies and network protocols, and the ability to contribute effectively to complex distributed systems. You should combine strong technical problem-solving skills with a collaborative mindset and an interest in mentoring and continuous learning. • At least 3 years of professional experience in software development and the software development lifecycle. • Strong programming experience in Go (Golang), C, or C++ within a Unix/Linux environment. • Good understanding of internet infrastructure, networking concepts, and protocols such as IP, DNS, routing, HTTP, and TCP. • Familiarity with web architecture and large-scale distributed systems. • Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or Microsoft Azure. • Knowledge of cloud-native technologies, infrastructure constructs, and deployment practices. • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to investigate and resolve complex technical challenges. • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively across engineering teams. • Demonstrated interest in learning new technologies and adapting to evolving technical environments. • Ability and willingness to share technical expertise, mentor colleagues, and support the professional development of other engineers. Benefits: • Fully remote working arrangement within India. • Opportunity to work on large-scale distributed systems and advanced web security technologies. • Exposure to complex network infrastructure and innovative internet security solutions. • Collaborative environment involving experienced engineering teams and external technology partners. • Opportunities to develop technical expertise and explore new career paths. • Access to professional development initiatives, including mentoring programs, internal learning events, and LinkedIn Learning. • Opportunities to expand technical knowledge, develop new skills, and take on different professional challenges. • Meaningful opportunity to contribute to improving the speed, reliability, and security of internet services worldwide.
