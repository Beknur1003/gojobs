Our integrated solution unifies data across risk teams to help organizations stop fraud in real time, prevent AI-driven attacks, and automate fraud and AML operations. Sardine’s platform is strengthened by one of the fastest-growing fraud consortiums in the market, spanning more than 6 billion profiled devices, 800 million consumers, and 3 million businesses worldwide. Leading companies including FIS, GoDaddy, Intuit, Edward Jones, ZoomInfo, and Checkout.com rely on Sardine to secure and grow trust in their products. Location: • Remote - US / Canada About the role: Fraud has moved to mobile, and so have the tools fraudsters use: emulators, rooted and jailbroken devices, hooking frameworks, app repackaging, and device farms. Sardine's Device Intelligence & Behavior Biometrics team builds the signals that tell genuine users apart from fraudsters, bots, and automated or manipulated devices, and our iOS and Android SDKs run inside the apps of fintechs and enterprises worldwide. We are looking for a Mobile SDK Engineer to strengthen our mobile fraud prevention capabilities. You will develop new device, integrity, and behavioral signals for our native iOS and Android SDKs, keep device identity stable across reinstalls and OS changes, and make the SDKs fast, lightweight, and easy for customers to integrate. What you'll be doing: • Design, develop, and maintain our native iOS and Android SDKs, keeping them lightweight, stable, and easy to integrate. • Research and implement new fraud signals: emulator and virtual device detection, root and jailbreak detection, hooking and instrumentation detection (for example Frida), app tampering and repackaging, remote access and screen sharing, and device farm patterns. • Improve device identity longevity across app reinstalls, OS upgrades, and platform privacy changes, using platform capabilities such as Keychain, App Attest / DeviceCheck, and Play Integrity where appropriate. • Collect sensor, touch, and interaction data that powers our behavior biometrics, with careful attention to battery, performance, and privacy. • Harden the SDKs against reverse engineering, tampering, and spoofed payloads. • Work with the Web SDK and backend (Go) teams on cross-platform features, such as linking native app sessions to in-app WebView sessions. • Test thoroughly across devices, OS versions, and integration setups to ensure compatibility, reliability, and stability. • Track iOS and Android platform changes ahead of release and adapt the SDKs before they affect customers. • Support customer developers during integration and troubleshoot issues in their apps. • Participate in code reviews, design discussions, and knowledge sharing with a distributed team. What We're Looking For • 5+ years of experience building native Android and / or iOS apps or SDKs. • Strong Android (Kotlin / Java) and / or iOS (Swift / Objective-C) fundamentals, and willingness to dive into lower-level concepts such as memory management, threading, and the platform runtime. • Solid grasp of mobile security concepts: sandboxing, code signing, entitlements and permissions, root and jailbreak behavior, and emulator characteristics. • Experience with reverse engineering, dynamic instrumentation, or similar investigative work on mobile apps. • Experience shipping a library or SDK that other developers integrate, with attention to API design, binary size, and backward compatibility. • Experience with mobile testing frameworks such as XCTest / Xcode UI Testing and Espresso. • Strong understanding of object-oriented programming principles and design patterns. • Clear written and verbal communication. • Understanding of how AI is used by fraudsters (automated tampering, hooking frameworks, emulators, synthetic devices) and how that shapes SDK defense and signal design. • Experience using AI to speed up investigation work: reading crash logs, analyzing device signal data, or comparing behavior across OS versions. Nice to Have • Background in mobile fraud prevention, security, or device intelligence. • Hands-on experience with Frida, Objection, LLDB, jadx, Ghidra, or similar tools. • Native code experience (C / C++ via NDK, or low-level iOS runtime). • Experience with cross-platform wrappers that customers use to integrate SDKs, such as React Native, Flutter, Expo, or Xamarin. • Experience writing backend services in Go, Java, C++, or Rust. • Experience with GCP. • Understanding of behavioral biometrics. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and release automation, using tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins, to automate the building, testing, versioning, and deployment of SDKs or applications Tech Stack • iOS SDK: Swift / Objective-C • Android SDK: Kotlin / Java • Backend: Go • Infrastructure: GCP / AWS Benefits we offer: • Generous compensation in cash and equity • Early exercise for all options, including pre-vested • Work from anywhere: Remote-first Culture • Flexible paid time off and Year-end break • Health insurance, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents - US and Canada specific • 4% matching in 401k / RRSP - US and Canada specific • MacBook Pro delivered to your door • One-time stipend to set up a home office — desk, chair, screen, etc. • Monthly meal stipend • Monthly social meet-up stipend • Annual health and wellness stipend • Annual Learning stipend Join a fast-growing company with world-class professionals from around the world.
