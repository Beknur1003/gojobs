Role Overview As a Senior Staff / Staff Engineer on the Security Infrastructure team, you will build the platform that enables Coupang to win and grow our customers’ confidence while rapidly expanding and scaling our services. Specifically, you will work on our dynamic authorization engine, which manages the security risks of all our customer interactions by looking for signs of unusual or potentially malicious activity. This is a critical system which protects both our customers and our company against damage from malicious activity. Our work supports teams across Coupang and thousands of microservices. We are looking for engineers who are passionate about building platforms at scale, with emphasis on usability, reliability and efficiency. The successful candidate will be a well-rounded engineer who balances strategic and design thinking with attention to detail in implementation and operations. If that sounds like you, come join us!   What You Will Do In this role, you will: • Develop our customer authentication and authorization platforms, and rapidly iterate while collaborating actively as part of a small, highly talented team. • Partner with teams across the company to understand their current needs and future direction. • Think systematically to identify technical solutions, balancing strategic goals and immediate impact. • Demonstrate excellence in architecture and design, resulting in robust and scalable systems. • Hands-on develop critical infrastructure components.  • Write back-end APIs in Java and Go, using industry-standard frameworks. • Work across teams to drive platform adoption, helping teams prioritize ruthlessly and “move with urgency”. • Dive deep into critical system issues, constantly raising the bar on Operational Excellence. • Serve as a role model for other engineers and shape our team culture. Minimum Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in computer science or related technical field.  • 8+ years professional software development experience, or 6 years’ experience with advanced degree.   • 2+ years’ experience leading technical projects.  • Fluent in one or more of C++, Java and Go. • Recent hands-on experience designing and developing large scale distributed systems.  • Proven track record of delivering mission critical systems. • Experience with cloud computing using AWS, Azure or GCP. • Deep understanding of Cloud applications design and architecture, good API design patterns, performance and scale. Preferred Qualifications • Masters or PhD degree in computer science or other related technical field. • Working knowledge of security protocols such as OIDC and OAuth2. • Working knowledge of IAM systems such as identity providers and role based authorization. • Experience working with Kubernetes, gRPC and Istio. • Experience building high-reliability and low-latency systems in the Cloud. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure as Code. • Experience handling multiple competing priorities in a fast-paced environment and leading the delivery of large-scale services for complex business offerings.
