Senior Software Engineer (Go & Blockchain) About the Role Join the DPM team and help build the next generation of decentralized prediction market infrastructure. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design and develop the backend systems powering decentralized prediction markets, blockchain integrations, execution services, and real-time market infrastructure. Working closely with the CTO, you'll help architect scalable, fault-tolerant distributed systems that combine blockchain technologies, low-latency backend services, and modern cloud infrastructure. This is a hands-on senior engineering role where you'll spend most of your time designing and building production systems, solving complex distributed systems challenges, and contributing to the long-term technical architecture of the platform. What You'll Do ● Design and build high-performance backend services using Go (Golang) ● Develop core services powering decentralized prediction markets ● Build integrations with blockchain protocols, smart contracts, and decentralized applications ● Integrate with blockchain networks, external services, and real-time market data feeds ● Implement reliable workflow orchestration using Temporal ● Design scalable event-driven microservices using NATS ● Build resilient services for blockchain transaction processing, market connectivity, and backend infrastructure ● Design and optimize distributed systems for scalability, reliability, and low latency ● Write automated tests, observability, monitoring, and operational tooling ● Collaborate with the CTO on architecture, technical decisions, and platform evolution Required Qualifications ● Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience ● 5+ years of professional Go (Golang) development ● 3+ years of blockchain/Web3 development ● Strong experience designing and building distributed backend systems and blockchain applications on EVM-compatible networks, including smart contract integration and production-grade transaction processing ● Deep understanding of blockchain architecture and transaction lifecycles, including transaction signing, gas optimization, nonce management, confirmations, and chain reorganizations ● Comfortable using modern AI-assisted development tools (such as Claude Code, Cursor, or similar) as part of the daily software development workflow ● Experience with PostgreSQL ● Experience with Temporal (or similar workflow orchestration platforms) ● Experience with NATS, Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar messaging technologies ● Experience with Kubernetes and modern CI/CD pipelines ● Strong debugging, troubleshooting, and production support skills Advantages ● Experience with prediction markets ● Experience with Central Limit Order Books (CLOB) ● Experience with Conditional Token Frameworks (CTF) ● Experience with wallet infrastructure or MPC wallets ● Familiarity with Terraform and cloud infrastructure Working at DPM At DPM, you'll join a small, experienced engineering team where you'll have significant ownership over architecture, technology choices, and the evolution of a next-generation decentralized prediction market platform. You'll work closely with the CTO to build highly scalable distributed systems that power secure, reliable, and high-performance blockchain applications.
