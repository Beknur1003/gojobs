This is a remote position. Shuru is an AI, Product and Technology Engineering company helping startups and enterprises build, scale and modernise digital products. Our teams work with high ownership, first-principles thinking and an AI-native approach to solve meaningful business problems and ship production-grade technology. We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer (Golang) to build scalable products, own design and architecture decisions, and mentor teammates. You will work closely with product and engineering teams to build secure, high-performance, and reliable backend systems using Golang (Go). Responsibilities • Own backend development from architecture and system design through implementation, deployment, and production support. • Build products from scratch and scale them as usage and business complexity grow. • Design, develop, and optimize scalable backend services using Golang (Go). • Design robust REST/gRPC APIs, database models, microservices, and event-driven workflows. • Build distributed systems with fault tolerance, reliable communication, and appropriate data consistency guarantees. • Make architecture decisions balancing performance, scalability, consistency, security, and maintainability. • Optimize backend services for low latency, high throughput, and efficient resource utilization. • Improve monitoring, logging, metrics, and alerting. • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines, containerized deployments, and cloud infrastructure. • Mentor teammates through design discussions, code reviews, and hands-on technical guidance. • Collaborate with product and engineering teams and take ownership of delivery and product outcomes. Requirements Requirements • Strong hands-on experience with Golang (Go) in building and operating production-grade backend systems. • Solid understanding of Go concurrency patterns, including goroutines, channels, context management, and synchronization. • Strong understanding of design patterns, architectural principles, and system design. • Hands-on experience with databases, data modeling, query optimization, and REST/gRPC API development. • Experience with microservices, event-driven architectures, asynchronous processing, and distributed systems. • Strong understanding of distributed consistency, concurrency, scalability, and failure handling. • Knowledge of application security, authentication, authorization, and secure data handling. • Working knowledge of DevOps fundamentals, including CI/CD, Docker, containers, and Kubernetes. • Experience with cloud platforms, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP); AWS experience is also acceptable. • Ability to work independently, communicate technical decisions, and mentor teammates in a remote environment. Preferred Background • Startup experience building products from 0 to 1 and scaling them from 1 to 100. • Experience owning architecture and delivering greenfield products using Golang. • Experience building high-concurrency, low-latency, and high-throughput backend services. • Familiarity with Go performance profiling, memory management, and optimization. • Experience in anti-fraud, commercial banking, fintech, or growth engineering is a plus. • Comfort working with ambiguity, taking ownership, and balancing delivery speed with engineering quality. Benefits • Competitive salary and benefits package. • Opportunity to work with a team of experienced product and tech leaders. • A flexible work environment with remote working options. • Continuous learning and development opportunities • Chance to make a significant impact on diverse and innovative projects
