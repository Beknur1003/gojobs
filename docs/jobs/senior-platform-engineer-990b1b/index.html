Senior Platform Engineer

Mitti Sydney опубликовано 5 ноября 2026

Гибрид Senior Полная занятость

Why join us? Mitti by SafetyCulture is a global tech company, just not the kind you're picturing. Our almost 1,000-person team builds tools that make work better for the three billion frontline workers who keep the world moving. We've got big tech scale, without the sign-off layers or corporate theatre. This next chapter is about scaling smart, powered by AI, a clear vision and real ownership. Big tech impact, without the big tech ick. Key responsibilities • Innovate and implement well established practices for Platform Engineering. • Design resilient and easy to use foundational infrastructure to support a growing customer base. • Using productivity tools, such as AI, implement eﬀective quality code. • Contribute to the strategy and design of engineering as a whole. • Facilitate open discussions that bring together the right people to provide the right outcomes. • Contribute to solution design early, helping prevent reliability problems before they ship. • Provide technical leadership to a small team, setting standards and direction while coaching engineers across the organisation on reliability and performance practices. Required skills & experience • In depth knowledge of Kubernetes, Istio and ArgoCD. • Understanding of network principles. • AWS foundational knowledge. • Golang, Python and Terraform. • Strong familiarity with GitOps and CI/CD practices. • Linux and Networking experience including troubleshooting. What you bring as an engineer • Strong platform development background. You've built and shipped production systems, not just observed them with a proficiency in Go/Python and Terraform. • Experience defining and implementing AWS managed solutions, utilising our strategic partnership to ship quicker. • A track record of working across codebases and domains you didn't build - diagnosing issues, contributing improvements and establishing patterns others can follow. • Comfort operating with all manner of customers, treating all members of staﬀ as builders. • Experience with architecture and system design, particularly in SaaS environments operating at scale. • An attitude to do better and push others to do better . What you bring as a senior engineer • You lead through influence, not authority. You build consensus quickly, find the fastest path forward through ambiguity and help teams make good decisions without creating bottlenecks. • You simplify complexity. Whether it's explaining a cross-domain failure mode to leadership or designing a pattern that replaces ad-hoc solutions across teams, you make things clearer and easier for others. • You multiply the people around you - through coaching, pairing, code review and creating tools and documentation that raise the bar for everyone. • You think in terms of the customer experience and the business, not just the system. • You understand why reliability matters, not just how to achieve it. • You adapt your approach to what's needed - sometimes that's deep technical problem-solving, sometimes it's aligning stakeholders, sometimes it's mentoring a mid-level engineer through a tough project. Nice to have • Cloudflare or other CDN based technologies • AI Automation • Knowledge of web technologies (Http 1.1 and 2) • Knowledge of gRPC • Known architectural patterns such as Cell Based Architecture. Success looks like • Our platform architecture is robust, resilient and provides answers before they become problems. • Reliability patterns and shared tooling are adopted as defaults, reducing the eﬀort required for individual teams to build reliable features. • Our platform shards at the data layer , allowing us to scale and contain our customers in smaller environments that carry less risk. • Incidents and alerts are mitigated with ease, using automated tools and great observability standards. • Implement a layer between development and platform, building out APIs to allow customers to use our infrastructure with relative ease. Key stakeholders • Product managers & group leads - partnering to determine what our external customers require and delivering a compliant, resilient system. • Engineering teams across groups - the primary consumers of the platform group who interact with us on a daily basis. • Observability team - Working alongside this team to facilitate a ready to use environment. • Engineering and product leadership - providing a great foundational architecture that allows for their teams to build on top of with little to no friction.
