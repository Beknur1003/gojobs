We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to design, build, and maintain cloud-native infrastructure and internal developer platforms. In this role, you will take ownership of critical platform components, drive complex infrastructure initiatives, and improve developer productivity through scalable tools, automation, and modern cloud technologies. You will collaborate with platform and application engineering teams to enhance infrastructure reliability, streamline deployment processes, and establish engineering best practices. The ideal candidate combines strong software development and cloud infrastructure expertise with hands-on experience in AWS, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code, and CI/CD, along with the ability to independently lead complex technical initiatives. Location: Mexico (Remote) This position is open exclusively to candidates who are currently residing in Mexico. Before applying, we encourage you to review our open positions and make sure the location requirements match your current location. Candidates who are not currently based in Mexico will not be contacted for this opportunity. Requirements: • 5+ years of professional experience in software development and infrastructure engineering. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. • Hands-on, production-level experience with AWS cloud services. • Strong experience with Kubernetes, preferably Amazon EKS. • Experience implementing and maintaining Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform and/or Pulumi. • Hands-on experience with CI/CD pipelines and automation, preferably using CircleCI. • Proficiency in at least one programming language, such as Ruby, Golang, or Python. • Experience designing, building, and operating scalable cloud-native infrastructure and internal developer platforms. • Proven ability to independently scope, prioritize, and execute complex technical initiatives with minimal supervision. • Experience operating large-scale production systems, with a strong understanding of infrastructure reliability, system failure modes, and troubleshooting. • Strong understanding of containerized workloads, infrastructure automation, and deployment best practices. • Strong problem-solving skills, ownership mentality, and a passion for improving developer experience. Nice to have: • Experience building and maintaining internal Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) solutions. • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools and LLM-powered solutions. • Experience integrating AI/LLM capabilities to automate internal support and operational workflows. • Experience mentoring engineers and driving technical standards across engineering teams. Responsibilities: • Design, build, maintain, and evolve core components of internal platform-as-a-service solutions, from architecture through production operations. • Lead complex and ambiguous infrastructure initiatives, independently defining technical approaches and driving execution. • Develop and maintain internal developer tools that improve engineering workflows, deployment reliability, and overall productivity. • Design, implement, and optimize cloud-native infrastructure using AWS and Kubernetes (EKS). • Implement and maintain Infrastructure as Code solutions using Terraform and Pulumi. • Build, manage, and improve CI/CD pipelines to streamline software delivery and deployment automation. • Apply best practices for containerized workloads, infrastructure automation, scalability, and operational reliability. • Leverage AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering productivity and explore LLM-powered solutions for internal support and operational automation. • Identify infrastructure bottlenecks, troubleshoot production issues, and drive continuous platform improvements. • Establish engineering best practices and contribute to improving developer experience across teams. • Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers while collaborating with platform and application engineering teams.
