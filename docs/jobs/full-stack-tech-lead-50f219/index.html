We are growing rapidly and constantly expanding our global contactless payment ecosystem. We are looking for a leader who is hands-on enough to dive into the code, yet visionary enough to shape our architecture, drive our engineering culture, and deliver robust, cloud-native solutions. Instead of maintaining the status quo, you will drive the technical direction of cross-functional squads, mentor talented engineers, and build the scalable technology that powers Payter’s global payment platform. Responsibilities • Lead the technical direction of cross-functional squads, owning architectural decisions (ADRs) and defining coding standards and agile best practices. • Design and build high-performance microservices (Go, Java) and responsive, AI-driven web interfaces (Angular) using clean architecture principles. • Architect and manage secure, cloud-native infrastructure (GCP, Kubernetes) and drive Infrastructure-as-Code and CI/CD pipelines. • Optimize system performance across SQL/NoSQL databases and ensure platform reliability through comprehensive testing, observability, and zero-trust security. • Mentor and coach engineers at all levels, while partnering closely with Product Managers to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions. About Payter At Payter, we are innovators, pioneers, and leaders in the dynamic realm of unattended/self-service contactless and cashless payment technology in a wide range of markets such as Electrical Vehicle Charging, Transportation, Retail, Hospitality, Vending, Charity, Parking, and beyond. The adaptable Payter platform accommodates a diverse range of payment technologies (NFC, EMV, ApplePay, GooglePay, etc.), international banking processes, closed-loop payment and loyalty schemes, and telemetry. Through continuous innovation and in-house development, we redefine how vendors connect with their customers, empowering them to boost revenue, enhance user experiences, and access real-time sales and performance data. We support a broad range of technologies, from Contact & Contactless EMV, Mifare, WiFi, 5G, Bluetooth, Touch Screens and more. Our state-of-the-art products have an extremely long service life, are of high quality, compliant with multiple international standards, boast great design, are user-friendly for all, multifunctional, and easy to integrate. • You have 7+ years of professional software engineering experience, with at least 2 years in a tech lead or staff engineer capacity. • You possess strong, production-grade experience with Go (Golang) and Java for building distributed systems and microservices. • You have deep expertise in JavaScript and TypeScript, with hands-on experience in Angular. • You have a proven track record designing and running cloud-native systems on at least one major cloud platform (GCP preferred), alongside a solid understanding of Kubernetes container orchestration. • You are experienced in building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, applying DevOps/GitOps principles, and using Infrastructure-as-Code tooling like Terraform. • You have demonstrated the ability to lead technical projects end-to-end and possess excellent communication skills to translate complex technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders. • You have familiarity with event-driven architectures (Kafka, NATS), GraphQL API design and security engineering best practices (OWASP, SOC 2).
