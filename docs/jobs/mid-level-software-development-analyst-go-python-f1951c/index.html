You will take part in building scalable solutions, working with modern architecture based on microservices, distributed systems and cloud environments, contributing from development through monitoring and continuous evolution of applications. **What you will do** - Develop and evolve platforms using **Python and/or Golang**; - Architect backend systems using good practices and consolidated market standards, such as **SOLID** and **Clean Architecture**; - Design, implement and evolve **APIs and microservices**; - Implement new features and iterate solutions in an agile way, keeping up with business needs; - Develop and maintain **unit and integration tests**; - Ensure delivery quality through code reviews, testing and technical analyses; - Configure and work in virtualized environments using **Docker**; - Take part in defining and executing **deployment and release** processes; - Document business rules, technical solutions and system architectures; - Carry out technical communication and collaborate with different engineering and product teams; - Work daily with tools such as **GitLab, Rancher, Jira and Confluence**; - Use relational and non-relational databases; - Monitor application indicators, logs and metrics through observability tools. #### Qualifications - Experience with **Python and/or Golang**; - Experience with **AWS** or other cloud computing services; - Experience with developing, consuming and versioning APIs; - Knowledge of **microservices** architecture; - Experience with **Git**; - Experience with **Docker** and **Kubernetes**; - Experience with relational and non-relational databases; - Hands-on experience with observability, monitoring and metrics environments; - Experience with distributed systems and asynchronous communication (**SQS, SNS or similar**); - Ability to work collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams. **It will be a differential if you have** - Experience with **FastAPI**; - Knowledge of **PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, MongoDB and Redis**; - Experience with **DataDog** and **Splunk**;
