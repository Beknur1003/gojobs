Software Engineering Manager Location: Makati City (Onsite) Work Schedule: Morning Shift Employment Type: Full-time Job Summary We are looking for a Software Engineering Manager to lead engineering teams while remaining actively involved in software development and technical decision-making. This role is ideal for a hands-on technical leader who can drive scalable system design, mentor engineers, and collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to deliver high-quality technology solutions. Key Responsibilities • Lead and mentor one or more software engineering teams, providing technical guidance, coaching, and performance support. • Design and develop scalable, secure, and maintainable software solutions aligned with business requirements. • Collaborate with product managers, business stakeholders, and technology leaders to define technical roadmaps and delivery priorities. • Participate in architecture discussions and make key technical decisions for complex systems and applications. • Write code, perform code reviews, and ensure adherence to engineering standards and best practices. • Support Agile development processes, including sprint planning, backlog refinement, estimation, and release planning. • Drive continuous improvement in software quality, development processes, automation, and deployment practices. • Work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure timely and successful project delivery. • Encourage innovation and foster a collaborative, high-performing engineering culture. Qualifications • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a related field. • Proven experience in a Software Engineering Manager, Engineering Manager, Tech Lead, or similar leadership role. • Strong hands-on experience in backend development using Java, Golang, Node.js, Scala, or other object-oriented/open-source technologies. • Experience with React, TypeScript, or JavaScript is preferred. • Strong knowledge of SQL, including MySQL or PostgreSQL. • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and modern containerized application environments. • Familiarity with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD, CircleCI, or similar. • Solid understanding of RESTful APIs, microservices, and service-oriented architecture. • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. • Experience in supply chain, logistics, warehouse management, procurement, or inventory systems is an advantage but not required. Preferred Skills • Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments. • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. • Ability to balance hands-on technical work with leadership responsibilities. • Passion for building scalable systems and developing engineering talent.
