At River we are building the world's most trusted financial institution to empower people to take ownership of their financial lives through Bitcoin, the world's only incorruptible digital money. We believe in a future where every person will have bitcoin savings, and every business will have bitcoin on the balance sheet. We obsessively design and build delightful products that help our clients buy, sell, secure, and use Bitcoin. Security is foundational to everything we build. Our clients trust us with their bitcoin, their dollars, and their personal information, and every engineer at River shares responsibility for protecting them. We're looking for a Staff Security Engineer who thinks like an attacker, builds like an engineer, and uses AI to find and fix vulnerabilities before anyone else does. This is a hands-on, high-ownership role across our applications, infrastructure, and corporate systems. You'll build AI-driven tooling that tests and hardens our systems continuously, and you'll fix what it finds. You'll work closely with company leadership and partner with teams across the company. What You Will Be Doing • Automate vulnerability discovery across our code, infrastructure, and dependencies with AI agents and other tooling • Prioritize building out our internal AI platform securely • Guide company security policies, strategy, and own our security roadmap • Test our applications, APIs, and infrastructure the way an attacker would, and drive findings to verified fixes • Threat-model new products and features with the engineers building them • Build detection and response capabilities, and lead investigations • Handle security reports from external researchers, from triage through resolution • Strengthen identity and access management across cloud and corporate systems • Harden endpoints and our software supply chain • Shape how River adopts AI tools and agents safely, in engineering and across the company • Partner with engineering, product and operations teams to protect our clients from emerging security threats • Evaluate security risks of vendors and integrations • Set standards and build paved roads that make the secure path the easy one, and mentor engineers across the company What We Look For In You • 8+ years in security engineering, software engineering, or offensive security • Hands-on experience pentesting or red-teaming web applications, APIs, and cloud environments, and the engineering skill to fix what you find • Strong cloud and infrastructure security fundamentals: IAM, containers and orchestration, networking, and infrastructure as code • You write production-quality code • You build with AI agents every day, and know how to secure them and where they fail • Sound judgment in balancing risk with broad company productivity • Clear communication with engineers, executives, and non-technical partners • You drive outcomes across teams and own them end to end • Good intuition for which security efforts will have the most impact Nice To Haves • Bitcoin or Lightning security experience • Experience at a bank, exchange, or other regulated financial institution • Detection engineering or incident response leadership • Applied cryptography • Familiarity with Elixir, Postgres, React, Golang, Google Cloud, and AWS Location
