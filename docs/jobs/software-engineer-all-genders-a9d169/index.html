Golang | .NET | Azure | Artificial Intelligence About Genetec At Genetec, we create exceptional outcomes for our customers with innovative strategies and software technologies that will keep their security environment robust and resilient. As a global leader in video surveillance software, the drive to go beyond expectations has been key to the Genetec experience since day one. Want to know what it is like to work for us? Check out our Kununu profile! Why this role matters Help shape the future of security investigations. You will build cloud-based services and AI-powered capabilities that help customers search, analyze, and act on security data at scale. From concept to production, you'll contribute to solutions used across the Genetec ecosystem. What it's like to work here You'll join a growing Vienna-based engineering team with a high degree of ownership and autonomy. We work closely across functions and locations, value knowledge sharing, and encourage engineers to actively influence both product and technical decisions. Key Responsibilities • Build and evolve hybrid solutions that connect Genetec’s on-premises and SaaS products. • Develop backend services and APIs primarily with .NET or Go. • Design data integrations and pipelines for multiple security data types. • Build investigation workflows across Genetec products. • Develop multi-domain search, analytics, and movement-analysis capabilities. • Integrate AI capabilities such as natural-language search, computer vision, semantic search, summarization, anomaly detection, and recommendations. • Productionize and maintain models delivered by research teams through the Genetec ML Platform. • Design for hybrid constraints such as data locality, bandwidth, retention, security, reliability, and scale. • Work with Product, UX, QA, Security, AI, and other engineering teams to shape solutions and deliver customer value. • Contribute to architecture, technical design, testing, CI/CD, observability, and production operations. • Participate in paid on-call and incident response. • Share knowledge and contribute to an innovative engineering culture. Requirements We welcome all qualified candidates, including those who may not meet every listed requirement. If you're excited about this role and believe you can make an impact, we’d love to hear from you. What you bring • Strong professional experience with either Go or C#/.NET. and willingness to apply both on the long run. • Experience designing and building APIs and production software. • Strong SQL and data-modelling skills. • Experience writing maintainable, tested code and participating in code reviews. • Familiarity with standard development practices including Git and CI/CD. • A pragmatic problem-solving mindset and the ability to work independently. • Professional proficiency in spoken and written English. • An interest in building products that solve complex customer problems. Nice to have • Experience with Azure or another cloud platform. • Experience with hybrid, distributed, or on-premises systems. • Experience with data pipelines, search, analytics, or large-scale integrations. • Experience productionizing AI or ML models. • Familiarity with containers, infrastructure as code, security engineering, or observability. • Experience with security, video, or other data-intensive enterprise products. • React and TypeScript experience. Frontend development is optional for this role. Benefits • An opportunity to make a meaningful impact in an international, diverse, and collaborative work environment. • A culture that encourages creativity and fresh ideas, giving you the freedom to challenge the status quo and shape how we work. • A role within an industry‑leading global company and an open, supportive atmosphere. • Corporate Discount Program offering access to a variety of partner perks. • Language classes and get‑togethers to support international collaboration. • Participation in international team events and conferences, fostering strong connections across regions. • Employee Performance Plan, recognizing your contribution to business success. • Hybrid work model with three days per week in our campus‑style Vienna office, supporting our collaboration and company culture. • Choice of subsidized lunch vouchers or a public transport ticket, helping reduce everyday costs.
