Job Description: At Remitly, we believe everyone deserves the freedom to access, move, and manage their money wherever life takes them. Since 2011, we've tirelessly delivered on our promise to customers sending money globally, providing secure, simple, and reliable ways to manage their money, ensuring true peace of mind. Whether it's supporting loved ones back home, growing a business across continents, or pursuing new opportunities abroad, we're not just here to move money— we're here to move our global customers forward. We're looking for builders, reimaginers, and global thinkers who want to work at the intersection of technology, trust, and transformation. If that's you and you're ready to do the most meaningful work of your career—we invite you to join over 2,800 passionate Remitlians worldwide who are united by our vision to transform lives with trusted financial services that transcend borders. About the Role: The Global Network (GNET) team is looking for an SDE 2 to join Remitly and help scale our funding and disbursement processing across our vast operational regions, alongside collaborating with other engineers towards setting a north star technical vision. As an SDE 2, you will bring exciting features to our customers by contributing to the expansion of our pay-in and pay-out networks globally. You will help build against a backlog of features designed to expand our Global Network powering all of Remitly's product offerings in creative ways. You Will: • Develop new partner integrations for our pay-in and pay-out networks, with the possibility of working with partners that support money movements for over 170 countries and growing • Deliver well-tested, observable, documented, efficient, scalable, and resilient solutions. • Participate in interviewing, code reviewing, mentoring, and ramping new hires. • Partner with PM to break down tasks, provide estimates, and discuss tradeoffs • Partner with Operations team members across the globe to provide technical recommendations to customer-facing problems • Work with partners around the globe and be a representative of the company and our engineering team to our global partners • Become an expert with tools, programming languages, and engineering processes used within the GNET team in a scalable cloud-based architecture • Write technical specifications and participate in design reviews • Mentor, promote best practices, and push for technical excellence across the company • Partner closely with your manager and promote the technical direction and roadmap You Have: • A degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field or equivalent experience • 4+ years of building, shipping, and maintaining consumer software products • 2+ years working with services deployed on a commercial cloud provider like AWS • General experience migrating legacy services into modernized architecture • Proficiency in Java, GoLang, or other general purposes languages • Proficiency in working with large-scale database systems like MySQL, DynamoDB • Proficiency in working with monitoring systems such as Prometheus and VictoriaMetrics • General knowledge of technical architecture and system design concepts • Clear and objective writing and in-person communication • A desire for learning new technologies and systems • Payments/Fintech experience - added bonus!
