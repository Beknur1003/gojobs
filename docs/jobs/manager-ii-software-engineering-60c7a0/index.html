A Career that Empowers You to Build Your Future The Software Engineering Manager II will lead a larger team of software engineers and play a crucial role in managing more complex projects. This role requires a deeper understanding of software architecture, project management, and team leadership, with a focus on driving successful project outcomes and fostering professional growth within the team. Your Responsibilities on the Team • Manage a team of mid to senior-level software engineers, providing guidance on complex technical challenges. • Lead the planning, development, and delivery of multiple software projects, ensuring adherence to timelines and quality standards. • Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to align software development efforts with business objectives. • Drive continuous improvement in software development practices, including code quality, testing, and deployment. • Conduct regular performance evaluations and support team members in their career development. • Assist in strategic planning for the software engineering department, including resource allocation and technology investments. • Mentor and develop engineering talent. Your Toolbox • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field; a Master’s degree is a plus. • 5+ years of experience in software development, with at least 2+ years in a management or leadership role. • Expertise in software architecture, design patterns, and development methodologies. • Strong project management skills, with experience managing complex software projects. • Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to make data-driven decisions. Technical Stack • GoLang for backend services • Flutter for frontend development • AWS Postgres database • MCP built on FastMCP, hosted on AgentCore Runtime Physical & Office/Site Presence Requirements: This is a remote opportunity. This is primarily a sedentary office position which requires the incumbent to have the ability to operate computer equipment, speak, hear, bend, stoop, reach, lift, and move and carry up to 25 lbs.
