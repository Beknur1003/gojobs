Software Engineer SMTS The Ajna team builds and operates Salesforce's internal Kafka-as-a-Service platform — the distributed messaging backbone that powers real-time data pipelines, alerting, analytics, and event streaming across every major Salesforce product and cloud environment. The platform serves a broad ecosystem of internal engineering teams across commercial, GovCloud, and sovereign cloud environments globally. This team pursues principled, high-impact architectural evolution with direct consequences at production scale. The team operates with distributed coverage across Pacific and India Standard Time zones, maintains continuous on-call coverage, and is actively invested in intelligent automation — including AI-powered tooling built in-house to support triage and auto-remediation at scale. What We Are Looking For * Strong distributed systems fundamentals, including deep understanding of Kafka internals (leader election, ISR management, consumer groups, log compaction, partition rebalancing) and the ability to reason about consistency and availability trade-offs in multi-tenant environments * Kubernetes engineering proficiency, including StatefulSets, Autoscaling, PersistentVolumeClaims, and Helm; demonstrated experience debugging production Kubernetes workloads * Infrastructure-as-code experience with Terraform for cloud resources (IAM, object storage, messaging, block storage) * Java engineering depth; the team's core services are Java-based and require clean, performant, operationally sound implementation * Operational ownership mindset: participation in on-call rotation, production incident investigation, and systematic root cause analysis * Strong written communication skills and the ability to produce architectural designs that influence decisions across team and organizational boundaries Your Impact: • Deliver cloud infrastructure automation tools, frameworks, workflows, and validation platforms on our public cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, Azure, or Alibaba • Designing, developing, debugging, and operating resilient distributed systems that run across thousands of compute nodes in multiple data centers • Using and contributing to open source technology (Spinnaker, Zookeeper, etc.) • Developing Infrastructure-as-Code using Terraform • Writing microservices on containerization frameworks such as Kubernetes, Docker, Mesos • Resolving complex technical issues and drive innovations that improve system availability, resilience, and performance • Eat, sleep, and breathe services. You have experience balancing live-site management, feature delivery, and retirement of technical debt • Participate in the team’s on-call rotation to address complex problems in real-time and keep services operational and highly available Required Skills: • • A related technical degree required • 2- 5+ years backend software development experience • Deep knowledge of programming in Java, Golang, Python, or Ruby • Experience using and contributing to open source technology: Kafka, Spinnaker, Zookeeper • Experience owning and operating multiple instances of a critical service • Experience with Agile development methodology and Test Driven Development • Experience with critical infrastructure services including, monitoring, alerting, logging, and reporting applications Benefits & Perks Check out our benefits site which explains our various benefits, including wellbeing reimbursement, generous parental leave, adoption assistance, fertility benefits, and more.
