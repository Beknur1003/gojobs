Job Description: Job Family Definition: Designs, develops, tests, deploys, and troubleshoots/debugs software Productivity Tooling and Applications used for our AI-driven Engineering workflows. Management Level Definition: Contributes to AI-Infrastructure (Fusion, Toby, etc.) and AI Native Ecosystem projects that align to business goals of migrating all internal workflows to be automated by AI Agents. Responsibilities: • Work with our Test/Development Engineers to determine “The Problem Statement”, define the Requirements, articulate the Specification of the Solution, and Design/Develop/Implement the Solution. • Design and develop new Applications or AI Agents that allow for fully automated Engineering workflows • Develop and enhance any of the existing SW tools/infrastructure using AI-LLMs to design, code, and generate extensive test suites that insure high quality of code delivered at MVP or FRS Required Experience and Skills: • Currently pursuing a BA or MS or PhD in CS/CE/EE with good experience in software development. • Minimum of two years of experience working with AI-powered coding assistants and agentic development platforms, like GitHub CoPilot (GHCP), etc. • Very good at programming (Python, C/C++, Java, ...), algorithm design and implementations. • Good verbal and written communication and presentation skills. • Self-motivated team player, problem solver (for self, and for others), and capable of working in a fast-paced R&D environment Preferred Qualifications: • Experience with data mining, machine learning, and artificial intelligence • Practice with various supervised and unsupervised models • Experience with deep learning and neutral network is a plus • Experience with big data, Hadoop and stream processing using kafka is a plus • Test automation or programming skills in Python, GoLang, or R is a plus Intern Education & Experience Required: Working towards a Bachelor's and/or Master's degree with a focus in Computer Science, Information Systems, or equivalent. Knowledge and Skills: • Experience or understanding of software systems design tools and languages. • Good analytical and problem-solving skills. • Understanding of design for software systems running on multiple platform types. • Good written and verbal communication skills; mastery in English and local language. • Understanding of basic testing, coding, and debugging procedures. • Effective written and verbal communication skills; mastery in English and local language.
