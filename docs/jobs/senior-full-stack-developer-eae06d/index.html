We're Hiring: Senior Full Stack Developer Location: Columbus, OH (onsite) Work Model: Hybrid — 4 days onsite, 1 day remote Type: Full-time Relocation notice — please read before applying. This is an onsite, Columbus-based role. Candidates who are not already local must be able to relocate to the Columbus, OH area immediately upon offer. We cannot accommodate a relocation timeline or a delayed start once the interview process is complete. Please apply only if you are local or fully certain you can relocate right away. About iVueit iVueit is a technology platform that gives enterprises on-demand, verified eyes on their physical locations. Through our network and mobile capture tools, customers request "Vues" — structured photo and video verifications of real-world sites — and get back reliable, actionable visual data at scale. We're a growing Engineering team building the backend services, web applications, and integrations that power this at production scale. About the Role We're looking for a Senior Full Stack Developer to join our Engineering team as a hands-on technical leader. This person will own significant backend and frontend work, lead the system design of new projects, and help guide the team in delivering them. The core of this role is Go on the backend and React on the frontend, paired with strong system design and architecture leadership. You'll design how projects are built, make the key architectural calls, and bring the team along with you — not just write features. We're also a modern engineering shop that expects responsible, effective use of AI development tools to raise both speed and quality.
