Swarm Aero is redefining air power, building the largest swarming UAV and the most versatile swarming aircraft network in the world. The company is moving quickly to launch the first aircraft designed specifically for swarming, as well as the Command & Control software to mobilize swarms of thousands of heterogeneous autonomous assets and empower human operators to achieve superhuman results. The team has created and exited multiple startups, negotiated defense deals worth billions of dollars, and designed and built 30+ novel aircraft, with aerospace experience from Scaled Composites, Airbus, Archer Aviation, Blue Origin, and Boom Supersonic. About the Role Most software engineers never see their code tested against reality. At Swarm Aero, that's the baseline. We are looking for a Staff Forward Deployed Engineer to own multiple initiatives across our customer programs and lead the forward deployed engineers working on them. You will work directly with U.S. government and military customers to understand the mission, decide what our C2 and onboard autonomy software can deliver, and set the technical plan to get it into the field. You will make the feasibility calls, keep everyone involved working from the same plan, and make sure your initiatives are ready for the releases that put new capability in operators' hands. This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You will still write code, debug systems in the field, and be on site when the work goes live. What You'll Do • Own multiple initiatives within a customer program, from early requirements through deployment. Turn mission needs into a technical roadmap for each one, and make the feasibility and tradeoff calls across them. • Lead the forward deployed engineers working on your initiatives by setting technical direction, breaking down the work, and clearing blockers. • Write and integrate C2 and onboard autonomy software for unmanned air and sea vehicles, and debug it in the field when something breaks. • Participate in and lead design reviews for your initiatives before work starts. • Run post-incident reviews after field issues, find the root cause, and put fixes in place so the same problem does not happen again. • Bring field data and lessons learned back to the core product team. • Work with partner teams to align on priorities and timelines, gather their feedback, and run internal testing before every deployment. • Plan how your initiatives fit into each contract release alongside core product work, and make sure they are tested and ready before the release ships. • Serve as the main technical contact for vehicle and sensor vendors, and lead the integration of their systems with ours. • Define success criteria with customers for each event, run the readiness review for your initiatives beforehand, and give the go/no-go recommendation. • Mentor forward deployed engineers and help with technical assessments of potential candidates. • Support deployments outside normal hours, including on-call response and rotational coverage aligned to mission tempo. • Travel domestically and internationally (up to 50%) to test sites and customer locations, and lead the team on site. This Role Is For You If • You have shipped software to the field and want to own a bigger share of the outcome. • You like making technical calls under pressure and explaining them to people who need a straight answer. • You enjoy helping other engineers grow while staying hands-on yourself. • You can turn an ambiguous customer request into a plan a team can execute.
