• Deadline: Ongoing • Salary: Competitive • Location: Cambridge • Degree required: Master's/PhD • Disciplines: Analytics, Software, Computer Science, Maths, AI About us MathWorks is the leading developer of mathematical computing software. Engineers and scientists worldwide rely on its products to accelerate the pace of discovery, innovation, and development. About this Programme • Are you studying Engineering, Computer Science, or other STEM subject and looking to make an impact in a dynamic, collaborative software engineering environment? • Are you looking to develop both your technical and leadership skills and contribute to high-quality software used by engineers and scientists around the world? The Engineering Development Group (EDG) at MathWorks is a proven, excellent technical and leadership development program designed to cultivate the next generation of leaders and innovators within our Software Development organisation. As a member of EDG, you’ll have the flexibility to select your own projects and be mentored by industry experts in areas such as software engineering, quality engineering, user experience, technical writing, and more. The program is designed to provide opportunities to support your learning and growth to successfully transition to the next step of your career at MathWorks. We offer technical learning opportunities in areas such as full-stack software development (with languages including C++, MATLAB, JavaScript and Go),high-performance computing, cloud computing integration, agentic AI, deep learning & text analytics, physical modelling, embedded systems, text analytics, code generation & verification and wireless communications. We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone feels supported. Apply today if you like the idea of working in a team that values your voice, your journey and your potential! Responsibilities As part of our Development organisation, you will join a diverse and collaborative team, contributing to impactful software engineering projects. Additionally, EDG team members will gain experience with MathWorks products and user workflows through direct interactions with customers. This unique blend of work is designed to foster continuous learning and professional growth. Specifically, you will: • Benefit from extensive learning opportunities to deepen your technical skills and knowledge. • Learn about MathWorks products and customer workflows through training, mentorship and collaborative on-the-job learning to build a user-centric perspective. • Leverage Agentic AI to support and enhance core engineering activities such as writing code, developing product demos, and investigating and resolving technical issues, while applying sound engineering judgment and best practices. • Develop and demonstrate leadership and initiative through opportunities to coach and mentor peers, and lead process improvement projects. • Explore different teams to discover your passion to transition into teams aligned with your skillset, interests, and MathWorks business needs. Qualifications • Masters/Ph.D. in Engineering, Computer Science, or another STEM subject with a consistent, well-rounded academic track record • Effective problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills • Curiosity, learning agility, and a willingness to explore new engineering tools and technologies, including AI-assisted workflows • Solid foundational programming skills in at least one language (e.g., C++, JavaScript, Python, Golang, and/or MATLAB) • Experience withMathWorks products, system design and/or object-oriented programming principles is a plus Why MathWorks? It's the chance to collaborate with bright, passionate people. It's the opportunity to accelerate the pace of discovery, innovation, and learning in engineering and science. And it’s a commitment to doing the right thing—for each individual, our customers, and the local community. We cultivate an enjoyable, participatory, and rational environment that champions individual growth, encourages initiative, values collaboration, shares success, and rewards excellence.
