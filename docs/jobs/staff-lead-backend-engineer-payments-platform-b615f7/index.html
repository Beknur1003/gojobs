About the Role For a small business, getting paid isn’t an abstract technical problem. It affects whether they can pay their people, buy supplies and take on their next opportunity. We connect money movement with financial context to help small businesses manage cash flow with greater confidence. Payments is one of Xero’s biggest areas of investment and growth. As a Lead Engineer on Payments Platform, you’ll join early-stage initiatives and make foundational architectural decisions for capabilities that power money movement across Xero and enable new product lines globally. Your work can span shared platform capabilities and customer-facing payment products, including global bill payments. You’ll stay hands-on in production code and proofs of concept while setting the technical direction other engineers and teams build on. It’s an opportunity to shape both the technology and the engineering practices of a growing team, with a direct connection to the problems small businesses face every da. The Technical Challenges • A shared payments platform: Build orchestration, risk and fraud, and multi-provider capabilities that work across products and markets, helping teams launch new payment products faster without rebuilding the same foundations. • Global bill payments: Make a complex financial process feel simple for small businesses while keeping it accurate, secure and connected to the accounting record. • High throughput at scale: Design reliable, secure distributed services that remain dependable under real-world production constraints. • Customer impact alongside architectural progress: Selectively rebuild and enhance existing systems, delivering value quickly while moving toward a coherent long-term architecture. • AI capabilities customers can trust: Apply AI to challenging product problems while protecting customer trust, and bring the same discipline to how we evaluate and operate those capabilities. Impact • First 6 months: You understand the payments platform, its consumers and the customer problems it enables teams to solve. You’ve shipped production code and aligned the team on the architecture and roadmap for at least one core capability, such as onboarding, orchestration, risk and fraud, or multi-provider support. Through mentorship, technical reviews and practical improvements, you’ve helped elevate the team’s engineering capability. • First 12 months: Your architectural decisions are operating in production and helping new payment products launch faster. You’ve helped establish engineering standards and clear ownership for the Vancouver Payments team, and your work is reaching small businesses through faster, safer ways to pay and get paid. Ownership You’ll own the technical direction for your area of the payments platform, from design and proof of concept through production and on-call. You’ll lead architecture, API design and technology decisions for that area, with end-to-end accountability for reliability, security and operational excellence. You’ll work closely with engineering and cross-functional partners to make trade-offs explicit and turn direction into delivery. Your impact will come from both your own technical contributions and the capability you build in others. Because both the initiative and the Vancouver team are early in their development, you’ll also help shape engineering practices, quality standards and AI-assisted ways of working that make the team more effective over time. Who You'll Work With • Lili Kan, GM/VP, Payments Platform Engineering. Lili brings extensive experience building platforms and leading engineering teams in Silicon Valley. She previously led Payment Intelligence engineering at Stripe, building systems that protect payments from fraud. Before that, she spent four years at Uber, working across platform services, systems for operating self-driving fleets, and Driver Safety & Insurance Engineering. • Jing Feng, Head of Engineering, Payments Platform. Jing brings engineering leadership experience from Uber, Apple, LinkedIn and Yahoo. Most recently, she led Risk Product Engineering at Uber, building capabilities to detect and prevent fraud and risk. What You’ll Do • Define and build core platform services: Lead the design and development of reliable, scalable, performant, and secure services for Xero's Payments Platform. • Architect the future of payments: Shape the technical foundation for Xero's money movement capabilities while unlocking new product lines. • Stay hands-on: Contribute directly to production code, proof-of-concepts, and technical deep dives. • Solve complex problems: Tackle challenging fintech and distributed systems problems with innovative solutions. • Drive technical excellence: Set a high bar for reliability, security, and scale through design reviews, mentoring, and best practices. • Collaborate cross-functionally: Partner with Product, Security, Risk, and Operations teams to translate complex business needs into resilient engineering solutions. • Mentor and grow engineers: Act as a role model, coach, and technical leader, fostering a culture of inclusion, collaboration, and excellence. • Ensure operational excellence: Lead on-call practices, build robust observability, and champion high-performance, reliable systems. • Drive innovation with AI: Champion the adoption of AI tools and practices across the team-from accelerating development workflows to exploring how AI capabilities can strengthen the platform. What You Bring Must-have: • 8+ Years of software engineering experience, with a proven track record as a Staff / Technical Lead delivering highly available, distributed backend systems at scale. • Deep expertise in system architecture, API design, and building high-throughput, reliable services—understanding how to design under the constraints of both new and existing systems. • Strong proficiency in at least one modern backend stack (e.g java, python, Golang, C++) and experience with cloud-native platforms • Experience with both SQL and NoSQL databases. • Demonstrated leadership in setting technical direction, solving complex problems, and mentoring senior and junior engineers. • Strong collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across engineering, product, and operations. • Proven ability to create clarity from ambiguity and break down complex problems into clear, actionable solutions that bring impact. • Hands-on credibility: you stay in code, design reviews and technical spikes, not only strategy. • Experience auditing codebases to uphold high standards for security, testability, and maintainability, with the ability to set and guide code design decisions. • Deep curiosity for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and hands-on experience working with AI tools-including using AI to accelerate engineering productivity, enhance developer workflows, and explore AI-driven capabilities within the platform. Nice to have: • Experience in payments, fintech, or other highly regulated, compliance-heavy domains • Proficiency in C#, .NET and AWS • A strong track record of partnering with Product, Design and other cross-functional teams to turn complex business needs into resilient engineering solutions. How We Work We’re bold in our vision and pragmatic in execution, delivering useful improvements along the way rather than waiting for a large rewrite. We invest in AI to learn faster and focus effort on higher-value problems, while engineers stay accountable for what they ship. We use shared frameworks to avoid reinvention, leaving teams room to innovate. Engineers own their work, including quality, reliability, and on-call. Leaders set context and priorities, and engineers help shape the solutions. Where we work We offer a hybrid model that blends the flexibility of working from home with the collaboration of the office, including designated boost days in our modern office spaces. This role is open to hybrid candidates in San Mateo & San Francisco, CA. We also welcome applications from candidates across Canada interested in relocating to Vancouver, and from remote candidates in Seattle, Washington. Our Hiring Process • TX phone screen (45 minutes): a conversation with your Talent Specialist about your background, motivations and the role. • Technical assessments: a coding assessment and a systems design assessment, run through an independent third-party provider and based on real-world scenarios. • Technical deep dive (45–60 minutes): with a Principal Engineer and the hiring team. You'll walk through a past project you led, covering how you thought about it, executed it and led through its complexity. • Values / Future Proof Xero (45 minutes): how you show up day to day, aligned with Xero's values and capabilities such as adaptability, ownership, customer thinking and AI fluency. • LEADership (45–60 minutes): real examples of how you lead, influence and deliver in ambiguous, fast-changing environments, assessed against Xero's leadership principles.
