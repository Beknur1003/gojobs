Role: Senior QA Engineer - Logistics Technology (SaaS) Location: Pune (Corporate Office) Role context and purpose: We are looking for an experienced Senior QA Engineer with strong logistics domain expertise and hands-on experience in manual testing, automation, API testing, integration testing, and database validation. The role involves ensuring quality across web, mobile, backend services, APIs, databases, and third-party integrations, with a strong focus on complex logistics workflows, real-world scenarios, edge cases, and end-to-end validation. Key Responsibilities: • Understand requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria and create comprehensive test scenarios and test cases. • Perform functional, regression, exploratory, smoke, sanity, integration, and end-to-end testing. • Test web, mobile, APIs, backend services, and third-party integrations across different roles and business scenarios. • Develop and maintain automated test suites for web, API, and mobile applications; identify suitable automation opportunities and improve regression coverage. • Perform API testing using Postman, including request/response validation, authentication, payloads, error handling, assertions, and regression testing. • Validate frontend, mobile, backend, API, and database behaviour, including data accuracy and transaction consistency. • Test React-based web applications, Flutter-based mobile applications, and Go/Python backend services. • Validate databases using MariaDB and TiDB, including data flow and transaction-level validation. • Simulate real-world logistics scenarios covering shipment/order lifecycle, freight planning, trip allocation, dispatch, tracking, delivery, POD, billing, cancellations, rescheduling, and operational exceptions. • Validate integrations with transporters, logistics partners, customer systems, and external platforms. • Identify, document, prioritize, and track defects with clear evidence and impact assessment. • Collaborate with Product, Engineering, Implementation, and Support teams for defect resolution and root-cause analysis. • Own QA activities for assigned features/releases, ensuring adequate test coverage and timely sign-off. What Will You Need? • B.E./B. Tech or Graduate • 4 to 7 years of hands-on experience in software QA/testing. • Strong logistics domain expertise, preferably TMS, WMS, freight management, transportation, shipment tracking, or supply chain platforms. • Strong experience in manual testing and QA automation. • Experience testing web, mobile, APIs, backend services, and integrations. • Hands-on testing experience with React and Flutter applications. • Experience testing Go (Golang) and Python-based APIs/services. • Strong hands-on experience with Postman. • Experience with functional, regression, integration, and E2E testing. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and complex business-scenario simulation skills. • Familiarity with Jira and test management tools. • Good communication and collaboration skills. Good to Have • Experience using AI tools for E2E and unit-test automation. • Experience in Product Management, Business Analysis, or Product Ownership. • Customer/application support, production issue investigation, or incident management experience. • Knowledge of CI/CD, Git, automated test execution, and test automation frameworks. • Exposure to performance, load, and reliability testing. • Proficiency in SQL and database validation. Key Competencies • Strong logistics/business-process understanding and attention to detail. • Ability to identify edge cases beyond happy-path scenarios. • End-to-end ownership of quality across the application stack. • Strong problem-solving and root-cause analysis. • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced Agile environment. • Quality-first mindset focused on preventing defects, not just identifying them.
