Developer (SAP Concur Travel Engineering AI & Core team)

Гибрид Senior Полная занятость
MySQLMicroservicesCI/CDAI/LLM
What you'll bring You have strong coding and debugging skills and hands-on experience building and operating cloud-based microservices in SAP Concur Travel. A solid understanding of APIs, distributed systems basics, and CI/CD practices is required. Experience with AI/LLM workflows, including prompt design, basic RAG patterns, and integrating AI services into applications, is important. Familiarity with observability (logging, metrics, tracing), performance tuning, and system monitoring is expected. An ownership mindset, combined with experience in testing, validation, and troubleshooting production systems, will set you up to contribute well. 3 to 6 years of relevant experience in software development with master’s or bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering or equivalent. What you'll build You'll design, develop, and maintain production-grade AI-enabled features and services in SAP Concur Travel, with a focus on scalability and reliability. LLM integration and basic RAG workflows, including prompt management and response validation, are part of your core work. You'll build and improve evaluation and benchmarking pipelines to measure AI output quality and system performance. Application health, latency, throughput, and system efficiency are areas you'll actively monitor and improve. Debugging and resolving production issues, contributing to incident analysis, and implementing stability fixes are all expected. You'll support and enhance CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows. Cross-functional collaboration to refine requirements and improve system design is a regular expectation. Where you belong You will join the SAP Concur Travel Engineering AI & Core team in Labs India. SAP Concur offers cloud-based services that make it simple to manage travel and expenses. SAP Concur supports more than 54,000 businesses and 96 million users across 150+ countries, processing over one billion transactions annually. You'll own features and modules end-to-end and work closely with product, data, platform, and AI teams to align on goals and deliver quality results. This team values direct communication, shared accountability, and continuous improvement. You'll participate in code reviews, share technical knowledge, and help build team capabilities as you develop deeper expertise in AI-native application development. We value the skills you bring and support you to build on them through our learning and development opportunities. These are the skills associated with this role: Core Skills- Hands-on Golang/C# development, cloud-native services, RDBMS proficiency (SQL Server or MySql etc), experience with AI/LLM APIs, Agentic AI basics, and data ingestion and preprocessing for AI. AI Adoption Capability, AI output quality assurance, AI-assisted development, context engineering, agentic orchestration, agentic engineering, application security, cloud architecture, observability, semantic retrieval, high availability engineering, troubleshooting, evaluations and benchmarking engineering, CI/CD.
