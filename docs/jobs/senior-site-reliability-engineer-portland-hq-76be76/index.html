Senior Site Reliability Engineer Company Overview: At VETRO, we believe visualizing data unlocks hidden potential, radically simplifying business operations and digitizing the future of connectivity. We focus on empowering network operators with clarity and control over their fiber networks, enabling them to operate more efficiently. Our platform serves as the physical network asset system of record, offering visibility and control from strategic planning to daily operations. We empower our customers to bridge the digital divide, unlock new opportunities, and maximize the value of their networks. Let's collaborate to shape the future of connectivity. Location: Portland, ME HQ as Hybrid Position Overview: Reporting to the Senior Manager of Operations, the Senior Site Reliability Engineer will be responsible for scaling the global VETRO FiberMap platform in order to deliver the next generation of GIS fiber management software. Key Responsibilities, including but not limited to: • Reliability & Availability: Establish and improve SLIs/SLOs, monitoring, alerting, and platform health. • Cloud Infrastructure: Build, operate, and optimize scalable cloud infrastructure using infrastructure-as-code and modern deployment practices. • Observability: Ensure we can quickly understand platform performance, failures, dependencies, and customer impact. • Incident Management: Improve detection, response, root-cause analysis, postmortems, and prevention of recurring incidents. Help mature production on-call procedures and participate in off-hours support rotations. • Automation: Automate deployments, remediation, infrastructure management, capacity planning, and repetitive operational work. • Performance & Scale: Contribute to the scaling and development of the VETRO FiberMap platform using modern cloud infrastructure tools and architectures. • Identify bottlenecks and improve application, database, infrastructure, and network performance as customer usage grows. • Optimize the performance of large datasets running on top of tools such as Postgres, NATS, mapping and tile services, and more. • Developer Enablement: Design and develop platform tools to empower software engineers to integrate and deliver software solutions with high quality and frequency. Improve CI/CD, deployment safety, environments, tooling, and developer self-service. • Security & Resilience: Apply security best practices to all aspects of the infrastructure. Partner with Engineering and Security Consultant on access controls, vulnerability management, disaster recovery, backups, and business continuity. Gather evidence for security audits such as SOC 2. • AI-Enabled Operations: Use AI and intelligent automation to accelerate troubleshooting, analyze logs and incidents, identify patterns, improve documentation, and reduce operational toil. • Cost Efficiency: Monitor cloud utilization and help ensure infrastructure spend scales efficiently with the business.
