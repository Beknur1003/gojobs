Summary: We are looking for an experienced Senior Systems Developer with strong system analysis and integration development capabilities to join our IT Systems team, with a primary allocation to the Finance Systems team as a dedicated integrations specialist. This role combines hands-on iPaaS development with the ability to translate complex finance business requirements into structured, reliable, secure, and well-documented integration solutions, while leveraging AI to drive process automation and standardization. You will design, build, and maintain integrations connecting critical finance systems such as NetSuite, Coupa, and Leapfin, working closely with the Finance Systems team who specialize in NetSuite and Coupa. The ideal candidate can operate independently, own integrations end to end, and work cross-functionally in a fast-paced SaaS environment. You will be expected to explore and apply AI-powered tools and techniques to accelerate development, improve process consistency, and unlock new automation opportunities across finance operations. As a steward of sensitive financial data, you will play a key role in upholding Clio’s high compliance and security standards across all integration touchpoints. This includes leading requirement gathering, translating business needs into technical specifications, data flow diagrams, and field mapping documentation • Build event-driven automations, webhook-based triggers, and bulk data synchronization processes with robust monitoring, error handling, and retry mechanisms to ensure data consistency and production-grade reliability • Support requirements • Document integration architectures, data flows, API specifications, and troubleshooting procedures to support operational continuity and audit readiness What You Bring • Strong
