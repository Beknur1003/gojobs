About HIVED 📦 At HIVED, we’re steamrolling forward as one of Europe’s fastest growing startups, and our momentum shows no signs of slowing. Based in London, we are a climate and logistics start-up building the first sustainable parcel delivery network at scale, powered by a 100% electric fleet. In an ever-growing industry where unreliable service has long been the norm, HIVED has been built from the ground-up to meet the demands of modern consumers and disrupt parcel delivery. A tech company at heart, our talent and technology is laser-focused on delivering the best possible delivery experience for end-customers and meeting the needs of our retail partners. From our drivers to our data engineers, we are constantly working to improve this customer experience that makes us deliver better. Already trusted by leading international brands such as John Lewis, Nespresso, Uniqlo, H&M brands and more, we are solidifying our position as the leaders in Europe to tackle this growing market. Our tight-knit team is made up of ex-Revolut, Bain, HelloFresh, ASOS, Apple and Google employees, and we are backed by some of Europe’s leading investors and VCs in climate-tech, logistics and mobility including Planet A Ventures, Maersk Growth, Pale Blue Dot VC, Eka Ventures, NordicNinja VC and the British government. We’re passionate about driving innovation and redefining the future of delivery. Role Overview 📝 We're looking for a Senior or Staff ML / Operations Research Engineer to take on some of the hardest problems at HIVED: the planning systems that decide how every parcel moves through our 100% electric delivery network. You'll join the Routing Squad, which owns HIVED's entire planning pipeline: demand forecasting, capacity planning and scheduling, sortation, our collections network and large-scale route optimisation. You'll design the optimisation, forecasting and ML models at the core of it, and bring them into production together with backend engineers, data scientists, analysts and our operations teams. This is a hands-on role with a lot of technical ownership. You'll shape how we model the network and your work shows up in the routes our drivers take, the cages filled at our depots and the parcels that arrive on time. The problems are as varied as they are hard. How much volume will each depot see next week, and how many drivers and vehicles do we need to meet it? How should parcels flow through sortation and our collections network so nothing is handled twice? How do we turn each day's parcels into routes that are efficient, reliable and on time? And how do we model the cost and geography of our network as we expand into new cities? What You'll Be Doing 🚀 • Build the models. Design and implement optimisation models and algorithms for routing, supply-demand matching, capacity planning and other core logistics challenges. • Turn ambiguity into models. Take complex operational problems, break them down, and see them through from first investigation to production. • Put models into production. Build and productionise optimisation, forecasting and ML models with our backend engineers, making them robust, observable and ready for peak volumes. • Explore new approaches. Lead the exploration and evaluation of new approaches, from linear programming and heuristics to machine learning-based optimisation. • Stay close to the operation. Work directly with operations teams, see how plans perform on the ground, and close the loop with data. • Raise the bar. Lead design and code reviews, mentor engineers, and set the standards for how we test and evaluate our models. What We're Looking For 🔎 • Typically 5+ years of experience building optimisation or ML models and shipping them into production systems. • A track record of technical ownership beyond your own work: you've shaped the modelling approach or direction of systems spanning multiple components or teams. • The ability to translate complex, ambiguous operational problems into technical solutions with measurable impact. • Solid coding skills: Python preferred, though experience in other languages like C++, Java or Go is also valued. • An appetite for hard optimisation problems: routing, scheduling, forecasting, supply-demand matching, or other constraint-heavy domains. • Strong collaboration and written communication skills, with the confidence to work across engineering, data science, analytics and operations. You also bring depth in at least one of: • Optimisation / operations research: linear and integer programming, constraint programming or heuristics, and integrating solvers such as OR-Tools or Gurobi into production systems. • Machine learning: forecasting and predictive models that feed planning decisions, from development to monitoring in production.
