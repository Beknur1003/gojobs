We focus on improving automation, security, reliability and platform capabilities, while enabling teams to build and operate services efficiently at scale. We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future. In this role you will design, build and support platform services that are used by engineering teams globally. You will help improve automation, reliability and security across our cloud environment, while contributing to initiatives involving platform engineering, artificial intelligence, governance and site reliability. You will also have the opportunity to work with modern technologies and continuously develop new skills. • 5+ years’ of experience developing and maintaining production-grade applications using Golang, Python, Ruby, Java, Node.js, C++ or Shell scripting. • Strong backend engineering expertise, including designing and building scalable microservices, RESTful APIs, distributed systems and highly available platform services. • Hands-on experience with Amazon Web Services, including CloudFormation, Virtual Private Cloud, Identity and Access Management, EC2, Lambda and cloud-native infrastructure automation. • Strong engineering mindset with a passion for platform engineering, automation, infrastructure as code, observability, reliability and continuous improvement. • Excellent problem-solving, analytical thinking and communication skills, with the ability to evaluate and implement modern technologies, including AI-assisted engineering tools. • Ability to quickly learn new technologies and work across cloud infrastructure, platform services and automation frameworks in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
