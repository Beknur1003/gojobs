About the Role We are looking for an experienced Engineering Lead to drive the design, development, and delivery of scalable gaming platforms and player-facing products. This role will lead a team of software engineers across backend, frontend, platform, and DevOps functions while collaborating closely with Product, Design, QA, Data, and Operations teams. You will play a key role in shaping technical strategy, developing engineering best practices, and ensuring the reliability, scalability, and security of our gaming ecosystem. Responsibilities • Lead and mentor a team of software engineers across multiple disciplines. • Own the end-to-end software development lifecycle, from architecture design to production deployment. • Drive the development of high-availability, low-latency gaming services and platforms. • Define technical roadmaps aligned with business and product objectives. • Collaborate with Product Managers and stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions. • Champion engineering excellence through code reviews, architecture reviews, CI/CD, and DevOps practices. • Ensure platform reliability, security, performance, and operational readiness. • Establish development standards, coding guidelines, and engineering best practices. • Manage technical risks, system scalability challenges, and production incidents. • Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Requirements • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related disciplines. • 8+ years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years in a technical leadership or engineering management capacity. • Strong experience building large-scale distributed systems, cloud-native applications, or high-traffic digital platforms. • Hands-on experience with technologies such as Java, C#, Golang, Node.js, Python, or similar. • Solid understanding of microservices architecture, RESTful APIs, containerization, and cloud platforms. • Experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform. • Familiarity with DevOps, CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes, Docker, and infrastructure automation. • Strong knowledge of system architecture, scalability, performance optimization, and software security. • Experience leading Agile development teams. • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills. Preferred • Experience in gaming, iGaming, online entertainment, digital products, or high-volume consumer platforms. • Familiarity with real-time systems, multiplayer platforms, payment integrations, or player engagement systems. • Experience managing distributed or regional engineering teams.
