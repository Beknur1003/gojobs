Job Summary : We are seeking an experienced Technical Lead - Golang & DevOps to lead the design, development, and deployment of scalable backend applications and cloud-native solutions. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in Golang, Microservices, DevOps practices, Cloud Platforms, and Containerization, along with proven experience in leading engineering teams and driving technical excellence. This role requires hands-on technical leadership, solution architecture, code reviews, and collaboration with cross-functional teams to deliver high-performance, reliable applications. Key Responsibilities : - Lead and mentor a team of Golang developers and DevOps engineers. - Design and develop scalable, secure, and high-performance backend applications using Golang. - Architect and implement microservices-based solutions following industry best practices. - Drive cloud-native application development and deployment across AWS, Azure, or GCP environments. - Establish coding standards, conduct code reviews, and ensure high-quality software delivery. - Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated build, testing, deployment, and release management. - Collaborate with Product, QA, Infrastructure, and Architecture teams to deliver robust solutions. - Troubleshoot complex technical issues and provide root cause analysis for production incidents. - Optimize application performance, scalability, and system reliability. - Implement monitoring, logging, and observability solutions to ensure system health. - Ensure security best practices are incorporated throughout the software development lifecycle. - Evaluate and adopt emerging technologies to improve engineering efficiency and platform performance. Required Skills : - 6+ years of experience in backend software development with strong expertise in Golang. - Proven experience leading technical teams and managing engineering deliverables. - Strong experience designing and developing Microservices and RESTful APIs. - Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and container orchestration. - Strong knowledge of DevOps practices and CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or GitHub Actions. - Experience with cloud platforms including AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP). - Strong understanding of distributed systems, system design, scalability, and performance optimization. - Experience with messaging systems such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar technologies. - Strong knowledge of relational and NoSQL databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, or Redis. - Experience with Linux environments, scripting (Bash/Python), and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or Ansible). - Familiarity with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack, or Datadog.
