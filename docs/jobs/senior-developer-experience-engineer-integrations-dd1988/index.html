Senior Developer Experience Engineer, Integrations

Customer.io Americas Remote опубликовано 20 июля 2026

Удалёнка Senior
CI/CDKotlinReactAI/LLM
Hi, I’m Josh, Engineering Manager at Customer.io. We’re looking for a curious, AI-forward Developer Experience Engineer to join our Integrations team and help us build the tools that developers rely on to integrate with Customer.io. About Customer.io Over 9,000 companies - from scrappy startups to global brands - use our platform to send billions of emails, push notifications, in-app messages, and SMS every day. Customer.io powers automated communication that people actually want to receive. We help teams send smarter, more relevant messages using real-time behavioral data. Under the hood: Go, React, Ember and AI help us ship fast and scale with confidence. What We Value Ownership You own problems end to end. You move fast, act like an owner, and thrive in ambiguity. You've led complex projects before, whether officially or not, and you're ready to do it again. Engineers with product taste You think like a developer using your tools, not just an engineer building them. You care about API design, developer experience, and everything in between. A healthy skepticism for “the way things are done” You bring rigor and creativity. Best practices matter - but never more than forward motion. What You’ll Do • Build and evolve Customer.io's SDKs across platforms - including Android, iOS, Node, and Go - and the developer tools that support them • Lead SDK initiatives end to end: set technical direction, write the code, manage the release, and own the outcome • Improve SDK delivery infrastructure - CI, automated testing, versioning, publishing, and release processes - so shipping is reliable and low-friction • Partner closely with product and design to turn loosely defined goals into a great developer experience • Participate in an on-call rotation as required, supporting the reliability of the systems you build • Use AI agents to make multi-file changes across unfamiliar codebases and languages by scoping the work, writing the prompt, and verifying the output • Bring strong customer empathy to the role - understanding what the engineers integrating with our SDKs actually need, and occasionally engaging directly with them What we're looking for • Expert-level competency in at least one established programming language, with strong software design and API design fundamentals • Experience building and evolving mature, production-quality software, with a clear understanding of how systems fit together and how other developers consume them • Exceptional fluency with current LLMs and agentic coding tools (e.g., Claude, Cursor, Codex) - using AI to understand unfamiliar codebases, work across languages, and run things in parallel or autonomously • High autonomy in ambiguous environments - you clarify the problem, validate its value, and drive implementation without close direction • Strong written and verbal communication, with the ability to explain technical decisions and tradeoffs clearly • Ability to work effectively across multiple languages and stacks; prior experience in every language we support (Swift, Kotlin, Node, Go) is not required Nice to have: experience building external-facing SDKs, APIs, or developer tools; experience owning CI, testing, versioning, or release infrastructure; or forward-deployed engineering experience.
