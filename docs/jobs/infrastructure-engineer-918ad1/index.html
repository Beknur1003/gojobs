Job title: Senior Infrastructure Automation Engineer Location: Alpharetta, GA Job Summary We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Infrastructure Automation Engineer to design, automate, and support enterprise infrastructure environments. The ideal candidate will have strong Linux systems engineering experience along with hands-on expertise in infrastructure automation, configuration management, cloud/virtualization technologies, and software development practices. The successful candidate will help improve infrastructure reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency through automation and modern engineering practices. Key Responsibilities • Design, implement, maintain, and troubleshoot enterprise Linux infrastructure, primarily Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) or equivalent distributions. • Develop and maintain infrastructure automation using tools such as Ansible, Terraform, Puppet, and Chef. • Build automation solutions and utilities using Python, Go/Golang, Perl, C++, and/or Ruby. • Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, deployment, patching, and lifecycle management processes. • Support VMware virtualization environments and/or AWS/VMC cloud infrastructure. • Collaborate with infrastructure, cloud, development, and operations teams to improve platform reliability and scalability. • Support server hardware operations, including firmware updates, hardware provisioning, maintenance, and lifecycle management. • Develop reusable automation frameworks, modules, scripts, and tools to reduce manual operational tasks. • Participate in infrastructure upgrades, migrations, capacity planning, and technology modernization initiatives. • Troubleshoot complex Linux, virtualization, automation, and infrastructure issues. • Follow software engineering best practices including version control, code reviews, testing, documentation, and continuous improvement. • Work within Agile methodologies and SDLC processes. • Use development and collaboration tools such as GitHub, Jira, and related CI/CD and testing platforms. • Create and maintain technical documentation, test cases, implementation procedures, and operational runbooks. Required Qualifications • Strong hands-on experience with Linux systems engineering, preferably RHEL. • Proven experience with infrastructure automation and configuration management tools such as Ansible, Terraform, Puppet, or Chef. • Programming or scripting experience with one or more of Python, Go/Golang, Perl, C++, or Ruby. • Experience with VMware and/or AWS/VMC environments. • Understanding of server hardware, firmware, and infrastructure lifecycle management. • Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills. • Experience working with Git/GitHub or similar source-control platforms. • Familiarity with Agile development practices and SDLC processes. • Experience with code reviews, testing, documentation, and collaborative development practices. Preferred Qualifications • Experience building enterprise-scale infrastructure automation frameworks. • Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices. • Experience with cloud infrastructure automation and Infrastructure as Code (IaC). • Experience with automated testing and test-case development. • Familiarity with Jira and Agile project management methodologies. • Experience supporting large-scale enterprise data center or hybrid-cloud environments.
