Optus is an Australian telecommunications company, delivering more than 11 million services to our customers every day across mobile, broadband and digital solutions. As a Senior Software Engineer - GoLang, you will play a critical role in designing, building, and supporting high-performance integration services and enterprise applications that underpin key OSS platforms across Optus. This role is ideal for a highly skilled GoLang engineer who has hands-on experience delivering production-grade applications end-to-end. You will be a technical leader within the team, driving engineering excellence, mentoring peers, and influencing architectural decisions while remaining deeply involved in coding, solution design, and delivery. What You’ll Do • Design, develop, deploy, and support enterprise-grade GoLang applications and integration services across the full software development lifecycle. • Build scalable APIs, microservices, and event-driven solutions using GoLang, ensuring high performance, reliability, maintainability, and security. • Lead technical design discussions, code reviews, and engineering decisions while mentoring engineers and championing modern software development best practices. • Collaborate with architects, product owners, and cross-functional engineering teams to deliver robust solutions that solve complex business and operational challenges. What You Bring • 7-8 years of strong commercial experience developing and deploying GoLang applications, with the ability to demonstrate end-to-end ownership of solutions delivered into production environments. • Deep expertise in GoLang development, including APIs, microservices, concurrent processing, design patterns, performance optimisation, testing, and code quality practices. • Experience working with REST APIs, Kafka, event-driven architectures, CI/CD pipelines, GitLab, and relational databases such as PostgreSQL or Oracle. • Excellent problem-solving and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to mentor others, influence technical direction, and thrive in a complex enterprise technology environment. The good stuff…. • Competitive remuneration and employee discounts. Make life easier (and more affordable) with $80 monthly credit and 25% off Optus products and unique shopping discounts with our retail partners. • Flexible working arrangements with opportunities to work three days in the office, two days remote or home. • Vibrant and collaborative office campus that includes cafes, convenience store, chill out zones, GP, post office, gym and on-site childcare centre. • Competitive leave policies, including additional 'Connected’ days to focus on culture, family, health, community, or whatever’s important to you. • We support growing families with inclusive, carer-neutral paid Parental Leave of up to 16 weeks. • Build meaningful connections through employee-led networks and diversity initiatives including Culture Connect, Elevate Women, Disability Network and Express Yourself (LGBTQIA+). • Put your wellbeing first with free access to counselling and support services, 24/7 — in-person, by phone, SMS, or video. • Free Optus bus from Macquarie University Metro Station (every 6-8 minutes); morning and afternoon.
