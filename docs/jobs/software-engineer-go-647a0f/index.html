What You Bring • Go Expertise: Proven Senior or Staff-level Golang experience in an ownership or lead capacity. • Self-Driven Mindset: A true self-starter who digs into complex, multi-week engineering challenges and solves them robustly without needing hand-holding. • Remote Setup: Ability to thrive in a fully remote environment, with excellent English communication skills and availability to work within the CET time zone. • Travel: Willingness to travel occasionally for team retreats and working sessions (at least twice a year). Bonus Points • Experience with GraphQL, API Gateways, Proxies, gRPC, or networking protocols. • Active open-source contributions. Ready for Your Next Challenge? If you are looking for a challenge and not afraid to dive into complex technical problems and want to leave a lasting impact on developer tooling - let's have a conversation 😊
