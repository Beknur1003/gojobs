Backend Developer (Net SQL)

Kamoro Maxima Integra South Jakarta, Jakarta опубликовано 22 сентября 2026

Офис

We are looking for a Backend Engineer who is passionate about building scalable, reliable, and high-performance backend systems. You will be responsible for developing and maintaining applications used by thousands to millions of users, while working closely with Product, Design, and other stakeholders. Responsibilities: • Build, enhance, and maintain reliable backend systems that support thousands to millions of users. • Design and implement features based on a multi-tenant architecture. • Improve application performance by optimizing backend services and database processes. • Conduct testing, troubleshooting, and debugging to ensure system stability and quality. • Work closely with Product Managers, UI/UX Designers, Developers, and other stakeholders throughout the development process. • Provide technical recommendations and business-oriented suggestions to continuously improve product quality. • Develop, integrate, and maintain APIs and backend services. Requirements: • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline. • Minimum 4+ years of professional experience in Backend Engineering. • Proficient in backend technologies such as C#/.NET, SQL, Node.js, and Golang. • Solid knowledge of server-side development, database management, and API design. • Understanding and practical knowledge of Microservices Architecture and Docker. • Experience working with at least one major cloud platform such as Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or AWS. • Familiar with Unit Testing and Integration Testing practices. • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities. • Demonstrates a strong willingness to learn, adapt, and explore emerging technologies.
