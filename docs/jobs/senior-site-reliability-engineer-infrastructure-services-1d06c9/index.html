Senior Site Reliability Engineer, Infrastructure Services

Do you want to help build some of the largest and most consequential enterprise and customer technology systems in the world? Join Apple’s Information Systems and Technology (IS&T) organization. IS&T is the engine behind everything Apple does for customers and for the people who build for them. It’s Apple’s central nervous system. Supporting 2.5 billion active Apple devices, processing billions of secure transactions, and keeping the technology that defines modern life running flawlessly, IS&T makes the impossible feel effortless.” Do you love building solutions to handle global complexity and immense scale? Imagine what you could do here. Infrastructure Services is part of IS&T and the foundation of Apple's global network operations — managing data center equipment and systems to deliver compute, storage, and networking services for teams across Apple, including its internal developer community. From individual facilities to a worldwide network, Infrastructure Services ensures the technology underneath everything works without question. We are seeking a Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) with deep expertise in cloud infrastructure, production Kubernetes, and platform resilience, combined with a forward-looking mindset in AI-driven automation. In this role, you will partner closely with engineering leadership and cross-functional teams to scale and modernize our cloud platform, elevate operational excellence, and embed intelligent automation across reliability and operations workflows. This is a hands-on technical leadership role for a senior engineer with extensive SRE or DevOps experience who can architect resilient distributed systems, build production-grade tooling, and influence long-term platform strategy. Minimum Qualifications • 8+ years of progressive experience in Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, or Platform Engineering roles supporting mission-critical distributed systems. • Deep production Kubernetes expertise, with hands-on experience architecting, securing, and maintaining large-scale clusters. • Extensive AWS experience, with in-depth knowledge of core AWS networking, compute, security, and storage primitives (Azure or GCP experience also valued). • Strong software engineering capability with proficiency in Golang or Java for developing automation services, operators, and backend tooling. • Demonstrated experience defining SLOs and SLIs, as well as instrumenting full-stack observability platforms such as Prometheus, Grafana, or OpenTelemetry. • Solid grounding in distributed systems, microservices architectures, networking protocols, high availability, and disaster recovery. • Proven track record in leading production incident response, conducting root-cause investigations, and driving preventative fixes. • Hands-on experience designing and operating automated CI/CD deployment pipelines using modern tooling such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or ArgoCD. Preferred Qualifications • 10+ years of progressive experience in Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, or Platform Engineering roles supporting mission-critical distributed systems. • Hands-on experience applying AI/ML or LLM-based tooling to SRE workflows, anomaly detection, or runbook automation. • Familiarity with orchestration and agentic frameworks such as LangChain or LangGraph. • Practical experience authoring and maintaining custom Kubernetes Operators and Controllers using Kubebuilder or Operator SDK. • Background in building Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) and self-service cloud infrastructure.
