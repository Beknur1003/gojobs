Location: • Remote - US / Canada About the role: Browsers are steadily closing the door on how device identity has worked on the web. Sardine's Device Intelligence & Behavior Biometrics team builds the signals that tell genuine users apart from fraudsters, bots, and AI agents, and we need an engineer to own how our Web SDK identifies devices reliably across Safari, Firefox, Chrome, Brave, and in-app WebViews, and how it reaches the browser as a true first party. You will own the Web SDK, its client-side identity storage, and the CDN reverse proxy integrations that sit in our customers' traffic. This is a production engineering role with a strong investigative side: much of the work starts with understanding exactly how a browser treats a cookie, a request, or a domain, and ends with shipping a change that keeps working for thousands of end users. What You Will Do • Lead our move to privacy-resilient first-party integrations: opt-in CHIPS partitioned cookies, true first-party cookie flows, and in-band CDN reverse proxy integrations. • Build and extend reverse proxy integrations across CDNs and origin setups (Cloudflare, AWS Lambda@Edge / CloudFront, Fastly, Akamai, F5, NGINX), including per-customer randomized paths for SDK loading and event ingestion. • Modernize the SDK's client-side identity storage layer, and design graceful degradation when persistence is limited. • Design device identity handoff between our native mobile SDKs and in-app WebViews, resistant to spoofing and abuse. • Instrument and measure device identifier longevity and churn by browser, version, and integration mode, and alert on regressions. • Track browser privacy changes (ITP, ETP / dFPI, CHIPS, Storage Access API, tracker lists) ahead of release, and turn them into SDK and integration decisions. • Own the performance, bundle size, and reliability of an SDK that runs on login, signup, and payment flows of fintechs and enterprises. • Work with backend (Go), mobile, dashboard, and customer success teams to roll out changes across existing customers without breaking installs. What We're Looking For • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with at least 3 years building production JavaScript / TypeScript that runs in the browser. • Strong TypeScript / JavaScript and deep knowledge of browser APIs, cross-browser differences, and WebView behavior. • Deep, practical knowledge of cookie and storage behavior across browsers: SameSite, partitioning, CHIPS, ITP / ETP, CNAME cloaking detection, and eviction rules. • Experience shipping and maintaining a third-party JavaScript SDK or library that runs on other companies' sites. • Experience building or operating reverse proxies or edge functions in production. • Solid web networking fundamentals: HTTP, CORS, cookies, DNS, TLS, caching, CDNs. • Good judgment on effort, risk, and migration paths in systems with many external integrators. • Clear written and verbal communication of trade-offs to engineering, product, and customer-facing teams. • Experience with at least one popular web framework or library, such as React, Angular, Vue.js, Next.js, or Svelte. Experience with multiple frameworks is a plus. • Experience with modern JavaScript development tools and build systems, including Node.js, npm, Babel, and bundlers such as Webpack, Vite, or Rollup. Familiarity with one or more of these tools is preferred. Nice to Have • Background in fraud prevention, bot detection, device fingerprinting, or web security. • Go experience. • Experience with JavaScript obfuscation, anti-tampering, or reverse engineering minified code. • Familiarity with automation frameworks (Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium) and how they differ from real browsers. • Experience with iOS (Swift) or Android (Kotlin / Java) SDK development, including WebViews and native to web bridges. • Public work such as browser bug reports, open source, blog posts, or talks. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and release automation, using tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins, to automate the building, testing, versioning, and deployment of SDKs or web applications Tech Stack • Web SDK: TypeScript • Edge: Cloudflare, AWS (Lambda@Edge / CloudFront), Fastly, Akamai, NGINX, F5 • Backend: Go • Infrastructure: GCP / AWS Benefits we offer: • Generous compensation in cash and equity • Early exercise for all options, including pre-vested • Work from anywhere: Remote-first Culture • Flexible paid time off and Year-end break • Health insurance, dental, and vision coverage for employees and dependents - US and Canada specific • 4% matching in 401k / RRSP - US and Canada specific • MacBook Pro delivered to your door • One-time stipend to set up a home office — desk, chair, screen, etc. • Monthly meal stipend • Monthly social meet-up stipend • Annual health and wellness stipend • Annual Learning stipend Join a fast-growing company with world-class professionals from around the world.
