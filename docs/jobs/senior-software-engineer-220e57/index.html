About our team:    We are the leader in Kubernetes Backup and Disaster Recovery. Veeam Kasten, our data management platform purpose-built for Kubernetes, provides enterprise operations teams with an easy-to-use, scalable, and secure system for backup and restore, disaster recovery, and application mobility with unparalleled operational simplicity. We are looking for a Senior Software Developer  to contribute to our Veeam Kasten data management platform, ensuring top-tier security and excellence. Your tasks will include:     • Owning (designing, developing in Go, and testing) significant features in the Veeam Kasten product related to backup and recovery of Kubernetes applications, databases, and storage systems. • Integrating Veeam Kasten with other Veeam products and expanding it’s capabilities • Collaborating with distributed teams across different time zones and regions. • Working with and contributing to the cloud-native open-source community. • Participating in technical design discussions, reviewing code, and providing feedback to other engineers. What we expect from you: • 8+ years of experience developing enterprise solutions in a high-level programming language such as C++, Java, or C#, and a willingness to code in Go. • Experience with distributed systems concepts and client-server architectures. • Expertise in developing, testing, and debugging production-quality, scalable, multi-threaded, and concurrent systems. • Strong knowledge of computer science fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, and concurrency). • Strong sense of ownership, delivering work end-to-end, aligning with stakeholders, maintaining high level of quality and following up with the delivered solutions in production. • A university degree in a technical field or equivalent industry experience. Will be an advantage:  • Experience developing in Go. • Experience developing containers, container schedulers, and orchestration platforms. • Experience developing complex systems with AI coding agent tools at scale. • Active contribution to OSS projects in the Kubernetes/Cloud-Native ecosystem. • Participation in CNCF Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) or Kubernetes Special Interest Groups (SIGs).
