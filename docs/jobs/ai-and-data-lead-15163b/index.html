AI and Data Lead DT One | Dubai | Full-time About Us DT One operates a leading global network for mobile top-up, digital value products and rewards. Through a single API connection, we link more than 2,800 partners, including mobile operators, money transfer operators, e-wallets, super apps and retail networks, across 190 countries. Our partners can offer over 23,000 products, from airtime and data top-ups to gift cards and bill payments. We have processed more than 1.2 billion transactions and reach a potential audience of over five billion people, helping to ensure that no one is left unconnected. Founded in 2005 and part of the Thunes group, DT One is headquartered in Singapore with regional offices in Dubai, Miami, Nairobi, London and Barcelona. For more information visit www.dtone.com Context of the role We're looking for a Data and AI Lead who is passionate about turning data into products and decisions, and wants to work in a disruptive, fast-growing and exciting environment. Every transaction on our network tells us something about what customers want, which products perform in which markets and how our partners can grow. This role will bring that intelligence together and put it to work across the business. You will combine a startup mindset with the scale of an industry leader. Working closely with the leadership team, product, engineering and commercial teams, you will set the direction for how DT One uses data, analytics and AI, build the team to deliver it, and make sure it has a measurable impact on growth and profitability. Key Responsibilities We're looking for a highly driven, commercially minded leader who is truly excited about having a meaningful impact within the company. In this role, you will own data and AI end to end, from strategy and platforms through to the products, insights and automation that our teams and partners use every day. • Define and drive the data and AI strategy and roadmap, making sure it is aligned with commercial, product and technology priorities • Build, lead and grow a multi-disciplinary team across data engineering, data science and machine learning, analytics and data governance • Own the data platform, bringing together transaction, product and partner data into a trusted, scalable foundation for real-time and batch analytics • Deliver data and AI products that drive growth, such as product recommendations, pricing and demand insights, partner performance analytics, and fraud and anomaly detection • Lead the adoption of AI, including generative AI, to automate operations and improve decision-making across the business • Establish data governance, quality, privacy and compliance standards that work across multiple markets and regulatory environments • Partner with the leadership team and business units to prioritise use cases, measure value delivered and manage the data and AI budget • Act as a senior voice for data and AI with partners and the wider Thunes group What we are looking for • 15+ years' experience in data, analytics and machine learning, including several years leading data and AI functions • Advanced degree (MSc or PhD) in Machine Learning, Computer Science, Statistics or a related field, or equivalent experience • A proven record of building and scaling multi-disciplinary data teams, including across multiple countries • Experience delivering data platforms and data products at scale in digital, e-commerce, fintech or other high-volume consumer businesses • Hands-on depth in applied machine learning in production, such as personalisation, recommendation, forecasting or customer analytics • Experience setting up data governance and working with data privacy requirements across multiple jurisdictions • The ability to translate business goals into a clear data roadmap, and to communicate with and influence senior stakeholders • Experience with fintech, payments or mobile data, or with deploying generative AI in production, is a plus • Interest in the Fintech industry and market innovations Our core services tech stack is hosted on Amazon Web Services. Our main tech stack consists of Golang, Python, Perl (Mojolicious), PostgreSQL, Vue.js, Docker and Nginx. We can offer you an environment with a culture of learning and feedback. You will have real ownership of data and AI across a network that operates 24/7 around the world, with the scope to build a team and shape how the business makes decisions. You will work with teams in different countries, and our environment and packages match the ambition of the company: building world-class teams. Sound like you? Apply now!
