Position Description: A Staff Backend Developer is a highly experienced software engineer responsible for designing, developing and evolving complex backend systems and services using PHP and/or Go (Golang), MySQL and related technologies. The role requires deep technical expertise, strong engineering judgment and a high level of ownership. A Staff Backend Developer is expected to independently drive complex technical work, contribute to architecture and technical decisions, and act as one of the key technical references within the development team. The role goes beyond implementation of individual tasks. A Staff Backend Developer is expected to understand the broader product and system context, proactively identify technical risks and improvement opportunities, and take responsibility for features and technical initiatives from initial design through implementation, testing, release, production validation and continuous improvement. A Staff Backend Developer is also expected to make extensive and effective use of AI-assisted engineering tools as part of their daily development workflow. They must have sufficient technical depth and engineering judgment to critically evaluate, validate and improve AI-generated solutions rather than relying on them without proper technical review.
