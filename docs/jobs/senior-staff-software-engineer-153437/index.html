Our work sits at the critical intersection of Cisco and Splunk integration, providing the critical infrastructure that powers real-time observability and security for our global enterprise customers. We are a high-performing, collaborative organization that prioritizes clean code, meticulous testing, and pragmatic architectural decision-making. Operating in a hybrid/remote environment, we champion technical excellence and continuous learning as our standards. This role offers an outstanding opportunity to address sophisticated distributed systems challenges at a scale unmatched in the industry. Your Impact In this role, you will lead the end-to-end delivery of sophisticated projects spanning multiple months, navigating the full software development cycle from requirements gathering and system design to deployment. You will set the technical vision for the team by establishing coding guidelines and design standards to ensure high-quality, maintainable, and serviceable products. A core part of your impact involves identifying and resolving system bottlenecks, engaging in deep-dive design and code reviews, and proactively improving our understanding of customer experiences. Additionally, you will participate in rotating on-call duties to diagnose and solve sophisticated customer issues, ensuring the reliability of our infrastructure. Success is defined by your ability to drive technical consensus across multi-functional teams while maintaining the performance and scalability of our large-scale distributed systems. Minimum Qualifications • 12+ years of Software Development experience, with a significant portion served in a lead or architect capacity. • Experience designing, shipping, and supporting high-performance, high-throughput, and low-latency data processing components. • Experience shipping and supporting both cloud-native services and on-premises software solutions using programming languages such as GoLang, C, or C++. • Experience designing, architecting, and implementing large-scale distributed systems with a focus on reliability, scalability, performance, and understanding of core concepts like consistency models and fault tolerance. • Preferred Qualifications • Delivering high-quality, maintainable code, and the ability to quickly adapt to new technologies. • Strong debugging and troubleshooting skills, with expertise in using Linux-based tools and diagnostic techniques. • A strong advocate for operational perfection, driving standard processes for monitoring, alerting, and incident response across the team and advocating for robust production readiness. • Experience leading the design and delivery of projects with a scope of 6 months or more, involving collaboration with horizontal (multi-functional) teams to influence technical direction and drive consensus.
