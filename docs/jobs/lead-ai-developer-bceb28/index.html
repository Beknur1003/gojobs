About the Role We are looking for a skilled AI Developer to design, develop, integrate, and deploy intelligent, production-ready AI applications. The ideal candidate must have strong hands-on experience in Python, FastAPI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, vector databases, and Machine Learning models and evaluating their performance. The candidate will build AI-powered applications, conversational assistants, intelligent automation workflows, and Python-based AI backend services. The role also involves integrating AI capabilities with existing application backends through REST APIs and developing reliable, scalable AI solutions for real-world business requirements. We are looking for someone with practical implementation experience who can turn AI concepts into working applications, evaluate their performance, and deploy them into production. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain production-ready AI applications using Python. • Build and optimize RAG pipelines for knowledge retrieval and context-aware responses. • Integrate LLMs from providers such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, or equivalent platforms. • Develop AI agents with tool calling, function execution, and multi-step workflow orchestration. • Build AI chatbots, conversational assistants, and natural-language interfaces. • Design and implement AI-powered workflow automation and intelligent business processes. • Develop AI backend services and REST API endpoints using FastAPI. • Integrate AI services with existing backend applications, REST APIs, databases, and third-party services. • Build AI agents that retrieve application data and execute authorized operations through existing APIs. • Develop document ingestion, preprocessing, chunking, embedding, indexing, retrieval, and reranking pipelines. • Implement vector databases and semantic search for efficient knowledge retrieval. • Apply Machine Learning techniques to suitable prediction, classification, recommendation, and other data-driven use cases. • Evaluate AI and ML models using appropriate performance metrics and test datasets. • Work with pretrained models and evaluate opportunities for fine-tuning when required. • Implement prompt engineering, structured outputs, context management, and response validation. • Develop multilingual AI capabilities and conversational workflows. • Implement AI evaluation, testing, guardrails, logging, monitoring, and hallucination-reduction techniques. • Optimize AI application quality, response latency, reliability, scalability, and model usage costs. • Deploy, troubleshoot, and maintain AI applications in production environments. • Follow secure development practices for API integrations, authentication, authorization, and data protection. • Collaborate with frontend, backend, mobile, QA, and DevOps engineers to integrate AI capabilities into applications. Mandatory Technical Skills 1. Python and Backend Development • Strong hands-on experience in Python programming. • Experience building production-grade backend services using FastAPI. • Experience developing and consuming REST APIs. • Understanding of asynchronous programming, request validation, response schemas, exception handling, and testing. • Ability to integrate AI services with existing backend systems and third-party platforms. 2. LLMs and Generative AI • Practical experience developing LLM-powered applications. • Experience integrating LLM providers such as OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or equivalent platforms. • Understanding of prompt engineering, context windows, structured outputs, and function/tool calling. • Ability to build reliable AI applications beyond basic chatbot demonstrations. 3. RAG Pipelines and Knowledge Retrieval • Hands-on experience designing and implementing RAG architectures. • Understanding of embeddings, document chunking, semantic search, metadata filtering, and retrieval optimization. • Experience with vector search and context retrieval. • Ability to evaluate and improve retrieval relevance and answer accuracy. 4. AI Agents and Workflow Automation • Experience building AI agents and agentic workflows. • Understanding of tool calling, API execution, state management, and workflow orchestration. • Experience with frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, or equivalent tools. • Ability to automate multi-step tasks using AI models, APIs, and external tools. 5. Machine Learning Fundamentals • Understanding of supervised and unsupervised learning. • Knowledge of common ML algorithms and their appropriate use cases. • Understanding of data preprocessing, feature engineering, training and validation datasets, and model evaluation. • Familiarity with metrics such as precision, recall, F1-score, and accuracy, where appropriate. • Ability to identify when a problem requires traditional Machine Learning versus an LLM-based solution. 6. Vector Databases and Data Integration • Experience with at least one vector database, such as Qdrant, Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus, or pgvector. • Understanding of similarity search, vector indexing, and metadata filtering. • Experience integrating AI applications with relational databases, NoSQL databases, and external services. 7. Production Deployment and Engineering • Experience deploying and maintaining AI applications in production. • Knowledge of Docker, Git, testing, CI/CD, logging, and monitoring. • Understanding of application security, scalability, reliability, and performance optimization. • Ability to troubleshoot production issues and maintain reliable AI services. Preferred Qualifications • Hands-on experience fine-tuning pretrained LLMs or other ML models for specific use cases. • Familiarity with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn, as appropriate to the solution. • Experience with model evaluation, experiment tracking, and dataset preparation. • Knowledge of deep learning fundamentals and neural network architectures. • Experience deploying AI applications on Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, or another suitable cloud platform. • Familiarity with managed AI services, including Azure OpenAI, where applicable. • Experience with speech recognition, text-to-speech, or voice-based AI applications. • Knowledge of event-driven architecture and distributed systems. • Familiarity with Go (Golang) is an added advantage. What Were Looking For • Strong practical implementation and problem-solving skills. • Ability to independently develop, test, integrate, and deploy AI solutions. • A solid understanding of Machine Learning and hands-on experience with modern Generative AI technologies. • Ability to connect AI capabilities to real application data, backend APIs, and business workflows. • Strong debugging skills and a focus on clean, maintainable, secure code. • Ability to work collaboratively with software engineering teams. • A willingness to learn, experiment, evaluate results, and improve AI systems continuously.
