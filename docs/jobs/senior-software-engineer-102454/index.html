TwinRed Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain опубликовано 17 ноября 2026

TwinRed is a leading service provider in the digital advertising sector, offering self-service and openRTB capabilities to its clients as well as operating a well-established affiliate network. TwinRed has offices in Los Angeles, Luxembourg and Barcelona and is looking for talented and highly motivated colleagues to join their team in Barcelona. Be part of an energetic and dynamic work atmosphere, with offices in the centre of Barcelona, Diagonal. WITH US, YOU WILL BE • Designing, building and maintaining high-performance, scalable and reliable systems handling large volumes of traffic. • Working hands-on with Go as a core part of our backend stack. • Occasionally contributing to a core service written in Scala, diving into legacy PHP code. • Working across different parts of the platform rather than being limited to one narrowly defined area. • Solving challenges involving application performance, networking, operating systems (Linux) and infrastructure. • Continuously profiling, optimizing and improving existing systems for performance, efficiency and reliability. • Working with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including BigQuery, Kubernetes clusters and other managed services, while occasionally supporting and maintaining on-premise infrastructure when needed. • Working with browser APIs and client-side JavaScript when the problem requires it. • Investigating complex production issues and developing practical, permanent solutions. • Contributing to technical decisions and the future direction of our platform. • Working directly with other engineers and business teams to turn real business requirements into technical solutions. • Sharing knowledge, reviewing code and helping other engineers grow. WHAT WE EXPECT FROM YOU • 5+ years of professional software development experience. • Strong professional experience with Go (Golang). • Excellent level of English. • Strong understanding of computer science and systems fundamentals, including memory allocation, cache locality, pointers and references. • Experience designing and developing scalable, reliable and optimized applications capable of handling high loads. • Basic knowledge of browser APIs and frontend development — you should be comfortable writing client-side code when needed. • Ability to quickly learn, adapt and dive into unfamiliar problems, including areas outside your previous experience. We value engineers who are willing to work across the stack rather than stay within a narrow specialization. • Ability to understand business needs and translate them into effective technical solutions. WOULD BE NICE IF YOU HAVE • Experience with Scala and/or JavaScript. • Previous experience or knowledge of AdTech and programmatic advertising, including ad-serving systems, DSPs, SSPs, OpenRTB and real-time bidding. WHAT WE VALUE We are not looking for someone who simply checks every technology box. We value engineers who are curious, technically strong and comfortable taking ownership. Someone who wants to understand why a system behaves the way it does, isn't afraid to investigate unfamiliar territory and cares about building solutions that perform well in production. IN RETURN, YOU WILL GET... • A hands-on role with real ownership and influence over the product • A small in-house engineering team where your work has a visible impact • Complex technical challenges involving high traffic, performance and scalability • Private health insurance • Company MacBook • Hybrid working model • Flexible working hours • Modern office in central Barcelona • Free coffee and snacks Send your application to career@twinred.com
