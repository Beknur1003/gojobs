Salesforce Commerce Cloud is seeking a Manager to lead the Delivery Operations team - representing Engineering Services in Hyderabad. Working closely with other teams within Engineering Services, the Delivery Operations’ main mission is to ensure that the global ECOM release deployments (60+ events a year) are performed as scheduled and with little to no disruption to our customers. As a manager of the team, you will be responsible for overseeing the success of these deployments, along with the development and support of the team’s release tooling and services. Throughout, attention is to be placed on helping drive visibility and availability initiatives, such as: safe change compliance, proactive monitoring/alerting, self-healing and resiliency. Responsibilities Support Release Management to ensure all ECOM releases are delivered to our first party and public cloud environments, as communicated to our customers Work closely with members of Engineering Services across the globe, to ensure the toolsets and deployment workflows are secure, safe, and reliable Engage SRE and various Engineering teams when deployment issues arise, to resolve technical issues in a timely fashion Drive the execution and delivery of features by collaborating with cross-functional teams, architects, product owners and engineers Be a multiplier and have a passion for the team and each team members’ success. Provide technical guidance, career development, and mentoring to team members, including senior engineers Ensure that the work the team is responsible for is carried out and executed in such a way as to comply with the company’s internal compliance policies and directives Help plan and execute via agile release cycles, driving excellence across the full software development lifecycle Incident management -
