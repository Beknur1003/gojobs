At F5, we strive to bring a better digital world to life. Our teams empower organizations across the globe to create, secure, and run applications that enhance how we experience our evolving digital world. We are passionate about cybersecurity, from protecting consumers from fraud to enabling companies to focus on innovation. Everything we do centers around people. That means we obsess over how to make the lives of our customers, and their customers, better. And it means we prioritize a diverse F5 community where each individual can thrive. At F5, we make apps faster, smarter, and safer. Join us within the Security Development Group in an exciting, fast-paced environment, charting the course for F5’s future. Our team is dedicated to providing solutions that help secure our customers’ applications and APIs. You’ll contribute to building modern services that are performant, reliable, secure, and scalable within a company known for its excellent culture. F5 products enable enterprises undergoing digital transformation to modernize legacy, monolithic applications and deliver new, microservices-based applications in a safe and secure manner. Position Summary This is a technical internship position designed to provide qualified students the opportunity to gain valuable, hands-on work experience in F5’s API security products. You will work with F5’s software engineers to undertake challenging assignments, learning new technologies, refining skills, and making meaningful contributions to the team. As part of the XC API Security team, you will design and implement features for our API security products. Primary Responsibilities: • Assist in the design, implementation, and maintenance of microservices-based system. • Collaborate with the XC API Security team to build scalable, secure, and maintainable software solutions, tools and features. • Participate in code reviews, debugging, and testing to maintain high-quality standards. • Engage in discussions on improving system performance and security. Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: • Basic knowledge of programming languages (experience with Golang is a plus). • Basic understanding of HTTP. • Basic understanding of APIs and how they function. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an eagerness to learn. • Ability to work effectively as part of a team and communicate technical concepts clearly. • Passionate about software development, with a proactive attitude towards learning and taking on new challenges. • Familiarity with Cloud Infrastructure & Architecture is an advantage Qualifications • 3rd- or 4th-year student working toward a degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. The duration of the internship is 1,040 hours. The candidate must be available to start immediately and should be able to work a minimum of 20 hours per week, up to 100 hours per month.
