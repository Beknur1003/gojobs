Senior Software Engineer, Frontend Department Engineering Who You Are You’ve been building web applications for a while now and are comfortable ramping up on an existing product quickly. You’ve mastered at least one frontend framework, and know how to build highly reliable, modular systems that many people can collaborate on at the same time without stepping on each other’s toes. You have a strong sense for good design, and won’t stop until the user experience is just right. As a Software Engineer, you will be a part of our Frontend Engineering team, focusing on delivering products and capabilities that power Justworks products. You will leverage your experience working with customer-facing applications and design with performance and security in mind. You will be working primarily in JavaScript/TypeScript and prior experience is preferred.
