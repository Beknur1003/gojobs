FullStack Engineer (Satellite Opearation Planning)

The Data Production Department is responsible for satellite operations, image data production, and distribution of data products from our proprietary small SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar) satellites, the "StriX" series. Within this department, the Satellite Operation Planning Unit is in charge of creating operation plans for our satellites, generating and registering command sequences to the satellite control system based on these plans, as well as managing data necessary for smooth operations. Responsibility In this position, you will join the Satellite Operation Planning Unit within the Data Production　Department, which is responsible for developing and operating the Spacecraft Control Subsystem for our small SAR satellite constellation, the "StriX" series. The Spacecraft Control Subsystem is a core component of our satellite operations, and our top priority is to ensure that the system operates reliably at all times. Details of work As the Spacecraft Control Subsystem is an in-house system, we are not only responsible for its development but also its ongoing operation and enhancement. The Spacecraft Control Subsystem is responsible for creating operational plans for our satellites, generating command sequences, and calculating essential mission-critical data, ranging from orbit propagation and satellite telemetry to mission planning parameters, to support the daily operational workflow. In this position, you will join the Satellite Operation Planning Unit, responsible for the development and operation of the Spacecraft Control Subsystem. Your primary focus will be on developing and enhancing the user-facing web interface and visualization tools used by our operators to manage satellite missions. You will work closely with our mission planning, backend development, and satellite operation teams to translate complex flight dynamics and operation planning data into intuitive dashboards and interactive tools, ensuring that our teams can understand satellite operation plans, and manage daily operations. Selling points of this role We are already operating several satellites in orbit, and with our fleet expected to grow rapidly, the Spacecraft Control Subsystem is more critical than ever. In this role, you will do more than just maintain the system; you will be instrumental in evolving its architecture and user experience. As we strive to achieve greater operational efficiency, you will have the unique challenge of reducing manual intervention through better design and automation. By developing the critical interfaces that our operators use daily, you are directly shaping the "human-in-the-loop" experience. You will be working at the core of our business, interfacing between the data platform and our mission-critical processing subsystems. You will have a direct impact on our operational capability—every dashboard, visualization tool, and interface improvement you deliver will directly contribute to the safety, speed, and usability of our satellite mission planning process.
