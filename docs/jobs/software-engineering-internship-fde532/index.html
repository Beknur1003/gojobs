About us GRXST Software Services Pvt Ltd, part of the esteemed GXS Bank Pte Ltd group of companies is technology center of excellence the digibank's operations across Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia. Driven by an ambitious vision, we aim to achieve banking and financial inclusion for everyone in our region. We are dedicated to constructing a cutting-edge digital bank rooted in data, technology, and trust to meet challenges head-on and serve our customers with excellence. About the Team We are the digibanks’ engineering team, and our mission is straightforward: leverage secure systems to build and operate them at production scale securely. Our engineering teams move quickly and are constantly innovating. Our engineers ensure they provide the right tools and processes to maintain high velocity. As a builder function, we create tools and processes that reflect the bank's highest standards and policies. Our engineering team takes ownership of multiple domains, including backend (GoLang), frontend (Mobile - Android and iOS), Cloud, Security/DevSecOps, SRE, privileged access management, runtime, containers, Kubernetes, cryptography, SaaS, and automated response. Qualifications: We are looking for enthusiastic, capable, and motivated Computer Science and/or Engineering majors who are eager to make an immediate impact on our business. The ideal candidates will have a strong interest in financial and banking services, along with exceptional coding skills. Our teams thrive on collaboration, so we seek individuals with excellent teamwork and proven leadership abilities. If you are passionate about shaping the future of banking by contributing across different stages of the software development lifecycle, you will fit right in. As part of our engineering team, you'll have the opportunity to learn and excel in your career while working with the latest technologies in respective engineering areas Key Skills: You should possess excellent coding skills, the ability to manage relationships with stakeholders, and exceptional problem-solving and analytical thinking abilities. We seek individuals who are intellectually curious, collaborative, and open to new challenges. Additionally, you should be capable of taking ownership of projects and seeing them through to completion. Responsibilities: Your responsibilities will vary and will be based on the team assigned. Based in our Bangalore office, you'll build innovative solutions that make a difference for our digibank in different parts of the region (e.g. Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia). You’ll create and improve the design, analytics, coding and testing for high-quality software and new products. You’ll own projects end-to-end, keeping teams and stakeholders up to speed on the progress of what’s being developed. This is only a full time 6 months internship opportunity
