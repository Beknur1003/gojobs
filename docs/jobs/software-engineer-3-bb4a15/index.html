If you want to be part of a dynamic team delivering cutting-edge cyber training solutions, this is the place for you. Amentum is seeking a Software Engineer to join the Mangrove Development team in Hanover, MD. The Mangrove development team develops, tests, and supports mission-focused cyber training solutions used across multiple locations. Mangrove is an enterprise-level project that interacts with a vCenter instance to allow course creators to develop interactive courses that require students to demonstrate their learning by grading exercises directly from student activities on Linux and Windows VMs. Team members work in a collaborative environment supporting the entire software development process, including requirements analysis, design, development, testing, and release. Engineers work closely with customers to identify new features, bug priorities, and release schedules. This position is ideal for software engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges, learning new technologies, and applying their skills across a diverse portfolio of software, infrastructure, and operational systems. Work Schedule: Contract core hours are 1000-1400. The Development team maintains flexible schedules, and currently supports 100% telework. Essential Responsibilities: The Software Engineer contributes to the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of the Mangrove LMS. Software Engineer 3 personnel apply technical expertise and institutional knowledge to guide development and implementation decisions, mentor junior engineers, and improve the team's applications and testing environment. Team members are expected to develop expertise across multiple areas of the software development lifecycle while contributing their strengths in specific technical domains.
