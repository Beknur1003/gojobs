Responsibilities The Data Protection team safeguards user data, business data, confidential company information, intellectual property, and other data requiring protection against unauthorized disclosure and exfiltration. We build and operate the platforms, data pipelines, and controls that help teams understand data risks and protect sensitive information across enterprise systems. Our focus is on effective, practical protection: understanding how data is accessed, used, and shared; applying controls appropriate to its sensitivity and risk; and enabling reliable, scalable security workflows without unnecessarily disrupting legitimate work. We are looking for a Software Engineer to build the engineering foundation of our Data Protection program. This is a software development role focused on backend services, data pipelines, platform integrations, and workflow automation. You will turn data protection requirements into production software that connects enterprise systems, processes security and data lifecycle signals, and automates operational workflows. You will work closely with Data Security Engineers, IT, Insider Security, and data platform teams to make data protection capabilities more scalable, reliable, and measurable. Responsibilities Data Protection Platform Engineering - Design, build, and maintain backend services and APIs for data protection capabilities, including data discovery and classification, protection coverage tracking, and policy lifecycle management. - Develop integrations with enterprise DLP platforms, data infrastructure, identity services, and case-management systems through APIs, event streams, and connectors. - Build reusable platform capabilities that enable security engineers to manage configurations, validate controls, and deploy changes through version-controlled, auditable workflows. - Design data models and storage systems for protection metadata, security events, workflow state, and audit records, with appropriate access and retention controls. Data Pipelines and Processing - Build and operate batch and streaming pipelines to ingest, normalize, enrich, and correlate data protection signals across enterprise systems. - Integrate data ownership, classification, access, and lineage context to support risk assessment, detection, and investigation workflows. - Implement reliable processing with schema validation, deduplication, retries, replay, and mechanisms to handle late or out-of-order events where needed. - Optimize pipeline throughput, latency, and storage costs while maintaining data quality and limiting unnecessary collection or exposure of sensitive data. Workflow Automation - Develop workflow orchestration for policy validation, event enrichment, alert routing, exception management, and remediation tracking. - Integrate automated workflows with approval processes and human review where actions may affect business operations or access to data. - Build reliable execution using idempotent operations, bounded retries, failure recovery, and auditable state transitions. Reliability and Engineering Ownership - Own features from requirements and technical design through implementation, testing, deployment, and production support. - Build observability for services, connectors, pipelines, and workflows, including metrics, logs, dashboards, and actionable alerts. - Write automated tests and use CI/CD, staged rollouts, and rollback strategies to deliver changes safely. - Investigate production issues, address performance and reliability bottlenecks, and maintain technical documentation and operational runbooks. - Collaborate with security and infrastructure partners to translate data protection needs into clear interfaces, service expectations, and maintainable software. Qualifications Minimum Qualifications - Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. - Backend Engineering: 2+ years of professional software engineering experience using Go (Golang), Java, or C++, with Go preferred. Experience building and maintaining production backend services and APIs. - Distributed Systems: Working knowledge of asynchronous processing, service-to-service communication, and distributed system failure modes, including retries, idempotency, and consistency trade-offs. - Big Data & Infrastructure: Experience working with high-volume data ecosystems or cloud-native infrastructure, such as Hive, Spark, Kafka, Flink, or Kubernetes; expertise in every listed technology is not required. - Database Engineering: Strong understanding of database design, SQL, and NoSQL systems, including indexing, partitioning, transactions, and how data is physically stored, retained, and deleted. - Software Delivery: Experience with automated testing, version control, code review, CI/CD, and production troubleshooting. - Collaboration: Ability to translate requirements into practical technical designs and communicate clearly with engineering, security, and business stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications - Data Protection & Privacy: Prior experience in data protection, DLP, privacy engineering, GDPR/CCPA-related technical controls, or data governance. - Security Engineering: Understanding of encryption at rest and in transit, identity and access management (IAM), least privilege, secrets management, and audit logging. - Workflow Orchestration: Experience with workflow engines such as Temporal or Airflow, or with building durable, event-driven automation across multiple systems. - Enterprise Integrations: Experience integrating security platforms, SaaS applications, identity providers, or case-management systems, including handling API rate limits and connector failures. - Classification & Enrichment: Experience integrating data classification, content inspection, or LLM-based analysis into production services, with appropriate evaluation and safeguards for sensitive data, reliability, latency, and cost.
