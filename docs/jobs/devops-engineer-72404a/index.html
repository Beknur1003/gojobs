DevOps Engineer

Cato Networks Tel Aviv District, Israel опубликовано 19 ноября 2026

KubernetesDockerAWSGCPAzureCI/CDPythonAI/LLM
Cato Networks is the converged network and security cloud for the AI era. As AI reshapes cybersecurity, enterprises face a dual challenge: unlocking AI’s potential while defending against AI-driven attacks. Cato addresses both through a unified, cloud-native platform, providing the visibility and context to prioritize risks and stop threats in real time across users, applications, data, and AI agents. Join us as we scale: take on ambitious technological challenges and shape the next generation of enterprise security at a company trusted by thousands of organizations worldwide. We’re looking for a visionary DevOps to join the R&D team. In this critical role, you will support the entire R&D operation, by providing automated tools and CI/CD processes. Responsibilities: • Be the owner of continuous delivery and deployment processes of all of Cato's software projects, build them from the bottom up, and keep extending them. • Collaborate with R&D and other departments to provide and improve self-service tools and automate whatever can be automated. • Help different departments be more productive in their day-to-day and improve overall developer experience. • Lead technical projects from discovery and design through implementation and rollout. • Partner with engineering teams to understand pain points, gather requirements, and improve developer workflows. • Define roadmaps and break down large initiatives into actionable plans and deliverables. • Contribute to our internal engineering portal and other developer-facing platforms. Requirements: • At least 5 years of experience with CI/CD pipelines and build tools (e.g. Git, Jenkins, Docker, Artifactory). • Experience in Docker & container technology. Bonus - Experience building and operating production systems on Kubernetes. • Proven programming and scripting experience - e.g. Go, Groovy, Python, Bash. • Excellent communication and teamwork skills. • Ability to work in a dynamic multi-task environment with context switches. • Strong problem-solving and systems-thinking skills. • Ability to independently identify gaps, propose solutions, and drive execution. • Comfortable working directly with engineers, technical leads, and engineering managers. • Strong sense of ownership and accountability. Preferred: • Knowledge of and a passion for configuration management tools such as Ansible, Chef, and Puppet. • Experience with cloud IaaS such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and SaaS platforms in general. • Experience with GitOps workflows and tools such as ArgoCD. • Software development experience in a backend language (Go is a strong advantage). • Experience designing developer self-service workflows. • Experience with Developer Experience (DevEx) initiatives. • Experience leading projects or owning significant technical initiatives end-to-end.
