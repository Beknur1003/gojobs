T-Ads' proprietary algorithms help us deliver privacy-first advertising at scale, rich consumer behavioral insights, and outstanding performance against our clients’ goals. You’ll use big data, cloud technologies, and metadata to accomplish this and help ensure we remain an innovative industry leader in AdTech. You'll be involved in the full lifecycle of product development, from ideation and design to implementation and support. Our collaborative environment encourages you to discuss the 'why' behind every project, helping shape the future of our technology. You'll work closely with other engineers, data scientists, and product managers, gaining a holistic understanding of how our systems come together. Success is measured by the effectiveness of data engineering solutions, team skill development, and contribution to data architecture innovation. The work impacts T-Ads organization, by enabling data-driven decision-making and advancing data infrastructure capabilities for business insights. Job Responsibilities: • Develop data engineering solutions that enable data pipelines, visualization, and analytical tools to support business requirements. • Design and develop data architectures across cloud platforms to ensure scalable data infrastructure. • Innovate, implement, support, and iterate on our scalable data pipelines and ETL processes. • Write robust, efficient, and highly maintainable code Python and Spark/Scala. • Contribute to team knowledge sharing and drive the advancement of new data engineering capabilities. • Translate business requirements into technical solutions with other engineers, product managers, and business stakeholders. • Mentor team members to build and enhance their data engineering skillsets and professional growth.
