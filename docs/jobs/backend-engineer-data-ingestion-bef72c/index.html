About the role As a Backend Engineer on Data Ingestion, you’ll work on the systems that bring captured activity into Scribe: receiving, batching, storing and handing it on to the AI pipeline, at the scale of hundreds of thousands of users and growing. The work is backend and infrastructure: throughput, reliability and cost. What you'll do • Build projects from design to launch, on established products and new ones. • Collaborate with PMs, designers and other engineers to execute the build and how. • Contribute to technical discussions, balancing short-term delivery with long-term architecture. • Uphold strong engineering practices: code review, test coverage and observability. • Build and operate high-throughput backend services and the queues and storage behind them. • Improve the performance and reliability of services, database queries, endpoints and caching across critical workflows. • Take part in the on-call rotation for the systems you build What we're looking for • 2+ years building experience building backend applications • Product-minded. You care about the user problem as much as the code • Comfortable with unclear problems and fast change • Strong Python or Go experience, or experience in another backend language and willingness to work across Python and Go • Familiar with queues, object storage, and relational or time-series databases • Has debugged a production problem from metrics and logs • User-centric, empathetic, and passionate about your work • Nice to have: AI/LLM product experience, startup experience Why you'll love working here • Hard, open problems. Nobody hands you a finished spec. You'll decide what to build with your PM and designer, then own how it performs. • The reach of a unicorn, the headcount of a startup. We went from $1M to $100M ARR in under four years, with fewer than 200 people. That means more pie than people: scope grows with your impact. Customers like T-Mobile, LinkedIn, HubSpot, New York Life and Northern Trust run on Scribe, so what you own reaches some of the biggest names in business. • Smart is the floor. Kind is the bar. Low ego, high standards. We compete as a collective and love to see each other win, and One Team, One Dream is one of the most shouted-out values in #values.
