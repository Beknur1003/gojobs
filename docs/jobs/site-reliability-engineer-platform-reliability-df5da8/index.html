Site Reliability Engineer - Platform Reliability

<div class="content-intro"><p>Elastic, the Search AI Company, enables everyone to find the answers they need in real time, using all their data, at scale — unleashing the potential of businesses and people. The Elastic Search AI Platform, used by more than 50% of the Fortune 500, brings together the precision of search and the intelligence of AI to enable everyone to accelerate the results that matter. By taking advantage of all structured and unstructured data — securing and protecting private information more effectively — Elastic’s complete, cloud-based solutions for search, security, and observability help organizations deliver on the promise of AI.</p></div><p></p> <h2>What is the role</h2> <p>Platform Infrastructure provides the infrastructure, tooling, processes and services that more than a thousand Elastic engineers depend on. We are about 110 people across ten teams in three groups: Core Infrastructure, Reliability Engineering, and Engineering Productivity &amp; Developer Experience. We run the multi-cloud platform underneath Elastic Cloud Hosted and Serverless, the shared Kubernetes platform other Elastic teams build on, the observability estate that shows Cloud engineers what production is doing, and the release path the whole Elastic Stack ships through.</p> <p>As an SRE here you take an engineering approach to reliability. You write the Go, Python and Terraform that replaces manual work, you carry the on-call rotation for what your team runs, and you work with Cloud engineering to close the gaps you find.</p> <p>Elastic is expanding its Serverless offerring, and most of what we build sits underneath it. Current work includes packing hundreds of customer projects onto shared clusters, PrivateLink and static IPs, new regions, a single identity layer across the stack, and new government regions. We are also replacing manually managed services with code-managed systems, which lets the platform grow without the on-call load growing with it.</p> <h2>Why join us</h2> <ul> <li>The scale is large. We run across multiple cloud providers and many regions, and some teams operate 200+ production deployments of our own product.</li> <li>We are customer zero for Elastic Cloud Hosted, Serverless and the observability and security products, so new features land on our systems first and our feedback goes back to the product teams.</li> <li>The engineers who use what you build are a thousand colleagues you can go and talk to.</li> <li>On-call follows the sun, which keeps the burden outside working hours small.</li> <li>We are fully distributed across EMEA, NASA and APJ and the work happens in writing, so you control how you run your day.</li> </ul> <h2>What You Will Be Doing</h2> <ul> <li>You own systems end to end as the DRI on a project, and you break the work down yourself.</li> <li>Own the outcome of projects on your team’s roadmap as the DRI, breaking the work down and pulling in help when you need it.</li> <li>Operate and harden the infrastructure that other Elastic engineers depend on, managed as code with Terraform, Go and Python.</li> <li>Build tools and services that remove manual work.</li> <li>Carry the on-call rotation for what your team runs, drive incidents to resolution and write RCAs that lead to lasting fixes.</li> <li>Improve observability, runbooks and documentation so the on-call load gets lighter over time.</li> <li>Review code and designs, and act as a second set of eyes on production changes to critical infrastructure.</li> <li>Raise risks and ideas without being asked, and mentor engineers with less experience.</li> <li>Give feedback to the Elastic product teams on the features that reach our systems first.</li> </ul> <h2>What You Bring</h2> <ul> <li>3+ years in SRE, platform engineering or infrastructure engineering, including real time operating what you built.</li> <li>Experience with Terraform at multi-workspace scale.</li> <li>Strong software engineering fundamentals in Go or Python, the languages most of what we build is written in.</li> <li>Extensive experience carrying on-call, resolving incidents under pressure and writing RCAs that hold up under review.</li> </ul> <h2>How you work</h2> <ul> <li>You take an ambiguous project, come back with a plan and own it to the end.</li> <li>You say when an estimate or a sequence is wrong before the team commits to it.</li> <li>You write clearly, because we work across nine time zones and the work happens in tickets and channels.</li> <li>You stay calm when something is broken, communicate while you work and write the follow-up that stops it happening
