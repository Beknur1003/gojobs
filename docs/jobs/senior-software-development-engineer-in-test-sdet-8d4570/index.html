About the Role We are looking for a Senior Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET) to join our Quality Engineering team. The role involves designing, developing, and maintaining scalable test automation solutions across UI, API, integration, and end-to-end workflows. The Senior SDET will work closely with Software Engineers, Product Managers, and other SDETs to drive quality throughout the development lifecycle. The role requires strong technical expertise, a quality-first mindset, and the ability to work effectively in a cloud-native environment. What You'll Do • Design, develop, and maintain robust test automation frameworks for UI, API, integration, and Performance testing. • Contribute reusable automation libraries, utilities, tools, and testing practices that can be adopted across engineering teams. • Participate in continuous improvement initiatives around test strategy, automation, CI/CD, quality metrics, and engineering practices. • Implement and support CI/CD pipelines integration. • Develop tools to simplify the testing process • Validate distributed and cloud-native systems involving APIs, microservices, queues, databases, and asynchronous workflows. • Preparing technical documentation and specifications • Mentor other SDETs and contribute to the overall Quality Engineering culture. Technologies You'll Work With Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, Golang, C#/.NET, Bash, Git, GitHub Actions, ADO, WireMock, GraphQL What You'll Bring • 8+ years of experience in test automation and software testing practices. • Strong programming experience in C#/.NET or a comparable programming language and shell scripting or other scripting languages like Python • Experience with UI automation frameworks (such as Playwright), API automation and REST APIs. • Experience with xUnit/NUnit or similar test frameworks. • Experience with performance and scalability testing frameworks • Ability to simulate real-world load conditions and analyse system behaviour under stress • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and Git-based development workflows. • Hands-on experience working with Kubernetes and related cloud-native projects • Understanding of container orchestration, service mesh, and observability tools • Ability to build reusable components and utilities for test and infrastructure automation • Strong ownership of projects and good communication skills Bonus Skills • Experience developing in Golang • Experience with public cloud APIs like AWS, Azure, Google. • Experience with GraphQL, gRPC, WebSockets, or other modern communication protocols.
