The weaponization of AI is driving a paradigm shift in the cyber security industry from proactive to preemptive security: clients must now invest more intelligence and effort in understanding their vulnerabilities before their adversaries do (Open AI). The only way to achieve this is to simulate future attack scenarios at scale, and to run them in high fidelity digital replicas of production environments (Gartner/Forrester). SimSpace serves as the AI Proving Grounds to generate hypersynthetic data which pressure tests controls, tools, teams, and agentic capabilities in full kill chain attacks hidden in realistic environmental traffic and patterns of life. Founded in 2015 by experts from U.S. Cyber Command and MIT Lincoln Laboratory, the platform was initially conceived to assist in Offensive Cyber Operations (OCO), to quickly replicate target environments to conduct mission rehearsal of human operators and their tools to deny (disrupt, degrade, destroy) or deceive (manipulate or exfiltrate data) adversaries. The company has since expanded to serve over a dozen allied governments in OCO/DCO as well as critical infrastructure enterprises around the world. The company is actively investing in the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe and the Middle East through direct sales and channel partners. SimSpace has launched the AI Proving Grounds Consortium, leading the revolution by creating an open platform for the InfoSec ecosystem to train, validate and operationalize their agentic capabilities before committing to production. Job Summary You will build services and workflows that turn AI-agent experiments into supported evaluation results for AI Proving Grounds (AIPG). Your software will connect execution, captured evidence and scoring so customers can investigate behavior and compare assessments under declared conditions. You will use AI-native development to take features from written requirements through code, tests, rollout and diagnosis. Work with coding agents to plan changes, refine implementations and investigate failures. You remain responsible for understanding and validating the result. Review shared-contract changes with the Staff Engineer and evidence assumptions with Data Scientists. What will you be doing at SimSpace? • Build backend services that connect experiment runs, evidence artifacts, scoring requests and result presentation. For example, a scoring-job workflow should bind results to versioned inputs and clearly report incomplete evidence. • Use coding agents to plan and implement well-scoped changes, generate tests and debug failures. Inspect generated code and verify behavior and access boundaries before release. • Implement APIs and data models that preserve identifiers, versions, permissions and evidence provenance through asynchronous processing. • Handle retries, duplicate requests, timeouts, missing input and partial failures so interrupted experiments can be diagnosed and recovered safely. • Write unit, integration and regression tests using reference cases from evaluation specialists. Check reported results against the underlying evidence. • Add logs, metrics and diagnostic tools that trace an assessment from execution to its reported outcome. • Clarify feature scope with Product, Data Science and engineering partners. Review code, document behavior and help diagnose and fix the capabilities you release. What are the qualifications to apply? • Experience independently designing, delivering and maintaining production software features through implementation, testing and rollout. • Practical experience using AI coding agents throughout feature delivery, with examples of clear task instructions, useful repository context, automated verification and correction of generated errors. • Strong programming, API-design and data-modeling skills in a production backend language. • Practical experience with asynchronous workflows, service integration and failure handling in distributed applications. • Ability to write automated tests that expose incorrect behavior and investigate defects across software and data boundaries. • Experience building access control, data validation, observability and supportability into feature work. • Ability to clarify requirements with specialists, explain design trade-offs, review code and raise delivery risks early. Who you are • You trace results back to their inputs and investigate discrepancies even when execution appears successful. • You own a feature through operational handover and use support feedback to improve the software and its tests. • You turn lessons from failed AI-assisted changes into better instructions, tests and reusable automation. Nice to have but not required • Experience with Go or Python in production services, data pipelines or evaluation tooling. • Experience with experimental systems, scoring workflows or interfaces for exploring analytical results. • Familiarity with cybersecurity, containers or cyber-range environments. Our Tech Stack Includes The planned platform uses Go operational services and Python evaluation components, service APIs, durable workflows, structured metadata, versioned artifacts, containers and automated tests. The development workflow centers on AI coding agents, written requirements, automated checks and code review.
