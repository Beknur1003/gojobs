The Role As a Senior Software Engineer, you will work alongside a supportive, high-impact team of skilled developers to improve how our teams deploy and access services, how data moves across our platform, and how we automate operations to enable future scale. Our teams specialize in developing robust and scalable Go microservices, leveraging a modern tech stack that includes AWS, Docker, Postgres, and Kafka. We are looking for engineers passionate about building high-quality solutions who thrive in a dynamic, collaborative environment. How You Will Make an Impact • Build Scalable Services: Design, develop, and maintain robust backend services and APIs that support our evolving platform. • Drive Architectural Evolution: Contribute directly to the transition to microservices by designing clean APIs and event-driven architectures. • Optimize Data Performance: Enhance database performance and data structures to support high-traffic, real-time applications and seamless data movement. • Collaborate Cross-Functionally: Partner with various teams to ensure technical solutions are perfectly aligned with business requirements and user needs. • Champion Engineering Excellence: Mentor fellow engineers and perform thorough code reviews to drive best practices in Golang. • Automate for Scale: Identify opportunities to automate operations, making it easier for other teams to deploy and access their services efficiently. • Solve High-Traffic Challenges: Apply pragmatic engineering to handle the complexities of moving data across a distributed platform at scale. What You Bring to the Team • Strong Technical Foundation: 4+ years of hands-on software development experience, including at least 2 years working with strongly-typed languages (Go, Java, C#, etc.). • Microservices Expertise: Demonstrated experience designing and developing microservices within cloud environments such as AWS, Azure, or GCP. • Code Quality: A proven ability to write maintainable, high-quality, and robust code with a pragmatic approach to problem-solving. • Data & Messaging Proficiency: Experience working with SQL databases and a solid knowledge of message queues (Kafka, SQS) and asynchronous processing patterns. • Effective Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to describe technical designs to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Infrastructure & Methodology: Familiarity with cloud-based infrastructure, basic networking, and Agile/Kanban methodologies.
