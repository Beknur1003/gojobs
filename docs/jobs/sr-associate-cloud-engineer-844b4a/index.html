JOB SUMMARY: CFSB is a full-service payments, lending and banking provider that delivers global financial services through a personal approach. CFSB is seeking a highly skilled Cloud Operations Engineer to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience with AWS, Terraform, Docker containers and networking. You will play a crucial role in designing, implementing, and maintaining our cloud infrastructure to ensure optimal performance, scalability, and security. Applicants must be permanently authorized to work for ANY employer in the U.S. We are unable to sponsor or take over sponsorship of any temporary employment visa now or in the future. Responsibilities: • Design, deploy, and manage scalable and secure AWS cloud environments using best practices. • Use Infrastructure-as-Code to automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration. • Ensure high availability and reliability of systems through proactive monitoring and performance tuning. • Participate in the implementation and automation of cloud security measures, governance policies, and auditing processes. • Coordinate issue resolution with operations, functional, and technical teams to provide a high level of service to our customers both internal and external and adhering to our strict SLAs for response and restoration times. • Collaborate with development teams to containerize existing applications and optimize deployment pipelines. • Well versed in managing incident calls during outages and involving the right people on the call and taking it to closure with root cause determination. • Develop and maintain documentation for known issues, workarounds, and standard operating procedures to assist in efficient problem resolution and knowledge sharing. • Monitoring the applications' stability, troubleshoot issues and resolve to ensure that client systems are operational, and any loss of service are restored completely. • Create scripts and automation tools to streamline operations and reduce manual efforts. • Implement automated monitoring, alerting, and remediation processes. • Possess good organizational skills and a keen attention to detail to anticipate any potential issues and take proactive measures to resolve. • Provide after-hours support to the business. Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: • Proficient with AWS services such as EC2, S3, VPC, RDS, IAM, ECS, EKS, Identity Center, Config, Security Hub and Lambda among others • Strong networking background, with hands-on experience with AWS Transit Gateway and hybrid architectures. • Strong DevOps background with passion for automation. • Proficient in at least one non-shell programming language (Java, .NET, Python, GoLang, Ruby among others) • Hands-on experience with Terraform for infrastructure as code. • Proficient in creating, managing, and deploying Docker containers. • Basic knowledge of change management processes and practices. • Good understanding of data governance & security, cryptography and automation. • Ability to work with stakeholders in high pressure situations. Education, Training, and Experience: • Bachelor’s degree in information technology, Computer Science or equivalent • 4+ Years Experience developing with Cloud Technologies (AWS, or Azure • 3+ Years Experience with Networking troubleshooting skill • AWS certification a plus About Community Federal Savings Bank (CFSB) Community Federal Savings Bank (CFSB) is a federally chartered bank founded in 2001 and headquartered in New York City. CFSB has focused on providing superior service and added value to clients though a solid understanding of relationship banking. We offer a full range of services, including loans, bill payments, card services, internet banking, and merchant processing.
