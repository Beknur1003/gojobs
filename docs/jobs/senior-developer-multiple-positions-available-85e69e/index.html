Expectations and Tasks: Resolves complex issues within own area of expertise and supports others in resolving issues. Drives topics to resolution in a high level of quality, speed, reliability, and efficiency. Works closely together with others and shares expertise to ensure product success. Education and Occupational Experience: Bachelor’s degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field of study and six (6) years of progressive post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or related occupation. Qualifications/Skills and Competencies Experience: Experience must include four (4) years involving each of the following: • Provisioning and managing cloud infrastructure environments utilizing GCP, OpenStack, and SAP Cloud Infrastructure; • Managing Kubernetes clusters, including authentication, RBAC, monitoring, and volume snapshot management; • Utilizing Gardener to provision and manage multiple Kubernetes clusters across different cloud providers, including Kubernetes version upgrade and node OS upgrade; • Linux operating systems and shell scripting; • Programming languages including Java, JavaScript, Python, or Golang; • Designing, consuming, and troubleshooting REST APIs or OData; • Building CI/CD environments utilizing Jenkins, ArgoCD, and JFrog Artifactory, and pipeline scripting utilizing Groovy; and • Observability frameworks and tools, including Prometheus and Grafana. Experience must involve two (2) years in the following: • Infrastructure as code utilizing Terraform and Ansible; • SAP HANA database administration, system upgrades, and lifecycle management utilizing HANA Database Lifecycle Manager (HDBLCM); • SAP Cloud Identity Services and SAP Business Technology Platform (SAP BTP) management, including account lifecycle management with BTP cockpit, and service provisioning with SAP BTP Operator; and • Implementing secret management utilizing HashiCorp Vault.
