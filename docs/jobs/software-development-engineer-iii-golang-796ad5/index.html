At SIXT, we are engineering the future of mobility, one system at a time. As a Senior Backend Engineer in our Data Science Finance team, you will design and build the backend infrastructure that powers our ML-driven financial risk steering platform. You'll work at the intersection of scalable backend systems and production ML, delivering solutions that directly impact how SIXT manages financial risk across millions of transactions. This is backend engineering with real business impact, in a team that ships fast and builds to last. YOUR ROLE AT SIXT • You design and build robust backend services in Golang that power the risk steering cycle and decision-making workflows • You build ML-powered systems together with Data Scientists and ML Engineers, from the first design session to the validated outcome in production, with one team working toward one goal • You understand the business and the processes to build solutions and not only write code • You are responsible for designing, developing, and operating end-to-end distributed systems on the cloud • You participate in code reviews, contribute to team knowledge sharing and maintain documentation • You work on event-driven systems and keep yourself up to date with the latest technologies to help you create scalable and resilient software YOUR SKILLS MATTER • Education You hold a B.Tech, B.E., or Master's Degree in Computer Science or a closely related discipline • Backend Expertise You bring strong, production-proven experience in Golang, plus confidence in at least one more language such as Python or Java • Experience You have 7-10 years of experience building cloud-native applications, solving scalability challenges, designing event-driven solutions, and ideally working on ML-powered products • Systems Design You have already designed or operated distributed systems and microservices architectures, with a clear understanding of the trade-offs that come with operating at scale, and experience working with cloud providers (AWS or similar) • Event-Driven Architecture You have good understanding of event-driven platforms, pub-sub and messaging using brokers such as Kafka, NSQ or RabbitMQ • AI Tools You actively use AI coding assistants, such as Cursor, Claude, to accelerate your work, while confidently recognising their limitations and knowing when engineering judgment must take precedence • Product Mindset You have a product-oriented mindset and attention to detail • Communication You act as a partner to Risk, Engineering, and Data Science;
