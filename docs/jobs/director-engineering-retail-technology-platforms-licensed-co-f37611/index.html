From the beginning, Starbucks set out to be a different kind of company. One that not only celebrated coffee and the rich tradition, but that also brought a feeling of connection. We are known for developing extraordinary leaders who share this passion and are guided by their service to others. We're looking for a director of engineering to own the full technology stack Starbucks offers its licensees. This leader is the single point of reference for the hardware and software solution that powers roughly 5,000 licensed stores\: Point of Sale, Point of Sale peripherals, order fulfillment, edge compute, endpoint management, and the API-driven integrations that connect Starbucks to each licensee's bespoke loyalty and sales systems. You will set the technology roadmap for that estate and make sure every licensee gets a reliable, supportable, and scalable store experience. Just as important, this role is charged with rationalizing and converging our store technology stacks across Company Operated, Licensed, and International markets. Today, those channels have grown along separate paths; you will define the target architecture, build the business case, and lead the multi-year path toward common platforms, so that we build once, deploy widely, and give every store, regardless of ownership model, the full benefit of Starbucks scale. You'll lead a team of engineering managers and senior engineers and work through influence well beyond it, partnering with licensee technology teams, the Licensed Stores business, Company Operated store technology, International technology leaders, Architecture, Product, and our hardware and software vendors. Success looks like a clear, trusted licensee technology roadmap, measurable improvements in store uptime and time-to-deploy, faster and more repeatable licensee integrations, and visible progress toward a converged store platform across channels. As a director, engineering, you will… • Own the end-to-end licensee technology stack across hardware, firmware and software, including Point of Sale, POS peripherals (payment devices, printers, scanners, displays), order fulfillment, edge compute, and endpoint management • Serve as the single point of reference and accountable owner for the licensed stores technology roadmap across approximately 5,000 stores, balancing licensee needs, Starbucks standards, and total cost of ownership \\ • Lead the rationalization and convergence of store technology stacks across Company Operated, Licensed, and International markets, defining the target-state architecture, sequencing the migration, and retiring redundant platforms and local variants • Define and govern the API and integration strategy that enables licensees to connect bespoke loyalty, sales, payments, and reporting systems to Starbucks platforms in a secure, scalable, and repeatable way • Drive the hardware lifecycle for the licensed estate, including device certification, refresh planning, vendor and OEM partnerships, and supply and deployment readiness • Establish edge compute and endpoint management standards that improve store reliability, security posture, patching and software distribution, and remote supportability at scale • Partner with licensee technology leaders and the Licensed Stores business to onboard new licensees and formats, translating business commitments into clear technical requirements, timelines, and support models • Raise the bar on operational excellence for licensed store technology, owning store-level availability, incident and problem management, release quality, and service-level performance • Build the business case for platform consolidation, quantifying duplication, cost to serve, and risk, and securing alignment and investment from technology and business executives • Lead and develop a team of engineering managers and senior engineers, setting direction, raising the technical bar, and growing leaders • Represent licensed and international store needs in enterprise technology forums, influencing Company Operated and digital roadmaps so that shared capabilities work for every channel We'd love to hear from leaders who bring...
