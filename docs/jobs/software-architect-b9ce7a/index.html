Software Architect

Virima Bangalore South, India опубликовано 2 сентября 2026

PostgreSQLMongoDBRedisKubernetesMicroservicesCI/CDPythonJavaAI/LLM
We are seeking an experienced Software Architect to lead the design and evolution of our next-generation AI-native cloud platform. This platform will serve as the foundation for intelligent, distributed, and highly scalable applications that power mission-critical workloads. You will shape the system architecture, champion best practices, and drive adoption of cutting-edge technologies across the engineering organization. Responsibilities Architect Scalable & Intelligent Systems • Design and evolve a cloud-native, distributed architecture capable of handling millions of transactions/events per second with strict low-latency and high-availability requirements. • Architect AI-native services, integrating machine learning, personalization, and generative AI into platform experiences. • Define data strategy for both transactional (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis) and analytical workloads (Apache Pulsar, Flink, Iceberg). Technical Leadership • Provide thought leadership on key architectural decisions, frameworks, and design patterns. • Drive adoption of event-driven, microservices-based, and real-time streaming architectures. • Lead architecture reviews, design discussions, and technical roadmaps. Innovation & Optimization • Identify opportunities for automation, performance optimization, and AI/ML-driven insights. • Introduce data-intensive computing practices for real-time analytics, stream processing, and graph-based models. • Continuously evaluate new technologies (e.g., vector databases, LLMOps frameworks, GPU acceleration). Cross-Team Collaboration • Partner with product managers, UX designers, data scientists, and DevOps teams to translate business needs into technical blueprints. • Ensure seamless developer experience and platform adoption across teams. Security, Reliability & Compliance • Embed security, compliance, and privacy as first-class concerns in all designs. • Architect for multi-region, multi-tenant, high-availability deployments. • Champion observability, monitoring, and incident response best practices. Mentorship & Team Development • Mentor engineers, fostering a culture of craftsmanship, innovation, and continuous learning. • Define engineering and architectural standards, ensuring system resilience and scalability. Technology Evaluation • Continuously evaluate emerging cloud, AI, and distributed systems technologies. • Introduce frameworks and tools that enhance developer productivity, system performance, and scalability. Requirements • Experience: 13+ years of software engineering with at least 5+ years in architecture leadership roles. • Distributed Systems Expertise: Deep experience with event-driven, microservices, and real-time streaming systems. • Core Tech: Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Apache Pulsar, Apache Flink, and Iceberg. • Programming Skills: Proficiency in Java, Golang, Python, or equivalent languages. • Cloud-Native Mastery: Expertise in Kubernetes, container-native design, CI/CD, and service mesh (Istio/Linkerd).
