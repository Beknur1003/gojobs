EDI Integration Developer Innovate AI Solutions | Colombia Position Overview At Innovate AI Solutions, we are looking for a skilled and detail-oriented EDI Integration Developer to join our engineering team and help strengthen our AI-driven logistics solutions. This is a highly technical, hands-on position focused on software development, EDI integrations, troubleshooting, and system optimization. We are looking for candidates with direct technical experience implementing and maintaining integrations, rather than professionals whose backgrounds are primarily managerial or supervisory. As an EDI Integration Developer, you will be responsible for developing, maintaining, and optimizing Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) workflows that enable seamless, secure, and reliable communication between our systems, clients, and business partners. Location and Work Authorization Requirements • Candidates must currently reside in Colombia. • Applicants must be legally authorized to work in Colombia. • Candidates must meet both requirements to be considered for this opportunity. Key Responsibilities • EDI Development & Integration: Map, translate, and process EDI documents to support seamless data exchange between internal and external systems. • Workflow Development: Design, implement, maintain, and optimize EDI integration workflows. • System Monitoring: Monitor EDI transactions, identify integration failures, and proactively resolve technical issues. • Platform Maintenance: Maintain, troubleshoot, and enhance our proprietary EDI solution to ensure reliability, performance, and scalability. • Partner Integrations: Collaborate directly with external EDI partners to establish, test, and maintain integrations. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with software developers, QA engineers, and other stakeholders to deliver high-quality technical solutions. • Code Quality: Participate in code reviews, implement best practices, and contribute to continuous technical improvement. • Technical Troubleshooting: Investigate bugs, performance bottlenecks, and integration issues, developing effective and sustainable solutions. Required Qualifications • Professional Experience: At least 2 years of hands-on professional experience in software development, system integrations, or a closely related technical role. • Database Management: Strong working knowledge of PostgreSQL, including querying, troubleshooting, and database optimization. • API Development: Practical experience developing, integrating, and maintaining RESTful APIs and web services. • Software Testing: Experience writing unit tests and feature tests to ensure code quality, functionality, and system reliability. • Version Control: Proficiency with Git or similar version control systems. • Technical Problem-Solving: Strong analytical skills and the ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues independently. • Communication & Collaboration: Excellent communication skills and the ability to work effectively within a collaborative engineering environment. • Hands-On Technical Background: Demonstrated experience actively developing, configuring, implementing, or troubleshooting technical solutions. Important: This is not a project management or team leadership position. Candidates must have practical technical experience and be comfortable working directly with code, integrations, databases, and system troubleshooting. Preferred Qualifications The following qualifications are considered a plus: • Experience with EDI document mapping, encoding, decoding, and translation. • Familiarity with EDI standards and transaction formats, including ANSI X12 or EDIFACT. • Knowledge of EDI communication protocols such as AS2, FTP, and SFTP. • Experience with open-source EDI interchange servers, such as Mendelson AS2. • Experience working with proprietary EDI interchange platforms. • Familiarity with the Go programming language (Golang). • Experience with tracking systems, particularly within the transportation or logistics industry. • Knowledge of monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus and Grafana. • Experience with microservices architecture and publish/subscribe systems, including Redis or Kafka. • Previous experience with logistics, supply chain technology, or B2B integrations. What We Offer • Competitive Compensation: A compensation package aligned with your qualifications and experience, including performance-based bonuses. • Flexible Working Hours: Flexibility to start your workday between 7:00 AM and 10:00 AM. • Professional Development: Opportunities to expand your technical expertise through training, conferences, and workshops. • Innovative Technology: The opportunity to contribute to AI-driven solutions transforming the logistics industry. • Work Equipment: An Apple MacBook provided for your professional use. • Health Insurance: Comprehensive health insurance coverage. • Paid Health Days: Access to paid health days without requiring a doctor's note. • Birthday Leave: An additional paid day off to celebrate your birthday. • Collaborative Engineering Environment: Work alongside a team focused on innovation, technical excellence, and continuous improvement. Eligibility Requirements Please apply only if you meet the following conditions: • You are currently residing in Colombia. • You have valid legal authorization to work in Colombia. • You have at least 2 years of relevant hands-on technical experience. • You are comfortable performing technical development and integration responsibilities as part of your daily work. We encourage qualified developers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges and building reliable integration solutions to apply. • Join Innovate AI Solutions and help us build the technology powering the future of logistics.
