**About the Role** iCapital is looking for a **Full-Stack Engineer** to join the **iCapital Identity Platform** team. This role will help define and deliver new features for one or more of iCapital's software platforms. You will work closely with Product Management and Design throughout the software development lifecycle—scoping requirements, reviewing work in progress, and addressing issues uncovered during automated and manual testing. The iCapital Identity frontend uses **TypeScript**, **React**, **GraphQL**, and **Relay**. The backend is built in **Elixir/Phoenix**, with some **Go** and **Node.js**, backed by **PostgreSQL**. Our infrastructure runs on **AWS** and is provisioned using **Terraform**. **Responsibilities:** - Leverage an **Elixir + Phoenix** backend running on Amazon’s latest serverless technologies to power a **TypeScript + React** frontend - Support existing products and features while helping design and build new ones - Design and develop integrations with other iCapital platforms - Collaborate regularly with stakeholders and partners to gather requirements, seek feedback, and provide updates **Qualifications:** - 10–15+ years of experience in a related field - Strong understanding of modern browser technologies and web fundamentals (HTTP, cookies, CSP, CORS, etc.) - Extensive experience developing full-stack web applications - Experience in collaborative environments where engineers define project vision, influence technical strategy, and continuously learn - Strong relational database fundamentals - Excellent written and verbal communication skills - Demonstrated proficiency and professional success coding in at least one programming language - Experience with Linux and strong command-line skills **Preferred/Additional Experience** - Writing production-ready code in **Elixir**, **Python**, **TypeScript**, **JavaScript**, **Go**, or **Ruby** - Optimizing queries in **PostgreSQL** - Developing with **React**, **Vue**, **Angular**, or **LiveView** - Deploying and configuring applications on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** - Prior experience in a security or penetration-testing role - Querying **Google BigQuery** or other large relational datasets - Technical leadership, team lead, or management experience We believe the best ideas and innovation happen when we are together. Employees in this role will work in the office four days, with the flexibility to work remotely one day (Friday). #### **Benefits** iCapital offers a comprehensive benefits package that includes a total compensation program consisting of competitive salary, annual performance bonus, and equity for all full-time employees; healthcare with 100% employer-paid health and dental insurance; and generous paid time off (PTO).
