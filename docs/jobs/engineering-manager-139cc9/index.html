Engineering Manager

Tanla Platforms Hyderabad опубликовано 20 августа 2026

Go — основной язык
AI/LLM
Mandatory : - Strong backend engineering background (preferably Golang) - Should be capable of reviewing architecture and code, even if not coding daily - Large-scale distributed platform experience - System Design - High-scale engineering - Delivery ownership - Team building - Hiring experience - Cross-functional stakeholder management - Technical roadmap planning - Engineering metrics - Production operations - Performance management Leadership Expectations : - Builds high-performing engineering teams - Owns execution end-to-end - Balances speed with engineering quality - Drives architecture through technical leaders - Mentors leads and senior engineers Job Scope : We are looking for an experienced leader who possesses top class leadership experience in building and scaling high performing teams who are responsible for mission-critical, reliable services. You will typically manage through leaders, and build critical cross-functional collaboration with key stakeholders and product management. You will be responsible for the business critical impacts delivered. What you should have : - Experience building innovative and complex software for at least 12+ years. - You have great interpersonal skills, deep technical ability, and a portfolio of successful execution. If you enjoy discussing anything from ML to data pipelines, to ontologies, to graphs, to data center topology and you measure the success of your projects in "nines," then we want you on the team. - Engineering Excellence : You have at least 5+ years of industry experience in a variety of contexts, during which you've built scalable, robust, and fault-tolerant systems. You have a solid knowledge of the whole web stack : front-end, back-end, databases, cache layer, architecture, etc. - Bias for action : You believe that speed and quality aren't mutually exclusive. You've shown good judgement about shipping as fast as possible while still making sure that products are built in a sustainable, responsible way - and you're comfortable making mistakes, provided you learn from them. - Mentorship/ Guidance : You know that the most important part of your job is setting the team up for success. Through mentoring, teaching, and reviewing, you help other engineers make sound architectural decisions, improve their code quality, and get out of their comfort zone. - Hiring know-how : You're a thoughtful interviewer who constantly raises the bar for excellence. You believe that what seems amazing one day becomes the norm the next day, and that each new hire should significantly improve the team. - Design and business vision : You help your team understand requirements beyond the written word and you thrive in an environment where you can uncover subtle details. Even in the absence of a PM or a designer, you show great attention to the design and product aspect of anything your team ships. - Management experience running team(s) at scale. - Hands-on software development experience. Code shouldn't scare you. - Strong experience in Design and Architecture for large scale systems. Why join us : - Impactful Work : Play a pivotal role in safeguarding Tanla's assets, data, and reputation in the industry. - Tremendous Growth Opportunities : Be part of a rapidly growing company in the telecom and CPaaS space, with opportunities for professional development. - Innovative Environment : Work alongside a world-class team in a challenging and fun environment, where innovation is celebrated.
