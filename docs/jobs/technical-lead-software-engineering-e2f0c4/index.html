KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Technical Leadership & Software Development • Lead one or more software development squads and provide technical direction, guidance, and mentorship. • Remain hands-on with software development, including writing and reviewing code. • Participate in technical design and architecture discussions for complex applications and systems. • Review code and ensure development work follows established engineering and quality standards. • Identify and implement practical and scalable technical solutions to complex business and engineering challenges. • Promote best practices in software development, architecture, and engineering processes. Solution Design & Digital Transformation • Design and develop flexible, scalable systems that can support business growth. • Translate business and stakeholder requirements into appropriate technical solutions. • Participate in the development of new digital solutions and improvements to existing systems. • Evaluate new technologies and approaches that may improve system performance, scalability, and development efficiency. Product & Delivery Collaboration • Work with the Head of Technology, Product teams, and other stakeholders to define technical priorities and delivery timelines. • Translate product requirements and business objectives into actionable technical tasks for the development team. • Provide estimates and recommendations for user stories and development activities. • Support backlog preparation and ensure requirements are sufficiently defined for development. • Participate in Agile ceremonies, sprint planning, and regular delivery activities. Team Leadership & Mentoring • Mentor and develop software engineers within the team. • Provide technical guidance and support when developers encounter complex issues. • Encourage knowledge sharing, collaboration, and continuous improvement within the engineering team. • Help establish a culture of accountability, quality, and effective problem-solving. QUALIFICATIONS • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a related field. • Strong professional experience in software development, particularly in backend development using Java, Golang, Node.js, Scala, or similar object-oriented/open-source technologies. • Proven experience leading, mentoring, or providing technical direction to software development teams. • Strong understanding of software engineering principles, application architecture, and system design. • Hands-on experience with React, TypeScript, or JavaScript. • Experience with relational databases and SQL, preferably MySQL or PostgreSQL. • Experience with container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with CI/CD and automated deployment tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD, CircleCI, or similar platforms. • Familiarity with GitOps environments such as ArgoCD, Kubestack, or similar tools. • Understanding of OpenAPI standards and service-oriented architecture. • Experience using modern coding/AI-assisted development tools is an advantage. • Strong English communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders. NICE TO HAVE • Experience in supply chain, logistics, warehouse management, procurement, inventory, or other retail technology environments. • Experience working in Agile/Scrum development teams. • Experience building and scaling enterprise applications or platforms.
