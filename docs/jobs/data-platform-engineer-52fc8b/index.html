Data Platform Engineer

BrightAI Palo Alto, CA опубликовано 26 октября 2026

KafkagRPCKubernetesTerraformAWSPythonAI/LLM
We are a high-growth company transforming how businesses operate by integrating AI, IoT, and cloud-native services into scalable, real-time platforms. As a Platform Data Engineer, you’ll play a critical role in building and maintaining the data infrastructure that powers our products, services, and insights. You’ll join a multidisciplinary team focused on ingesting, processing, and managing massive streams of sensor and operational data across a wide array of devices—from drones and robots to industrial systems and smart environments. Responsibilities • Design, build, and maintain scalable, reliable, and high-throughput data ingestion pipelines for structured and semi-structured data. • Implement robust and secure data lake and SQL-based storage architectures optimized for performance and cost. • Develop and maintain internal tools and frameworks for data ingestion using Python, Golang, and SQL. • Build and maintain inference pipelines to support model serving and near real time prediction workflows • Develop and maintain internal tools and deployment processes to support ML development workflow , including experimentation, testing, deployment and monitoring. • Collaborate cross-functionally with Cloud, Edge, Product, and AI teams to define data contracts, schemas, and retention policies. • Use AWS cloud infrastructure (including Argo Workflows, S3, Lambda, Glue, Kinesis, Athena, and RDS) to support end-to-end data workflows. • Employ Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) practices using Terraform to manage data platform infrastructure. • Monitor data pipelines for quality, latency, and failures using tools such as CloudWatch, SumoLogic, or DataDog. • Build and maintain ingestion pipelines that land curated sensor and operational data into Delta tables on Databricks to support ML experimentation and model training. • Continuously optimize storage, partitioning, and query performance across large-scale datasets. • Participate in architecture reviews and ensure the platform adheres to security, compliance, and best practice standards. Skills and Qualifications • 5+ years of professional experience in software engineering or data engineering. • Strong programming skills in Python and Golang. • Deep understanding of SQL and modern data lake architectures (e.g., using Parquet, Iceberg, or Delta Lake). • Hands-on experience with AWS services including but not limited to: S3, Lambda, Glue, Kinesis, Athena, and RDS. • Proficiency with Terraform for automating infrastructure deployment and management. • Hands-on experience with Databricks for ML workflows: MLflow experiment tracking and model registry, Model Serving, and building training datasets in Delta Lake (PySpark/Spark SQL). • Experience working with real-time or batch data ingestion at scale, and designing fault-tolerant ETL/ELT pipelines. • Familiarity with DVC for dataset versioning and management. • Familiarity with event-driven architectures and messaging systems like Kafka or Kinesis. • Strong debugging and optimization skills across cloud, network, and application layers. • Excellent collaboration, communication, and documentation skills. Bonus Points • Experience working with time-series or IoT sensor data at industrial scale. • Exposure to gRPC and protobuf-based data contracts. • Experience supporting ML pipelines and feature stores. • Working knowledge of Kubernetes concepts. • Databricks Machine Learning certification (Professional) • Prior startup experience and/or comfort working in fast-paced, iterative environments.
