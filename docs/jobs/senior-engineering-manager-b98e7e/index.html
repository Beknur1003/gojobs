Impact The Senior Engineering Manager – Marketing, Incentive Platform & CMS role is a senior people and technology leader within Shipt’s Member Marketplace and Marketing engineering organization. This role reports directly to the Director of Engineering and is responsible for leading a multi-disciplinary group of 10+ backend, web, and mobile engineers across multiple agile pods. This leader oversees the core capabilities powering Shipt’s promotional marketing ecosystem, customer segmentation, multi-channel campaign orchestration, and Content Management System (CMS) tooling. Driving long-term technical strategy, engineering excellence, delivery predictability, and team health across diverse experiences, the Senior Engineering Manager operates with broader ownership, deeper architectural influence, higher cross-functional visibility, a wider breadth of stakeholders, and increased responsibility for scaling teams, managing complex initiatives, and shaping engineering culture. Senior Engineering Managers collaborate closely with Staff & Principal Engineers, Product Managers, UX Designers & Managers, Data Scientists, Marketing & Merchandising Operations, and partner engineering teams to execute against multi-quarter roadmaps, strengthen engineering fundamentals, develop talent to meet engineering expectations of the next level, remove organizational bottlenecks, and guide engineers through ambiguity with clarity and strategic direction. This is a high-impact role that requires strong technical depth, exceptional leadership maturity, strategic decision-making, and the ability to steward mission-critical tech assets and engineering teams through platform modernization, peak retail readiness, and AI-driven transformation. What You’ll Need to Be Successful Strategic Technical Leadership • Lead and influence architectural direction across full-stack domains, including high-concurrency backend microservices (Golang, Kafka, PostgreSQL, Redis, Snowflake, GraphQL/Apollo Federation) and frontend/CMS experiences (React, React Native, Server/Client-Driven UI). • Partner with Engineering, Product, UX, Data Science, and Marketing/Merchandising Operations leaders to define multi-quarter modernization plans, system migrations, quality bars, and domain boundaries across promotions, audience targeting, campaign orchestration, and CMS authoring tools. • Ensure teams consistently adopt modern engineering practices, including event-driven patterns, real-time budget and state-machine orchestration, deep observability, performance optimization for peak retail traffic, and AI-assisted development. • Oversee the technical strategy for your areas of responsibility, ensuring alignment with Shipt- or department-wide architectural principles and long-term company goals. • Drive cross-team technical initiatives that improve stability, reliability, and operational excellence across complex, interconnected services (e.g., Campaign Navigator, Order & Membership Promotions, Audience Builder/Delivery, and CMS platforms). Cross-Discipline Leadership • Own end-to-end delivery for customer-facing promotional experiences, internal marketing/CMS tooling, and core backend capabilities, ensuring predictable execution against roadmaps, OKRs, and quarterly commitments. • Lead technical planning cycles — capacity planning, dependency mapping across frontend/backend pods, long-range planning, and risk mitigation. • Establish unified prioritization and intake frameworks that protect engineering focus, minimize context-switching, and streamline cross-functional requests. • Establish processes that improve productivity, throughput, rollout safety, code quality, and release cadence (including CI/CD modernization). • Ensure high operational readiness across all owned services — SLOs, observability instrumentation (Grafana, DataDog, Rollbar), dashboards, runbooks, on-call rotations, and proactive peak-season load testing. People Leadership & Organizational Scaling • Manage 10+ engineers across backend, web, and mobile disciplines, spanning mid-level to Staff levels. • Guide career development through structured mentorship, progression frameworks, and personalized growth plans — actively developing Senior engineers into tech leads and Staff engineers, and supporting Staff engineers stepping into broader organizational influence. • Establish high talent density through strong hiring, rigorous performance management, and targeted mentorship programs. • Scale and size teams sustainably, anticipating skill gaps, capacity needs, and future organizational structures across Incentive Platform and CMS pods. • Build and reinforce a culture of high accountability, ownership, and continuous improvement toward the demonstration of Shipt Values. Cross-Functional Stakeholder Management & Influence • Serve as the primary engineering partner to Product, UX, Data Science, Growth Marketing, and Retailer Operations for your areas of responsibility. • Translate product strategy, internal marketing user needs, and business outcomes into clear technical direction and execution plans. • Influence prioritization and sequencing decisions across competing stakeholder backlogs through strong technical judgment and data-driven reasoning. • Build strong relationships with internal stakeholders across Member Marketplace, Checkout & Pricing, Personalization, Loyalty, Infrastructure, and Data Science. • Communicate progress, risks, blockers, trade-offs, and decisions — early, clearly, and often — to executive leaders and cross-functional partners. Engineering Excellence & Continuous Improvement • Champion AI-assisted engineering practices — expanding the use of AI coding assistants, automated test agents, refactoring agents, and intelligent developer workflows (e.g., automated ticket/spec generation and support workflows). • Improve engineering fundamentals across code quality, testing maturity, observability, scalability, and architectural hygiene. • Establish and continuously improve product team working agreements drawing from expertise and experience with multiple agile software development methodologies. • Lead and refine agile ceremonies (e.g., sprint planning, cross-pod backlog refinement, demos, retrospectives) to ensure crisp acceptance criteria and cross-pod alignment. • Lead post-incident reviews and systemic corrective action initiatives across multiple teams. • Establish and drive engineering metrics (DORA, quality KPIs, incident metrics, velocity, cycle times). • Promote a culture that values learning, experimentation, craftsmanship, and psychological safety. Candidate Profile Qualifications • Bachelor’s or advanced degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent practical experience. • 12+ years of professional engineering experience, preferably building high-throughput customer-facing systems, growth/marketing platforms, or enterprise content/campaign tooling. • 6+ years of formal engineering people leadership, including managing multiple engineers, tech leads, or pods across different disciplines. • Proven experience designing and delivering distributed, high-availability, scalable systems. Technical Leadership Proficiencies Full-Stack & Distributed Systems Expertise • Ability to guide engineers across multiple disciplines: • Backend: High-concurrency microservices (Golang preferred), event-driven architectures (Kafka), relational and analytical data stores (PostgreSQL, Snowflake, Redis), and API gateways (REST, GraphQL / Apollo Federation). • Frontend & CMS: Modern web and mobile application frameworks (React, React Native), Server/Client-Driven UI (CDUI) architectures, and internal content management / campaign authoring interfaces. Architecture, Systems, & Modernization • Understanding of large-scale system design, domain-driven architecture, state-machine orchestration, event modeling, and service contracts. • Experience guiding modernization efforts — service decomposition, platform/vendor migrations (e.g., CRM/channel integrations, CI/CD pipelines), performance rewrites, and observability upgrades. Engineering Metrics & Operational Maturity • Strong fluency in SLOs/SLIs, incident management, root cause analysis, and reliability frameworks (e.g., graceful degradation, circuit breakers, idempotency). • Experience using engineering metrics to guide team-level continuous improvement and peak-traffic readiness. Stakeholder Management & Business Alignment • Understanding of e-commerce, retail marketplaces, promotional mechanics, audience segmentation, or content management workflows. • Ability to align engineering roadmaps across multiple product managers with business OKRs, customer outcomes, and long-term platform strategy. Team Scaling & Resource Planning • Proven ability to scale engineering teams, including capacity planning, performance management, delegation coaching, and leveling strategies. • Experience in headcount planning, budgeting inputs, and strategic forecasting. • Familiarity with agile software development methodologies across multi-pod environments. Leadership Behaviors • Strategic Thinker: Operates at multi-quarter horizons with strong architectural and organizational awareness across interconnected domains. • Talent Multiplier: Develops senior engineers into effective tech leads and future Staff/management leaders; builds durable, high-performing teams. • Influential Communicator: Navigates ambiguity, aligns competing stakeholder priorities, and influences across engineering, product, and business leaders. • Operationally Excellent: Instills accountability, high-quality craftsmanship, and strong on-call and peak-season readiness. • Culture Builder: Models a high degree of professionalism and emotional maturity, and champions Shipt Values of Drive, Rally, Positivity, Authenticity, and Caring. • AI-Forward Leader: Encourages responsible adoption of AI-assisted engineering practices appropriate to engineering level and helps position Shipt for AI-native development. • Courageous & Principled: Makes difficult decisions when needed, prioritizes ruthlessly to prevent team burnout and context-switching, and knows how to pragmatically decide when to ship, when to “do it right,” and when to pay down tech debt.
