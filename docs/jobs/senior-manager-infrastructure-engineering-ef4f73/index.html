The Senior Manager, Infrastructure Engineering leads a team of engineers responsible for the platforms and systems that power our global engineering organization. This role balances hands-on technical leadership with people management, driving the continued evolution toward cloud-native, containerized, and highly available infrastructure while building a high-performing, engaged team. The Senior Manager partners closely with engineering leadership, product, and cross-functional stakeholders to reduce developer friction, strengthen system reliability and security, and ensure infrastructure investments scale with business growth. BigCommerce, part of the Commerce brand family, helps merchants increase sales at every stage of their growth. From small startups to mid-market businesses and large enterprises, we provide the leading e-commerce platform. Our customers can then concentrate on what's most important: growing their businesses. We enable our customers to build, innovate, and grow, collectively reshaping the e-commerce industry.
