As a Sr. Software Engineer I, you will champion best practices within the engineering team and serve as a mentor to the team. You will report to the Sr. Engineering Manager. Responsibilities: • You will lead the technical design and architecture of major components in our large-scale distributed backend service, creating new features and updating existing ones • You will build server-side systems that deliver new functionality within the larger Digital Platform. • You will reduce technical debt associated with legacy systems through innovation and automation. • You will work with customers, product managers, and business leaders to understand customer needs and deliver incrementally on their highest priority features and requirements. • You will spend 40-60% of your time hands-on coding. Qualifications: • 9+ years of experience in software engineering • Expertise in Java, Golang, and Kafka • 6+ years of experience working on online services (HTTP via REST) at Internet consumer scale • 6+ years of experience working with technologies like AWS, Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform • Expertise with the Software design / architecture process • Experience with DevOps, Continuous Delivery, and Agile development • Experience working on high traffic services and highly scalable systems (>2 million DAU, hundreds of thousands requests per second) • Technical leadership or mentorship experience • Experience with technologies such as memcache, queuing, and NoSQL in production systems and other OSS tech Bonus: • Experience with Maven/Gradle • Experience with load testing and system analysis • Experience with other languages such as C++ or C# • Some experience leading or guiding engineers • Experience working with remote teams • Domain experience in economics, commerce, and/or fraud area This is a hybrid remote/in-office role.
