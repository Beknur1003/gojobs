Why You’ll Love the Job: You’ll build the backend systems that power affordable, subscription-based healthcare for thousands of patients. You’ll own real services end-to-end — from design to production — and see your work directly shape how people access care. You’ll work closely with Product, Architecture, and Frontend in a fast-paced, entrepreneurial environment where your technical decisions carry weight and your ideas matter. Role Overview: We’re looking for a Software Engineer II who owns the development of well-scoped backend services and features that power Subscribili’s platform. You work independently on assigned problems, write production-grade Go, and partner with Product and senior engineers to turn requirements into reliable, well-tested systems. Reports To: Engineering Manager Requirements Key Responsibilities: This role is organized around five core areas. Each describes what we expect you to own and deliver. Design & Development • Design, build, test, and deploy backend services and APIs in Golang for features of moderate complexity. • Translate product requirements into clean, maintainable code following established patterns and standards. • Integrate with frontend applications, third-party APIs, and databases. Code Quality & Testing • Write unit and integration tests; treat testing as part of “done.” • Participate actively in code reviews — both giving and receiving feedback. • Follow team conventions for readability, error handling, and documentation. Architecture & Technical Direction • Contribute to design discussions and propose approaches for your features. • Make sound implementation-level decisions; escalate larger architectural calls. Collaboration & Influence • Collaborate with Product, Architects, and Frontend to scope and deliver work. • Communicate progress, blockers, and trade-offs clearly. Operations, Reliability & Security • Monitor and troubleshoot the services you own; fix bugs and address performance issues. • Follow secure-coding practices and handle data responsibly — including PHI — across backend systems. Core Technical Skills: The capabilities we expect you to bring to the role, grouped by area. Languages & Foundations • Solid proficiency in Golang and its ecosystem. • Working understanding of concurrency, goroutines, and channels. • Strong problem-solving, debugging, and analytical skills, with the ability to deliver scoped projects independently. • Working knowledge of Python is a big plus. AI-Assisted Development • Uses AI coding tools effectively but treats generated code as a draft — reviews it, runs and tests it locally to confirm correctness, and catches security and performance issues before raising a PR. APIs & Architecture • Experience building RESTful APIs (gRPC a plus). • Hands-on experience developing within a microservices architecture. Data Stores • Core: a relational database — PostgreSQL and/or MySQL. • Nice to have: exposure to DynamoDB (NoSQL) and/or Redis (caching) — these are a plus, not a requirement. Cloud & Infrastructure • Comfortable working in a cloud environment (we run on AWS), with Docker, CI/CD pipelines, and Git. • Our backend runs on AWS Elastic Beanstalk and Lambda; specific experience with these services isn’t required — if you know Docker or any container-orchestration tooling, you’ll get up to speed quickly. • As our platform scales, we’re expanding into container orchestration (ECS/EKS, Kubernetes), so container experience is a strong plus. Bonus Points: • Experience in healthcare, fintech, or analytics-driven platforms. • Familiarity with event tracking, system monitoring, and payment workflows. • Understanding of compliance, data privacy, or healthcare regulations (e.g., HIPAA/PHI). Benefits • Competitive Salary: Attractive package based on your experience, skills, and impact. • Equity Options: Share in Subscribili’s growth and long term success. • Health and Wellness: Comprehensive health coverage for you and your family, including medical benefits, term insurance, and complimentary annual health checkups. • Office Meals: 2 meals per day provided in office to support convenience, collaboration, and team connection. • Onsite Role with Flexible Hours: This is a full time onsite role based in HSR Layout, Bangalore. • Generous Leave: Paid vacation, sick leave, and parental leave. • Learning and Growth: Access to courses, workshops, and leadership development opportunities. • Career Development: Opportunity to build and lead the People function as Subscribili scales. • Mission Driven Culture: Join a team that cares deeply about employees, patients, providers, and the future of affordable healthcare.
