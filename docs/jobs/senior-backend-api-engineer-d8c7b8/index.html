The company serves a diverse client base across healthcare, government, digital health and health insurance sector. Spike Technologies is looking for an experienced backend developer to join our team in building cutting-edge Data Engineering and Generative AI technology. In this role, you will be responsible for the development of scalable systems that serve our enterprise clients in the HealthTech industry. You'll be working with billions of proprietary data points derived from Wearables, IoT devices, other sensors and will be building microservices infrastructure to deliver this structured data feed and GenAI insights for our clients near-real-time. For the reference, our tech stack is as follows: AWS, Python, Golang, Typescript, Kubernetes, Postgres • Experience working with messaging/queuing systems • Working knowledge of relational databases Good to have: • Experience with Kubernetes and Docker • Experience with AWS or other cloud provider • Experience with systems design • Experience in MLOps • Ability to move fast as it's part of Spike's culture What we offer: • Competitive salary in the range of 4,500 to 8,000 eur gross (and more in exceptional cases) per month depending on your qualifications. • Annual bonus. • Hybrid working model (offices in Vilnius, Lithuania and Palo Alto, CA) • Personal health fund, optical allowance, referral bonus, team events. • Dynamic growth: dive into AI and Data tech, seizing high-growth opportunities with cutting-edge projects. • Talented peers: collaborate with a smart, ambitious team comprising data scientists, PhDs, Cambridge alumni, and tech industry pros (formerly from Bloomberg, Amazon, and Google). • Solid backing: enjoy stability and ample resources for success, supported by market leader Raintree.
