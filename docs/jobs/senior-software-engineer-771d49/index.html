Senior Software Engineer

GetGo Technologies Pte Ltd Singapore, Singapore опубликовано 3 ноября 2026

Гибрид Senior
KubernetesDockerAWSGCPMicroservicesCI/CDKotlinTypeScriptReactAI/LLM
At GetGo, our vision is to build a world where everyone can move around freely and sustainably. As we continue to expand our geographic reach and champion sustainability, we are looking for those ready to join us on a ride in creating a meaningful future of mobility. The Senior Software Engineer owns features end to end across GetGo’s carsharing platform, from resilient backend services on AWS to the web and mobile experiences our members use every day. With a strong technical foundation and comfort across the stack, you’ll drive reliability, performance, and engineering excellence while working with AI-assisted tools to ship faster without compromising quality. Responsibilities • Technical Expertise: Strong proficiency in backend technologies (e.g., Golang, C#) and hands-on experience with microservices architecture and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP), plus working knowledge of at least one frontend or mobile stack (React or Svelte with TypeScript/JavaScript, or Kotlin/Swift). You’re comfortable contributing to client-side code and design APIs with the consumer’s experience in mind.. • AI Literacy: Hands-on experience using AI coding assistants (e.g., Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor) in your daily workflow, paired with the judgment to critically review, test, and own AI-generated code. Familiarity with writing clear specs or prompts that drive AI-assisted development, and awareness of risks like hallucinations, security gaps, and data privacy. Experience integrating LLM APIs into products is a plus. • Problem-Solving: Proven ability to analyze complex technical challenges and implement practical, systemic solutions. • Effective Communication: Ability to articulate complex technical ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Operational Excellence: Solid understanding of Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines to ensure seamless feature deployment. • Domain Knowledge: Intermediate to advanced knowledge of software design patterns and agile development methodologies. • Proven Track Record: 3–5 years of professional software engineering experience, with a portfolio of shipped features or services running in production at meaningful scale. • You care about craft and culture — writing clean, maintainable code, supporting your teammates, and continuously pushing the quality bar higher — in line with our GetGo Values: • Driven by Purpose • Stay Curious and Humble • Collaborate with Empathy • Make it Better • Get It Going! Requirements • Translate PRDs into clear technical specifications and deliver features end to end, writing well-tested, high-quality code (using AI-assisted tools where they add value) and shipping it to production through our CI/CD pipelines. • Own the health, stability, and high availability of your services with minimal guidance, monitoring key metrics, resolving bottlenecks, and proactively driving improvements to performance, scalability, and developer experience. • Collaborate closely with Product, Design, QA, Operations, and other cross-functional partners to shape requirements, clarify trade-offs, and deliver solutions that solve real user and business problems. • Work with Tech Leads and the SRE team to review designs and changes, ensuring alignment with technical specifications and catching issues before they reach production. • Troubleshoot complex technical issues and conduct root cause analysis to implement preventive measures and avoid recurrence. • Mentor junior engineers through technical guidance and code reviews, and document service operations and troubleshooting procedures, raising code quality and spreading knowledge across the engineering team.
