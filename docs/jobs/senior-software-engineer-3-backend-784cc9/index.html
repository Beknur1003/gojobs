We are looking for an experienced Backend Engineer who can take ownership of critical technical challenges and build high-performance, scalable software systems. The ideal candidate is a strong individual contributor who thrives on solving complex engineering problems, contributes to architectural decisions, and helps raise the technical bar across the team. Responsibilities: • Own and deliver critical backend components end-to-end. • Solve complex, high-impact technical challenges. • Collaborate closely with architects on Low-Level Design (LLD) and technical roadmaps. • Re-architect and optimize existing algorithms and implementations. • Drive improvements in system performance, scalability, and reliability. • Participate in and lead code reviews, design discussions, and technical decision-making. • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers, promoting strong engineering practices across the team. Requirements: • Strong backend development experience in Java / Python / Golang / Erlang. • Hands-on experience with microservices and scalable, distributed system architecture. • Solid understanding of REST APIs and third-party integrations. • Strong knowledge of RDBMS, SQL, and database design. • Strong fundamentals in OOP, design patterns, and frameworks. • Proven experience in algorithmic development and complex problem-solving. • Experience designing and implementing non-trivial multi-process / multi-threaded systems. • Strong grasp of version control (e. g., Git) and software engineering best practices. • Prior experience with code reviews, technical mentoring, and design discussions. • Bachelor's or master's degree in computer science, software engineering, or a related field. • Candidates from premier institutes preferred.
