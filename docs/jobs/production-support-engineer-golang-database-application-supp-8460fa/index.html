About Company: Big Oh Notation Pvt. Ltd. are an ISO 27001 & CMMI Level 3 certified IT product and services company specializing in AI-led digital transformation and enterprise software engineering. We are the technology partner for AI-driven products, including Digilawyer, Costimizer.ai, and Botshot.ai, delivering scalable solutions across intelligent automation and applied AI systems. Job Description: Production Support Engineer (Golang, Database & Application Support) Experience: 3–5 Years Location: Noida Employment Type: Full-time Job Summary We are looking for a Production Support Engineer with 3–5 years of experience in application/production support, database management, and troubleshooting. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of Golang, strong SQL/database knowledge, and experience in resolving production issues and ensuring application stability. Roles & Responsibilities • Provide L1/L2 production and application support for business-critical applications. • Monitor application performance, investigate incidents, and troubleshoot production issues. • Write and execute SQL queries to investigate data-related issues and validate database records. • Work with databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, or other relational databases. • Possess basic knowledge of Golang (Go) to understand code, debug issues, and support application troubleshooting. • Analyze application logs, identify root causes, and coordinate with development teams for permanent fixes. • Handle incident management, root cause analysis (RCA), and preventive actions. • Support deployments, application releases, and post-deployment validation. • Maintain incident documentation, support runbooks, and knowledge-base articles. • Work in shifts or participate in on-call support, if required. Required Skills • 3–5 years of experience in Production Support, Application Support, or Technical Support. • Basic knowledge of Golang (Go). • Strong SQL and database troubleshooting skills. • Understanding of Linux/Unix commands and log analysis. • Knowledge of REST APIs, HTTP, and application debugging. • Experience with ticketing and incident management tools. • Good analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills. Preferred Skills • Experience with monitoring tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, or Kibana. • Knowledge of scripting, shell commands, and automation. • Familiarity with Git, CI/CD, and deployment processes. • Understanding of distributed systems and microservices. Ideal Candidate A candidate with strong production support and database troubleshooting experience, along with basic Golang knowledge, who can investigate incidents independently and collaborate with development teams to maintain application reliability.
