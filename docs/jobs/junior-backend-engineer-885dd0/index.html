Junior Backend Engineer

H2 GAMES PTE. LTD. Singapore опубликовано 21 сентября 2026

Junior English C1
MySQLMicroservicesLinuxPythonJavaPHPReact
We are seeking an Entry-level Backend Engineer to join our dynamic team. In this role, you will support the development and maintenance of back-end systems that power our business operations and mobile game platforms. You will work closely with experienced engineers to build reliable, scalable, and high-performance internal systems while growing your technical skills in a fast-paced environment. Job Responsibilities： • Assist in designing, developing, and maintaining internal systems and platforms. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to support integration between back-end systems, front-end platforms, and databases. • Help monitor and improve the performance, scalability, and reliability of back-end systems. • Troubleshoot technical issues and support smooth day-to-day system operations. • Participate in code reviews, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement of development processes. • Prepare and maintain technical documentation for system designs, implementations, and configurations. • Learn and apply best practices in software engineering, coding standards, and system architecture. Job Requirements： • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • 0–2 years of professional experience in software development or internships in a related role. Fresh graduates are encouraged to apply. • Basic programming knowledge in one or more languages such as Java, Golang, Python, or PHP. Experience with Java or Golang is an advantage. • Familiarity with common Internet technologies such as Linux, MySQL, caching technologies, APIs, and basic microservices concepts is a plus. • Understanding of software development fundamentals including clean code practices, version control, and debugging. • Willingness to learn new technologies and continuously improve technical skills. • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving mindset. • Good teamwork and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams. • Proficiency in English and Chinese to communicate with cross-functional teams, including Chinese-speaking counterparts. • Exposure to front-end technologies such as JavaScript, CSS, React, or Vue.js is a plus.
