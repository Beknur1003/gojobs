Senior Software Engineer II (Golang) | Austin, TX (Hybrid) We're working with a high-growth Series B technology company that operates a large, distributed network of connected devices deployed across thousands of locations nationwide, delivering real-time content and data at scale. The role: You'll join the backend team as a hands-on senior engineer, helping build and scale the systems that power the company's real-time device network and content delivery across a large, distributed fleet nationwide - with real input into architecture and technical direction as the platform is consolidated onto a modern Go codebase. You'll: • Design, build, and maintain high-scale backend services in Go • Deliver backend projects end-to-end - from design through to production - improving reliability, performance, and resiliency across distributed systems • Build and evolve the systems that manage content delivery and device coordination across a nationwide network • Design and evolve API-first services supporting both customer-facing and internal applications • Contribute to system architecture and technical design for future initiatives • Partner directly with Product to scope, plan, and execute backend work What we're looking for: • 4+ years of professional software engineering experience • Proficiency in Go, with real experience building and operating high-scale backend systems in production • Solid grasp of distributed systems concepts and trade-offs • Experience building API-first, customer-facing services • Experience working with relational databases and data stores in production • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical degree Package: • Base: $160k - $180k + 5% bonus + equity • Strong health coverage (medical, dental, vision) with employer HSA contribution • Unlimited PTO, annual learning & development stipend • On-site 3x/week in Austin - hands-on, collaborative engineering culture
