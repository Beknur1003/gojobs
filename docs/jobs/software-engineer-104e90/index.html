Meet the Team The Cisco Common Platform Services (CCPS) organization is transforming how Cisco delivers software-as-a-service by providing a modern, consistent, and secure platform for all! With demonstrated ability, our global team brings innovative methodologies to building and managing hybrid cloud environments. We focus on platform management services, continuously evaluating infrastructure and operations to ensure efficiency and precision. Our expertise in IaaS and PaaS, combined with repeatable and portable delivery models, enables the rapid and secure deployment of applications. Within CCPS, the Cloud Software & Solutions (CSS) group designs and develops cloud platform solutions with an emphasis on quality, stability, simplification, user experience, scalability, performance, and portability. We are looking for a senior Operating System Software Engineer to join our team and build scalable cloud-native applications and services. Your Impact • Design and implement robust, high-performance backend services and APIs using Golang. • Define, implement, and automate operating-system security and hardening standards, primarily for Linux environments. • Establish secure OS baselines using recognized standards, and ensure systems remain compliant throughout their lifecycle. • Partner with security and infrastructure teams to manage vulnerabilities, patching, access controls, audit logging, and security remediation. • Troubleshoot complex production issues and improve system availability, observability, performance, and reliability. Minimum Qualifications • 6+ years (BS) or 4+ years (MS) of experience in software development, systems engineering, or cloud/platform engineering roles. • Strong proficiency in Go and experience building scalable, concurrent backend services and APIs. • Deep proficiency in Linux system administration (RHEL/Ubuntu/Win), including advanced shell scripting, performance monitoring, and system-level debugging. • Strong understanding of security principles and proven experience in applying security hardening to production systems. • Experience leveraging AI tools to improve productivity, automate routine tasks, accelerate problem-solving, and enhance engineering quality. Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Python for scripting, automation, or data processing. • Experience with configuration management and infrastructure automation tools such as Ansible, Puppet, Chef, or Terraform. • Knowledge of cloud-native technologies, including Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS or GCP. • Familiarity with SELinux or AppArmor, identity and access management, security scanning, and compliance automation. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, observability platforms, and agile development methodologies.
