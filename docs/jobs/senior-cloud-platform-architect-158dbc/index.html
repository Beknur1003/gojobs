The Role We are hiring a Senior Cloud & Platform Architect to serve as a strategic technical partner to AArete’s US-based Cloud & Infrastructure Lead. This is a senior advisory and hands-on architecture role for someone with deep experience in cloud platform engineering, IT automation, AIOps, and product infrastructure at scale. AArete’s US-based infrastructure lead has extensive knowledge of the company’s existing environment, systems, and healthcare data operations. What we need from this role is a highly experienced partner who brings a wealth of experience in cloud architecture best practices, production-grade automation, AIOps implementation, and product development infrastructure that the team hasn’t had the opportunity to build yet. You will advise, co-design, and build alongside the US lead. This role spans all AArete cloud platforms and future product offerings. You will bring external perspective and depth to an environment that is rapidly evolving from a consulting-driven delivery model to a product-oriented engineering organization. You will work as part of a globally distributed team spanning the US, UK, and India. You will be expected to collaborate daily with US-based engineers, architects, and leadership across time zones.
