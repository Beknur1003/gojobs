AI Agent Engineer

H2 Games Singapore, Singapore опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

English C1
MySQLRedisElasticsearchPythonAI/LLM
Team Introduction At H2 Games, we combine creativity, technology, and data to drive user growth in smarter, more efficient ways. We continuously explore and apply emerging AI technologies to build growth products and scalable platform capabilities that enable cross-functional teams to innovate and iterate faster. You will work closely with product, creative, data, and engineering partners to turn new ideas into practical product capabilities, improve the team's ability to explore and experiment, and create long-term value for business growth. Job Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain AI Agent applications and platforms, focusing on scalability, reliability, and practical business applications. • Build and optimize AI Agent workflows, including task planning, orchestration, tool calling, context management, and integration with internal and external systems. • Develop and maintain backend services and APIs to support AI Agent capabilities and automated business workflows. • Integrate LLM capabilities into existing business systems, translating complex business requirements into practical AI-powered solutions. • Design and implement evaluation, monitoring, and debugging mechanisms to continuously improve Agent performance, accuracy, and reliability. • Collaborate with product, engineering, and other cross-functional teams to explore new AI applications and drive their implementation. • Apply engineering best practices to ensure system performance, code quality, security, and maintainability. Job Requirements • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field. • Strong programming skills in Python or Golang, with solid knowledge of backend development, system architecture, and software engineering best practices. • Hands-on experience designing and developing LLM-based AI Agents, including task orchestration, tool/function calling, and integration with external APIs or services. • Familiarity with AI Agent frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, or similar frameworks. • Solid understanding of LLM application development, including prompt engineering, RAG, context management, and structured outputs. • Experience working with databases, caching systems, and middleware such as MySQL, Redis, message queues, or Elasticsearch. • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to translate business requirements into scalable, reliable, and maintainable technical solutions. • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools and an interest in applying emerging AI technologies to improve development efficiency. • Proficiency in both English and Chinese to facilitate effective communication with Chinese-speaking counterparts.
