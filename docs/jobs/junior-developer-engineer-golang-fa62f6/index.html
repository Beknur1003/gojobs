About the Role: We are hiring a Junior Developer Engineer with 5-6 years of experience in backend development, with a strong focus on Golang (Go). You will be involved in developing scalable services and contributing to all phases of the development lifecycle. Key Responsibilities: • Develop, test, and deploy backend services and APIs primarily using Golang. • Work closely with senior developers and product teams to translate requirements into technical solutions. • Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code. • Participate in code reviews and sprint planning. • Troubleshoot and resolve bugs and performance issues. • Stay updated with the latest industry trends and best practices in Golang development. Required Skills: • Proficiency in Golang (Go) with production-level experience. • Understanding of RESTful APIs, microservices, and concurrent programming. • Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git). • Familiarity with cloud services, preferably AWS or GCP. • Knowledge of SQL and/or NoSQL databases. • Understanding of containerization tools like Docker is a plus. Qualifications: • Bachelors degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field. • 5-6 years of software development experience with at least 2+ years in Golang. • Strong analytical and debugging skills. • Good communication and teamwork abilities.
