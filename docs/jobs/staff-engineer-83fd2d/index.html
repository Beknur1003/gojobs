Shape the technical direction at Lendo – and help build the future of our Product & Tech organization. Do you want to influence both technology and how engineering works across an entire organization? As Staff Engineer at Lendo, you will play a key role in shaping our technical direction, architecture and engineering practices across Product & Tech. Lendo is a successful fintech company with a strong financial position, ambitious plans and a modern Product & Tech organization. We are continuously developing our offering and exploring new products, technologies and ways of creating value for our customers. You will work from our modern office in central Stockholm, where collaboration, great coffee, snacks and a social environment are part of everyday life. The Opportunity • You get the opportunity to influence both technology and the wider Product & Tech organization. • You will work directly with senior stakeholders and be part of the Product & Tech leadership team. • Lendo combines a modern technical environment with an ambitious product and technology roadmap. • Your impact will go beyond writing code – you will help shape architecture, technical standards, engineering capabilities and ways of working. • You will join Lendo at an exciting time, with new products and exciting product initiatives ahead. As Staff Engineer, you will be part of the Product & Tech leadership team, working closely with Engineering Managers, the Head of Technology and other key stakeholders to shape the technical direction across Product & Tech. You will act as a technical compass across our engineering organization, helping teams make sound technical decisions while staying close enough to the engineering work to understand and solve complex challenges. You will: • Own and evolve Lendo´s technical strategy, architecture and engineering standards. • Set technical direction across multiple agile teams and create alignment around architecture and ways of working. • Facilitate our Tech Lead forum and drive cross-team technical initiatives. • Work with Product from an early stage to turn business needs into scalable technical solutions. • Stay hands-on when needed through coding, prototyping and technical problem-solving. • Mentor engineers and act as a technical sounding board across backend and frontend. • Participate in technical skills assessments of engineers, helping ensure a consistent and high technical standard across the organization. • Support teams in areas such as resilience, security, operational risk and major technical incidents. • Work closely with our Data/ML team and explore how AI and modern technologies can create business value. The Team and Workplace The environment is modern, product-driven and forward-looking, with several new products and initiatives on the roadmap. This means you get the opportunity to influence areas that are still evolving, rather than simply stepping into an already established structure. The role sits close to the wider Product & Tech leadership, rather than being limited to one individual development team. You will work closely with senior stakeholders and have the opportunity to influence decisions across the broader technology organization. About You We are looking for a senior engineer with a strong architectural mindset who enjoys solving complex problems and influencing others through technical expertise rather than formal authority. You have: • Extensive experience in software engineering, architecture or a similar senior technical role. • Strong knowledge of modern software architecture and experience working across multiple teams. • Experience with cloud environments, ideally GCP, and technologies such as Kubernetes and Terraform. • Experience with or a strong understanding of Golang, TypeScript and/or React. • The ability to move comfortably between strategic technical discussions and hands-on engineering. • Strong communication skills and the ability to turn complex technical topics into clear decisions and actions. • A pragmatic approach to balancing technical quality, business value and speed. • A genuine interest in AI and modern technology. Experience from a regulated or high-trust environment and/or working closely with Data/ML teams is a plus. Most importantly, you are curious, collaborative and pragmatic. You take ownership, challenge existing solutions when needed and enjoy helping both people and technology move forward. In this recruitment process, we are collaborating with Ada Digital. Therefore, all applications should be submitted via Ada Digital's website. You can easily apply using the button below.
