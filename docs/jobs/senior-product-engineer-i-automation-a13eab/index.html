high-performing, resilient backend services using Go, PHP, AWS, Kubernetes, DynamoDB, PostgreSQL, and MySQL to effortlessly handle millions of users. • Write Exemplary Code: Craft clean, elegant Go code for our newest automation features while actively maintaining and leveling up our core PHP services. • Elevate the Craft: Conduct thoughtful code reviews, lead technical design discussions, write clear architectural design docs, and establish SLOs to keep our incident rates low. • Multiply Team Impact: Mentor and pair with engineers, guiding their career growth, while acting as a trusted partner to Product and Design to shape our product roadmap. • Tackle Complex Challenges: Balance long-term strategic roadmap delivery with Tier-1 customer escalations and technical debt, diving in to troubleshoot mission-critical issues across the stack. 🛠️ Your Superpowers • Backend Mastery: 4-8 years of software engineering experience with deep expertise in Go and performance-driven batch processing systems, ideally paired with working knowledge of PHP. • System Design Genius: Proven track record of designing, building, and operating scalable microservices, distributed systems, and domain-driven architectures at enterprise scale. • Cloud & Infrastructure Expertise: Advanced, hands-on experience with AWS, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, and data stores like DynamoDB,
