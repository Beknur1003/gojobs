JOB DESCRIPTION DevOps Engineer London, UK Role overview We are looking for a DevOps engineer to join our clients technology team with experience building and scaling services in a cloud environment. You will establish configuration management, automate our infrastructure, implement continuous integration, and ensure that members of the technology team are up to speed with DevOps best practices to achieve a continuously deployable system. You will be responsible for all Disaster Recovery and regular system maintenance, as well as performing or coordinating regular security audits, using internal and external resources.
