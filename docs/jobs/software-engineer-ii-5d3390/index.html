What We Are Looking For - Scope of Role We are looking for a talented Software Engineer II to join our global engineering organization. The successful candidate will contribute to the design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of modern software applications and services. This role requires strong software engineering fundamentals, a growth mindset, and the ability to collaborate effectively within cross-functional Agile teams. The Software Engineer II is expected to independently deliver features and components while continuously improving software quality, performance, security, and maintainability. The individual should be comfortable working across backend, cloud-native, and client application environments and demonstrate a willingness to learn emerging technologies and engineering practices What Will You Do - Responsibilities for Employment • Design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software solutions following engineering standards and best practices. • Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and support. • Collaborate with product managers, designers, quality engineers, and other stakeholders to deliver business value. • Develop and maintain RESTful APIs, backend services, and user-facing applications where required. • Implement automated testing, code quality controls, and CI/CD practices. • Troubleshoot and resolve software defects, performance issues, and production incidents. • Contribute to cloud-native deployments and containerized application environments. • Participate in code reviews and continuously improve development practices. • Create and maintain technical documentation. • Additional responsibilities as assigned. Who You Are - Qualifications Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. Years and Type of Experience: 3-6 years of professional software development experience. Experience working with modern programming languages, software frameworks, source control systems, and Agile development methodologies Key Skills, Knowledge & Capabilities: • Proficiency in one or more modern programming languages such as Golang, Java, C#, TypeScript, or similar. • Experience developing APIs, backend services, or modern client applications. • Familiarity with Flutter or comparable frontend/mobile application frameworks. • Understanding of distributed systems and microservices architectures. • Familiarity with relational and NoSQL databases. • Basic understanding of container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Claude Code) and the ability to apply them to improve development productivity and code quality • Exposure to cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud Platform, or Alibaba Cloud. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and DevOps practices. • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities. • Effective verbal and written communication skills. How We Lead the DS Way - Key Leadership Behaviors • Actively articulates and promotes Dentsply Sirona’s vision, mission and values. • Advocates on behalf of the customer. • Promotes high performance, innovation and continual improvement. • Consistently meets Company standards, ethics and compliance requirements. • Clear and effective communication with stake holders, which span across multiple levels, socio-geographic areas and functional expertise.
