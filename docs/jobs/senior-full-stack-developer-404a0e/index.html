About Venn Venn is a fast-growing technology, building the intelligent operating layer for modern residential living. Our platform helps leading U.S. real estate operators connect residents, property teams, payments, services, maintenance, and community into one seamless product experience. We’re now taking Venn to the next level by turning each partner’s data, knowledge, and way of operating into a Company Brain, and building tailored applications and AI agents on top of it. We work at the intersection of PropTech, AI, data, and real-world operational systems, with one clear promise- help operators serve millions of residents with the care they would give to a few close friends. Our current tech arsenal includes: Go, AWS, Kubernetes, Jenkins, React Native (Expo), React.js, Temporal, SNS, Kinesis, S3, Snowflake, AWS Bedrock, and many more tools that help us build exceptional products. About the role We are looking for developers who understand and believe in software engineering best practices (design, clean code, testing, code reviews, automation, etc) and who are also aware of bad practices and anti-patterns. The must • 7+ years of programming experience, using Golang, TypeScript, React • Proven experience in developing web applications • Strong analytical thinking, systematic problem-solving skills • Have practiced Test-Driven Development and/or Continuous Integration & Delivery The nice • Experience in other languages and frameworks such as React native and GraphQL • Worked on numerous projects in diverse business domains • Experienced in cloud environment development • Degree in computer science or equivalent • Have contributed to, or managed an open-source project Venn is an equal opportunity workplace. We believe in the strength of inclusive communities and thrive to build a work environment that mirrors the diversity of the cities that we love.
