Reporting Lines: This role is part of EBB Technology Team and will report into the Engineering Manager under Payment Acceptance Technology. Roles & Responsibilities: • Own hands-on engineering delivery end-to-end, with direct accountability for designing, coding, testing, deploying and operating production-grade software. • Design, build and maintain scalable services, APIs, microservices, integrations, automation and reusable engineering capabilities, writing high-quality production code. • Leverage AI coding tools, copilots and engineering agents across development, refactoring, testing, debugging and code reviews to improve engineering productivity and quality. • Build and integrate AI and Agentic AI capabilities, including LLM-powered services, RAG, AI agents, tool/function calling and agentic workflows. • Engineer data and integration solutions, including APIs, event-driven integrations, data transformations and pipelines connecting applications, cloud, infrastructure, security, risk, asset and operational data. • Build and operate cloud-native solutions, preferably on AWS, applying Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and DevSecOps practices using technologies such as Terraform or CloudFormation. • Own functional and non-functional testing, including unit, integration, end-to-end, performance, resilience and security testing, with a strong focus on test automation and quality engineering. • Apply SRE and production engineering practices, including observability, monitoring, reliability, performance optimisation, incident management and hands-on production troubleshooting. Essential Skills: • 8–12 years of hands-on software engineering experience, with a strong track record of designing, coding, testing and delivering complex enterprise software and technology transformation initiatives. • Hands-on programming expertise in Java, Golang, Python, TypeScript/JavaScript or similar, writing high-quality, maintainable and production-grade code. • Proven experience building scalable distributed systems, including APIs, microservices, serverless and event-driven solutions, with direct ownership from design through production. • Strong hands-on use of AI coding tools, copilots and engineering agents to accelerate coding, refactoring, testing, debugging, code reviews and developer productivity. • Experience coding and integrating AI and Agentic AI capabilities, including LLMs, RAG, AI agents, tool/function calling and agentic workflows into applications.
