Buoyant | Senior Software Engineers – Kubernetes Control Plane (Go/Rust) & Rust Data Plane / Networking | REMOTE – US / Canada / South America / Central Europe | Full-time Buoyant is the company behind Linkerd, the CNCF-graduated service mesh for Kubernetes. We're a small, fully remote engineering team building a new cloud-native infrastructure product and tackling challenging problems in distributed systems, Kubernetes, networking, and systems programming. We're hiring for senior engineering positions: 1. Senior Software Engineer – Kubernetes Control Plane (Go/Rust) We're looking for engineers who have personally designed and built production Kubernetes controllers/operators, including CRDs, reconciliation loops, and Kubernetes API integrations. Strong Go and/or Rust experience required. Experience with Kubebuilder, controller-runtime, client-go, kube-rs, admission webhooks, Gateway API, or Kubernetes-native commercial products is particularly relevant. Apply: tally.so/r/dbN9bD 2. Senior Software Engineer – Rust Data Plane / Networking We're looking for engineers with hands-on experience building production networking software that processes live traffic, including proxies, gateways, load balancers, or other L4/L7 traffic-handling systems. Strong systems programming skills in Rust or C/C++ required, with production Rust highly preferred. Experience with Tokio, TCP/IP, HTTP/2, gRPC, TLS/mTLS, asynchronous networking, concurrency, and performance optimization is valuable. Apply: tally.so/r/RWd5Vj Both are hands-on engineering opportunities with significant architectural ownership, working directly with a small, highly technical team. Location & Working Hours Fully remote — United States, Canada, South America, or Central Europe. Candidates in the Americas must maintain at least five hours of daily overlap with Pacific Time. Central European candidates must accommodate agreed collaboration hours with the U.S.-based engineering team. Buoyant is not offering immigration sponsorship for these roles. Candidates in South America or Central Europe may be considered under company-approved employment or independent-contractor arrangements, subject to country-specific eligibility and approval.
