This is a UK-based role. You will be required to interview in person. About SailPoint SailPoint is defining the next era of identity security. Our unified, AI-powered platform seamlessly secures both the Human Fabric of your workforce and the Agentic Fabric of AI and non-human identities, completely eliminating organizational blind spots. Built on core principles like zero-standing privilege and contextualized risk, our platform transforms identity from a vulnerability into a strategic business advantage. Today, we are trusted by some of the largest, most complex organizations in the world to deliver intelligent, autonomous identity security at scale. About the role As a Software Engineer on the Platform Engineering team, you will design, build, and run mission-critical platform services that enable applications to define policies, make real-time decisions, and react dynamically to changes across millions of identities in our Identity Graph. In a typical day, you will write high-performance Golang code, develop robust full-stack features using Angular on the frontend, and implement resilient microservices deployed on AWS. You will collaborate closely with distributed team members, partner with data scientists on AI-driven features, and optimize distributed cloud architectures for low-millisecond latency. About the team You will join one of our UK Platform Engineering teams dedicated to designing, building, and operating core services for SailPoint's Identity Security Cloud. We are a collaborative group of backend and UI specialists transitioning to full-stack feature ownership. We value knowledge sharing, high code quality, and regular collaboration, meeting in person once per quarter for a one- to two-day team offsite. Roadmap for success 30 days: • Complete SailPoint engineering onboarding, configure your development environment, and obtain repository access. • Review architectural documentation for the Identity Graph and policy engine to understand domain concepts and platform standards. • Shadow teammates during sprint ceremonies, design discussions, and code reviews. • Deliver and merge your first Golang pull request with comprehensive unit tests. 60 days: • Build and deploy your first end-to-end microservice feature within the policy engine. • Write robust unit and integration tests adhering to platform code coverage standards. • Participate actively in peer code reviews, offering actionable feedback. • Familiarize yourself with monitoring dashboards, alerting systems, and on-call runbooks. 6 months: • Independently deliver scalable, full-stack platform features using Golang and Angular. • Partner with the Data Science team to integrate AI-related features into policy decision workflows. • Join the primary 24x7 on-call rotation (approximately one week in 20) to support platform availability and reliability. • Identify and implement automated CI/CD pipeline improvements to accelerate release cycles. 1 year: • Lead technical design and implementation for high-throughput, low-latency microservices handling millions of identity access requests. • Mentor team members and champion engineering best practices across microservices architecture and cloud infrastructure. • Drive performance tuning initiatives across distributed cloud databases such as DynamoDB, Aurora, or Snowflake. • Contribute to architectural roadmaps and technical strategy for core platform initiatives. • Requirements • Bachelors degree in related field, or equivalent professional experience • 2+ years of professional software development experience • Proven backend development experience with Golang building scalable microservices and SaaS solutions • Frontend experience with Angular or modern JavaScript/TypeScript frameworks • Strong understanding of automated testing practices, including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing, along with debugging proficiencies • Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS) and distributed cloud data stores (e.g., DynamoDB, Aurora, Snowflake) • Experience working with CI/CD tools and pipelines such as Jenkins or CloudBees • Ability to participate in a
