About GreedyGame GreedyGame powers app and game monetization and growth through two lines of business: PubScale, our platform for publishers -a single dashboard spanning ad monetization (AdX, Immersive Ads, Offerwall), user acquisition (Grow), analytics (Insights), and in-app gaming (Plug); and our own B2C consumer apps (Incent Apps, House of Apps) that put reward and offer-based experiences directly in front of millions of users. You'll work at the inyersection of ad- tech, real-time data and consumer- scale traffic About the Role We're looking for a hands-on Tech Lead to own backend architecture across our platform and consumer products. This is a role for someone ready to move beyond owning individual services and take ownership of systems, architecture, people, and technical decisions. You'll lead a team of 4–10 engineers while remaining deeply hands-on, and work directly with Product and founders to shape what we build and how we scale it. What You'll Solve & Own • Build and scale backend systems handling billions of requests and events per day. • Design real-time or near-real-time pipelines for ads, clicks, conversions, offers, rewards, and analytics. • Solve complex problems around scale, performance, reliability and data consistency, across B2B and B2C products. • Own architectural decisions across services, databases, caching, queues, and infrastructure. • Build and evolve backend services using Go and Kubernetes. • Lead a team of 4–10 engineers through technical direction, mentoring, and code reviews while staying hands-on. • Drive engineering quality through code reviews, observability, CI/CD, and production ownership. • Identify and drive meaningful adoption of AI-assisted development practices across the team. What We're Looking For • 6–10 years of software engineering experience with strong backend expertise. • Deep hands-on experience with Go/Golang; working knowledge of React. • Experience technically leading 4–10 engineers, while remaining hands-on. • Proven track record of building and scaling applications with significant traffic or user volume. • Strong system design fundamentals and experience with high-throughput, real-time, or event-driven systems. • Strong ownership, product thinking, and ability to make pragmatic technical decisions. • Regular user of AI-assisted development tools. • Startup or fast-paced product-company experience preferred. Good to Have • Ad-tech, programmatic advertising, app monetization, or rewards experience. • AWS/GCP, observability, and high-volume event/analytics systems. • Experience building both B2B platforms and B2C products.
