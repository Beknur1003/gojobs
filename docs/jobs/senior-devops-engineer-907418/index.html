Project description The team is responsible for building in-house platform between trade process systems, You will be working with highly skilled engineers on Web Services (APIs), Trade processing system, FM FX, Derivatives etc. domain. Micro service and Devops and get to work in modern software development environments and practices. As part of the FMTPT team, you will have an opportunity to contribute to the evolving technical direction of the team as we adapt to this rapidly changing space. As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will focus on automated deployments and Server Environment and participate into multiple agile software delivery teams to deliver the business value to the Business. You will understand the different technology silos within the Bank and how to interface with them. You will be part of the team that constantly analyse and improve the Murex Trade processing platform from a technical point of view as well as from user experience view. You'll keep yourself updated with the latest software technologies and sharing the knowledge to team.
