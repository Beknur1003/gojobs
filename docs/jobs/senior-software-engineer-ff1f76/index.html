Overview We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer with strong expertise in Golang, cloud-native application development, and AWS-based solutions to support Mastercard's Cloud Connect initiatives. The role will focus on building secure, scalable connectivity platforms that enable customers to connect to Mastercard services from public cloud environments and private networks through modern cloud-native architectures. Role • Design, develop, and maintain secure, scalable, and resilient cloud-native applications and services using Golang and AWS technologies to support Mastercard's Cloud Connect initiatives. • Collaborate cross-functionally with product, architecture, engineering, and operations teams to design scalable solutions, systems, APIs, and databases that meet functional and non-functional requirements. • Contribute to software architecture, technical design reviews, and architectural decision-making, ensuring solutions are maintainable, performant, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards. • Design and optimize data structures, algorithms, and processing patterns to support high-volume distributed systems and efficient resource utilization. • Participate in the complete software development lifecycle, including requirements analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, monitoring, and ongoing support. • Design, develop, and maintain automated unit, functional, and integration tests while promoting test-driven development practices. • Conduct code reviews and provide technical mentorship to engineering team members, promoting coding standards, software quality, maintainability, and engineering best practices. • Apply secure coding principles throughout the software development lifecycle, proactively identifying and remediating vulnerabilities through code reviews, security testing, and adherence to Mastercard security standards. • Contribute to operational excellence by implementing observability, monitoring, logging, alerting, and reliability best practices for cloud-native services. • Perform root cause analysis, troubleshoot complex production issues, and lead performance tuning efforts to improve system reliability, scalability, and efficiency. • Work with business partners and stakeholders to understand business needs and translate requirements into technical solutions.
