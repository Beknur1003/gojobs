Senior Software Developer, Cerberus

Гибрид Senior
KafkaGCPReactAI/LLM
As a Senior Software Developer, you will spearhead ground breaking innovation and foster a culture of excellence in AI-driven engineering on our facial recognition and identity platform. The Cerberus real-time identity platform is a cornerstone of our global success, driving essential identity, compliance, and operational functions within highly regulated jurisdictions. The platform leverages cutting-edge AI technology to deliver robust and scalable solutions. The engineering team designs and implements robust facial recognition and identity systems. It works in a full-stack environment to support global expansion, ensuring high-quality software delivery. This role sets the standard for a talented group of developers to innovate and excel in delivering reliable solutions. It embraces an AI-first culture and champions the adoption of next-generation tooling and image analysis to accelerate delivery and enhance code quality. This role is eligible for inclusion in the Company’s hybrid work from home policy. • Expert knowledge of Kafka, SQL, .Net, Golang or React. • Proven experience building highly scalable systems, preferably in the cloud (GCP valued). • Proficiency in developing customer-centric front-end solutions. • Exposure to NoSQL datastores. • Leadership experience within development teams and technical project delivery. • Capability in technical leadership, mentorship, pairing opportunities, and code reviews. • Strong technical documentation skills. • Deep understanding of large-scale, highly available, multiuser, service-based systems. • Familiarity with on-call rotations and incident response. • Act as a point of escalation for the team both in and out of office hours. • Lead the development of scalable, maintainable, and performant systems. • Apply active governance around overall solution design and code quality. • Ensure solutions meet the needs of the Business. • Manage work estimation and the production of solution documents. • Maintain a deep understanding of the technical structure, domain, and architecture of the systems. • Guide a small team of developers in the design and development of solutions. • Own technical solutions and their hands-on delivery from development to live. • Promote knowledge sharing and mentor team members.
