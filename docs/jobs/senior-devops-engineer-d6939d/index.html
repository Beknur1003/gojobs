Requirements • We're looking for candidates with 5+ years of hands-on experience as a DevOps / SRE and deep expertise in the following technologies: • Linux: Advanced administration and OS-level tuning — real-time kernels, process scheduling, memory management, cgroups, CPU isolation, and I/O optimization. • Cloud platforms: Understanding of and experience in building complex systems focused on high availability (HA), low latency and high-performance computing (HPC) • Networking: Deep understanding of TCP/IP, UDP, multicast, Ethernet, VLANs, and PTP at the wire level. Experience with low-latency network configurations and familiarity with kernel bypass technologies (DPDK, RDMA) is a strong plus. • Containerization: Deep-dive knowledge of Docker and Kubernetes, including performance trade-offs of containerized workloads and low-overhead deployment patterns. • IaC: Proven experience with Ansible, Terraform, and Helm in production environments. • Hardware Performance: Working knowledge of how CPUs, NIC queues, memory, and storage behave under pressure. Hands-on experience with hardware tuning (BIOS configs, CPU isolation strategies, NIC queue management). • Monitoring: Practical experience with the Prometheus / Grafana stack; ability to build latency-aware dashboards and fire meaningful alerts rather than noise. • Scripting: Proven track record writing production scripts in Bash and Python. Golang is a significant plus. • CI/CD: Strong grasp of core CI/CD tools and experience with secret management using Vault. • Security: Understanding of DevSecOps principles and hands-on experience configuring security policies in Kubernetes (Zero Trust, RBAC, secure code delivery). • Databases: Solid working knowledge of SQL and/or NoSQL databases under operational conditions. • On-Call & Incident Response: High sense of ownership for production systems — willing to participate in on-call rotations and available to respond to critical incidents outside business hours when the situation demands. • Extra Credit / Big Pluses • Linux: Deep-dive into kernel-level optimization — from syscalls and network card tuning to DPDK or FPGA-based networking. • Proven experience operating High Availability / Ultra-Low Latency solutions in live trading environments. • Experience with hybrid infrastructures (on-premise + cloud) — colocations, cross-connects, and proximity hosting. • Background in building and operating Big Data and Data Analytics platforms. • Understanding of business processes in the FinTech / Banking / Crypto / HFT sector. • Proven track record as an SRE with formal SLO / error budget frameworks. • DevSecOps: Vulnerability detection, enforcing security best practices, and experience with scanning tools (Trivy or Snyk). • Blockchain / crypto-native background is a bonus. Responsibilities • Hybrid Infrastructure (Baremetal and Clouds): Administer Linux servers (Ubuntu, CentOS, RHEL), drive incident resolution, and take end-to-end ownership of system reliability. • Low-Latency Systems Operations: Proactively monitor, troubleshoot, and tune latency-sensitive trading systems and exchange connections across multiple asset classes. You treat P99 latency as a first-class metric. • OS & Hardware Tuning: Go deep on the Linux stack — CPU pinning, NUMA topology, IRQ affinity, kernel parameter tuning, NIC offloading, and BIOS-level configurations. If it affects the tick-to-trade path, you own it. • Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Automate deployment and configuration using Ansible, Terraform, and Helm with performance reproducibility as a hard requirement. • CI/CD: Build and maintain rock-solid CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI, Jenkins, ArgoCD) including automated performance regression checks. • Monitoring & Observability: Design and operate comprehensive monitoring and logging stacks (Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics, Grafana, ELK). Build latency-aware dashboards and alerting — P99/P99.9, jitter, throughput, and error rates. • High Availability & DRP: Design and implement architectures adhering to HA, scalability, and Disaster Recovery principles — zero regressions while continuously reducing latency. • Cross-Team Collaboration: Partner with quant, trading, and network engineering teams to squeeze every drop of performance from the full stack — from hardware up to application logic.
