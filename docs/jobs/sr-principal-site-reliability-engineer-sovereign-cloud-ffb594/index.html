Job Summary Your Career Palo Alto Networks runs a large infrastructure and is one of the largest GCP customers. As a Senior Principal SRE and DevOps Engineer for the CDL/SLS team, you will be part of a team supporting the services running on this infrastructure supporting the Sovereign Cloud. Our Infrastructure Platform stack includes Terraform, Kubernetes, GitLab CI/CD, GitOps, Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Docker, GCP, Vault, Kafka, MySQL, Python, Bash, and Go.
