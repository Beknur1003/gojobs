About the role We are looking for a Golang Backend Engineer to join a company and contribute to the development of a modern, scalable software product. You will be responsible for building and maintaining backend services, designing APIs, and working with cloud infrastructure. The role involves developing reliable, high-performance systems and contributing to technical architecture and engineering decisions. Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain backend services using Golang. • Build and integrate RESTful APIs and third-party services. • Develop scalable and high-performance backend solutions. • Work with cloud infrastructure and containerized applications. • Contribute to system architecture and performance optimization. • Work with Kubernetes and Helm for application deployment and management. • Participate in CI/CD processes and improve development workflows. • Collaborate with the team to deliver reliable and maintainable software. Requirements: • Strong commercial experience with Golang. • Solid understanding of backend development principles and software architecture. • Experience designing and developing RESTful APIs. • Hands-on experience with at least one cloud platform: AWS, GCP, or Azure. • Experience with Kubernetes and Helm. • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes. • Good knowledge of databases, data structures, and software development best practices. Nice to have: • Experience with Python. • Familiarity with React.js or Vue.js. • Experience with AI-powered solutions and data processing. • Knowledge of microservices and distributed systems architecture. If you enjoy building scalable backend systems, solving complex engineering challenges, and working with modern technologies, we'd love to hear from you!
