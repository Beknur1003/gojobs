Do Work That Matters Be part of the team driving enterprise-wide transformation at CommBank. Corporate Technology delivers the modern platforms, enterprise data and critical systems that power the bank’s Operations and Corporate functions. You’ll collaborate across diverse teams, solve complex technology challenges and build your career while shaping the future of one of Australia’s leading organisations. As a Staff Software Engineer, you’ll provide hands-on technical leadership within the Complex Investigations group, helping develop and evolve the technology that supports economic crime investigations. You’ll work across the software engineering lifecycle, from solution design and development through to testing, deployment and production operations. Bring your ambition and we’ll help you grow, belong and shape what’s next through meaningful work, technical leadership and opportunities to make an impact at scale. See yourself in our team You’ll join a collaborative engineering environment focused on building high-quality technology and raising standards across teams. The Complex Investigations and Assisted Investigations crews work with a shared application landscape, with this position providing full-stack engineering capability to the Complex Investigations group.
