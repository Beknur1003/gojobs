About the role Join the Performance Solutions and Engineering team to improve the speed, efficiency, and reliability of Splunk. You’ll design and build performant solutions, automation tools, and benchmarks that help teams find bottlenecks, catch regressions, and measure customer impact. You’ll work on scoped features and collaborate with experienced engineers and cross-functional partners to deliver the engineering roadmap. What you’ll do • Design, implement, test, and deploy focused performance improvements in Splunk products. • Lead small to medium features from technical design through delivery. • Run benchmarks and analyze results to identify bottlenecks, regressions, and opportunities for improvement. • Support investigations using profiling, flame graphs, and other performance tools. • Build or improve Python scripts, automation tools, and benchmark tooling. • Contribute tests and performance checks to CI workflows. • Troubleshoot production issues and take part in root cause analysis and postmortems. • Document findings, communicate technical approaches, and share knowledge through presentations and code reviews. • Bring forward ideas that address customer challenges and help track whether delivered improvements are being adopted. Minimum qualifications • Bachelor’s degree and 5 years of related experience, or a master’s degree and 3 years of related experience, or a PhD. • Experience with Python, C++ and/or Golang • Experience with data structures, memory use, and basic concurrency. • Experience running tests, interpreting results, and explain findings clearly. Preferred qualifications • Experience leading projects with limited complexity and contributing to software engineering roadmaps. • Strong understanding of Agile engineering practices and ways to improve the software development lifecycle. • Experience with secure coding principles and threat modeling in production environments. • Ability to coordinate project timelines and communicate technical specifications to stakeholders. • Exposure to profiling or benchmarking tools such as perf, eBPF, or flame graphs. • Experience with distributed systems, search, indexing, caching, or storage. • Familiarity with AWS, Git, CI, Docker, Kubernetes, or cloud platforms. • Experience mentoring peers or interns. • Experience using AI coding tools and validating their output with tests and measurements.
