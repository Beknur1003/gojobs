We are looking for an experienced Senior Go Developer to design, build, and scale our next-generation slot engine from the ground up under high-load conditions (10k+ RPS). Responsibilities • Design and develop a high-performance, scalable slot engine from scratch • Ensure mathematical precision, RNG integration, and absolute data consistency • Analyze and refactor existing Node.js modules, with potential migration to Go • Optimize and maintain high-load services handling 10k+ requests per second • Work with PostgreSQL partitioning, Redis state management, and messaging systems • Collaborate directly with the CTO and core engineering team • Contribute to architectural decisions and technical strategy Requirements • 5+ years of backend development experience • Strong expertise in Go and deep understanding of concurrency and performance • Experience working with high-load distributed systems • Experience with PostgreSQL, including complex transactions and optimization • Experience with Redis and message brokers (RabbitMQ or similar) • Strong understanding of ACID principles and data consistency • Experience working with legacy systems and refactoring is a strong advantage • Ability to work independently in a remote environment Nice to Have • Domain experience in iGaming (slot engine mechanics, math models, RTP calculations, or payout logic). • Prior experience executing tech stack migrations (e.g., Node.js to Go). • Familiarity with ClickHouse, Docker, Kubernetes (K8s), and AWS infrastructure. Tech Stack • Backend: Go 1.20+ • Legacy/Migration: Node.js • Storage & Cache: PostgreSQL (Partitioning, ACID), Redis, ClickHouse • Infrastructure & Messaging: RabbitMQ, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS What we offer • Fully remote format • Opportunity to build new game products from scratch • Direct collaboration with product stakeholders and decision-makers • Fast decision-making and startup-like environment • A chance to influence the future portfolio of instant and crash games Please note that feedback on your application will be provided within two weeks if a positive decision is made regarding your candidacy.
