Senior Developers (2 Positions) | 12-Month Contract + Extension | Hybrid • 12-month contract with an additional 12-month extension option • Locations: Brisbane, Adelaide, Canberra (Deakin), Geelong, Richmond or Sydney (Surry Hills) • Hybrid working arrangements - minimum 3 days onsite per week • Australian Citizenship required - must hold or be eligible to obtain a Baseline security clearance • Opportunity to work with modern API integration technologies, including GraphQL, GoLang, REST APIs and enterprise integration platforms We are seeking two experienced Senior Integration Developers to join a large federal government agency, contributing to the development and enhancement of a major API integration platform supporting critical digital services. Working within a collaborative, multidisciplinary Agile team, you will play a key role in designing, developing and implementing secure, scalable integration solutions that connect internal and external systems. This is an excellent opportunity to work with modern integration technologies, contribute to a significant digital transformation initiative and deliver solutions that improve customer experiences. Your duties will include: • Design, develop and implement secure, scalable APIs and integration solutions across complex enterprise environments. • Contribute to the development and enhancement of a major API integration platform. • Develop and maintain integrations between internal and external systems using RESTful services and web services. • Work collaboratively within multidisciplinary Scrum teams, using Jira and Confluence to support Agile delivery. • Prepare and maintain technical specifications, system design documentation and integration requirements. • Collaborate with development, testing and operations teams to ensure reliable solution delivery. • Support CI/CD pipelines, source control processes and DevOps practices to improve development and deployment efficiency. Skills and experience we are looking for: • Strong experience with modern integration architectures and enterprise platforms such as Kong, Workato, Boomi, MuleSoft, Salesforce or comparable technologies. • Demonstrated experience designing and developing integration solutions using GraphQL, GoLang and RESTful services. • Experience building secure, scalable and reliable APIs across complex technology environments. • Hands-on experience with source control tools such as GitLab and package-based development. • Proven experience implementing and supporting CI/CD pipelines. • Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical audiences. • Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively within cross-functional Agile delivery teams. Highly desirable: • Experience using Atlassian Jira, VS Code and modern DevOps practices. • Understanding of accessibility principles and a strong interest in accessible application design and development. • Ability to manage competing priorities and adapt effectively within a fast-paced delivery environment. Application Process This role requires candidates to be Australian Citizens who hold or are eligible to obtain an Australian Government Baseline security clearance.
