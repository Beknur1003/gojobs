What They're Looking For • Strong production experience with Go, TypeScript, and React. • Experience building WebSocket-based or real-time applications. • Solid understanding of distributed systems, API architecture, and PostgreSQL. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, AWS, and production deployments. • Engineers comfortable owning systems end to end, from development through deployment and monitoring. Nice to Have • WebRTC, video streaming, or real-time communication infrastructure. • Robotics, ROS, or 3D visualization. • AI/LLM application development. • Experience supporting fleets of connected devices. If you're a full-stack engineer who enjoys working across complex systems and wants to build software powering the next generation of robotics, I'd love to connect.
