StackAdapt is the leading technology company that empowers marketers to reach, engage, and convert audiences with precision. With 465 billion automated optimizations per second, the AI-powered StackAdapt Marketing Platform seamlessly connects brand and performance marketing to drive measurable results across the entire customer journey. The most forward-thinking marketers choose StackAdapt to orchestrate high-impact campaigns across programmatic advertising and marketing channels. About the Team Activations is part of StackAdapt's Data Hub product, our customer data platform. Advertisers bring their first-party data into Data Hub: CRM lists, website and app events, product catalogs and store locations. Activations turn that data into something they can use. We build audiences, keep them in sync with the channels advertisers buy on, and feed products and locations into dynamic creative. We are a small team of senior and mid-level engineers. We work mostly in Go, on event-driven services that run at large scale, using Kafka, gRPC and TiDB on AWS. Advertisers spend against the audiences we build, and privacy law shapes how we handle their data, so correctness matters as much as speed. The team runs flat. Senior engineers lead projects from design to launch. You will set the technical standard those project leads work to: how we design, review and ship. You will help every engineer on the team get better at it, mostly by building alongside them. You will work closely with our Data Hub architect, who sets technical direction across the whole domain and decides designs that cross Data Hub teams. You decide designs inside Activations, and the two of you work together on the ones that cross the line. You also own the technical calls the team makes day to day, and the case for changing direction when what we learn in production says we should. What You'll Be Doing • Owning the technical health of the team's systems. You will own audience evaluation, CRM and programmatic audience syncs, and the product and location feeds behind dynamic creative. You will decide where reliability, cost and tech debt work goes, and make the case for it on the roadmap. • Building. You will write production code every week and take on the hardest work yourself: how we evaluate audiences at scale, how we keep long-running syncs reliable, and how activation grows beyond email to new identifiers. • Setting the review bar. A growing share of our code is written with AI coding agents. You will define what a good review looks like when an agent wrote the first draft, review the risky changes yourself, and teach the team to catch what agents miss: concurrency, data correctness, idempotency, privacy. • Coaching engineers. You will help senior engineers lead their own projects, and help mid-level engineers grow into senior ones. Expect to do this through design reviews, pairing and code reviews that teach. • Making the team faster with AI, carefully. You will find where AI tools save real time for us, such as review assistants, test generation, runbooks and on-call triage. You will build or adopt those tools, measure whether they work, and drop the ones that don't. • Turning product needs into technical plans. You will work with Product and the engineering teams around us to shape multi-month plans. You will find unknowns and cross-team dependencies early, and explain trade-offs in plain language. • Leading on reliability. You will take part in the on-call rotation, lead root cause analysis for team incidents, and make sure the fixes hold. You will set the team's standards for testing, observability and alerting. What You'll Bring to the Table • 8+ years building backend systems, with several years as the engineer others turned to for design decisions on a team that owns production services. • Strong Go, or deep experience in another backend language and a willingness to work in Go every day. • Experience designing and running distributed, data-heavy systems: event streaming (Kafka or similar), workflow orchestration, high-volume batch or streaming pipelines, and SQL/NewSQL stores at scale. • Has led a multi-month project across teams, from design to production. • Has led incident response and root cause analysis for production services. • A track record of raising a team's engineering standards beyond code review, through artifacts others adopted: design docs, review guidelines, shared libraries, CI checks, runbooks. • Real experience with AI coding tools: you use them to build, you know where they fail, and you have helped others use them well. • Evidence that engineers grew because of you, through mentoring, sponsorship, or by stepping back so someone else could lead. • Clear writing. Most of our decisions are made in design docs and pull requests. • Good judgment about trade-offs: when to ship the simple thing, when to pay down debt, and when to push back. • Bonus: Ad tech, marketing technology or customer data platforms (identity resolution, audience segmentation, CRM integrations). • Bonus: Privacy and data deletion compliance (CCPA/CPRA, data broker deletion requests, GDPR). • Bonus: Temporal, gRPC/protobuf, TiDB or Vitess.
