The Engineering and Technology team is at the core of the Shopee platform development. The team is made up of a group of passionate engineers from all over the world, striving to build the best systems with the most suitable technologies. Our engineers do not merely solve problems at hand; We build foundations for a long-lasting future. We don't limit ourselves on what we can or can't do; we take matters into our own hands even if it means drilling down to the bottom layer of the computing platform. Shopee's hyper-growing business scale has transformed most "innocent" problems into huge technical challenges, and there is no better place to experience it first-hand if you love technologies as much as we do. Job Description: • Lead end-to-end architecture design and implementation of critical Marketplace Order Operations systems, including return/refund flows, clearing and settlement pipelines, and seller operation capabilities, ensuring performance, scalability, and fault tolerance. • Drive platform-level modularization and codebase unification, abstracting common modules such as scheduling frameworks, notification services, rule engines, and database access layers to improve delivery speed and maintainability across business lines. • Own strategic technical roadmaps, aligning architecture decisions with business goals, and guide the team through large-scale projects from inception to delivery. • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Operations, SRE, and Data teams to deliver reliable, high-availability services in multi-region deployments. • Champion performance optimization through large-scale concurrency handling, caching strategies, async processing, and database performance tuning. • Evaluate and integrate emerging technologies to enhance development efficiency, system resilience, and cost-effectiveness. Requirements: • Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science or related field • At least 7 years of backend development experience in large-scale, business-critical systems; at least 3 years in architecture leadership roles. • Strong hands-on expertise in Golang, Java, C++, with solid knowledge of distributed systems, microservice orchestration, and event-driven architecture. • Proven track record of building complex systems from 0 to 1, including seller platforms, campaign management systems, rule-based recommendation logic, and governance tools. • Familiar with MySQL, Redis, Kafka, Kubernetes, gRPC, Protocol Buffers and performance tuning in high-concurrency environments. • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills; able to independently own critical modules and drive cross-team architectural initiatives. • Excellent communication and leadership abilities; capable of guiding diverse teams and managing cross-regional, cross-product-line projects. • Experience in e-commerce, ads platforms, or high-throughput transaction systems is a strong plus. Preferred Qualifications • Background in performance-critical, high-availability systems with multi-market deployments • Deep expertise in Domain-Driven Design and applying it to complex business logic and microservice boundaries. • Experience integrating AI or large language models to automate integration workflows or improve developer tooling. • Contributions to engineering process improvements, including CI/CD pipeline optimization, developer tooling, and operational monitoring frameworks.
