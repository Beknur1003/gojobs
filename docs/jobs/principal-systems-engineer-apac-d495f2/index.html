Principal Systems Engineer – APAC What You'll Be Doing This role will be responsible for delivery actives as follows: • Managing Bare Metal Compute Lifecycle States • Inventorying Racks, Hosts, and Components within the hosts (NICs, SSDs, GPUs, etc) • Applying and Validating Firmware of the hosts • Running Stress test on the host networking and host components • Reading and Applying BIOS settings • Installing and Managing Host Operating Systems via remote connection • Managing & Operating Host OOB management subsystems. • Reading and parsing host telemetry About You • Masters/ Bachelor Degree in Computer Science/Technology or equivalent • At least 10+ years of relevant experience in Network and System; experience in Coding. Experience in delivering and operating large-scale production system (e.g. 1000+ servers instances) • Deep understanding of operating systems, computer networks and high-performance applications • Proficient in at least 3 programming languages (Java / Python / C / C++/ GoLang/ Shell scripting • Systematic problem-solving approach, strong communication skills, a sense of ownership, and drive • Proven ability to deliver products and experience with the full software development lifecycle • Must be able to travel up to 50% of the time Technical Experience • Expertise in Linux systems • Familiarity with system-level architecture, data synchronization, fault tolerance, and state management • General enterprise storage, networking, or computing experience • Experience with Server/GPU hardware architecture and system management • Experience with Infiniband or RoCE networking • Hands-on experience designing, developing, and operating public cloud service data planes • Good understanding of databases and SQL (MySQL) and caching technologies (Redis, Memcache, etc.) In All We Do, Our Core Values Guide Us Relentless Innovation At Nscale, we constantly push the boundaries of innovation, embracing creative risks to shape the future.
