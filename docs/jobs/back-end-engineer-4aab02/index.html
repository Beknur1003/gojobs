Company Description Plaee is a technology partner for Prediction Markets and Sweepstakes operators. Born from the need for a more flexible, scalable alternative to legacy iGaming platforms, we empower operators to launch and scale with confidence. Our platform delivers seamless player experiences, drives higher engagement, and streamlines operations for long-term growth. We invest in continuous R&D to ensure our platform evolves with market demands and compliance shifts, building robust, always-on systems that run 24/7 without interruption. Based in Ramat Gan, we're a fast-growing team that values innovation, high standards, and building technology that solves real-world challenges. Role Description This is a full-time role for a Back End Developer based in Ramat Gan. The Back End Developer will be responsible for designing, building, and maintaining high-performance, event-driven microservices. Responsibilities include developing real-time systems that handle millions of transactions, optimizing back-end processes for scale, and collaborating with cross-functional teams. The role involves building robust APIs, working with message queues, and consistently improving our distributed architecture to support rapid growth. Requirements Qualifications • 6+ years of experience in Back-End Development • Proficiency in NestJS, TypeScript, and/or Golang • Experience with PostgreSQL, MySQL, or MongoDB • Knowledge of Kafka, Redis, and BullMQ (or similar message queue systems) • Strong understanding of event-driven and microservices architecture • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions, and YAML configuration • Basic understanding of Kubernetes and Terraform • Problem-solving skills and attention to detail • Ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment • Experience in Gaming, Fintech, or iGaming platforms is a plus • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
