The Opportunity The Kargo Workflow Platform powers our entire AdTech engine, and we're modernizing it. As a Senior Engineer on the team, you'll design and build distributed services that are reliable, observable, and built to last — owning projects end-to-end and partnering across engineering, product, and data. We're looking for a self-starter who finds problems, frames them, and fixes them, and who makes the engineers around them better in the process. The Daily To-Do • Design and build distributed services for the Workflow Platform modernization, thinking about failure modes, retry logic, and observability from the start • Own significant projects end-to-end — from design through deployment and post-launch optimization — with minimal hand-holding • Improve platform health through latency reductions, fault tolerance, and observability enhancements with measurable outcomes • Use AI-assisted development tools fluently to ship faster, and actively share what's working with teammates • Raise the quality bar through code reviews and design discussions, contributing well-reasoned opinions • Partner with Product, Data, and cross-functional stakeholders, translating fluently between technical and non-technical • Mentor less experienced engineers through pairing, documentation, and patient, constructive guidance Qualifications • 5–8 years of software engineering experience, with meaningful time in distributed or high-scale backend systems • Strong proficiency in at least one modern, high-performance language; TypeScript and/or Golang strongly preferred • Solid understanding of distributed systems concepts: consistency, availability, fault tolerance, event-driven architecture, and service orchestration • Experience building and maintaining microservices in production at meaningful scale; async messaging (Kafka, Pub/Sub) or workflow engines a strong plus • Comfortable with CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure), and debugging production issues using monitoring and distributed tracing • Active user of AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, or equivalent) with real, demonstrated productivity gains • B.S. or higher in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or equivalent practical experience;
