Observe by Snowflake is an AI-powered observability platform built on the Snowflake Data Cloud and engineered for scale. The Infrastructure team at Observe by Snowflake is responsible for building, scaling, and operating the development and production environments that power our observability platform. We are a small, highly collaborative team with a broad scope, focused on delivering reliable infrastructure while continuously improving the systems that support our engineers and customers.
