About the Role: Our mission is to make all of our customers’ security-relevant data continuously available for automated detection and response, threat hunting, and other Falcon use cases. To enable this, the systems behind NG-SIEM are growing to accommodate >100 PB of event and action data ingested every day, up to 10 years of retention, and dozens of millions of queries per hour across large sections of the data stored. As a new Engineer on the LogScale Search team you will be responsible for delivering this scale to customers by developing new features as well as improving existing functionality. You will be part of a new team that will be building and scaling Federated Search in LogScale that will enable customers to combine their data within LogScale to data that they have in their own data stores (like AWS Athena). You will be part of a distributed team of highly collaborative engineers who share a strong passion for the product. In this role you will get a chance to work on hard engineering problems that do not have well-defined solutions, and drive high-impact projects to completion. You will drive the company’s vision of a fast, reliable and best-in-class log storage solution. Teams in LogScale have a great amount of autonomy, and own a part of the LogScale experience, from ideation and development to deployment and feedback. This includes ensuring high quality of the features they deliver, by writing automated tests, and performing manual tests. Moreover, we do not just throw over features to our operations teams, but we constantly observe and monitor new features in production, with LogScale dashboards of course. An important part of our work is making sure that LogScale continues to be scalable in terms of efficiently ingesting and digesting events, querying for the right data, while also ensuring the stability of the clusters. This role is hybrid, requiring 2-3 days per week on-site at one of the posted locations. What You’ll Do: • Work in a team of friendly, trustworthy and knowledgeable colleagues supporting each other through code reviews, pairing, documentation, design reviews and technical guidance • Lead by example on customer and product focus, as well as on hands-on operational excellence by monitoring and following up on features released to customers and troubleshooting critical customer issues. • Deliver cross-team platform-level initiatives and projects by owning or participating in scoping, designing and developing of new features to open-ended problems • Develop the microservices in Golang to integrate with LogScale backend and extending our product to keep LogScale scale with increasing ingest and query load while ensuring high availability • Collaborate with your peers across teams on defining and delivering new solutions, design documents, technical talks and improving our internal documentation • We are a remote first company so you must bring your excellent verbal and written communication skills to bear when you are working with teams across the globe What You’ll Need: • A passion for or personal interest in distributed systems and curiosity of how our running systems are doing • 3+ years experience in software development in general and the ability to juggle making pragmatic tradeoffs between short-time delivery needs vs long-term product goals • Fluency in both functional and object oriented programming and the ability to pick up new languages on the go • A can-do attitude, and that you thrive collaborating in a team and are not afraid of taking on responsibilities • Demonstrated track record of operational excellence when complex distributed services • Ability to communicate and articulate technical concepts crisply to individual contributors and management. • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes. Bonus Points: • Experience programming in Golang or Scala • Experience with commercial clouds (AWS, Azure, GCP) • Hands on experience in large-scale (distributed) system architecture, high scalability and availability, and optimization - over 1 trillion requests per day or more than 10 PB of data per day • Exposure to or experience with Log Management or cybersecurity products #LI-SS1 This role will require the candidate to periodically undergo and pass additional background and fingerprint check(s) consistent with government customer requirements.
