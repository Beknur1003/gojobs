About the Role Join the DPM team and help build the next generation of decentralized prediction market infrastructure. As a Team Lead, you will lead a team of talented engineers while remaining deeply involved in the design and development of the backend systems powering decentralized prediction markets, blockchain integrations, execution services, and real-time market infrastructure. Working closely with the CTO, you'll help define the technical direction of the platform, drive engineering excellence, mentor developers, and ensure the successful delivery of scalable, production-grade distributed systems. This is a hands-on leadership role where you'll continue to spend a significant portion of your time designing, building, and reviewing production code while leading the team's technical execution. What You'll Do • Lead and mentor a team of backend and blockchain engineers • Own the team's technical execution, delivery, and engineering quality • Design and build high-performance backend services using Go (Golang) • Develop core services powering decentralized prediction markets • Build integrations with blockchain protocols, smart contracts, and decentralized applications • Integrate with blockchain networks, external services, and real-time market data feeds • Design scalable event-driven microservices using NATS • Implement reliable workflow orchestration using Temporal • Build resilient services for blockchain transaction processing, market connectivity, and backend infrastructure • Drive architecture discussions and make pragmatic technical decisions • Perform code reviews and promote engineering best practices • Coach engineers through design, implementation, debugging, and production incidents • Collaborate closely with Product and the CTO to define priorities and technical roadmaps • Design and optimize distributed systems for scalability, reliability, and low latency • Improve observability, testing, CI/CD, and operational excellence Required Qualifications • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience • 5+ years of professional Go (Golang) development • 2+ years of technical leadership or team leadership experience • 3+ years of blockchain/Web3 development • Strong experience designing and building distributed backend systems and blockchain applications on EVM-compatible networks, including smart contract integration and production-grade transaction processing • Deep understanding of blockchain architecture and transaction lifecycles, including transaction signing, gas optimization, nonce management, confirmations, and chain reorganizations • Experience leading technical design discussions and delivering complex software projects • Comfortable using modern AI-assisted development tools (such as Claude Code, Cursor, or similar) as part of the daily software development workflow • Experience with PostgreSQL • Experience with Temporal (or similar workflow orchestration platforms) • Experience with NATS, Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar messaging technologies • Experience with Kubernetes and modern CI/CD pipelines • Strong debugging, troubleshooting, and production support skills • Excellent communication, mentoring, and collaboration skills Advantages • Experience with prediction markets • Experience with Central Limit Order Books (CLOB) • Experience with Conditional Token Frameworks (CTF) • Experience with wallet infrastructure or MPC wallets • Familiarity with Terraform and cloud infrastructure • Experience growing and mentoring engineering teams in fast-paced startup environments Working at DPM At DPM, you'll join a small, experienced engineering team where you'll have significant ownership over architecture, technology choices, and the evolution of a next-generation decentralized prediction market platform. As a Team Lead, you'll play a key role in shaping both the technology and the engineering culture while remaining deeply involved in building the platform. You'll work closely with the CTO to deliver highly scalable, distributed systems that power secure, reliable, and high-performance blockchain applications.
