(Senior) Software Engineer - Pricing (m/f/d)

SIXT Munich, BY, de опубликовано 24 июня 2026

Гибрид Senior
C++AI/LLM
Pricing is the engine of our business, and you’ll own it. In your role will take a business goal and drive it all the way to a validated outcome in production — shaping the solution with Product, designing it, shipping it, and proving it moved the metric it was meant to move. It is car-rental yield management — perishable inventory, real-time decisions, made millions of times — and one of the most consequential systems at SIXT. Join Team Orange in Pullach close to Munich and own it. YOUR ROLE AT SIXT • You own pricing problems end to end — from ambiguous business intent to a running production system you are accountable for: writing it, testing it, deploying it, and monitoring it live • You partner with Product as a peer, co-owning the outcome: translating business goals into technical strategy, challenging unclear framing, and proposing better solutions instead of waiting for a spec • You turn ambiguity into clarity — specifications with measurable acceptance criteria, the real edge cases named, and a concrete plan for validating the result after launch • You make quality structural by encoding standards as automated checks so whole classes of issues are blocked rather than caught in review, and root-cause problems so they don’t recur — because in pricing, mistakes are expensive • You use AI tooling as a genuine engineering lever — moving faster from spec to working C++, accelerating root cause analysis, generating test coverage — and help define where it actually earns its place in the pricing stack • You set technical direction for performance, reliability, and correctness across the pricing stack, and raise the bar for the engineers around you YOUR SKILLS MATTER • Experience You are experienced in C++ as your primary language in a latency- and correctness-critical pricing core where the details matter • Ownership You build scalable distributed systems, without anyone telling you how; Golang is a strong plus as we build a service layer around the core • Systems Thinking You reason honestly about trade-offs in reliability, performance, and correctness, design clean subsystem contracts, and debug across services to genuine root cause — you know where things break and why • Motivation You ask what problem we are solving before what should I build and measure your success by outcomes, not lines of code • Soft Skills You are proactive, even in a high-stakes domain where precision matters and the numbers you move are real • Team Spirit You collaborate with Product and engineering peers, challenge unclear framing confidently, and compound your judgment on architecture and outcomes WHAT WE OFFER • Extra benefits Enjoy discounts on SIXT rent, share, ride and SIXT+, attractive vehicle leasing offers, and exclusive deals with partners for travel, tech, fashion and more • Mobility boost We support you with a monthly mobility allowance of €20 per month for even more freedom • Future security We contribute to your retirement plan and support you with capital-forming benefits to ensure you are well covered • Feel-good atmosphere Stay active with our modern SIXT gym, various leisure activities like the gaming area or the SIXT choir, and enjoy our high-quality employee restaurant • Flexibility Enjoy 30 days of vacation and a hybrid working model with flexible hours • Giving back Take one day each year to volunteer at a charitable organization dedicated to supporting children About us: We are a globally leading mobility service provider with a revenue of €4.00 billion and around 9,000 employees worldwide. Our mobility platform ONE combines our products SIXT rent (car rental), SIXT share (car sharing), SIXT ride (taxi, ride, and chauffeur services), and SIXT+ (car subscription), giving our customers access to our fleet of 350,000 vehicles, the services of 4,000 cooperation partners, and around 5 million drivers worldwide. Together with our franchise partners, we are present in more than 110 countries at 2,000 rental stations. At SIXT, top-tier customer experience and outstanding customer service are our highest priorities.
