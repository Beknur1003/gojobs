Tasks • Architect, build, and own core product features across our entire stack, from AI (Voice AI, STT, TTS, Microsoft Foundry, MCP, Deep Agents, LangChain, Langfuse) to Python (optionally Golang and/or TypeScript), Postgres, and Microsoft Azure infrastructure. • Design and implement scalable microservice architectures on a modern infrastructure stack. • Develop production-grade AI products, including features driven by LLMs, agents, and advanced audio ML. • Integrate a wide variety of third-party APIs, MCPs, and data stores to expand our platform's capabilities. • Get hands-on with Voice AI, building systems for audio analysis and processing, VAD, noise cancelation, turn management, and implementing robust evaluation frameworks for evals and agent orchestration. • Champion a security-first mindset, ensuring the highest standards for data, application, and infrastructure security. • Establish and drive a comprehensive testing culture, from unit testing to contract and penetration testing.
