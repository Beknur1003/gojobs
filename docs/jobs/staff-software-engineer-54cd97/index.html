Staff Software Engineer

Intuit Bangalore, Karnātaka, India опубликовано 9 сентября 2026

Go — основной язык Lead
KubernetesAWSAI/LLM
At Intuit, we are committed to powering prosperity around the world with our cutting-edge technology platforms that solve the most important financial challenges. As part of our Development Ecosystem (DevX) organization, we are developing the next-generation AI-native development platform. We invite you to join our talented team as a Staff Software Engineer focused on AI native infrastructure provisioning, Kubernetes runtime platform and open source streaming (Numaflow platform) endeavours. As a Staff Software Engineer at Intuit, you will play a significant role in designing and implementing our Kubernetes-based runtime environment, which is fundamental to driving the Intuit platform's innovative solutions at scale. We are looking for a technical leader with profound expertise in Kubernetes (specifically Amazon EKS), Golang, and AWS to drive major capabilities and ensure operational excellence in high-impact projects. Responsibilities • Lead the architecture and implementation efforts of multiple capabilities or sections within our AI native Kubernetes platform. • Serve as a technical leader and domain expert across multiple teams. • Drive and guide innovative work that may include the development of new-to-the-world technology solutions. • Influence technology and product direction through detailed technical documents and RCA’s. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify, share, and address risks impacting our business. • Drive customer-focused design and development with a deep understanding of customer pains and needs. • Advocate and lead by example to encourage contributions to codebases and innovations across Intuit. • Mentor and assess engineers to foster a talented and inclusive technical team. Qualifications • 8+ years of professional experience in software engineering. • Considerable expertise in Kubernetes, especially with Amazon EKS, including operator patterns, Kubernetes clients, controllers, shared informers, and caching mechanisms. • Proficiency in Golang and extensive experience with AWS. • Demonstrated ability to lead multiple scrum teams (10 to 35 engineers). • Experience in leading technical design, architecture, and code reviews. • Strong ability to simplify technical designs and operations to enhance team understanding and performance. • Proven track record of operational excellence and ability to innovate and simplify processes and designs. • Deep commitment to improving and expanding the skills and knowledge of your team. Intuit provides a competitive compensation package with a strong pay for performance rewards approach. This position may be eligible for a cash bonus, equity rewards and benefits, in accordance with our applicable plans and programs (see more about our compensation and benefits at Intuit®: Careers | Benefits). Pay offered is based on factors such as job-related knowledge, skills, experience, and work location. To drive ongoing fair pay for employees, Intuit conducts regular comparisons across categories of ethnicity and gender.
