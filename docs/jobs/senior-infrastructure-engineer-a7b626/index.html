The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience operating reverse proxies in production environments using technologies such as NGINX, HAProxy, F5, Traefik, or Envoy. Required Skills • Hands-on experience with L4/L7 reverse proxy technologies in production environments, including any of the following: • NGINX • HAProxy • F5 • Traefik • Envoy • Strong production experience with Go (Golang). • Good understanding of TLS/mTLS, including: • SNI and ALPN • Client certificate validation • Certificate lifecycle management. Preferred Skills • JWT, OAuth2, and OIDC, with hands-on experience implementing or debugging token validation. • Protobuf and gRPC, including schema development and debugging gRPC transport issues. Other Relevant Skills • Policy Management: OPA/Rego policy implementation, including Kubernetes admission control. • Distributed Storage: Redis, DynamoDB, TTL, replication, and failover consistency. • Identity Platforms: ForgeRock, Okta, Ping Identity, or Auth0. • Kafka / Event Streaming: Producing and consuming events, configuring topics and consumers, and implementing event-driven services. • Observability: Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry (OTel).
