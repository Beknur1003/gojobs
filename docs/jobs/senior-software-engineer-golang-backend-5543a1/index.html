Golang Engineer – Trust & Safety Backend Location: Singapore We are looking for a Golang backend engineer to build the systems that keep our platform safe, trustworthy. You will report to Lead Engineer, Electronic Arts. What you'll do • You will develop high-scale backend services in Go for Trust & Safety. • You will develop APIs and services for reporting, moderation, enforcement, appeals, and policy workflows. • Help us in process real-time safety signals and high-volume platform events. • Integrate moderation models, ML/AI services, and third-party safety systems. • Help us to build internal tools and APIs for moderation and operations teams. • Partner with us to Improve reliability, observability, scalability, and auditability of safety systems. • Partner with product, data, ML, security, and client engineering teams. What we're looking for • 9+ Years of experience in Production experience working with Golang. • 8+ years of experience building scalable backend services and distributed systems. • 5+years of experience with REST/gRPC APIs, databases, caching, queues, and event-driven systems. • Experience with cloud infrastructure and containerized services. • Good Debugging and production. • Experience with Trust & Safety, fraud, moderation, abuse prevention, or risk systems.
