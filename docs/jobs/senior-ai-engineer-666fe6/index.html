Seal Security is a rapidly growing cybersecurity startup building an AI-powered AppSec engineer that works as another member of your team. Instead of the painful cycle of triaging vulnerabilities, creating tickets, and spending engineering hours on library upgrades, Seal automatically remediates open-source vulnerabilities with fully compatible, standalone security patches - no version bumps, no dependency headaches. We're backed by top-tier VCs, led by world-class cybersecurity and GTM experts, and recently closed our Series A funding, with numerous Fortune 500 customers. We are looking for a Senior AI Engineer who thrives on solving complex challenges across the entire stack. As a key member of our R&D team, you'll own features end-to-end - from backend services and infrastructure to frontend interfaces and developer tooling - leveraging the latest advancements in LLMs to push the boundaries of innovation. If you're a versatile engineer who's passionate about driving innovation, wearing multiple hats, and delivering world-class products, we'd love to meet you! Responsibilities • End-to-end development of software components across the full stack - research, design, development, testing, rollout, and monitoring. • Contribute to technological and architectural decisions. • Conduct design and code reviews. • Analyze and improve the efficiency, scalability, and stability of system component. Requirements • Comfortable working across the stack - backend, frontend, infrastructure, and tooling. • 7+ years of experience in software engineering with high-level programming languages (e.g., Python, Golang, Node). • Extensive experience in security research (e.g., vulnerability research, exploits). • Experience with continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD). • Micro-services based architecture experience. • An agile mindset adaptable to a startup's dynamic environment.
