Senior AI engineer

Devoteam København, dk опубликовано 17 ноября 2026

Senior Полная занятость English C1
GCPCI/CDPythonAI/LLM
About the Role We are looking for a strong AI Engineer (Senior to Principal level) to join our Applied AI Studio. In this role, you will design, build, and deploy bespoke, production-grade AI solutions for our Nordic clients. You will own the full lifecycle - from solution architecture and model orchestration to scalable backend services, user-facing integration, and MLOps - while acting as a trusted technical advisor to both clients and internal teams. What You Will Do • Design, Develop & Deploy: Architect and build cutting-edge, end-to-end AI solutions tailored to the real-world needs of our Nordic clients. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Eata engineers, Cloud Engineers, UX designers, project, change and product managers, and Google experts to solve complex problems and deliver measurable impact. • Client Engagement & Advisory: Confidently present technical architectures and AI solutions to client stakeholders - translating complex algorithms into clear business value while cultivating and expanding long-term customer relationships. • Mentorship & Knowledge Sharing: Guide junior colleagues in AI engineering best practices, champion knowledge sharing, and foster a culture of continuous learning within the studio. • Stay Ahead of the Curve: Continuously explore emerging AI capabilities, frameworks, and architectural patterns to keep our studio at the forefront of the industry. What We Are Looking For Core Experience & Skills • End-to-End Fullstack & Software Engineering: Proven track record building production software end-to-end. Python is a must, with strong experience in backend/API development and integrating AI into complete applications. • Solution Architecture: Experience designing scalable, secure, and maintainable system architectures for AI-powered products. • AI & Agentic Frameworks: Hands-on experience with ADK, PyTorch, LangChain, and LangGraph. • MLOps & Cloud: Experience with MLOps practices (deployment, monitoring, evaluation, CI/CD for AI/ML), preferably on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Communication & Leadership: Strong ability to communicate with both technical peers and executive client stakeholders, paired with a passion for mentoring. • Fluent in English, both spoken and written (proficiency in any other Nordic language is a plus). Nice to Have • Proficiency in additional programming languages such as Go (Golang) or C#. • Experience working in client-facing consulting or studio environments across the Nordics. Why Join the Applied AI Studio? • Work on diverse, high-impact AI projects with leading Nordic organizations. • Collaborate directly with multidisciplinary teams and Google experts. • Shape architectural standards, mentor growing talent, and work with the latest tools in applied AI.
