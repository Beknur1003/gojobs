Job Description: Senior Software Engineer We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Senior Software Engineer with a strong command of Golang or any strong backend language to join our dynamic team. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in designing and developing high-performance, scalable, and secure server-side applications. Your expertise will be instrumental in building robust, reliable, and efficient solutions that drive the success of our products. Responsibilities: • Lead the design and development of sophisticated, highly available, scalable, and secure server-side applications. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand requirements, architect solutions, and deliver high-quality software products. • Design and develop reliable backend systems supporting high-volume transactional and payment-related workflows. • Build and maintain integrations with external platforms and service providers, ensuring reliability and resilience across distributed systems. • Mentor and guide junior engineers, sharing engineering expertise and best practices to foster skill development within the team. • Analyze and optimize the performance, scalability, and reliability of existing applications, making strategic improvements where necessary. • Design and implement automated unit and integration tests to ensure code quality, maintainability, and stability. • Stay up-to-date with the latest advancements in software technologies, recommending their adoption when appropriate. • Champion code reviews, architectural discussions, and technical documentation to maintain high development standards. • Troubleshoot and resolve complex production issues, providing robust solutions and continuously improving system reliability. • Contribute to the recruitment and hiring process by participating in interviews, evaluating candidates, and providing input on hiring decisions. Requirements • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. • 5+ years of experience in software development, with substantial experience in backend systems and cloud infrastructure. • Strong proficiency in designing and developing high-performance and concurrent applications. • Experience with distributed systems, microservices architecture, and containerization technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes. • Good understanding of building reliable transactional systems, including concepts such as data consistency, idempotency, concurrency, and failure handling. • Experience working with relational databases and messaging/event-driven technologies would be advantageous. • Solid knowledge of software testing methodologies and tools, including unit and integration testing. • Demonstrated ability to lead projects, collaborate effectively across teams, and mentor junior engineers. • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills, with the ability to tackle complex technical challenges. • Prior experience in FinTech, payments, banking, e-commerce, or other high-volume transactional systems would be an added advantage. If you are a passionate and driven Software Engineer with a proven track record in backend development, eager to work with the latest technologies and contribute to the development of cutting-edge applications, we encourage you to apply. Join our dynamic team and be part of shaping the future of our innovative projects.
