Cloud Engineer Choosing Capgemini means choosing a company where you will be empowered to shape your career in the way you’d like, where you’ll be supported and inspired by a collaborative community of colleagues around the world, and where you’ll be able to reimagine what’s possible. Join us and help the world’s leading organizations unlock the value of technology and build a more sustainable, more inclusive world. You’ll have the opportunity to collaborate with a leading European mobility services provider, headquartered in Germany and backed by nearly 90 years of expertise. As part of this journey, you’ll contribute to the launch of a new Tech Hub in Porto, helping to drive innovative, technology-led solutions that are transforming how businesses move while contributing to a more sustainable future. YOUR ROLE • Design, deploy, and maintain cloud infrastructure across production and non‑production environments. • Implement and manage Infrastructure as Code (IaC) to ensure repeatable, auditable, and scalable deployments. • Operate and evolve cloud platforms with a strong focus on availability, performance, security, and cost efficiency. • Support and optimize CI/CD pipelines and cloud-native deployment workflows. • Troubleshoot cloud infrastructure and platform issues, participating in incident response and root cause analysis. • Collaborate with application, SRE, and security teams to ensure systems are cloud‑ready and operationally sound. • Contribute to cloud governance, including access control, networking, monitoring, and compliance standards • Experience operating production cloud environments with defined SLAs and operational processes. • Ability to handle cloud incidents, performance issues, and infrastructure failures in a structured manner. • Experience working in Agile / DevOps environments with cross‑functional teams. • Strong documentation skills, including architecture diagrams and operational runbooks. • Ability to balance engineering decisions with operational, security, and cost considerations YOUR PROFILE • Strong hands-on experience with public cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud). • Solid knowledge of Linux-based systems, system troubleshooting, and cloud networking fundamentals (VPC/VNet, routing, DNS, load balancing). • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools, preferably Terraform, as well as CloudFormation, ARM, Pulumi, or Ansible. • Experience with containerization and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes). • Strong understanding of Kubernetes concepts, including Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs). • Familiarity with Envoy as an API Gateway and service networking technologies. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, automation, and related tools (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps). • Hands-on experience with monitoring, logging, and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, and Splunk. • Solid understanding of networking fundamentals and distributed systems. • Knowledge of Go (Golang).
