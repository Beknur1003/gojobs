Senior Data Platform Engineer - Golang/ClickHouse

HyrMe Bangalore опубликовано 16 июля 2026

Go — основной язык Senior
PostgreSQLMySQLClickHouseKafka
What You'll Build: - Design high-throughput event ingestion services in Go that process and validate large-scale event streams into ClickHouse with automatic schema evolution. - Build and operate CDC pipelines using Debezium and Kafka to replicate PostgreSQL/MySQL data into ClickHouse with strong guarantees around correctness, ordering, and replay. - Develop secure, multi-tenant query APIs powering customer dashboards, internal analytics, and billing workflows. - Build a no-code analytics layer that enables customers and internal teams to create dashboards without writing SQL. - Own ClickHouse architecture, including sharding, replication, materialized views, query optimization, capacity planning, observability, and production reliability. - Drive engineering best practices through design reviews, documentation, testing, and architecture discussions. What We're Looking For: Experience: - 5+ years building backend or data platform systems in production. - Proven experience building scalable platforms used across engineering teams. Must-Have Skills: - Strong production experience with ClickHouse (MergeTree, sharding, replication, materialized views, query optimization). - Expert-level Go (Golang) for high-performance backend services. - Hands-on experience with Kafka, event-driven systems, and streaming architectures. - Experience building Debezium-based CDC pipelines. - Strong understanding of distributed systems, scalability, reliability, and performance optimization. Nice to Have: - Spark or Airflow for batch processing and backfills. - Experience building multi-tenant SaaS platforms with RBAC and tenant isolation. - Familiarity with BI tools such as Superset, Metabase, or Cube. - Experience with billing or metering systems.
