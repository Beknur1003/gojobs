About Us At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company. At Cloudflare, we’re not looking for people who wait for a polished roadmap; we’re looking for the builders who see the cracks in the Internet that everyone else has simply learned to live with. We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a "normalized" problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in. Locations: Austin TX, Champaign IL, Lisbon Portugal, London England, Denver CO, Atlanta GA, Washington DC, Location (all hybrid): Austin, TX; Champaign, IL; Seattle, WA; Atlanta, GA; Washington, DC; London, UK; Lisbon, PT About the team Cloudflare is redefining the way networks are run at scale, and some of the largest networks in the world now run their traffic through us. Our Network Services product suite (Magic Transit, Cloudflare WAN, Cloudflare Network Firewall, Spectrum, and more) puts the connectivity, scale, and performance of Cloudflare's global network in front of customers' applications, network infrastructure, and private networks, providing a single pane of glass for network connectivity, visibility, and network functions like DDoS protection, firewalling, and load balancing. We are a team of engineers and product managers who obsess over quickly building products that solve real customer problems, using techniques that take advantage of Cloudflare's unique architecture and global network footprint. Our teams build and operate the software behind these products: the data plane that processes customer traffic at the packet level as it moves through Cloudflare's network, the on-ramps and routing that get traffic to and from customers, and the control planes and APIs that configure all of it. We own what we build in production, and a lot of our work happens close to the kernel and the NIC. About the role We're hiring engineers at several levels across a few closely related teams. There's one application and one interview process, and we'll work out with you which team fits your interests and experience. Some of the things these teams work on: • The data plane behind Magic Transit and Cloudflare WAN, including Unified Routing (our userspace forwarding plane, which gives each customer flexible forwarding of their traffic) and Fast-Path, which uses XDP and eBPF to forward very large single flows • The on-ramps customers use to reach us (GRE, IPsec, PNI), and the tunnel health checks that move traffic away from failing paths • An anycast BGP implementation for customer tunnels, where sessions survive being moved between servers and data centers • Cloudflare Network Firewall, a low-latency userspace packet firewall written in Rust, with rate limiting, intrusion detection, and packet capture • Unimog, our XDP-based L4 load balancer, which touches nearly every connection into Cloudflare and forwards packets between servers and between data centers • Spectrum, a TCP/UDP reverse proxy written in Go that brings Cloudflare's network to non-HTTP applications (e.g., gaming, SSH, MQTT, custom protocols, ...), plus TCP Workers • The APIs and control plane systems that let customers express complex network configurations simply, and that push that configuration to every server in our network You'll build, deploy, and operate your team's systems on top of one of the largest edge networks in the world. Most of our code is Go and Rust (plus C for eBPF) on Linux, and the work ranges from kernel networking (XDP, tc, netfilter, namespaces) through userspace packet processing to distributed systems and APIs. You'll partner with some of the best engineers and product managers, on our teams and across Cloudflare, to solve big problems that have a meaningful impact in the lives of our customers and users. We're looking for curious, empathetic engineers who are excited to join us in changing the way networking is done! Examples of desirable skills, knowledge, and experience • Systems programming experience, especially in Go, Rust, or C • A solid understanding of L3 and L4 networking: IP, TCP, UDP, routing, tunneling (GRE, IPsec), NAT, and anycast; L2 is useful too • Experience building on Linux networking: eBPF/XDP, tc, netfilter/nftables, network namespaces, sockets, etc. • Experience with high-performance or userspace packet processing (e.g., AF_XDP, DPDK, VPP), software routers, or routing protocols such as BGP • Debugging, profiling, and optimizing systems where throughput and latency matter • Building and shipping large-scale, reliable, distributed systems, including APIs and control planes • Operating what you build: on-call, incident response, and root cause analysis • Comfort reading unfamiliar code and safely changing systems that have been in production for years • Owning work end to end, from design through deployment, and working across teams to land shared solutions • Clear written and verbal communication; we design in written specs and review each other's work Bonus points • Network security systems: firewalls, IDS/IPS, rate limiting, or packet capture • L4 load balancers or proxies (e.g., HAProxy, Envoy, nginx stream), TLS, or TCP stack internals • VPN and tunneling implementations (e.g., IPsec/IKE, WireGuard, GRE) • Building or running networks: ISPs, data center networks, or router and network OS development • Flow or connection analytics pipelines (e.g., ClickHouse, Kafka) • Contributions to open-source networking projects (e.g., the Linux kernel, DPDK, BIRD, FRR, GoBGP) Compensation • Compensation may be adjusted depending on work location. • For Champaign IL based hires: Estimated annual salary of $168,000 - $210,000. • For Washington, DC based hires: Estimated annual salary of $185,000 - $230,000. • For Seattle, WA based hires: Estimated annual salary of $185,000 - $230,000. • For Portugal based hires: Estimated annual salary is between €66,000 - 83,000.
