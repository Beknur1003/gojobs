Product Engineer (Go, Billing)

Data Driven Lab опубликовано 7 октября 2026

Go — основной язык English C1
FinTech
The company considers specialists residing in Cyprus or EU countries. Data-Driven Lab is an international IT company developing products for fintech, edtech, and analytics industries. Alongside product development, we help businesses grow with a full range of digital marketing services. We are looking for a Product Engineer (Go) to join the team accountable for the internal billing, ledger, and financial operations. The mission is to deliver the best possible customer experience during cash operations: deposits, withdrawals, transfers, etc. We expect you to • Build the internal ledger in Go: accounting views, double-entry postings, balances, FX handling, client funds segregation, separate ledgers. • Implement support of trading logic in billing: fees, margins, collaterals, commissions, swaps, adjustments and reversals, FX reevaluation. • Ensure financial correctness: automate reconciliation across payment/trading/PSP sources, define matching logic and tolerances, automate mismatch detection, implement correction mechanisms and preserve an immutable audit trail. • Build data products: APIs and exports for management reporting, for customer self-verification and statements, for customer support transparency, for regulatory reporting. • Fluent English: daily communication in international teams, technical discussions, documentation. What is important to us • You consistently put customer value and team goals above everything else when making any decision during your work. • Engineering mindset: ability to build new under uncertainty, understand big complex systems, navigating root causes, and choose mindful solutions. • You built an accounting-grade ledger/billing core in Go. • You worked in a regulated and audit-heavy environment: ISO 27001, PCI DSS, DORA. • You designed automated reconciliations. • You built financial data products: accounting views/APIs/exports for internal and external usage. • Production reliability experience: observability, resilience practices, incident management, safe releases. • Commitment to engineering quality through writing tests and automating quality checks (integration / contract / E2E). We offer • Direct feedback loop with customers: you will see how you impact their experience and their success. • True peer-to-peer relationships within teams with no managers, team leads or any other roles defined for them — everything is managed by you and your teammates through collaboration based on agreements, not titles. • Non-fading startup pace: autonomous decision-making inside the team, and the ability to choose the way the product evolves. • Engineering-driven environment: we value engineering experience and creative thinking combined, so you’ll be able to handle the process end-to-end. • No gatekeepers: as our teams are completely autonomous, the development, security, and sustainability are owned by you. • Comfortable conditions: competitive salary, medical compensation, fully remote working options.
