This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Software Engineer – Golang (Banking/Banking Management) based in Brazil. We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to help build and scale the core data infrastructure powering a modern financial ecosystem. In this role, you will contribute to the development and evolution of a high-performance Read Ledger, serving as a reliable source of transactional data for banking products and services. You will work with mission-critical systems handling large volumes of financial data, where consistency, availability, security, and scalability are essential. Using Go and Elixir, you will design and improve distributed systems and microservices while ensuring data integrity and operational excellence. You will collaborate with engineering teams to drive architectural improvements, establish best practices, and deliver resilient solutions. This is an opportunity to tackle complex technical challenges and directly influence the reliability and quality of financial services. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, maintain, and evolve new features for mission-critical financial systems using Go (Golang) and Elixir. • Contribute to the architecture, development, and scalability of the Banking Read Ledger, ensuring it remains a reliable source of transactional data across the financial ecosystem. • Build and maintain high-performance distributed systems capable of processing large volumes of financial data while ensuring consistency, availability, and integrity. • Ensure production systems meet strict non-functional requirements, including performance, scalability, security, availability, resilience, and seamless integration with the broader technology ecosystem. • Monitor application performance and behavior after deployment, proactively identifying bottlenecks, operational risks, and opportunities for optimization. • Promote software quality through automated testing, continuous code inspections, and adherence to engineering best practices. • Participate actively in code reviews, providing technical feedback and contributing to the continuous improvement of the team's engineering standards. • Maintain clear and up-to-date technical documentation to ensure traceability of system changes, architectural decisions, and product evolution. • Identify gaps and inefficiencies in existing processes, systems, and architecture, proposing innovative and sustainable technical solutions. • Collaborate with other engineers to improve system reliability, technical architecture, and the overall performance of the financial data platform. Requirements: • Solid professional experience in software development using Go (Golang) and Elixir. • Strong understanding of software engineering principles, distributed systems, and microservices architecture. • Experience implementing automated testing strategies and maintaining high code quality through continuous code inspection. • Strong knowledge of relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL, MySQL, or similar technologies. • Understanding of scalable application architectures, data consistency, system availability, and performance optimization. • Ability to identify technical and architectural challenges proactively and recommend effective, innovative solutions. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work on complex, mission-critical systems. • Adaptability and enthusiasm for learning new technologies, tools, and engineering practices. • Collaborative mindset, proactive communication, and willingness to contribute to code reviews and shared technical decisions. • Strong sense of ownership and commitment to delivering reliable, maintainable, and high-quality software solutions. Nice to have: • Knowledge of or interest in the financial services industry, particularly banking operations and transactional statements. • Experience with public or private cloud platforms, such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud. • Familiarity with messaging systems and event-driven architectures, including Kafka, RabbitMQ, ZeroMQ, or IBM MQ. • Knowledge of advanced architectural patterns and approaches, such as Event Sourcing, CQRS, Hexagonal Architecture, and Lambda Architecture. • Experience with non-relational databases (NoSQL) and data modeling. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and automation tools, preferably GitHub Actions.
