About the Role: CrowdStrike is seeking an experienced Engineering Manager to lead a cloud backend team within our AI Detection and Response (AIDR) group. In this role, you will lead a team of talented engineers responsible for building and operating the next generation of AIDR provisioning and orchestration systems. When customers onboard to the AIDR platform, your team ensures they become operational seamlessly — from initializing customer stores and configuring security policies, to setting up management groups, provisioning data stores, and enabling configuration across dependent services. Your team manages the automation of this complex orchestration to deliver a frictionless customer experience at massive scale. You'll play a critical role in enhancing existing provisioning flows and architecting next-generation systems that support millions of AI security events per second across petabytes of critical data. This is a player-coach role — while your primary focus is leading and growing your team, you will also contribute code, prototype solutions, and stay deeply embedded in the technical work when needed. This is an opportunity to lead at the intersection of people leadership and technical excellence, building both high-performing teams and high-performing systems that protect organizations worldwide from sophisticated AI-based threats. PLEASE NOTE: This role is hybrid, requiring 2-3 days per week on-site at one of the posted locations.
