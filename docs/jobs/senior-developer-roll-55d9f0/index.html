ADP is hiring a Senior Developer – Roll • Do you thrive in a challenging environment, love production systems, curious by nature with a thirst for pushing the limits? • Are you inspired by transformation and making an impact on the lives of millions of people every day? Well, this may be the role for you. Ready to design what's next? In this role, you will be building the Roll product, one of the most important Human Capital Management solutions at ADP and in the world. Roll by ADP is the first chat-based payroll app for small business. You will start your day on a scrum call to sync up the sprint work and goal with your team. You will spend most of your day designing, developing and testing your code and supporting your team members. Every week you will be able to deploy your code in production using automated continuous integration and continuous deployment. You don't need to know every technology, but you're an enthusiastic creator who stays current and looks forward to trying new code. You're always looking for new ways to make tools better for our users. You will work closely with a small team of front-end designers, data scientists, developers, and research specialists who will value your expertise in software programming, latest web technologies and frameworks. You are comfortable collaborating, but equally capable of working independently. As part of our team, you'll find exciting challenges, get opportunities to grow your career, and develop solid friendships as we design what's next for ADP and the industry.
