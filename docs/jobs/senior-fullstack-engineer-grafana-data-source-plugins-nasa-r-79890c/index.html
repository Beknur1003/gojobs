Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer, Grafana Data Source Plugins The Opportunity: As a Senior Full-Stack Engineer in the Grafana Data Source Plugins squad, you will take a leading role in developing and enhancing a diverse portfolio of data source plugins that retrieve the data powering Grafana dashboards used by teams worldwide. Our team focuses on continuously improving data source plugins, ensuring seamless experience for human and agent users alike. A key aspect of this role is guaranteeing the smooth operation and reliability of our plugins in the Grafana Cloud, helping us deliver on our commitment to world-class observability tools. What You’ll Be Doing: • Some data source plugins we own: • Prometheus, Loki, Infinity, MySQL, PostgreSQL, AWS Cloudwatch, and Clickhouse. The full catalog is listed here. • A typical “day in the life” could include: • Building or improving Grafana plugins to integrate third party services or databases into Grafana • Supporting the operations and reliability of our plugins in Grafana Cloud • Coming up with suggestions for process improvements • Integrating data sources with agentic workflows such as Grafana Assistant • Providing technical support in customer escalations • Working closely with the rest of the Grafana Division What Makes You a Great Fit: • You know how to transform ideas into working code, you are able to design a solution, get feedback and deliver large projects. • You are a self-driven individual who understands the power of feedback loops in making roadmap decisions and driving our product forward. • You are a motivated self starter with a bias towards action, and willing to take on any challenge • You are customer focused. We build everything with our users in mind. You have a passion for creating intuitive products that fit customers’ needs. • You are an excellent communicator and you enjoy collaboration • You are comfortable working in a remote-first company Requirements: • Solid experience with Golang & Typescript • Experience with Kubernetes & microservices • Experience writing clean, robust, and performant software that is easily maintained by others • Experience participating in an on-call rotation and performing operations/SRE tasks Bonus Points For: • You have been a user of Grafana and Grafana Data Sources • You have expertise in one of the underlying databases we support • You have strong experience working with a Product Manager or external stakeholders In the United States, the Base compensation range for this role is CAD $164,490 - $197,389.
