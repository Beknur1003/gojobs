Senior Infrastructure Engineer - Kubernetes Architecture

Role Overview : As a Senior Infrastructure / Platform Engineer, you will design, own, and evolve scalable microservices architectures on Kubernetes across multi-cloud environments (GCP, Azure, and AWS), ensuring multi-tenant isolation, security, and performance at enterprise scale. Key Responsibilities : - Cloud & Kubernetes Architecture : Design multi-tenant, microservices-based systems and define reliability contracts that ensure enterprise-grade performance, security, and scalability. - Platform Development : Build core platform components using Golang and Python, managing service-to-service communication. - DevOps & Infrastructure as Code : Drive platform engineering practices using IaC (Terraform), Helm, GitOps (ArgoCD/Flux), and CI/CD pipelines. - Performance & Cost Optimization : Optimize systems for performance, latency budgets, and cost-per-request. - Observability & Operations : Participate in on-call rotations, lead incident response, and perform root-cause analysis. Requirements : - Experience : 5+ years of experience in Platform, Infrastructure, or Backend Engineering. - Core Languages : Strong proficiency in Golang and Python. - Containers & Orchestration : Production experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and microservices architecture. - Cloud Infrastructure : Hands-on experience with at least one major cloud provider (GCP, Azure, or AWS); multi-cloud exposure is a strong plus. - Distributed Systems & Messaging : Familiarity with message queues (Kafka, Pulsar, NATS, or PubSub), idempotency, and backpressure. - Education : Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
