AI Engineer Location: Remote – South Africa Reporting to: AI/ML Manager About the Role: We are seeking an experienced AI Engineer to design, develop and deliver AI and machine learning solutions within an AWS environment. This is a hands-on technical role combining software engineering, machine learning, solution design and client engagement. You will work directly with clients during discovery and assessment engagements, helping identify opportunities where AI and ML can deliver measurable business value. You will then contribute to solution design and take projects through to technical implementation. The ideal candidate will be comfortable engaging with clients, translating business challenges into technical solutions and delivering production-ready AI and ML applications. As the AI/ML function continues to grow, there are two potential career progression paths: senior technical leadership or delivery and team leadership. Key Responsibilities: 1. Client Discovery & Assessments • Participate in client discovery workshops and AI/ML assessments, with the opportunity to independently lead engagements. • Gather business, technical and operational requirements from a range of stakeholders. • Assess business objectives, existing infrastructure, data readiness, technical constraints and success criteria. • Identify opportunities where AI and machine learning can deliver practical business value. • Translate assessment findings into recommendations and potential project engagements. 2. Solution Design & Project Definition • Translate business challenges into practical AI and ML solution architectures. • Evaluate different models, technologies and architectural approaches, considering feasibility, cost, risk and scalability. • Contribute to proposals for proofs of concept, MVPs, migrations and production workloads. • Translate assessment outcomes into structured delivery plans, workstreams and technical deliverables. • Provide technical guidance and recommendations to both business and technical stakeholders. 3. Hands-on AI & ML Engineering • Design, develop, test and deploy machine learning and Generative AI solutions using AWS services. • Build and integrate GenAI workflows using AWS Bedrock, including prompt engineering, embeddings, retrieval-based architectures and agentic solutions. • Develop machine learning pipelines using AWS SageMaker for model training, deployment and inference. • Develop production-quality Python applications and services. • Monitor, troubleshoot and continuously optimise deployed AI solutions. • Build and integrate APIs supporting machine learning and Generative AI applications. Key Requirements: • 3+ years of hands-on experience in applied machine learning, AI engineering or software engineering, with substantial practical AI exposure. • Practical experience delivering machine learning solutions using AWS SageMaker. • At least 1 year of hands-on experience with AWS Bedrock. • Strong Python programming skills and experience developing production-quality applications. • Experience delivering Generative AI, LLM-based or machine learning solutions in production or near-production environments. • Good understanding of machine learning concepts, model selection, evaluation and deployment. • Ability to communicate technical approaches, architectural decisions and trade-offs to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Comfortable engaging directly with clients and leading technical discovery discussions. • Experience developing and integrating APIs for AI and ML systems. • Experience with Git-based development workflows, CI/CD pipelines and Linux environments. • Working knowledge of AWS Lambda and AWS services supporting knowledge bases and retrieval-based solutions. • Experience with Terraform or an equivalent Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tool. Advantageous Experience Experience with any of the following would be beneficial: • Amazon OpenSearch • Amazon RDS • AWS Step Functions • Amazon ECS or EKS • AWS managed AI services, including Rekognition, Transcribe, Polly and Comprehend. • Golang • A Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology or a related discipline. Career Progression This position offers two potential development paths, depending on individual strengths, interests and business requirements. Senior Technical Leadership • Lead complex AI and ML solution design. • Drive technical decision-making and engineering standards. • Provide technical direction while remaining actively involved in hands-on development. Delivery & Team Leadership • Support the AI/ML Manager with project delivery and operational oversight. • Coordinate workstreams, resources and technical deliverables. • Mentor and support other engineers. • Develop towards a broader leadership and second-in-command position within the function. Why Consider This Opportunity? • Work on real-world AI and machine learning projects across multiple industries. • Gain exposure to a broad range of AWS technologies and services. • Build and deploy practical Generative AI and LLM-based solutions. • Participate in client-facing solution design and technical consulting engagements. • Access company-sponsored AWS certification examinations. • Develop your career towards either senior technical leadership or delivery management.
