We're looking for a Platform Developer – Hyperspace CI/CD to join our team in Frankfurt in a Hybrid working model. In this role, you will contribute to the development and maintenance of the Hyperspace Portal – the developer-facing interface of SAP's internal development platform serving over 50,000 developers. You will focus on implementing CI/CD pipelines and tooling, ensuring seamless onboarding experiences and robust delivery processes. This position provides the opportunity to work on high-impact development initiatives, optimize deployment workflows and create value by enhancing automation and scalability across engineering operations. Responsibilities • Design, develop and deploy frontend and backend components for CI/CD and pipeline creation within the Hyperspace Portal • Lead technical architecture planning, estimation and technical breakdown of development tasks • Conduct technical investigations and resolve system defects during development and post-release phases • Design and implement scalable CI/CD pipelines to improve deployment speed and reliability • Optimize and maintain existing CI/CD workflows for better performance and maintainability • Integrate CI/CD pipelines into the Hyperspace Portal and related infrastructure tools • Provide operational support during business hours to address incidents and onboarding requests • Develop automation scripts to streamline recurring operational tasks and improve efficiency • Document technical solutions and produce onboarding guides and knowledge-sharing materials Requirements • Strong experience in software development with proficiency in Golang, JavaScript, Python or similar programming languages • Solid understanding of CI/CD concepts with hands-on experience implementing pipelines • Familiarity with Kubernetes architecture and resource modeling • Proficiency in Git, Docker and related tooling for development lifecycle management • Knowledge of agile methodologies and experience working in iterative development environments • Strong problem-solving and technical investigation skills • Ability to communicate effectively in English, both verbal and written • Experience in producing technical documentation and architecture diagrams
