About the Role - Staff Engineer – Backend Engineering Aviatrix Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) delivers the intelligence, network, and security services of Aviatrix's multi-cloud platform as a managed offering, built on modern microservices architecture. We're seeking a Staff Engineer for our SaaS Platform team to bring technical depth to backend engineering, contribute to distributed microservices and cloud-native infrastructure design on AWS, mentor junior engineers, and raise the bar on reliability, performance, and security. Responsibilities Architecture & Technical Contribution • Own design and delivery of backend platform features, breaking down complex projects into scoped tasks • Contribute to architecture discussions on scalability, fault tolerance, multi-tenancy, and operational simplicity • Collaborate on core platform services using AWS-managed services (Aurora, MSK/Kafka, ClickHouse) • Evaluate trade-offs on technology choices, system boundaries, and API contracts; write clear design docs Backend Engineering Excellence • Design high-performance microservices using gRPC, REST, and event-driven patterns (Golang preferred) • Reinforce best practices for observability, resiliency, and SLO-driven operations • Contribute to supportability (runbooks, diagnostics) and apply security best practices across service boundaries • Participate in design reviews, capacity planning, and incident retrospectives Mentorship & Collaboration • Mentor junior engineers through code reviews, design discussions, and 1-on-1s • Collaborate with Product Management; participate in on-call and incident RCA; support recruiting as needed • Requirements Experience & Education • 7+ years software engineering experience, with some mentoring experience • BS in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience) • Experience on a SaaS/PaaS product with high-availability, multi-tenancy exposure;
