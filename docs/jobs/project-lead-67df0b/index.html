About pi-labs pi-labs is a deep technology company building next-generation platforms for digital forensics, investigations, and intelligence. We combine advances in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Multimodal Intelligence, and distributed systems to solve some of the most complex challenges in analyzing and correlating information at scale. Our products transform vast volumes of structured and unstructured data into actionable intelligence through multimodal AI, intelligent document processing, facial recognition, multimedia analytics, entity resolution, graph analytics, and semantic search. Designed for mission-critical environments. Innovation is at the core of everything we build. Our engineering teams work on large-scale AI models, high-performance computing, GPU acceleration, distributed data systems, and advanced analytics to deliver solutions that operate reliably under demanding real-world conditions. At pi-labs, you'll work alongside engineers, AI researchers, and domain specialists who are passionate about translating cutting-edge research into production-grade products. If you enjoy tackling technically challenging problems and building technologies that redefine the future of digital investigations and intelligence, pi-labs is the place to make a meaningful impact. Position 1: - Technical Lead – Full Stack Engineering We’re hiring a Technical Lead for our Full Stack Engineering team in Pune. This is a hands-on leadership role. You’ll shape the architecture, write real code, mentor engineers, and take products from idea to production. We’re building the next generation of our cloud-based products and want someone who has done this before. 📍 Pune | 💼 8 to 12 years | 🕒 Full-time What you’ll be doing: · Designing and building scalable, cloud-native applications on a microservices architecture · Leading backend development in Golang and Python · Driving frontend development with React, JavaScript, and TypeScript · Designing robust APIs, distributed services, and system integrations · Managing containerized applications with Docker and Kubernetes · Owning CI/CD, deployment automation, application monitoring, and production reliability · Leading and mentoring engineers, running code reviews, and setting engineering best practices · Working with product and business teams to turn requirements into technical solutions · Driving end-to-end product development in an Agile environment What we’re looking for: · 8 to 12 years in software engineering, including experience in technical leadership · Strong hands-on expertise in Golang and Python · Good experience building frontend applications with React · Strong understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems · Practical experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud-based deployments · Experience designing scalable, reliable, and maintainable applications · Comfort working in a fast-paced environment where requirements evolve · Strong problem-solving, ownership, and team leadership skills Good to have: · Experience building applications powered by Large Language Models (LLMs) · Understanding of RAG architectures, vector databases, and LLM integrations · Exposure to Audio AI, Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), and speech-to-text technologies · Experience with AI/ML frameworks, model serving, and GPU-based inference Who should apply? Someone who can think architecturally, code hands-on, lead engineers, and deliver products end to end. If you enjoy building from the ground up and owning engineering outcomes, we’d like to hear from you.
