Software Engineer – Cloud-Native Geospatial & Analytics Platform (Bilingual Environment) Are you a software engineer looking to use your English in a cutting-edge, global-standard tech environment while building modern cloud infrastructure from the ground up? We are partnering with a fast-growing innovator in satellite data and Earth observation analytics to find a Software Engineer for their core solutions engineering team. In this role, you will help transform complex satellite and multi-source geospatial data into intuitive, actionable insights for real-world business clients. Why Apply for This Role? • High-Impact Space & Satellite Domain: Contribute to an analytics platform that turns sophisticated Earth observation data into practical solutions for industries outside the space sector. • True Engineering Autonomy: Join a lean, fast-learning team where you have the freedom to influence system architecture and decide how new features are designed and built. • Direct Business Visibility: Build internal products that directly empower sales teams and business operations, playing a vital role in scaling the company’s infrastructure during a period of rapid growth. • Global & Inclusive Culture in Japan: Collaborate daily with talented specialists across multiple disciplines in a diverse, multicultural workplace where both English and Japanese communication are valued. • Modern Cloud-Native Stack: Gain broad, flexible experience across the web stack using Kubernetes, Docker, microservices, and modern languages like Python, Rust, Go, and React. What You Will Do • Design, build, and maintain full-stack web pipelines that deploy and run geoscience algorithms in production. • Develop internal efficiency tools that streamline the entire algorithm lifecycle—supporting both data scientists (producers) and sales teams (consumers). • Collaborate cross-functionally with internal stakeholders to gather requirements, design solutions, and ship impactful products. Required Qualifications • Bilingual Communication: Japanese fluency paired with conversational to business-level English for collaborating across a diverse engineering team. • Software Engineering Experience: 2+ years of hands-on software development experience, including building and maintaining internal tools from initial requirements through deployment. • Cloud & Containerization: Proven experience building customer-facing, cloud-native systems on a major cloud provider using Kubernetes and Docker. • Architecture & Environment: Solid experience designing microservices and working within Linux-based systems and Git workflows. • Agile Mindset: Comfort working in a collaborative, flexible environment that follows agile development practices. Preferred Tech Stack & Bonus Skills • Languages & Frameworks: Hands-on experience with Python, Rust, Go (Golang), JavaScript, React, or GCP APIs. • Cloud & DevOps: Familiarity with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), GitHub Actions, CI/CD pipeline management, and containerized workloads. • Data & Developer Tooling: Exposure to Kubeflow, ML training pipelines, Flask, Jupyter Notebooks, Python Poetry, or custom VSCode tooling. • Domain Bonus: Prior experience working with GIS, map data, or geospatial services. Interested in learning more? Apply directly or send a message to discuss this confidential opportunity and see how your background aligns with the team.
