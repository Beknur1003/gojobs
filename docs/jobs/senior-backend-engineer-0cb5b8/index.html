Job description Adwake is a tech-enabled services company powered by Entravision. We combine data-driven insights, machine learning, digital media capabilities, and performance platforms, including Playback Rewards, the next-generation loyalty and rewards platform. Our mission is to help users discover new games & apps and get to earn real rewards for completing different tasks and milestones. While on the opposite side, we help mobile developers connect with new and engaged audiences. The Role in a Nutshell We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to help build and scale the platform behind our consumer rewards and offerwall products. You will work on a cloud-native, event-driven backend on Google Cloud, where reliability, correctness and fraud prevention matter as much as speed of delivery. You will own features end to end, from APIs and integrations with marketing and payment partners to the infrastructure they run on. You will work closely with product, operations and partner teams, and help shape the platform's architecture as it grows. Key Responsibilities • Design, build and maintain NestJS services and REST APIs for the rewards app, the offerwall, partner integrations and internal tooling. • Build and scale asynchronous, event-driven workflows using Pub/Sub, Eventarc, Cloud Functions and BullMQ/Redis queues. • Own the offer and reward lifecycle: offer ingestion and automation, targeting rules, conversion postbacks, reward calculation, and payouts through gift-card and payment providers. • Integrate with ad networks, attribution partners (MMPs such as AppsFlyer) and third-party services for identity verification, fraud prevention, email, CRM and messaging. • Model and query data in Firestore, Redis and BigQuery; publish analytics events to the data warehouse and keep schemas consistent across services. • Provision and evolve GCP infrastructure with Terraform: Cloud Run, Pub/Sub, IAM and service accounts, Artifact Registry, Cloud Scheduler, and monitoring alerts and SLOs. • Maintain CI/CD pipelines (CircleCI, Docker, Cloud Run deployments) and keep staging and production environments healthy. • Ensure reliability and correctness of all data flows: idempotent reward issuance, distributed locking, encryption and auditability. • Instrument services with structured logging, Sentry and Cloud Monitoring and take part in incident response. • Write tests, review code, and help raise engineering standards across the team. Required Skills and Qualifications • 5+ years of professional backend development, with production services you have designed, shipped and operated. • Strong TypeScript and Node.js knowledge, including async patterns, typing and modular code organization. • Hands-on experience with NestJS: modules, dependency injection, guards, pipes, interceptors and testing. • Experience designing and running REST APIs, including authentication (JWT, API keys), request validation (Zod or class-validator) and rate limiting. • Solid production experience on Google Cloud Platform, especially Cloud Run, Cloud Functions, Pub/Sub, BigQuery, IAM and Cloud KMS. • Infrastructure as code with Terraform, including writing and reviewing changes to shared GCP resources. • Firebase / Firestore data modeling, plus Firebase Auth and Firebase Admin SDK. • Redis for caching and job queues; experience with BullMQ or a similar queueing system. • Event-driven and asynchronous architecture: message queues, retries, idempotency and dead-letter handling. • Docker and CI/CD pipelines (CircleCI) deploying containerized services. • Testing with Jest: unit tests, mocking and integration tests with Supertest. • Observability: structured logging, error tracking (Sentry) and metrics-based alerting. • Clear written and spoken English, and comfort working across engineering, product and operation teams. Preferred Skills • Experience in rewarded engagement apps, offerwalls, or loyalty programs. • Background in performance marketing or mobile adtech: CPI/CPA offers, server-to-server postbacks, mobile measurement partners (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Singular) and ad networks. • Experience with fraud prevention, device fingerprinting and identity verification (KYC, age verification) in consumer apps. • Experience with reward fulfillment and payments: gift-card and incentive platforms (e.g. Tremendous), and KYC platforms. • Golang for lightweight, high-throughput services such as postback proxies. • Front-end experience with React, Vite, MUI and TanStack (Router, Query, Table), to maintain our internal admin tool. • Analytics engineering with BigQuery, GA4 or Snowflake, and event-schema design. • Integrations with Algolia, Intercom and CRM tools like Brevo. • Publishing and versioning private npm packages shared across multiple services. • Familiarity with mobile app backends (push notifications via FCM, device management) for Android and iOS clients. • Gmail API or email parsing. Why Join us? • Work on a high-impact, high-availability platform serving millions of users. • Collaborate with a talented and passionate team in a fast-paced, innovative environment. • Competitive salary, comprehensive benefits, and flexible remote work options. • Opportunities for professional growth and hands-on experience with cutting-edge technologies.
