Kleinanzeigen reaches 36M+ monthly users and is Germany’s leading online classifieds market. We are a sustainability-focused platform where you won't just write code; you will power the services that bridge our high-traffic backend with seamless user experiences. Joining the Edge team means working on the bedrock of Kleinanzeigen, dealing with high-throughput, low-latency and highly-efficient services that require a 'you build it, you run it' mindset, offering you deep exposure to modern infrastructure and platform architecture. Responsibilities Who You Are • Architectural Mindset: You have proven experience building systems at scale (load balancing, service discovery, dynamic routing, circuit breakers). You understand not just how to build features, but how they perform under load. • Owner’s Mentality: You seek out challenges, take the lead on troubleshooting complex production issues, and are proactive in proposing long-term technical solutions. • Technical Mentor: You elevate the engineers around you through thoughtful code reviews, collaborative pair programming, and a commitment to sharing best practices. • Effective Communicator: You can navigate trade-offs between speed and technical debt and explain your decisions clearly to the wider team. • Tech Stack: You have strong experience in Java/Go, and you are comfortable balancing that deep backend work with frontend execution using React/TypeScript. Requirements What You’ll Own As a Backend-Heavy Full Stack Engineer, you’ll be a central driver for the services that power user privacy and media delivery at scale. You will own the full development lifecycle: from design and implementation to deployment and maintenance. Your impact will include: • Core Systems Architecture: Designing and scaling critical microservices using mostly Java and Spring Boot that support 36M+ monthly users. • Full Lifecycle Responsibility: Applying "build it, run it" mentality—you aren't just writing code; you’re ensuring robustness, scalability, and observability in production. • End-to-End Delivery: Crafting seamless user experiences by integrating our robust backend APIs with high-quality, maintainable frontends in TypeScript and React. • Strategic Engineering: Driving improvements in our development practices, testing standards, and CI/CD pipelines to keep our team velocity high and our systems healthy. For frontend components, you will maintain high standards using tools like Vitest and Playwright. Our Tech Toolbox We believe in picking the right tool for the job. Our core environment is built on modern technologies, including: • Backend: Java, Spring Boot, Golang, python • Frontend: NextJs, React, TypeScript • Infrastructure & Messaging: AWS, Kafka, CloudFormation, Kubernetes We value technology-agnostic engineers who are comfortable navigating this stack and are open to learning new technologies as our systems evolve. How We Work • Autonomy: We operate in a highly autonomous, cross-functional environment where teams own their services end-to-end. • Methodology: We follow Agile methodologies with a focus on code quality, pair programming, and CI/CD best practices. • Balance: We work in a hybrid configuration that values flexibility, deep focus time, and collaborative problem-solving. Benefits At Kleinanzeigen, we care about our people — their wellbeing, growth, and life beyond work. Our benefits are designed to support you holistically, giving you flexibility and opportunities to thrive. ✨ Flexibility • 26 business days of paid annual holidays • 20 days “work from anywhere” • Flexible working time (hybrid) • Paid additional parental leave for both parents • Extra paid days off for special occasions (e.g. your wedding or a move) ❤️‍🩹 Wellbeing & Support • Private health insurance • Gym Pass (may cover up to 70% of gym membership fees) • 40€ per month as a remote working allowance. 📚 Growth & Development • Personal development budget • Learning opportunities to grow your skills and career • A unique position in an international and dynamic environment • A flat, collaborative culture where your feedback actively shapes our processes 🛍️ Perks & Everyday Benefits • Competitive compensation package (Base salary + annual bonus) • Flexible Remuneration Program (tax-efficient options for transport, restaurant vouchers and childcare) • Corporate benefits and exclusive discounts • An attractive referral program Kleinanzeigen is an equal opportunity employer that values diversity and inclusion. We welcome people of all backgrounds, races, religions, colours, national origins, gender identities, sexual orientation, ages, marital status, or disability status.
