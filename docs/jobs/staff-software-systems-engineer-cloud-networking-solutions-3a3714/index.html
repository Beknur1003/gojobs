About the Role : We are seeking an accomplished Staff Software Systems Engineer - Technical Lead to drive the development of our Network Automation and Orchestration products. In this strategic technical leadership role, you will own the end-to-end design and development of cloud networking solutions, mentor technical teams, and establish best-in-class engineering practices. Key Responsibilities : - Technically lead the team of professionals to deliver Network Automation and Orchestration products. - Own the overall design and development artifacts including functional and design specifications, code, and unit/integration test cases. - Introduce and passionately drive the implementation of best-in-class engineering practices such as Test-Driven Development and Design Principles. - Develop Epics, Stories, and Tasks for execution and collaborate across teams for complete solution integration. - Establish technical standards and mentor team members in adherence to best practices. - Drive architectural decisions and technical implementations for scalable, maintainable solutions. Required Skills and Expertise : Core Competencies : - Complete exposure to the entire Product Life Cycle (MUST HAVE). - Exceptional written and verbal communication skills. - Proficiency in local and remote debugging. Architecture and Design : - Microservices-based architecture and best practices. - Hands-on experience with Docker containers, Kubernetes (K8s, K3s) clusters, and Helm charts. - Exposure to cloud application development. - Sound data and information modeling techniques using YANG, UML, JSON, and related tools. Programming and Development : - Strong coding and debugging experience with Java or Golang. - Linux OS proficiency, advanced data structures, and IPC mechanisms. - Intricate understanding of multi-threading, routines, network programming, and object-oriented methodologies. - Agile Development Model with CI/CD, Jenkins pipeline, and Git environment. Data Management and Messaging : - Database concepts and deployment models : RDBMS, NoSQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, MariaDB. - Messaging services such as RabbitMQ and Kafka. - Working experience with webhook and web sockets. Networking and Protocols : - OpenAPI, OpenConfig, NETCONF, Streaming Telemetry, GNMI, gRPC, and XMPP. - Knowledge of L2/L3 OSI layer protocols : 802.1Q, LAGs, VxLAN, EVPN, ACLs, Route Policy, MPLS/VPLS, BGP, L2 Forwarding, and L3 Routing. Preferred Qualifications : - Experience with network orchestration platforms and automation frameworks. - Background in building scalable, highly available distributed systems. - Experience mentoring junior engineers and leading technical teams. - Familiarity with containerized deployment architectures and orchestration platforms. - Prior experience with telemetry and streaming analytics. Qualifications : - B.E/B.Tech/MS/M.Tech or equivalent in CS/EEE/ECE. - 8 - 12 Years of experience.
