Senior AI Engineer at Fourkites Team : Appointment Manager & Alan | Engineering - Facilities | India At FourKites we have the opportunity to tackle complex challenges with real-world impacts. The FourKites platform helps customers operate global supply chains that are efficient, agile and sustainable. Join a team of curious problem solvers that celebrates differences, leads with empathy and values inclusivity. As a Senior AI Engineer on the Appointment Manager and Alan teams, you will work at the frontier of agentic AI and live operational supply chain software. Appointment Manager is a mature, business-critical inward scheduling platform - enabling FourKites and our clients internal teams to manage and optimize appointment workflows. Alan is an outward-facing AI agent that brings intelligent, agentic appointment scheduling directly to our clients - about a year old, built on Pipedream, and rapidly gaining traction. LangGraph adoption is planned as the next evolution of our agentic architecture across both products. This is a full-stack ownership role - you own the entire SDLC from requirements to release, with no dedicated QA, operating in a Kanban flow augmented by AI agents (FAST, QA Agent, repo-level subagents, Claude Code) at every stage. Grooming is async and lightweight - the PM leverages agents to write features, stories, and acceptance criteria. The approach is deliver first, improve next. You are not just a consumer of AI tooling - you actively shape how these agents get smarter. What you'll be doing : - Design and implement agentic workflows in Appointment Manager and Alan; drive LangGraph adoption as it is introduced into the stack - Build and maintain microservices and workers in Java and/or GoLang on Kubernetes - Contribute to Pipedream-based workflow orchestration on the Alan agent; contribute to LangGraph integration as planned - Async grooming - consume agent-generated features, stories, and ACs from PM; clarify requirements and raise gaps as needed - Write production-grade source code and unit tests; get PRs reviewed by peers - Write and manage test cases in TestRail; implement and maintain automation suites - Own release management, regression, and sanity testing end-to-end - Manage PagerDuty on-call responsibilities for a live operational product - Operate as man-in-the-loop across AI agent tooling (FAST, QA Agent, repo subagents, Claude Code) at every stage of delivery - Actively contribute to the stability, accuracy, and capability improvement of internal AI agents - Continuously learn and stay up-to-date with agentic AI frameworks, workflow technologies, and industry trends - Guide, mentor, and review the work of fellow developers and senior developers on the team - Conduct code reviews across AM, Alan, and where needed other teams, ensuring quality, consistency, and engineering best practices - Be a technical reference point for agentic architecture decisions within and beyond the Facilities engineering group About the team : Appointment Manager is a mature, business-critical inward scheduling platform - enabling FourKites and our clients internal teams to manage and optimize appointment workflows at scale. Any bug or defect has immediate real-world impact on our clients supply chain operations. In Facilities engineering, bugs are treated one priority higher than other products. Alan is an outward-facing AI agent that brings intelligent, agentic appointment scheduling directly to our clients - automating and orchestrating scheduling workflows on their behalf. About a year old and rapidly gaining traction, Alan is deeply workflow-driven, built on Pipedream, and growing rapidly. LangGraph adoption is planned across both AM and Alan as the next evolution of our agentic architecture. The team operates on Kanban - continuous flow, no sprint ceremonies, work pulled not pushed. Grooming is async and lightweight - the PM uses agents to write features, stories, and ACs; engineers consume, clarify, and deliver. The approach is deliver first, improve next. Every engineer owns the full delivery lifecycle : design, implementation, QA, test automation, release management, and on-call. AI agents (FAST, QA Agent, repo-level subagents, Claude Code) augment every stage of the SDLC. We are aggressively pushing the boundary of what AI-augmented software delivery looks like, and we want you to be a central part of that. Who you are : - 6-8 years of overall engineering experience with hands-on experience in agentic AI and workflow-based systems - Hands-on experience with LangGraph - this is non-negotiable - Proven experience deploying and operating AI agents in production environments - Experience designing agentic workflow architecture - workers, workflow managers, and services - Production-grade microservices in Java or GoLang (one mandatory, other a plus) - Strong experience with Kubernetes for deploying and managing services - Experience with Kafka for event-driven architectures - Familiarity with ArgoCD for release and deployment management - Experience with PostgreSQL and/or ElasticSearch - Hands-on with TestRail or equivalent test management tooling - Full SDLC ownership experience - dev, QA, test automation, and release with no dedicated QA support - PagerDuty or equivalent on-call experience on a live operational product - Experience with Amazon Web Services or Azure - Pipedream experience is a plus - Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills - Ability to work independently with high ownership in a fast-moving, Kanban-based environment - Demonstrated ability to mentor and guide developers and senior developers - through code reviews, design discussions, and day-to-day collaboration - Comfortable being a technical reference point for peers and cross-team engineers on agentic systems and workflow architecture Who we are : FourKites, the leader in AI-driven supply chain transformation for global enterprises and pioneer of real-time visibility, turns supply chain data into automated action. FourKites Intelligent Control Tower breaks down enterprise silos by creating a real-time digital twin of orders, shipments, inventory and assets. This comprehensive view, combined with AI-powered digital workers, enables companies to prevent disruptions, automate routine tasks and optimize performance across their supply chain. FourKites processes over 3.2 million supply chain events daily - from purchase orders to final delivery - helping 1,600-plus global brands prevent disruptions, make faster decisions and move from reactive tracking to proactive supply chain orchestration.
