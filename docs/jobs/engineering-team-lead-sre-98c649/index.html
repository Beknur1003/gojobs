The SmartRecruiters is looking for an Engineering Team Lead that specializes in SRE. If you are passionate about keeping production up and running in a great manner, we have a spot with your name on it! In this role you can expect spending 60% of the time on hands-on contribution, 40% for managerial activities. You will: • Improve, automate and grow SmartRecruiters observability and reliability tooling • Be an advocate for modern SRE practices • Partner with Java, Python and Node.js engineers across R&D department to pursue highly available and reliable systems • Respond to production incidents and client threats, lead remediation, and drive follow‑up improvements • Document standards, best practices, and policies for monitoring, alerting, incident response, and reliability • Leading SRE-related initiatives across SmartRecruiters • Refine and drive DevOps culture for the whole organization • Provide technical vision and leadership for the team • Mentor and coach members of the engineering team We want you to: • Make a difference • Have a positive, can-do attitude • Do the right thing, not the "easy" thing • Partner with our engineering team • While not strictly required, most of our Team Leads have 7+ years of professional experience • Working knowledge of SRE and observability industry standards and best practices (SLIs/SLOs, error budgets, incident management, on‑call) • Solid experience with AWS/GCP, Kubernetes, IaC tools and practices, monitoring tools (ELK stack), running and troubleshooting distributed applications • Knowledge of the Linux operating system, with a focus on troubleshooting performance issues • Very good scripting skills (Bash, Golang or Python) • Strong communication skills, with a good understanding of English, both verbal and written • Ability to coach and influence developers, lead company-wide initiatives, guide high-performing team of SRE engineers • Commercial software development experience in JVM stack is a plus • Understanding of networking standards, including TCP/IP, DNS, VPN and load balancing is a plus SmartRecruiters is proud to be an Equal Employment Opportunity employer.
