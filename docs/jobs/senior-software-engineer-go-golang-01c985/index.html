Job Description As a Senior Software Engineer (Go/GoLang) focusing on Go and Distributed Systems, you will be part of a high-performing software team and a trusted engineering partner for global tech enterprises. Work Schedule : 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (GMT+2) Duties and Responsibilities • Design, build, and maintain microservices using Go, utilizing Python as a secondary stack. • Work on highly available distributed systems running at a global scale. • Build and optimize APIs that interact directly with real-time data pipelines. • Own the scalability, performance, and reliability across all the services you touch. • Take an active part in Agile ceremonies, technical discussions, and peer reviews. Required Skills & Qualifications • 5+ years of solid backend engineering experience. • At least 2 years of recent, hands-on experience using Go as your primary language in production work. • Strong command of Go concurrency, including goroutines, channels, sync.Mutex, WaitGroup, context, and detecting and fixing race conditions. • Solid understanding of microservices architecture and event-driven design. • Hands-on experience with AWS (specifically ECS, S3, CloudWatch, and similar tools) and PostgreSQL. • Proven experience with high-load systems, including the readiness to walk through a past project and discuss what broke at scale. • A strong sense of ownership, a real problem-solving mindset, and English proficiency. • Experience using Python for services, tooling, or data work. • Familiarity with Airflow or another ETL orchestration tool. • Background in e-commerce, logistics, fintech, or another high-traffic domain, alongside experience with CI/CD, containerization, and monitoring tools.
