Technical Product Manager - Cybersecurity SaaS Platform

Astra Security Bangalore, India опубликовано 2 сентября 2026

Lead
MicroservicesPythonPHPTypeScriptAI/LLM
Role Overview: At Astra Security, we just launched the coveted API Security Platform and are now venturing into Autonomous Pentesting using AI - an experience that helps teams discover, test and secure Web & Mobile applications, Cloud and APIs at scale. As a Technical Program Manager, you’ll orchestrate multi-team initiatives including Platform engineering, Vulnerability Scanners (for DAST, Cloud, API & Mobile) and new scanners - driving them from 0→1 to GA. You’ll pair strong business analytics with hands-on fluency in either TypeScript (preferable) / Go (Golang) / Python to shape scope, trade-offs and execution alongside SDEs and other Leads. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role working directly with the CTO, Engineering Manager and Product team. Here's what's in it for you. Technical Expertise: • Be the go-to expert in one or more core technologies (TypeScript or PHP - Laravel/Symfony) and take initiative to learn new ones. • Design and implement medium-to-large features that push the platform forward while reducing technical debt and improving maintainability. • Lead architectural discussions and ensure smooth integration across platform services, scanners and microservices. • Be hands-on with writing code, contributing a good amount of time with development tasks as required. Project Management: • Lead and manage the full project lifecycle along with the Tech leads & engineers in ensuring timely delivery of high-quality software. • Develop and maintain detailed project plans, monitor progress and report on project status to stakeholders. • Balance priorities and trade-offs across multiple initiatives, keeping alignment between engineering execution and business objectives. • Own and improve SDLC best practices across the organization. Communication and Collaboration: • Collaborate closely with engineers, QA, product owners, leadership and other stakeholders to keep initiatives aligned and outcomes successful. • Effectively communicate project goals, timelines, and expectations to cross-functional teams. • Coordinate team members providing effective feedback and moderating discussions. Risk Management: • Identify and assess potential risks early, proactively implementing mitigation strategies to keep initiatives on track. • Handle unexpected challenges with resilience and adaptability. Ownership and Operations: • Own the production operation, ensuring timely delivery of technical projects and monitoring system performance. • Evolve the architecture to support future requirements and define its SLAs. Leadership and Management: • Step in as an Engineering Manager when needed, providing clarity, leadership and guidance to the team. • Support team growth by managing career development, performance, expectations and overall well-being. • Define and adapt processes that fit the team’s maturity level, balancing agility with the discipline needed for scale. Impact and Evolution: • Champion innovation by pushing boundaries in autonomous pentesting, vulnerability scanners, and new product initiatives. • Make an impact on the whole tech organization/product engineer teams. • Lead the 0→1 journey of upcoming products in the platform along with respective Tech Lead/SDEs.
