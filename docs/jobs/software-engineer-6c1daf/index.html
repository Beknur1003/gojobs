Job Summary As a Software Engineer, your responsibilities will include developing and maintaining cloud-based solutions. You will focus on solving complex problems, testing, automating and collaborating with the Software Engineering team to deploy features in a production environment. The team is responsible for maintenance & enhancements to existing technologies & products. Job Requirements • Excellent Problem solver, and a proficient coder. • Strong oral and written communication skills. • Proficient with any of the languages C, C++, Golang or Python. • Thorough understanding of Linux or other Unix-like Operating Systems. • Strong in Data Structure and algorithms. • Understanding of Container based technologies preferably Kubernetes & Dockers and experience with Cloud service APIs (e.g. AWS, Azure or GCP) is desirable. • Understanding of Block/File technologies having hands-on experience is desirable. • Continuously monitor, analyze, and measure system health, availability, and latency using Google native tooling. • Engage in incident management processes including the 24X7 Oncall rotations ( as per the sun model) to resolve production issues within agreed SLAs/SLOs. Education • B.E/B.Tech or M.S or Mtech in Computer Science or related technical field. • 2 years of experience and must be hands-on with coding.
