Senior Backend Developer PayPlus is looking for a self-motivated Senior Backend Developer to help build and scale our payments and commerce platform using Node.js and Go (Golang). Strong Node.js expertise is required; hands-on Go experience is a significant advantage. You will take a leading technical role in developing new products and enhancing existing systems, combining hands-on development with architectural ownership. The ideal candidate has experience with large-scale applications, takes responsibility for production outcomes, and brings strong communication and collaboration skills. Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using Node.js, TypeScript, and Go. • Write clean, efficient, well-tested, and maintainable code. • Own features from technical design through implementation, deployment, and production support. • Develop APIs, database integrations, third-party interfaces, and event-driven workflows. • Improve application performance, database efficiency, and system resilience. • Collaborate with product, frontend, QA, and infrastructure teams to meet business, security, and compliance requirements. • Troubleshoot production issues, investigate root causes, and implement lasting solutions. • Contribute to architectural decisions, lead code reviews, and mentor other developers. • Improve automated testing, deployment processes, and engineering standards. Required Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • 6+ years of relevant backend development experience, with demonstrated ownership of complex production services. • Strong hands-on expertise in Node.js, JavaScript, and TypeScript. • Deep understanding of asynchronous programming, the Node.js event loop, error handling, and performance optimization. • Strong knowledge of software design principles, object-oriented design, and database design. • Experience designing REST APIs and integrating with external services. • Understanding of data consistency and reliable service communication, including timeouts, retries, and idempotency. • Experience with automated testing, Git, and collaborative development practices. • Understanding of application security, authentication, authorization, and secure data handling. • Experience working with Agile or Scrum methodologies. • Ability to work independently, prioritize work, and communicate technical decisions clearly. • Strong teamwork, problem-solving, and mentoring skills. • Good written and spoken English. Preferred Qualifications • Production experience with Go (Golang), including goroutines, channels, context cancellation, and building concurrent services a significant advantage. • Experience designing and operating microservices-based architectures. • Experience with relational, NoSQL, or graph databases, including PostgreSQL • Experience with message brokers and event-streaming platforms, such as RabbitMQ or Kafka. • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines. • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure, preferably AWS. • Familiarity with GraphQL or gRPC. • Working knowledge of Vue.js or React. • Experience with Redis, caching strategies, or workflow orchestration platforms such as Temporal. • Experience in payments, fintech, billing, subscriptions, or high-volume transaction processing.
