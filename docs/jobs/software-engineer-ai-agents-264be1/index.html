OUR STORY: 🇪🇺 Join Scaleway and shape the sovereign cloud of tomorrow ! Since 1999, we have been designing secure, sustainable infrastructures aimed at supporting the most ambitious companies. Historically known for our dedicated servers (Dedibox), we made a strategic shift to cloud computing in 2015. Staying true to our principles of simplicity, flexibility, and technical excellence, we have become one of the leading players in Europe in the sector. With the rise of artificial intelligence, we have strengthened our commitment, supported by the Iliad Group, which is investing €3 billion to develop a serious, sovereign AI alternative to American and Asian giants. Every day, thanks to our fast-growing portfolio of cloud and AI products (bare metal, containerization, serverless, AI, etc.), Scaleway proudly serves thousands of customer across the private and public sector, from corporations like France Télévisions or Hachette Livre, to fast-growing startups like Photoroom and Biolevate, to institutions like the City of Copenhagen. 📍 Our offices are located in Paris, Lille, Toulouse, Rennes, Rouen, Bordeaux and Lyon. WHY WE NEED YOU? As Scaleway continues to expand its AI ecosystem, we are building a new AI Agentic Platform to enable customers to build, deploy and operate AI agents at scale. Our ambition is to provide a powerful and reliable platform that helps businesses automate complex workflows, develop intelligent chatbots and orchestrate multi-agent systems. As a Software Engineer, you will play a key role in designing and developing this platform, building the tools and infrastructure needed to simplify AI agent development, ensure scalability and reliability, and deliver a seamless developer experience. This is an opportunity to build a new AI product from the ground up, tackle challenging engineering problems and contribute directly to Scaleway's growing AI ecosystem. YOUR FUTURE TEAM You will join our AI Agents team, composed of several Software Engineers and an Engineering Manager, working together to build and scale Scaleway's AI Agentic Platform. You will collaborate closely with: • The AI Inference team, to ensure seamless integration, performance and compatibility with Scaleway's LLM catalog. • The AI Agents Product Manager, to shape the product roadmap, define priorities and drive the platform's development. • The SRE teams, to ensure reliable deployments, scalability and operational excellence in production. You will evolve in a collaborative, highly technical environment, with the opportunity to contribute to architectural decisions, influence product development and help shape the future of AI agents at Scaleway. 🗓️ YOUR DAILY ROUTINE INCLUDES: • Design and develop the Scaleway AI Agentic Platform API • Deploy and ensure security, reliability, availability and quality of service of the production platforms • Research and develop new features, build tooling, do technical research, anticipate issues, write architectural designs and documentation 🔍 ABOUT YOU: HARDSKILLS • Senior profile with extensive experience in Software Engineering, complex systems architecture. • Significant experience in API or software development • Go / Golang software development • Deploying and operating Kubernetes Applications and Operators • Working with Containers (Image creation, deployment) • Understanding of LLMs and their usage and extension patterns • Optimization of LLM agents and workflows • Working with AI Agents harnesses and frameworks (Langchain, CrewAI, etc.) • Using AI Coding agents • Writing REST and gRPC APIs • Designing, querying, optimizing SQL Databases (PostgreSQL) • Orchestrating reliable asynchronous task workflows • Experience with using cloud services SOFTSKILLS • Fast learner with high adaptability to new technologies • Strong curiosity and willingness to dive deep into emerging AI tools • Proactive mindset and readiness to take initiative • Technical autonomy and ownership of your scope • Clear communicator, capable of explaining complex systems simply WHAT YOU WILL FIND AT SCALEWAY ?
