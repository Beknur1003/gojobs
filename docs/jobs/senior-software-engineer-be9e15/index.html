Senior Software Engineer

Superlaw Bengaluru, Karnataka, India опубликовано 17 ноября 2026

Senior
DockerPythonAI/LLM
About the job Experience: 3-5 years Position: Individual contributor; senior backend engineer Reporting to: CTO Superlaw is hiring a Senior Backend Engineer to build and improve the backend systems that power our AI products for legal and governance workflows. This is a hands-on individual contributor role combining Python development, backend service and API design, and ownership of reliable software delivery. You'll work directly with the CTO and the product and engineering teams to take features from requirements to release, and grow toward independently owning core parts of our backend. About Superlaw Superlaw builds AI solutions for legal and governance workflows, with an initial focus on the insurance sector. Our products include an AI assistant, drafting engine, and an intelligence layer that integrates into customers' existing systems. Our platform spans multiple services, data stores, and external integrations. Success requires clean design, careful engineering, and consistent follow-through. What you'll do Build and maintain backend services • Develop and maintain backend applications and services, primarily in Python. • Design and build APIs that connect our frontend, internal services, and external systems. • Work with databases, data models, and queries to support product functionality and efficient data access. • Coordinate integrations with external systems alongside the CTO and technical team. Improve reliability and code quality • Write clean, maintainable code, backed by automated tests and clear technical documentation. • Debug issues, investigate performance bottlenecks, and improve the reliability of existing systems. • Participate in code reviews and apply good practices for authentication, access controls, and secure data handling. Take ownership and collaborate • Own backend features from requirements through implementation, testing, release, and ongoing improvement. • Work with the CTO, frontend engineers, and product team to define technical approaches, agree on interfaces, and resolve dependencies. • Communicate progress, technical decisions, and delivery risks clearly. The CTO will initially lead architecture direction and final technical decisions. You'll own the implementation, quality, and delivery of the features assigned to you. What we're looking for • 3-5 years of professional software engineering experience, including hands-on backend development. • Strong proficiency in Python as your primary language, with experience building and maintaining backend applications or services. • Hands-on experience with Docker and Git, including containerised development and collaborative version control. • Demonstrated ability to build and integrate APIs, with a solid understanding of request handling, validation, and error handling. • Working knowledge of databases, data modelling, and writing efficient queries. • Evidence of writing readable, maintainable code and working with automated testing, debugging, and code reviews. • Strong grasp of backend fundamentals, including performance, reliability, and application security. • Strong written and spoken communication, with the ability to explain technical decisions to technical and non-technical teammates. • Strong ownership, disciplined follow-through, and comfort working independently in an early-stage startup. Experience that would be particularly valuable • Exposure to Go (Golang). • Familiarity with cloud environments and automated build, testing, and deployment workflows. • Experience with background tasks, asynchronous processing, or integrations with external services. • Experience integrating AI capabilities into software products. You don't need to meet every preferred qualification. We value relevant results, curiosity, sound judgment, and ownership. Why join Superlaw? • Work directly with the CTO and contribute to decisions about how our backend systems are built. • Take ownership of meaningful features and see them through to use in the product. • Build expertise in AI solutions for legal and governance workflows. • Help improve engineering practices, code quality, and the reliability of a growing platform. • Grow toward owning core backend systems and, as the business scales, technical leadership. How to apply Apply through LinkedIn with your resume. Include a brief note describing a backend system or feature you've worked on, your specific contribution, and why you want to help build Superlaw. You're welcome to share a GitHub profile or relevant project links.
