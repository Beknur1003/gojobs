Who We Are Vultr is on a mission to make high-performance cloud infrastructure easy to use, affordable, and locally accessible for enterprises and AI innovators around the world. With 33 global cloud data center locations, Vultr is trusted by hundreds of thousands of active customers across 185 countries for its flexible, scalable, global Cloud Compute, Cloud GPU, Bare Metal, and Cloud Storage solutions. In December 2024 Vultr announced an equity financing at a $3.5 billion valuation. Founded by David Aninowsky and self-funded for over a decade, Vultr has grown to become the world’s largest privately-held cloud infrastructure company. Vultr Cares • 100% company-paid insurance premiums for employee medical, dental and vision plans. • 401(k) plan that matches 100% up to 4%, with immediate vesting • Professional Development Reimbursement of $2,500 each year • 11 Holidays + Paid Time Off Accrual + Rollover Plan • Commitment matters to Vultr! Increased PTO at 3 year and 10 year anniversary + 1 month paid sabbatical every 5 years + Anniversary Bonus each year • $500 stipend for remote office setup in first year + $400 each following year • Internet reimbursement up to $75 per month • Gym membership reimbursement up to $50 per month • Company paid Wellable subscription Join Vultr Vultr is seeking a Software Engineer to join the Core Cloud Engineering team and help build the out-of-band (OOB) control plane that sits underneath every server in our fleet. This is the layer that makes a rack of bare metal behave like cloud -- IPMI gateways, Redfish abstractions, DPU control and provisioning, serial-over-LAN, and the automation that ties them together. If you like working close to the hardware, writing systems code in Rust (with some Go and PHP in the mix), and shipping infrastructure that other engineers depend on every day, this is a strong fit. You will be writing real production code from day one against real hardware in real datacenters, with a team of engineers who will help you go deep fast. Key Responsibilities • Build and maintain services that interact with server BMCs over IPMI and Redfish across a heterogeneous hardware fleet (Dell, Supermicro, HPE, AMD, NVIDIA platforms) • Develop control-plane tooling for DPU provisioning and lifecycle management (NVIDIA BlueField, AMD Pensando) • Implement and harden serial-over-LAN, virtual media, and remote console workflows • Write Rust services (and Go/PHP where appropriate) that are observable, well-tested, and safe to run unattended at fleet scale • Contribute to internal SDKs and shared libraries used by other infrastructure teams • Partner with datacenter operations and hardware vendors to debug firmware, BMC, and DPU behavior in production • Participate in design reviews, code reviews, and on-call rotations for the OOB control plane • Improve test coverage, CI/CD pipelines, and tooling that lets us ship hardware automation safely • Document interfaces, runbooks, and failure modes so the system stays operable as it grows Qualifications • 2+ years of professional software engineering experience building backend services or systems software • Production experience in at least one of Rust, Go, or C/C++ -- and willingness to ramp on Rust as the primary language • Solid working knowledge of Linux -- processes, networking, systemd, basic kernel concepts • Comfortable with HTTP APIs, JSON, gRPC, and at least one of the common RPC/IPC patterns • Familiarity with at least one of IPMI, Redfish, BMC tooling, PXE/iPXE, or bare metal provisioning -- or strong systems fundamentals and clear interest in learning it • Comfortable reading vendor documentation, RFCs, and packet captures to figure out how something actually works • Strong written communication -- can write a clear design doc, PR description, or incident postmortem • Bonus: exposure to DPUs, SmartNICs, SR-IOV, IOMMU, or virtualization stacks (KVM/QEMU/libvirt) • Bonus: experience with BGP, VXLAN, or datacenter networking Compensation $80,000 -
