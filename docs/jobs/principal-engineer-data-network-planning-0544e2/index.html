Job Summary The Principal Engineer Data Network Planning at Lightpath plays a critical role in shaping and executing network strategies to optimize the company's next-generation fiber-optic network. This role requires a blend of technical expertise, strategic vision, and leadership skills to oversee the design, architecture, and implementation of scalable and secure network solutions. Key responsibilities include developing and enforcing network policies, leading network platform decisions, and ensuring the network meets performance and reliability standards. The role also involves collaborating closely with various business units, including Sales Engineering, Product, and Operations, to design and deliver custom network solutions that exceed customer expectations. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in telecommunications, with extensive knowledge of network protocols (such as MP-BGP, OSPF, LDP, ISIS, SR and MPLS), experience with network devices and services, and a proven track record in managing large-scale network projects. They will also possess strong project management skills and the capability to stay ahead of industry trends and technological advancements. The Principal Engineer will be instrumental in ensuring that Lightpath’s network infrastructure is robust, scalable, and aligned with the company’s strategic objectives, all while maintaining a focus on cost-effective solutions and continuous improvement. the core network. • Prepare Engineering Cost and Planning estimates & Projections for Large Scale Projects and Customer Solutions.
