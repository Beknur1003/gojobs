We're BlueCat—a Great Place to Work for good reason. Our team solves critical network challenges for some of the world's largest organizations. In simple terms, we manage the systems that keep networks running smoothly, securely, and reliably—the backbone infrastructure that powers digital transformation for enterprises globally. Our Intelligent Network Operations platform delivers and enables AI-driven agentic ops at scale, automating and simplifying how companies manage, secure, and optimize their networks. But what makes us different is how we work: we believe great work happens in an environment where you're trusted, heard, and supported. With teams globally, we're building a workplace culture that values collaboration and integrity as much as innovation. If you're looking to advance your career with a company that invests in its people, this is it. About the role: As a Senior Software Engineer on BlueCat’s Advanced DDI team, you’ll design, build and run core services of the BlueCat Cloud Platform. This platform delivers enterprise-grade DNS, DHCP and IPAM (DDI) to customers around the world. Our services are built in Rust and Go and run on Kubernetes. You’ll own significant pieces of the system end to end, write production code every day, and help raise the quality of the team’s work, on systems where safety, reliability and performance matter most. You’ll work closely with product, security, SRE and infrastructure partners, and you’ll help shape how our team uses AI-assisted development. We welcome engineers early in their senior career as well as those with deep experience. If you’ve been owning features and services and want to grow your scope, we’d like to hear from you. What you'll do: • Design, build and maintain high-performance services in Rust and Go that tolerate real-world failure: network partitions, cascading failures, resource exhaustion and unexpected traffic. • Take ambiguous problems and turn them into clear designs with trade-offs, then lead them through delivery. • Own the reliability of your services: telemetry, alerting, guardrails and operational workflows that reduce downtime and speed recovery. • Partner with product, security and SRE to define requirements and keep our services secure by default. • Contribute to design reviews and code reviews, and mentor mid-level and junior engineers. • Use AI coding agents in your daily work, and contribute shared skills, prompts and practices that make them effective for the whole team. What we're looking for: • Typically 5+ years of software development, including building and running production services. Experience with distributed systems is a strong plus. • Degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • Sound judgment that balances technical quality against delivery and real-world constraints. Technical depth • Solid production experience in at least one of Rust or Go, and the willingness and ability to work productively in the other. You don’t need to be an expert in both. We’ll help you ramp up. • Rust: ownership, async and concurrency patterns. • Go: goroutines and channels, idiomatic design. • Working knowledge of networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP and HTTP/S. You can reason about latency and performance when something misbehaves. • Experience running services on Kubernetes and Docker containers, including image construction, debugging and security basics. AI-assisted development • Hands-on experience using AI coding assistants and agents in daily engineering work, with good judgment about when to trust, verify or override their output. • Experience configuring or extending agents, such as writing skills, prompts or project instructions. • A habit of reviewing and testing AI-generated code to the same standard as any other change. Reliability and security • You build for failure: sensible timeouts and retries, observability, and tests that cover the unhappy paths. • You think about how your code behaves in CI/CD, deployment and production, not only in development. • You follow secure development practices and flag risks early. Collaboration • You work effectively with product, security, SRE and infrastructure. • You give clear, constructive code reviews and enjoy helping teammates grow. Bonus points • Experience with both Rust and Go, including Tokio. • Python for tooling and automation. • Experience with agent workflows or internal developer tooling around AI. • Microservices, service mesh or high-availability system experience. • Familiarity with DNSSEC, DHCP failover, IPAM or other DDI technologies. • Open-source contributions in Rust or Go.
