Site Reliability Engineer

PT Fita Sehat Nusantara Jakarta опубликовано 17 сентября 2026

Офис
GraphQLKubernetesTerraformGCPMicroservicesCI/CDTypeScriptNode.js
Fita is a health-tech platform that aims to plug the preventive healthcare gap in Indonesia with localized content that has been curated by certified fitness trainers & nutritionists. We are looking for Site Reliability Engineer to ensure the optimal resources of infrastructure can be delivered through agile process to support system/software development process by tightly engaged with Engineering Team. Ultimately, you will execute and automate operational processes fast, accurately and securely. In Fita, you possess absolute ownership over the systems in your business scope of control. As we have a very dynamic culture, a creative and analytical individual who is not afraid to speak up, take charge, and be bold would be the next rockstar candidates. Our Tech Stack: • Golang and Typescript for Backend • Kubernetes for Backend • GraphQL for API Gateway in NodeJS and Apollo • Google Cloud Platform in Indonesia region with services BigQuery, Pubsub, GKE, Cloud SQL • Flutter for Mobile Apps Responsibility: • Participate in the entire software development process including design, development, delivery, monitoring, and improvement • Provide technical assistance to improve system performance, capacity, reliability, and scalability • Scale systems sustainably through mechanisms like automation, and evolve systems by pushing for changes that improve reliability and velocity • Closely collaborating with other engineering and product teams to ensure that expected system behavior is understood and monitoring exists to detect anomalies • Participate in continuous improvement and execution of quality and timely major incident root cause analysis and blameless post-mortem activities to ensure we take action to avoid similar problems in the future Requirements: • Preferably 1+ years of experience as Site Reliability Engineer/Software Engineer/Systems Engineer or similar software engineering role • Bachelor degree in Computer Science or related fields, or equivalent professional experience • Solid understanding of Agile Methods and Infrastructure as Code • Experience and advanced understanding of Observability, CI/CD and release management • Experience working with large-scale distributed systems with understanding of microservices architecture concepts • Experience in Kubernetes, Kubernetes Ingress preferred • Experience in Infrastructure-as-Code and Automation (ex : Terraform, CloudFormation, Ansible, Packer and Git, GitHub) preferred
