AI/ML Lead Engineer - Full Remote, South Africa - Up to R130,000 CTC *Applicants must be based in South Africa* We’re looking for a hands-on AI / ML Engineer to join a specialist engineering team delivering real-world machine learning and generative AI solutions on AWS, for an AWS Premier Tier Partner. This is not a research or theory-heavy role. It’s about building, integrating, and shipping, taking AI workloads from early discovery through to MVPs and production-ready solutions for clients across multiple industries. They tend to work with start-ups or small to medium size businesses. You’ll work closely with clients during technical discovery and delivery, while collaborating with experienced cloud and DevOps engineers to deploy scalable, production-grade ML and GenAI systems. What you’ll be doing • Owning delivery of ML and GenAI workloads from discovery through to deployment • Designing, building, and deploying solutions using AWS SageMaker and AWS Bedrock • Implementing GenAI workflows (prompt pipelines, embeddings, retrieval-based patterns) • Building Python-based services and APIs to support AI workloads • Contributing to PoCs, MVPs, and client proposals • Working with DevOps teams on CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) • Engaging directly with technical and non-technical stakeholders during delivery What we’re looking for • 3+ years’ hands-on experience in applied ML, AI engineering, or software engineering with real AI exposure (5+ total years experience with engineering or data science) • Ability to lead projects end to end (including deployment with Kubernetes), mentor junior and mid level engineers. • Strong Python skills with experience writing production-level code • Practical experience delivering ML workloads using AWS SageMaker • Hands-on experience with AWS Bedrock or similar managed GenAI platforms • Experience deploying GenAI / LLM-based solutions into production or near-production • Experience building and integrating APIs • Comfortable working in cloud-native, Linux-based environments • Confident leading technical discussions with clients Nice to have • Experience with OpenSearch, RDS, Step Functions, ECS/EKS • Exposure to AWS managed AI services (Rekognition, Transcribe, Comprehend, etc.) • Experience with Golang • Background in consulting or client-facing delivery environments
