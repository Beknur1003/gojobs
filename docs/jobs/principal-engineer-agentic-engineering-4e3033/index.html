Our agents already write, review and test code. Are you excited about taking that to 100 PRs a day per engineer? The way software gets built has changed more in the last two years than in the previous twenty. Inspired by what engineers at places like SpaceX and Anthropic are doing, some of our engineers have started to approach escape velocity, pairing deep engineering judgment with fleets of AI coding agents to ship at a pace that would have sounded absurd not long ago. We're looking for a Principal Engineer in Sydney to take what's working and scale it across the global engineering organisation. We've already automated large parts of the workflow, including coding, code review and QA. You'll figure out what it takes to go from here to 100 PRs a day: the tooling, the review and testing practices, the CI/CD pipelines, the guardrails, and the culture. Then you'll make it the way Freelancer builds software. This isn't a cog-in-the-machine role. If you join a mega-cap tech company as the 10,000th hire, you may struggle to see your impact. If you join a startup, you may get to play with the latest tools but on toy problems at toy scale. At Freelancer you get both: frontier engineering practices applied to a real, global, internet-scale marketplace with tens of millions of users. What you'll do • Push the frontier. Take the agent workflows we already run across coding, code review and QA, and work out what the next order of magnitude demands, from task decomposition and agent orchestration to deployment at very high throughput. • Scale the platform. Extend and harden the internal tooling, infrastructure and CI/CD that already carry agent-generated code to production, so they hold up at many times today's volume. • Keep quality high as volume multiplies. Scale the automated testing, static analysis, observability and security checks we already rely on, so 100x the velocity stays sustainable rather than reckless. • Lead by example. Ship serious volume yourself on the workflows we've built, then make them better and spread them. Mentor senior engineers and help every team, across frontend (Angular, TypeScript, RxJS) and backend services (messaging, payments, translations, file services, and a notification system sending millions of notifications an hour), go from early adopter to full speed. • Measure what matters. Track throughput, lead time, change failure rate and real user impact as we scale, so each step up means more value shipped and not just more PRs. Must haves: • Tertiary degree in Computer Science or Engineering (or related), or equivalent depth of experience • Extensive experience as a senior or staff-level engineer on large-scale production systems • Hands-on, heavy use of AI coding agents (e.g. Claude Code or similar) in your daily work, with strong opinions on what works and what doesn't • Fluency in several languages such as Python, Golang, PHP, Node.js, or TypeScript • Deep experience with relational databases and SQL, preferably MySQL • Strong background in CI/CD, automated testing, and developer tooling • Linux and cloud experience, preferably AWS • A track record of changing how an engineering team works, not just what it builds • Excellent written and verbal communication; you can persuade sceptics with evidence • Strong ownership: you don't need hand-holding, and you finish what you start Nice-to-haves: • Experience building internal developer platforms or agent orchestration tooling • Experience with SOA, microservices, and containerisation • Experience with public-facing APIs • Strong knowledge of web security and OWASP best practices • Open source contributions, especially to AI developer tooling • MS or PhD degree Why Freelancer: • Real scale, real impact. You'll solve complex, global problems on a marketplace serving users across 247 countries, regions, and territories. Our mission is to provide one billion jobs. • Meritocracy. We actively promote from within and are known for growing business and product leaders. • Ship constantly. We already deploy to production multiple times a day, and you'll take that much further. • Life at Grosvenor Place. Harbour views, a fully stocked kitchen, beer taps, and a bar with a breathtaking view. • Friday lunches from a rotating selection of local restaurants. • Weekly Town Halls with open Q&A with our CEO (ask him anything). • Quarterly hackathons, two days of building, games, food, and prizes. • Team events such as speed boats on the harbour, escape rooms, karaoke, laser tag, and poker nights. If you want to help define what software engineering looks like in the agentic era, and prove it at internet scale, we'd love to hear from you.
