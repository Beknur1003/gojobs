Get to Know the Role You will be responsible for owning test strategy planning, priorisation, execution, maintenance, and promoting automation and best practice in testing to ensure high product quality in FundsCore platforms. You will be participating in our full software development life cycle, including contributions to our product or system design. And as a Software Engineer in Test (SET), you are expected to contribute to fixing bugs and driving our Operational Excellence, such as performing On-Call duties. You will report to a Senior Engineering Manager and work onsite at Grab HCMC, Vietnam office.
