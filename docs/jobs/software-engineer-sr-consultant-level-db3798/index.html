About Us Visa is a world leader in payments technology, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories, dedicated to uplifting everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid. At Visa, you'll have the opportunity to create impact at scale — tackling meaningful challenges, growing your skills and seeing your contributions impact lives around the world. Join Visa and do work that matters – to you, to your community, and to the world. Progress starts with you. Job Description Visa’s Technology Organization is a community of problem solvers and innovators reshaping the future of commerce. We operate the world’s most sophisticated processing networks capable of handling more than 65k secure transactions a second across 80M merchants, 15k Financial Institutions, and billions of everyday people. While working with us, you’ll get to work on complex distributed systems and solve massive scale problems centered on new payment flows, business and data solutions, cyber security, and B2C platforms. Job Opportunity: We are looking for dedicated, curious, and energetic Software Engineers who embrace solving complex challenges at scale. As a Visa Software Engineer, you will be an integral part of a multi-functional development team inventing, developing, and operating software products and services that help Visa manage a broad portfolio of critical risk programs efficiently at scale. Your work will accelerate Visa business into new markets and sectors and empower and enable multiple product and business groups across Visa to deliver innovations faster. Key Responsibilities: • Partner with product owners to gather and refine requirements across product teams to create roadmap, execute and adapt the roadmap in the fast-paced environment. • Own Visa’s enterprise-wide risk platform that empowers employes to drive organizational and product improvements through collaborative idea sharing and the entire lifecycle from ideation to delivery. • Design, implement, and enhance systems and shared technologies end-2-end that enable and improve Visa offerings which touch 40% of the world population while influencing Visa’s internal standards for quality, observability, scalability, security, and reusability. • Make a difference on a global or local scale through mentorship and continued learning opportunities • Use and integrate GenAI and agents in day-to-day activities across SDLC and devops to improve ideation to production delivery cycle time and quality • Come up with innovative ideas to integrate GenAI to enhance and build new product capabilities and agents, and deliver measurable impact to users • Drives advanced architect solutions that are robust and scalable, considering integrations with other solutions across the internal technical ecosystem, and external services Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager. Qualifications Basic Qualifications: • Master's degree in Math, Science, Engineering, or Computer Science, Information Systems or related field AND minimum five (5) years of experience in a directly related field. OR Bachelor's degree in Math, Science, Engineering, or Computer Science, Information Systems or related field AND minimum eight (8) years of experience in a directly related field. OR Minimum eleven (11) years of directly related experience. Preferred Qualifications: • Ability to provide technical leadership to their team to successful technical outcomes, ensuring sound engineering decisions that balance business needs along with engineering and operational excellence • Strong product mindset and customer focus to contribute meaningfully to planning and collaborate with PMs and team leads to shape team vision. • Experience in one or more of the high-level programming languages (e.g., Java, Python, C#, Golang). • Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases (e.g., MySQL, Cassandra, MongoDB). • Experience with using GenAI, copilots, and agentic software for improving agility, quality, and security in SDLC • Proven experience architecting, building, deploying, and operating large scale, highly available, fault-tolerant, and elastic microservices in cloud-native environments. • Proficiency in REST/GraphQL/gRPC API design, event-driven architectures, and message queues • Familiarity with front-end development with React or Angular • Experience building AI-powered product capabilities is a plus • Experience in developing and integrating with any Governance, Risk, and Compliance platform is a big plus.
