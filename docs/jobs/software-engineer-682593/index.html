Software Engineer Melbourne, Australia Build software that powers conversations around the world. At Dubber, we're building technology that helps organisations capture, understand and unlock the value of conversations using AI. Our platform is embedded into some of the world's largest telecommunications networks and trusted by service providers and enterprises across APAC, North America, the UK and Europe. Every day, millions of conversations flow through our platform, helping businesses improve compliance, gain customer insights, and transform conversations into actionable intelligence. Dubber is an ASX-listed technology company (ASX: DUB) with a global reach and team operating primarily in APAC, North America, UK and Europe. The Opportunity We're looking for experienced Software Engineers to join our Melbourne engineering team. You'll work alongside talented engineers, product managers and designers to build cloud-native applications, develop new AI-powered capabilities, and deliver software used by customers around the world. This is a hands-on engineering role where you'll contribute across the full software development lifecycle, from design and architecture through to implementation, testing and deployment. If you enjoy solving complex technical problems, building scalable systems and working with modern technologies, we'd love to hear from you. What You'll Be Doing As part of one of our cross-functional engineering squads, you'll: • Design and build scalable cloud-native applications • Develop modern web applications using React and TypeScript • Build backend services using C# .NET and/or Go • Design and consume RESTful APIs • Work closely with Product and Design to deliver new features • Contribute to architecture and engineering discussions • Improve system performance, reliability and security • Write clean, maintainable, well-tested code • Continuously improve our engineering practices and tooling What You'll Bring You'll have experience in several of the following: • Commercial software engineering experience • React and TypeScript • C# .NET and/or Go • Kubernetes and modern cloud platforms • REST API design and development • Node.js or other backend technologies • Automated testing and CI/CD practices • Agile software development • Software architecture and design patterns Bonus points if you've worked with Ruby or other programming languages. A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline is welcomed but not essential if you have equivalent industry experience. About You You're someone who enjoys building quality software and working with great people. You: • Enjoy solving challenging technical problems • Take ownership and pride in your work • Like collaborating and sharing ideas • Are curious and always looking to learn • Care about writing clean, maintainable code • Thrive in an environment where everyone contributes Why Join Dubber? You'll be joining a global engineering team that's building technology used at massive scale. We offer: • Competitive salary and performance bonus • Opportunities to work with modern cloud and AI technologies • A collaborative engineering culture with room to influence technical direction • Career growth in a global technology company • The opportunity to build products that make a genuine impact for customers around the world If you're looking for a role where your work matters and you can help shape the future of conversational AI, we'd love to hear from you.
