Title: Software Developer Company: ALXICORN Location: Fully Remote Job Type: Full-time About Us ALXICORN is a UK-based technology company operating within the realm of Blockchain, FinTech, RegTech, Computer Vision, RPA and Data Automation. We build innovative, high-performance financial systems that challenge traditional trading infrastructure in-order to help shape the future of centralised and decentralised finance. Our remote engineering teams in Bangladesh thrive in a research first, reverse engineering driven culture, where deep technical exploration meets real-world system development. We don’t just use technology, we break it down, understand it, and rebuild it better. Position Summary We are seeking ambitious individuals who thrive on technical challenges and enjoy working with unconventional technology stacks. You'll play a key role in turning innovative concepts into robust, real-world software solutions that deliver meaningful value. As a Software Developer at ALXICORN, you'll work hands-on with backend systems, blockchain applications, and intelligent document processing platforms. You'll design and build automation solutions across a variety of domains, leveraging computer vision and Robotic Process Automation (RPA) technologies to solve complex business challenges. Working closely with engineers and researchers, you'll help transform innovative ideas into scalable, production-ready software that delivers reliable, high-quality solutions aligned with project requirements. In this hybrid role, you will split your time between core backend, trading, and automation systems and building AI agents (role specific). You will design agents that plan, use tools, and complete multi-step tasks reliably and safely. What we are looking for Someone who loves to build by researching, enjoys solving complex problems, and is excited by the following domains: • Blockchain & Web3 • FinTech & Financial Infrastructure • Automation Pipelines • Computer Vision-Based Technology • Robotic Process Automation • AI Agents & Agentic Workflows • LLM Applications (prompting, RAG, tool use) This role is made for you if you thrive on tackling complex, real-world engineering challenges at the intersection of finance and cutting-edge technology. Key Responsibilities • Develop and optimize backend services and automation scripts across Python, TypeScript, Golang, and Rust • Design and improve robotic process automation systems to streamline operational workflows • Design and build multi-step AI agents with tool/function calling, planning, memory, and error recovery • Develop, version, and maintain prompts, system instructions, and agent skills for each role • Build RAG pipelines (embeddings, vector databases, document ingestion) for company knowledge and JD/CV data. • Conduct technical research on document processing systems and provide reliable solutions for that • Build and maintain data collection systems, web scrapers, and API integrations that support real financial infrastructure • Contribute to exchange-related systems including market data feeds, trade tracking, and live analytics • Work on real-time, high-frequency trading systems where performance and precision are critical • Conduct deep technical research on blockchain technologies, exchange architectures, and APIs — research is a core part of this role, not an afterthought • Work with time-series databases and structured data to store, process, and analyze system outputs • Debug issues and drive improvements in performance, stability, and system reliability • Collaborate with engineers on system design, architecture decisions, and technical planning • Support blockchain and centralized exchange research, including proof-of-concept development • Participate in code reviews, sprint planning, and technical discussions • Monitor logs and production environments to identify and resolve issues proactively • Maintain daily work logs, task updates, and KPI tracking using internal tools • Provide support to team members where required. Required Skills & Qualifications • Strong fundamentals in one or more of the following languages: Python, TypeScript, Golang, or Rust • Proven experience working with APIs, web-based systems, and third-party integrations • Solid understanding of data structures, object-oriented programming, and software design principles • Thrive for the developing robotic process automation based application • Hands-on experience with LLM APIs (any major provider or open-source model), including tool/function calling and structured outputs • Understanding of prompt engineering, context management, and RAG • Familiarity with vector databases ( pgvector, Qdrant, Pinecone) • Awareness of AI safety and data privacy, especially for sensitive data • Genuine interest in blockchain, cryptocurrency, financial systems. • Strong analytical thinking and the ability to break down and solve complex technical problems • Sound understanding of networking concepts including IP addressing, proxies, and routing • Strong understanding of database optimization and working with time-series or structured data • Sound understanding of event-driven architecture and asynchronous systems • Ability to produce clear technical documentation and communicate system designs effectively • Comfortable working in a remote, KPI-driven environment with a high degree of ownership • A collaborative team player who can also execute independently under pressure and tight deadlines • Highly motivated to learn continuously, absorb feedback, and grow within a fast-moving technical environment Experience Level • Mid To Senior: 2–5 years • LLM/agent experience is a plus, but strong engineers willing to learn quickly are encouraged to apply. Work Schedule • Hours: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM BST (4:00 AM – 1:00 PM UTC) • Weekends: Saturday & Sunday off
