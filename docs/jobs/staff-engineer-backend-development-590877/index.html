About the Role : We are looking for a Staff Backend Engineer to drive the design and development of highly scalable, reliable, and high-performance backend systems. As a Staff Engineer, you will work on complex technical problems, influence architecture and engineering practices, and collaborate closely with engineering, product, and other cross-functional teams. This role requires strong hands-on engineering capabilities along with the ability to provide technical direction across teams. Responsibilities : - Design, build, and own scalable, reliable, and high-performance backend services and systems. - Drive architecture and technical design decisions for complex, large-scale systems. - Solve challenging problems related to scalability, reliability, performance, distributed systems, and data consistency. - Lead the development of critical backend components from design through deployment and production ownership. - Establish and promote engineering best practices around code quality, testing, observability, reliability, and performance. - Identify technical bottlenecks and drive initiatives to improve system performance, availability, and developer productivity. - Work closely with Product, Engineering, and other stakeholders to translate business requirements into robust technical solutions. - Provide technical mentorship and guidance to senior engineers and engineering teams. - Conduct and contribute to design and code reviews, ensuring high engineering standards. - Influence technical roadmaps and contribute to long-term architecture and platform strategy. - Take ownership of ambiguous, cross-team technical challenges and drive them towards resolution. Requirements : - 10+ years of experience in backend/software engineering. - Strong hands-on expertise in at least one of the following : Java, Golang, Python. - Strong understanding of Data Structures and Algorithms, Object-Oriented Design, and System Design. - Proven experience designing and building distributed, scalable, and fault-tolerant systems. - Strong understanding of microservices, APIs, databases, caching, messaging systems, and cloud-native architectures. - Experience working with systems handling high scale, traffic, data volume, or complex business workflows. - Strong understanding of performance optimisation, observability, reliability, and production engineering. - Ability to make sound architectural decisions while balancing scalability, reliability, complexity, and business requirements. - Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to influence technical decisions across teams. - Demonstrated ability to mentor engineers and raise the overall engineering bar. Good to Have : - Experience working in a high-growth product or technology company. - Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure. - Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, and modern DevOps practices. - Experience with distributed databases, Kafka or other messaging systems. - Experience driving large-scale technical migrations or platform modernisation initiatives. - Contributions to engineering standards, architecture frameworks, or developer productivity initiatives. Expectations : - Building backend systems that are scalable, reliable, maintainable, and performant. - Driving meaningful architectural and technical improvements across teams. - Successfully owning and delivering complex, high-impact engineering initiatives. - Improving engineering quality, reliability, and developer productivity. - Becoming a strong technical point of reference for backend engineering within the organisation.
