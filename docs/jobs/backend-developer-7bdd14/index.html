Position Overview We are looking for a Backend Developer with 2–4 years of experience to join our Tech Solutions team in Gurgaon. The ideal candidate should have strong hands-on experience in Golang, microservices, REST APIs, Redis, SQL/PostgreSQL, and cloud platforms. Key Responsibilities • Design and develop scalable Golang microservices and REST APIs. • Contribute to system design and distributed architecture, focusing on scalability, reliability, and performance. • Implement Redis caching and work with SQL/PostgreSQL databases. • Write unit, integration, and E2E tests. • Develop Shell/Bash scripts and contribute to CI/CD automation. • Work with GCP or equivalent cloud platforms. • Troubleshoot production and performance issues. • Collaborate with Product, QA, DevOps, and Engineering teams. • Own features from requirement analysis through deployment and production support. Requirements • 2–4 years of backend development experience. • Strong hands-on expertise in Golang/Go and microservices. • Strong understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems. • Good knowledge of system design, scalability, availability, and performance. • Hands-on experience with REST APIs, Redis, SQL/PostgreSQL. • Strong understanding of Go concurrency, goroutines, channels, context, and error handling. • Experience with unit, integration, and E2E testing. • Good knowledge of Shell/Bash, Git, and CI/CD. • Practical experience with GCP or equivalent cloud platforms. • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills. • Exposure to Docker/Kubernetes is an added advantage. Benefits • Opportunity to work on scalable, performance-driven technology solutions. • Exposure to microservices, distributed systems, cloud, and modern backend technologies. • Cross-functional collaboration with Product, QA, DevOps, and Engineering teams. • End-to-end ownership and strong opportunities for technical growth.
