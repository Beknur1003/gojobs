Company Overview Makerble is a pioneering platform that empowers enterprises to track, measure, and analyze their impact. Learn more from Role Overview We are seeking passionate and motivated individuals to join our team. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to a meaningful cause while gaining valuable experience in software development. As a volunteer, you will work closely with our development team to enhance and maintain our platform. Responsibilities • Collaborate with the development team to design and implement backend services using Golang and the Gin framework. • Develop and integrate REST APIs to enhance platform functionality. • Assist in database management and migrations using Postgres and Atlas. • Work with Kafka for message brokering and real-time data processing. • Contribute to web development projects and design patterns. • Engage in problem-solving and logical thinking to address technical challenges. • Participate in code reviews and contribute to improving code quality. • Work closely with DevOps concepts (Azure Cloud) Required Skills • Proficiency in Golang and experience with the Gin framework. • Understanding of REST API development. • Familiarity with Postgres and backend development. • Basic knowledge of Kafka for data streaming. • Strong problem-solving and logical thinking skills. • Experience with Atlas for database migrations is a plus. • Awareness of Ruby on Rails is an added advantage. Who Should Apply This volunteer role is ideal for individuals who are truly passionate about our vision and mission. If you are eager to make a difference and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic environment, we encourage you to apply. Note: This is a volunteer role. Please don't apply if you're looking for a full-time paid role Originally posted on Himalayas
