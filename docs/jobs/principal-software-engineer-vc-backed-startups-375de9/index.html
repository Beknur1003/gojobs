At SignalFire, we partner with top early-stage startups that are shaping the future of technology. Our portfolio spans 200+ innovative companies across AI, cybersecurity, healthtech, fintech, developer tools, and enterprise SaaS. We’re looking to connect with exceptional Principal Front-End, Back-End, and Full Stack Engineers who are excited about driving technical strategy, shaping architecture, and building scalable full stack applications at high-growth startups. By joining SignalFire’s Talent Network, your profile will be shared with our portfolio companies, giving you visibility into exclusive early-stage opportunities that may not be publicly listed. Who Should Join? We’re looking for engineers who are: ✔ Passionate about architecting scalable, high-performance full stack applications ✔ Excited to lead technical decision-making and mentor engineering teams ✔ Experienced in designing end-to-end systems, from modern front-end UIs to robust back-end services Typical Roles & Responsibilities • Define and drive technical architecture, best practices, and full stack development strategy • Design, build, and maintain scalable, high-performance applications across the front-end and back-end • Develop APIs, microservices, and cloud-native solutions to support large-scale applications • Optimize performance, security, and reliability across the application stack • Work closely with founders, product managers, and engineering teams to align business and technology goals • Establish and lead code review, testing, and deployment best practices • Mentor and guide senior engineers, fostering a strong engineering culture Common Qualifications While each startup has its own hiring criteria, many principal full stack engineering roles in our network look for: • 8+ years of experience in full stack development, with a strong record of technical leadership • Expertise in front-end frameworks (React, Angular, Vue.js, Svelte) and back-end frameworks (Node.js, Django, Flask, Ruby on Rails, FastAPI) • Strong experience with databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, DynamoDB, Redis) • Proficiency in cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization tools (Docker, Kubernetes) • Experience with distributed systems, high-availability mentoring engineers and leading large-scale technical projects 💡 Technologies You Might Work With: • Languages & Frameworks: JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Ruby, Golang, Java, React, Angular, Vue.js,
