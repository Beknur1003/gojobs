About the Role: In CrowdStrike’s Asset Platform team we work on large scale distributed systems, processing over 3 trillion events a day - and this traffic is growing daily. This position is for a role in one of CrowdStrike’s Core Asset Platform teams. We’re looking for people with limitless passion, a relentless focus on innovation and a fanatical commitment to developing and shaping our cybersecurity platform. Interested in working for a company that sets the standard and leads with integrity? Join us on a mission that matters - one team, one fight. This role is hybrid, on-site at one of the posted locations. The candidate should be comfortable visiting the office twice a week once the development center is established. What You’ll Do: • Lead backend engineering efforts from rapid prototypes to large-scale applications across CrowdStrike products. • Leverage and build cloud-based systems in a micro-service driven environment. • Brainstorm, define, and build collaboratively with members across multiple teams. • Obsess about learning, and champion the newest technologies & tricks with others, raising the technical IQ of the team. • Be mentored and mentor other developers on web, backend and data storage technologies and our system. • Constantly re-evaluate our product to improve architecture, knowledge models, user experience, performance and stability. • Be an energetic ‘self-starter’ with the ability to take ownership and be accountable for deliverables. • Use and give back to the open-source community. • We embrace a remote-first culture and this role is fully remote. What You'll Use: • Go (Golang) • OpenSearch / ElasticSearch • Redis / Valkey • Kafka • Kubernetes • GIT • AWS / GCP What You'll Need: • Degree in Computer Science (or commensurate experience in data structures/algorithms/distributed systems). • The ability to scale backend systems – sharding, partitioning, scaling horizontally are second nature to you. • The desire to ship code and the love of seeing your work run in production. • Deep understanding of distributed systems and scalability challenges. • Deep understanding of multi-threading, concurrency, and parallel processing technologies. • Team player skills – we embrace collaborating as a team as much as possible. • A thorough understanding of engineering best practices from appropriate testing paradigms to effective peer code reviews and resilient architecture. • The ability to thrive in a fast paced, test-driven, collaborative and iterative programming environment. • The skills to meet your commitments on time and produce high quality software that is unit tested, code reviewed, and checked in regularly for continuous integration. • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
