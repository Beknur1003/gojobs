Full Stack GoLang Developer About KloudMate KloudMate is an Observability platform for Developers and Businesses, building applications on distributed systems. KloudMate detects performance bottlenecks in modern distributed applications, identifies errors and boosts developer productivity by reducing Mean-Time-To Resolve (MTTR), helping businesses improve application performance, shrink operating costs, increase customer satisfaction and grow revenues. The Role This is a remote-first, full-time product engineering position with a core focus on building KloudMate platform. You'll be responsible in building, maintaining and deploying extremely high-volume, fault-tolerant, scalable distributed system that uses technologies such as using OpenTelemetry, Golang, Clickhouse, Kafka, Redis, React, AWS/Azure, Kubernetes, docker etc. Responsibilities • Play a leading role in architecting, designing, and developing and maintaining complex OpenTelemetry observability platform. • Be self-motivated and thrive in a fast-paced environment with minimal supervision. • Demonstrate a strong desire to learn and continuously improve your skills. • Performs the evaluation of new platforms tools and technologies. Required Skills • Minimum 3 years of experience in Golang, familiarity with concurrency in Go, using goroutines and channels for effective concurrent and parallel processing, gRPC is must. • Experience with relational and OLTP databases, including PostgreSQL, Clickhouse • Experience working with queues and stream processing using Kafka & Redis. • Experience with observability practices and OpenTelemtry is preferred. • Experience with container and container orchestration technologies, such as Docker and Kubernetes. • Strong analytical, problem-solving/quantitative skills. What We Offer Compensation: Competitive salary in the range of 8 to 16 LPA CTC annually. Location: Remote-first with the flexibility to work from anywhere. We have offices in Bangalore, India, and occasional visits are encouraged to foster team connections. We also host annual off-sites at beautiful locations (beaches or mountains!). Benefits: • Comprehensive health insurance. • Generous PTO (20 days) + National/Regional holidays + Compensation Day off. • Remote-friendly work environment. • Professional development opportunities. • Fun and engaging company events. • Strong emphasis on work-life balance (including celebrating birthdays and anniversaries!). • Opportunity to work from home. Cutting-edge Technologies: Work with the latest and greatest technologies on a brand new codebase. Growth and Impact: As an early-stage employee, you'll have significant ownership and the chance to make a real difference. Healthy Work Culture: We value a 40-hour workweek and asynchronous communication. Investment Day: Similar to Google's 20% time, we encourage employees to dedicate Fridays to explore and develop ideas that benefit KloudMate. Hiring Process • 30-minute general interview to get to know you and your experience. • A take-home coding exercise or a 2-hour live coding session with discussion. • A 30-minute chat with our founders to discuss the vision and your potential fit. Ready to join a passionate team and build the future of cloud observability? We look forward to hearing from you! Originally posted on Himalayas
