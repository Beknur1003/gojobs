Good to Have: • Prior experience in quick-commerce, e-commerce, food-tech, or logistics platforms. • Exposure to event-driven architectures and domain-driven design. • Experience mentoring junior engineers or leading small technical initiatives. • Contributions to open-source Go projects or active involvement in the Go community.
