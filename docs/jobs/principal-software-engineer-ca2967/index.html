Overview The AKS Arc team builds AI infrastructure, container management and orchestration services designed for the hybrid environments, enabling cloud-powered features for containerized workloads anywhere outside of Azure Cloud. This team delivers container solutions on premises, at the edge, multi-cloud and across diverse infrastructures, helping organizations simplify the deployment, management, and scaling of containerized applications. As a Principal Software Engineer on the AKS Arc team, you will design and deliver large-scale solutions that enable seamless management of customers’ Kubernetes clusters and AI workloads. This position provides the chance to work on cutting-edge technologies shaping the future of AI/ML inferencing, retraining with LoRA, reinforced learning (RL), cloud-native computing, while gaining deep expertise in Azure services, the Kubernetes management plane, and the broader cloud-native OSS ecosystem. You will collaborate with teams that prioritize technical excellence, innovation, and continuous learning, contributing to reliable and intuitive solutions for customers worldwide.
