About the Role As a Site Reliability Engineer, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the performance, reliability, and availability of our systems. Your expertise will directly impact the user experience and system efficiency. What You'll Do - Architect and maintain scalable systems while following best practices in software development and operational processes. - Work closely with development teams to ensure that new features are built with a focus on reliability and scalability from the outset. - Monitor system performance proactively and resolve issues to sustain high availability. - Automate routine operational tasks to enhance system reliability through innovative tooling and efficient processes. - Engage in on-call rotations and respond promptly to production incidents to mitigate impact. - Analyze incidents post-mortem to determine root causes and establish preventive strategies for future incidents. What We're Looking For - Strong programming skills in Golang and Python. - Hands-on experience with Kubernetes for managing containerized applications. - Solid understanding of system architecture and cloud infrastructure principles. - Familiarity with monitoring, logging, and alerting tools to track system health. - Exceptional problem-solving abilities and resilience under pressure. Qualified professionals who are committed to building reliable systems are encouraged to apply for the Site Reliability Engineer position.
