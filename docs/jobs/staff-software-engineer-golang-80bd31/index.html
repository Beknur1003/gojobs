The Role Tesla is currently seeking a highly motivated software engineer team leader to join the factory software team in Shanghai, Tesla. The candidate is expecting to be specialized in server-side development for BS architecture software that is also comfortable working as a full-stack developer (with frontend development experience and DevOps skills). The ideal candidate will have a passion for building world-class software and a track record of success creating highly available and scalable systems. Responsibilities： • As the team leader, lead a backend development team of 3-8 engineers, responsible for the overall architecture design, technology selection, and core feature development of quality related software. • Develop high-performance, scalable backend services using Golang to support core modules such as business workflows, data management, and auditing. • Drive AI innovation initiatives, integrating machine learning, predictive analytics, intelligent anomaly detection, automated decision-making, and other AI capabilities into the quality management system. • Establish and implement team technical standards, code review processes, CI/CD pipelines, and DevOps best practices to ensure delivery quality and engineering efficiency. • Lead the team through the full software development lifecycle—from requirements analysis and system design to deployment and maintenance assuring on-time, high-quality project delivery. • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Frontend, Data Science, QA, and Business teams to identify quality management pain points and deliver AI-driven innovative solutions. • Manage team talent development and performance, conduct regular 1:1 coaching and career development planning, participate in recruitment interviews, and build a high-performing technical team. • Continuously track emerging technologies (Golang ecosystem, LLMs, large model applications, etc.), evaluate and introduce AI and engineering technologies suitable for the team. Requirements Must Qualifications • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • 8+ years of backend development experience, with at least 3 years in a Team Leader or technical lead role with full team management responsibilities. • Proficient in Golang, with deep expertise in concurrency models, performance optimization, microservices architecture, and distributed system design. • Hands-on experience with production-grade AI/ML integration or leading AI-driven product features. • Familiar with cloud-native technology stack (Docker, Kubernetes, AWS/Azure/GCP), message queues, relational and non-relational databases, REST/gRPC. • Strong team leadership, communication, coordination, and project execution skills; able to effectively lead the team to deliver results. Preferred Qualifications • Familiarity with ISO 9001, IATF 16949, or other quality management system standards and manufacturing quality processes. • Experience developing enterprise-grade quality management, manufacturing execution, or compliance auditing systems. • Open-source contributions, technical papers or patents in AI/quality management, or experience applying computer vision/NLP in quality inspection.
