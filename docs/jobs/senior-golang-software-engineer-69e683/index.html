About the role We are looking for an experienced Senior GoLang Software Engineer to join a major technology programme focused on building and evolving a next-generation platform. You will work as part of an Agile product delivery team, contributing throughout the software development lifecycle, from technical design and development to testing, troubleshooting and continuous improvement. The role is a good fit for an engineer with strong GoLang expertise and hands-on experience building scalable, cloud-native solutions using microservices. Your responsibilities • Design, develop, test and maintain software components and integrations using GoLang. • Build scalable and reliable microservices and REST APIs. • Translate functional requirements and approved designs into robust technical solutions. • Contribute to technical design and architecture discussions. • Participate in code and design reviews and contribute to engineering standards and development best practices. • Work with containerised applications using Docker and Kubernetes. • Contribute to solutions running in AWS environments. • Work with Kafka for event-driven and distributed architectures. • Develop and integrate solutions using PostgreSQL. • Collaborate with product, engineering and testing teams throughout Agile delivery. • Troubleshoot development and production issues and perform root-cause analysis. • Resolve defects identified during development, testing and post-release activities. • Contribute to CI/CD processes and continuous delivery practices. • Create and maintain clear technical documentation. • Proactively communicate technical risks, dependencies and delivery challenges. What you bring • Approximately 6–9 years of software engineering experience, with strong hands-on experience developing applications using GoLang. • Strong software design, coding, debugging and problem-solving skills. • Experience building microservices and RESTful APIs. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and Docker. • Experience working with AWS or similar cloud environments. • Experience with Kafka or similar event-streaming technologies. • Good knowledge of PostgreSQL or similar relational databases. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and modern software delivery practices. • Experience contributing to architecture and technical design discussions. • Good understanding of scalable and distributed software architectures. • Experience working within Agile software development teams. • Comfortable collaborating across international and cross-functional engineering teams. • Strong English communication skills.
