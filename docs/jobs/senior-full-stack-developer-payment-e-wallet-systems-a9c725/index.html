Senior
PostgreSQLMySQLMongoDBRedisKafkaRabbitMQPHPAI/LLMFinTech
What We're Looking For • Strong full-stack development experience with PHP/Laravel and/or Golang, preferably with Fiber or similar frameworks. • Proven experience building and maintaining payment systems, digital wallets, or fintech applications, beyond basic third-party payment gateway integration. • Strong understanding of e-wallet architecture and payment transaction lifecycles, including transaction processing, balance management, transaction history, and payment status handling. • Hands-on experience developing Money-In and Money-Out systems, including: • Money-In: Virtual Accounts (VA), QRIS, Payment Links, bank transfers, payment gateways, and switching integrations. • Money-Out: Bank transfers, disbursements, PPOB, QRIS, payment gateways, and switching integrations. • Solid understanding of financial transaction integrity, including double-entry ledger concepts, idempotency, duplicate transaction prevention, transaction consistency, and failure/reversal handling. • Experience with payment reconciliation, settlement processes, refunds, and transaction monitoring. • Strong understanding of RESTful APIs, authentication, authorization, OAuth2, JWT, and secure API integrations. • Experience with relational and/or NoSQL databases, such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, and MongoDB, including query optimization and transaction management. • Familiarity with Redis, Kafka, RabbitMQ, or other message-broker systems for asynchronous processing and event-driven architectures. • Proficiency in frontend technologies, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and ReactJS, Svelte, or VanillaJS. • Understanding of secure software development practices, particularly for applications handling financial transactions and sensitive data. • Experience troubleshooting production issues, optimizing system performance, and maintaining high availability and reliability. • Familiarity with AI-powered coding assistants such as Claude Code, DeepSeek, or Codex is a plus. What You'll Do • Design, develop, and maintain scalable full-stack applications for payment and e-wallet services. • Build and improve payment processing capabilities across Money-In and Money-Out flows, including bank, QRIS, payment gateway, and switching integrations. • Develop backend services and APIs that support wallet transactions, balance management, transaction histories, and payment status tracking. • Implement reliable financial transaction processing, including ledger management, idempotency, transaction consistency, and error handling. • Develop reconciliation, settlement, refund, and transaction monitoring capabilities. • Build frontend interfaces and integrate them with secure backend services. • Optimize database performance, caching, asynchronous processing, and message-queuing systems. • Identify and resolve production issues related to payment failures, transaction discrepancies, performance bottlenecks, and system reliability. • Collaborate with Product, QA, Design, and other engineering teams to deliver reliable financial products. • Contribute to system architecture, code reviews, technical decisions, and engineering best practices. Preferred Qualifications • Previous experience building an e-wallet, digital payment platform, payment gateway, payment switching system, or other transaction-heavy fintech application. • Experience handling real-world financial transactions in production environments. • Understanding of payment security, audit trails, and controls for preventing inconsistent or duplicate financial transactions. • Experience designing scalable and fault-tolerant systems for high-volume transaction processing.
