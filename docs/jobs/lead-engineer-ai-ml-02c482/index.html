Lead Engineer AI/ML

Lead Полная занятость

What success looks like in this role: • Works closely with cross-functional project team members to ensure an integrated approach to AI / ML model application. • Leading the design and development of Generative AI applications leveraging LLMs, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, and multi-agent frameworks • Works with AI Engineers, AI experts and associated operations and other technical personnel to deliver end-to-end solutions designed to enhance user experience and deliver superior results. • Collaborates on the definition of AI / ML model development and application standards. • Employs creative and leading-edge thinking to reconcile end user requirements with technical feasibility and business viability. • Collaborates with model builders and recommends architectures to ensure timely and quality service delivery. • Provides targeted feedback and peer review for AI / ML and allied team members. • Leverages current understanding of AI / ML and software engineering to transform ideas into specific model, development and product recommendations. #LI-LS1 You will be successful in this role if you have: • BS/BE Degree with 8+ years of relevant experience. • Strong hands-on experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and their application in agent-based and autonomous systems. • Solid understanding of Agentic AI concepts, including planning, reasoning, and multi-agent collaboration. • Solid understanding and hands-on experience agent orchestration frameworks and AI tooling ecosystems (e.g., CrewAI). • Proficiency with Azure AI Services, including Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Doc Intelligence, Translator, etc • Strong understanding of Azure OpenAI GPT models, including capabilities, limitations, prompt engineering, function calling, tool use, and structured outputs. • Strong programming skills in Python, with experience integrating AI services, APIs, and data pipelines. • Proficiency in back-end development using Python, and/or Golang, with a focus on API-driven architecture. • Experience with databases such as PostgreSQL, and familiarity with vector databases. • Working knowledge of at least one front-end framework such as React or Angular for UI • Hands-on experience with cloud-native development, containers, and microservices. • Demonstrated experience in cluster using Kubernetes, Docker, Helm & platforms like Rancher • Familiarity with UI/UX tooling such as Figma for rapid prototyping and design collaboration. • Cloud certifications (Azure Developer, Azure AI Engineer, or equivalent) are an added advantage.
