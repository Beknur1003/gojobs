About ANDPAD Vietnam: ANDPAD Vietnam is a key part of ANDPAD Inc., the provider of Japan‘s No.1 cloud-based construction project management service. With over 684,000 users, our platform is a leader in digitalizing the construction industry, from communication and scheduling to quality and order management. Our team of Software Engineers at ANDPAD Vietnam enjoys a dynamic international environment focused on creative engineering. Instead of handling repetitive tasks, you‘ll be involved in developing innovative SaaS products that solve real-world problems for our clients and users. The Opportunity: We are hiring a Senior Backend Engineer and we don‘t care which language you write today. In the AI era, syntax is cheap. An engineer with strong backend fundamentals - system design, data modeling, testing discipline, production operations can become productive in a new language within weeks, especially with AI-assisted tooling. So this position is language-agnostic by design. We evaluate you on core engineering skills, not familiarity with our current stack. If you‘ve built and operated serious backend systems in Java, Python, C#, PHP, Node.js, Elixir, or anything else - you‘re a candidate. While our hiring process is language-agnostic, please note that our primary development stacks are Ruby on Rails and Golang. We support engineers in transitioning to these technologies if they are not already familiar. Job Scope: • Design, build, and operate backend services that power a leading B2B SaaS platform for the construction industry. • Design clean, maintainable, and extensible software architecture. • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks (query optimization, caching, async processing). • Drive software quality through automated testing, code reviews, observability, and CI/CD practices. • Review and validate AI-generated code to ensure maintainability, correctness, and security. • Mentor engineers and raise the team‘s engineering bar.
