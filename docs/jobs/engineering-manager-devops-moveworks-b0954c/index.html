As a technical lead and manager of the DevOps/DevSecOps team, you will be responsible for leading the team in building and scaling the AI cloud infrastructure and developer platform. As our platform and teams grow fast, the infrastructure team is tasked with designing and operating reliable and resilient foundational services as well as frameworks that allow our product to scale seamlessly and our engineering teams to build customer facing features very rapidly. • Lead, grow, and mentor a team of DevOps/DevSecOps engineers, and build a culture of technical excellence, ownership, and continuous improvement. • Own the design and evolution of Moveworks' build, release, and deployment systems, including CI/CD pipelines, artifact management, progressive delivery, and rollbacks, with a focus on speed, reliability, and developer experience. • Drive infrastructure-as-code and GitOps practices across our cloud environments (Terraform, Helm, Argo CD, or similar) so that infrastructure changes are repeatable, reviewable, and auditable • Build security into the software development lifecycle: SAST/DAST/SCA scanning, container and image scanning, policy-as-code guardrails, etc. • Partner with the security team on vulnerability management, cloud security posture, IAM and least-privilege access, secrets management, and incident response • Co-own on-call, incident management, and postmortem practices for the systems the team runs with other Infra leads. • Run project management for the team: turn ambiguous requests into scoped projects with clear milestones, owners, and success criteria; maintain the roadmap; manage cross-team dependencies and risks; and communicate status to stakeholders and leadership. • Set the vision, strategy, and multi-quarter roadmap for DevOps/DevSecOps, aligned with company goals for product, engineering, security, and compliance. • Hands-on technical leader with a strong sense of ownership who drives complex platform and security initiatives from conception through delivery and measurement. • 2+ years of experience managing a team of 6+ engineer. • 6+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, SRE, platform, infrastructure, or security engineering • Deep experience with CI/CD systems (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Buildkite, GitLab CI, or similar) and release engineering at scale. • Strong experience with Kubernetes, containers/Docker, and cloud infrastructure on AWS/GCP/Azure Hands-on experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Pulumi, CloudFormation) and configuration management. • Practical knowledge of application and cloud security: secure SDLC, vulnerability management, IAM, network security, and secrets management (Vault, AWS Secrets Manager, or similar). • Proven project management skills with planning and delivering multi-quarter, cross-team projects. • Proficiency in at least one of Python/Golang/Bash Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, OpenTelemetry, or similar). • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to influence all levels of the organization and explain security and platform tradeoffs in terms of business value. • Desire to operate at the speed of a startup inside a global enterprise platform, with a high degree of ownership. • BS+ in computer science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • A high level of curiosity about engineering outside your immediate discipline and a strong desire to keep learning. • Experience supporting SOC 2, ISO 27001, or FedRAMP audits, especially automating compliance controls, is a plus.
