About Us ELGO AI is an enterprise Agentic AI platform that enables organizations to build, deploy, and manage production-grade AI solutions across business functions and systems. Our platform combines AI agents, workflows, enterprise integrations, governance, evaluation, and observability to help organizations move from experimentation to reliable AI deployment at scale. Our mission is to be a transforming force for responsible, safe, and scalable AI adoption in enterprises. We work with large enterprises and public-sector organizations across Singapore and the broader Asia-Pacific region, solving complex real-world problems through production-grade AI systems. The Role We are looking for a hands-on Full Stack Software Engineer to build and scale ELGO AI’s core platform and enterprise applications. You will work across the frontend, backend, APIs, databases, integrations, and deployment infrastructure, and collaborate closely with our AI Engineers who own the AI backend and agentic capabilities. This role requires strong ownership across the full software development lifecycle, from technical design and development to deployment and production support. Main Responsibilities · Build and maintain frontend applications usingReact.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. · Develop backend services, APIs, and business logic, primarily using Golang. · Design and maintain relational and NoSQL databases. · Build integrations with external APIs, authentication systems, and enterprise applications. · Work closely with AI Engineers to integrate AI services into the product layer. · Design for scalability, reliability, security, performance, and maintainability. · Troubleshoot and resolve frontend, backend, and production issues. · Write clean, reusable, well-tested production code. · Contribute to code reviews, technical standards, and architecture decisions. · Support cloud deployment, CI/CD, monitoring, logging, and containerized applications. What We Are Looking For Full-Stack Experience: At least 4 years of relevant software engineering experience building production applications. Frontend: Strong experience with React.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. Backend: Strong backend development experience. Golang experience is highly preferred. Databases: Experience with relational and NoSQL databases such as PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, or equivalent. DevOps & Cloud: Practical experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP, Docker, CI/CD, monitoring, logging, and production deployment. Strong Engineering Fundamentals: Good understanding of API design, software architecture, testing, performance, scalability, security, and maintainability. High Ownership: Able to independently take features from technical design through production deployment and ongoing improvement. Problem Solver: Comfortable working through ambiguous technical problems and making pragmatic engineering decisions. Enterprise Experience Preferred: Experience building B2B SaaS platforms, enterprise software, workflow applications, or system integrations is highly preferred. Key KPIs Success will be measured through: • Quality and reliability of delivered software • Timely delivery of product features • Application and API performance • Production stability • Code quality and maintainability • Effectiveness in resolving technical issues • Contribution to platform scalability and engineering productivity Why Join Us? You will work across a broad technical stack and have meaningful ownership over how ELGO AI’s platform is built and scaled. As an early member of a growing engineering team, you will have significant influence over our architecture, engineering practices, and technical direction. Location This is primarily an on-site role based in Singapore. We are currently looking for candidates who are Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents and do not require a working visa. How to Apply Send your resume and a brief cover letter to talent@elgo.app with the subject line: [Your Name] - Full Stack Software Engineer
