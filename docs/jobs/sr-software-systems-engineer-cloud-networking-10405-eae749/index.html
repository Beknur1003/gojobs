Position: Sr Software Systems Engineer –Cloud Networking Domain: Cloud Networking and Orchestration Experience Level: 5 to 10 Years Qualification: B.E/B.Tech/MS/M.Tech or equivalent in CS/EEE/ECE Location: Only Chennai About the Role We are seeking an accomplished Sr Software Systems Engineer – Technical Lead to drive the development of our Network Automation and Orchestration products. In this strategic technical leadership role, you will own the end-to-end design and development of cloud networking solutions, mentor technical teams, and establish best-in-class engineering practices. Your expertise in microservices, containerization, and cloud-native development will be instrumental in shaping the future of Extreme's cloud networking platform. Key Responsibilities • Technically lead the team of professionals to deliver Network Automation and Orchestration products • Own the overall design and development artifacts including functional and design specifications, code, and unit/integration test cases • Introduce and passionately drive the implementation of best-in-class engineering practices such as Test-Driven Development and Design Principles • Develop Epics, Stories, and Tasks for execution and collaborate across teams for complete solution integration • Establish technical standards and mentor team members in adherence to best practices • Drive architectural decisions and technical implementations for scalable, maintainable solutions Required Skills and Expertise Core Competencies • Complete exposure to the entire Product Life Cycle (MUST HAVE) • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills • Proficiency in local and remote debugging Architecture and Design • Microservices-based architecture and best practices • Hands-on experience with Docker containers, Kubernetes (K8s, K3s) clusters, and Helm charts • Exposure to cloud application development • Sound data and information modeling techniques using YANG, UML, JSON, and related tools Programming and Development • Strong coding and debugging experience with Java or Golang • Linux OS proficiency, advanced data structures, and IPC mechanisms • Intricate understanding of multi-threading, routines, network programming, and object-oriented methodologies • Agile Development Model with CI/CD, Jenkins pipeline, and Git environment Data Management and Messaging • Database concepts and deployment models: RDBMS, NoSQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, MariaDB • Messaging services such as RabbitMQ and Kafka • Working experience with webhook and web sockets Networking and Protocols • OpenAPI, OpenConfig, NETCONF, Streaming Telemetry, GNMI, gRPC, and XMPP • Knowledge of L2/L3 OSI layer protocols: 802.1Q, LAGs, VxLAN, EVPN, ACLs, Route Policy, MPLS/VPLS, BGP, L2 Forwarding, and L3 Routing Preferred Qualifications • Experience with network orchestration platforms and automation frameworks • Background in building scalable, highly available distributed systems • Experience mentoring junior engineers and leading technical teams • Familiarity with containerized deployment architectures and orchestration platforms • Prior experience with telemetry and streaming analytics What We Offer Extreme Networks offers an innovative environment where you will drive transformative cloud networking solutions.
