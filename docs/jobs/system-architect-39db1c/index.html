System Architect

MySQLMongoDBRedisKubernetesDockerAWSMicroservicesCI/CDJavaNode.js
Job Description: We are seeking a skilled Software Architect to design and develop scalable,secure and high-performance software solutions. The successful candidate willwork closely with engineering, product and DevOps teams to translate businessrequirements into robust technical architectures. Key Responsibilities: • Design and develop scalable distributed systems using Java, Golang and Node.js. • Architect microservices-based applications and cloud-native solutions on AWS. • Design high-performance backend systems using concurrency, caching and event-driven architecture. • Lead architecture and development of enterprise and financial technology applications. • Design and optimise SQL and NoSQL databases, including Redis and MongoDB, using appropriate indexing, sharding and replication strategies. • Implement CI/CD pipelines and containerised deployments using Docker and Kubernetes. • Conduct system profiling, load testing and performance optimisation to improve reliability and scalability. • Design secure and highly available systems incorporating authentication, encryption and industry security practices. • Collaborate with engineering, product and DevOps teams to establish technical standards and deliver business solutions. • Provide technical guidance on software architecture, distributed systems and application performance. Requirements: • Diploma/Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related discipline. • At least 10 years of relevant software development and architecture experience. • Strong hands-on experience in Java, Spring Boot and distributed systems. • Experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD. • Strong knowledge of microservices, concurrency, networking and database technologies. • Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases such as MySQL, Redis and MongoDB. • Good understanding of software design principles, data structures and algorithms. • Experience in financial technology, trading platforms or other high-performance systems will be advantageous.
