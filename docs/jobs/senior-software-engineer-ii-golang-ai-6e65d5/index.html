Senior Software Engineer II - Golang & AI

Hiring Eye Hyderabad опубликовано 6 июля 2026

Go — основной язык Senior
PostgreSQLMySQLClickHouseRedisKafkagRPCKubernetesDockerMicroservicesAI/LLM
We are looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer II (Go & AI) to build scalable cloud-native backend systems for AI-powered applications. You will design and develop distributed systems, high-performance APIs, and multi-tenant platforms while working on modern AI agent frameworks and cloud technologies. Key Responsibilities: - Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using Go (Golang). - Build high-performance REST/gRPC APIs and distributed microservices. - Develop AI agent orchestration platforms and integrate with LLMs and AI frameworks. - Build secure, multi-tenant cloud services with authentication, authorization, and API gateways. - Work with Kubernetes, messaging systems, and distributed data stores. - Optimize system performance, scalability, reliability, and observability. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality products. - Mentor junior engineers and drive engineering best practices. Required Skills: - 7+ years of backend software development experience. - Strong expertise in Go (Golang). - Experience with Microservices, Distributed Systems, and Cloud Platforms (SaaS/PaaS). - Hands-on experience with LLMs, AI Agents, or Agentic AI frameworks. - Strong knowledge of REST APIs, gRPC, and API design. - Experience with Kafka, Redis, PostgreSQL/MySQL, Kubernetes, and Docker. - Knowledge of ClickHouse, Snowflake, or similar data platforms is a plus. - Experience with LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, or MCP is preferred. - Strong problem-solving, system design, and mentoring skills.
