Software Test Engineer – IP Routing & AI/Data Center Networks

Nokia India опубликовано 14 сентября 2026

Гибрид Полная занятость

We are looking for SW Test engineers who wish to be a part of developing and evolving the current and next generation of routers that form the core of anything internet. These routers are at the heart of Nokia’s IP product offerings, from data centers (including AI fabrics), to providing strong, resilient low latency and dependable IP backhaul for service providers and mission critical networks. In this role, you will have the opportunity to work with one of the best engineering teams in the industry. We value your expertise and offer an environment where you can thrive both professionally and personally. There are more customers we want to win, more networks to penetrate, more technologies to enable and more applications to support. We want to make smaller products, and we want to make bigger products. We want to build products based on newer standards and newer ASICS in the market. • Own software features end to end and ensure their quality is robust across products and platforms • Specialize in datacenter and AI networks • Write modular, lean, and efficient automation code including libraries and infrastructure • Work closely with product management and software developers to meet customer requirements • Bachelor’s degree with 3–8 years of relevant experience in networking and software automation. • Exposure to any of the below domains: • Layer-2 switching and layer-3 routing • Routing protocols (ospf, isis, bgp) • Multicast (pim, igmp) • Segment Routing • Mpls (ldp, rsvp-te) • Evpns with vxlan, mpls, sr-te underlays • QoS (policers, schedulers, shapers) • Automation experience in python, golang or any programming language • Experience in virtualized environments such as kvm, dockers, and Kubernetes • Contribution to SONiC test infrastructure and automation frameworks • Good communication skills to work with different engineering teams • Proven aptitude for methodical problem solving, good communication skills, strong attention to detail, and the ability to work well in a close-knit team.
