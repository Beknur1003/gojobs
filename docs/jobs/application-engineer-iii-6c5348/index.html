The firm offers investments, advice, and retirement services to tens of millions of individual investors around the globe—directly, through workplace plans, and through financial intermediaries. Vanguard India Vanguard’s office in India is a significant milestone in our global expansion. We are committed to establishing an enduring technology center in Hyderabad, Telangana and are excited to be adding talent who will focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI), mobile, and cloud-based technologies that drive our business outcomes and deliver a world-class experience for our clients. Role Summary Responsible for design, development, testing, deployment, and operational support of mission-critical liquidity management and cash management platforms. Works closely with senior engineers and business partners while gradually taking ownership of increasingly complex capabilities. The role follows a product-team model where engineers are responsible for both development and operational support of the applications they build. Required Technical Skills • Minimum 5 years of experience in software development, system integration, database design, and back-end architecture. • Bachelor’s degree (B.E./B.Tech) in relevant fields such as Computer Science, or a Master’s degree/Diploma in Computer Science. • Learning mindset, Production support mindset, Financial domain knowledge • Backend - Strong hands-on experience in Python and/or Java, Knowledge of Golang preferred, API development, Microservices architecture, SQL and data access patterns • Frontend Angular or React • Cloud & DevOps - AWS, ECS, Lambda, S3, CloudFormation, GitHub, CI/CD pipelines • Engineering Practices - Agile methodologies, Jira, Confluence, Automated testing, Debugging and troubleshooting, Production support Key Responsibilities Software Engineering • Design and develop application enhancements. • Build secure, scalable cloud-native solutions. • Create automated tests and maintain code quality standards. • Participate in code reviews. Product Support • Monitor production systems. • Troubleshoot incidents and defects. • Participate in support rotations when required. • Drive root-cause analysis and preventive improvements. Collaboration • Work closely with architects, tech leads, and business stakeholders. • Contribute to design discussions. • Document solutions and system behaviors. Learning & Growth • Develop expertise in liquidity management and cash management platforms. • Learn new technologies rapidly. • Continuously improve engineering and domain knowledge. Location This role is based in Hyderabad, Telangana at Vanguard India. Only qualified external applicants will be considered.
