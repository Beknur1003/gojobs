Senior Software Engineer, B2B Platform Services Location: Belfast, NI / Hybrid (This role requires working in-office 3 days per week.) About us: EverQuote is a leading AI-powered growth solutions partner for regulated property and casualty insurance entities, enabling the largest insurance carriers and thousands of agents to maximize customer acquisition across digital channels. Fueled by our proprietary data assets and our AI traffic engine, EverQuote is transforming the way providers attract and engage consumers to grow market share. Joining our team puts you at the forefront of this transformation. You will collaborate with a group of builders to solve complex problems and reshape a massive industry. If you want to drive real-world impact and do the best work of your career, you’ve found your home. We move fast, follow the data, and hold ourselves to high standards. If that's how you work too, this is the role for you. About the role: As a senior member of the engineering team you will be instrumental in planning, designing, and implementing applications that will scale with EverQuote systems. In this role, you will build applications and services that meet the needs of the business while also being scalable, performant and easy to maintain. This is a full-stack role with a backend focus, where you will be expected to take full ownership of large pieces of business critical functionality. You will be joining a team of talented, collaborative engineers eager to grow and deliver. What you'll do: • Design and delivery of key business applications and solutions. • Enhance code quality, productivity and velocity through the use of AI assisted technology. • Adhere to our Software Development processes whilst improving our Engineering culture by identifying and addressing inefficiencies in our processes. • Proactively engage with cross functional stakeholders including Product Managers and Architecture team. What you bring to the team: • 5+ years of professional programming experience including experience at a software product company. • Degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience. • Experience in one or more of the following languages Typescript, Golang, Python or other server side languages. • You have worked in a microservice-based architecture and are proficient in Docker and Kubernetes. • Experience building, deploying and monitoring cloud based applications, systems & infrastructure at scale (ideally on AWS). • Deep experience designing systems and communicating designs to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Nice to have: • You are passionate about sharing knowledge and mentoring teammates. • You are able to quickly diagnose and solve code problems, as well as help others unblock themselves. • You have an entrepreneurial mindset, want to grow a business, and love working with a team. • You are comfortable using data to support and validate your concepts. • You thrive in a fast-paced work environment where everyday contributions have a big impact.
