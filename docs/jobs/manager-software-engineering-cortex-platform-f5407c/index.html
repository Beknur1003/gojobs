Job Summary Palo Alto Networks' Cortex Cloud team is looking for a seasoned and accomplished Engineering Manager to lead the development of next-generation cloud security solutions and operate highly scalable cloud platforms. In this role, you will lead high-performing engineering teams, drive technical execution, and foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and operational excellence while delivering secure, reliable, and scalable services. As an Engineering Leader, you will: • Lead and grow high-performing software engineering teams building innovative cloud security products and platforms. • Drive the technical vision and execution of cloud-native services in collaboration with Product Management, Architecture, and cross-functional engineering teams. • Own the successful delivery of features and platforms throughout the product development lifecycle, from planning and design through implementation, testing, deployment, and ongoing operations. • Foster an engineering culture centered on quality, customer obsession, innovation, and continuous improvement. Key Responsibilities • Lead the technical strategy and execution for building world-class cloud security solutions, partnering with architects and senior engineering leaders on technology and architectural decisions. • Build, develop, and retain high-performing engineering teams through hiring, mentoring, coaching, and career development. • Drive engineering execution by owning planning, prioritization, estimation, and predictable delivery across multiple initiatives. • Collaborate closely with Product Management, Software Engineering, Quality Engineering, and Customer Success teams to deliver high-quality Security-as-a-Service offerings. • Lead engineering teams responsible for designing, building, deploying, and operating scalable cloud security platforms and services with end-to-end ownership. • Champion engineering excellence by driving software quality, reliability, security, observability, and operational best practices. • Establish and monitor engineering metrics, including service reliability, availability, performance, and delivery efficiency. • Drive operational excellence through incident management, root cause analysis, automation, and continuous service improvements. • Contribute to architecture, design, and technical discussions while empowering engineers to own technical decisions and execution. • Foster a culture of diversity, inclusion, intellectual curiosity, innovation, accountability, and continuous learning. • Partner closely with Customer Support and Customer Success teams to improve customer experience and product reliability. • Effectively balance multiple priorities in a fast-paced, highly collaborative environment. Qualifications Required Qualifications • 8+ years of experience in software engineering, including developing enterprise-scale cloud services at large organizations or high-growth startups. • 2+ years of experience leading and managing software engineering teams. • Proven experience delivering highly scalable, distributed cloud-native applications and services. • 5+ years of experience designing and building cloud-based architectures on AWS, GCP, or Azure. • Hands-on programming experience in one or more of the following languages: Golang or Python. • Strong understanding of distributed systems, microservices architectures, and cloud-native application design. • Deep understanding of database technologies, including SQL, NoSQL, and columnar databases. Experience with analytical data platforms such as BigQuery is a plus. • Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and modern container orchestration platforms. • Experience building and operating production services with a strong focus on scalability, reliability, observability, and performance. • Strong understanding of DevOps principles, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and cloud operations. • Working knowledge of cloud security concepts and secure software development practices. • Experience leading Agile software development teams delivering full-stack cloud applications. • Strong leadership skills with the ability to make data-driven decisions, balance technical trade-offs, and drive execution across multiple stakeholders. • Excellent communication, collaboration, and stakeholder management skills. Preferred Qualifications • Experience building AI-powered applications, conversational AI platforms, or developer productivity tools. • Experience with large-scale analytics and data processing platforms. • Experience working in cybersecurity or cloud security domains. • BS/MS in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent military experience. #LI-NP1 #LI-HYBRID Compensation Disclosure The compensation offered for this position will depend on qualifications, experience, and work location.
