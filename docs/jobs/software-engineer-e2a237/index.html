Team/ Role Paragraph: Blockchain Insights is Coinbase's internal onchain intelligence platform. It combines address attribution from internal teams and external vendors with multi-chain transaction data, and powers KYT, risk scoring, and sanctions screening. Compliance, investigations, and risk teams rely on Blockchain Insights to trace funds and keep Coinbase safe and compliant, and a growing share of that use comes through AI agents via our MCP server. As an engineer on the team, you'll build the data pipelines, services, and agent-facing tools that make this intelligence accurate, fresh, and easy to act on. What you’ll do: • Build and scale Golang services and Databricks/Kafka pipelines that ingest, normalize, and serve address metadata and transaction history across a growing set of blockchains. • Improve data freshness, accuracy, and provenance so investigators and risk systems can trust data and see where it came from. • Extend Blockchain Insights' AI surface, including our MCP server and agent workflows, so analysts and engineers can query onchain intelligence directly from tools like Claude and Cursor. • Use AI agents across the development lifecycle (design, implementation, testing, review, and incident triage) and automate repetitive operational work such as data onboarding and oncall triage. • Partner with compliance, investigations, and product teams to turn their needs into a quarterly roadmap, and help set the technical direction for the platform. • Write high-quality, well-tested code and help keep a platform that critical compliance workflows depend on reliable. Required Skills and Experience: • 3+ years of software engineering experience, including designing, building, scaling, and maintaining production backend services. • Solid grasp of service-oriented architecture and of data-intensive systems such as streaming pipelines, large-scale storage, and query performance. • High standards for code quality and testing, applied equally to code you write by hand and code generated with AI. • Daily use of AI coding tools, with good judgment about where they speed you up and where they need close review. • Ability to break ambiguous problems into well-scoped tasks with clear context and acceptance criteria, for yourself, your teammates, or an agent. • Passion for building an open financial system that brings the world together. • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
