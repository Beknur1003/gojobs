Senior DevOps / Platform Engineer (ID: 4028)

STAFIDE Amsterdam, Netherlands опубликовано 25 октября 2026

As a Senior DevOps / Platform Engineer, you will: • Support and maintain a CI/CD platform used by a large-scale IT engineering organization. • Manage and maintain CI/CD tooling, supporting automated and self-service onboarding of development teams. • Work with Microsoft Azure, Kubernetes (K8s), Ansible Tower, CI tools, and software artifact repositories. • Develop and maintain automation and CI/CD pipelines following DevOps and SecDevOps principles. • Develop and maintain solutions using Go/Golang as a core development skill. • Support the infrastructure underpinning CI/CD tooling across cloud and on-premises environments. • Install, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) applications on cloud and on-premises infrastructure. • Provide L2 support for critical business applications and technical issues. • Troubleshoot infrastructure, operating system, application, and network-related issues and drive incidents through to resolution. • Administer and maintain Linux-based environments, including operating-system-level configuration and administration. • Implement infrastructure automation using Infrastructure as Code (IaC), including Ansible playbooks. • Support infrastructure alignment, standardization, and operational improvements across the development tooling environment. • Collaborate with DevOps and engineering teams to resolve issues related to CI/CD platforms and development tooling. • Manage incidents and implement infrastructure and application changes in accordance with agreed SLAs. • Contribute to automation and self-service capabilities that simplify the onboarding and usage of development tooling. • Work with technologies such as Azure DevOps, SonarQube, Nexus Lifecycle, Fortify, Nexus Repository Manager, Confluence, Ansible Tower, and NeoLoad. • Continuously improve the reliability, security, scalability, and usability of the CI/CD platform. What You Bring to the Table: • 6–8 years of overall professional experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering, or related roles. • Minimum 3 years of hands-on Operations/Infrastructure Engineering experience. • Strong hands-on foundation in cloud infrastructure, particularly Microsoft Azure. • Strong experience with Kubernetes (K8s). • Strong expertise in Go/Golang. • Hands-on experience creating and maintaining CI/CD pipelines. • Strong understanding of DevOps and SecDevOps principles. • Experience with Ansible and Infrastructure as Code, including Ansible playbooks. • Experience with Ansible Tower is advantageous. • Strong understanding of Linux operating systems, particularly system administration and operational support. • Experience providing L2 support for critical business applications and production issues. • Experience installing, configuring, maintaining, and troubleshooting COTS applications in cloud and/or on-premises environments. • Experience with infrastructure and application automation. • Experience troubleshooting network hardware and software-related issues. • Experience managing incidents and implementing technical changes according to SLA requirements. • Experience with CI tooling and software artifact repositories is advantageous. • Knowledge of PostgreSQL or other relational databases is advantageous. • Experience with Azure DevOps and related development tooling is advantageous. You should possess the ability to: • Operate and maintain Azure and Kubernetes-based infrastructure supporting enterprise CI/CD platforms. • Develop automation and tooling using Go/Golang. • Design, implement, and maintain reliable CI/CD pipelines. • Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration using Ansible and IaC practices. • Administer and troubleshoot Linux environments at the operating-system level. • Provide effective L2 operational support for critical business applications and infrastructure. • Troubleshoot complex infrastructure, application, network, and platform issues and perform root-cause analysis. • Install, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot COTS applications across cloud and on-premises environments. • Automate repetitive operational activities and develop self-service capabilities. • Apply SecDevOps and security-by-design principles throughout the development and deployment lifecycle. • Manage incidents, changes, and operational activities within defined SLAs. • Collaborate effectively with development, DevOps, infrastructure, and platform teams. • Learn and expand knowledge of enterprise CI/CD platforms and development tooling while supporting engineering teams. • Identify opportunities for standardization, automation, reliability, and operational improvement. What we bring to the table: • The opportunity to work on a large-scale enterprise CI/CD and development tooling platform. • Exposure to a broad technology landscape including Azure, Kubernetes, Go, Ansible, Azure DevOps, SonarQube, Nexus, Fortify, and other DevOps tooling. • Opportunities to work across cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, automation, CI/CD, SecDevOps, and infrastructure as code. • The opportunity to support and improve platforms used by a large community of IT engineers. • Collaboration with experienced DevOps, infrastructure, platform, and development teams. • Opportunities to contribute to self-service onboarding, automation, infrastructure standardization, and continuous improvement.
