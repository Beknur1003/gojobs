About the Position: In this role, you will help design, build, and own mission-critical solutions built for our in-house Security Services teams, enabling them to deliver high value services to our global customers. You will work cross-functionally with UX designers, product managers, and other developers to understand problems, develop solutions in an iterative cycle, and deliver solutions that directly impact our global customers. Your overarching goal is to deliver high-value services that help our Security Services teams protect our customers. Responsibilities • Collaborate with the cross-functional teams to continuously improve solutions that enhance stakeholder effectiveness and operational efficacy • Work with the latest technologies especially AI and broaden your skillset • Grow your experience in the Cybersecurity Operations space • Watch your work come alive daily through a mature CI/CD pipeline • Solve complex engineering challenges related to scalability, reliability, performance, latency, throughput, and cost efficiency in cloud-native environments.
