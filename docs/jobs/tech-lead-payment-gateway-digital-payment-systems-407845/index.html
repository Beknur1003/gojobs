What We're Looking For • Strong experience in software engineering, with a proven track record of designing, developing, and maintaining production-grade financial technology or payment systems. • Hands-on experience in a technical leadership role, including system architecture, technical decision-making, code reviews, and mentoring engineering teams. • Strong backend development experience using Golang and/or PHP/Laravel, with the ability to evaluate and implement scalable backend architectures. • Proven experience building or architecting payment gateways, e-wallets, payment switching systems, digital payment platforms, or other transaction-heavy fintech applications. • Strong understanding of end-to-end payment processing, including: • Money-In: Virtual Accounts (VA), QRIS, Payment Links, bank transfers, payment gateways, and switching integrations. • Money-Out: Bank transfers, disbursements, PPOB, QRIS, payment gateways, and switching integrations. • Deep understanding of financial transaction processing, including wallet architecture, ledger management, balance integrity, idempotency, transaction consistency, duplicate transaction prevention, and reversal handling. • Experience with payment reconciliation, settlement, refunds, transaction monitoring, and exception handling. • Strong understanding of payment integration standards, RESTful APIs, authentication, authorization, OAuth2, JWT, webhooks, and secure API communication. • Experience designing distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architectures, and asynchronous processing using technologies such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, and Redis. • Strong database design and optimization skills, particularly with PostgreSQL, MySQL, or other transactional databases. • Experience designing systems for high availability, scalability, fault tolerance, disaster recovery, and production resilience. • Strong understanding of application security, data protection, access controls, audit trails, and secure handling of financial transactions. • Ability to diagnose complex production incidents, identify root causes, and implement long-term technical solutions. • Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work closely with Product, QA, DevOps, Security, and other stakeholders. What You'll Do • Lead the technical design, architecture, and development of payment gateway and digital payment systems. • Define scalable and maintainable system architectures that support Money-In, Money-Out, payment processing, and financial transaction management. • Establish engineering standards, coding guidelines, architectural principles, and best practices across the development team. • Oversee the implementation of critical payment components, including transaction processing, ledger management, reconciliation, settlement, refunds, and payment status tracking. • Ensure transaction integrity and reliability through appropriate architectural controls, including idempotency, consistency management, retry mechanisms, and failure recovery. • Design and optimize distributed systems, databases, caching, and asynchronous messaging to support transaction-heavy workloads. • Lead technical reviews, troubleshoot complex system issues, and guide the team in resolving production incidents. • Mentor developers, review code, improve engineering capabilities, and promote effective collaboration within the team. • Work closely with Product, QA, DevOps, Security, and business stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical solutions. • Drive continuous improvements in system performance, reliability, security, maintainability, and development efficiency. • Evaluate new technologies and make technical decisions based on scalability, security, business needs, and long-term maintainability. Preferred Qualifications • Previous experience leading engineering teams in a payment gateway, e-wallet, digital banking, payment switching, or fintech company. • Proven experience designing or scaling payment systems handling real-world financial transactions in production. • Experience integrating with banks, payment service providers, QRIS providers, switching networks, and other financial institutions. • Understanding of Indonesian payment ecosystem requirements and relevant regulatory or security considerations. • Experience with cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, containerization, Kubernetes, and observability tools. • Experience working in regulated financial environments with strict transaction reliability, auditability, and security requirements.
