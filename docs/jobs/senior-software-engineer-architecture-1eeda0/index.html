About Us Rho is the modern banking platform built for the AI era. Startups and growth-stage companies can open accounts in minutes, issue cards, manage expenses, pay bills, and close the books – all in one connected platform backed by real human support. About the Role Our team is looking for a Senior Software Engineer (Python) to join our Architecture team remotely in Poland to help build & scale Rho’s all-in-one finance platform. If you are eager and interested to learn, here is the tech stack that you will be working with: • Python and Go • GraphQL and gRPC • PostgreSQL • Docker and Kubernetes • Google Cloud Platform (GCP) Qualifications What we're looking for: • Deep expertise in Python, async programming and performance optimization • Experience building web apps and services to support a great product • Knowledge of distributed systems and event-driven architectures including trade-offs made to either support higher load or lower latency within an application • Experience in building CI/CD pipelines, familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Tools • Experience working within complex, regulated environments, coordinating across multiple partners and stakeholders. • Experience in a start-up or enterprise within a highly technical market segment, such as AI, security, finance and blockchain, or infrastructure Nice to have: • Proficiency in Go/TypeScript/Ruby or similar technologies • Proficiency building & deploying applications with Docker and Kubernetes • Experience with message brokers and event streaming platforms • Experience working in a cloud computing platform like GCP or AWS • Experience working in finance including an understanding of how different payment networks work, e.g. ACH, Fedwire, SWIFT • Knowledge of accounting or financial software • Experience mentoring junior engineers Salary range: 100,000 - 160,000 USD net per year What we offer In return we’re prepared to offer a competitive package. We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity while recruiting for our team. We welcome all qualified applications.
