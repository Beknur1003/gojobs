Meet the Team We are currently looking for talent to join our Intersight development team. Cisco Intersight is a cloud-based system management platform for Cisco UCS (Unified Computing System) and many other products which enables IT organizations to analyse, simplify and automate their environments. It is driven by analytics and machine learning to provide actionable intelligence for IT operations/management. Your Impact This is a unique opportunity to be part of a dynamic core team and help shape the future of compute, network and storage management technology as well as product direction. Minimum Qualifications • Education & Experience: B.E/M.Tech. with 12+ years of experience. • Core Competencies: A successful candidate will bring deep technical expertise, especially in cloud native technologies, and the ability to work within a fast-moving, start-up-like environment to deliver solid code that has a broad business impact. • System Focus: The ideal candidate should demonstrate a system-level focus while understanding the impact of every component. Broad knowledge of and exposure to system management software are essential for success in this role. • Experience designing and implementing software systems with high emphasis on scalability and availability. • Experience working on SaaS Enterprise Software or Cloud Infrastructure with exposure to Microservices architecture. • Solid knowledge of Object-Oriented Concepts and Design. • Fundamentally strong in Data Structures and Algorithms. • Expertise in programming languages like GoLang, Java or C/C++. • Expertise in working with Dockers, Kubernetes environments. • Experience with AWS. • Strong analytical and debugging skills. Preferred Qualification • Excellent communication skills and a proven ability to manage competing high priority tasks. • Independent, accountable, strong sense of ownership, aggressive, communicative, sense of urgency and flexible/adaptable. • A passion for quality, for doing the right thing for the customers, the product, and for the company.
