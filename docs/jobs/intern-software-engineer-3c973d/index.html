Job Requisition ID # 26WD101095 Position Overview We are looking for undergraduates who have the talent and ambition to become skilled software engineers. During your placement with us, we will ensure that you are challenged and given every opportunity to grow your knowledge and experience with the support of an experienced Autodesk developer. The intern will be working with the team in Krakow, which develops various technologies related to frontend and backend in such products as Fusion Simulations and Autodesk CFD. The internship will be focused on improvements and development in one of those products. The goal of the internship project is to enhance development processes and increase the quality of the code and test automation.
