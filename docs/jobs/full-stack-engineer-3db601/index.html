As a Full Stack Engineer, you’ll join a Cloud Engineering organization where engineers are encouraged to think beyond traditional frontend and backend boundaries. You’ll have the opportunity to own meaningful features end to end, work across different layers of the stack, and contribute to products and infrastructure used at scale. We are also actively evolving how software is built in the AI era. You’ll work in an environment where AI-assisted development is part of the engineering process, with room to experiment with new tools, workflows, and ways of delivering software faster without compromising quality. This is an opportunity to have real technical ownership, influence how we build, and grow beyond a single engineering discipline while working with experienced teams across Frontend, Backend, Platform and QA. Location: This role is 100% remote as long as you’re in the EET or Central Europe time zone +/- 2 hours. Interview Process: 3 stages - HR conversation ➡️ Values/Technical discussion ➡️ Live Technical interview Technologies: Python, Golang, TypeScript, NextJS, JavaScript, Kubernetes, Docker, Microservices Reporting to: Frontend Tech Lead & Frontend Manager Your team: You’ll join a team of 8 colleagues, (1 Manager, 1 Tech Lead, 5 Senior Developers, YOU!) What will make your journey with us amazing? A supportive manager who cares about your well-being and is invested in your professional growth. A culture of continuous learning, with clear targets and feedback. A global company with over 2500 employees located in more than 26 countries around the world, including offices in 3 countries: Ukraine, Portugal, and India. What will you do: • Build and maintain customer-facing features across frontend applications and backend services. • Design and integrate APIs that support reliable, scalable product experiences. • Own features end to end, from technical design and implementation through testing and deployment. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver initiatives. • Use AI-assisted development tools to accelerate implementation while reviewing, validating, and improving generated code. • Improve code quality, performance, reliability, and maintainability across the systems you contribute to. What will you bring: • Strong professional experience building modern web applications across the full stack, with particular depth in backend engineering. • Advanced knowledge of Python and/or Golang, with hands-on experience designing and building backend services, REST APIs, and databases. • Solid experience with Docker and Kubernetes, and comfort operating and deploying services in containerized environments. • Experience with microservices architectures and building systems that scale reliably under increasing load and traffic. • Working knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript and modern frontend frameworks such as NextJS, with the ability to build and maintain customer-facing features. • Ability to design, implement, test, and troubleshoot features across the full application stack. • Experience using AI coding tools or agents as part of the development workflow, with the ability to review, validate, and improve AI-generated solutions. • Strong software engineering fundamentals, including testing, code quality, performance, security, scalability, and maintainable system design. What’s in it for you: Embrace a 100% remote lifestyle with this opportunity! Work with flexibility in a supportive environment where you have the autonomy to manage your time, while also staying connected with the team through daily check-ins and shared office hours. We value collaboration and commitment to team goals, balancing independence with structured support to ensure we all succeed together. • Invest in your growth with dedicated learning resources and support. • Thrive in a culture rooted in truth, trust, and transparency. • Unleash your creativity and explore new ideas with 2 dedicated R&D days each month! • Stay ahead of the curve with weekly team knowledge-sharing sessions. • Escape the meeting marathon with 3 meeting-free days per week. • Enjoy generous vacation policies to recharge when you need it. • Be a part of a unique team, not just another "cloud-shop" - we run our own infrastructure! #NamecheapCareers #HackYourCareer #equalopportunity
