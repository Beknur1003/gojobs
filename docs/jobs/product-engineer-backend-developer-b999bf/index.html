About E2B E2B is a fast-growing Series A startup with over $37M in funding and 8-figure revenue. We are building the Agent Cloud and power the infrastructure behind AI labs and some of the most widely used consumer and enterprise agents, including Manus, Genspark, Lindy, Groq, and Artificial Analysis. Our team includes original authors of Firecracker, the AWS microVM technology powering Lambda, along with alumni of Cognition, JetBrains, Zapier, and Wish. About the role Your job will be to own and scale the backend that powers the E2B Dashboard and SDK -the APIs, data, and services developers rely on to run, monitor, and debug their sandboxes. You're the backend counterpart to our dashboard engineers: they own the experience, you own the services and data behind it, and together you ship product end-to-end. This is product/application backend - APIs, data layer, auth, billing, usage metrics, real-time log/metric pipelines. It is not our low-level infrastructure (Firecracker, orchestration, the sandbox runtime) - that lives with our Core and Platform teams, who you'll integrate with but not duplicate. The split is roughly 80% Go backend, 20% TypeScript. You live in Go, but you're comfortable dropping into the Next.js app to wire things up end-to-end and unblock the frontend rather than throwing work over a wall. You'll build enterprise-grade services that scale to 100k+ weekly active users. Responsibilities include: • Owning the Go services behind the dashboard and SDK. APIs, data models, and the contracts the frontend builds on • Designing auth/authorization, billing, and usage-metering surfaces • Building the real-time backends (WebSockets, SSE) that feed sandbox logs and metrics to the dashboard • Optimizing data access and server-side performance like query design, caching, response times • Partnering closely with dashboard engineers to ship features end-to-end, and with Core/Platform on the boundaries where product meets infrastructure • Making architectural decisions around observability, testing, and security best practices This role requires a high amount of ownership and the ability to take projects from 0 to 1. You'll have deep autonomy to make backend and product decisions. If you see a better way to build something, you build it. What we're looking for • 3-5+ years shipping production backend services, with deep expertise in Go • Strong API design with REST/RPC, contracts, versioning, and an eye for the interface the frontend needs • Solid with PostgreSQL: schema design, query optimization, migrations, and reasoning about data at scale • Backend fundamentals: authentication/authorization, server-side performance, and building real-time data flows (WebSockets, SSE) • Working TypeScript(Next.js is a plus) competence. You won't live here, but you can move into the app-router frontend to wire up a feature end-to-end and not be lost • Understand data fetching patterns on frontend side • Excited to work in person from San Francisco on a devtool product • Ownership mentality: you see projects through from conception to launch to iteration • Comfortable talking to customers and explaining technical tradeoffs • Not being afraid to take ownership of your part of the product • Great communication, curiosity, and interest in the AI space If you join E2B, you'll get a lot of freedom. We expect you to be proactive and take ownership. You'll be taking projects from 0 to 1 with the support of the rest of the team. What it’s like to work at E2B We’re a fast-growing startup with in-person (4 days on-site, 1 day WFH) offices in San Francisco and Prague, Czech Republic. We already generate 8-figure revenue and work directly with top-tier AI companies like Manus, Genspark, Lindy, Groq, Artificial Analysis and other exciting teams pushing the frontier of AI. We offer healthcare, vision, and dental insurance, unlimited PTO, 401k, and a variety of perks for in-office employees.
