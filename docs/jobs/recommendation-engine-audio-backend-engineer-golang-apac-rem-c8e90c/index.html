Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world. What You'll Do • Core Backend & Audio Pipeline Development: Build, optimize, and maintain high-concurrency Golang services supporting both recommendation pipelines (data ingestion, ranking, personalized delivery) and real-time audio processing workflows (Speech-to-Text / ASR and Text-to-Speech / TTS). • Dynamic Model Routing & Orchestration: Design and implement intelligent routing mechanisms that dynamically select and switch between third-party or internal AI/audio models in real time based on network conditions, latency, cost, and environment status. • System Architecture & Quality Elevation: Take full ownership of existing live production services—re-architecting bottlenecks, improving audio/recommendation output quality, and ensuring scalable, low-latency, and fault-tolerant data flows. • Observability & Reliability: Build end-to-end observability covering strategy effectiveness, audio conversion quality, latency, and serving health, driving continuous performance and cost improvements. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with algorithm, data, product, and business teams to translate product requirements into robust engineering solutions. What We're Looking For • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • 5–10 years of backend engineering experience with expert-level proficiency in Golang and recent hands-on production experience in high-concurrency systems. • Strong Engineering & Architecture Skills: Proven ability to independently design, refactor, and scale complex backend architectures, distributed systems, microservices, databases (SQL/NoSQL), message queues (e.g., Kafka), and caching layers. • Dynamic Routing & Multi-Model Orchestration: Hands-on engineering experience integrating multiple external/internal AI APIs or models, with the ability to build fallback, load-balancing, and environment-aware dynamic routing logic. • Recommendation or Audio Systems Background: Solid understanding of recommendation pipelines (ranking, personalization) or real-time audio processing workflows (ASR/TTS). • Communication & Ownership: Strong sense of ownership and communication skills, capable of stepping into a live production environment to diagnose issues, elevate system quality, and collaborate effectively across teams. Nice to Have • Audio Engineering Experience: Prior background building backend systems for voice/audio processing, speech recognition (ASR), voice synthesis (TTS), or real-time audio streaming in Golang. • Tier-1 / Large-Scale Tech Experience: Experience working at major tech companies ("Big Tech") handling high-traffic, large-scale production systems. • Data & LLM Workflows: Experience with data pipelines, feature stores, LLM integration, or AI Agent workflows.
