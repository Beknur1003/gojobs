About the Opportunity: Are you a talented Public Cloud Network – Security Engineer looking for their next challenge? Do you have a passion for building cutting-edge enterprise products and a hands-on approach to engineering? Join Citi's Cloud Technology Services (CTS) team and be part of our commitment to transform Citi technology, leveraging game-changing Cloud capabilities to drive agility, efficiency, and innovation. We are providing our businesses with a competitive edge by leveraging public cloud scale and enabling new infrastructure economics As an Network Security Engineer within the Network Infrastructure Team, you will play a pivotal role in shaping and executing our public cloud strategy. You will be part of a Public Cloud team that continues to deliver big by designing and managing the platform that enables the automated deployment of foundational resources globally across multiple public clouds. This foundation enables the business to deploy critical workloads, from building a cloud base High Performance Compute (HPC) platform to run huge risk calculations, enabling Citi to leverage GenAI at scale, all the way to enabling payment solutions, this team is at the forefront of innovation. Role Description: The role of Public Cloud Network – Security Engineer will be instrumental in ensuring Citi public cloud resources deployed in GCP and AWS are secured using industry standard tools and best practices. You will be responsible for designing, certifying, deploying and managing the security network infrastructure resources within GCP and AWS that secure all traffic flows between resource in the cloud and on-premises. The role requires an engineer with expert technical skills and the ability to collaborate with stakeholders and partners throughout the full resource provisioning life cycle, from inception and design, to deployment, operational management and iterative refinement. You will have a voice within the team to influence strategic decisions, contribute to the creation of roadmaps and engage with key technology and business stakeholders. The Network Infrastructure Team is based in multiple locations across the globe and comprises of Architects, Cloud Engineers and Security Engineers. What you’ll do: • Technical Expertise: design and build highly resilient and scalable security solutions that support the business requirements as part of the organisations public cloud strategy. • Engineering Excellence: follow engineering best practices and industry standards using modern tooling that enables CI/CD and favours automation, auditability, automated testing, infrastructure and policy as code. • Collaborative Development: work with a talented team of cloud engineers across the wider Public Cloud team who are responsible for Network, Project / Account Provisioning, Platform, Data, Pipelines, and Operations. You will also work closely with the business functions who are creating / migrating service and applications to the cloud environment. • Innovate and Automate: identify and develop automated solutions as part of continual improvement to reduce the time to market of cloud services, enhancing client satisfaction and delivering business value. • Compliance Advocacy: ensure adherence to relevant standards, policies, and regulations, prioritizing the protection of Citi's reputation, clients, and assets.
