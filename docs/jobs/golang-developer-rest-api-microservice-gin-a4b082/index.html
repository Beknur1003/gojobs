Microservices
This role is a great fit for experienced professionals with a solid background in Golang development, particularly those who thrive in creating scalable applications. As a Golang Developer, your expertise will be crucial in designing robust RESTful APIs and microservices that cater to client requirements and enhance our product functionality. You will be responsible for developing and maintaining high-performance applications, ensuring they are optimized for reliability and efficiency. Your tasks will include working collaboratively with various teams to define and implement new features, troubleshooting and debugging issues to enhance application performance, and participating in code reviews to promote best practices within the team. Staying current with industry trends and technologies will also be part of your role, ensuring that our solutions remain innovative. Key Responsibilities: - Develop and maintain high-performance applications using Golang. - Design and implement RESTful APIs and microservices architecture. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features. - Troubleshoot and debug applications to optimize performance and reliability. - Participate in code reviews and contribute to team knowledge sharing. To excel in this position, you should have proficiency in the Golang programming language, experience with RESTful API development, a strong understanding of microservices architecture, and familiarity with the Gin framework. Your problem-solving skills and attention to detail will be integral as you contribute to building advanced solutions. In this role, you will have the opportunity to deepen your technical expertise while working on challenging projects that impact the organization positively.
