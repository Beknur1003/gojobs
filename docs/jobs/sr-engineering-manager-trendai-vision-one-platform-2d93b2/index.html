TrendAI™, the global AI security leader and enterprise business unit of Trend Micro, empowers organizations with full AI visibility and consolidated security that inspires confidence, drives innovation, and eliminates risk. At TrendAI™, we’re always seeking exceptional talent; people who want to collaborate with the best and push boundaries together. Here, your work goes beyond building a career. You will help protect what matters and play a vital role in shaping a safer, more trustworthy AI-powered future. AI Fearlessly. Position Summary: We're looking for a deeply technical engineering leader to drive the development of high-quality, AI-powered applications within the Vision One platform at TrendAI. This role is first and foremost for someone who lives and breathes software, architecture, and security — someone who can earn the trust of senior engineers through technical credibility, not just seniority. You'll lead a team of 8–12 developers, shaping both the technical direction and the people behind it. You'll remove blockers, raise the bar on quality, and ensure the team ships work that matters to customers. If you love hard technical problems, care deeply about security, and want to do it all in the context of building next-generation cybersecurity products, we want to talk to you. Work Location: Ottawa, Ontario, On-site / Hybrid: 3 days in office What You’ll Do: In your technical leadership capacity, you will: • Drive architectural decisions for cloud-native, distributed systems — microservices, event-driven systems, REST APIs — with a security-first mindset • Stay hands-on: review code, prototype solutions, and contribute directly when needed • Own and enforce security practices across the SDLC, including threat modeling, secure coding standards, and vulnerability management • Lead the team's adoption of an AI-augmented software development lifecycle (AI-SDLC), including spec-driven development using agentic AI • Stay current with competitors' solutions and the market landscape, translating that knowledge into product and technical decisions • Champion continuous improvement through experimentation, retrospectives, and cross-team knowledge sharing In your product ownership capacity, you will: • Collaborate with stakeholders to define functional and non-functional requirements, translating them into clear, actionable specs for your team • Lead project planning and execution, ensuring the team has direction, resources, and clear priorities to deliver high-quality work on time • Own communications and coordination for your areas of Vision One, keeping customer experience at the center of every decision In your people management capacity, you will: • Hold regular 1:1s with direct reports and support their career growth through mentorship and honest feedback • Recruit for your teams: from job descriptions to interviews to closing and onboarding • Run semi-annual talent reviews and annual salary assessments, including the conversations that come with them • Support team members with HR, IT, and administrative issues as needed What You Should Have: Required: • 7+ years of software development experience, with strong proficiency in one or more of: Java, Golang, Python, JavaScript. • 5+ years in cloud-native application development, including infrastructure as code and CI/CD pipelines • Experience building and deploying containerized applications using orchestration technology such as Kubernetes • Solid experience with microservice architectures, REST API design, and event-driven systems • Passion for cybersecurityExperience or strong familiarity with AI agents, agentic frameworks, and protocols such as MCP and A2A • 3+ years managing software engineering teams, with a proven ability to lead and grow them • Excellent communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences Preferred: • Knowledge of AI security risks: prompt injection, adversarial inputs, data privacy, model integrity • Experience with LLM guardrails, safety layers, or responsible AI practices • Familiarity with AI/ML development: model training, fine-tuning, prompt engineering, or building AI-powered features in production systems • Knowledge of the cybersecurity threat landscape, or a background in threat intelligence, malware analysis, or security research Expected base salary range: $150,000 – $190,000 per year (CAD) Bonus/incentive eligibility: Eligible for the company-wide
