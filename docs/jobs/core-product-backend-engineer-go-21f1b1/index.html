We're looking for a mid-senior Backend Engineer with strong Go and PostgreSQL skills. You'll join an early-stage, venture-backed startup as one of its first backend hires. The company is building a TikTok-style endless scroll of user-generated hypercasual mini-games. Your job is to build the core services behind the game feed, user data and content delivery, and to shape the product's technical direction for years to come. This is a founding-level role with real ownership, for someone who wants to build from the ground up and not maintain someone else's system. Details: • Location: Sofia, Bulgaria, on-site 5 days a week • Employment: Full-time (no contractor, consultant or B2B setups) • Level: Mid-Senior • Relocation: Supported for strong candidates (1–1.5 months' notice maximum) What you'll do • Design and build the Go services that power the game feed, user data and content delivery • Own PostgreSQL schema design and query performance as the product scales • Own deployments and keep services reliable (Linux, Docker) • Set up observability (logging, metrics, tracing) from day one • Work on data pipelines and recommendation / feed-ranking logic • Design REST APIs for iOS and Android clients • Work closely with the founders on technical and product direction Tech stack Go, Fiber, PostgreSQL, Docker, Linux, REST APIs, data pipelines, recommendation systems Requirements • 3+ years of production Go experience, having shipped and maintained services under real traffic • Solid PostgreSQL experience beyond basic CRUD, including schema design and query optimisation • Hands-on experience with Linux and Docker, including owning deployments • Based in Sofia or willing to relocate there • Ready for full-time, 5-day on-site work Nice to have • Experience with recommendation systems, feed ranking or ML-adjacent data pipelines • Background in hyper-casual gaming or rapid-prototyping environments • Experience designing APIs for mobile clients • A startup or founder-led mindset, comfortable with speed and ambiguity Hiring process • Online interview to discuss your experience and mutual fit • In-person technical challenge and interview at the Sofia office
