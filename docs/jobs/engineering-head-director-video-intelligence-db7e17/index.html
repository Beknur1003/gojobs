Engineering Head/Director - Video Intelligence

Talent Scout Management Solutions/ PIVOTAL Bangalore; Kolkata опубликовано 16 октября 2026

JavaC++AI/LLMFinTech
Head/Director of Engineering Experience: 20+ years in Software Engineering Location: Kolkata/Bangalore Reporting to: CTO Team Size: 50+ Engineering professionals About Company: Company is an Indian video intelligence and technology company building solutions across Video Management Systems (VMS), AI Video Analytics, Traffic Management, Face Recognition, Cloud/VSaaS and Edge Computing. Our technology is deployed across 150+ cities, enterprises, public-sector organisations and critical infrastructure, with more than 300,000 cameras streaming through our platform. Our engineering organisation works across video streaming, computer vision, AI, cloud, edge, embedded systems and enterprise-scale platforms. We are looking for an experienced Head/Director of Engineering to lead our engineering organization and play a critical role in scaling our technology platform for the next phase of growth across India, the US, MEA and APAC. The Opportunity: This is a hands-on engineering leadership role for someone who enjoys solving complex technology and scale problems not simply managing delivery. You will own engineering across a large, mission-critical video platform where performance, reliability, scalability, security and cost efficiency directly impact customer outcomes. Our platform operates across on-premise, edge and cloud environments, supporting demanding video workloads and large-scale deployments. The role will involve modernizing a mature technology platform while continuing to deliver for existing customers. Key Responsibilities: 1. Engineering Strategy & Architecture: - Define and execute the 2 - 3 year engineering and architecture roadmap in partnership with the CTO/founder. - Drive convergence across on-premise, edge and cloud architectures. - Build scalable architectures for video streaming, AI/ML inference, analytics and distributed platforms. - Drive the evolution towards multimodal video intelligence and GenAI-enabled capabilities. - Ensure technology decisions balance innovation, scalability, reliability and cost. 2. Engineering Delivery: - Own engineering delivery across the complete product portfolio. - Ensure predictable releases, high quality and adherence to engineering commitments. - Establish strong engineering processes around planning, development, testing, release and production support. - Work closely with Product, R&D, Presales, Sales and Technical Support to deliver customer commitments. - Lead engineering execution for large government and enterprise programmes. 3. Performance & Scalability: - Make performance, latency, channel density and cost per camera core engineering metrics. - Improve video processing and inference efficiency across GPU, edge and cloud environments. - Drive optimisation of video pipelines and distributed systems at scale. - Ensure the platform can support increasing camera volumes without compromising reliability or customer experience. 4. Technology & Product Engineering: - Lead engineering across: Video Management Systems, Video Streaming, AI/Computer Vision, Video Analytics, Traffic Management, Face Recognition, Cloud/VSaaS, Edge Computing, Embedded Systems, QA & Automation. - Ensure architectural consistency across multiple deployment environments. - Modernise legacy systems while continuing to support a large installed customer base. 5. Reliability, Security & Quality: - Own engineering standards for uptime, data integrity, backward compatibility and security. - Establish robust engineering practices for mission-critical and large-scale deployments. - Ensure compliance with relevant government and security standards, including STQC and government-grade benchmarks. - Build engineering systems that can operate reliably under real-world field conditions. 6. AI & Engineering Productivity: - Drive responsible adoption of AI-assisted software development and agentic engineering tools. - Establish guidelines for the use of AI-generated code, including code review, security, provenance and quality controls. - Identify opportunities to improve engineering productivity without compromising reliability or security. - Partner with R&D on productization of deep-learning and computer-vision systems. 7. People & Organisation Leadership: - Lead and develop a 50+ member engineering organisation across systems, AI/CV, platform, embedded engineering and QA. - Build a strong second line of Engineering Managers, Senior Managers and Architects. - Hire, develop and retain high-quality engineering talent. - Build succession and leadership capability within the engineering organisation. - Address organisational bottlenecks, single points of failure and capability gaps. - Create a culture of ownership, accountability, technical excellence and continuous improvement. 8. Customer & Business Partnership: - Act as the engineering face of the organisation for strategic customers, partners and system integrators. - Participate in critical customer escalations and solution discussions. - Translate customer and market requirements into engineering priorities. - Partner with Product and Business teams to align technology investments with business objectives. What We Are Looking For: Essential Experience & Skills: - 20+ years of experience in designing software architecture for distributed computing platform with at least 8+ years leading large engineering teams, including managers. - Strong experience leading engineering organisations of 50+ professionals. - Proven experience owning products at scale across architecture, quality, performance and cost. - Strong understanding of cloud technologies and distributed systems. - Hands-on technical depth in one or more of: C++, Java, Golang, Real-time video/streaming, High-performance computing, Distributed platforms. - Strong understanding of image processing, AI and computer vision. - Ability to review architecture and code and challenge technical decisions credibly. - Experience with production-grade deep-learning systems, including: Model accuracy, Inference performance, Cost optimisation, Evaluation methodology, Production monitoring. - Demonstrated experience hiring, developing and retaining strong engineering talent. - Experience turning around a challenging engineering team, product or programme. - Strong experience working in a high-ownership, fast-paced technology environment. Strongly Preferred: - Experience in one or more of the following will be highly valuable: Video Management Systems / Video Analytics, Video streaming and media pipelines, GStreamer / FFmpeg, ONVIF / RTSP, Edge AI / inference, NVIDIA Jetson / TensorRT, ONNX Runtime / OpenVINO, MLOps and model drift detection, On-premise to multi-tenant SaaS transformation, Public-sector / government technology deployments, Large-scale enterprise or critical-infrastructure programmes, Tender-driven technology environments and compliance audits. Leadership Expectations: - We are looking for a leader who is technically credible and intellectually curious. - Remains close enough to technology to understand complex engineering problems. - Communicates bad news early and transparently. - Takes ownership rather than waiting for perfect processes or information. - Can balance short-term delivery with long-term architecture. - Is comfortable making difficult technology and people decisions. - Builds strong leaders rather than becoming the single point of decision-making. - Treats privacy, security and responsible AI as fundamental engineering responsibilities. Why Join Company?: - Lead engineering for a large-scale video intelligence platform. - Work across AI, computer vision, cloud, edge, video streaming and distributed systems. - Influence technology strategy across India and international markets. - Work directly with senior leadership and the technology leadership team. - Own technology that operates in the real world across smart cities, transportation, banking, manufacturing, education, aviation and retail. - Opportunity to build and scale a strong engineering organisation from Kolkata. - Leadership role with visibility across research, product development, production and field deployment. - If you are passionate about leading an innovative, energetic, and technology-driven engineering team, we invite you to be part of Company' growth journey!
