About the Client Our client is a payment orchestration platform dedicated to optimizing and scaling payment flows for online businesses. They provide intelligent routing, real-time analytics, and partner with various payment service providers. With a PCI DSS-certified gateway, they enhance acceptance rates, reduce costs, and ensure secure transactions. Since its founding in 2019, the company has served mid-market and enterprise clients worldwide, including those in high-risk sectors like iGaming. The company is headquartered in Lithuania and has offices in Malta and Cyprus, with R&D centers in Poland and Georgia. Role Overview Join an exciting project as a Senior Golang Engineer focused on transforming test infrastructure. You will tackle the challenge of migrating and redesigning a Java-based test automation environment to Golang, enhancing its reliability and scalability. This remote position offers the opportunity to influence architectural decisions and integrate cutting-edge technology in a fully remote team. Key Responsibilities • Analyse the existing Java-based test automation infrastructure and contribute to its transformation into Golang. • Design, develop, and maintain reliable and scalable test infrastructure using Golang. • Migrate, redesign, or replace existing Java components where required. • Contribute to architectural decisions and help define the technical approach for the transformation. • Ensure the stability of existing testing processes throughout the migration. • Integrate the new infrastructure with CI/CD pipelines and the broader development environment. • Collaborate closely with QA, Backend, and DevOps engineers. • Troubleshoot technical issues and improve the performance, reliability, and maintainability of the infrastructure. • Write clear technical documentation and follow engineering best practices. • Take ownership of assigned technical areas and deliver them independently from analysis to implementation. Requirements • Strong commercial experience in Golang at Senior Engineer level. • Hands-on experience developing test automation infrastructure, testing frameworks, or internal engineering tools. • Experience with Java and the ability to understand and work with an existing Java codebase. • Experience migrating, modernising, or redesigning backend systems or infrastructure. • Strong understanding of software architecture, APIs, backend systems, and microservices. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes. • Strong debugging and problem-solving skills. • Ability to work independently, make sound technical decisions, and take ownership of delivery. • Good communication and cross-functional collaboration skills. • Professional working proficiency in English and Russian. Key Soft Skills • Strong analytical thinking and the ability to approach complex problems methodically. • Excellent stakeholder management skills to effectively communicate with cross-functional teams. • Adaptability to fast-paced environments, with the ability to pivot and adjust strategies quickly. What We Offer • Competitive salary aligned with experience • Collaborative and transparent team culture • High-impact role in a scaling fintech company • Recognition of personal milestones and achievements • Social benefits
