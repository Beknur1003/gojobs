(+ we're working a 4 day work week - Fridays off!) 🏡 Contract: Permanent Salary: £72,000 - £82,500 + benefits listed below About Cuvva We're on a mission to give everyone affordable access to a car anytime, anywhere 🚙 We offer short-term car insurance through our world-class app, creating truly flexible products that meet people’s real needs. Using lightning-fast technology to unlock better experiences and fairer prices for our customers, Cuvva is building the future of insurance every day. Cuvva was the first to sell hourly insurance via an app in the UK back in 2016. And we haven’t stopped innovating since. Join us. 🚀 Our Product Engineering teams At Cuvva, we run lean, highly collaborative, cross-functional product teams. As our new Full Stack Engineer, you'll initially join our internal product incubator squad, focused on exploring and developing new product opportunities for the business. The team moves quickly and values ownership, autonomy and action, making it well suited to engineers who are comfortable working through ambiguity, testing ideas quickly and learning through iteration. This isn't a role where you'll spend months delivering pre-defined tickets. You'll work alongside Product and Design from the earliest stages of discovery, helping shape ideas, challenge assumptions and build products that reach customers quickly. We believe our best engineers don't just write code - they influence product direction, improve how we work, and solve meaningful customer problems. At Cuvva, people come from a wide range of backgrounds and bring different but complementary skill sets. Within Engineering, you're not required to have a university degree; many of us are self-taught. Our Tech Stack: • Frontend: JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Redux (with Redux-Sagas) • Backend: Golang & NodeJS , with PostgreSQL and MongoDB • Architecture: Microservices, APIs, and serverless services Infrastructure & tooling: AWS, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, Redis, CI/CD pipelines • Practices: Automation-first, metrics-driven, incident write-ups and observability baked in Our 2026 Engineering Strategy: We’re not just here to write code, we’re here to redefine how insurance works. Our 2026 engineering strategy is built on five principles: • AI-first: We’re investing in automation and building AI-powered features to serve customers better and faster • Proactive mindset: We take initiative, spot problems early, and act fast • Data-driven decisions: Hypotheses are tested. Gut feel isn’t good enough • Pareto principle: Get 80% of the value with 20% of the effort – polish only when it matters • Testing-first mindset: Stability matters. We test, we ship, we learn About the role: As a Full Stack Engineer (Backend leaning), you'll help shape the next generation of Cuvva's products, taking ideas from early experiments through to production and putting them in the hands of more than 1.5 million drivers. You’ll help bridge the gap between disciplines, collaborating with backend, web, product and design to deliver seamless, user-centric solutions. You won’t just ship code, you'll have the autonomy to challenge ideas, influence technical decisions and help shape the future of our products from discovery through to production. Whether that’s building new features, new services, building out internal dashboards, expanding our design systems, or optimising developer experience. You’ll move between product delivery and platform improvement, bringing a positive, proactive and collaborative mindset to the team. On a day-to-day basis you’ll: • Write clean, maintainable, scalable, and well-tested code across Frontend and Backend (React, Go, Node etc.). • Collaborate closely with Designers, PMs, and fellow Engineers to scope, design and deliver features end-to-end. • Build and maintain services, APIs and internal dashboards that improve both customer experience and internal operations. • Support infrastructure changes, deployment pipelines, and automation initiatives. • Contribute to design systems and ensure pixel-perfect implementation on the frontend. • Proactively identify and solve both functional and technical problems in the product. • Share knowledge across teams, helping to raise the bar for the full stack discipline at Cuvva. • Participate in standups, retros, and team ceremonies, keeping work visible and moving smoothly. • Support onboarding, mentor teammates where appropriate and contribute to improving our engineering practices. You’ll do great here if you have: • Solid Backend knowledge, ideally with Golang and/or NodeJS, plus experience with APIs and microservices. • Strong experience with JavaScript/TypeScript and frontend frameworks (React, Redux). • Familiarity with AWS, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, and serverless services. • An understanding of databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB) and scaling distributed systems. • Take ownership of features from idea through to production, balancing speed, quality and customer impact. • An automation-first mindset, using AI thoughtfully to accelerate development, improve developer productivity and maintain high standards of engineering quality. • A proactive, collaborative approach – you like working with teammates across disciplines and sharing what you know. • An eye for continuous improvement, always looking for better ways to build software, improve developer experience and raise engineering standards. • Comfortable with ambiguity – you're curious, adaptable, and enjoy turning uncertain ideas into valuable products. It’s a bonus if you have: • Worked in a startup or scale-up environment. • Operated as a Product Engineer, owning problems end-to-end, not just writing code. • The ability to use data and experimentation to inform engineering and product decisions. • The ability to pivot with enthusiasm and maintain speed when priorities shift.
