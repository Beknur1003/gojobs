About the Role Card Issuing Team provides APIs for businesses to launch their card program without having to go through the long PCI DSS process. What You Will Do Our goal is to build a Card Issuing platform for South East Asia enabling Merchants to easily create their own debit/credit card products. We are building a zero-to-one product therefore there are a lot of gaps, but also opportunities for contributions Responsibilities: • -Working with third party APIs to enable local and global payments for the cards • Building reliable and scalable systems. Managing downtimes and migrations • Build and maintain APIs exposed externally for businesses to issue visa cards • Design and develop internal tools for business operations, including day to day platform management and transactions reporting What We Are Looking For • Interested in bringing to life a zero-to-one product that is first to market in the region • Knowledge of iterative, sprint based product development style • Knowledge in Golang • Knowledge of Git and GitHub • Knowledge of RESTful APIs • Knowledge of microservices • Knowledge of SQL, database design and tuning of database performance Nice to have • Knowledge of GraphQL • Knowledge of queue systems Learning Outcomes • Working knowledge in Golang, and MySQL • Learn to manage small to mid sized engineering projects, prioritization and choosing the highest leverage projects to work on
