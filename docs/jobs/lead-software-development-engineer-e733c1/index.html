Razorpay is one of India’s leading full-stack financial technology companies, powering the way businesses move, manage, and grow money. Founded in 2014 by Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar with a simple vision - to simplify payments for Indian businesses - we’ve since grown into a fintech powerhouse driving India’s digital payment revolution. Razorpay powers millions of businesses with a smarter, scalable stack that goes beyond transactions to help them truly build and grow. From building AI-native agentic payments, to AI-assisted fraud detection and real-time risk intelligence to automated reconciliation, smart payouts, and predictive financial insights, we are embedding intelligence across our stack to make money movement faster, safer, and more efficient. In close collaboration with ecosystem partners - including banks, networks, regulators - we are pioneering industry-first solutions that are shaping the next era of fintech Across India, Singapore and Malaysia, our products span everything from seamless checkouts to payroll automation - powering a fintech ecosystem that’s redefining how money moves across Asia. Today, that ecosystem supports everyone from early-stage startups to some of India’s largest enterprises, enabling them to accept, process, and disburse payments at scale while expanding into new ways of managing money more efficiently. Our scale speaks volumes: Razorpay processes $180+ billion in annualized transactions, powering leading businesses like Airbnb, Facebook, WhatsApp, Airtel, CRED, BookmyShow, Zomato, Swiggy, Lenskart, Mirae Asset Capital markets, Indian Oil, National Pension Scheme - and over 100 of India’s unicorns. With strong roots in India and growing operations in Southeast Asia, we are shaping the next chapter of financial technology across the region. We are backed by global investors including GIC, Peak XV Partners (formerly Sequoia Capital India & SEA), Tiger Global, Ribbit Capital, Matrix Partners, MasterCard, and Salesforce Ventures, having raised over $740 million to date. Strategic acquisitions - including Ezetap (POS and offline payments), Curlec (Malaysia expansion), BillMe (digital invoicing), and POP (rewards-first UPI) - along with earlier moves in fraud prevention, payroll, and lending, have further strengthened our platform and widened our footprint across Asia. But what truly sets Razorpay apart is our culture. At Razorpay, ownership is our oxygen - you own what you build, with no micromanagement or red tape, just the runway to make your ideas fly. Learning is a lifestyle - if you’re curious, you’ll feel at home here. People > Pedigree - we hire for attitude, hustle, and hunger more than degrees. Transparency thrives over titles - this is where interns question CXOs and CXOs say “thank you.” Guided by our values of Customer First, Autonomy & Ownership, Agility with Integrity, Transparency, Challenging the status quo and a strong belief that Razorpay grows with Razors, you’ll be part of a 3000+ strong team building not just products, but the financial infrastructure of the future. About the Role: A Lead Software Development Engineer (Lead SDE) at Razorpay is a well-grounded, innovative, quality-focused product thinker and business-conscious engineer - not a mere coder. Armed with deep technical context and extensive experience, you will build production-grade, highly scalable software and see nuances and edge cases that others might overlook. At our scale, engineering creates a massive impact across global digital payments. Beyond technical excellence, you are expected to operate with a strong ownership mindset in ambiguous environments, act as a technical leader, mentor peers, and actively influence and shape Razorpay’s culture in the right way. What you will build: • Architecture & Design Thinking: Drive ambiguous requirements into clean, low-level designs (LLD) with clear interfaces, data flows, and non-functional requirements (NFRs) before execution. • Engineering Leadership & AI Augmentation: Act as the "tech lead of an AI agent loop". Lead development by utilizing AI-augmented workflows and engineering assistants strategically—handling prompt decomposition, constraint management, and rigorous code validation. • Scalable Systems Delivery: Build full-fledged applications and payment infrastructure from scratch, driving features to completion. Transition systems seamlessly across complex architectural scales. • Code Quality & Integrity: Maintain high-quality implementation standards with clean abstractions, SOLID principles, and modular, maintainable code. Take full ownership of all code codebases, performing independent navigation, debugging, and strict verification without AI assistance when required. • Product & API Strategy: Actively participate in and drive product and design discussions. Shape our public-facing REST APIs for developers worldwide and brainstorm new directions to make online payments easy and accessible. • Robust Testing & Resilience: Implement comprehensive verification strategies—including unit, integration, adversarial, and known-risk testing—to ensure error-free functionality under multi-limit and distributed failure modes. • Collaboration & Mentorship: Mentorship of engineering peers, clearly articulating technical trade-offs, defending architectural decisions, and engaging in constructive discussions across teams to prioritize end-user value. What we are looking for: • 5+ years of experience in professional software technology, preferably within a fast-paced product company. • Expert working knowledge and deep fluency in at least one backend programming language (e.g., GoLang, Java, Python). • Strong grasp of both High-Level Design and Low-Level Design. Proven understanding of distributed systems fundamentals, data storage trade-offs caching, and event-driven architectures. • Resiliency Patterns: Solid understanding of system failure modes and modern mitigation patterns. • Familiarity with Agentic Workflows. Ability to break complex engineering problems down into structured, promptable tasks while maintaining complete accountability for code safety, security gaps, and hallucination detection. • Understanding of web architectures, HTTP protocols, and REST API conventions. • Demonstrated ability to challenge the status quo, evaluate complex technical trade-offs rationally, and display immense agility when iterating on products at scale. • A strong willingness to learn and unlearn new technologies to deliver the best possible product outcome.
