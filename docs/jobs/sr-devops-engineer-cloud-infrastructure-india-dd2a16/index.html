About the Role: We are looking for a Sr. DevOps Engineer (Cloud Infrastructure) to join our team and help ensure the availability, performance, and scalability across our critical multi-cloud infrastructure. You will work closely with development and operations team to build infrastructure, automate processes, enhance system reliability and improve observability You will also lead efforts to work closely with engineering teams to optimize their cloud footprints across GCP, AWS and many more. Key Responsibilities: Cloud Infrastructure & Provisioning: Design, architect, and maintain scalable multi-cloud infrastructure across GCP and AWS using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform). Kubernetes Operations: Manage and operate Kubernetes (GKE) clusters in production on a regular basis, ensuring high availability, security, and workload scalability. Observability & Monitoring: Implement robust observability, telemetry, and monitoring across infrastructure and microservices to enhance system reliability and proactive detection. Incident Management & On-Call Support: Participate in on-call rotations, lead incident response, perform root cause analysis (RCA), and drive long-term reliability improvements. Multi-Cloud & Service Management: Provision, configure, and maintain various GCP and AWS cloud services supporting production workloads. CI/CD & Automation Integration: Build and optimize automated deployment pipelines and platform tooling to facilitate smooth engineering workflows. Security & Best Practices: Enforce cloud infrastructure security standards, networking governance, access controls, and operational best practices. Required Skills & Qualifications: Experience: 4+ years in GCP or other public cloud platforms; Kubernetes (GKE) in production. Containerization: Strong experience with Docker and Kubernetes. CI/CD Expertise: Hands-on experience in Jenkins pipelines (Groovy), GitHub Actions, ArgoCD or equivalent systems. Scripting: Comfortable with scripting for automation (Bash, Python preferred); familiarity with Node.js/GoLang is a plus. Observability: Experience tracking and reporting key metrics for workloads, networking etc. Tooling: Proficient in Terraform and other IaaC tools; Cloudflare, networking, or edge security Collaboration: Robust communication skills; ability to work closely with developers, QA, and platform teams to streamline workflows. .
