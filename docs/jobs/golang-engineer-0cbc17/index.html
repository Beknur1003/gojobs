Job Summary We are looking for an experienced Golang Engineer to develop, maintain and troubleshoot secure Go-based microservices within a modern cloud-native environment. The successful candidate will have strong experience with Go, REST APIs, microservices, containerisation, Kubernetes and Helm, along with a solid understanding of secure coding, Agile development and continuous delivery practices. Key Responsibilities • Develop, maintain and troubleshoot secure Go microservices. • Design and integrate REST APIs within microservices-based architectures. • Work with containerisation technologies and cloud-native platforms. • Deploy and manage applications using Kubernetes and Helm. • Follow secure coding and software development best practices. • Contribute to code management, Agile development and continuous delivery processes. • Troubleshoot application and deployment issues. • Work collaboratively with engineering teams while also managing tasks independently. • Analyse technical issues and develop effective solutions. • Support continuous improvement of application development and deployment practices. Required Experience & Skills • Strong hands-on experience with Golang / Go. • Experience maintaining and troubleshooting secure Go microservices. • Strong understanding of REST APIs. • Experience with microservices architecture. • Experience with containerisation technologies. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes. • Hands-on experience deploying applications using Helm. • Strong understanding of secure coding practices. • Experience with code management and Agile development practices. • Experience with continuous delivery / CI/CD. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team. Desirable Experience • Experience with Java development. • Familiarity with SQL databases. • Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Dynatrace and Splunk.
