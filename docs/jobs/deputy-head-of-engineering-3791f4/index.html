We are looking for a Deputy Head of Technical (Growth Engineering & AI Platform) to architect and lead our clients next-generation AI-native growth platform. This is a 0-to-1 leadership role that sits at the intersection of AI Agent Development, Growth Engineering, and Data Platform. You will own the end-to-end technical strategy for global user acquisition, data intelligence, and AI transformation - directly supporting C-level decision making and enabling automated, intelligent growth operations. Key Responsibilities 1. AI & Agent Development • Architect and develop scenario-based AI Agents for business use cases such as data enquiry, marketing performance analysis, management intelligence, and creative operations. • Design reusable Agent framework including orchestration workflow, tool/function calling, MCP-based integration, prompt management, context engineering, and Agent evaluation pipelines. • Build enterprise AI applications leveraging LLM & Multimodal capabilities (e.g., RAG-based analytics, executive insights, automated creative production). 2. Paid Acquisition & Growth Engineering • Own the end-to-end global growth platform (Golang + Big Data) supporting multi-region campaign management, attribution, user growth analytics, and scalable acquisition operations. • Lead AdTech initiatives: Attribution, Marketing API integration, Advertising Delivery, RTB, and growth optimization. • Build ML models for User Segmentation, LTV / ROI Prediction, and Creative Performance Evaluation to drive budget allocation and targeting strategy. 3. Data Platform & Data Governance (Batch + Streaming) • Build and own the company-level data platform with Lakehouse architecture, real-time and batch ETL pipelines, unified data services, and analytics infrastructure. • Establish best practices for data warehouse architecture, data modeling, metrics architecture, and data asset management. • Ensure data quality, governance, and availability to support BI and AI applications. 4. Advertising & Recommendation Systems • Develop and optimize advertising delivery and recommendation systems including RTB, growth optimization loops, and creative tagging/classification using multimodal LLM. 5. Technical Leadership • Build the technical team from scratch - hiring, mentoring, and establishing SOPs, engineering standards, and delivery processes. • Drive cross-functional coordination across Product, Marketing, UA, Data, and Operations (China + Overseas markets). • Own cloud infrastructure and DevOps strategy on GCP. 6. Business-Oriented Partnership • Directly partner with CEO, COO, and HODs to translate business goals into technical solutions with data-driven decision making. Requirements • 12+ years in Data Platform, Growth Engineering, or AdTech, with at least 3+ years in a Tech Lead / Head of Engineering role building teams from 0-to-1. • Proven experience architecting large-scale growth platforms and data platforms (batch + streaming). • Hands-on expertise in LLM Agent development: RAG, Vector DB, Function Calling, Agent Orchestration. • Strong ML background: XGBoost, clustering, behavioral modeling, deep learning for LTV/ROI prediction, multimodal understanding. • Strong engineering background: Golang, Java (Spring Boot), Python, Spark, Flink, Kafka, BigQuery / MaxCompute / Paimon. • Experience with Google Cloud Platform: Vertex AI, Gemini, Agent Development Kit (ADK), GKE, BigQuery. Tech Stack You'll Lead LLM & AI: Google Gemini, Vertex AI, Prompt Engineering, Embeddings, Function Calling Agent: ADK, LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, Tool Calling, Context Management RAG: Vertex AI Vector Search, Semantic Search ML: PyTorch, XGBoost, LightGBM, Scikit-learn Data & Infra: Golang, Java, Python, Spark, Flink, Kafka, BigQuery, Lakehouse, GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD
