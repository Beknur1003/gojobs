#RunwiththePack At Arctic Wolf, we’re on a mission to end cyber risk. We are a global leader in security operations, helping organizations stay resilient against evolving cyber threats through industry-leading security operations, threat intelligence, and a people-first approach to cybersecurity. More than 3,000 Pack members across the world work together every day to protect our customers and empower security teams with new levels of speed, accuracy, and intelligence at scale. What sets us apart is our relentless focus on our customers, innovation at scale, and a collaborative culture where talented people can do meaningful work that truly makes a difference. We’re constantly looking for talented people to join the Pack and help us take our performance to the next level. About the Position: We are seeking a highly talented Senior Core Platform Services Developer to help build and evolve foundational platform capabilities that enable Arctic Wolf's engineering teams to move faster, reduce duplication, and deliver consistent, scalable solutions across the organization. You will be a key member of the Core Platform Services team, responsible for the Access Control Platform, a critical service that helps Arctic Wolf deliver world-class security operations at scale for customers around the globe. Responsibilities • Architect, design, and develop well-structured, testable, efficient, and secure software solutions. • Solve complex technical problems that require analysis, sound judgment, and the ability to adapt existing approaches to new challenges. • Act as a force multiplier for the team through code reviews, design discussions, technical guidance, and collaboration with other developers. • Participate throughout the full software development lifecycle, including design, implementation, testing, continuous integration, and deployment. • Mentor and support junior and intermediate developers, helping elevate engineering excellence across the team. • Evaluate and resolve a broad range of technical issues, proposing creative and effective solutions. • Leverage AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity, design quality, implementation, testing, and code review processes. Team & Culture We foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, and continuous learning. Teams regularly showcase their work through monthly Platform R&D Demos, providing visibility into projects and opportunities to learn from one another. In addition, our annual department-wide Hackathon brings engineers together from across the organization for four days of collaboration and experimentation. While the focus is innovation and creativity, many Hackathon projects have gone on to influence or become part of our products. About You You are an experienced software developer who can independently deliver high-quality solutions with minimal guidance. You enjoy working on impactful projects, have a strong foundation in software engineering principles, and are comfortable tackling challenging technical problems. Beyond your individual contributions, you enjoy collaborating with others, sharing knowledge, and mentoring fellow engineers. Qualifications • Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • Professional programming experience with Golang, Java, C++, or another advanced object-oriented programming language. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills. • Experience building and supporting scalable, highly available backend services. • 5+ years of professional software development experience. • Experience working with AWS technologies and cloud-native architectures. • Experience developing and operating applications in Kubernetes environments.
