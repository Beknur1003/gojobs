About the Team: The CrowdStrike Detection Engine (CDE) team is building a powerful, next-generation platform designed to be a force multiplier for security teams. Our platform empowers customers who leverage multiple Falcon products to create and manage advanced, unified detections and content. This core platform capability is a strategic imperative for CrowdStrike, aiming to provide a competitive advantage that is difficult to replicate, ensuring our customers have unparalleled detection and response capabilities across the entire Falcon ecosystem. About The Role: We are seeking a highly experienced and motivated Sr. Backend Engineer II to join the CDE Platform team. In this role, you will be instrumental in designing, building, and scaling the foundational services that orchestrate data, content, and execution logic across CrowdStrike’s products (e.g., NGSIEM, FCS, Endpoint). You will be a key driver in creating a highly performant, scalable, and resilient platform that processes internet-scale data streams and enables real-time detection generation. This is a hybrid role based out of our office in NYC. Candidate should be comfortable visiting the office twice a week once the development center is established. What You’ll Do: • Lead the design and implementation of core platform services that ingest, process, and route security events from different sources to various execution contexts. • Drive the architecture for the Detection Content Management Platform (DCMP/CMS), creating advanced CI/CD and DevOps tooling to enable content authors to treat detections as code. • Develop highly scalable and fault-tolerant microservices, leveraging languages like Go and technologies such as Kubernetes, Flink, and distributed databases (e.g., RocksDB). • Contribute to the evolution of the Unified Detections Management (UDM) system, ensuring a consistent and comprehensive detection experience for all customers. • Serve as a technical leader, mentoring mid-level and senior engineers, and driving cross-functional alignment on complex technical challenges. • Architect and implement features related to cross-cutting concerns like Policy, Flight Control, and Observability, ensuring platform stability and operational excellence. • Collaborate closely with Product Management and other engineering teams (Engine, UDM, Content) to translate strategic vision into well-defined, executable technical roadmaps. What You’ll Use: • Go (Golang) • Java8 • Python • git • Cassandra • Kafka • OpenSearch • Postgres • Redis • AWS Cloud ​ ​What You’ll Need: • 8+ years of professional software development experience, with a focus on large-scale distributed systems and internet-scale challenges. • Deep expertise in one or more modern programming languages (Go preferred). • Proven track record of architecting and delivering critical, highly available, and scalable platform services in a cloud environment (GCP, AWS, or Azure). • Extensive experience with data streaming technologies (e.g., Kafka, Flink) and distributed computing concepts. • Strong understanding of cybersecurity or SIEM principles, data models, and content/rule authoring lifecycles is a significant plus. • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical trade-offs to both technical and non-technical audiences. • Experience in a Staff-level role, driving multi-quarter initiatives, and influencing technical direction across multiple teams. • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
