Software Engineer (Full-Stack Development & IT Support) Organization: Sathya Educare Competency Trust (SECT) Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka (On-site) Employment Type: Full-time Experience: 1+ year or strong relevant internship experience Department: Technology / Digital Learning ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a hands-on Software Engineer to take ownership of our digital platforms and technology infrastructure. The role involves full-stack product development using Go (Golang), React.js, MySQL, and AWS, along with AI/LLM integrations and office IT support. We need someone who enjoys solving real-world problems, building reliable technology, and taking responsibility from development to deployment and maintenance. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES 1. Full-Stack Development • Build and maintain backend services and REST APIs using Go (Golang). • Develop responsive web applications using React.js. • Design and manage MySQL databases and optimize queries. • Integrate third-party services and APIs. 2. Cloud, Performance & AI • Deploy and maintain applications using AWS. • Implement caching, performance optimization, and security practices. • Explore and integrate AI/LLM capabilities, including RAG and AI APIs. • Work with Git, Docker, and CI/CD processes. 3. Office IT Support & Maintenance • Maintain office computers, laptops, software, and operating systems. • Troubleshoot hardware, software, printer, Wi-Fi, and network issues. • Manage IT assets, system security, backups, and software updates. • Coordinate with vendors for IT repairs and maintenance. 4. Ownership & Collaboration • Take ownership of assigned projects from development to deployment. • Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code. • Identify and resolve technical issues independently. • Collaborate with team members and translate requirements into technical solutions. REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS & SKILLS • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering, or a related field. Strong practical projects may be considered in place of a degree. • Minimum 1 year of software development experience or relevant internship experience. • Working knowledge of Go (Golang), React.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, and MySQL. • Understanding of REST APIs, database design, and backend architecture. • Familiarity with AWS, Git, and Docker. • Strong Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) fundamentals. • Basic computer hardware, operating system, and networking knowledge. • Interest in Generative AI and LLM technologies. • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Good to Have: Redis, microservices, CI/CD, AWS services, Docker Compose, RAG, embeddings, vector databases, and AI agents. WHO SHOULD APPLY? We are looking for someone who: • Sees technology as a means to create meaningful impact, not just a career opportunity. • Wants to build something meaningful and grow with the organization. • Takes ownership of outcomes rather than waiting for instructions. • Enjoys solving problems, experimenting, and learning new technologies. • Is willing to work across software development, deployment, and IT infrastructure. • Can work independently while collaborating effectively with a team. • Is curious about AI and passionate about building practical technology solutions. • Values commitment, integrity, accountability, and continuous improvement. WHO SHOULD NOT APPLY This role is NOT for someone who: • Is looking only for a salary, designation, or routine 9-to-5 job. • Wants to work exclusively on coding and is unwilling to handle basic IT support responsibilities. • Prefers completing assigned tasks without taking ownership or initiative. • Is unwilling to troubleshoot, learn, experiment, or solve unfamiliar problems. • Is interested only in using existing technologies but not understanding how systems work. • Is uncomfortable receiving feedback, adapting, or working in a growing organization. • Lacks genuine interest in building and maintaining technology solutions. OUR EXPECTATION We do not expect you to know everything from day one. We expect strong technical fundamentals, curiosity, accountability, and the willingness to take ownership. At SECT, we are not simply hiring someone to write code. We are looking for someone who wants to take responsibility for our technology, build solutions that matter, and make this work a meaningful professional missionnot just another job.
