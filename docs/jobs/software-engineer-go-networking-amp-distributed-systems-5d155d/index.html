Your role As a part of the project team, you will be responsible for: • Designing, building, deploying, and optimizing large-scale global traffic routing platforms • Developing high-performance distributed traffic infrastructure • Building and managing large-scale distributed systems and networking software • Executing complex migrations of legacy network systems and codebases in large-scale environments • Developing core network routing tools Do we have a match? As a Software Engineer, you must meet the following criteria: • At least 5 years for Mid / 7 years for Senior of relevant work experience • Proficiency in Golang with extensive experience in designing and building distributed systems • At least 5 years of experience in building and managing large-scale geo-distributed networking software and solutions with relevant software design and development experience • Deep understanding of environments built around Envoy. Solid experience with Envoy Proxy (Data Plane) architecture combined with Go Control Plane • Advanced knowledge of network protocols from L3 to L7, network infrastructure, and observability mechanisms • Strong understanding of the Linux network stack, practical experience with eBPF, and hands-on skills in low-level socket/kernel performance tuning • Practical knowledge of Rust specifically for developing and maintaining Envoy WASM filters • Fundamental understanding of C and/or C++ to support low-level system integrations and debugging More reasons to join us • Flexible working hours and approach to work: fully remote, in the office or hybrid • Professional growth supported by internal training sessions and a training budget • Solid onboarding with a hands-on approach to give you an easy start • A great atmosphere among professionals who are passionate about their work • The ability to change the project you work on
