About the Role: We are seeking a talented and motivated Senior Software Engineer with accounting and financial experience. This role offers an exciting opportunity to thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment within a rapidly growing team, with abundant prospects for leadership and innovation. This position is ideal for professionals who began their careers in accounting or finance and later transitioned into software engineering — individuals who now want to leverage that domain expertise to architect and scale technical solutions that solve complex financial challenges in fintech. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design, develop, and optimize high-impact systems that power Sezzle’s financial reporting, reconciliation, and automation workflows. You’ll partner closely with cross-functional teams — including Accounting and Finance — to ensure data integrity, streamline financial processes, and build robust, auditable systems that meet enterprise-grade standards. Your role will extend beyond development. You’ll mentor engineers, shape technical direction, and bring a strong sense of ownership to every project you lead. You should be able to build high-quality, innovative, and fully performing software in compliance with coding standards, technical design, and regulatory best practices. Compensation: For this senior development role, with 8+ years of experience, the compensation range is $5,000 - $9,500 USD per month. This range acknowledges the extensive expertise, leadership capabilities, and significant contributions expected at this level, offering a competitive salary to reflect the value of advanced skills and experience.
