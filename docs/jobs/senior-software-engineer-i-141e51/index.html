What We Are Looking For - Scope of Role We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer I to join our engineering organization. This role is responsible for designing and delivering complex software systems while providing technical leadership within a product, platform, or engineering team. The successful candidate will drive engineering excellence, contribute to architectural decisions, mentor other engineers, and lead technical initiatives. The individual will remain hands-on while influencing software quality, scalability, reliability, and operational effectiveness. What Will You Do - Responsibilities for Employment • Lead the design and implementation of complex software components, services, and platforms. • Develop scalable, maintainable, and secure distributed systems. • Drive technical discussions and architecture decisions within the team. • Translate business requirements into technical solutions. • Mentor junior engineers and support technical growth across the team. • Establish engineering best practices covering software quality, testing, observability, and deployment. • Design and implement cloud-native solutions running on Kubernetes and public cloud environments. • Optimize application performance, reliability, and operational efficiency. • Participate in incident analysis, root cause investigations, and continuous improvement initiatives. • Collaborate effectively with product, architecture, and engineering leadership. • Additional responsibilities as assigned. Who You Are - Qualifications Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. Years and Type of Experience: 6-10 years of progressive software engineering experience. Demonstrated success delivering complex software systems and leading technical initiatives. Key Skills, Knowledge & Capabilities: • Strong expertise in Golang or equivalent modern backend technologies. • Experience building scalable microservices and distributed systems. • Experience with Flutter or modern client application frameworks. • Strong understanding of software architecture and design patterns. • Experience deploying and operating applications on Kubernetes. • Promote effective use of AI-assisted engineering practices to improve team productivity, code quality, and delivery efficiency. • Experience developing solutions on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud Platform, or Alibaba Cloud. • Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, and automated deployment practices. • Familiarity with messaging platforms and event-driven architectures. • Strong understanding of reliability, security, scalability, and performance engineering. • Proven mentoring and technical leadership capabilities. • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. How We Lead the DS Way - Key Leadership Behaviors • Actively articulates and promotes Dentsply Sirona’s vision, mission and values. • Advocates on behalf of the customer. • Promotes high performance, innovation and continual improvement. • Consistently meets Company standards, ethics and compliance requirements. • Clear and effective communication with stake holders, which span across multiple levels, socio-geographic areas and functional expertise.
