Cloud Development Engineer Description - As a Software Engineer, Cloud Services, you will create scalable, reliable backend systems that support HP Workforce Experience mission to transform the way people work. We focus on designs and tools rooted in strong engineering principles like abstraction, composition, virtualization, automation, and iterative development cycles. This role is responsible for designing, implementation, and maintenance of complex software systems that meet specific business needs or technical requirements. The role works at the intersection of software development and system architecture to ensure the successful development and deployment of software solutions that address a wide range of challenges and requirements. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain backend services and RESTful APIs using Java and Spring Boot. • Collaborate with front-end developers, product managers, and other engineers to deliver end-to-end features. • Integrate with databases and external services, ensuring performance, security, and reliability. • Participate in code reviews, debugging, and performance optimization efforts. • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and support application deployment in cloud and local environments. • Installs, configures, tests and maintains operating systems, application software, and system management tools, with a high degree of expertise and independence. • Monitors and tests application performance for potential bottlenecks, identifies possible solutions, and works with developers to implement those fixes. • Designs enhancements, updates, and programming changes for portions and subsystems of systems software, including operating systems, compliers, networking, utilities, databases, and Internet-related tools. • Analyzes design and determines coding, programming, and integration activities required based on general objectives and knowledge of overall architecture of product or solution. • Writes and executes complete testing plans, protocols, and documentation for assigned portion of application; identifies and debugs and creates solutions for issues with code and integration into application architecture. • Creates and maintains detailed technical documentation, including system architecture diagrams, design specifications, and user manuals. Education & Experience Recommended • Minimum of 4 years of software development experience. • Four-year or Graduate Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or any other related discipline or commensurate work experience or demonstrated competence. • Strong proficiency in Java, JavaScript, GoLang and Spring Boot. • Experience building RESTful APIs and microservices. • Familiarity with databases such as Mongo, MySQL or PostgreSQL. • Understanding of version control systems, preferably Git. • Knowledge of unit testing frameworks and debugging practices. • Experience with event-driven architectures, RabbitMQ/AMQP a plus • Experience or at least familiarity with variety of AWS services, like Lambda, SNS, SQS, IAM, etc. • Infra-as-code (terraform/terragrunt) • Familiarity with Docker and containerized deployments. • Understanding of distributed systems and scalable architecture. • Full Stack Development a plus • Datadog dashboard observability Cross-Org Skills • Effective Communication • Results Orientation • Learning Agility • Digital Fluency • Customer Centricity • Cross functional team collaboration   Disclaimer This job description describes the general nature and level of work performed in this role.
