Software Developer The Opportunity: Are you looking for an opportunity to not just develop software, but to help build a system that will have a positive impact? As a full-stack developer, you can solve a problem with a complete end-to-end solution in a fast-paced, agile environment. We’re looking for a developer with the skills needed to build mission critical software from vision to production ready system. We have an opportunity for you to develop your Digital skills and establish your new career. You’ll join a rigorous training program that combines skills assessments, a comprehensive curriculum, functional mentorship, a capstone digital challenge, and support to place you on your first digital project. Equipped with the foundational digital skills to accelerate your career, you’ll join a team and apply those skills to support our clients’ critical missions. Work with us as we build systems to change Modern Software Development for the better. Work with us as we shape systems for the better. Join us. The world can’t wait. You Have: • 5+ years of experience in software development • Experience with Java, Kotlin, Go, Golang, Rust, or TypeScript • Experience with designing and implementing modular software architectures • Knowledge of open application programming interface and providing access to data • Knowledge of database schema design and structured query language • Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and containerization technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes • Knowledge of Agile fundamentals • Secret clearance • Bachelor’s degree Nice If You Have: • Experience with open-source map libraries, including OpenLayers Map OL, or Cesium • Knowledge of open-source communication protocols, including MQTT, gRPC, or WebSockets • TS/SCI clearance Clearance: Applicants selected will be subject to a security investigation and may need to meet eligibility requirements for access to classified information; Secret clearance is required.
