Hybrid Working Pattern - 3 days (Mon, Tue, Thurs) per week from Tower Bridge office Who We Are Zoopla is one of the UK’s most instantly recognisable property brands. In fact, we’re known and loved by over 91% of the nation (and we’re working hard on the other 9%). Our mission is to help the nation make better home decisions - by connecting everyone to their home and giving them personalised insights to help with moving, managing or financing. Over 50 million people visit Zoopla every month to access exclusive data and information on every UK property, search over 500,000 homes for sale and rent, find the best agents and secure the latest mortgage deals. We’re a growing, dynamic team that embraces innovation and isn’t afraid to push the boundaries. We’re only just starting our journey to redefine the digital property landscape, with much more to explore and achieve. Join us, and transform the way the nation makes home decisions. The Role As a Full-Stack Engineer at Zoopla, you will work to build and enhance features for our property marketplace that connects consumers and customers. If you enjoy being in a commercially driven organisation where the systems you touch may be used by millions of people in the UK we have something for you. While we have a diverse set of technologies and services running here at Zoopla, we don't expect you to know all of them and would love to hear from you if you have experience in just a few of them. What we value more is the ability to work in a cross functional team to problem solve and a willingness to sometimes jump into unfamiliar code to achieve your goals. On the frontend you’ll work with React, React Native Nx and TypeScript. On the backend it's Node.js, Next.js, and GraphQL, all written in TypeScript, but also Golang and Python. Our data stores include both SQL and NoSQL, and our systems run on AWS. AI tools have cut the time to build from days to hours in many cases. We want engineers who use that speed to test bold ideas, prototype new approaches, and ship the ones that work. You will work across the full stack to do it. Requirements What we’re looking for in Zoopla Engineers: • You’re skilled in TypeScript and have built both sides of a product: UIs in React or React Native, and backend services and APIs in Node.js (REST or GraphQL) backed by SQL or NoSQL databases. Nice to have: Python, Golang. • You’ve designed and shipped systems that real users depend on. You choose the right architecture, weigh trade-offs (scale, cost, complexity, speed to ship), and write down your decisions. You can lead a design discussion and take feedback. • You practise your engineering craft: maintainable code, code review, pair programming, and test coverage. • You build products that are stable, scalable, secure, observable, and performant. • You’ve worked with cloud infrastructure and know your way around Terraform (or equivalent) and AWS. We want engineers with a DevOps mentality. • You’re commercially driven and you spot opportunities to improve the product. You care about delighting users. • You work closely with colleagues across the business and understand how your work ties to business goals. • You’re accountable for performance, playing your part in achieving team goals and ensuring your contribution positively impacts overall success. • You are proactive and can work autonomously in a fast-paced agile team, alongside data, product and design. You take ownership of outcomes and respond to change without missing a beat. • You deeply make use of AI. You keep up with new tools and techniques, and you actively use them in your work. We take diversity and inclusion seriously, and to succeed here, you should too! All qualified applicants will be considered, without regard to race, colour, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, age, disability, health, qualifications, or time unemployed. Where you’ll be At Zoopla, we embrace hybrid working but emphasise the importance of also spending time together. You’ll have the opportunity to work remotely (at home) for two days per week with Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays as all-Zoopla days in the office together. Our home in Tower Bridge is a buzzing hub for collaborative and individual working, with areas to socialise and exercise - within easy reach of London Bridge, Tower Hill and Bermondsey stations. On our doorstep there are plenty of great food spots including a new indoor street food market and landscaped areas to sit by the riverside. Benefits • Our hybrid setup means you’ll join us in the Tower Bridge office 3 days each week • 25 days annual leave + additional leave benefits Including extra days for length of service, plus other leave perks • Cycle to work and electric car schemes • Free Calm App membership • Enhanced Parental leave • Fertility Treatment Financial Support • Group Income Protection and private medical insurance • Gym on-site in London • 7.5% pension contribution by the company • Discretionary annual bonus up to 10% of base salary
