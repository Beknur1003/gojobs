Company: P.I.V.O.T Security Location: On-site, (New Delhi, Dehradun or Mumbai office) Experience: 1 to 3 years in offensive security development About Company: P.I.V.O.T Security is a fast-growing leader at the forefront of offensive security. We protect organisations against highly sophisticated cyber threats through our services and offensive security platforms, finding and fixing the gaps attackers would exploit before they do. Our products cover browser security and data loss prevention, phishing simulation and awareness training, mailbox hardening, breach and attack simulation, application and API security testing, dark web threat intelligence, and an offensive cyber range for hands-on training. Research drives what we build. Our team studies live attacker tradecraft, publishes original research, has disclosed vulnerabilities to major vendors, and presented at international security conferences like Microsoft's BlueHat Asia. We also contribute to the AI and LLM security community through research on agentic model and MCP bypass techniques, and we are a research partner to academic institutions including IIT Bombay and UPES. That work becomes product capability, and our red team, penetration testing, DFIR and compliance services feed the same techniques back into the platforms. We are a DPIIT-recognised startup and serve banks, government bodies, manufacturers and technology companies in India, with GCC and international reach through partner brands. About the Role: We are looking for an Offensive Security Engineer who writes production-quality code and wants to apply it to offensive security. You will join our product engineering team and work hands-on across our full product portfolio: STEP (our breach and attack simulation platform), WARUS (our agentic application security platform), Enforce (our browser security platform), Lock-E for enterprise email hardening, Phish-E and PhishIQ for phishing simulation and awareness, BreachedB4 for dark web threat intelligence and the PIVOT Labs cyber range. You will build and extend cyber kill chain and adversary emulation capabilities alongside our red team and DFIR practitioners, and you are expected to move between products as the roadmap and client needs demand rather than owning a single platform. This is a developer role first. You will design, build, test and ship software that our security teams and clients use, own delivery for the components you build, and see how your work performs against real environments. Key Responsibilities: • Cross-product development: build and extend features across our full product portfolio - STEP (breach and attack simulation), WARUS (SAST and DAST), Enforce (browser security), Lock-E (email hardening), Phish-E and PhishIQ (phishing simulation and awareness), BreachedB4 (threat intelligence) and PIVOT Labs (training range), moving between products as priorities shift • Product and kill chain development: build and extend product features and cyber kill chain and adversary emulation capabilities across STEP and allied platforms • BAS platform development: build and improve attack simulation modules, agents, orchestration logic, result collection and reporting, mapping simulated techniques to frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK • Application, browser and email security: develop and improve detection, simulation and hardening capabilities across WARUS, Enforce, Lock-E, Phish-E and PhishIQ, covering web applications, APIs, the browser layer and enterprise email • Threat intelligence and training: contribute to BreachedB4 dark web threat intelligence and to the PIVOT Labs offensive security training range • Build product expertise: develop deep, hands-on expertise across the portfolio over time, going deep in the products you own while staying able to build in the others • New initiatives: help P.I.V.O.T shape and deliver new offensive security initiatives as the product roadmap and client needs evolve • Client engagement: take part in client calls for scoping, delivery and technical discussion, and travel to client sites for delivery when required • Engineering practice: write clean, modular, tested and documented code, follow version control discipline, take part in code reviews and act on reviewer feedback • Secure development: apply secure coding practices to everything you build - security tooling must itself be secure • Other security activities: support red team, adversary simulation and DFIR activities and other cyber-related work as assigned • Delivery management: own delivery of your components end to end, from planning through release, keeping timelines and quality on track for internal teams and clients Requirements: • 1 to 3 years of hands-on experience in offensive security development • OSCP or above (for example OSEP, OSCE, CRTP, CRTO, CREST or recognised Red Team certifications) - we hire on demonstrable, hands-on ability • Programming: proficiency in C, C++, Node.js and Golang, and the ability to pick up other relevant languages as the work needs • Low-level development: comfort working with low-level languages and systems-level code • Offensive security knowledge: solid understanding of the MITRE ATT&CK framework, the cyber kill chain and command and control (C2) concepts • Engineering practice: modular design, testing, debugging, Git workflows and code review • Ability to read unfamiliar code and documentation and become productive quickly • Good to have: contributions to BAS, red teaming or adversary emulation tooling, responsible vulnerability disclosures, open source contributions or CTF participation, and exposure to client-facing security work
