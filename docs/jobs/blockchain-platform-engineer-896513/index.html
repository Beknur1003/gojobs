Our official partners include the National Football Team of Ukraine, FC Barcelona, Lifecell, FACEIT and VISA. The future of Web3 starts with you — join us as a Blockchain Platform Engineer! Requirements: — Experience: 4+ years of professional experience in software engineering, where the last 2+ years was hands-on experience in the blockchain domain. — Strong production Golang: you write and ship services in Go, and you are comfortable reading, debugging and patching upstream Go codebases. — Blockchain Expertise: Extensive experience with Ethereum, including a deep understanding of its protocol, smart contract development, and associated technologies. — Hands-on experience in developing, deploying, and maintaining smart contracts on EVM chains (Solidity). — Understanding of L2s and optimistic rollup fundamentals: block derivation from L1, batch submission, state proposals, the withdrawal flow, and forced inclusion. — Understanding of OP Stack components and architecture. — Kubernetes and CI/CD: familiarity with Kubernetes for container orchestration and CI/CD tools for deployment automation. — Monitoring Tools: Familiarity with monitoring tools such as Prometheus and Grafana for performance tracking. — Cloud Platforms: Familiarity with cloud platforms (preferably AWS) and experience with containerization (Docker) is a strong plus. Would be a plus: — Rust at the level of reading and patching code. — Understanding of fault proofs: dispute games, bisection, FPVM, as well as ZK-based approaches. — Experience in conducting and successfully passing smart contract security audits. — Experience in developing blockchain nodes, bridges, indexers, etc. Responsibilities: — Deploy, upgrade, and operate OP Stack components. — Set up monitoring and alerting: Prometheus, Grafana, PromQL dashboards, instrumentation of domain metrics (economic, bridge, sequencing). — Take part in the on-call rotation, drive incident response, and write post-mortems and runbooks. — Work with the smart contracts, security and product teams on launches, network upgrades and hard fork activations. — Foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and professional growth. — Work closely with cross-functional teams to define project requirements and ensure alignment with business objectives. — Stay updated on advancements in blockchain technology and Ethereum, and apply new features and best practices to enhance our solutions. Work conditions: Immerse yourself in Crypto & Web3: — Shape the Future — Work on high-impact blockchain products at the forefront of Web3. — Innovate & Create — Your ideas drive real-world solutions and technological breakthroughs. — Global Reach — Build scalable solutions for businesses and users worldwide. — Web3-Powered Culture — A team of visionaries who believe in decentralization and innovation. Work-Life Balance & Well-being: — Modern equipment. — Comfortable working conditions, and an inspiring environment to help you thrive. — 24 calendar days of paid leave. — Additional days off for national holidays.
