Summer 2027 Internship: Data Center Infrastructure Management (DCIM)

LE043 Quality Technology Services, LLC Suwanee, GA опубликовано 26 августа 2026

Стажёр
PythonJavaC++
QTS' summer internship program will provide you first hand experience into the fast paced world of the data center industry by providing weekly lunch and learns, a comprehensive orientation program, and site based activities, socials and volunteering events. Every intern will be assigned initiatives tied to real QTS objectives helping with anything pertaining to the whole life-cycle of a data center build and/or company operations. The DCIM Software Engineering intern has two potential scopes of responsibility. On the PLC software design side, the individual will gain valuable exposure to collaborating with a team working on automation solution based tools to be leveraged across the organization. For those more interested in the Project Management side, you'd act as a Project Analyst, reporting to a PM, tracking deliverables, organizing data, and performing research around topics as necessary. To be considered, applicants MUST HAVE prior PLC exposure/experience in a classroom or internship setting. Technology Intern: • Build relationships with key stakeholders across the organization • Facilitate changes and IT based tools and access for staff at all levels • Leverage project management tools to properly track ongoing deliverables across projects • Maintain updated budgets, schedules, and status reports for assigned projects. • Use programming languages like: Python, JAVA, C++, Golang • Leverage controls engineering methodologies and tools BASIC QUALIFICATIONS • Actively pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering, Data Science, or a related field. • Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite, especially PowerPoint for presentations and Excel for data analysis and budgeting. • Strong verbal and written communication skills. • Critical thinking and problem-solving abilities. • Must be a US Citizen PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: • MUST HAVE prior PLC exposure/experience in a classroom or internship setting. • Previous internship or co-op experience in construction, finance, technology, HR, facilities engineering, or related industries. • Experience with data analysis tools such as Tableau or Power BI. • Experience with programming languages like Python, Java, etc. • Involvement in community activities outside of schoolwork (athletics, clubs, volunteering, part-time employment). • Current full-time enrollment in one of QTS’s partner institutions (listed below) or a Blackstone Launchpad affiliate school: • Auburn University • Kansas State University • Georgia State University • Southern Adventist University • Texas A&M University • University of Kansas We conform to all the laws, statutes, and regulations concerning equal employment opportunities and affirmative action.
