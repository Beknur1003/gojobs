About NCR VOYIX NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide. NCR Voyix is seeking a mid-level Software Engineer to join our team in Atlanta. In this role, you will contribute to the development of scalable, modern platforms that power critical business services and support our continued evolution in cloud-based technologies. Our team focuses on building reliable and efficient systems that enable seamless data exchange across distributed environments. You’ll work on solutions designed for high performance, resilience, and scalability, helping ensure consistent and dependable system operations. As part of this team, you’ll contribute throughout the software development lifecycle while collaborating with cross-functional partners to deliver high-quality solutions. This role is ideal for engineers who enjoy solving complex technical challenges, working across different layers of the stack, and building systems that are robust, scalable, and impactful.
