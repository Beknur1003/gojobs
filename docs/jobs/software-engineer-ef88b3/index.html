Meet the Team The Cisco Intersight team is at the forefront of Cisco’s AI Infrastructure strategy, serving as the intelligent management platform that orchestrates and simplifies complex hybrid cloud environments. We provide the control plane that enables organizations to deploy, monitor, and optimize AI-driven workloads across distributed infrastructure, playing a critical role in the broader Cisco AI story. Our team is a high-performing, collaborative group of engineers who thrive on solving complex distributed systems challenges in a fast-paced environment. We pride ourselves on a culture of technical excellence and innovation, where every team member is empowered to influence the future of cloud management. What makes us truly exciting is our mission to build the "brain" of the modern data center, enabling seamless AI operations at scale. Your Impact Architect scalable microservices using Golang to streamline the deployment and lifecycle management of AI infrastructure. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate Kubernetes and containerization technologies that improve the reliability of hybrid cloud operations. Drive the development of virtualization interfaces, such as VMware vCenter or RedHat OpenShift, to ensure seamless interoperability across diverse hardware environments. Design robust, agentic frameworks that automate complex infrastructure tasks to improve operational efficiency and reduce manual overhead. Champion the delivery of high-quality, production-ready code to ensure our platform remains the industry standard for AI-ready infrastructure management. Minimum Qualifications • Proficiency in Golang for developing scalable, high-performance backend services. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes, Podman, or Docker within complex containerized environments. • Deep understanding of cloud fundamentals and experience managing Hyper-Converged Infrastructure (HCI) solutions. • Proven track record working with virtualization platforms, specifically VMware vCenter or RedHat OpenShift. • Demonstrated ability to design and implement robust software solutions within distributed systems. Preferred Qualifications • Experience building or integrating AI and Agentic frameworks to automate infrastructure workflows. • Strong grasp of software design principles and architectural patterns for cloud-native applications. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts to diverse stakeholders. • Strong behavioral skills, including the ability to mentor peers and collaborate effectively in a team-oriented environment. • Passion for continuous learning and the ability to adapt to evolving AI infrastructure technologies.
