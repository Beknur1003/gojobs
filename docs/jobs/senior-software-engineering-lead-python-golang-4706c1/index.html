Senior Software Engineering Lead - Python/Golang

Wenger & Watson Inc. Pune опубликовано 26 августа 2026

SeniorLead
KafkaMicroservicesCI/CDPython
Role Overview: The role requires a strong technical leader who can drive architecture and engineering decisions while remaining hands-on with critical components of the platform. You will work closely with product, engineering, data, and infrastructure teams to design and deliver high-performance systems capable of processing large volumes of data. Key Responsibilities: - Lead the architecture, design, development, and delivery of scalable backend and data-intensive applications. - Design and build robust data ingestion frameworks capable of processing high-volume, high-velocity, and heterogeneous data from multiple sources. - Develop reliable and scalable data pipelines for batch and real-time data processing. - Build backend services and data-processing components using Python and Golang. - Design APIs, microservices, and distributed services that support data ingestion and processing workflows. - Design mechanisms for data validation, transformation, enrichment, deduplication, and error handling within ingestion pipelines. - Work on real-time and near-real-time data processing using event-driven architectures and streaming technologies. - Design fault-tolerant and highly available data processing systems with appropriate retry, recovery, checkpointing, and monitoring mechanisms. - Optimize existing data pipelines and backend services for performance, scalability, reliability, and cost efficiency. - Establish engineering best practices around code quality, testing, documentation, deployment, observability, and production readiness. - Lead technical discussions, architecture reviews, design reviews, and code reviews. - Mentor senior engineers and technical teams while establishing strong engineering standards. - Collaborate with Product, Data Engineering, DevOps, SRE, and other technology teams to deliver end-to-end solutions. Technical Skills: - Strong hands-on programming experience in Python and/or Golang. - Extensive experience in backend software development and distributed systems. - Strong understanding of data ingestion architectures and large-scale data pipelines. - Experience designing batch and real-time data processing pipelines. - Strong experience with relational and NoSQL databases, SQL, and database performance optimization. - Hands-on experience with messaging and streaming technologies such as Kafka. - Strong understanding of containerization, CI/CD, monitoring, logging, and production deployment practices. System Design & Architecture: - Strong expertise in designing distributed, highly available, and scalable systems. - Ability to translate business requirements into scalable technical architectures. - Strong understanding of horizontal scaling, caching, partitioning, replication, load balancing, and fault tolerance. Leadership & Collaboration: - Lead a team of experienced software engineers and provide technical mentorship. - Drive engineering excellence and promote clean, maintainable, and scalable code. - Define technical standards and development practices across engineering teams. - Partner with engineering leadership to define technical roadmaps and modernization initiatives. Qualifications: - 15 to 20 years of overall software engineering experience. - Proven experience building large-scale backend systems and data platforms. - Strong background in data ingestion, data pipelines, distributed systems, and scalable architectures. - Experience leading technical teams and complex engineering initiatives. - Excellent communication and stakeholder-management abilities.
