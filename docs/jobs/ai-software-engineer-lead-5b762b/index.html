AI Software Engineer, Lead The Opportunity: Are you looking for an opportunity to combine your technical skills with big picture thinking to make an impact in national defense? You understand your customer’s environment and how to develop the right systems for the mission. Your ability to translate real-world needs into technical solutions makes you an integral part of delivering a customer-focused engineering solution. As an engineer on our team, you have the chance to shape the development of systems across multiple defense, civilian, and space domains by supporting advanced system design, test, and evaluation by combining physics-based modeling, AI/ML techniques, autonomy, cyber, and platform engineering expertise. Your customer will trust you to not only design and develop these solutions but also evolve them with advanced technology. On our team, you’ll be able to grow your skills by merging multiple technical engineering disciplines to create solutions that drive missions. Join us. The world can't wait. and digital twins • Experience writing clean, maintainable, and test-driven code for new applications or improving legacy front-end systems • Experience with Java, Kotlin, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, C/C++, or Golang • Experience with AI assisted development, machine learning operation pipelines, big data, data science, or unbounded data streams • Experience contributing to solutions within a collaborative, Agile, and cross-functional team environment • TS/SCI clearance • Bachelor’s degree in an Engineering, Science, or Technology field Nice If You Have: • Experience with
