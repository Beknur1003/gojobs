What you'll do at Jamf: At Jamf, we empower people to be their best selves and do their best work. Our engineering teams are at the heart of our operations, and the Developer Experience unit is committed to providing them with the best tools and environments to achieve excellence. You will join a team of five highly experienced engineers located in the Czech Republic and Poland, working with engineering around the globe. You will maintain and extend our Jenkins instances and self-hosted GitHub Actions runners, and support the GitHub organization itself, which covers repository administration, access management, and repo migrations. You will work on ECR and Artifactory configuration, policies, and image synchronization. A development environment is part of any good CI/CD. You will develop and maintain custom tooling written in Go to support the development environment, collaborating with engineering teams to identify needs and implement effective solutions. You'll be working in EKS (Elastic Kubernetes Service) clusters in AWS, ensuring the high availability and performance of our services. Configuration as a code is a must, so everything is managed and applied via Terraform and stored inGitHub. This role is offered as remote in Poland. We are only able to accept applications who have sponsorship to live and work in Poland. #LI-Remote What you can expect to do in this role: • Develop, deploy, and maintain a development environment by identifying and applying automation, tools, and techniques. • Ensure security best practices are identified and integrated into system designs and configuration. • Assist in evaluating, planning, configuration, and implementation of new software services. • Develop custom tools and automation through programming and scripting. • Support Software Development by maintaining and scaling our Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery environment as needed. • Member of an Agile Team providing code reviews, velocity estimation, planning input, and participating in other Agile Methodology workflows. • Work and communicate effectively with Product Owners, Development teams, and Cloud and Delivery teams. • Create and maintain technical documentation.
