What You Will Do: As a Software Engineer at Independent Software, you will develop, enhance, and maintain a Kubernetes-native application that orchestrates and deploys workloads within containerized environments. You will work with modern cloud technologies, microservices, and web-based interfaces to support mission-critical systems. You will collaborate with engineering and infrastructure teams to improve application functionality, troubleshoot complex technical issues, and ensure reliable software deployment and operations. Key Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain cloud-native applications using Go (Golang), TypeScript, and Python. • Develop and enhance web-based user interfaces using TypeScript, HTML, and CSS. • Build, deploy, and manage containerized applications and microservices using Kubernetes, Docker, and Helm. • Support Kubernetes orchestration, workload provisioning, and infrastructure integration using Rancher, RKE2, and related technologies. • Implement and maintain GitOps workflows using ArgoCD and other deployment automation tools. • Develop and integrate RESTful APIs, event-driven services, and messaging technologies. • Troubleshoot application, container, networking, and infrastructure issues across complex environments. • Collaborate with software, systems, and infrastructure teams to support integration, deployment, and operational requirements. • Maintain technical documentation, follow development standards, and contribute to continuous improvement. Required Skills and Qualifications: • Strong professional software development experience, including backend and cloud-native applications. • Proficiency in Go (Golang), including experience developing production services. • Experience developing web applications using TypeScript, HTML, and CSS. • Experience with Kubernetes, Helm, and GitOps workflows using ArgoCD. • Hands-on experience with Docker, RKE2, or similar container orchestration technologies. • Experience with infrastructure provisioning, hardware/software integration, Ansible configuration management, and cloud technologies. • Experience with Rancher and Containers as a Service (CaaS). • Understanding of microservices architecture, RESTful APIs, and system design. • Familiarity with event-driven architectures and messaging technologies such as Kafka. • Experience working with SQL databases in containerized environments. • Strong troubleshooting, debugging, communication, and problem-solving skills. Desired Skills: • Experience with Atlassian tools such as Jira, Bamboo, and Fisheye. • Experience with Agile development, continuous integration, and CI/CD pipelines. • Linux or UNIX system administration experience. • Knowledge of SIGINT systems and data flows. • Experience with Kafka, NiFi, MetalLB, and MinIO in production environments. • Familiarity with infrastructure automation using cloud-init or Terraform. • Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus or Grafana. • Experience with Rancher technologies, including Harvester, Longhorn, and KubeVirt. • Experience with AWS services such as EKS, S3, Lambda, and RDS. • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment and maintain technical documentation. Education and Experience: Candidates must meet one of the following education and experience combinations based on the position level. Level 2: • High School Diploma/GED or Associate's Degree: 18 years of relevant software engineering experience. • Bachelor's Degree or higher in Computer Science or a related technical field: 14 years of relevant software engineering experience. Level 3: • High School Diploma/GED or Associate's Degree: 24 years of relevant software engineering experience. • Bachelor's Degree or higher in Computer Science or a related technical field: 20 years of relevant software engineering experience.
