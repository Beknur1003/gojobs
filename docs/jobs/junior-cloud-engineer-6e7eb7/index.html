Junior Cloud Engineer Choosing Capgemini means choosing a company where you will be empowered to shape your career in the way you’d like, where you’ll be supported and inspired by a collaborative community of colleagues around the world, and where you’ll be able to reimagine what’s possible. Join us and help the world’s leading organizations unlock the value of technology and build a more sustainable, more inclusive world. You’ll have the opportunity to collaborate with a leading European mobility services provider, headquartered in Germany and backed by nearly 90 years of expertise. As part of this journey, you’ll contribute to the launch of a new Tech Hub in Porto, helping to drive innovative, technology-led solutions that are transforming how businesses move while contributing to a more sustainable future. YOUR ROLE • Design and maintain cloud-native infrastructure and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible). • Build automation, tooling, and CI/CD pipelines to improve reliability and scalability. • Collaborate with development teams to ensure applications are resilient, observable, and cloud-ready. • Troubleshoot platform issues, support incident resolution, and perform root cause analysis. • Strong knowledge of SRE principles, Linux administration, and distributed systems. • Strong experience working with Golang. • Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and Kubernetes CRDs. • Familiarity with Envoy as an API Gateway. • Experience with observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, or Splunk. • Solid understanding of networking fundamentals. • Experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP is a plus. YOUR PROFILE • Strong hands-on experience with at least one public cloud provider (AWS/ Azure/ Google Cloud). • Solid programming skills in at least one language such as Java, Python, used for automation or tooling. • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform preferred; CloudFormation, ARM, or Pulumi acceptable). • Experience with containerized workloads and orchestration platforms. • Familiarity with CI/CD systems and developer workflows (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions Azure DevOps, etc.). • Good understanding of Linux systems.
