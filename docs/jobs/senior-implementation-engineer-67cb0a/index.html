Company Description Air is the leader in Enterprise Readiness. Our mission is to establish readiness as a real-time condition that is continuously achieved. Today, a dangerous Readiness Gap exists between what the front line needs and what is delivered. Our AI-native platform, Air Enterprise Readiness, aligns development, production, delivery, and sustainment into one coordinated execution system for government agencies and industrial suppliers. By revealing true capacity, exposing real constraints, coordinating resources, and executing at the speed of operational demands, the front line gets what it needs to succeed. Job Description Air is looking for motivated, curious, and data-driven individuals to join our team as Senior Implementation Engineers. Senior Implementation Engineers at Air have a quantitative mindset and firm understanding of how to use technology and data to solve hard customer problems. Senior Implementation Engineers are responsible for developing new methodologies to answer client questions with Air's data, leveraging quantitative skills to configure Air's technology to achieve customer outcomes, communicate data-driven insights to executives in the national security community, and oversee cross-functional teams to produce compelling program insights, data visualization, and briefings. In order to do this job well, you must be analytical, understand people, have a strong drive to use technology to solve the hardest problems, have business acumen, and want to learn everything, particularly about the most pressing national security challenges confronting the nation and how data and machine learning can help address them. You understand the US Government and Defense acquisition processes and the importance of effective and efficient program management, supply chain risk management, and program and technology modernization. You ask hard, uncomfortable questions. You have a professional acumen that allows you to lead conversations with top government agency officials. You know there are always multiple answers to hard problems, and you know how to engage in a constructive dialogue to find the best path forward. This role is a full-time position located out of our office in Arlington, VA. This role requires 60% travel to client sites across the United States - CONUS and OCONUS Scope of Responsibilities • Develop expertise in Air's clients, offerings and data, and support customers as an Air product and data expert • Execute the successful implementation of software and data to Air's customers and provide platform training • Use coding languages (SQL, Python) to conduct technical platform configuration and data integration • Build and manage client relationships • Lead execution teams and/or workstreams • Collaborate with Air Engagement Managers and Product Managers to capture market demand and user needs for inclusion into Air's offerings • Proactively drive utilization of the product using targeted training and engagement strategies that drive mission outcomes and analytical insights for platform users • Develop and maintain documentation such as tailored user guide reference materials • Conduct research and develop quantitative analytic reports using Air technology and data • Orally and visually present data-driven findings in a concise and compelling manner to internal teams, as well as government officials • Demonstrate knowledge in areas such as US Government programs and operations, federal acquisitions and program management, government procurement practices, supplier assurance, federal contracting, Planning, Programming, Budgeting, and Execution (PPBE), Supply Chain Risk Management, and Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) analysis. Depending on your background and expertise, you could also be responsible for: • Provide technical workflow development and configuration • Communicate data-driven insights to executives in the national security space • Conduct in-depth quantitative and qualitative analyses on problems of importance to U.S. national security community using data visualization, compelling written papers and briefings • Provide product demonstrations in support of sales pursuits efforts • Mentor team members to increase skill and domain expertise Qualifications • U.S. Citizenship is required • Bachelor’s Degree in International Relations, Political Science, International Security, Business Administration, Business Analytics, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science or similar fields, or 6+ years of equivalent experience Required Skills: • Proficiency in at least one programming language - SQL, Python, or R - sufficient to independently write, debug, and adapt code in a client-facing setting. Working ability in one is required; comfort across several is preferred. Familiarity with Excel, React, Typescript, Java, C, or GoLang is a plus. • 6-10 years experience performing national security-related analytics; preference for research, quantitative analysis, data manipulation, and data visualization skills • Knowledge of defense acquisition life cycle and program management, technology scouting, and supply chain risk management • History of engaging with and presenting to federal Government stakeholders • Excellent professional demeanor with a willingness to work outside of your comfort zone in a fast-paced environment without hesitation • Strong aptitude for solving open-ended issues in unpredictable, unstructured environments • Outstanding listening, verbal, and written communication skills • Ability to manage multiple work-streams and projects, learn quickly, and work independently with little supervision Desired Skills: • Current possession of a U.S. security clearance, or the ability to obtain one with our sponsorship • Experience in or exposure to the nuances of a startup or other entrepreneurial environment • Master’s degree in International Relations, Political Science, International Security, Business Administration, Business Analytics, Economics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science or similar fields • 3-7 years of experience in user support or user engagement with a focus on platform and product utilization • Experience with advisory services, software-as-a-service (SaaS), information services, analytic tools, or data analytics industries • Demonstrated ability to conduct relevant business case studies and market analysis using internal and open-source data sources We firmly believe that past performance is the best indicator of future performance.
