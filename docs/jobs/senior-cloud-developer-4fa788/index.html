CHEP helps move more goods to more people, in more places than any other organization on earth via our 347 million pallets, crates and containers. We employ approximately 13,000 people and operate in 60 countries. Through our pioneering and sustainable share-and-reuse business model, the world’s biggest brands trust us to help them transport their goods more efficiently, safely and with less environmental impact. What does that mean for you? You’ll join an international organization big enough to take you anywhere, and small enough to get you there sooner. You’ll help change how goods get to market and contribute to global sustainability. You’ll be empowered to bring your authentic self to work and be surrounded by diverse and driven professionals. And you can maximize your work-life balance and flexibility through our Hybrid Work Model. Job Description As a Senior Cloud Developer, you will act as a core engine for our cloud platform, designing and implementing highly scalable, resilient, and secure AWS infrastructure. You will champion automation by driving GitOps patterns, managing Kubernetes environments, and embedding deep observability into our ecosystem. Additionally, you will bridge the gap between cloud infrastructure and data platforms, designing reliable data streaming pipelines and optimization frameworks to support high-throughput data engineering workloads. Key Responsibilities May Include: • Design, engineer, and implement robust security solutions to protect cloud infrastructure, containerized applications, and data, ensuring alignment with best practices. • Collaborate with software engineering teams to configure and maintain secure, scalable, and resilient CI/CD pipelines, incorporating security controls throughout the development lifecycle. • Build and maintain customized integrations and dashboards for proactive threat monitoring, intrusion detection, and vulnerability scanning, ensuring timely responses to security incidents. • Perform regular security audits, threat modelling, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing, automating these processes wherever possible to detect and respond to potential breaches. • Advise on risk mitigation strategies, providing recommendations to enhance security posture and addressing identified vulnerabilities across cloud environments. • Collaborate with system administrators, network engineers, and developers to implement security best practices, including access controls, encryption, logging, and monitoring across cloud platforms. • Configure and document security policies, access management protocols, and vulnerability management processes to ensure compliance and governance standards are met. • Develop and implement incident response plans, ensuring quick and effective action in the event of a security breach or vulnerability exploit. Senior Cloud Developer, Bangalore Key Responsibilities • Automate Infrastructure: Design and maintain infrastructure using Terraform via GitOps continuous delivery workflows. • Manage Containerization: Architect, scale, and optimize production-ready Kubernetes (EKS) clusters. • Embed Observability: Implement comprehensive monitoring, tracing, and logging frameworks across all cloud services. • Build Data Pipelines: Architect and maintain event-driven streaming and ETL pipelines for high-volume data engineering jobs. • Develop Cloud Tooling: Write clean, maintainable backend code in Python, Go to build internal tools and data utilities. • Optimize Performance: Monitor system health, debug complex distributed data systems, and continuously improve cloud reliability and costs. Technical Skills • Cloud Platform: Advanced expertise in AWS core services (IAM, VPC, EC2, EKS, RDS, S3, Lambda). • Orchestration & IaC: Deep operational knowledge of Kubernetes and Terraform. • Data & Streaming Stack: Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, Apache Kafka, and Change Data Capture (CDC) mechanisms. • Observability Stack: Strong experience with OpenTelemetry (OTel), Prometheus, and the Grafana stack (Loki, Tempo, Mimir). • Programming Languages: Professional proficiency in both Python or Golang. Functional & Domain Expertise • Data Engineering & ETL: Deep understanding of designing, building, and optimizing batch and real-time ETL/ELT pipelines. • Event-Driven Architecture: Mastery of data streaming, message queuing, pub/sub patterns, and handling real-time data sync via CDC. • Cloud-Native Architecture: Strong understanding of microservices design, 12-factor app methodologies, and serverless patterns. • Site Reliability Engineering (SRE): Solid grasp of distributed tracing, metrics aggregation, error budgeting, and building self-healing systems. Experience Requirements • Total Experience: 6+ years of professional software engineering, DevOps, or data infrastructure experience. • Cloud & Container Focus: 3+ years of dedicated experience managing AWS environments and production Kubernetes workloads. • Data Pipeline Delivery: Proven track record of implementing Kafka clusters, configuring CDC solutions, and managing relational data layers like PostgreSQL. • Observability Implementation: Demonstrated success setting up OpenTelemetry pipelines and building advanced Grafana dashboard ecosystems. Preferred Qualifications • Hands-on experience with Databricks for unified data analytics and processing. • Official certifications such as AWS Certified Data Engineer, AWS DevOps Engineer, or Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA). • Experience with service meshes (e.g., Istio, Linkerd) and GitOps controllers (e.g., ArgoCD, Flux). Why Join This Team? You will join a forward-thinking, highly collaborative engineering team that genuinely values modern engineering principles over legacy processes. We bridge the gap between bleeding-edge cloud infrastructure and high-throughput data platforms, bypassing manual processes in favor of true GitOps automation.
