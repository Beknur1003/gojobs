*Note: The deadline for applications is 11:59 pm (PST) on January 15th, 2027. Services at Apple encompasses some of the world’s most innovative software including Apple Music, App Store, Apple Books, Podcasts, Apple TV+, Apple News+ and more. Services reach millions of iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Apple Watch and Mac customers in over 150 countries Worldwide. The Evolve Rotation Program is a full-time, two-year technical rotation program within Services at Apple for new graduates early in their careers. The program launches in Summer 2027 and offers you the opportunity to grow both personally and professionally through mentorship, curated projects, and assignments across multiple teams. We are looking to identify and develop the next generation of technical leaders. During the Evolve Rotation Program, you will be placed on projects that allow you to work on significant, high-impact work on various Engineering teams. Rotations are designed to deepen your domain knowledge and strengthen your technical expertise within Backend Engineering, Machine Learning, Full Stack Engineering, or iOS Engineering. Upon successful completion of the program and subject to business need and performance, we will make our best efforts to help you transition into a non-rotating full-time employee role within Apple. Program Eligibility: • Required majors include: Computer Science or Computer Engineering. • Must be graduating Winter 2026 or Spring 2027. This is a 2 year program which starts July 2027 and runs through June 2029. Opportunities within Evolve are available across multiple locations and technical tracks, with the highest demand in Backend Engineering and Machine Learning. Site specific needs: Cupertino, CA Focus Areas: All Domains - Backend, iOS, Fullstack, AI/ML Seattle, WA Focus Areas: Backend (Infrastructure, Security, Cloud) and AI/ML New York, NY Focus Areas: Backend and AI/ML. Preferred Background & Interest: Financial Services & Banking Technologies Evolvers will benefit from a combination of on-the-job work experience and development opportunities. Over the course of two years, you will rotate through two distinct teams, gaining valuable experience and exposure to multiple projects designed to help you develop a strong foundation. Teams you could rotate within include: Apple Wallet, Payments & Commerce, Apple Services Engineering, Apple Maps, and/or Apple Applications. Minimum Qualifications • Minimum of 6+ months of internship experience & proven proficiency with one or more of the following programming languages or technologies: Java, Python, Objective-C, Swift, JavaScript, HTML, CSS3, Hadoop, Cassandra, SolrCloud, and Kafka • Must have one or more of the following skills depending on your focus area: C, C++, AWS, Client/Server Architecture, Golang, Rust, Postgres, SQL, NoSQL, Scala, Spark, React, Angular, Node.js, Spring, Kubernetes, Generative AI, Agent Design, Foundation Model Tuning, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Networking/Distributed Systems, and the ability to quickly learn new technology stacks • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science or Computer Engineering, must be graduating Winter 2026 or Spring 2027. Preferred Qualifications • Strong Communication & Interpersonal skills: Conveying sophisticated ideas to multifaceted audiences, brainstorming with teams, and optimally debating ideas • Attention to detail, data-driven decision making skills, ability to handle ambiguity • Able to present complex projects in a focused manner to senior leaders • Demonstrated curiosity, ownership, and learning velocity, especially around new frameworks, cloud services, and AI tooling
