Senior Golang Backend Engineer — Consumer Products

Location: Remote (APAC) Employment Type: Full-time About A5 Labs A5 Labs is a Bay Area technology company developing advanced AI, gaming, and security solutions for global online platforms. We are looking for a Senior Golang Backend Engineer to design, build, and maintain high-performance backend systems supporting large-scale consumer applications. You will work on business-critical services that demand high concurrency, low latency, data consistency, and production reliability. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using Golang (Go). • Build and optimize high-concurrency microservices and distributed systems supporting consumer-facing applications. • Develop reliable APIs and business workflows involving transactions, user activities, and real-time processing. • Implement fault-tolerant mechanisms, including idempotency, retries, message queues, and failure recovery. • Optimize database performance, SQL queries, indexing strategies, caching, and system throughput. • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks, race conditions, memory issues, and concurrency-related problems. • Build and maintain asynchronous processing pipelines using technologies such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or similar messaging systems. • Take ownership of production services, including monitoring, troubleshooting, incident resolution, and continuous improvement. • Collaborate closely with product managers, engineers, and QA teams to deliver reliable features. Requirements • Strong hands-on experience developing and operating Golang backend services in production. • Solid understanding of Go concurrency, including goroutines, channels, mutexes, context cancellation, and synchronization. • Proven experience designing high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems. • Strong understanding of microservices, REST/gRPC APIs, event-driven architectures, and asynchronous processing. • Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or MySQL, including query optimization, indexing, and transaction management. • Experience with Redis and message queues such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or NSQ. • Understanding of distributed-system reliability, including retries, idempotency, message delivery guarantees, and data consistency. • Experience investigating production performance issues and improving system scalability. • Strong problem-solving skills, engineering ownership, and the ability to work independently in a remote environment. • Good English communication skills. Nice to Have • Experience with gaming, fintech, payments, trading, e-commerce, or other high-traffic B2C platforms. • Experience developing transaction-heavy systems such as wallets, orders, rewards, or payment processing. • Familiarity with distributed transactions, Saga patterns, transactional outbox, and reconciliation workflows. • Experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD. • Experience with profiling tools, observability platforms, and production incident response. What We Offer • 100% remote work across APAC. • Competitive compensation in USD. • Quarterly performance bonuses. • Generous paid annual leave. • Equipment allowance and professional development support. • Flexible working arrangements. • Opportunities to solve challenging engineering problems with an international team.
