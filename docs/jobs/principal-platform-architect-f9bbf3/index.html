Principal Platform Architect DT One | Dubai | Full-time About Us DT One operates a leading global network for mobile top-up, digital value products and rewards. Through a single API connection, we link more than 2,800 partners, including mobile operators, money transfer operators, e-wallets, super apps and retail networks, across 190 countries. Our partners can offer over 23,000 products, from airtime and data top-ups to gift cards and bill payments. We have processed more than 1.2 billion transactions and reach a potential audience of over five billion people, helping to ensure that no one is left unconnected. Founded in 2005 and part of the Thunes group, DT One is headquartered in Singapore with regional offices in Dubai, Miami, Nairobi, London and Barcelona. For more information visit www.dtone.com Context of the role We're looking for a Principal Solutions Architect who is passionate about building platforms at scale and wants to shape the technical direction of a fast-growing, global business. As part of the Engineering team, you will be a key technical voice in how our products and partner integrations are designed end to end, working across squads, products and a diverse range of teams. You will combine a startup mindset with the scale of an industry leader. Working on our core platform and services, you will help us create the future of the company by shaping the target architecture, modernising the platform and making it faster and simpler for new products and partners to go live. Key Responsibilities We're looking for a highly driven, technically excellent architect who is truly excited about having a meaningful impact within the company. In this role, you will own the end-to-end architecture for the products and integrations you lead, from how a product is catalogued and priced through to how it is fulfilled. • Own the end-to-end architecture for new products and major partner integrations, covering product catalogue, ordering, real-time fulfilment and transaction processing • Define the core domain models and API standards that our products, partners and customers are built on, using open, industry-standard API specifications wherever they exist • Lead the evolution of legacy components towards well-bounded, cloud-native microservices on AWS and Kubernetes, without disrupting live traffic • Embed DevSecOps, API management and observability into how we build, so that security and quality are designed in from the start • Translate commercial opportunities into clear target architectures, setting out options, trade-offs, cost and time to market for product and business leaders • Guide build, buy or partner decisions, and lead the evaluation of new technologies and initiatives • Review designs across squads, mentor engineers and champion engineering best practice, including design reviews, architecture decision records and code reviews • Provide L3 support and technical leadership on critical production issues What we are looking for • 10+ years' experience in software engineering and architecture, including several years in a lead or architect role shaping technical direction across a large platform • Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience • Proven track record of designing high-volume, real-time platforms with complex product catalogue, ordering and fulfilment flows • Hands-on experience leading monolith-to-microservices transformations on AWS or an equivalent cloud • Deep expertise in API design and management, Kubernetes and DevSecOps practices • Experience leading and growing engineers, for example as a tech lead or engineering lead, and explaining architectural trade-offs clearly to senior business leaders • Strong Java/JVM or Golang background; we will support strong Java engineers in picking up Go • Exposure to payments, wallet or settlement flows, or to connectivity products such as data bundles or real-time provisioning, is a plus • Interest in the Fintech industry and market innovations Our core services tech stack is hosted on Amazon Web Services. Our main tech stack consists of Golang, Python, Perl (Mojolicious), PostgreSQL, Vue.js, Docker and Nginx. We can offer you an environment with a culture of learning and feedback. You will have real ownership of the architecture of a platform that is used 24/7 around the world and needs to be reliable, secure and massively scalable. You will work with technology teams in different countries, and our environment and packages match the ambition of the company: building world-class engineering teams. Sound like you? Apply now!
