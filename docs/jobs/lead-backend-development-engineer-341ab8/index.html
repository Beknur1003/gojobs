Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Responsibilities: • Deeply understand business requirements, author technical proposals and system designs, and lead the team to achieve business objectives; • Responsible for the planning, development, and optimization of shared technical components, driving their adoption into business applications; analyze system bottlenecks, resolve complex issues, and perform system performance tuning; • Explore emerging industry technologies and approaches to strengthen the company's technical capabilities. Requirements: • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science or a related field; • 5+ years of backend development experience, with 2+ years of architecture experience in Golang; • Proficient in commonly used Golang frameworks, language features, and data structures, with deep understanding of their characteristics and underlying implementation; • Skilled in API development with a solid understanding of concurrency principles; experience building high-concurrency, high-load systems; • Familiar with distributed systems; proficient in mainstream service frameworks and message middleware, with understanding of their implementation principles; • Strong logical thinking and business comprehension skills; good coding practices with clean structure and consistent naming conventions; • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes automation or hands-on DevOps implementation is a plus.
