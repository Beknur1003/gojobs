Software Engineer II Your Responsibilities on the Team • Work with project stakeholders (product owners, QA, designers, and the team lead) to understand and clarify functional and non-functional requirements for assigned work. • Promote a collaborative team environment and work closely with colleagues to achieve business objectives. • Contribute to the team's technical roadmap and help support and onboard peers. • Adhere to architecture, design, implementation, security standards and best practices. • Build and maintain systems that meet SOX IT General Controls, including access provisioning, user access reviews, and change management, under the guidance of senior engineers. • Investigate development problems and propose solutions within assigned features or components. • Participate in production support: deployments, incident triage, observability, and fixing security findings in existing systems. • Implement and maintain integrations with internal and third-party systems based on designs from senior engineers. • Document the features, APIs, and test results you deliver. • Write high-quality code that satisfies customer needs and strives for simplicity, clarity, and testability. • Implement unit and integration tests and conduct functional and performance testing where appropriate. • Participate in code reviews, both giving and receiving feedback, to promote high quality business outcomes. • Contribute to and use shared, reusable software components to enable faster delivery. • Diagnose and resolve bugs and performance issues in existing software. Requirements • Bachelor's degree in computer science, computer information systems or equivalent degree, and 2+ years of professional software engineering experience; OR Master's degree in computer science or a related field. • Growth mindset and a willingness to adapt and learn new skills, technologies, and frameworks. • Strong problem solving and analytical skills. • Experience working in Agile teams, with good communication and collaboration skills. • Ability to clearly explain technical work to teammates and non-technical stakeholders, verbally and in writing. • Experience building or deploying applications on a cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP); familiarity with microservices and containers. • Solid proficiency in at least one modern software development language (Go, Java, C#, etc). • Experience designing relational schemas and writing SQL; Postgres preferred. • Experience with front end development practices and frameworks. • Working knowledge of RESTful APIs. • Experience with Git, CI/CD pipelines, and debugging and troubleshooting issues. Preferred • Experience in Treasury, Corporate Finance, or banking domain. • Hands on experience with Golang, K8s, and Containerized applications.
