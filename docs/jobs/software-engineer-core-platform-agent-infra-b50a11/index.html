ABOUT BASETEN Baseten powers mission-critical inference for the world's most dynamic AI companies, like Cursor, Notion, OpenEvidence, Abridge, Clay, Gamma, and Writer. By uniting applied AI research, flexible infrastructure, and seamless developer tooling, we enable companies operating at the frontier of AI to bring cutting-edge models into production. We're growing quickly and recently raised our $1.5B Series F, led by Altimeter Capital, Conviction Partners, and Spark Capital. Join us and help build the platform engineers turn to ship AI products. THE ROLE AI agents need more than models. They need compute to execute code, storage to maintain state, and networking to interact with the world. Our Agent Infra team is building that cloud from the ground up, including isolated sandboxes, compute orchestration, networking, and storage. We're looking for a hands-on Software Engineer to build the core systems that turn raw infrastructure into a scalable, programmable compute platform. You'll work across the control plane and scheduling layer, building the services that determine where workloads run, how resources are allocated, and how customers securely access and manage their compute. This is an opportunity to shape the foundational architecture of a new cloud platform, tackling distributed systems challenges at the intersection of infrastructure, performance, and scale. RESPONSIBILITIES • Design and build the control plane that manages compute resources, workload lifecycles, and product services. • Develop distributed scheduling and resource allocation systems that balance performance, utilization, and reliability. • Build high-throughput platform services for authentication, authorization, quotas, and rate limiting. • Design APIs and abstractions that make complex infrastructure simple and programmable for developers. • Improve the scalability, fault tolerance, and operational reliability of core platform services. • Work closely with compute, networking, and storage engineers to bring new infrastructure capabilities into production. REQUIREMENTS • Strong software engineering background building production distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, or large-scale backend services. • Hands-on experience building control planes, schedulers, orchestration systems, or other foundational infrastructure services. • Experience designing highly scalable APIs, services, and systems that operate reliably under high load. • Strong understanding of distributed systems concepts, including concurrency, consistency, fault tolerance, and resource management. • Proficiency in Go, Rust, C++, or similar languages, with a willingness to work in Go. • (mention something about messaging and eventbus) • Comfortable navigating complex technical tradeoffs and owning systems from design through production. • Bonus: Experience with multi-tenant platforms, custom schedulers, resource isolation, or cloud infrastructure. BENEFITS • Competitive compensation, including meaningful equity • (U.S. only) 100% coverage of medical, dental, and vision insurance for employee and dependents • Flexible PTO policy • Paid parental leave • Fertility and family-building stipend through Carrot • (U.S. only) Company-facilitated 401(k) • Exposure to a variety of ML startups, offering unparalleled learning and networking opportunities.
