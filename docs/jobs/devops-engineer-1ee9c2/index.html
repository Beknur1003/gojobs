Docyt is a Silicon Valley-based company that leverages AI to automate the back-office of small businesses. Our digital platform includes multiple services that make managing small businesses a breeze. We use cutting-edge technologies and engineering processes in order to deliver software of the highest quality to our customers. Automation is at the heart of Docyt’s engineering philosophy. We are building automated pipelines with the minimum human intervention that enables us to rapidly iterate and deliver new features to our customers as soon as they are ready. We are an agile company with high standards for software quality, security, and reliability that are baked into our architecture and processes. Like any growing company, we are facing growth-related challenges that include a broad spectrum of problems of scaling the application in the cloud and making the engineering team efficient by providing an easy and repeatable way to test their changes in development environments.
