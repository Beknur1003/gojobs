Senior Platform Engineer (Crossplane & Backstage)

Sabenza IT & Recruitment Johannesburg, South Africa опубликовано 21 октября 2026

Senior
KubernetesTerraformAWSPrometheusGrafanaCI/CDPythonJava
We are looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to design, build and operate scalable, production-grade platforms and self-service capabilities that empower engineering teams. You will shape cloud-native foundations, automation and internal developer platforms that improve reliability, speed and developer experience. What you will do: · Design and build self-service platforms, platform APIs, automation and system-level tooling used by other engineering teams. · Architect and operate highly available, secure and scalable cloud-native platforms on AWS. · Implement and evolve Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, CDK, Pulumi and Crossplane. · Build and operate Kubernetes environments with strong GitOps practices (ArgoCD / Flux, Helm and Kustomize). · Develop Kubernetes Operators and contribute to platform extensibility. · Implement monitoring, observability and reliability practices using CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and X-Ray. · Drive CI/CD and DevOps practices with tools such as GitHub Actions and GitLab CI/CD. · Apply AWS best practices and the Well-Architected Framework across platform design and operations. · Collaborate with engineering teams to reduce toil, improve developer experience and enable self-service consumption of platform capabilities. Requirements What you bring: · 5+ years of experience in Platform Engineering, building self-service platforms and abstractions for engineering teams. · 6+ years of hands-on experience with public cloud, with deep expertise in AWS. · 6+ years of experience with containerisation and container platforms. · 2–3+ years of software engineering experience using languages such as Java, JavaScript, Golang or Python. · Expert knowledge of AWS cloud architecture, services and the Well-Architected Framework. · Strong experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration principles (CKA certification preferred). · Strong experience with GitOps using ArgoCD / Flux and Helm / Kustomize. · Hands-on experience with Crossplane, ArgoCD and Backstage (non-negotiable). · Proven experience building Kubernetes Operators (non-negotiable). · Strong experience implementing monitoring and observability solutions (CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, X-Ray). · Strong understanding of DevOps practices and CI/CD tooling (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD). · Proven track record of building complex, production-grade platforms and making them available for self-service use. · Solid understanding of cloud-native systems, distributed systems and service architecture trade-offs.
