About Us Symbio, part of the Aussie Broadband Group, is revolutionising global communications. Our platform delivers the full communications stack, from carrier infrastructure to enterprise collaboration solutions, across multiple Asia-Pacific markets. Our goal is to be the telco people love – our customers, team members, shareholders, and partners. Listed on the Australian Stock Exchange (ASX: ABB), the Aussie Broadband Group collectively supplies more than 1 million telecommunications services, operates two Tier 1 voice providers in Australia and owns fibre infrastructure. Our culture is built on five values that guide everything we do: Don't be ordinary, be awesome | Think BIG | No bullsh*t | Be good to people | Have fun Are you graduating in 2026 or have you recently completed a degree in Computer Science or Software Engineering? Ready to kick-start your career and work alongside experienced engineers building innovative products and platforms? We are looking for an enthusiastic, curious and driven Graduate Software Engineer to join our team commencing 9 February 2027. This is an exciting opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a collaborative environment, where you'll learn from experienced professionals, contribute to real projects and develop your technical skills from day one. In this role, you'll help design, build, maintain and support software solutions that deliver real value to our customers and business, while building a strong foundation for your career in software engineering. This role is based in Sydney, with a hybrid work model that combines office and remote working. What you’ll be doing • Design, develop, maintain and support software applications and platforms. • Build new features and capabilities that meet customer and business requirements. • Contribute to high-quality, resilient and scalable software solutions. • Participate in Agile ceremonies and collaborate with business and technology stakeholders. • Troubleshoot, test and enhance existing applications. • Continuously develop your technical skills and contribute to engineering best practices. What we’re looking for • Completed a relevant degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline within the last two years. • 0-3 years' professional experience in software engineering, software development, or a related field. • Exposure to Golang, Java or Python (Golang experience is highly regarded) • Knowledge of object-oriented programming principles. • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. • A genuine passion for technology and software development. • A desire to learn, innovate and drive efficiency. • Curiosity, resilience and creativity, combined with a collaborative team-first mindset. • A commitment to continuous learning and improving your technical capabilities. We're looking for people who are curious, motivated and eager to learn. If you bring the right attitude, we'll help you build the skills.
