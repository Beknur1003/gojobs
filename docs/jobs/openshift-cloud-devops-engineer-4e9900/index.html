About the Role As a Cloud DevOps Engineer specializing in OpenShift and Kubernetes, you will play a crucial role in designing, deploying, and maintaining state-of-the-art cloud container platforms for our global clients. You will drive automation, enhance reliability, and ensure the seamless operation of cloud infrastructures, supporting the company’s mission to deliver secure, scalable, and resilient technology solutions. Working in an Agile, collaborative environment, you will be at the forefront of technological innovation, continuously evolving your skills and contributing to high-impact projects. Responsibilities • Take end-to-end responsibility for the OpenShift landscape of clients, ensuring robust design, installation, development, configuration, and maintenance of the platform. • Collaborate within a global, cross-functional team to develop and manage operational tasks, prioritizing automation, reliability, maintainability, security, and customer satisfaction. • Utilize CI/CD frameworks to support rapid lifecycle management of cloud platforms and services. • Provide L2-L3 support for incidents, problem resolution, and service requests, collaborating with internal teams and third-party vendors to resolve technical issues and implement changes. • Implement, monitor, and enhance automation and self-healing solutions to minimize manual intervention and reduce operational toil. • Address reliability risks by managing error budgets and proactively improving platform stability. • Develop and maintain comprehensive internal designs and user documentation to support operational excellence. • Contribute to IT Service Management (ITSM) processes, ensuring adherence to best practices and compliance standards. • Participate in Agile ceremonies and embrace DevOps/SRE methodologies to drive continuous improvement. Skill Requirements • Azure Associate certification or equivalent knowledge level. • OpenShift or Kubernetes certification. • 3+ years of professional experience for mid-level roles; 5+ years for senior roles. • Hands-on experience with HELM or Kustomize. • Proficiency in at least one of the following: Ansible, GoLang, or Terraform. • Demonstrated security mindset across all activities. • Solid understanding of CI/CD concepts and toolsets such as Jenkins or GitHub Actions. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to resolve complex technical issues efficiently. • Adaptability to work in fast-paced, dynamic environments with evolving priorities. • Proven ability to work autonomously while contributing effectively to a collaborative team environment. Required Language Thresholds • English (B2/C1: Independent User Level) Preferred Skills • Understanding of Agile development, SCRUM, or SRE concepts is a plus. • Willingness to participate in standby/on-call duties as required. • Eagerness to continuously learn and adapt to emerging technologies and industry trends. • Excellent communication skills and a stress-resilient, professional attitude.
