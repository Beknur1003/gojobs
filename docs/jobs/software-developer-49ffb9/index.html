APIs & Microservices • Design, develop, and maintain RESTful APIs and microservices. • Ensure services are scalable, secure, and performant. Performance & Reliability • Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize application performance. • Identify and resolve system bottlenecks, latency issues, and scalability challenges. AI Platform Enablement • Develop platform features that integrate with machine learning workflows, big data systems, and cloud-native services. • Support automation, orchestration, and operational efficiency across the AI platform ecosystem.
