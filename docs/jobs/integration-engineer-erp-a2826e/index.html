Join BLP and help make the world’s business processes autonomous! Our agentic platform helps finance and operations teams worldwide remove manual work from their core business processes, so work runs end-to-end with greater control and significant productivity gains. Today, over 550 enterprise customers use BLP across 1,300+ deployed projects. In the processes we automate, customers achieve productivity improvements of 5 to 10 times. To date, BLP remains majority employee-owned, with Goldman Sachs holding a minority stake as a growth investor. Our success is driven by deep expertise in technology and business processes, a superior product with strong product-market fit, reflected in an expanding customer base that includes Fortune 500 companies. BLP is one of Switzerland's fastest-growing SaaS scale-ups; we are now entering our next phase of growth. From our headquarters on Zürich's Bahnhofstrasse, we're expanding globally, with a growing presence across Europe and North America. Join us and help make autonomous operations the new standard. About the role BLP only works when it talks to our customers' ERP systems. As an Integration Engineer, you build and own those connections and keep live integrations running for our customers. Every new ERP system is a new problem to solve. You'll take ownership of integrations across ERPs such as Microsoft Dynamics, Oracle, Infor, IFS and abas, and help us connect to the next one. What you’ll do • Design and implement interfaces for diverse protocols: REST, SOAP, OData, direct DB access and file transfers. • Explore new ERP systems and determine the best way to connect them. • Build new ERP integrations, often in close collaboration with pilot customers. • Own existing integrations end to end, including issue resolution, documentation and driving improvements to ensure market fit, reliability and efficient installation. • Enable technical project managers and integration specialists to consult on, install and configure existing integrations. What you'll bring • Solid programming skills in at least one backend language. We build our integrations in Go. If you come from Java, C#, Python or similar and are keen to pick up Go fast, that works for us. • Hands-on integration experience with at least one ERP system (e.g., SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, Oracle, Infor, IFS, ABAS or similar), ideally more than one. You have built or maintained native interfaces yourself, not only used the system as a functional consultant or key user. • A degree in computer science or a related field, or equivalent experience. • A structured, self-reliant way of working, and ideally experience in a client-facing role. • Fluent English. German is a plus, as most of our customers are in the DACH region. Nice to have: • Experience writing production code in Go. • Familiarity with modern engineering practices: Git, code reviews, automated testing and CI/CD. • A good understanding of purchase-to-pay data in ERP systems: suppliers, purchase orders, receipts and supplier invoice matching. • Experience with both cloud and on-premise ERP environments, and the constraints of each (e.g., no direct database access or API rate limits in cloud ERPs). Why BLP • Real impact: your integrations decide whether a customer goes live. • Breadth: Oracle, Infor, Microsoft Dynamics and many other ERPs, plus EDI and e-invoicing standards. • Growth: fast-tracked growth, with promotions and a bonus scheme that includes company shares. • Team: regular team events. Practical information • Location: We have an office in Zurich, London, Munich and Cologne, hybrid with 3+ office days per week in those locations or remotely from anywhere in Europe. • If you work remotely, we pay for regular visits and stays in Zurich. • Employment: full-time, permanent.
