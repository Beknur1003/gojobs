Who We Are Entain is fearlessly transforming the global racing, sports and wagering industry, creating unforgettable customer experiences and pushing beyond traditional boundaries. Part of a FTSE 50 company with nearly 30,000 employees across 30+ countries including Europe, the USA, Australia and New Zealand, Entain ANZ powers leading brands like TAB, Trackside and Ladbrokes. At Entain, our customers are at the heart of everything we do, and Finance underpins the commercial decisions that drive performance, sustainable growth, and world-class customer experiences. Your Impact We’re looking for a curious and motivated Junior/ Mid Engineer to join our Technology team in Bowen Hills. This is a backend-focused opportunity for someone with around one to two years of commercial development experience who is keen to grow into a highly capable engineer over the long term. You’ll work alongside experienced developers and technical leads, contributing to services and platforms that support Entain’s products. While technical foundations are important, we’re particularly interested in people who demonstrate curiosity, strong problem-solving ability, enthusiasm for learning and the potential to progress beyond a junior level. A Day in the Life • Contribute to the design and implementation of efficient, scalable and robust solutions to suit project requirements. • Backend engineering to implement tasks using compiled languages (Golang/Python). • Data querying and manipulation using database technologies, including relational databases and nosql solutions (mongo). • Be active in team collaboration - issue resolution, code review, showcases, discussing solutions with other engineers, production of documentation. • Diagnose and triage errors using logging and metric tools – Loki logs, Grafana etc. • Frontend engineering to complete smaller tasks using JavaScript and associated libraries (React). Your Strengths • Exposure to a compiled language. Golang experience preferred but not necessary. • Basic understanding or experience with NoSQL and relational databases. • Basic understanding or experience with Apache Kafka or similar queueing technology. • Familiar with REST and/or gRPC APIs • Ability to design and implement small feature requests or bug fixes autonomously. • Understanding of the concepts of microservices vs service-oriented architectures. • Mid-level development skills in JavaScript. • Experience with containerised applications: Kubernetes, Docker. • Experience engaging with staff across different teams to facilitate outcomes. Life at Entain At Entain, it’s more than a job - it’s a place to grow, connect, and make an impact. We value individuality, celebrate collaboration, and create space for bold ideas that move people. Here, you’ll help shape the voice of some of Australia and New Zealand’s most recognised brands - while developing your own craft in a supportive, ambitious team. Perks You’ll Love • Hybrid & Flexible: Balance your life and work. • Recognition & Rewards: Celebrate wins and enjoy retailer discounts. • Wellbeing Perks: Support for your health and happiness. • Workday Extras: Free breakfast, snacks, and onsite barista. • Office Vibes: Pool table, live sports, and fun social activities.
