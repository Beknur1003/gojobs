Overview: SOFTSWISS continues to expand the team and is looking for a Head of Engineering. We need a true, experienced and accomplished professional who shares our culture and values to lead our engineering organization through its next phase of exponential growth. Purpose of the role: As Head of Engineering, you will be responsible for establishing and maintaining a transparent and manageable engineering structure while scaling the organization and technical infrastructure to the enterprise level to support multi-fold growth in team size, traffic, and customer base. You will drive the transformation of our engineering practices, organizational structure, and delivery processes while maintaining the highest standards of system reliability and stability. This role requires a strategic leader capable of restoring control and structure, fostering accountability and communication across teams, and delivering results without compromising quality. Key responsibilities: • Strategic Leadership: Translate company goals into clear engineering strategy, ensuring alignment with product and business priorities. • Organizational Development: Assess and reorganize the current engineering setup; build scalable processes and clear accountability. • Team Scaling: Grow the engineering organization while maintaining quality, ownership culture, and delivery discipline. • Middle Management: Develop leads, architects, and delivery owners to ensure manageability and reduce top-level micromanagement. • Cross-functional Collaboration: Strengthen cooperation between engineering, product, and delivery teams for predictable releases. • Delivery Excellence: Balance delivery speed with technical quality and architectural soundness. • Technical Leadership: Oversee architecture evolution, infrastructure scalability, and technical decision-making. • Engineering Culture: Build a culture of ownership, transparency, and continuous improvement. Support professional development and knowledge sharing. • Process & Metrics: Establish clear metrics for productivity, delivery, and quality. Maintain transparency through monitoring and reporting. • Stakeholder Management: Act as the main engineering representative for executives and key partners. • Infrastructure Reliability: Ensure high system availability, performance, and disaster recovery standards. Required Experience: • 10+ years in software engineering, including 5+ years in senior leadership (Head/Director/VP level). • Proven success scaling engineering teams (from ~30 to 100+ engineers). • Experience restoring structure and transparency in complex organizations. • Deep expertise in building and operating high-load distributed systems with strict SLA requirements. • Strong technical background in modern cloud-native architectures (microservices, Kubernetes, event-driven systems). • Experience with open-source technology stacks similar to ours (Go, PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka, Elasticsearch). • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills • Experience with geographically distributed teams. • Background in betting, gaming, or other high-traffic, high-availability environments. • Native Russian speaker with fluent English (written and spoken). Nice to have: • Experience with sports betting, iGaming, or fintech platforms. • Hands-on experience with our specific technology stack (Golang, Kubernetes ecosystem). • Experience with React/React Native for web and mobile platforms. • Experience with compliance or regulated environments. • Open-source contributions or active participation in engineering communities. Our Benefits: • Private health insurance • Sports benefits • Comprehensive Mental Health Program • Free English lessons (online) • Local language courses • Paid time off • Maternity leave support • Referral program rewards • Upskilling, internal workshops, and participation in professional conferences and corporate events
