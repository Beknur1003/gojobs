Top 3 Reasons To Join Us The Job About AIRR Labs AIRR Labs is a fast-growing E-commerce and Social Commerce company helping brands build meaningful connections with consumers across Southeast Asia. Our Technology team develops AI-powered platforms and internal products that enable business teams to operate at scale, automate complex workflows, and drive measurable business outcomes. We are building the operational backbone of modern commerce — combining intelligent automation, scalable infrastructure, and data-driven decision-making. As a Backend Engineer, you will play a key role in designing and building the systems that power our next generation of commerce solutions. Job Summary: We are seeking a skilled Backend Engineer to join our team. You will design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable, and secure server-side applications that power our products and services. The ideal candidate is passionate about building efficient systems, has strong problem-solving skills, and thrives in a collaborative environment. Key Responsibilities: • Design and build high-concurrency backend services using Go or Java to support global e-commerce scale. • Develop and optimize AI-powered search features, including vector-based semantic search and integration with LLM-driven workflows. • Build and manage scalable cloud infrastructure on AWS, ensuring high availability and optimized performance for large-scale media assets. • Implement event-driven architectures and microservices that communicate via high-performance message brokers. • Build and maintain robust CI/CD pipelines to automate testing and deployment in a containerized environment. • Collaborate on the design of complex database schemas (Postgres) and search index mappings (OpenSearch) to ensure data integrity and query efficiency. Your Skills and Experience What We're Looking For • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 2+ years of backend development experience. We are also open to exceptional early-career engineers with strong technical foundations. • Strong proficiency in Go (Golang) or Java. • Experience building and operating cloud-native applications on AWS. • Solid understanding of relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL. • Experience working with containerized environments using Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with modern observability and monitoring tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, and Loki. • Experience working with search technologies such as OpenSearch or Elasticsearch. • Understanding of distributed systems, event-driven architecture, and high-concurrency applications. • Strong problem-solving skills and a passion for building scalable products. • Good English communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively within regional teams. Preferred Skills (Optional): • Experience with Vector Databases (e.g. OpenSearch Vector Engine). • Knowledge of building and optimizing Digital Asset Management (DAM) or content delivery systems. • Understanding of Infrastructure as Code using Terraform or AWS CDK. Why You'll Love Working Here What We Offer: • Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI technologies that transform how businesses create content. • A collaborative, innovative environment with opportunities for professional growth.
