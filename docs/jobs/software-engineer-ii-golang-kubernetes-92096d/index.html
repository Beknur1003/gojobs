About NCR VOYIX NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide. Title: Software Engineer II - Edge Platform Location: Chennai Experience: 2 – 5 years About Us NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global technology company leading how the world connects, interacts, and transacts with business. Our assisted and self-service solutions and comprehensive support services address the needs of hospitality and retail sectors in more than 40+ countries. Role Overview Join our market-leading, innovative Edge platform team at NCR Voyix. As a Software Engineer II, you will be an integral part of the Edge team, designing and developing distributed platform capabilities primarily using Go and Kubernetes. You will take on complex development tasks, participate in technical design and code reviews, troubleshoot containerized applications, and collaborate with QA and internal stakeholders to deliver reliable, scalable, and high-quality solutions. Key Responsibilities · Take on complex development tasks and own moderately complex features within the team. · Design, develop, and test scalable and maintainable micro services. · Develop and troubleshoot applications running on as containers . · Involve in code reviews to maintain engineering quality. · Participate in technical design activities. · Liaise with QA and internal stakeholders to ensure solution quality and release readiness. Core Skills · Proficiency in Programming: Very strong hands-on experience developing production-quality applications ideally using Go. Strong understanding of Go’s concurrency model, goroutines, channels, interfaces, error handling, testing, and performance considerations. Ability to write efficient, scalable, maintainable, and well-tested code. · Strong development skills: Strong understanding of clean code, software design principles, SOLID principles, automated testing, debugging, and agile development practices. · Linux OS: Working knowledge of Linux, including Bash scripting, processes, filesystems, networking, installation, and troubleshooting. Nice to Have Skills (ideally two skills in this list) · Kubernetes & Containerization: Strong hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes, including workloads, Services, configuration, storage, networking, resource management, health checks, scheduling, and troubleshooting. Good understanding of Kubernetes architecture and cloud-native application design. · Git / CI/CD: Strong experience with Git and familiarity with GitHub, GitHub Actions, or similar CI/CD tooling. · Cloud computing: Exposure to cloud platforms, ideally Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including familiarity with GKE and related cloud services. · Additional technologies: Familiarity with Service Mesh principles, CouchDB, OpenTelemetry (OTEL), Grafana, GCP Cloud Logging, Helm, and GitHub/GitHub Actions. Experience with KCC, kubeadm, or GraphQL is nice to have. Basic Qualifications · Degree in a numerate, Computer Science, Engineering, or related technical discipline. · Excellent programming skills, with strong preference for substantial hands-on Go experience. · Strong hands-on experience developing and troubleshooting Kubernetes-based applications. · Strong grounding in Computer Science.
