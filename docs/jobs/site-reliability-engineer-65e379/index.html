The role involves implementing automation, monitoring systems, and ensuring high availability and performance across AWS/GCP platforms. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to handle deployments, incident management, and continuous improvements. Strong expertise in DevOps tools, containerization, and infrastructure as code is essential.
