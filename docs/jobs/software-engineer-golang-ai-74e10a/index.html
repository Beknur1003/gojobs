About the Company We are an AI-powered B2B startup helping revenue teams understand what is changing inside their target accounts. Our platform continuously tracks companies, competitors, stakeholders, and other relevant sources, transforming fragmented signals into actionable intelligence for enterprise customers. We are a small startup that has been operating for around four years. We work directly with enterprise clients, move fast, and maintain a high level of service. We are now in a growth stage and expanding our engineering team to increase our capacity to deliver. About the Role We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer with strong Go experience and hands-on experience building AI-powered products. You will primarily work on backend systems, APIs, data processing, and AI workflows. This may include RAG, LLM agents, prompt engineering, retrieval, and vector search. This is a high-autonomy role. We are looking for someone who can take ownership of a problem, make technical decisions, and deliver solutions without constant supervision. What You'll Do • Build and maintain backend services in Go • Develop features involving LLMs, RAG, AI agents, and prompt engineering • Build APIs, integrations, and data-processing pipelines • Work with NoSQL databases • Contribute to retrieval, search, and AI workflows • Take ownership of features from design to production • Troubleshoot and solve production issues independently • Collaborate closely with a small engineering and product team • Occasionally handle simple Vue.js frontend tasks Requirements Must have: • 5+ years of commercial software engineering experience • 2+ years of commercial Go/Golang experience • Hands-on experience with at least some of: • RAG • AI/LLM agents • Prompt engineering • LLM-powered applications • Experience with NoSQL databases • Strong backend development fundamentals • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision • Comfortable working in a fast-paced startup environment • Ability to handle simple frontend tasks in Vue.js, or willingness to learn Nice to Have • AWS • Agentic application development • Vector databases • Python • Production RAG systems • Enterprise SaaS experience • Experience with data-intensive applications and integrations What We Value Ownership — You can take a problem from idea to production. Autonomy — You don't need constant direction or control to make progress. Pragmatism — You focus on solutions that work rather than unnecessary complexity. Speed — You are comfortable working in a small team where priorities can move quickly. Product mindset — You care about the customer problem, not just the code.
