As a Senior Site Reliability Engineer, your role covers the entire life-cycle of a product, from helping developers with architecture and delivery to on-call incident response and triage. Your primary focus will be automation and continuous integration/delivery with an emphasis on solving operations issues fleet-wide using software engineering best practices. You will report to the Senior SRE Manager. Responsibilities: • You will design, build, and operate distributed, large-scale, cloud-based infrastructure using modern open-source software solutions. • You will participate in the on-call and incident response for live mission-critical services. • You will use automation technologies to ensure repeatability, eliminate toil, reduce mean time to detection and resolution (MTTD & MTTR). • You will perform root cause analysis and post-mortems with an eye towards future prevention. • You will design and build CI/CD pipelines to reduce the cycle time for new product versions. • You will create monitoring, alerting and dashboarding solutions that improve visibility into EA's application performance and business metrics. • You will build common services and tooling used by different product engineering teams to accelerate the time to market of new features for game teams Qualifications: • 10+ years of experience supporting high-availability production-grade infrastructure and applications in a cloud computing environment • 10+ years of experience supporting high-availability production-grade infrastructure and applications with defined SLIs and SLOs. • 5+ years of experience with Containerization technologies such as Kubernetes and Docker • Network experience, including an understanding of standard protocols/components and the OSI 7 layer network model • Automation and orchestration experience including any of the following: Terraform, Helm, Chef, Ansible • Experience writing code in Python, Golang, C++, or Java. • Experience working with distributed systems. • Experience with AI-assisted coding tools is a nice-to-have.
