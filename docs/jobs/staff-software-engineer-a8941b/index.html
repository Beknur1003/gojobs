This role is part of our Prerender team, one of our growing brands at saas.group. For nearly a decade, Prerender has empowered marketers to create dynamic, complex sites at scale, making their JavaScript websites easily indexable by search engines. We eliminate indexing and crawling limitations, allowing our customers to gain the upper-hand on HTML sites and rank higher on SERPs. More than 100,000 websites around the globe trust Prerender to serve more than 2.7 billion web pages to crawlers to improve organic search ranking. You lead major architectural evolutions, coach engineers in XP principles, and help teams make sound technical decisions that scale across the organization.
