Job Information : Role : Title - Phoenix Team : Platform Engineering Type : Returnship program for Women in Career break Location : Bengaluru Mode : Onsite Duration : 6 months About Phoenix : Phoenix is Myntras initiative designed to be a launchpad for women returning to their careers after a break. This six-month program provides a supportive environment that helps ensure a smooth transition back to work. With structured onboarding, customized learning and development opportunities, mentorship, hands-on projects, and best-in-class benefits, we aim to create a space where you can confidently re-discover and grow your career with us. Launched in 2021, Phoenix has thrived over the years, and with Myntras unwavering commitment to inclusivity, we proudly present Phoenix 5.0 as the 5th batch. During your time in Phoenix, you'll collaborate with some of the best talent in the e-commerce industry and work on projects aligned with your interests and abilities. This experience is designed to empower you, build your skills, and potentially open the door to full-time opportunities at Myntra. Myntra Engineering : The Myntra Engineering team develops the technology platform that drives our customers' shopping experience and ensures the efficient movement of products from suppliers to their final destinations. Our work spans a variety of areas, including building massive-scale web applications, creating engaging user interfaces, developing big-data analytics, mobile apps, workflow systems, and inventory management solutions. As a lean technology team, every individual's contribution is significant. You will have the chance to be part of a rapidly expanding organization and gain comprehensive exposure to all components of a complete e-commerce platform. The Team - Platform Engineering : If you're passionate about tackling complex problems, building platform services for immense scale, and crafting a world-class e-commerce experience, Myntra Platform Engineering is the ideal environment for you. The Myntra Platform Engineering team focuses on building products and solutions across several critical domains : - Security and Compliance - Internal Developer Platforms - High-Scale Observability Solutions - Software Quality & Test Engineering - Continuous Integration, Testing, and Delivery Ultimately, the team is responsible for ensuring the high quality and reliability of services built by Myntra Engineering, serving our customers, partners, and sellers. Experience : - 3 to 4 years of experience in large-scale application development - Experience in development on NodeJS / Golang / Python. - Experience on working with MySQL, Mongodb etc. - Good Software Engineering fundamentals in Design Patterns; Operating Systems - Thread, Concurrency, Memory Management, Parallel Programming, Computer Networking and Protocols - TCP/IP, DNS etc & Distributed computing. - Working experience will be an additional advantage. - Experience in Linux/Unix systems, Cloud Computing, CI/CD, and building systems is desirable. - Experience with containerisation and orchestration tools (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes). Education : - Engineering degree from a premier institution is required.
