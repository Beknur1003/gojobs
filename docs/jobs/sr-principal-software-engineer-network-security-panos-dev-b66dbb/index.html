Job Summary We are seeking innovative engineers to design and develop security features for our next-generation firewalls. As a key member of the Layer-7 Security group, you will take ownership of your focus areas, applying your programming skills to solve real-world security problems. You will collaborate with multi-functional teams including product management, quality assurance, and field support throughout the entire software development cycle. This role requires a drive to build global products and a passion for bringing new ideas to the security discipline. Responsibilities • Architect & Implement: Design, build, and maintain real-time, high-throughput security services and networking infrastructure. • Technical Leadership: Lead complex engineering projects end-to-end—from initial architecture and design reviews to deployment and production monitoring. • Operational Excellence: Promote engineering best practices, including secure coding standards, comprehensive code reviews, performance tuning, and robust system architecture. • Elevate Systems Ownership: Architect and own critical control-plane and data-plane components for the GlobalProtect server infrastructure, ensuring high availability, fault tolerance, and scalable performance • Advanced Troubleshooting: Diagnose and resolve complex server-side and network-level issues independently, even when presented with minimal diagnostic information. • Test Automation & Quality: Drive a culture of quality by advocating for aggressive automated testing, continuous integration/continuous deployment (CI/CD), and high code coverage. • AI-Driven Productivity: Leverage AI-assisted development techniques and tools to maximize coding efficiency, streamline debugging, and accelerate project delivery. Qualifications Required Qualifications • Experience & Industry Background: 10+ years of professional software development experience in the enterprise networking or cybersecurity industry. • Core Engineering Skills: Proven proficiency in C, Go (Golang), Python programming within a Linux/Unix development environment. • Distributed Systems Architecture: Extensive experience with multi-threaded programming, asynchronous I/O, and designing large-scale, high-performance, or distributed server architectures. • Networking Expertise: Hands-on experience developing TCP/IP networking software and understanding lower-level network protocols. • End-to-End Ownership: Demonstrated track record of leading and successfully executing complex technical projects from conception to release. • Strategic / Cross-Functional Impact: Proven ability to mentor senior engineers, collaborate across product management and architecture teams, and influence long-term technical roadmaps. • Education: B.S. degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical discipline (or equivalent practical experience). Preferred Qualifications • API & Pipeline Integration: Experience with RESTful API design, implementation, and integration, alongside familiarity with modern CI/CD pipelines. • AI & Automation Mindset: Active experience using AI-assisted tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, LLM coding assistants) to boost engineering velocity and operational efficiency. • Automation First: A strong advocate for test-driven development (TDD), automated integration testing, and performance profiling. • Agile & Collaborative Attitude: A team player with a strong "can-do" attitude who thrives in a fast-paced environment and rapidly adapts to new technologies and platforms.
