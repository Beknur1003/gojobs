Senior Software Engineer We have an opportunity for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Universal DDI engineering team in Bangalore, India, reporting to a Senior Manager, Engineering. In this pivotal role, you will design, develop, and debug reliable, scalable software modules for Infoblox Universal DDI products. You will work primarily with Golang and cloud-native technologies to deliver secure, maintainable, and testable services that help customers manage DNS, DHCP, and IP address management across hybrid and multi-cloud environments. Collaborating closely with product architects, senior engineers, QA, product management, support, and distributed engineering teams, you will contribute across the software development lifecycle. You will also use approved AI-enabled development tools responsibly to improve productivity, automate routine work, strengthen quality, and support informed technical decisions while applying sound engineering judgment.
