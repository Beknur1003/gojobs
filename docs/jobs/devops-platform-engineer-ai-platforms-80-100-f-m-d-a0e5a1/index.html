LGT is the largest Private Banking and Asset Management group in the world to be owned by an entrepreneurial family. As the family office of the Princely House of Liechtenstein, we have years of experience in the management of sizeable sums of assets. Job Description About the Role We are looking for a DevOps Platform Engineer – AI Platforms to help operate, further develop and stabilize our AI and infrastructure platforms. The role sits at the intersection of platform engineering, automation and AI enablement, supporting the delivery of reliable, scalable and maintainable platform capabilities for internal teams. It is suited to someone with strong engineering fundamentals, a practical mindset and an interest in shaping an evolving platform environment. Key Responsibilities • Further develop and stabilize our AI and infrastructure platforms • Help internal teams leverage AI services, platform capabilities and technical solutions effectively • Automate repetitive operational tasks, technical processes and deployment workflows • Create, maintain and continuously improve scripts and automation solutions using Go and Windows PowerShell • Support the deployment and operation of containerized workloads on OpenShift and Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS) • Contribute to the implementation of resilient, scalable and maintainable platform solutions for AI-related and infrastructure use cases • Support troubleshooting, root-cause analysis and optimization of platform components • Collaborate in engineering projects from conception through implementation • Drive continuous improvement of operations, engineering standards and automation practices • Work closely with infrastructure, development and project teams across functions and locations Requirements • Experience in DevOps, platform engineering, system engineering or infrastructure engineering • Good knowledge of automation, scripting and modern operational processes • Hands-on experience with Golang, Windows PowerShell and container technologies such as OpenShift and/or AKS • Good understanding of infrastructure platforms, modern platform architectures and operational platform services • A structured, independent and solution-oriented way of working, combined with strong team spirit and good command of German and English Nice to Have • Experience with AI-related services or AI platform environments • Interest in the combination of AI enablement and infrastructure automation • Familiarity with shared platform services used by multiple teams • Exposure to observability, operational resilience and continuous improvement practices • Experience supporting the adoption of new platform capabilities in enterprise environments Why Join This Role • Opportunity to help shape an evolving role at the intersection of AI and platform engineering • Work on platforms and services with real impact across teams • Contribute to automation, standardization and scalable technical solutions • Collaborate with a broad network of infrastructure, development and project stakeholders Please note that applications submitted via recruitment agencies cannot be considered for this position.
