About Aware Health Everyday, 50% or more of the workforce show up to work with pain, joint stiffness, muscle strain, or other physical impairments due to musculoskeletal (MSK) and orthopedic conditions. Too often, these conditions are misdiagnosed and treated passively with opioids, injections or costly surgeries. These expensive and risky treatment methods can often be avoided altogether with more modern approaches to preventing, diagnosing and treating MSK pain. At Aware Health, our mission is to empower individuals to reach their goals through compassionate care, accurate diagnostics and precision treatment. We are a team of learners and collaborators driven by results and integrity. Our AI-powered platform ensures patients get the right care—avoiding inappropriate and frequently unnecessary tests, drugs, and surgeries. We are growing rapidly and looking for ambitious candidates who are excited to embrace the challenges of a startup environment while improving the health of millions of people. About the Job We are looking for a highly-motivated and hands-on Director of Engineering with experience working in a fast-paced startup environment and a track record of building reliable, scalable systems that power AI-driven experiences. Flexibility, proactivity, and a healthy sense of urgency are vital to this role. You will work closely with our cross-functional teams to design, engineer, and deliver technology solutions. You will need expertise in software engineering and system design, with proficiency in Golang (our main backend), and TypeScript. You have solid experience working with AI toolsets, LLMs, inference engineering, and building AI-powered solutions. Our tech stack includes Golang, TypesScript, Python, Postgres, BigQuery, LangChain, AWS, GCP, and AI/ML models. This is not a pure management role, and we are specifically looking for someone with the experience to manage development workflows and infrastructure decisions while remaining actively involved in the codebase and day-to-day technical execution. Your ability to be hands-on is important — we are a small but mighty engineering team, and everyone is responsible for delivering code, with the assistance of AI coding tools. That being said, you will be responsible for managing the quality and maintainability of our code, and minimize AI slop. Our culture is ambitious yet humble, and we care greatly about the impact we make. This is not a remote role—we are hiring only in the San Francisco Bay Area (or relocation) as we value in-person collaboration, requiring three days per week from our downtown San Francisco office. While we prioritize well-being, we are not a 9-to-5 culture, and at times critical deadlines may require extra effort. We balance high-impact work with time to recharge. We are looking for smart, driven people. Please consider applying if you believe you are a strong candidate, even if you don't check off all our requirements below. Direct applicants only — we are unable to respond to recruiters or agencies.
