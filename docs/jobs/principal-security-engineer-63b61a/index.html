Breadth, speed, and impact are part of what makes this role exciting. Deep expertise in security engineering, application security, and AI development, combined with strength in cloud security, identity and access management, cryptography, and a passion for supporting and developing others will be part of your success in this role. We are deeply AI-forward, and this role is a chance to reinvent how security is delivered across Remitly. If you are technically fearless and are passionate about shaping how financial services are secured in a rapidly changing world, we want you on our team. You Will: • Set and Demonstrate Technical Direction: Set the direction and demonstrate it through hands-on delivery of ambitious solutions across a breadth of security domains. Your success and the team’s will be intertwined. • Advise Leaders Across the Company: Serve as the trusted technical advisor to engineering leaders and executives through VP, at the intersection of security and software engineering. You will build trust across teams and set the standard for secure design. • Ship at an Uncommon Pace: You land production features and controls in your first month. By your first year, work that was once manual runs on automation the team trusts, and the paved roads you built are simply how new services get built. • Navigate Security and Business Enablement Tradeoffs: Balance security investment against the pace of innovation in a rapidly evolving technical field. Partner with leaders across the company to empower them to make principled security risk decisions.
