Senior Product Engineer - AI Decisioning Platforms (Full Stack)

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Product Engineer to build intuitive, trustworthy, and scalable user experiences for AI-powered claims and payments platforms. This role goes beyond traditional development and focuses on creating products that enable operational users to review, validate, override, and trust AI-generated decisions while maintaining complete auditability and regulatory compliance. You will work closely with product teams, end users, and AI systems to design and build user-facing applications, backend services, and decision-review workflows that support critical business operations. Key Responsibilities • Build and maintain user-facing applications for claims, payments, and AI-assisted operational workflows. • Design review and override interfaces enabling users to validate, approve, or reject AI-generated decisions. • Develop scalable frontend applications using React and TypeScript. • Build and maintain backend services using Python or Go. • Design robust APIs and data models supporting multiple clients and business use cases. • Collaborate directly with business users to understand workflows and improve product experiences. • Implement audit trails, decision tracking, and compliance-ready reporting capabilities. • Own production deployments, monitoring, troubleshooting, and on-call responsibilities. • Create automated tests, quality frameworks, and evaluation scenarios to ensure platform reliability. • Work with AI-assisted engineering tools and review AI-generated code for production readiness. • Improve accessibility and user experience in line with WCAG standards. • Support real-time workflows and state management for operational applications. Mandatory Requirements • 8+ years of experience in Full Stack Product Development. • Strong hands-on experience with React, TypeScript, and Modern JavaScript. • Experience building backend services using Python or Golang. • Deep understanding of API design, microservices architecture, and database design. • Experience with cloud-native applications, Kubernetes, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines. • Strong software testing practices including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing. • Experience building enterprise SaaS products or operational business applications. • Excellent stakeholder communication and user-focused product mindset. • Experience working with AI-enabled products or intelligent workflow systems.
