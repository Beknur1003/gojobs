Kubernetes Production Engineer - Python/Golang

Tenarai Bangalore опубликовано 31 июля 2026

KubernetesPythonJavaAI/LLM
Job Description: We are hiring Kubernetes Production Engineers to provide production support and operations for our Kubernetes platform (IKS). You will own the release and rollout of Kubernetes and its add-ons, keeping a business-critical platform reliable at scale. This role blends deep Kubernetes engineering with hands-on production ownership ideal for engineers who enjoy debugging hard problems and improving the systems that prevent them. Key Responsibilities: - Manage the release and rollout of Kubernetes versions and associated add-ons across environments. - Provide production support and operations for the Kubernetes platform. - Debug and resolve complex cluster, workload, networking, and add-on issues. - Automate operational workflows and strengthen platform tooling. - Contribute to runbooks, incident reviews, and reliability improvements. - Collaborate with global engineering teams on escalations and platform initiatives. Tech Stack: - Go (Golang) and Python - Kubernetes development, SRE, and debugging at depth - Cloud-native ecosystem: containers, operators, add-ons, observability tooling - Java (preferred) Required Skills & Experience: - 5-10 years of professional software or infrastructure engineering experience. - Proven, verifiable results operating large-scale production platforms e.g., measurable reliability improvements, reduced incident rates, or successful platform version rollouts. - Strong programming skills in Go and/or Python. - Strong expertise in Kubernetes development, SRE practices, and debugging. - Experience with managed Kubernetes services at scale. - Experience operating production infrastructure, including on-call participation. - Experience building and/or leveraging AI agents (e.g., agentic workflows, LLM-based automation, AI-assisted engineering tools) to improve productivity and outcomes. Preferred Qualifications: - Working knowledge of Java. What We Look For: - Strong sense of ownership - takes end-to-end responsibility for outcomes, not just tasks. - Self-motivated and proactive; able to operate independently with minimal supervision. - A good team player who collaborates effectively across teams, geographies, and time zones. - Proven track record of delivering measurable results in previous roles.
