This role drives automation excellence across the software development lifecycle, ensures high engineering quality, and enables teams to deliver software safely, efficiently, and at scale. Responsibilities • Lead the design, implementation, and optimization of end-to-end CI/CD pipelines, identifying opportunities for automation and continuous improvement. • Develop, maintain, and enhance complex automation scripts and tooling to meet evolving application and infrastructure requirements. • Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues arising from the integration and collaborative use of DevOps tools and platforms. • Collaborate closely with developers, architects, and infrastructure/hosting teams to design, maintain, and evolve DevOps workflows, platforms, and environments. • Ensure CI/CD pipelines adhere to deployment standards, release requirements, security controls, and monitoring guidelines. • Design, build, and maintain scalable, resilient application platforms with a strong developer-first mindset. • Take ownership of decisions related to application deployment strategies, automation standards, and platform reliability. • Support and maintain CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and application runtime environments. • Participate in follow-the-sun operations to ensure platform stability and availability across multiple regions. • Collaborate effectively across shifts and provide clear handoffs to ensure operational continuity. • Mentor and guide junior engineers, promoting best practices and continuous learning. • Work closely with cross-functional teams to drive high levels of collaboration and coordination (approximately 50% of the role). • Participate in follow‑the‑sun operations to ensure platform stability, availability, and operational continuity across multiple regions. Job Requirements Education Requirements • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
