Within our #OneTeam, you'll shape the automation-first framework that lets every product team ship securely and at speed on top of OPCP, our On-Prem Cloud Platform. Our Tech Stack 🚀 · Kubernetes · GitOps · FluxCD / ArgoCD · Ansible · Terraform · OpenStack · Linux · CI/CD · Python · Bash · Go · Observability (Metrics, Logs, Traces) · LLMs · Agentic Coding Tooling Your Role💻 As a Site Reliability Engineer, you'll work at the intersection of platform engineering, AI-driven tooling and security by design. Together with your team, you'll build the framework to package and deploy software solutions on top of OPCP - turning vague product ideas into repeatable, observable and self-healing services. You'll work on the conception, automation and operation of the platform, drive continuous improvements and help new teams onboard with the framework and OPCP. Working hand in hand with developers, security specialists and AI researchers, you keep an overview in ambiguous situations, analyze systems and make well-founded decisions based on that analysis. AI-assisted engineering is part of our daily toolchain because it materially improves how we build and operate the platform – from spec-driven development with LLM assistance and knowledge-base-driven retrieval for incident response and design to agentic coding workflows and faster runbook authoring and review. We are investing in this direction as a structural part of our engineering practice and are looking for teammates who already work this way – not because it is trendy, but because they have seen it compound their own output. Your Tasks • Design, build and maintain the Cloud Store packaging framework that standardizes how software is delivered on OPCP. • Extend and evolve the AI substrate - LLM-driven code assistants, knowledge-base retrieval and agentic coding hooks - that amplifies our engineering output. • Create and curate agentic coding environments: custom agents and sub-agents, slash commands, hooks and prompt engineering pipelines that become everyday developer tools. • Own the knowledge-base layer that powers AI-driven incident response, design assistance and runbook generation. • Drive our Kubernetes stack forward and implement robust GitOps pipelines (FluxCD / ArgoCD) for continuous delivery. • Write, publish and continuously improve OPCP onboarding runbooks and best-practice guides for internal product teams. • Analyze opaque system behavior, surface root causes and deliver concrete, data-backed improvement proposals - even when initial requirements are unclear. • Develop, version and operate the Infrastructure as Code stack (Terraform, Ansible) that underpins the platform, and take part in our on-call rotation.
