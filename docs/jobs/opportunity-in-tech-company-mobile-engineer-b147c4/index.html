About the role: This opportunity gives you the chance to develop, maintain, and distribute our mobile apps, which are our core product, interacting with a deep tech problem directly in the application. We are looking for long-term players, professionals who are looking to grow and evolve with us over time. Additionally, individuals who have worked at product-focused companies tend to stand out, since they usually bring a deep understanding of building and scaling solutions with the end user in mind. Lastly, experience in start-up environments is highly regarded, as it often reflects adaptability, initiative, and the ability to thrive in dynamic, fast-paced settings. What you’ll do: • Collaborate with product, design, and other engineers for new features. • Design and implement key functionality in the apps. • Develop tooling to improve our development practices. • Participate in code reviews to raise the quality of our code and our product. • Own your code in production. • Work with our CX team when users are having trouble with the app. What you’ll bring or Qualifications: • Expertise in mobile SDKs and application development, including common design patterns. • Our team possesses cross-team collaboration skills, and we are highly cross-functional, bringing everyone to the table to address our problems. • A beginner’s mindset, always striving to understand. • Own and solve problems. • Willing and able to work on open problems. • Being open to solving whatever needs to be solved. Our Tech Stack • iOS: Swift, UIKit, RxSwift, Golang. • Android: Kotlin, RxJava, Golang Interview Process • Silver Screening Interview. • Silver Technical Interview. • Client Screening Interview. • TakeHome Challenge. • DeepDive Interview • Behavioral Interview.
