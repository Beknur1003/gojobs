Lead Golang Platform Engineer

OmneNEST Technologies Mumbai опубликовано 23 октября 2026

We are looking for a Lead Golang Platform Engineer to own the stability, performance, and reliability of mission-critical backend platforms supporting real-time market data, transactions, portfolio systems, and enterprise integrations. This is a hands-on technical leadership role focused on distributed systems, production incident management, performance optimization, and platform ownership rather than feature development alone. Responsibilities: • Own production stability and act as the L3/L4 escalation point for backend and middleware systems. • Lead root cause analysis (RCA) for production incidents, latency spikes, Kafka lag, database contention, and memory issues. • Drive non-functional requirements (NFRs), including performance, throughput, high availability, fault tolerance, and resource efficiency. • Design and optimize distributed systems and event-driven microservices using Golang, Kafka, PostgreSQL, and Redis. • Ensure reliable event processing through idempotency, offset management, retries, dead-letter queues, event replay, and backpressure handling. • Optimize PostgreSQL queries, indexing, partitioning, connection pooling, and Redis caching for high-performance workloads. • Build configurable, rule-driven workflows and high-performance backend services. • Improve observability through logging, tracing, metrics, Prometheus, and Grafana. • Lead vendor knowledge transfer, take ownership of legacy codebases, and establish backend engineering standards. • Mentor engineers on production debugging, distributed systems, and code quality. Requirements: • 6-8+ years of backend engineering experience, preferably in fintech, trading, or transaction-heavy systems. • Strong hands-on expertise in Golang, including concurrency, context propagation, runtime behavior, and memory profiling. • Strong production experience with Apache Kafka, including consumer groups, rebalancing, offset management, and lag diagnostics. • Proven expertise in PostgreSQL performance tuning and database optimization. • Experience troubleshooting live production incidents and driving permanent corrective actions. • Strong understanding of distributed systems, event-driven architecture, fault tolerance, and high-throughput APIs. • Demonstrated ownership of non-functional requirements (NFRs), performance benchmarks, and production reliability. Good To Have: • Experience with low-latency trading platforms, exchanges, or regulated financial systems. • Production exposure to Kubernetes and Redis performance optimization. • Experience with WebSockets, streaming systems, ClickHouse, and Grafana. • Familiarity with CockroachDB or other distributed databases. • Experience taking over vendor-built or legacy platforms. Ideal Candidate: • Someone who can remain calm during critical production incidents, diagnose complex distributed-system issues, optimize runtime performance, and take end-to-end ownership of platform reliability while providing technical guidance to the backend team.
