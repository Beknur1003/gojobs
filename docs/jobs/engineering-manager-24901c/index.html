The Engineering Manager oversees the development of seamless collaborative applications, both cloud-connected SaaS and customer managed single tenant products. This role serves as a technical hands-on leader working with various cross functional members of our product-engineering organization and from time-to-time interacting with other key members throughout the Bluebeam organization. • Leads team(s) throughout software development lifecycle. • Recruits, mentors, coaches, retains, inspires engineering individual contributors and tech leads on a day-to-day basis. • Sets and tracks goals and objectives with each assigned team member. • Participates and assists with expense approval and budget planning. • Creatively solves problems and clear roadblocks that often cross technical and human factor domains. • Conducts and attends meetings with key stakeholders to present facts that lead to quality decisions. • Communicates effectively and provides clarity in a 360 fashion. • Collaborates with architects, tech leads, product delivery managers, product managers, engineers, and quality engineering to build and deliver excellence • 12+ years in industry with progressive growth in responsibility • 4+ years technical leadership and managing teams of 5 or more engineers • Strong leadership, character, high emotional intelligence, detail oriented organizational skills • Strong growth minded leader, with a resilient will who embrace change and learn quickly • Must have Amazon AWS experience (EC2, S3, SQS, SNS, Kinesis, ELB, Lambda) experience with service/microservices oriented computing architecture in a containerized environment. • Experience implementing infrastructure as code, including building and integrating into CI/CD pipelines. (Prioritizing candidates with Terraform and Jenkins experience) • Strong skills in at least one programming language: GoLang, C# on .Net, Python • Strong knowledge of REST API and web protocol and technologies (HTTPS, CORS, Postman, JSON, XML.) • Familiarity with cloud native technology and cloud transformation landscape.
