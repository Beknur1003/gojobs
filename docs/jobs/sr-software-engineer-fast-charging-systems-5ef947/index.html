Sr. Software Engineer - Fast Charging Systems

Electric Era Technologies Seattle, WA опубликовано 19 ноября 2026

Senior
LinuxPythonC++TypeScript
The Job & Role: We do things differently at Electric Era. If you’ve ever wanted to really own software engineering design decisions and take control of your designs all the way through to implementation we have that here. Everyone, from our junior engineers to our CEO, has a strong ownership mentality. We have a small, lean, hard-charging team; we can’t afford not to have people obsessed with owning engineering outcomes. If you want your stuff to see the light of day fast, we have that here. No miles of red tape or bureaucracy. If it’s important, it happens. If it’s easily messed up, it’s simplified, then given a process. If the process is onerous, it’s deleted. We don’t have time to sit around, so you can guarantee that things move fast. The best idea wins; everyone can and is expected to contribute to good system design, to challenge bad requirements and decision decisions, and always move the puck forward. Electric Era is looking for strong software engineers excited about solving this industry’s most difficult problem that want everything described above. You will be part of a world-class team that owns the complete product life cycle deliverable from ideation, development, implementation, testing and troubleshooting real problems in the field. The Responsibilities: • Developing the interoperability stack that allows our charging stations to communicate with every electric vehicle, so that vehicles successfully charge every time • Designing, building, and supporting software architectures across our interoperability stack, including but not limited to development in: • Charger-to-vehicle communication protocols (such as ISO 15118 and DIN 70121) • Charger-to-backend communication protocols (such as OCPP) • Security infrastructure for vehicle and backend communication, including PKI and certificate management • Inter-device communication methodologies (using CAN, Ethernet, and serial protocols) • Telemetry systems to enable remote observability of every charging session • Build observability tools that capture, decode, and analyze charging session data so the team can remotely diagnose and root-cause any failed or degraded charging session • Build test infrastructure for unit, integration, and hardware-in-the-loop testing of the interoperability stack, including automated regression testing against a wide range of vehicle behaviors • Plan and execute interoperability testing with vehicles and partners, and work closely with hardware engineers to turn findings from the field into fixes • Take ownership of formal code review, test, and acceptance criteria for interoperability software • Manage software deliverables through production and into the field, participating in on-call rotations and supporting your product in a real-world environment Preferred Qualifications & Skills: • 5 to 7+ years of experience in software engineering development, preferably in fields where managing complex software systems with unpredictable behaviors or ambiguous specification • Experience developing mission-critical software for IoT or cloud services, or embedded Linux and networked device software • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field • Demonstrated track record showcasing end-to-end ownership of business-critical software products through a production lifecycle • Development experience in C, C++, Python, Golang, or TypeScript • Experience building observability, logging, or diagnostic tooling for systems deployed in the field • Experience building test infrastructure, including automated, hardware-in-the-loop, or protocol conformance testing • Experience debugging network and protocol communication issues using packet capture and analysis tools • Understanding of security infrastructure, including PKI and certificate management for vehicle and backend communication, is a plus • Capability to support operations on-call rotations as needed • Strong verbal and written communication skills Compensation Salary Range (level-dependent): • Senior: $160k to $200k + equity • Staff: $180k to $220k + equity
