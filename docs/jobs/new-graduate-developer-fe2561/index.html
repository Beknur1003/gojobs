About this opportunity Have you ever wondered how your mobile app messages reach their destination? Your phone connects to the cell tower and then what? This is where the Transport Network comes into play. We are developing a cloud-native transport network automation product that uses AI and machine learning to provide advanced analytics and automation for IP, microwave, and optical fronthaul networks. We are looking for a recent graduate or early-career developer with a strong technical foundation, interest in modern software development, and motivation to learn quickly in an experienced agile team. You will join a team that builds automation for real network use cases. The work connects cloud-native software, test automation, data, and AI-supported developer workflows. What you will do You will design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software for our network automation product. As part of an agile team, you will solve technical problems, improve product quality, and build solutions that meet customer needs. You will use AI-augmented development in a practical and responsible way. This may include AI coding assistants, agentic workflows, and AI support for design, testing, debugging, documentation, and developer workflow automation. We believe you have a curious mind, good communication skills, and a strong drive to learn. You are ready to share information, ask questions, and improve your skills together with the team. You will bring • MSc in Computer Science, Communication Engineering, Security and Cloud Computing, or a related technical field, or close to graduation • Good programming skills in at least one language, such as Golang, TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, or a similar language • Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, databases, and software design principles • Interest in test automation and product quality • Practical experience with AI-augmented development, such as coding assistants, prompt-based problem solving, AI-supported testing, or AI-supported documentation • Understanding of agentic workflows, including how software agents can use tools, follow steps, call APIs, and support multi-step development tasks • Interest in the wider technology space and motivation to keep learning as tools, platforms, and AI capabilities change • Initiative, teamwork skills, and enthusiasm for continuous learning and professional development Seen as an advantage • Hands-on experience with AI development tools, retrieval-based AI solutions, tool-calling workflows, or software agents • Experience of at least one of the following libraries: Angular, ReactJS, or React Native • Experience in HTML, REST APIs, CSS, and its preprocessors • Experience with Robot Framework, other test automation frameworks, or automated quality checks Note: We are working in a hybrid setup, combining office days in Jorvas, Finland, with remote working days.
