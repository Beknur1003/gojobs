Senior Engineering Manager - Data

LSEG Bengaluru, India опубликовано 3 ноября 2026

Гибрид Senior
PostgreSQLTerraformAWSCI/CDPythonRustJavaC++FinTech
## ROLE SUMMARY Are you a hands-on engineer who raises the bar for everyone around you? Join us at FTSE Russell! We're establishing a team of engineers passionate about software craftsmanship, quality, and substantial impact. As a Senior Engineering Manager, you'll own the technical direction of the software that powers the indices trusted by financial institutions worldwide. We build primarily in Python, so that's our preference — but depth of engineering judgement matters more to us than the language on your CV. If you've led the design and delivery of serious systems in Java, C#, C, C++, Scala, Go, or Rust and you're motivated to lead in Python, we'd like to talk. At this level we expect you to become fluent quickly and to bring the cross-language perspective with you. This is a hands-on(60%) + People manager role (40%) — You'll still be writing and reviewing code every week. What changes at this level is scope: you own architecture and technical direction in a defined area, you mentor and grow the engineers around you, and you raise quality across the team through reuse, good design, and hands-on support. The systems handle serious volumes of financial data, so there's real depth in the data problems — but you're being hired as a software engineer who leads through code. If you're excited about building systems that matter and making the engineers around you better, we'd love to meet you. ## WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING We're building the next-generation index data platform at FTSE Russell — and we want you to shape it with us: - Staying hands-on — designing and writing production code, primarily in Python with 10 +yrs of experience , and setting the standard for what good looks like through the code you ship - Owning technical direction and architecture for a defined area of the platform, and making the trade-offs explicit so the team can move fast with confidence - Leading design of software systems — services, APIs, shared libraries, and the interfaces between them — with resilience, performance, and reusability designed in - Driving engineering quality across the team: raising the bar in code review, reducing duplication through well-designed shared components, and championing testing, refactoring, and secure development practices - Mentoring and coaching engineers — pairing, reviewing, and growing capability so that good practice outlives any one project - Digging into design and risk trade-offs — failure modes, operational readiness, security, and cost — and resolving the blockers that slow the team down - Designing data-intensive software across relational databases (Aurora PostgreSQL), object storage, and AWS services, with careful attention to schema design and evolution at scale - Building software that processes data at scale on AWS — Lambda, containerised services, and where appropriate Glue, EMR Serverless, and lakehouse table formats such as Apache Iceberg - Embedding observability and quality controls so issues are found before customers find them - Influencing and collaborating across teams, and translating between technical and non-technical audiences - Building deep knowledge of the financial indices domain and using it to guide team-level design ## WHAT YOU'LL BRING Essential — this is the bar: - A substantial track record of building and shipping software. Applications, services, platforms, or libraries you designed, built, ran in production, and evolved over years — not just pipelines and scripts - Python is our preference, but not a hard gate at this level either. Either you already lead in production Python, or you have equivalent depth in Java, C#, C, C++, Scala, Go, or Rust and are motivated to make Python your primary language here. Engineers who have worked across several languages often bring the sharpest design judgement, and we value that - Depth in software design and architecture — you can decompose a complex problem, choose the right boundaries, and explain why. You know which patterns earn their keep and which are cargo cult - You write and review excellent code. In Python: type hints, linters, strong pytest discipline, and an instinct for readable, maintainable code — and the equivalent rigour in any other language you've worked in - You are genuinely hands-on and intend to stay that way. Leading through code is the point of this role - Proven experience mentoring and levelling up other engineers — through code review, pairing, design guidance, and setting standards - Strong ownership of technical outcomes — you take responsibility for what production does, not just what merges - Deep experience with relational databases (e.g. Aurora PostgreSQL) — modelling, performance, and evolution under real load - Solid AWS experience and strong CI/CD, automation, and testing practice - Comfort with data-intensive systems — batch processing, ETL, schema evolution, and reasoning about correctness at volume - Excellent communication — you tailor how you explain design decisions to technical and non-technical audiences and create shared understanding - You value collaboration over solo heroics and thrive in Agile teams ## NICE TO HAVES Beneficial, but not what we're screening on: - Apache Spark / PySpark and experience optimising large-scale distributed processing - Big data experience — designing systems for datasets and workloads well beyond single-machine scale - Breadth across languages used in data-intensive and performance-critical systems — Scala, Java, C++, Go, or Rust — and the judgement to know when reaching for one is warranted - Apache Iceberg, Delta Lake, or similar open table formats, and lakehouse architecture - Databricks, Snowflake, or comparable data platforms - CI/CD tooling such as GitLab CI, Jenkins, or GitHub Actions - Infrastructure as code (Terraform, CDK) and platform/DevOps exposure - Data quality frameworks like Great Expectations or Deequ - Experience in financial markets, index data, or investment analytics - Experience in finance domain (investment banking, asset/portfolio management) or in any product company --- Join us and be part of a team that values innovation, quality, and continuous improvement.
