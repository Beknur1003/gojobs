Job Description Summary Job Description Summary GE HealthCare is looking for a talented and motivated Software Engineer to join our Advanced Visualization team. This team develops core services that enable our products to run cutting-edge medical applications. As a Software Engineer, you will be part of an Agile Scrum team, contributing to feature development, automated testing, verification campaigns, bug fixes, and documentation. Job Description Roles and Responsibilities In this role, you will: • Take responsibility for delivering medium or high-complexity feature tasks within agreed timelines. • Ensure that all delivered code meets GE Healthcare quality standards and adheres to established best practices. • Contribute to automated testing by developing tests for assigned components and end-to-end workflows. • Participate in software delivery within a global environment. • Participate in documentation activities for quality management purposes. • Break down problems and estimate time required. • Understand performance parameters and application performance. • Work closely with Product Owner (PO) and architects to understand product requirements & vision. • Proactively share information across the team, to the right audience with the appropriate level of detail and timeliness. Qualifications / Requirements Education Qualification • Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science or “STEM” Majors (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) with minimum 2 years of experience Technical Expertise: • Hands-on experience with programming languages utilized in the product, with a focus on those used for the components owned by the team at the level of ability for delivery independently (mainly Java) • Experience with Unix / Linux side programming • Understand the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) process, including key deliverables and the ability to plan and organize tasks accordingly Differentiators: • Knowledge in C++ or GOLang • Bash or Python knowledge • Experience with Docker/Kubernetes • Experience in creating automated tests Personal Attributes: • Has the ability to break down problems and estimate time for development tasks. • Ability to take ownership of small and medium sized tasks and deliver those in time. • Open to continuous learning and growth. • Flexible mindset and ability to adapt. • Strong team player with good collaboration skills. • Proactive, self-confident, and motivated. • Possess a solution-oriented mindset with strong critical thinking abilities. • Proactive in expressing ideas and speaking up confidently. • Good command of English (written and spoken) • Learns organization vision statement and decision making framework. Able to understand how team and personal goals/objectives contribute to the organization vision Additional Information Relocation Assistance Provided: No
