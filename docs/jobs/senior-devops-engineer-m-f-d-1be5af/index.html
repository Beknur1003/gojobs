Want to build and operate reliable platforms that help teams deliver great products? You’ll join a collaborative engineering team working at the intersection of development, operations, and architecture. As a Senior DevOps Engineer (m/f/d), you will help shape our cloud platform, solve complex technical problems, and turn ambiguous challenges into practical, scalable solutions. You will work closely with engineers and stakeholders across the domain and help raise our engineering standards through knowledge sharing and mentoring. • Architecture and ownership: You contribute to architectural decisions, address technical debt, and make pragmatic trade-offs across reliability, scalability, security, and cost. • Reliable platforms: You design, build, and operate secure, scalable, and maintainable cloud platforms and services. • Automation and delivery: You improve automation, infrastructure, deployment processes, observability, and operational efficiency. • Distributed systems: You work with distributed software, event-driven architectures, and event streaming. • Incremental delivery: You break complex initiatives into smaller workstreams and deliver value step by step. • Root-cause problem solving: You investigate recurring issues and create lasting solutions instead of relying on short-term fixes. • Team enablement: You share knowledge, mentor less experienced engineers, and promote collaboration, transparency, and shared responsibility. • Professional experience: You have several years of experience in DevOps, platform engineering, SRE, or a related field. • AWS expertise: You have strong hands-on experience with AWS and cloud-native infrastructure. • Go experience: You have professional experience with Go (Golang), ideally for automation, platform tooling, or backend services. • Distributed systems: You understand distributed systems, asynchronous processing, and event streaming. • Engineering excellence: You are experienced with automation, infrastructure as code, continuous delivery, observability, security, and reliability. • Technical leadership: You can make sound architectural decisions and explain technical trade-offs clearly • Delivery mindset: You have experience leading technical initiatives and delivering complex work incrementally. • Collaboration and mentoring: You enjoy working with others, sharing knowledge, and supporting the growth of your colleagues. • Ownership and learning: You take responsibility for outcomes and continuously look for better long-term solutions. • Flexibility for Your Lifestyle: Enjoy family-friendly working hours and a generous home office policy, allowing you to stay agile and flexible in any situation. • Ergonomic Work Environment: For your office days, we provide ergonomic workstations that offer you a comfortable and healthy workspace. • Independent Work: With short decision-making paths, we enable you to work autonomously and actively contribute your ideas – we provide space for you to take on responsibility. • Grow with Us: Unlock your potential with numerous opportunities for growth and development, along with an annual development budget to help you achieve your professional and personal goals. • Team Spirit: Team spirit is important to us – we regularly host events and parties where fun is guaranteed. • Mobility Your Way: Whether you prefer a train ticket or parking – we support your choice of preferred mobility. • Stay Active: Keep fit with a discounted membership at Fitness First or Urban Sports Club, or use our in-house fitness room to stay active after work and enhance your work-life balance. • Secure Your Future: Think about your future – we offer subsidies for company pension plans so you can plan long-term with us. • Tailored Benefits: We consider your personal life situation – whether you're a parent or love to travel, we offer customized benefits to suit your lifestyle. Your Perspective: You will help shape how we build and run reliable technology at auxmoney. Your work will enable teams to deliver faster and more safely while improving platform reliability, maintainability, and cost efficiency. You will have the autonomy to own meaningful technical decisions, lead challenging initiatives, and make a lasting impact through better systems, better processes, and stronger engineering teams. For us as an employer, equal opportunities and diversity are especially important. Therefore, we welcome applications from mothers, fathers, people with disabilities and people from the LGBTQIA+ community. Please feel free to let us know if, for example, you would like us to use a gender-neutral pronoun, if you need barrier-free access to our office, or if you would like us to allow more time for the application process.
