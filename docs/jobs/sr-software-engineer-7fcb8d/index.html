We're seeking a passionate Sr Backend Software Engineer to play a key role in designing, developing, and maintaining the Access Review feature, which is an innovative addition to ServiceNow’s Veza’s IAM platform. In this role, you'll work closely with cross-functional teams to build scalable, robust solutions that address the evolving needs of our customers. This is an exciting opportunity to make a significant impact, while working with the latest technologies in identity and access management. You will: • Design, develop, and maintain backend services and APIs for our IAM platform, ensuring they deliver high performance, scalability, and reliability. • Collaborate with product managers, software engineers, UX designers, and security experts to build features that align with business goals and meet security requirements. • Lead the development and maintenance of the Access Review feature, a cutting-edge addition to our IAM platform. • Build and manage identity lifecycle management features, including user provisioning, de-provisioning, and access control policies. • Implement logg ing, monitoring, and auditing functionality to ensure platform visibility and compliance with regulatory standards. • Continuously enhance the performance, security, and scalability of the IAM platform through code reviews, testing, and refactoring. • Stay up-to-date with industry trends and emerging technologies in identity and access management, leveraging them to drive innovation and elevate the platform. • 7+ years of proven experience in backend software development, with a focus on identity and access management, security, or related areas. • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. • Strong proficiency in object-oriented programming languages such as Golang and Java. • Experience designing and implementing RESTful APIs and microservices architectures. • Solid understanding of authentication and authorization protocols, standards, and best practices. • Experience with cloud platforms, such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform, and familiarity with IAM services and features. • Experience with containerization and orchestration technologies, such as Docker and Kubernetes, is a plus. • Knowledge of database technologies (SQL and NoSQL), caching mechanisms, and distributed systems. • Excellent problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively communicate technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
