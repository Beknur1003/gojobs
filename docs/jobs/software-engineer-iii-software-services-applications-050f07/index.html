Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential. About Agility Robotics Agility Robotics builds Digit — the first humanoid robot doing real, paid work in warehouses and distribution centers today. Our mission is to build robot partners that augment the human workforce, and Digit is already working alongside people at real customer sites through our Agility Partner Program. Humanoid robotics is one of the most closely watched spaces in tech right now, and very few engineering teams get to write the software that commands and manages a fleet of humanoid robots operating in the real world. About the Team You'll join the Software Services (backend) team, part of the broader Applications & Platform Software Engineering organization building Arc — our cloud application for robot fleet management, robot operations, and fleet data collection. The team spans Senior Staff to SWE III, so you'll have direct access to experienced engineers as you ramp up: you'll get an assigned mentor for your first 90 days, and after that you'll work closely with whichever senior or staff engineer is leading your project. What You'll Do • Build backend APIs and services for Arc, our cloud-based robot fleet management platform. • Own well-scoped features end-to-end, and contribute to larger ones alongside senior engineers. • Turn product requirements into technical designs — write design docs, build proof-of-concepts, and ship code. • Help redesign our system and refactor services as we scale and evolve. • Work cross-functionally with product, UX, front-end, robot hardware/software, customer solutions, and operations teams. • Support what you build through its full lifecycle, including a flexible, team-designed on-call rotation (roughly once every 3-4 weeks). Technical Skills & Requirements Core Requirements • BS or MS in computer science or related field or equivalent industry experience. • At least 4 years of experience in a software engineer role. • Strong proficiency in Java and Python and Rust or Go. Our backend is mostly in Java and Python right now but we are looking at adopting Rust or Go. • Experience developing backend cloud systems and associated concepts, frameworks and technologies - application frameworks (Spring Boot), databases (SQL and NoSQL), messaging systems (Kafka, MQTT), APIs (REST, gRPC) and data formats (Json, Protobuf). • Experience with AWS - DynamoDB, RDS (Postgres), S3, SQS / SNS. • Experience with SDLC processes, practices, workflows and tools - Github, CI/CD processes, Testing approaches (unit tests, end to end tests), deployments, feature flags, Docker, Kubernetes etc. • Experience starting with feature requirements and working with them towards end-to-end solutions. • Experience working in fast paced environments sometimes with changing priorities. Bonus Qualifications • Experience with Robotics. • Experience with Fleet Management applications and Cloud - Edge communications. This a hybrid position based out of one of our Salem, Pittsburgh, or Fremont offices.
