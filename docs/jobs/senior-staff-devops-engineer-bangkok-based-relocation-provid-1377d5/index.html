About Agoda At Agoda, we bridge the world through travel. Our story began in 2005, when two lifelong friends and entrepreneurs, driven by their passion for travel, launched Agoda to make it easier for everyone to explore the world. Today, we are part of Booking Holdings [NASDAQ: BKNG], with a diverse team of over 7,000 people from 90 countries, working together in offices around the globe. Every day, we connect people to destinations and experiences, with our great deals across our millions of hotels and holiday properties, flights, and experiences worldwide. No two days are the same at Agoda. Data and technology are at the heart of our culture, fueling our curiosity and innovation. If you’re ready to begin your best journey and help build travel for the world, join us. The Opportunity Agoda’s DevOps Engineering team builds developer platforms, automation, and internal tools that improve engineering productivity and security across Agoda’s R&D organization. We’re looking for a strong software developer with solid DevOps expertise—someone who enjoys writing code, designing systems, and building tools that make software delivery faster, more reliable, and more secure. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with technical ownership across software development and DevOps. In this role, you’ll get to: • Build developer platforms, internal tools, and web portals across the full SDLC. • Scale CI/CD and security automation across approximately 1 million pipelines per month. • Own core developer systems, including source control, CI, service discovery, and deployment platforms. • Design highly available, fault-tolerant systems and improve developer experience. • Drive technical decisions and engineering best practices as a hands-on individual contributor. What you’ll need to succeed: • 5+ years in Software Engineering, DevOps, or Platform Engineering, including several years in a senior or principal capacity. • Strong software development skills across the full SDLC, preferably using Go or Python. • Experience building software systems, internal platforms, or developer tools. • Solid CI/CD, DevOps, containerization, and cloud-native knowledge, including Git, Docker, and Kubernetes. • Strong system design and Linux administration skills. • Autonomous, data-driven decision-making and ownership of outcomes. • A passion for learning new technologies and automating large-scale processes. It’s great if you have: • Built developer platforms or engineering productivity tools. • Developed full-stack applications using React.js and Go. • Worked with Ansible, OpenStack, Consul, or Vault. • Used SQL/NoSQL databases such as PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, or MSSQL. • Improved observability, reliability, security automation, or developer experience. #bangkok @#thailand Please review our Hiring Process Guidelines before your interview — click here to learn how interviewing at Agoda works.
