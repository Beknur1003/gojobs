ABOUT LUXCLUSIF is a technology-driven global B2B platform founded in 2013 and part of the Farfetch Group since 2021. Dedicated to redefining the luxury paradigm by offering premium pre-owned fashion to a global audience, Luxclusif has pioneered the global luxury resale market with advanced data-driven solutions to facilitate the buying and selling of high-end pre-owned items. With operations spread across the globe and strategic warehouses in key locations, we ensure a seamless supply of luxury goods, handling everything from item curation, quality control, and operations logistics to technology integrations and sales, delivering an unmatched resale experience for brands, retailers, and end-customers. TECHNOLOGY Our Tech Team is responsible for designing, developing, and evolving the internal products that power our business. As a B2B company specialising in second-hand luxury items, we rely on technology to optimise every stage of our operations, from buying and inbound logistics to selling and post-sales support. We are a global team with members across Portugal, Brazil, and the Philippines. PORTO Our office is near Porto, in the north of Portugal, and is located in a vibrant business hub. It offers a dynamic and welcoming environment where our employees can connect and network with a large community of tech professionals. THE ROLE As a Senior Infrastructure Engineer in the Tech Platform team, you will play a critical role in building the foundation of our entire technology ecosystem. Your mission is to engineer a seamless, scalable, and reliable platform that empowers engineering teams to deliver software faster and with higher quality. In this role, you will be a key technical contributor dedicated to evolving our cloud-native infrastructure. You will focus on driving the adoption of Self-Service capabilities and Infrastructure as Code to reduce cognitive load for our developers, while maintaining a high bar for security, resilience, and operational excellence. WHAT YOU'LL DO • Design and build robust cloud-native infrastructure, focusing on automation, scalability, and high availability for our core systems. • Operate and maintain a wide range of infrastructure components, including Linux and Kubernetes-based systems, using automated processes and pipelines. • Drive the creation of self-service capabilities, ensuring that the infrastructure layers we build empower engineering teams across the company to be autonomous. • Ensure the reliability, performance, and scalability of large-scale distributed systems, guaranteeing the platform is robust enough for high-traffic events. • Contribute to technical strategy and roadmaps, ensuring the platform's evolution aligns with global company goals and delivers consistent value. • Act as a key technical partner for Principals, Engineering Leads, and developers, translating business needs into scalable technical solutions to accelerate global productivity. • Identify opportunities to optimize cost efficiency and reliability through automated processes and modern infrastructure patterns. • Provide technical mentorship and guidance to other engineers, fostering a culture of technical excellence, psychological safety, and continuous learning. WHO YOU ARE • You have a proven track record of designing, building, and maintaining complex platform infrastructure and distributed systems. • You possess a strong technical background in multi-cloud infrastructure (e.g., Azure, GCP), container orchestration (Kubernetes), and modern CI/CD patterns (ArgoCD, Jenkins, etc), allowing you to make and guide complex technical decisions. • You understand the software development lifecycle deeply and have hands-on experience with development languages (e.g., Python or Golang) to effectively build tools for other developers. • You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a relentless focus on continuous system improvement and automation. • You have exceptional interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively and influence technical stakeholders across the organization. • You prioritize security and compliance as core pillars of a reliable platform.
