Role Overview : As a Full Stack Software Engineer, you will take ownership of the end-to-end development lifecycle, bridging the gap between sophisticated backend architecture and intuitive frontend interfaces. You will work closely with cross-functional product and design teams to translate business requirements into high-quality, maintainable code. Key Responsibilities : - Architect and implement scalable backend services using Golang to ensure high availability and low latency for enterprise-grade applications. - Develop responsive, user-centric frontend components using React.js to provide seamless interactions across various devices. - Optimize database queries and API integrations to improve overall system performance and data retrieval efficiency. - Collaborate with product managers and stakeholders to define technical specifications and deliver features that meet evolving business needs. - Conduct thorough code reviews and implement automated testing protocols to maintain high standards of code quality and system reliability. - Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical bottlenecks within the production environment to minimize downtime and enhance user satisfaction. Required Skillset : - Demonstrated expertise in building high-concurrency backend systems using Golang and managing complex state in frontend applications with React.js. - Proven ability to design and consume RESTful APIs and integrate them with modern database technologies. - Strong analytical skills to deconstruct complex business problems into modular, efficient technical solutions. - Excellent communication abilities to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders and work effectively within a distributed team. - Ability to thrive in a remote-first or flexible work environment, maintaining high levels of productivity and self-discipline. - A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field, supported by 5 - 8 years of professional experience in full-stack development.
