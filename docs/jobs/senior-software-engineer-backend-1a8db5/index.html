Get to Know the Role You will work with other experienced engineers on the team to develop our backend services, ensure the robustness and of the system which provides APIs used by several mobile and web app. You will be reporting to an engineering manager who is based in Vietnam. You will work onsite in CMC Creative Space office. The Critical Tasks You Will Perform • You will work with the product/design/business team to understand requirements, provide product feedback, own and implement the product from start to finish. • You will create and lead the technical discussions within engineering teams • You will write code that is reusable, scalable and testable.
