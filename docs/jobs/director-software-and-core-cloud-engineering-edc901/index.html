Director - Software and Core Cloud Engineering

Careernet Bengaluru опубликовано 5 ноября 2026

Офис Полная занятость

Key Skills: AWS Cloud, Databricks, Python, Golang, Snowflake, Data Engineering, Multi-Cloud Management, People Management, Customer Strategy Roles and Responsibilities: • Lead three to four engineering squads, develop senior technical talent and establish a high-performance engineering culture. • Define and execute the multi-year roadmap for multi-cloud management, translating platform strategy into quarterly plans, OKRs and measurable outcomes. • Own cloud governance capabilities including access management, entitlement controls, policy guardrails, account lifecycle and resource attribution. • Drive cloud economics initiatives spanning cost attribution, unit economics, FinOps tooling, rightsizing automation and optimization feedback loops. • Set engineering standards for reliable, secure and observable platform services, including SLO-based operations, operational reviews and blameless post-mortems. Skills Required: • 17 to 25 years of software and cloud engineering experience, including substantial engineering leadership experience at Director level or equivalent. • Deep AWS Cloud engineering experience with the architectural depth to engage principal engineers on enterprise-scale cloud platforms. • Strong background in Multi-Cloud Management, cloud governance, entitlement systems and distributed platform architecture. • Proficiency in Golang and Python, with the ability to review production code, infrastructure changes and security-sensitive platform designs. • Experience delivering Data Engineering platforms and working with Databricks and Snowflake in large-scale enterprise environments. Good to Have: • People Management experience across managers, principals and senior engineering teams. • Customer Strategy experience involving platform consumers, product leadership and global technology stakeholders. Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or an equivalent technical discipline.
