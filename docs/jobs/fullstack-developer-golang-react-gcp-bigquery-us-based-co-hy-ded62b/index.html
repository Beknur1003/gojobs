Job Overview We are looking for an experienced Full Stack Developer with 6-7 years of hands-on software development experience to join our engineering team. The ideal candidate should have strong expertise in Golang, React.js, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and BigQuery, along with a solid understanding of modern product development practices. The candidate will be responsible for developing scalable and reliable applications, understanding business requirements, collaborating closely with customers and cross-functional teams, and contributing throughout the Product Development Life Cycle (PDLC). Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable full-stack applications using Golang and React.js. • Develop robust backend services, APIs, and microservices using Golang. • Build responsive and user-friendly web applications using React.js. • Design and implement data solutions using Google BigQuery. • Develop, deploy, and manage applications and services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Participate in the complete Product Development Life Cycle, including requirement analysis, design development, testing, deployment, and production support. • Work directly with customers to understand business needs, technical challenges, and expectations. • Conduct effective requirement gathering and analysis and translate business requirements into clear technical specifications. • Collaborate with Product Managers, Architects, QA, DevOps, and other engineering teams to deliver high-quality products. • Take ownership of technical solutions and drive features from conception to production. • Troubleshoot production issues, identify root causes, and implement long-term solutions. • Participate in code reviews and ensure adherence to coding standards and best practices. • Contribute to system architecture and technical design discussions. • Identify opportunities to improve application performance, scalability, reliability, and maintainability. • Communicate technical concepts effectively to technical and non-technical stakeholders. Required Technical Skills Backend • Strong hands-on experience with Golang (Go). • Experience building REST APIs, microservices, and distributed applications. • Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, concurrency, and Go programming practices. • Experience with SQL and database concepts. Frontend • Strong hands-on experience with React.js. • Good knowledge of JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML, and CSS. • Experience developing responsive and scalable web applications. • Understanding of frontend architecture and state management. Cloud & Data • Strong working knowledge of Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Experience with BigQuery and large-scale data processing. • Understanding of GCP services, cloud architecture, security, monitoring, and deployment. • Experience with CI/CD and cloud-based application deployment is preferred. Product & Business Skills • Strong understanding of the Software/Product Development Life Cycle. • Excellent requirement gathering and analysis skills. • Ability to understand business problems and translate them into technical solutions. • Experience interacting directly with customers and stakeholders. • Ability to independently drive requirements, clarify ambiguities, and communicate technical solutions. • Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. • Ability to work effectively in an Agile/Scrum development environment. Preferred Skills • Experience with microservices and distributed systems. • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with GCP services such as Cloud Run, GKE, Pub/Sub, Cloud Storage, Cloud SQL, and Cloud Functions. • Experience with CI/CD tools and DevOps practices. • Knowledge of system design and scalable architecture. • Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams. • Exposure to observability, logging, monitoring, and production troubleshooting. Key Competencies • Strong Golang Development • Strong React.js Development • GCP & BigQuery Expertise • Requirement Gathering • Customer Interaction & Stakeholder Management • Product Development Life Cycle • System Design & Problem Solving • Technical Communication • Ownership & Accountability • Collaboration & Teamwork
