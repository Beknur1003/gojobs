About the Role Crusoe's Platform Engineering team builds the services that automate how we manage our cloud fleet. As a Senior Software Engineer, you'll design and ship modular microservices on top of our underlying infrastructure, turning stakeholder requirements into platform capabilities that directly drive feature delivery and revenue. What You'll Be Working On • Design, build, and operate microservices that automate fleet provisioning, lifecycle, and capacity management • Extend existing platform services with new features that directly drive customer-facing delivery • Turn ambiguous stakeholder requirements into clear, modular designs and drive them to production • Build on GCP and managed Kubernetes, working closely with infrastructure and cross-functional teams • Own the reliability and observability of the services you ship What You'll Bring to the Team • 5+ years building and operating production backend or platform services • Strong proficiency in at least one backend language such as Go, Python, Java, or C# • Experience designing microservices and APIs (gRPC/REST) in distributed systems • Hands-on experience with Postgres and event-driven systems such as Kafka • A track record of leading projects end-to-end and raising the bar for engineers around you Bonus Points • Experience building on GCP, AWS, or managed Kubernetes • Capacity management or fleet/hardware lifecycle experience • Neocloud or GPU cloud infrastructure experience • Experience with Go • Observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana) Benefits • Industry competitive pay • Restricted Stock Units in a fast growing, well-funded technology company • Health insurance package options that include HDHP and PPO, vision, and dental for you and your dependents • Employer contributions to HSA accounts • Paid Parental Leave • Paid life insurance, short-term and long-term disability • Teladoc • 401(k) with a 100% match up to 4% of salary • Generous paid time off and holiday schedule • Cell phone reimbursement • Tuition reimbursement • Subscription to the Calm app • MetLife Legal • Company paid commuter benefit; $300 per month Compensation Compensation will be paid in the range of $170,000 - $205,000.
