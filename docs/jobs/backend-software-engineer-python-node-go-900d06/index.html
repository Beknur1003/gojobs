Backend Software Engineer (Python/Node/Go)

truckoo GmbH Germany опубликовано 2 октября 2026

Удалёнка Senior Полная занятость
MicroservicesPython
in a nutshell We are looking for a veteran software-developer 1) who enjoys being part of the whole software-development lifecycle 2) who loves integrating 3rd-party-applications 3) has an inclination towards Dev/Ops-Engineering ​ 4) with ample experience in building RESTful APIs We are looking for a software developer who feels at home in serverless cloud-native microservice-architectures. We have a multitude of different microservices, serverless functions and frontends in need of tending, petting and further development to satisfy the hunger for new features from our customers and co-workers. Our Python, Node and Go cloud-native application is in a continuous flux and expanding rapidly. We love discovering new technologies and using the right tool for the right job. what does truckoo offer High Impact Role: Shaping the tech and story of our company Work from home: Fully distributed, as long as you are available, enthusiastic and willing for meet ups in person at regular intervals Strong team: We are young, fun to work with and have a clear vision for our company Growing with us: We always invest in people and their regularly renewed technology set Competitive salary: We want you to be on truck :-) what are we looking for Python/Node/Go with Dev/Sec/Ops experience • Fluency in Python and additionally either Go or Typscript experience • Experience with microservice development and interest in microservice design patterns • Feeling comfortable working with Third Party APIs and data-integration flows • An intrinsic devsecops philosophy and solid grasp of the whole software-development lifecycle • Good understanding of RESTful API-design and the ability to implement those • Knowledge of testing tools and frameworks like pytest, jest and writing integration, e2e and unit tests • Optionally a solid grasp of NoSQL and SQL databases • Optionally knowledge regarding API-Management You have several years of experience in backend application development? You know how to design and implement microservice architectures, keeping an eye on all the nitty-gritty stuff? Then drop us a note and join the team! how to apply Please ​drop us a note with your CV and make sure that all your documents and data are complete and up-to date to make it possible for us to factor everything in the review process. After the quick application review, you have a first ​video call to get to know each other. The ​small coding challenge that is language-agnostic and will rather be broad than specific should not take you longer than 1 or 2 evenings. We then invite you to ​a second video call with our lead developer to discuss the results of the coding challenge. Before ​we proceed to make you an offer, we invite ​you to meet our team so you get the full truckoo picture :-) Originally posted on Himalayas
