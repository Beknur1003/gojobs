Job Description Summary We Need You – and So Do the Patients GE HealthCare is a global leader in medical technology and digital solutions, focused on improving lives when it matters most. We believe every idea counts, every voice matters, and every contribution helps build a healthier world. Our solutions support radiologists, cardiologists, and surgeons worldwide, enabling advanced analysis of CT, MRI, PET, and other medical images to drive better clinical decisions. In this role, you will work as a developer within a cross-functional, agile Scrum team, contributing to the Imaging Framework product. You’ll collaborate in a globally distributed environment with multiple teams, where quality, resilience, and scalability are essential to delivering technology patients can rely on. This is your place to grow, thrive, and unlock your potential. designs, implement prototypes, and establish an execution plan. • Deliver according to the Definition of Done, including: • Test automation at multiple levels (unit, integration, end‑to‑end) • High-quality documentation to support quality and regulatory requirements • Maintain the existing by correcting defects and supporting users. • Provide clear, proactive, and timely status updates to ensure transparency and alignment. • Actively participate in continuous improvement by proposing and implementing changes to tools and processes to make teams more efficient Requirements Technical
