Senior Software Engineer Fully Remote (Must operate on US East Coast Time) The Opportunity Our client is an AI-powered claims agent on a mission to level the playing field for consumers. They help everyday people get paid for corporate violations ranging from data breaches and privacy issues to antitrust cases. Having recently closed a $3.6M seed round and already processed over 65,000 claims (with a projected $75M+ recovery), they are ready to scale. As Engineer #2, you will report directly to the CTO, Jon Chen. This isn’t just a coding role; it’s a foundational role where you will help shape the engineering culture, influence the product roadmap, and build the AI engine that drives consumer justice. What You’ll Do • Lead 0 to 1 Projects: Take products from initial concept to full production, working closely with leadership and cross-functional teams. • Architect the Stack: Work across a modern stack—Go, React, AWS, and Python—with a focus on creating seamless, high-performance user experiences. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Marketing, Legal, and Operations to design technical solutions that drive growth and platform reliability. • Set the Bar: As an early hire, you will define engineering best practices, raise the quality of the codebase, and mentor future team members. What You Bring • Experience: 3–8 years of full-stack software engineering experience, specifically within a startup or as a founder. • Proven Track Record: You have successfully shipped significant products or features from scratch (0 > 1). • Technical Proficiency: Strong command of Go (Golang), React, TypeScript, and AWS (Lambda, CDK, DynamoDB). • Domain Knowledge: Previous experience in Fintech or LegalTech is highly preferred. • Mindset: Low ego, highly collaborative, and comfortable with the ambiguity of an early-stage startup. Culture & Perks • Impact: You are building a tool that puts money back into the pockets of consumers. • Autonomy: Fully remote work environment. • Growth: Significant equity stake (1–2%) in a high-growth, venture-backed company.
