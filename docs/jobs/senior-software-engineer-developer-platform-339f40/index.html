Alpaca Remote - Europe, Americas опубликовано 16 ноября 2026

Who We Are: Alpaca is a US-headquartered, global leader in agent-first brokerage infrastructure for stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, fixed income, 24/5 trading, and more. Amongst our subsidiaries, Alpaca is a licensed financial services company, serving hundreds of financial institutions across 40 countries with our institutional-grade APIs. This includes broker-dealers, investment advisors, wealth managers, hedge funds, and crypto exchanges, totalling over 10 million brokerage accounts. Our global team is a diverse group of experienced engineers, traders, and brokerage professionals who are working to achieve our mission of opening financial services to everyone on the planet. We're deeply committed to open-source contributions and fostering a vibrant community, continuously enhancing our award-winning, developer-friendly API and the robust infrastructure behind it. Alpaca is proudly backed by $400 million in funding from top-tier global investors including Portage Ventures, Spark Capital, Tribe Capital, Social Leverage, Horizons Ventures, Opera Tech Ventures, SBI Group, Derayah Financial, Unbound, Peak XV, Elefund, and Y Combinator. Our Team Members: We're a dynamic team of 400+ globally distributed members who thrive working from our favorite places around the world, with teammates spanning the USA, Canada, Japan, Hungary, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK, and beyond! We're searching for passionate individuals eager to contribute to Alpaca's rapid growth. If you align with our core values—Stay Curious, Have Empathy, and Be Accountable—and are ready to make a significant impact, we encourage you to apply. Your Role: We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to build the developer platform that agents and partners use to integrate and build on. You evolve Alpaca’s API standards, governance, documentation and the SDKs, CLI, MCPs and other AI tools for end users to build, integrate and operate on Alpaca. You play a central role to define the API and the tooling that makes Alpaca the easiest and fastest trading infrastructure to build, integrate and operate on. You ensure the Developer and Agent surfaces are consistent, reliable, secure, and easy to evolve. You will also build backend systems that will power API experiences across the end users journeys. This is a hands-on role. You will lead through architecture, code, design reviews, and mentorship. You will partner with Product, Compliance, Risk, and Platform Engineering teams. Things You Get To Do: • Build the developer and agent platform for developers and partners to integrate and build on top of Alpaca, including SDK/CLI/MCP architecture and API design and evolution. • Build backend systems that will power API experiences across the end users journeys. • Contribute to API standardization, versioning, security, governance and documentation across external APIs. • Own and evolve services responsible for API governance and the developer experience. • Drive best practices for observability, performance, and reliability at the application layer. • Review API and architectural designs across teams and guide complex technical decisions. • Contribute to shared frameworks, libraries, and tooling. • Mentor senior engineers and raise the bar for backend and API craftsmanship. Who You Are (Must-Haves): • 5+ years of software engineering experience, with ownership of production systems that other developers use. • Shipped a developer-facing API, SDK, or developer platform as a primary contributor. • Strong expertise in API design, including schemas, compatibility, and responses other developers can rely on. • Proficiency in Golang, C# or Java (or equivalent backend experience). • Deep understanding of observability and operational excellence. • Experience defining and influencing engineering standards across teams. • Strong architectural judgment and ability to balance long-term design with delivery. • Familiarity with frontend systems (React/TypeScript) and API consumption patterns. • Algo trading experience (either professional or personal). Who You Might Be (Nice-to-Haves): • Experience with MCP architecture. • Experience with agent identity, authorization and safety, such as OAuth, delegated access and policy enforcement systems. • Experience or understanding of financial markets • Production experience with Go. • Experience working at a startup. • Experience working remotely. How We Take Care of You: • Competitive Salary & Stock Options • Health Benefits • New Hire Home-Office Setup: One-time USD $500 • Monthly Stipend: USD $150 per month via a Brex Card Alpaca is proud to be an equal opportunity workplace dedicated to pursuing and hiring a diverse workforce.
