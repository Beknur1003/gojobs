Senior Backend Developer - Microservices Architecture

WITS Innovation Lab Mohali опубликовано 22 июня 2026

Senior
DockerMicroservicesPythonTypeScriptNode.jsFinTech
Company Overview : Wits Innovation Lab is a technology-driven firm based in Mohali, dedicated to building scalable digital solutions and high-performance software products. We operate at the intersection of innovation and engineering, serving diverse global clients across the fintech, e-commerce, and enterprise SaaS sectors. Our culture is rooted in technical excellence, collaborative problem-solving, and a commitment to delivering robust, production-grade systems that handle significant user traffic. Role Overview : As a Senior Backend Developer, you will be the backbone of our engineering team, responsible for architecting and maintaining complex server-side systems. You will work closely with frontend engineers, product managers, and DevOps teams to translate business requirements into efficient, high-availability services. Your work will directly influence the scalability and reliability of our platforms, ensuring a seamless experience for thousands of end-users while maintaining clean, maintainable codebases that support long-term product growth. Key Responsibilities : - Design and implement scalable microservices architecture to ensure system modularity and high performance for our core product offerings. - Develop and maintain high-traffic RESTful webservices to facilitate seamless data exchange between internal systems and external client applications. - Optimize backend performance by identifying bottlenecks and implementing efficient database queries and caching strategies. - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to integrate new features and resolve technical debt, ensuring the stability of the production environment. - Mentor junior developers by conducting code reviews and promoting best practices in software design and documentation. Required Skillset : - Demonstrated expertise in building production-ready applications using Node.js, Python, or Golang, with the ability to choose the right tool for specific architectural needs. - Proficiency in TypeScript to ensure type safety and maintainability across large-scale backend codebases. - Strong understanding of containerization and orchestration using Docker to streamline deployment pipelines and environment consistency. - Proven ability to design and manage complex microservices architectures, including inter-service communication and data consistency patterns. - Excellent communication skills with the ability to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders and work effectively in an on-site environment in Mohali. - A minimum of 4 - 8 years of professional experience in backend development, showcasing a track record of delivering high-quality software solutions.
