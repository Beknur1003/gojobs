Backend Engineer (SDE 3) Payments Platform Role Overview : We are looking for an experienced Backend Engineer (SDE 3) to build and scale high-performance payment infrastructure. This role is ideal for engineers who enjoy solving complex backend challenges, designing distributed systems, and taking end-to-end ownership of mission-critical services. You will develop highly scalable, low-latency backend systems, optimize performance and infrastructure efficiency, and contribute to building reliable, secure, and resilient payment platforms that operate at scale. Key Responsibilities : Backend Development : - Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services for high-throughput, low-latency payment systems. - Build secure, reliable, and fault-tolerant microservices using modern backend technologies. - Develop APIs and distributed services capable of handling large-scale transaction workloads. System Design & Ownership: - Take complete ownership of backend services from architecture and implementation through deployment, monitoring, and maintenance. - Design scalable system architectures that prioritize performance, resilience, and operational efficiency. - Troubleshoot production issues and continuously improve system reliability. Performance & Scalability: - Optimize backend services for throughput, latency, and infrastructure cost. - Improve database performance, caching strategies, and distributed system efficiency. - Identify bottlenecks and implement scalable solutions for growing workloads. Infrastructure & Observability: - Strengthen system observability through monitoring, logging, tracing, and alerting. - Build highly available and resilient services using cloud-native infrastructure. - Collaborate with infrastructure teams to improve deployment automation and operational excellence. Engineering Excellence: - Follow engineering best practices including code reviews, automated testing, documentation, and CI/CD. - Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code. - Contribute to architecture discussions and mentor junior engineers when needed. Cross-functional Collaboration: - Partner with Product, Frontend, Infrastructure, and Platform Engineering teams to deliver high-quality solutions. - Participate in design reviews, sprint planning, and technical discussions. - Drive continuous improvements in engineering processes and system reliability. Required Qualifications: - Strong hands-on experience in Golang backend development. - Solid understanding of distributed systems, concurrency, and system design. - Experience designing and building scalable backend services and microservices. - Strong expertise with PostgreSQL and relational database design. - Experience working with caching technologies such as Redis. - Familiarity with messaging systems including RabbitMQ and Amazon SQS. - Experience deploying and operating applications on AWS. - Knowledge of observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, and ClickHouse. - Experience with containerization, CI/CD, and Infrastructure as Code using Docker, Terraform, and GitLab CI/CD. - Strong ownership mindset with experience delivering features from concept to production. - Passion for writing clean, maintainable, and efficient code.
