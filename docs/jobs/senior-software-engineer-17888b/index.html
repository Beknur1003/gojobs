Duties: • Solve highly complex technical problems and deliver innovative, scalable, maintainable solutions by implementing architectural patterns and design standards with minimal supervision. • Champion efficient software development methodologies aligned with Agile and Lean principles, contributing hands-on to development, design, code review, and optimisation of critical software components. • Drive adoption of DevOps practices, CI/CD platforms, and automation toolchains whilst embedding security, reliability, scalability, and performance considerations into architecture and development processes. • Promote and enforce secure coding practices and integration of security controls throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), leveraging modern platforms, frameworks, and open-source technologies. • Demonstrate deep expertise in modern technologies including Angular, Java, Spring Boot, Python, Golang, Docker, and Kubernetes, optimising cost and flexibility through open standards. • Serve as a technical bridge between engineering, operations, QA, and other teams to design efficient testing strategies, enable seamless deliveries, and foster continuous learning and collaboration. • Embed observability, monitoring, logging, and reliability engineering practices into development processes as non-negotiable quality attributes. Qualifications: Education & Experience: • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field. • 4+ years' proven experience in software development. Nice to Have: • Experience with Grafana and UML design modelling techniques. • Knowledge of event-driven systems, real-time data processing, and ML/AI platforms. • Exposure to UX/UI design principles. Skills & Attributes: • Self-starter with strong time management and attention to detail. • Excellent communication skills in English (spoken and written). • Effective stakeholder and matrix management in virtual, diverse teams. • Proven ability to drive results and support continuous improvement. • Strong collaborative and influencing skills with global mindset and cultural awareness. • Resourceful problem-solver with ability to translate information into actionable insight. • Ability to work effectively across cross-functional and cross-cultural teams.
