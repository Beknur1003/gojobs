Job Description JOB DESCRIPTION Backend Software Engineer Engineering • Golang Microservices • Fintech ROLE Senior Software Engineer LOCATION Hybrid, Hyderabad Role Purpose We obsess about our customers. We are constantly innovating and working iteratively to solve problems in the world of money that conventional banking will not touch. As a Backend Software Engineer, you will support the development of elegant, performant, maintainable, operable and secure Golang microservices. You will help design and architect our systems, contribute to continuous improvement, and play a key role in making the engineering team the best it can be. Projects You Will Contribute To · Building our vision of a connected platform that integrates seamlessly with best-in-class apps, tools and services. · Connecting the financial world for customers by bringing together account and transactional data that enables better financial decisions. · Developing spend analytics, insights and recommendations powered by data and machine learning. · Building a financial product marketplace, including credit, savings and other products designed to improve customers’ financial lives. Current Technology Stack · Languages: Golang; PHP is a bonus · Messaging: Kafka and RabbitMQ · Data: Event-driven architecture, Snowplow and Python for ETL · Databases: PostgreSQL, MongoDB and BigQuery · DevOps culture: TDD, BDD, Scrum, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes and AWS What You Will Be Doing · Delivering clean, architecturally sound and maintainable server-side code; full-stack experience is a bonus. · Collaborating with stakeholders across the business to build, measure and learn while creating a world-class product. · Partnering with product and engineering managers to continuously refine and iterate development processes. · Motivating the team through roadblocks to ensure features ship as effectively as possible. · Guiding design discussions and helping the team balance flexibility, expediency, scalability and performance. · Championing new technologies and promoting strong development practices. · Keeping an eye on the future while proactively and pragmatically tackling technical debt. What You Will Bring · In-depth knowledge of Golang; PHP experience is a plus. · Experience building microservices, ideally deployed in containers on Kubernetes. · A solid grounding in agile development practices. · Experience with both relational and NoSQL datastores. · Experience with real-time, low-latency, high-frequency transaction-based systems is a bonus. · The ability to diagnose problems across the stack, including client, HTTP/network, server, database and operating system, with sound rationale for decisions. · Proven experience building large, concurrent and distributed applications. · Experience developing and running large-scale production environments in partnership with DevOps or SRE teams. · Experience with event-driven architectures is a bonus. · A genuine interest in working in a fast-paced start-up environment, ideally in fintech or payments. · Experience mentoring or coaching other engineers is a bonus for L5. · A strong appetite for learning and continuous improvement. What Success Looks Like · You ship clean, reliable and secure code that scales. · Our systems are more resilient, performant and maintainable because of your contributions. · Product teams see you as a partner who helps ideas become reality quickly without sacrificing quality. · Technical debt is reduced rather than ignored, and future-proofing is balanced with delivery. · You bring energy and pragmatism to design discussions, guiding the team towards better solutions. · You are recognised as someone who makes the team stronger through coaching, knowledge-sharing and raising the bar.
