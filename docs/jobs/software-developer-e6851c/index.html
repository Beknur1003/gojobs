About the Role: We are seeking a talented and motivated Back End Software Developer to join our Software Engineering team. You will be working collaboratively to design, develop, deploy and maintain back-end services that power our innovative solutions. Responsibilities: • Design and maintain robust, scalable back-end services • Develop clean, well-documented, and testable code, adhering to industry best practices • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues, ensuring high availability and performance • Contribute to the automation of our infrastructure and deployments • Contribute to improving our engineering practices and standards • Lead and participate in system design and architectural discussions Qualifications: Technical Skills • Proficiency in programming languages such as Golang (Go), Python or Typescript • Expertise with library and web service APIs including REST and gRPC • Expertise with message-bus architectures and message queue implementations • Experience working with microservice architectures • Solid understanding of Linux operating systems • Experience with containerization technologies and virtualization (Docker, VMware) • Experience with SQL and relational databases • Networking and security protocols (TCP/IP, TLS) Professional Skills • Bachelor’s degree or higher in computer science or a related field • 4+ years of professional software development experience • Excellent written and verbal communication, collaboration, and problem-solving skills. • Reliable and demonstrates a professional work ethic. • Intellectual curiosity and the ability to learn new technologies quickly.
