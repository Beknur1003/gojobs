Got Drive? Join AnyVan as we make moving anything, anywhere miles better and build a career that moves just as fast. Back in 2009, our Chairman Angus saw half-empty vans everywhere and knew there had to be a better way! That's how AnyVan began. He set out to create the world's most efficient logistics technology and help halve the number of wasted miles by filling those empty vans. Since then we've become the nation's favourite way to move, with over 150k five-star reviews and 5 million customers in the UK and Europe. Our team of 400 AnyVanners across London, Cape Town and Bogota is proving that moving doesn't have to cost the earth by helping save 8,520 tonnes of Carbon each year. Build systems that move the real world, with AI at the core At AnyVan, we're building one of the most sophisticated logistics optimisation platforms in Europe. We connect customer demand with real-time driver availability, using intelligent matching, dynamic pricing, and AI to deliver a world-class service at market-beating prices. By routing jobs to vans already on the road, we reduce wasted miles, cut emissions, and ensure drivers earn fairly. It's logistics, pricing, and marketplace dynamics, driven by software and increasingly by machine intelligence. We're hiring for two Senior Engineer roles. Both sit in the same engineering team, share the same stack and ways of working, and put AI at the heart of what they build. The difference is the problem you'll focus on: one role is all about the AI agents behind our Sales and Customer Service teams, the other is about Allocations, getting our transport partners matched to the right jobs. Let us know in your application which one interests you most. Role 1: Senior Engineer, AI Agents (Sales & Customer Service) In this role, you'll help build and scale the AI agents behind our Sales and Customer Service teams. These agents handle real conversations with real customers every day, answering questions, qualifying leads, quoting moves and resolving issues across phone, chat and messaging. You'll own them end to end, from designing how they reason and which tools and data they can use, to the evaluation, guardrails and observability that keep them accurate, on-brand and safe in production. You'll work closely with our Sales and CS leaders and the agents who use these tools on the front line, so you can see the impact of your work in conversion, response times and customer satisfaction. This is a hands-on role for someone who wants to move LLM-powered systems past the demo stage and into something thousands of customers rely on. Role 2: Senior Engineer, Allocations In this role, you'll work with our Allocations team on the data and back-end engineering problems behind how our transport partners access jobs, get booked and are allocated against the right work. You'll focus on the data, platforms and tools that make that happen. This is the engine behind routing jobs to vans already on the road, so you'll see the impact of your work directly in how efficiently jobs are matched and how fairly drivers earn. We move fast: We're embedding AI and machine learning across our core platform, not as experiments, but as production systems that drive real business outcomes: • Real-time, intelligent pricing that models true job-level costs and responds dynamically to supply, demand, and market conditions • Predictive demand and routing models that forecast driver availability and optimise job allocation before a customer even books • LLM-powered tooling across our internal and customer-facing products, from intelligent quoting to driver workflow automation • A two-sided marketplace where drivers can buy and sell jobs to maximise utilisation — powered by smart matching algorithms This is genuinely leading-edge work: production AI systems, at scale, on problems that matter. How our engineering teams deliver We're product-led and agile. Most teams run Scrum with two-week sprints, but we care far more about outcomes than rituals. Engineers are trusted to challenge assumptions, adapt processes, and ship things that matter. Senior engineers here are not heads-down implementers. You'll: • Work closely with product, data, and commercial stakeholders • Influence technical direction and architectural decisions • Balance speed with long-term sustainability • Regularly ship meaningful improvements to production Our technology We're pragmatic and cloud-first, running on AWS, with the flexibility to choose the right tool for the job: • Exploring: Go, Rust, React, Node.js • Core stack: Python, Vue.js & Nuxt, TypeScript • Legacy (retiring where we can): Java, PHP, Angular You don't need experience in everything, we value strong fundamentals and good engineering judgement over keyword matching. What you'll be doing • Designing, building, and operating production-grade systems that scale • Tackling complex technical challenges across backend, infrastructure, data flows, and AI/ML pipelines • Writing clean, pragmatic, well-tested code with long-term maintainability in mind • Collaborating with technical and non-technical stakeholders to shape solutions • Championing testing, observability, and quality improvements • Mentoring engineers and raising the technical bar across the team You'll thrive here if you have • Experience in a scale-up or fast-growing product environment • Strong backend engineering skills (Go, Java, Python, or similar) • A solid understanding of distributed systems, scalability, and performance • Experience with CI/CD, modern deployment practices, and cloud infrastructure • Comfort working with ambiguity and evolving requirements • A bias toward action — shipping value quickly, then iterating • A collaborative mindset and the ability to influence beyond your code • Bonus: exposure to AI/ML systems, Building AI agents, marketplaces, logistics, pricing, or e-commerce Location and eligibility • UK-based, able to commute to Hammersmith.
