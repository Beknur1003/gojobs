[Job-32571] Senior Observability Engineer, Brazil

ciandt Brazil опубликовано 10 ноября 2026

Удалёнка Senior
KubernetesDockerTerraformCI/CDJavaAI/LLM
At CI&T, we help large enterprises transform the potential of AI into real business impact with AI Deployment, AI-native execution, and tech-integrated business solutions. With 30 years of experience in technological transformation, we accelerate innovation with expertise in Agentic SDLC, Application modernization, Data & AI, Martech and Business strategy. We are 8,000 CI&Ters across more than 25 countries, collaborating to build solutions with real impact. AI is already part of how we work, evolve, and innovate every day. We are looking for an Observability Engineer to join our team and work for a large multinational company in the food and beverage sector. This is an opportunity to handle monitoring and system reliability in a high-scale, complex environment. If you have experience in SRE and are seeking challenges in large-scale operations, this is the role for you! Position also available for Mid-Level Developers interested in transitioning to the SRE role. If you're a developer who's already used to working on international projects, using English evryday, and wants to migrate to observability, this is the ideal place for you! Responsibilities: Performance Monitoring and Analysis: Monitor and analyze the performance of production systems, creating high-quality alerts to improve incident detection and prevention. Preferred tools: AppDynamics (advantageous), New Relic, DataDog, Dynatrace, or similar. Software Architecture Support: With prior experience in software development, understand the solution’s architecture and identify opportunities to enable monitoring and custom alerts. Preferred languages: Java and/or Golang (not mandatory). Troubleshooting: Investigate real-world production issues using reported metrics to identify root causes, collaborating with the application team. Collaboration: Work closely with development teams to improve the reliability and performance of services. Requirements for this challenge: Practical experience with performance monitoring tools (AppDynamics is a plus, but New Relic, DataDog, or Dynatrace are also acceptable). Background in software development, with a solid understanding of architecture and system integration. Knowledge of programming languages such as Java and/or Golang (preferred, not mandatory). Experience with SRE practices and DevOps culture. Experience in troubleshooting and solving production issues. Advanced oral and written communication skills in English. Nice to have: Familiarity with Kubernetes, Docker, or other containerization technologies. Knowledge of process automation and infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible, etc.). If you like it, just apply and good luck!
