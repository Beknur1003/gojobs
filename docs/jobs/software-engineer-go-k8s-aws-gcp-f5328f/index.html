We're partnering with an exciting early-stage startup building next-generation networking and security infrastructure for AI agents. The company is developing a cloud-native platform focused on secure communication, distributed systems, and scalable infrastructure. We're looking for talented Backend Software Engineers to join a growing engineering team and help design, build, and scale the company's core platform. This is a highly hands-on engineering opportunity for someone who enjoys solving complex technical problems, working with modern cloud technologies, and taking ownership of meaningful projects in a startup environment. Startup experience and startup mindset is a must. What You'll Do: • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services and APIs. • Build cloud-native applications and distributed systems using modern programming languages. • Develop and deploy services in Kubernetes environments running on AWS. • Contribute to the architecture and development of core infrastructure components. • Work on backend systems supporting networking, security, and communication between services. • Troubleshoot performance, reliability, and scalability challenges across distributed environments. • Collaborate with a small engineering team to build new features and take products from concept to production. What We're Looking For: • 3+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a strong backend development focus. • Golang experience preferred, but open to engineers with strong object-oriented programming backgrounds in Java, Python, C++, or similar languages. • Hands-on Kubernetes experience, including deploying, managing, or developing containerized applications. • Strong AWS experience, with an understanding of cloud infrastructure and services. • Experience designing and developing REST APIs, microservices, or distributed backend applications. • Solid understanding of software engineering fundamentals, data structures, and system design. • Experience building and maintaining production software. • Ability to work independently and thrive in a fast-paced, early-stage environment. Nice to Have: • Experience with networking infrastructure, proxies, API gateways, or load balancers. • Familiarity with Layer 4–7 networking concepts. • Experience with cloud security, authentication, authorization, or identity management. • Exposure to Kubernetes networking, service meshes, or infrastructure automation. • Experience with AI agents, LLM infrastructure, or emerging AI technologies. • Previous experience working at a startup or on a small engineering team.
