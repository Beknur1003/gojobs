Job Summary Your Career Build the automation that helps engineering teams deliver reliable software faster. As a Senior Staff Software Development Engineer in Test (SDET), you will shape test automation strategy for complex backend services, APIs, and distributed systems. You will architect scalable frameworks, integrate AI-assisted testing into engineering workflows, and help developers build systems that are easier to test, troubleshoot, and maintain. This is a hands-on technical leadership role with ownership from architecture and implementation through adoption across teams. You will partner with Development, DevOps, and Security to solve challenging quality problems, strengthen testing infrastructure, and improve engineering productivity. Your Impact • Lead test automation architecture: Design, build, and evolve scalable frameworks and reusable tooling for backend services, APIs, and distributed systems. • Advance AI-assisted testing: Evaluate and integrate generative AI tools, including GitHub Copilot and large language models (LLMs), to support test generation, test selection, synthetic test data, and root cause analysis. • Own testing infrastructure: Build and optimize CI/CD pipelines and reusable components for provisioning, configuring, and tearing down Linux and Windows test environments. • Validate real-world scenarios: Reproduce enterprise customer environments involving DNS, public key infrastructure (PKI), firewalls, and multiple operating system distributions. • Influence product testability: Partner with Development, DevOps, and Security to align test architecture with product design and identify quality risks early. • Strengthen test coverage: Clarify acceptance criteria, develop test plans, and collaborate with developers on complex end-to-end scenarios within Agile development processes. • Extend technical leadership across teams: Coach engineers on testable design, appropriate testing levels, and automation practices through design reviews, workshops, and hands-on collaboration. • Drive measurable improvements: Use quality metrics to identify coverage gaps, prioritize improvements, and evaluate the effectiveness of testing tools and processes. Why Join Us? Your work will improve how teams build, test, and deliver software. You will have the ownership to solve complex engineering problems, the space to experiment with emerging technologies, and the opportunity to turn successful approaches into tools and practices used across the organization. Join a collaborative team that values initiative, technical depth, and practical improvements to product quality and developer productivity. Work Arrangement This hybrid role is based in Salt Lake City, Utah, with three days per week in the office. You must be able to collaborate effectively with engineering teams working across U.S. time zones. Qualifications Required Qualifications • 6+ years of experience in software development engineering in test, test automation, or platform quality engineering—or equivalent backend software engineering experience with a strong focus on testing. • Strong software development skills in Python or Go (Golang), including writing maintainable, well-documented code. • Experience designing, building, and extending test automation frameworks for enterprise backend services, APIs, and distributed systems. • Demonstrated use of generative AI tools or machine learning techniques to improve development or testing workflows, automate repetitive tasks, or generate synthetic test data. • Strong understanding of Linux/Unix and Windows operating system fundamentals. • Working knowledge of networking protocols, including TCP/IP, DNS, and HTTP/HTTPS. • Experience building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or GitLab CI. • Experience configuring and working with containerized environments using Docker and Kubernetes. • Strong debugging and root cause analysis skills across applications, services, infrastructure, and operating systems. • Ability to lead technical initiatives independently, manage competing priorities, and guide adoption across engineering teams. Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and configuration management tools such as Terraform or Ansible. • Experience building or emulating large-scale enterprise customer environments.
