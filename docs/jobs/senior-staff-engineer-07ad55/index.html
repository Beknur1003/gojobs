About the Role Zensar is looking for a Senior Staff Platform Engineer / Principal Platform Engineer (Kubernetes & Cloud Infrastructure) to serve as the technical authority for our Kubernetes-based compute platform, owning it from design through ongoing evolution. This platform underpins the security, networking, efficiency, and developer-experience capabilities our Cloud Infrastructure organization provides and keeps behavior consistent across multiple cloud providers. Your work will give infrastructure, and product teams a reliable, scalable, and secure foundation for shipping high-quality features at scale. This is a hybrid opportunity requiring three days onsite per week in Morrisville, North Carolina. The position is open to both full-time employees (FTE) and contract professionals, offering competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits for eligible employees, and excellent career growth opportunities. What You'll Do • Lead the technical direction of high-visibility, high-impact initiatives • Mentor and guide a geographically distributed engineering team • Identify and deliver improvements that lower the cost of running the cloud platform • Bring teams into alignment on objectives, expected outcomes, and delivery schedules • Own prioritization, timelines, and delivery commitments for your projects • Help shape the long-range, multi-year roadmap for the cloud platform • Architect, build, test, ship, operate, and continuously improve the compute platform What You Bring • Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in Computer Science or a related discipline, or comparable professional experience • Experience working in infrastructure engineering • Deep experience with at least one major public cloud GCP, AWS or Azure and its core infrastructure services, such as EC2 • Expert-level, hands-on Kubernetes engineering skills, including: • Developing custom Kubernetes controllers • Designing and implementing admission webhooks • A track record of defining technical strategy and landing large, cross-team projects • A strong commitment to efficiency, availability, quality, and great developer experience Nice to Have • Prior work at cloud-native, DevOps, or infrastructure-centric companies operating Kubernetes in production at scale • Advanced Golang skills • Handson experience with Istio or Envoy Interested? • Apply or reach out directly to discuss how your experience aligns with this opportunity.
