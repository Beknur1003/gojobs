About Seoul Robotics Seoul Robotics is an autonomous driving company with a mission to solve first and last-mile logistics challenges, backed by a team of industry experts from Google, Coupang, Hyudai Motor Company, GM and Continental. This position offers a great opportunity to advance your professional career in the cutting-edge autonomous robotics industry, where AI/ML is embedded at the core of the product. With members from over 10 different countries, you have the chance to work in a unique international environment in the heart of Seoul, where English is our primary language. Our global clients provide opportunities to work overseas in various areas in Asia and the US. [Our Story and Technology] - Powering the Future of Autonomy with LV5 CTRL TWR (YouTube LINK) - Media (Chosun Biz LINK, PR Newswire LINK) [Our Principles] ■ Add Value ■ Earn Trust ■ Own Your Job ■ Keep Growing ■ Respect Diversity Mission of the Role We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Senior Backend Engineer to join our team dedicated to autonomous driving technology. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in Go service development, a good understanding of RESTful API design and experience building fault-tolerant, real-time systems. You will play a crucial role in maintaining and expanding the backend responsible for streaming live fleet state to operators and relaying commands to the autonomy stack. Key Responsibilities • Design and implement services in Go, following a domain-driven design. • Build and maintain event pipelines between the operator-facing layer and autonomy stack. • Define and extend RESTful API contracts that teams can build against with confidence. • Deliver real-time state to operators over event-driven and streaming connections, with a focus on robustness and redundancy. • Maintain and extend operator-facing data in PostgreSQL through versioned migrations. • Maintain and extend service instrumentation so system behavior stays observable as new features land. Basic Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent work experience. • 5+ years of work experience in backend software engineering, or a related field. • Experience building resilient services (e.g. graceful degradation and recovery). • Solid experience with Go or similar backend programming languages and concurrent high-traffic systems. • Hands-on experience designing and building RESTful APIs, preferably using a Go framework (e.g. Fiber, Gin). • Comfortable working with relational databases, ORM, query optimization and schema migrations (e.g. Postgres, GORM). • Comfortable working with message-driven architectures and event pipelines (e.g. Kafka). • Proactive approach to identifying problems, performance bottlenecks, and areas for improvement. • Excellent communication skills in English and the ability to work cross-functionally with developers, QA, and operations teams. Preferred Qualifications • Experience working on large-scale distributed systems. • Experience working with real-time or streaming systems (e.g. WebSockets or similar). • Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools such as Prometheus, Grafana. • Familiarity with TypeScript and ability to support the frontend team.
