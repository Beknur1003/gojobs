Platform Engineer, Software Engineering – Aladdin - Director

About this role Your team Aladdin is a unified investment operating system used across BlackRock and by technology clients around the world. Aladdin Platform Engineering builds and runs the shared technology foundation on which Aladdin applications depend, giving the team impact across the platform. As part of this organization, you will lead a small, focused team of experienced engineers who turn non-functional requirements (NFRs) into practical standards that can be tested and enforced automatically, and who apply AI engineering to assist platform engineers, including developing platform services and managing observability. You will work closely with platform teams that own the underlying technologies and with application teams across Aladdin that act on the findings. Your role and impact We are looking for an experienced engineering leader with solid application development and application architecture experience, who can define best practices and has a track record of driving technology transformation on a modern stack. The ability to learn quickly and understand the current Aladdin architecture is key. In this role, you will own the technology strategy for NFR standards and their enforcement, and for AI engineering initiatives that assist the platform team, helping Aladdin move from reacting to incidents to preventing them and helping platform engineers work more effectively. You will define a shared standard for what “production-ready” means, informed by incident history, and establish automated checks on code changes. You will also develop platform services and AI-enabled tooling, and manage observability across the platform. Applications will receive clear assessments and actionable remediation guidance, while leadership will gain visibility into the health of the portfolio. You will report to the head of the Aladdin Platform organization and be accountable for improving platform-wide outcomes rather than for a single product. Your responsibilities In every role at BlackRock, you'll be expected to apply sound judgement and critical thinking to solve complex problems, adapt as the business evolves, and combine the curiosity to explore new approaches and technologies with the rigor to challenge the results. The scope of this role also includes the following responsibilities: • Own NFR standards and their enforcement across the Aladdin platform, turning incident learnings into standards that are checked automatically. • Lead a small engineering team and build and run the NFR toolkit that assesses applications against those standards, and the AI-enabled tooling that supports platform engineers. • Deliver a quality gate in the build and release pipeline that runs NFR checks on every pull request. • Deliver a quality passport and fitness dashboard that shows application scores over time and provides a clear list of required fixes. • Negotiate and agree specifications with platform owners, beginning with in-memory data store and relational database platforms and extending coverage to additional platforms. • Drive remediation across application teams you do not manage and give leadership clear visibility into portfolio health. • Define and promote application development and architecture best practices across Aladdin, based on a quick and thorough understanding of the current architecture. • Lead AI engineering efforts that assist platform engineers, such as AI-assisted development, operations and troubleshooting workflows. • Develop platform services that platform and application teams can reuse. • Manage observability for the platform, including monitoring, logging, tracing and alerting, to improve detection and resolution of incidents. • Own a multi-quarter roadmap, including cost visibility by platform and application, and report progress against platform-wide incident reduction. Your experience • At least 17 years of experience in large-scale system design, development and deployment across software engineering, platform engineering, site reliability or production engineering, including experience managing engineers and owning technology strategy and multi-quarter roadmaps. • Strong programming skills in Java, C++, Golang, Python or similar, with solid application development experience in a Linux environment. • Experience deploying distributed systems, including distributed data stores, event streaming platforms and container orchestration, with cloud deployment and infrastructure-as-code technology. • Solid application architecture experience, with the ability to establish and communicate best practices and to quickly learn and understand an existing, complex architecture. • Experience with AI engineering, such as applying AI and large language model technologies to assist engineers, and with developing platform services and managing observability (monitoring, logging, tracing and alerting). • Experience defining and enforcing non-functional requirements, including resilience, performance, scalability, security and operability, across large, multi-team platforms. • Hands-on experience with continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines and automated quality gates, using incident and post-incident data to drive standards and measurable reductions in production incidents. • Proven ability to influence and negotiate standards with platform owners, drive change across teams you do not manage, and present portfolio-level health, risk, budget and cost visibility to senior leadership. • Ability and interest to learn new technologies or programming languages as needed, with openness to guidance from others.
