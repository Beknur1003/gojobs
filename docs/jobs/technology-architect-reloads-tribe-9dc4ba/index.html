Job Description The Reloads Tribe is seeking a Solution Architect to design resilient, high-performance systems that support airtime reloads, top-ups, and credit/loan solutions at scale. You will play a key role in shaping the architecture of real-time financial services that millions of subscribers depend on daily. Weʼre looking for an architect who brings strong engineering discipline, DevOps maturity, and hands-on experience in building scalable and automated systems using modern cloud technologies. Your Day in the Life at your Role 🛠 Design & Develop reliable, secure, and scalable solutions for replenishment and loan services within the prepaid ecosystem. Translate complex business needs into well-architected systems using microservices and event-driven patterns. 👥 Lead the design and implementation, and oversee end-to-end cloud-native solutions using AWS and GCP. 🤝 Collaborate with squads to implement DevOps practices—including CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure-as-code, and full automation. 🔍 Perform architecture reviews and ensure alignment with enterprise standards and tribe-level strategies. 💡 Guide and ensure optimal data modeling and storage using both NoSQL MongoDB and relational databases PostgreSQL. What You Bring to the Table Core Skills and Qualifications: • At least 15 years of total experience and with minimum 10 years of relevant experience in Solutions Architecture. • Proven experience as a Solution Architect, with prior background in software engineering or system integration roles. • Deep understanding of DevOps principles, including CI/CD, IaC (Infrastructure as Code), and environment automation. • Familiarity with airtime reloads, credit, and loan transaction flows or similar telco financial services. • Strong hands-on experience with cloud platforms such as AWS and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Proficient in GoLang, or comfortable working with teams using Go for backend services. • Solid understanding of modern system design, database modeling, and integration architectures. Preferred Tech Stack & Tools • Cloud Platforms: AWS and GCP • Databases: Relational (PostgreSQL), NoSQL (MongoDB) • Programming: GoLang (preferred) • DevOps & Automation: CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code tools (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation), GitOps approaches Apply now and help lead the architecture behind Globeʼs reload and loan platforms—ensuring speed, scale, and reliability at every transaction. • Architect mission-critical systems for reload and credit services used by millions of Filipinos. • Join a high-impact team that values delivery velocity, automation, and system resilience. • Shape next-generation digital financial experiences in one of the countryʼs leading tech-driven telcos.
