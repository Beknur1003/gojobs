Software Developer (Mid-Level)

We are seeking a talented Software Developer (Mid-Level) who will contribute independently to the development, integration, and support of modern applications using Java and Go, while gaining exposure to frontend/UI technologies and scripting. This role will focus on delivering high-quality, scalable, and secure solutions, with ownership of assigned components and customer issues from start to finish. The successful candidate will apply sound technical judgment, take responsibility for low-level design decisions, and demonstrate a solid understanding of software security principles, including practical cryptography and PKI, within a collaborative team environment. Here are the core functions, responsibilities, and expectations for this role: • Independently investigate, troubleshoot, and resolve customer and internal issues, including root-cause analysis and implementation of effective solutions. • Design and implement backend services, UI applications, third-party integrations, and REST services using Java/Spring, Golang, frontend technologies (such as Angular), and/or scripting solutions. • Own and maintain integrations with third-party products and external systems, including handling API changes, failures, and version upgrades. • Take responsibility for delivering complete solutions, including production code, unit and integration tests, design documentation, performance analysis, and product support artifacts. • Participate actively in code reviews, both receiving and providing constructive feedback to improve code quality and maintainability. • Apply low-level design principles to components and services, considering performance, security, scalability, and maintainability. • Perform scripting and automation tasks using shell scripting on Linux and PowerShell on Windows to improve efficiency and reliability. • Work with containerized applications using Docker and contribute to deployment, monitoring, and troubleshooting in production environments. • Demonstrate awareness of production systems by analyzing logs, metrics, and alerts, and participating in incident analysis and remediation. • Additional tasks associated with this position may be assigned in response to company initiatives and business needs. More information about this role: • This is a full-time hybrid position reporting to our Ottawa office 3 to 4 days a week. • The targeted compensation package for this role is between CAD 95K and CAD 115K, subject to internal equity and years of experience. We may make further adjustments through an approval process if the targeted compensation range needs to be modified based on business needs and market trends. Education: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or equivalent is strongly recommended Experience: • 3 - 6+ years of relevant corporate or commercial experience in a software development role. • Java and Golang are required or strongly preferred, with hands-on exposure to development technologies such as Java (Maven, Spring framework) and Golang toolchains required or strongly preferred. • Solid experience designing and consuming REST APIs, working within microservices-based architectures, and integrating with external systems. • Experience developing software in a professional environment, including source control, bug tracking systems, testing frameworks, and CI/CD pipelines. • Strong working knowledge of Linux environments is required. • Strong English language communication skills, both written and verbal, are required. Technical Knowledge (Preferred) • Strong understanding of software security principles, including authentication, authorization, practical cryptography, and Public Key Infrastructure (PKI). • Experience integrating with third-party products, services, and APIs. • Working knowledge of Docker and containerized application concepts. • Experience with frontend technologies such as Angular, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS is a plus. • Proficiency with scripting languages such as Bash or PowerShell is a plus. Ideal Candidate Profiles, Talents, and Desired Qualifications: • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently. • Demonstrated ownership of tasks and the ability to deliver solutions with minimal supervision. • Ability to make sound technical decisions at the component level and raise risks or concerns proactively. • Strong collaboration and interpersonal skills for working effectively in a global team environment. • Clear and confident communication skills, capable of explaining technical topics to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Ability to adapt to changing priorities and operate effectively in a fast-paced environment.
