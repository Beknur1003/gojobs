Lead Software Engineer Your Responsibilities on the Team • Collaborate with project stakeholders (product owners, UX researchers, data team, QA team, project managers, cross team leads and designers) to understand functional and non-functional requirements. • Promote a collaborative team environment and work closely with colleagues to achieve business objectives. • Manage technical direction for and mentoring of a team of software engineers, including defining and driving a team roadmap • Adhere to architecture, design, implementation, security standards and best practices. • Design and operate systems that meet SOX IT General Controls, including access provisioning, user access reviews, and change management. • Investigate and propose solutions to development and design problems. • Own production health for the team's systems: deployments, incident triage, observability, and fixing security findings in existing systems. • Conduct analysis to determine integration needs, and design and plan integrations. • Prepare technical documentation, including software design evaluation plans and test results. • Write high-quality code that satisfies customer needs and strives for simplicity, clarity, and testability. • Implement unit and integration tests and conduct functional and performance testing where appropriate. • Participate in code reviews to promote high quality business outcomes. • Develop reusable software building blocks to enable faster delivery. • Improve performance of existing software by diagnosing and resolving critical issues. Requirements • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, computer information systems or equivalent degree • 7+ years of experience with cloud-providers and cloud-based architecture • Proven growth mindset and a willingness to adapt and learn new skills, technologies, and frameworks. • Excellent problem solving and analytical skills. • Proven track record for managing and mentoring a team of developers to achieve company goals. • Proven track record for architecting, scaling and implementing complex technical solutions. • Proven track record of leadership in Agile environments, with excellent communication and collaboration skills, and a passion for mentoring and empowering team members. • Demonstrated ability to communicate complex technical information in a condensed manner to various stakeholders verbally and in writing. • Strong hands-on experience with cloud-based software architecture (microservices, containers, AWS/GCP/Azure) with deep understanding of advanced principles of client-side and server-side development. • Deep understanding of one or more modern software development language (Go, Java, C#, etc) • Strong relational data-modeling skills, including designing auditable, ledger-style or event-sourced schemas; Postgres preferred. • Experience with front end development practices and frameworks • Solid understanding of RESTful APIs and integration patterns • Experienced with a version control platform like GitHub or GitLab. • Knowledge in building and tooling processes such as CI/CD pipelines, version control (Git), and package managers, coupled with extensive experience in debugging and troubleshooting complex issues.
