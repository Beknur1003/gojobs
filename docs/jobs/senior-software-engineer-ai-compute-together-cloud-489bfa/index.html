You’ll own massive greenfield projects across their full lifecycle – scoping the problem and writing PRDs with our PMs, designing the system, and building it through to GA launch • Build the GPU and network virtualization stack: the hypervisor, kernel, and SDN work that makes GPU compute and DC networking high-performance, portable, and strongly isolated across heterogeneous hardware • Build and maintain our in-DC IaaS layer: the services, Kubernetes operators, and libraries that provision and manage compute, storage, and networks in our data centers, including VMs, parallel filesystems, VPCs, and InfiniBand partitions. Implement and harden the bring-up path for a new Vera Rubin data center with thousands of GPUs • Scale the distributed GPU scheduling and global management plane: the control-plane services behind on-demand and reserved clusters, including the automation that onboards new capacity and raises per-cluster limits • Harden the monitoring and automated remediation layer for fault tolerance: the work into milestones that ship incrementally, and improve the reliability of what’s already in production • Raise the bar around you: code review, design feedback, and mentoring junior engineers •
