Position Overview Zynga is looking for an Engineering Manager in the Chicago, Austin or Toronto areas to lead a newly formed Core Services team within our larger Development and Infrastructure Systems organization in the Central Technology group. Core Services is a small, distributed team with a mix of engineering skill levels that builds and maintains a number of central services critical to both Central Technology and all games across Zynga. The optimal candidate will have experience as a senior engineer working on and maintaining large-scale distributed backend services, and at least four years of experience as an Engineering Manager. You will work with the team on a day-to-day basis in partnership with the technical lead to ensure timely, high quality outcomes and technical excellence. What You'll Do: • Ensure the team has clearly defined and scoped projects, overseeing timeline scoping and task breakdown with the engineers • Manage a pod of engineers on the team on a daily basis, both in their tasks as well as morale, career growth and future planning • Drive standards for technical excellence within the team in partnership with the technical lead • Review and give feedback to engineering architectures • Help to monitor and improve engineering process • Balance maintenance of a wide variety of live services and tools with updates and forward-looking enhancements and refactoring initiatives. What You Bring: • B.Sc. in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent education / work experience • 4+ Years experience as an Engineering Manager • 4+ Years industry experience as a Software Engineer • Ability to build and maintain roadmaps and schedules for a diverse set of services, balancing support with new projects • Ability to manage, mentor and lead a distributed team of engineers. • Able to work collaboratively with engineering stakeholders including production, product, and customer engineering teams. • Hands-on experience with low-latency systems and massive-scale backend online services infrastructure in an AWS environment • A background building backend services in modern languages such as Java or Golang, with the depth to give meaningful feedback and mentorship on microservice architecture, API design, and implementation on a large-scale platform. • Experience with infrastructure-as-code and Kubernetes deployments • Updates knowledge constantly, always seeking to learn about new technologies • Excellent communication skills Bonus Points: • Experience defining and maintaining SLOs for live services • Experience with test-driven development and testing frameworks • Familiar with observability and monitoring platforms such as Datadog • Experience with JS frameworks, both frontend and backend • Experience with IaC frameworks such as Terraform and Helm • Comfortable with low-level investigation of infrastructure and services • Mobile development experience with a focus on iOS and/or Android What We Offer You: Zynga offers a world-class benefits package that helps support and balance the needs of our teams.
