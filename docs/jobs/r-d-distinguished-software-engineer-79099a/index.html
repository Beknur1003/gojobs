Job Description: Job Description Join the Avi Engineering team as a Distinguished Engineer to deliver Load balancing and Security solutions for the AI Era. The team has a storied history of innovation and an extensive patent portfolio. We foster a culture of intellectual curiosity turning creative ideas into tangible solutions. You’ll fit right in if you're someone who adopts a first-principles approach to problem-solving, love deep-diving into distributed systems to optimize scale and performance, and enjoy the challenge of making data flow seamlessly at a massive scale. This on-site position is based in Palo Alto, California. You will join a global, cross-border team of talented engineers operating across India, Germany, and the USA. Key Responsibilities As a Distinguished Engineer, you will be architecting and building high-performance, cloud-scale load balancing and security solutions. You will play a critical role in envisioning and delivering highly resilient, microservices-based solutions in the emerging world of AI based workloads. • Architectural Leadership: Lead the design and implementation of Load balancing and Security solutions for AI workloads, including but not limited to AI agents, LLMs, and Gateways. • Scale & Performance: Build and optimize data pipelines capable of handling millions of records, ensuring the platform scales elastically in AI environments. • Tech Stack Orchestration: Leverage a sophisticated stack to build durable, stateful workflows to manage complex backend processes. • Data Strategy: Design schemas and optimize queries across a polyglot persistence layer (OpenSearch, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Neo4j) to provide time sensitive insights to customers. • Mentorship: Act as a key technical leader by conducting deep-dive code reviews, driving engineering best practices, and mentoring junior engineers. Required Skills & Experience Professional Experience • Education: BS/MS/PhD in Engineering Computer Science or a related technical field. • Experience : Bachelors with 20+ years; Masters with 18+ years; PhD with 15+ years of related experience • Proven experience building complex highly distributed systems • Proven experience leading the end-to-end lifecycle of a product, from initial design to production deployment and maintenance. Programming Proficiency • Expert Level (GoLang & Python): Deep mastery of Go (concurrency, profiling, and performance tuning) and Python (data manipulation and backend services). • Moderate Level (C++): Ability to maintain and optimize performance-critical components or interface with lower-level system libraries. Cloud-Native Architecture & API Design • Microservices at Scale: Proven experience designing and delivering production-grade microservices for high-throughput, cloud-scale environments. • Communication Protocols: Proficiency in gRPC, Google Protocol Buffers (Protobuf), and RESTful API design, with a deep understanding of the HTTP/S stack. • Data Persistence & Search: Hands-on experience with one or more of the following: OpenSearch/Elasticsearch, PostgreSQL, Redis, or Neo4j (or their equivalents). Desired Skills & Qualifications • Networking Domain Expertise: Strong understanding of L4–L7 networking, TCP/IP, HTTP/S protocols, and load-balancing principles. • Advanced Data Engineering: Background in building data pipelines specifically for security and intelligence platforms (e.g., threat detection, anomaly detection, or predictive analytics). • Analytics Background: Familiarity with time-series data, stream processing, and large-scale data visualization backend logic.
