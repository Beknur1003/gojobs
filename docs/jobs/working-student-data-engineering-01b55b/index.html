Working Student Data Engineering

Bosch Group Cluj-Napoca, Cluj, ro опубликовано 25 сентября 2026

Гибрид Стажёр

We are looking for university students that are interested in gaining experience in Data Engineering. As a Working Student in the Data Engineering group, you will work with us on creating software products with a strong focus on security, quality and automation for continuous data flow in the scope of vehicle-to-cloud ecosystem, working together with an internationally distributed team, in a hybrid working model. Become part of our team and join us in creating state of the art data products. As a member of our team you take part in topics such as: • Develop data integration solutions for large scale automotive R&D applications • Design, test and document, reliable and excellent quality data integration solutions • Design Proof-of-concepts and pitch for implementation. • Contribute to the architecture and implementation of software products for (near)-real time processing. • Develop solutions for scalable and automatic data processing, involving Extract, Load, Transform (ETL/ELT) in Big Data frameworks • Create and maintain data visualizations in close collaboration with domain experts • Work in an agile environment with an international team of experienced colleagues • Learn new technologies under the guidance of your mentor Technology stack: • Python (with focus on data libraries: pandas, numpy, scipy etc.) Optional: Golang, Scala, Java • Apache Spark / Pyspark • Big Data platforms: on premises (Hadoop) and Cloud (Azure Databricks/Synapse/Data Factory) • Automation: Azure DevOps/Pipelines / Apache Airflow / Jenkins • Streaming and Messaging: Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, MQTT • Virtualization: Docker/Kubernetes • Relational DBs (Oracle, mySQL) and noSQL DBs (MongoDB) • Data Visualization (e.g. Tableau, PowerBI) • Version source control (e.g. Git, Bitbucket) • Bachelor or master’s studies in progress in Computer Science, Automation, Electronics and Telecommunications, Informatics, Mathematics or comparable fields • Basic Python programming skills • Affinity to working with data • Organized, accurate, thorough, and able to monitor work for quality • Excellent communication skills in English, both spoken and written LikeABosch Benefits: Your work-life balance is valuable to us, so we offer you: • 25 Days of annual leave, because work-life balance is essential to us; • Flexible working hours. Your health and well-being is important to us, so we have you covered: • Medical subscription; • Accident insurance; • Sport activities and well-being initiatives. We believe in a Life-Long Learning approach, so you can take advantage of: • Technical and soft skills trainings; • Access to e-learning platforms; • Local and Global career development programs; • Opportunity to attend conferences; • Bookster subscription. You will be part of a product company. You will contribute to developing end-to-end products that are delivered to Global OEMs. On top of these, we are offering: • A monthly budget which can be used for several different services on an online benefit platform; • Annual bonus; • Lunch discounts. Ready to work LikeABosch? We live by our values, have an open feedback culture and there is plenty of room for innovative ideas. All because a team is only as valuable as its members.
