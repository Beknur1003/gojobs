About Vanguard Founded in 1975, Vanguard is one of the world's leading investment management companies. The firm offers investments, advice, and retirement services to tens of millions of individual investors around the globe—directly, through workplace plans, and through financial intermediaries. Vanguard’s India Office Vanguard’s office in India is a significant milestone in our global expansion. We are committed to establishing an enduring technology center in Hyderabad, Telangana and are excited to be adding talent who will focus on Artificial Intelligence (AI), mobile, and cloud-based technologies that drive our business outcomes and deliver a world-class experience for our clients. Role Summary Provides technical leadership for mission-critical liquidity management and cash management platforms supporting Vanguard investment operations. Responsible for architecture, design, engineering quality, operational excellence, mentoring engineers, and driving modernization initiatives. The role is an Individual Contributor leadership position, not a people manager role. The expectation is to coach, mentor, and elevate the technical capability of the team while remaining hands-on in design and development. Key Responsibilities Technical Leadership • Lead design and implementation of complex software solutions. • Serve as the primary technical advisor for the team. • Drive architecture decisions with architects and senior stakeholders. • Ensure solutions meet scalability, resiliency, security, and performance requirements. Team Development • Coach and mentor engineers. • Create growth opportunities for TS02 engineers. • Ensure quality engineering practices are consistently followed. • Drive peer reviews and technical excellence. Product Ownership • Operate as part of a product team with end-to-end accountability. • Own development, production support, reliability, and continuous improvement. • Lead incident response and problem resolution efforts. Business Partnership • Collaborate with business stakeholders and portfolio management teams. • Translate investment and liquidity management requirements into engineering solutions. • Communicate effectively with both technical and non-technical audiences. Modernization & Innovation • Drive adoption of cloud-native patterns. • Support modernization of liquidity management systems. Required Technical Skills • Minimum 8 years of experience in software development, architecture, and system integration. • Bachelor’s degree (B.E./B.Tech) in Computer Science or IT, or Bachelor’s in Computer Applications (BCA), or Master’s in Computer Applications (MCA). • Learning mindset & coaching ability, Product ownership mentality, financial domain knowledge Backend • Strong experience in Golang, Python, and/or Java • Hands-on experience with API design and microservices architecture • Experience with PostgreSQL and DynamoDB • Event-driven and distributed systems experience preferred Frontend • Angular and/or React • Ability to contribute across the full stack when required ​Cloud & DevOps AWS Cloud, ECS / Fargate, Lambda, S3, Storage Gateway, IAM, CloudFormation, GitHub, CI/CD pipelines Engineering Practices Agile delivery (Scrum and Kanban), Jira, Confluence, TDD / automated testing, Production support and operational ownership, Experience using AI-assisted development tools (GitHub Copilot, Claude, etc.) preferred Location This role is based in Hyderabad, Telangana at Vanguard’s India office.
