About the Job zerohash is looking for an experienced Engineering Manager to lead the team architecting and building our open banking data aggregation platform. This is a high-performing team tackling deeply technical, adversarial problems in browser internals and secure automation. We’re looking for a true technologist with a love of programming and a drive for technical excellence. We believe the best managers are technologists first, with a product mindset, who seek to make a larger impact across the organization. You will lead the team building secure, agent-based automation infrastructure (e.g., Chrome Manifest V3 extensions, iOS WKWebViews), transforming unstructured web data into programmable APIs that bridge traditional finance and digital assets. Responsibilities • Research and design new methods that push the boundaries of secure web automation • Lead a team of engineers building the core automation runtime, guiding architecture for resilient UI interactions, reliable programmatic data entry, and robust session routing. • Partner closely with product and program managers to ensure the open banking roadmap is well understood and the team can plan and execute on expected outcomes. • Work with the team on technical planning to ensure requirements — such as secure network interception, complex multi-step authentication flows (MFA, Passkeys), and session management — are captured in the technical solution. • Partner with other Engineering Managers to drive the broader technical roadmap forward. • Support the engineering team in achieving a high level of technical excellence in browser automation, extension development, and secure data extraction. • Manage processes to help the team do its best work and interface effectively with the rest of zerohash. • Recruit, coach, and develop engineers, specifically those specializing in web automation, browser internals, and distributed systems. • Help engineers establish career development plans within the evolving open banking and web automation space. • Contribute to engineering-wide initiatives as a member of zerohash’s engineering management team. • Participate in essential technical, design, and product discussions with leaders across product and operations teams. • Work with a wide range of stakeholders to build a clear vision for your team’s role in the open banking ecosystem. • Oversee the success of your team’s projects, driving resiliency, scalability, and coverage across dynamic DOM structures, varying mobile/desktop portal designs, and application suspension states. • Introduce industry-leading engineering patterns and practices that give the team confidence to operate at scale. • Make consistent development contributions so as to maintain competency with the codebase and system architecture. Requirements • 10+ years of hands-on software engineering experience, deeply focused on advanced web automation, browser internals, extension development, or complex frontend-to-backend data extraction. • 5+ years of engineering management experience, including managing senior and staff-level engineers. • BS or MS degree in Computer Science or Engineering OR equivalent years of work experience. • Proven track record of managing teams that have delivered complex, production-grade browser automation, extension, or data infrastructure products. • Deep proficiency in TypeScript and JavaScript, specifically regarding DOM manipulation, modern web APIs, and strict browser execution environments. • Strong backend experience, ideally with Golang (or Node.js/Python), to guide the design of operational infrastructure. • Strong security fundamentals: deep understanding of secure message passing across trust boundaries (JS bridges, isolated vs. main worlds), secure authentication handshakes, and mitigating DOM-based injection risks. • Experience guiding teams through distributed systems challenges, specifically managing persistent connections and stateful, multi-hop sessions across ephemeral client runtimes. • Comfort with ambiguity; the ability to independently lay out and test clear hypotheses, and to coach a team to do the same without well-defined direction. Preferred • Proven track record of shipping production-grade browser extensions (Chrome MV3) or complex web automation within mobile native environments (iOS WKWebView, Android WebView). • Strong understanding of WebAuthn / Passkey protocols and how to integrate them into automated flows. • Previous engineering leadership experience at a major financial data aggregator or building zero-knowledge/client-side data extraction tools. • Understanding of cryptocurrency and blockchain technology, including major protocols, on-chain transaction flows, and common crypto security practices.
