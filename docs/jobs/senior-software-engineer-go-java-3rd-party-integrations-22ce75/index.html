Our Purpose At SentinelOne, we are driven by a clear purpose: to give the advantage to those who secure our future. As AI reshapes how organizations build, operate, and innovate, the responsibility to protect them becomes more critical than ever. When you join SentinelOne, your work helps protect global enterprises, critical infrastructure, and the technologies shaping tomorrow. If you are motivated by meaningful challenges and want your impact to be real, measurable, and global, you will find purpose here. About Us SentinelOne is a company at the intersection of AI and security, pioneering a new operating model for cybersecurity. Our AI-native platform unifies protection across endpoint, cloud, identity, data, and AI systems to deliver autonomous detection and response with clarity and speed. By combining real-time analytics, intelligent automation, and a unified data foundation, we reduce noise, simplify complexity, and empower security teams to focus on what truly matters. Our teams are builders, problem-solvers, and innovators committed to shaping the future of security. If you are excited to solve hard problems alongside talented, mission-driven people, we invite you to help us build a safer future for humanity. What Are We Looking For? We’re looking for people who are relentlessly curious and committed to continuous learning. AI is reshaping every function across our business, and we enable every team member, regardless of role or level, to build fluency in AI tools and concepts. Those who thrive here actively seek out new solutions, experiment thoughtfully, and apply what they learn to drive better, faster, smarter outcomes. As a Senior SW Engineer you will work on Singularity Marketplace - multi-tenant, event-driven platform that enables third-party integration applications to safely deploy, execute, and stream data across our AI SIEM ecosystem. You'll help build the next version of the Marketplace Catalog - the front door for every third-party integration our customers discover, evaluate, and install. You'll be re-thinking how partners publish and version their applications, how customers browse and trust what they find, and the APIs and services that make discovery fast and reliable at scale. You'll have the opportunity to build products using cutting-edge AI-native development practices, and integrate AI throughout the development lifecycle - augmenting design, code generation, testing, and security practices to increase velocity while maintaining the highest standards of code quality and security. What Will You Do? Primary responsibilities include: • Design, build, test, document, deploy, and operate large-scale applications, primarily in Go and partially in Java. • Contribute to architecting high-volume, low-latency services capable of processing millions of events per second. • Translate business and functional requirements into robust, scalable, operable solutions that work well within the overall data architecture. • Participate and collaborate with cross-functional teams in the organization to understand the business requirements and to deliver solutions that can scale. • Improve system reliability and data integrity through observability, performance analysis, capacity planning, and operational excellence. • Understand & maintain existing codebase by regular re-factoring and applying requested fixes and features. • Evaluate and adopt technologies that help solve challenges associated with scale, reliability, and large volumes of data. • Use AI-assisted engineering workflows thoughtfully to accelerate development without compromising quality or security. • Help establish and promote engineering practices that improve code quality, security, testing, and delivery velocity. • Participate in technical discussions and design reviews, provide high-quality code reviews, and mentor other engineers. What Skills and Knowledge Will You Bring? Ideal candidates will have: • Strong proficiency in at least one high-level programming language, preferably Go and/or Java, with the ability and interest to work across both • Deep understanding of distributed systems, event-driven architectures, microservices, concurrency, and data processing • Comfort with AI-native development practices - leveraging AI tools and workflows to enhance code quality, security, and development velocity • You care about delivering customer value and want to hear directly from your customers on how to improve and evolve your applications • A collaborative approach to development through design discussions, code reviews, and cross-functional partnerships • Familiarity with AWS and/or GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, Postgres, Redis, and Kafka (big advantage) • Ability to swiftly delve into new products and comprehend their inner workings • Proven capacity to collaborate effectively with both local and remote teams across various time zones • Excellent communication skills, as you'll regularly liaise with product and other engineering teams • A huge plus would be previous experience in developing security-related products Why SentinelOne? AI is redefining how the world operates and rewriting the rules of security in real time, and SentinelOne was built for this moment.
