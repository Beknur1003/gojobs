The Message Delivery team is the backbone of Omnisend’s multichannel communication stack — ensuring that millions of marketing messages reliably reach inboxes, phones, and browsers every day. We’re responsible for the reliable, fast, and compliant delivery of email, SMS, and push notifications. Our scope includes: Managing message sending infrastructure; Enabling message rendering and personalization across channels; Powering message tracking, analytics, and logs for customers and support; Collaborating with Product, Support, and Customer Success to deliver high-quality CX We are looking for a Senior Full-stack Engineer with a preference for backend development. People that are proactive, product-minded and enthusiastic about crafting the impactful software product for our customers find a best fit at Omnisend. What you’ll do @Omnisend: • Contribute significantly to product development decisions • Design, develop, and test features • Resolve technical challenges for seamless system functionality and optimal performance • Maintain features with effective monitoring, logging, and alerting • Share best practices with team members We expect that you’ll bring: • Hands-on experience (5+ years) implementing and maintaining scalable, highly available, high load systems • Customer centricity • Ideally, experience with Golang or alternatively proficiency in any backend programming language with the willingness to learn Golang • Familiarity with modern software build pipelines and tools • Proficiency in any database • Experience with real time distributed messaging tools, like NSQ, Pub/Sub • Working experience with any cloud provider (at Omnisend we use Google Cloud Services) • Experience with event based architecture How we work and what we value: • Company-wide OKR • Empowered product teams that solve customer and business problems • Objective-based planning and experimentation-driven delivery • Product-led growth • We look at product metrics daily, we speak to our customers and do it often • Outcomes and bringing value to our customers over outputs and feature factories • Ship to learn.
