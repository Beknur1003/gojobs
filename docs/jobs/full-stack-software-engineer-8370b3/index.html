About Us We are a venture-backed manufacturing startup building the most advanced automated production facilities in the United States. We are on a mission to make American manufacturing economically viable — through intelligent machinery, automation, and a relentless focus on execution. We are early, we are moving fast, and we are looking for exceptional people who want to build something that matters. Role Summary We’re hiring a Full-Stack Software Engineer - someone who will design and build the software systems that run our machines and the business around them. This role owns the full stack: real-time machine data, backend services, and the operator interfaces used to monitor and control production. It is a hands-on, ownership-heavy role for someone who has experience shipping products in fast-paced startup environments. Responsibilities • Design and build full-stack web applications for internal tooling, machine monitoring, operations management, and public store fronts • Architect and implement a custom ERP system from scratch - production scheduling, inventory tracking, equipment status, and order management with a high degree of automation and minimal manual data entry • Build machine orchestration interfaces with real-time dashboards and control surfaces that integrate with industrial equipment over well-known protocols • Design and maintain web applications and services that solve core problems • Contribute to development of a robust CI/CD pipeline - automated testing, containerized builds, and deployment • Build AI and automation into the systems that power the manufacturing floor • Work directly alongside controls and electrical engineers to define integration contracts between software and machine hardware Preferred Experience • 6+ years of full-stack software engineering experience, including meaningful time at startups with broad ownership of the application stack • Experience building and supporting live applications and services • Broad knowledge of software architecture and able to consider the tradeoffs between centralized vs distributed and monolith vs microservice • Deep experience with building multi-tiered applications and services in Golang • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript proficiency for building functional, data-dense internal tooling interfaces • RDBMS and PostgreSQL fluency in schema design and writing and reading raw SQL • Experience working in a CI/CD environment with test, build, and release pipelines • Care deeply about engineering excellence with Git hygiene, documentation, and constructive code review • Comfortable using AI to increase velocity and reduce defects but to implement everything or make every decision • Effective communicator and collaborator in a distributed team environment • Flexible with schedule and hours; thrives in a startup environment where priorities shift and urgency is real Nice to Have • Familiarity with OPC-UA or other industrial automation and communication protocols • Knowledge of event streaming technologies like NATS or MQTT • Experience with document or time-series databases • Container orchestration with Kubernetes, ECS, Fargate or Cloud Run • Prior work experience in a manufacturing domain • Experience at an early-stage hard tech or deep tech startup • Any prior team lead or mentorship experience Additional • Willingness to travel as needed for vendor visits, factory acceptance testing, and on-site equipment commissioning • Comfort with a startup pace - this role demands flexibility, self-direction, and a bias toward getting things done over waiting for perfect conditions
