About Us Symbio, part of the Aussie Broadband Group, is revolutionising global communications. Our platform delivers the full communications stack, from carrier infrastructure to enterprise collaboration solutions, across multiple Asia-Pacific markets. Listed on the Australian Stock Exchange (ASX: ABB), the Aussie Broadband Group collectively supplies more than 1 million telecommunications services, operates two Tier 1 voice providers in Australia and owns fibre infrastructure. We are looking for an enthusiastic, curious and driven Graduate Software Engineer to join our team commencing 9 February 2027. This role is based in Sydney, with a hybrid work model that combines office and remote working. What you’ll be doing • Design, develop, maintain and support software applications and platforms. • Build new features and capabilities that meet customer and business requirements. • Contribute to high-quality, scalable and resilient software solutions. • Collaborate with Engineering, Product and Operations teams to solve complex business challenges. • Participate in Agile ceremonies and work closely with business and technology stakeholders. • Troubleshoot, test and continuously improve existing applications and services. • Learn and apply engineering best practices, coding standards and modern development methodologies. What we're looking for • A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline completed within the last two years. • 0-3 years' professional experience in software development, software engineering, or a related field. • Exposure to programming languages such as Golang, Java, Python, JavaScript, or similar. • Understanding of software development principles and object-oriented programming. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • A passion for technology and creating customer-focused solutions. • A desire to learn, innovate and continuously improve. • Excellent communication and collaboration skills. • Curiosity, resilience and a team-first mindset. • A commitment to ongoing learning and professional development. We're looking for people who are curious, motivated and eager to learn. If you bring the right attitude, we'll help you build the skills.
