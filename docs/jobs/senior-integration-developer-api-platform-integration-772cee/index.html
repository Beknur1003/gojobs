Senior Integration Developer (API & Platform Integration) 12 Month Contract + 12 Month Extension Option | Hybrid Working | Multiple Locations We are seeking 2 x Senior Integration Developers to join a major enterprise technology transformation program delivering modern API and integration capabilities across a large and complex environment. This is an exciting opportunity to play a key role in the delivery of a new API Integration Platform, working with modern integration technologies, cloud-based services and agile delivery teams to build secure, scalable and reliable digital services. About the Role As a Senior Integration Developer, you will work within a high-performing multidisciplinary team responsible for the design, development and implementation of APIs and integration services connecting internal and external systems. You will contribute to the ongoing enhancement of a strategic integration platform, helping deliver innovative digital solutions while following modern engineering and DevOps practices. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop and maintain secure, scalable APIs and integration services. • Build integrations between internal and external systems using RESTful services and web technologies. • Develop and support modern API platforms and integration architectures. • Produce and maintain technical specifications and system documentation. • Collaborate closely with development, testing, architecture and operations teams. • Participate in agile ceremonies and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives. • Ensure solutions align with architecture standards, security requirements and best practices. Essential Skills & Experience • Experience with enterprise integration platforms such as Kong, Workato, Boomi, MuleSoft, Salesforce, or similar technologies. • Strong experience developing and supporting RESTful APIs and GraphQL services. • Hands-on development experience with GoLang. • Experience working with source control tools such as GitLab and package-based development approaches. • Experience implementing and supporting CI/CD pipelines. • Strong problem-solving and technical design capabilities. • Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders. Highly Regarded • Experience with Jira, Confluence and VS Code. • Knowledge of DevOps principles and modern software engineering practices. • Experience working in agile delivery teams. • Passion for accessible and inclusive digital experiences. • Ability to manage competing priorities in fast-paced delivery environments. What's on Offer? • Initial 12-month contract. • 12-month extension option. • Hybrid working arrangements. • Locations available across: • Brisbane • Adelaide • Canberra • Geelong • Richmond • Surry Hills • Opportunity to work on a high-profile national digital transformation initiative. • Collaborative and supportive team culture. • Exposure to modern API, integration and cloud technologies.
