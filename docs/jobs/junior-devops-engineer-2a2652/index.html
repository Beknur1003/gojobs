Come join our team to contribute to the Docklin Orchestrator pipeline system used in complex telecommunications environments. We are looking for a Junior DevOps Engineer with solid Linux fundamentals, basic scripting or cloud-native knowledge, good English communication, and a proactive learning mindset. You will support to develop feature, platform operations, troubleshooting and practice with general technologies used in Radio system such as Golang and Python programming, DaaS (Docklin as a Service), Argo workflow, ArgoCD, Linux, Bash script, microservices architecture, and Kubernetes technologies, CI/CD pipelines, ..., This is a growth-oriented role. You are not expected to be fully productive from day one; structured knowledge transfer, shadowing, and hands-on practice will help you gradually take ownership of real tasks. Your responsibilities include but are not limited to the following: • Develop the Docklin pipeline system • Understand the requirement, design, implement and test new requirements • Troubleshoot issue, suggest and implement solution when needed • Take design ownership and make architecture changes in the long-term • Continuously improve together with the team • Stay current with emerging cloud technologies and industry trends, applying new knowledge to improve and innovate our solutions. • Support customer/stakeholder • Communicate progress, questions, and risks clearly in English, and collaborate proactively with local and international team members. At Endava, you will live with Agile & Lean principles where employees are empowered at all levels to collaborate cross-functionally, to take pride in collective ownership, to improve yourself by continuously learning, practicing & sharing with others. If you have just graduated from University with a Bachelor Degree in either Information Technology, Computer Science or a related degree with: • Have passion with automating workflow (e.g. building, testing, deploying, …) • Familiar with DevOps tools and practices, including containerization (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm), CI/CD pipelines. • Knowledge about Linux/Unix working environment • Knowledge about Bash script, Python • High teamwork spirit • Eager to learn new technologies • Good at English communication Nice to have • Hands-on exposure through internships, labs, coursework, or personal DevOps projects. • Experience with Golang language • Familiarity with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenSearch/ELK.
