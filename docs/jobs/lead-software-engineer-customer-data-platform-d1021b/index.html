Get to Know the Team • Clickstream Intelligence Platform (Scribe) is Grab's in-house app instrumentation and insights platform that captures and analyzes user clickstream activity across all Grab apps. The platform supports observability, real-time personalization, and business analytics by processing tens of billions of events per hour at petabyte scale. • Customer Data Platform (CDP) enables Grab Teams to onboard and register Grab's customer data and make it available for Online Serving, ML Models, Segmentation, and Real-time targeting to power personalization use cases across the company. Our platform is leveraged across many mission-critical Grab services such as app personalization, experimentation, ads serving, and marketing. Get to Know the Role We are looking for a Technical Lead for either Scribe or CDP team that has a strong breadth of different technical stacks: • Web Portal: React.JS and Golang Backend. • Online Serving: AWS infrastructure, MySQL, Redis, AWS Dynamo DB, Scylla DB. • Data Processing / Storage: Apache Flink, Apache Spark, Trino, StarRocks, Hive, and Delta Lake. • Applied AI / ML: LLM, Python Backend, Vector DB. You will report to Engineering Manager in Singapore and work onsite at Grab Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia office. The Critical Tasks You Will Perform • Build platform capabilities to facilitate scalable onboarding of user data that can be easily re-used across the company. • Improve the serving capabilities of CDP's user data. • Develop scalable real-time data pipelines and architecture that is cost efficient, while allowing non-technical teams to use these real-time capabilities. • Lead an engineering team with diverse technical stacks. What Essential Skills You Will Need • Software Engineering, Computer Science, or related undergraduate degree. • Minimum of 7+ years of experience in Software Engineering with well-proven track record of leading an engineering team with diverse skillsets. • Proficient in at least one of the following programing languages: Python, Go, or Scala • Experience developing large-scale backend systems and has good understanding of technical trade offs and evaluations needed to allow high load, low latency systems, while maintaining system reliability and cost efficiency. • Experience building APIs, working with OLTP DBs, and operating in a distributed systems environment. • Strong English communications with prior work experience in regional and global environment Life at Grab We care about your well-being at Grab, here are some of the global benefits we offer: • We have your back with Term Life Insurance and comprehensive Medical Insurance. • With GrabFlex, create a benefits package that suits your needs and aspirations. • Celebrate moments that matter in life with loved ones through Parental and Birthday leave, and give back to your communities through Love-all-Serve-all (LASA) volunteering leave • We have a confidential Grabber Assistance Programme to guide and uplift you and your loved ones through life's challenges • Balancing personal commitments and life's demands are made easier with our FlexWork arrangements such as differentiated hours What we stand for at Grab We are committed to building an inclusive and equitable workplace that provides equal opportunity for Grabbers to grow and perform at their best.
