Build your reliability engineering career by keeping the firm’s foundational infrastructure resilient, observable, and automated alongside a team of experienced engineers. As a Software Engineer III at JPMorgan Chase within the Infrastructure Platforms Foundational Services (IPFS) SRE organization, you use code and infrastructure automation to configure, maintain, monitor, and optimize the platforms and services in your scope. You solve complex, broad reliability problems with simple, straightforward solutions and help apply service level indicators (SLIs) and service level objectives (SLOs) to resolve issues before they impact customers. As a significant contributor to your team, you share your knowledge of end-to-end operations, availability, reliability, and scalability. Job Responsibilities • Designs, develops, tests, and maintains secure, high-quality production code and tooling that enhance the availability, reliability, observability, and scalability of platforms and services in scope • Uses automated CI/CD pipelines to deliver infrastructure and configuration changes that improve reliability with staged rollouts, automated change validations and rollback • Implements and tunes service level indicators (SLIs), service level objectives (SLOs), and SLO-based alerting, using error budgets and analysis of telemetry and incident data to inform reliability priorities • Participates in on-call, triaging and resolving incidents. Contributes to blameless post-incident reviews and completes corrective actions • Identifies and eliminates toil by building automation, self-healing remediation, and tooling, and maintains accurate runbooks and operational documentation • Uses enterprise-authorized AI capabilities within the work environment to accelerate reliability work (e.g., incident triage, log and telemetry analysis, runbook and test automation), validating outputs and handling operational data according to sensitivity and security requirements • Contributes to resiliency testing, including failover and recovery validation, chaos engineering, and GameDay exercises • Collaborates with technical experts and stakeholders to solve complex problems Required qualifications, capabilities, and skills • Formal training or certification on software engineering or site reliability engineering concepts and 3+ years applied experience • Proficient in site reliability principles (SLIs/SLOs, error budgets, toil reduction) and observability tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, Dynatrace, or Splunk • Proficiency in at least one of Java, Go (Golang), or Python, with hands-on experience in application development, debugging, and automated testing. Working knowledge of Terraform, CI/CD tools, and containers (Docker, Kubernetes) • Solid understanding of the SDLC and agile practices, including code review, application resiliency, and security, with experience querying data stores (e.g., SQL) • Hands-on experience with production on-call, incident response, and root cause analysis • Solid Linux fundamentals and working knowledge of networking (TCP/IP, DNS, load balancing, routing, firewalls) and how to troubleshoot it • Experience using enterprise-authorized AI capabilities to improve engineering workflows, with strong validation habits and awareness of data sensitivity • Clear communicator who works well in a collaborative team with limited supervision Preferred qualifications, capabilities, and skills • Experience building AI-assisted or agentic automation (e.g., LangChain, MCP (Model Context Protocol), RAG (retrieval-augmented generation)) for operational use cases • Experience with on-premises data-center infrastructure such as enterprise networking, storage, data center services, or data protection and recovery • Experience with configuration management (e.g., Ansible, Puppet) and infrastructure monitoring (e.g., SNMP, NetFlow) • Experience building RESTful APIs and event-driven integrations (e.g., Kafka) • Knowledge of security and compliance in highly regulated industries. Exposure to chaos engineering and disaster recovery testing • Cloud or infrastructure certifications (e.g., AWS, Kubernetes CKA, Red Hat, Cisco) This position is subject to Section 19 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act. As such, an employment offer for this position is contingent on JPMorganChase’s review of criminal conviction history, including pretrial diversions or program entries.
