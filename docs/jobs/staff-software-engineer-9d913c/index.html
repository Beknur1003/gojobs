The weaponization of AI is driving a paradigm shift in the cyber security industry from proactive to preemptive security: clients must now invest more intelligence and effort in understanding their vulnerabilities before their adversaries do (Open AI). The only way to achieve this is to simulate future attack scenarios at scale, and to run them in high fidelity digital replicas of production environments (Gartner/Forrester). SimSpace serves as the AI Proving Grounds to generate hypersynthetic data which pressure tests controls, tools, teams, and agentic capabilities in full kill chain attacks hidden in realistic environmental traffic and patterns of life. Founded in 2015 by experts from U.S. Cyber Command and MIT Lincoln Laboratory, the platform was initially conceived to assist in Offensive Cyber Operations (OCO), to quickly replicate target environments to conduct mission rehearsal of human operators and their tools to deny (disrupt, degrade, destroy) or deceive (manipulate or exfiltrate data) adversaries. The company has since expanded to serve over a dozen allied governments in OCO/DCO as well as critical infrastructure enterprises around the world. The company is actively investing in the Americas, Asia Pacific, Europe and the Middle East through direct sales and channel partners. SimSpace has launched the AI Proving Grounds Consortium, leading the revolution by creating an open platform for the InfoSec ecosystem to train, validate and operationalize their agentic capabilities before committing to production. Job Summary You will lead the technical design of complete user and attack emulation workflows for AI Proving Grounds (AIPG). Customers need to configure activity across applications, verify its effects and recover when execution fails. This is an AI-native fullstack role with an emphasis on backend engineering. Use coding agents across design, implementation, tests and diagnosis, and build repeatable workflows for other engineers. You will write critical code and guide design across features and shared interfaces. The Engineering Manager owns people, staffing and delivery priorities. Scenario Development supplies the adversary content MCO integrates with legitimate user activity. What will you be doing at SimSpace? • Lead technical design across MCO features and implement critical paths spanning configuration, application interactions, emulation services, execution and result inspection. • Establish reusable agent instructions, repository context and spec-driven workflows. Build automated checks, review generated changes and coach engineers to use the workflow effectively within approved data and access boundaries. • Define APIs and versioned contracts for identity, state, permissions, supported environments and observable effects. Agree changes to shared components with their owning teams. • Build and review asynchronous execution, retries, cancellation, recovery and cleanup. For example, reconcile a timed-out action before retrying so a workflow does not duplicate a transaction. • Establish end-to-end tests that check actions under a declared identity, intended effects, compatibility and safe recovery or cleanup. Diagnose failures at the responsible component. • Coordinate interfaces with Scenario Development, AIPG DEN, evaluation, Range Automation and Platform engineers, making dependencies and acceptance responsibilities clear. • Mentor engineers through implementation and review. Document technical decisions and improve reusable components, development tools and diagnostics across features. What are the qualifications to apply? • Experience leading technical design across related features, contributing production code and helping other engineers carry the design through deployment and operation. • Evidence of shipping and maintaining software with AI coding agents and improving the workflow for others, including task decomposition, useful repository context, independent verification and correction of generated errors. • Production fullstack engineering experience, with strong backend service design and programming skills and the ability to work across application and execution boundaries. • Experience defining APIs and component contracts that remain usable as clients, systems and execution environments change. • Ability to diagnose failures involving application state, user or service identities, operating environments and asynchronous services. • Experience establishing automated tests, observability, security boundaries and operational recovery for integrated workflows. • Technical leadership through mentoring, code review, written designs and collaboration with neighboring component owners. Who you are • You connect technical decisions to customer behavior and test the workflow across the boundaries that could cause it to fail. • You make practical reuse decisions, simplify repeated integration work and explain the risks of shortcuts. • You improve how the team works with agents, judging progress through reliable delivery, review effort and rework. Nice to have but not required • Experience with user or adversary emulation, cybersecurity tooling or simulation platforms. • Experience with Windows or Linux integration, browser or desktop automation, or agent workflows. • Experience with Go, Python, containers or modern web applications. Our Tech Stack Includes The team works across Go and Python services, application and browser integrations, distributed execution, containers and automated qualification.
