Your Opportunity BDO Digital is looking for a Senior GCP Cloud Engineer to join their growing team. The individual will own the following responsibilities: • Develop cloud native solutions for a cutting-edge CI/CD ecosystem • Learn from a great team of fellow engineers in building real-world cloud scale solutions across public and private clouds • Contribute to a team of like-minded platform engineers building platforms in Azure and GCP public clouds • Promote automation and infrastructure best practices • Lead the installation, upgrades and integration of the current and future Kubernetes platform(s), technologies and tools. • Fearlessly face challenging technical problems, use your superpowers to solve them, and help your peers by sharing your unique solutions • Create awesome educational material for fellow engineers, like sample cloud native apps and starter code • Continuously learn new technical skills and practice by applying them in your day-to-day work • Provide mentorship to peers • Excellent oral and written communication skills, as well as technical, analytical, and problem-solving ability • Ability to work flexible hours when required • Develop criteria for and participates in the evaluation of third party vendors and software as well as due diligence exercises. • Manage all aspects of testing and verification ensuring all tasks are performed for all interfaces. • Responsible for defining, planning and executing high level team activities, such as recommendations for architecture and process improvements • Provide direction to support staff in conducting performance tuning and capacity planning activities within the Cloud Infrastructure platform. How do we define success for your role? • You demonstrate BDO's core values through all aspects of your work: Integrity, Respect and Collaboration • You understand your client’s industry, challenges, and opportunities; clients describe you as positive, professional, and delivering high-quality work • You identify, recommend, and are focused on effective service delivery to your clients • You share in an inclusive and engaging work environment that develops, retains and attracts talent • You actively participate in the adoption of digital tools and strategies to drive an innovative workplace • You grow your expertise through learning and professional development. Your experience and education Must have: • A passion for making developers more efficient through DevOps, CI/CD, and Infrastructure as Code • 3+ years of experience with Infrastructure Automation and Infrastructure as Code (GCP) • 3+ years of experience with cloud networking and security setup, configuration, and troubleshooting • 3+ years of experience with developing Terraform modules (preference Enterprise) • 3+ years of experience with Kubernetes (GKE) setup, configuration, and optimization • 3+ years of experience with Python or GoLang • Experience with Jenkins, GitHub, Ansible, Artifactory, Helm • Communication skills necessary to work on a geographically distributed team. Nice-to-Have: • HashiCorp Terraform Associate Certificate • CKA certification • Experience with K8 custom operators development • Experience developing software • Experience working in a SAFe, Kanban, or Scrum development methodology The expected range of compensation for this role is $84,000 - $128,000 annually.
