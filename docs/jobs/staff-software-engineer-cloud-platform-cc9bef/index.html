Summary Join Guidewire’s Product Development and Operations (PDO) team in Bengaluru, where we build and operate the industry’s most trusted cloud platform for P&C insurance. Our team is dedicated to delivering innovative, scalable, and secure solutions that empower insurers worldwide. We thrive in a collaborative, agile environment, leveraging modern technologies and a pairing culture to drive continuous improvement and customer success. As a Staff Software Engineer, you will play a pivotal role in designing and developing Guidewire’s multi-tenant cloud platform. You’ll tackle complex engineering challenges, contribute to the evolution of our cloud infrastructure, and help shape the future of insurance technology. This is an opportunity to work with cutting-edge tools, solve impactful problems, and be part of a team that values curiosity, learning, and technical excellence.
