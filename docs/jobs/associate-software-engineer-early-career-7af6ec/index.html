About us At Viasat, the work you do helps people stay connected in ways that matter, from everyday moments to critical missions across air, land, sea, and space. We're a place for people who are curious, bold, and motivated by meaningful work. Here, you'll have the opportunity to make an impact, explore new possibilities, and grow your career in ways that align with your interests and goals. When you're ready to take on something new, you'll find leaders who support your development and help you turn your ambition into action. What you'll do The Viasat Engineering function is responsible for designing, building and maintaining a robust and flexible global satellite and ground network that incorporates Viasat and partner assets to deliver internet services to customers worldwide. Our teams focus on innovation and creativity, enabling us to stay ahead of the curve in the rapidly evolving satellite communications industry. By leveraging their expertise and innovative spirit, these teams at Viasat are helping to bridge the digital divide and connect people, businesses, and communities worldwide. The day-to-day We're seeking a highly motivated Associate Software Engineer to join our Integration & Delivery team. • You will work on variety of projects including embedded (C, C++) to full stack web services development (Python, Go, AWS, Google Cloud) • You will work with ground systems project teams, getting hands-on experience solving problems, building, operating and validating software components for Viasat • Develop, test, and deploy software applications • Implement automation solutions to streamline workflows and reduce manual efforts • Participate in code reviews and regular team meetings • Collaborate with other developers and product managers • Adding new features to internal tools • Troubleshoot and resolve issues to maintain a high performing product Additional Details • This position is site-based. Site-based employees work 3+ days (60%+) per week from a Viasat office or work location within a standard five-day work week. Viasat embraces a flexible work environment and onsite/hybrid work is determined by individual roles and teams. Relocation assistance is available pending eligibility. What you'll need • Education: Completion of Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field. • Experience: This role is intended for someone with 0-3 years of full-time, relevant industry experience. • Citizenship: This position requires a valid Work Permit or Visa for the country you would be located and working from. • Additional: • Demonstrated interest in generative AI and AI-assisted software development through coursework, projects, or research. Experience using Claude or similar AI development tools is a plus. • Coding proficiency in at least one of the following languages: Python, Golang, Java, C/C++, Rust, Clojure, or similar languages • Familiarity or experience working with cloud services like Google Cloud Platform and/or AWS • Demonstrated ability to think out of the box, taking up initiatives and problem solving What will help you on the job • Coursework or experience in designing, writing, and testing software in at least one of the following domains: embedded systems, networking, communications, web services/full stack, data & analytics • Prior experience with modern software engineering tools and processes such as GitHub, Jira, K8s, Agile/Scrum, CI/CD, DevOps • Excellent oral and written communication skills • Ability to work autonomously and as part of a team Salary range $95,000.00 - $150,000.00 / annually.For specific work locations within San Jose, the San Francisco Bay area and New York City metropolitan area, the base pay range for this role is $118,000.00- $177,000.00/ annually At Viasat, we consider many factors when it comes to compensation, including the scope of the position as well as your background and experience.
