Senior Backend Engineer - Golang / Kubernetes / Private Cloud Location: Eindhoven, Netherlands (Hybrid - 2 days onsite per week) Contract Type: Contract 12 months Rate: €80-€90 per hour Hours: Full-time Language: Fluent Dutch required The Opportunity We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to join a team building and maintaining a private cloud platform using modern cloud-native technologies, including Golang, Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift. You will play a key role in a greenfield engineering environment, developing scalable backend services, automating infrastructure and contributing to the architecture of a secure, reliable and high-performing cloud platform. We are looking for someone who is passionate about technology, enjoys solving complex technical challenges and wants to contribute across the entire software development lifecycle. What You Will Be Doing • Designing and developing scalable backend services and microservices. • Building and maintaining a private cloud platform using Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift. • Developing platform orchestration and infrastructure automation solutions. • Implementing GitOps workflows, CI/CD pipelines and automated testing. • Improving platform reliability, security and observability using technologies such as OpenTelemetry. • Collaborating with experienced engineers on greenfield development and architectural decisions. Key Requirements • Strong experience in senior backend software engineering. • Hands-on experience with Golang, although strong experience with Rust, C# or Kotlin will also be considered. • Experience with Kubernetes and/or Red Hat OpenShift. • Strong understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems. • Experience with GitOps and modern deployment practices. • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, preferably GitHub Actions. • Experience with observability, monitoring and OpenTelemetry. • Experience with automated testing, TDD or BDD. • Experience working within Agile teams and greenfield environments. • Fluent Dutch communication skills. Desirable Experience • Private cloud and on-premise infrastructure. • Platform orchestration and automation. • On-premise virtualised networks. • Infrastructure as Code. • Building secure, scalable and highly available cloud-native platforms. Additional Information • €80-€90 per hour, all-inclusive. • Two days per week onsite in Eindhoven, with flexibility to attend customer locations. • Full-time engagement. • Willingness to transition into permanent employment through a Detavast arrangement. If you are an experienced backend engineer with a passion for cloud-native technologies, platform engineering and building complex systems from the ground up, I would love to hear from you. Apply directly or get in touch for a confidential conversation.
