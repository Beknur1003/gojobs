Whip Media is transforming how the world's largest media and entertainment companies manage, distribute, and monetize content across the global streaming supply chain. Our flagship product, Helix, is an AI-native system designed to radically optimize content operations — enabling data-driven decisions that maximize revenue, streamline distribution, and scale profitable strategies. Trusted by top studios, networks, and streaming platforms, we sit at the intersection of media and technology, redefining the business of content. Helix is our next-generation platform, built with long-term scale, efficiency, and operational excellence in mind, with Go as its primary codebase. Alongside it, we continue to run and evolve the established Java and Spring services that serve customers today, built on PostgreSQL, Python, and Amazon Web Services throughout. Our platform helps leading media and entertainment companies operate more efficiently, with greater visibility and control over digital distribution. The Role: As a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer, you'll work on a small development team designing, building, and maintaining mission-critical web-based software for major film studios, TV broadcasters, and cable operators in a high-energy environment. You'll work across both worlds: building new services in Go on Helix, and supporting and migrating what's already in production in Java and Spring. You'll also build the front-end features that go with your APIs — data-dense interfaces where the challenge is making large, complex result sets fast and understandable. We're also actively building AI into how we work and what we ship — agent-assisted development and LLM-powered features on top of our platform data. We're looking for someone who already works this way and can help set the bar for the rest of the team.
