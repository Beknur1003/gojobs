Description: SPS Commerce is a leading provider of cloud-based supply chain management solutions, serving a global network of retail trading partners. We foster a collaborative and inclusive work environment where innovation and continuous improvement are highly valued. Join SPS Commerce and be part of a dynamic team that's transforming the global retail supply chain! Position Summary: SPS Commerce’s Platform Engineering organization delivers the highly available infrastructure services and deployment automation that empower our product engineering teams to build and ship secure, reliable, and cost-effective software. We treat operations as a software engineering discipline by applying automation, infrastructure as code, automated testing, and rigorous code review to eliminate systemic failure risks at the source rather than reacting to recurring incidents. Our platform function is organized into four specialized pillars: Cloud Operations, Continuous Improvement, Container Orchestration, Runtime & Edge, and Global Deployment Platform. Working in close partnership with product and development teams, we take ideas from concept to production safely, quickly, and efficiently in that exact order of priority. Global Deployment Platform owns the path from commit to production: the CI/CD and deployment automation product teams use to build, release, and roll back software across SPS's global environments. The team makes deployments safe by default, fast, and consistent through reusable pipelines and extensions, deployment guardrails such as gates and locks, artifact management, and internal platform services that product teams can adopt with minimal friction. As a Platform Engineer you will engineer and operate platform services with increasing independence and deliver well-defined projects end to end. You will use automation and other technologies to intelligently cope with challenging failures while collaborating with various engineering organizations to resolve failure risks at the source. Key responsibilities: • Build reusable pipeline templates, Azure DevOps extensions, and deployment patterns that make the secure, reliable path the easy path • Build and maintain internal platform services and self-service tooling that product teams use to manage and deploy their services • Implement deployment guardrails such as automated checks, gates, environment locks, approvals, and rollback so deployments are safe by default • Implement secure deployment identity: OIDC workload identity federation for service connections, least-privilege use of IAM roles provisioned through the Cloud Operations process, and secrets handling in pipelines • Instrument the deployment path and maintain the team's deployment dashboards (e.g., deployment frequency, lead time, failure rate, and rollback rate) in partnership with Continuous Improvement, Observability using the company's shared observability tooling • Work with product teams to adopt the platform and to resolve deployment failures at the source • Operate and improve artifact repositories (e.g., JFrog Artifactory, Azure Artifacts) and the pipeline steps that publish, scan, and promote build artifacts • Participate in the on-call rotation and incident response, and drive remediation work to durable fixes • Collaborate with Technology Engineering, Development, and Product Management to help develop, scale, and improve production systems and services • Partner with service teams to provide appropriate documentation, cross-training, architecture planning, capacity management, and recommendations for future state • Write clean and correct code, write test plans, and identify code quality improvements when reviewing code • Build and operate AI-assisted automation on the deployment path, such as self-healing pipelines and automated triage of failed deployments, running on SPS platform infrastructure • Use AI coding assistants effectively: apply approved AI coding tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Claude) in day-to-day engineering, following Platform Engineering guidance for secure use with SPS CI/CD pipelines, code review processes, and IaC toolchains such as Terraform and CloudFormation Required Qualifications:  • 2+ years of experience in a similar role with a Bachelor's degree; or 1 year and a Master's degree; or a PhD without experience; or equivalent work experience • Experience with Python, Golang, or .NET with a software engineering mindset • Deep understanding of and hands-on experience with building and operating CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation on platforms such as Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, or Jenkins • Experience with infrastructure as code (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation) and immutable, scalable infrastructure • Experience with Linux, Git-based workflows, and Amazon Web Services; exposure to Microsoft Azure is a plus • Working knowledge of deployment identity and access: IAM roles, OIDC workload identity federation, service connections, and secrets management • Problem solving and collaboration skills, with the ability to deliver defined projects with limited guidance Preferred Experience: • Experience with Kanban or other Lean/Agile delivery practices • Experience building Azure DevOps / Github Actions extensions • Experience with progressive delivery techniques such as canary, blue/green, and feature flags • Experience with deploying to container and serverless platforms such as Kubernetes, ECS, and AWS Lambda • Experience with artifact management and software supply chain security • Experience with deployment metrics and DORA measurement Location:  This role is in our Warsaw office, Poland, and the team is in-office 2 days per week. What We Offer: • Team Mission: Build the core deployment platform used across SPS Commerce with long term meaningful architectural and automation work ahead. • Modern Tech: Work with Python, Go, .NET, Azure DevOps, Terraform, and AWS while building AI-assisted self-healing pipelines and automated failure triage. • International Team & Exposure: Collaborate daily with the Global Engineering team • Career Growth & Culture: Clear advancement paths backed by an active, collaborative Warsaw hub with frequent team events and celebrations.
