Zscaler (NASDAQ: ZS) accelerates digital transformation so customers can be more agile, efficient, resilient, and secure. The Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange platform protects thousands of customers from cyberattacks and data loss by securely connecting users, devices, and applications in any location. Distributed across 200+ public data centers globally and thousands of private sites at the edge, the SASE-based Zero Trust Exchange is the world’s largest in-line cloud security platform. We believe the future of work is Human + AI and are building an AI-native enterprise where human potential is amplified by machine intelligence to solve the world’s hardest security challenges. Driven by deep customer obsession, we are committed to the mission, outcome, and to each other. We bring these commitments to life through three core behaviors: ownership and collaboration, trust through outcomes and impact, and a challenge culture with ongoing feedback. Ready to make an impact at the company pioneering security transformation in the AI era? Join us at Zscaler. Role We are looking for a Principal Software Development Engineer, AI Security to join our team as a founding member and foundational architect of our AI Security team. In this high-impact Hybrid role (based in Santa Clara, CA or Bellevue, WA with a 3 days in office requirement), you will report to the Director of Software Development Engineering in the AI Security department. You will be responsible for designing resilient, low-latency security infrastructure and distributed systems engineered to scale for hundreds of millions of users. What You’ll Do (Role Expectations) • Architect, develop, and optimize a low-latency, high-throughput AI Security plane utilizing systems languages such as C++/Rust/Go, leveraging asynchronous I/O models and service-oriented architectures • Build resilient, distributed, and horizontally scalable systems, emphasizing concurrency, fault tolerance, and robust messaging protocols • Implement and maintain gRPC services and APIs to ensure seamless integration of the AI Security plane with control and orchestration infrastructure • Systematically enhance performance across the entire stack, including LLM models, by employing profiling tools for both kernel-space and user-space components • Lead complex, multi-functional technical initiatives, driving architectural roadmaps, system design reviews, technical documentation, code reviews, and mentorship of engineering staff Who You Are (Success Profile) • You act like an owner. You drive the mission forward with purpose, seamlessly bridging high-level architectural strategy with detailed, hands-on execution. • You thrive in ambiguity. You navigate complex, uncharted territory with confidence, seeing evolving requirements as the foundation to build scalable, meaningful systems. • You are a problem-solver. You actively run toward complex technical challenges, engineering resilient solutions that deliver maximum impact. • You are a high-trust collaborator. You deeply empathize with internal and external stakeholders, fostering a culture of constructive candor, mentorship, and shared ambition. • You are a continuous learner. You maintain relentless technical curiosity, constantly exploring innovative ways to enhance security, scalability, and system performance. What We’re Looking For (Minimum Qualifications) • Foundational understanding of AI/ML technologies and experience leveraging, securing, or positioning AI-driven solutions to optimize outcomes within your functional domain • 10+ years of software engineering experience designing and delivering production-grade systems • Strong hands-on systems programming experience in C++/Rust/Go, with a solid grasp of concurrency, memory management, and high-performance design • Proven ability to design and implement horizontally scalable, highly available, and observable distributed systems • Strong command of Linux internals, including kernel-user space interaction, networking, sockets, and namespaces • Demonstrated expertise in performance instrumentation, containerized environments, Git workflows, and CI/CD pipelines What Will Make You Stand Out (Preferred Qualifications) • Experience architecting real-time threat detection, input/output sanitization, or guardrail frameworks for LLM/generative AI applications, and validating AI-driven products using MLOps principles • Deep, advanced expertise in systems programming with Rust, specifically utilizing asynchronous runtimes such as Tokio or async-std for low-latency, mission-critical services • Expertise in performance optimization across Linux networking stacks, Kubernetes networking, service meshes, gRPC, Protobufs, and L4-L7 protocols #LI-YC2 #LI-Hybrid Zscaler’s salary ranges are benchmarked and are determined by role and level.
