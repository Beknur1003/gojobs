About Ajaib Ajaib is an Indonesian investment platform helping people access and manage investments across stocks, mutual funds, crypto, and other digital investment products. Trusted by more than three million customers, Ajaib is focused on making investing more accessible, affordable, and intuitive. As the platform continues to grow, building reliable, scalable, and secure technology is fundamental to serving customers and supporting the business. We’re looking for a Staff or Principal Backend Engineer to shape the architecture and technical direction of the backend systems powering Ajaib’s investment, crypto, money, platform, and shared-services products. The role You’ll be one of Ajaib’s most senior individual contributors, leading through technical expertise, architectural judgement, and influence rather than people management. You’ll work closely with Engineering Managers, Product Managers, senior engineers, and engineering leadership to solve complex technical problems and build reliable backend systems that support millions of customers. At Staff level, you’ll own architecture across multiple squads, lead cross-team initiatives, improve engineering standards, and mentor Senior Engineers. At Principal level, you’ll define long-term technical direction across a major engineering domain, lead company-wide technical initiatives, and act as a trusted technical adviser to engineering and product leadership. This is a hands-on technical-leadership role where you’ll work through ambiguity, influence decisions without formal authority, and balance short-term delivery with long-term technical sustainability.
