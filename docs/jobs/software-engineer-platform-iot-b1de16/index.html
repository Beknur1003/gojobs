Software Engineer, Platform IOT

Who we are: Motive empowers the people who run physical operations with tools to make their work safer, more productive, and more profitable. For the first time ever, safety, operations and finance teams can manage their drivers, vehicles, equipment, and fleet related spend in a single system. Combined with industry leading AI, the Motive platform gives you complete visibility and control, and significantly reduces manual workloads by automating and simplifying tasks. Motive serves nearly 100,000 customers – from Fortune 500 enterprises to small businesses – across a wide range of industries, including transportation and logistics, construction, energy, field service, manufacturing, agriculture, food and beverage, retail, and the public sector. Visit gomotive.com to learn more. About the Role: The Platform IoT team runs the device cloud that keeps Motive devices connected. We own the full path between devices and the cloud, including provisioning and identity, messaging, firmware updates and certificates, configuration delivery, data ingestion, and monitoring. Our platform supports more than 1 million connected devices across North America and Europe. As a Software Engineer on the team, you’ll build and monitor the services behind these systems and own projects end-to-end—from design and well-tested code to safe deployment and production operations. You’ll work with backend, firmware, and product engineers to solve the challenges of keeping a large fleet secure, up to date, and reporting data. We’re looking for an infrastructure or cloud engineer with strong engineering fundamentals, depth in at least one of our focus areas, and a commitment to production ownership. This role is based in San Francisco and requires two days (Tuesdays/Thursdays) per week in the office. What You’ll Do: • Design, build, and deploy reliable, maintainable, and performant services in Go and Ruby, AWS Lambda functions, and Terraform-managed infrastructure. • Build and monitor the platform that teams use to roll out firmware and configuration to devices. • Improve dashboards, alerts, and observability so the team can identify and address issues proactively. • Support new hardware launches, including provisioning, factory enablement, and related device-side features. • Build and improve Kafka, Flink, and MQTT data ingestion pipelines for device telemetry and files. • Participate in the team’s on-call rotation and improve runbooks and tooling to make on-call easier. • Help manage technical debt by consolidating approaches and making pragmatic decisions about what to improve and when. • Write clear design documents, provide thoughtful code reviews, and keep documentation current for the systems you own. • Collaborate effectively with teammates across time zones. What We’re Looking For: • 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, including building and operating backend or distributed services in production. • Proficiency with AI tools and automation, including AI-assisted coding. • Strong coding skills in Go or a similar language (such as Java, Python, or Rust) and a willingness to work in Go. • Experience building and operating services on AWS, including serverless services such as Lambda and infrastructure as code such as Terraform. • Understanding of networking and security fundamentals, including TLS, certificates, and authentication between services and devices. • Experience with monitoring, logging, and alerting, and a habit of instrumenting the systems you build. • Ability to debug issues across system boundaries and explain findings in plain language. • Clear written communication skills, including the ability to write design documents that teammates in other time zones can act on. • Preferred: Experience with Ruby. • Preferred: Working knowledge of messaging and streaming systems such as MQTT and Kafka. • Preferred: Experience with connected devices, IoT platforms, or firmware delivery. • Preferred: Experience with Kubernetes, stream processing frameworks such as Flink, or factory provisioning flows for hardware products.
