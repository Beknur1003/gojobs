This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Software Engineer, Data Solutions based in Canada. Join an innovative, AI-driven technology environment as a Senior Software Engineer, where you will help build and shape a new generation of scalable data products and infrastructure. You will play a key role in transforming large volumes of proprietary, licensed, and third-party data into reliable, reusable solutions that power core products, advanced analytics, and AI-powered applications. Working with billions of daily data events, you will design and operate sophisticated batch and streaming pipelines while developing the foundational data infrastructure that supports intelligent AI agents. You will collaborate closely with Product, Data Science, Engineering, and external partners to identify opportunities and deliver high-impact technical solutions. This role offers significant technical ownership, opportunities to influence architectural decisions, and the chance to contribute to how an AI-forward organization uses its data. You will also mentor fellow engineers and help establish engineering standards in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, deploy, and maintain end-to-end data products, including audience datasets, signals, data enrichments, APIs, and supporting services. • Build and improve foundational data infrastructure that provides AI agents and intelligent applications with rich, reliable context about audiences, campaigns, advertising inventory, and performance. • Develop and operate scalable batch and real-time data processing pipelines using technologies such as Apache Flink, Apache Spark, and Apache Kafka. • Publish and manage data products within an Apache Iceberg-based data architecture, establishing clear standards for data schemas, freshness, ownership, and reliability. • Create reusable frameworks and processes for integrating third-party and partner datasets, enabling efficient onboarding and consistent data access across multiple products. • Transform raw platform data into actionable insights, including audience characteristics, inventory signals, and performance benchmarks that enhance targeting capabilities and product recommendations. • Collaborate with Product Management, Data Science, Engineering, and external data partners to identify business needs, define technical requirements, and determine development priorities. • Lead engineering projects throughout their entire lifecycle, from technical architecture and design to implementation, production deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement. • Develop scalable data solutions that support multiple internal products and enable new capabilities across the broader technology platform. • Leverage AI-powered tools and agents in daily engineering workflows while contributing to the development of best practices for AI-assisted software development. • Participate in technical design discussions, architecture reviews, and code reviews to maintain high engineering standards. • Mentor other engineers, share technical knowledge, and contribute to the continuous improvement of development practices and team capabilities. Requirements: • At least 5 years of professional experience as a Backend Engineer, Data Engineer, Platform Engineer, or in a closely related software engineering role. • Strong programming skills in at least one of the following languages: Java, Scala, Python, or Go. • Proven experience designing, developing, and delivering data products or data platforms that are used and relied upon by other engineering teams or external customers. • Hands-on experience with distributed data processing, streaming architectures, and modern lakehouse or data warehouse technologies. • Familiarity with technologies such as Apache Flink, Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, Apache Iceberg, Amazon Redshift, ClickHouse, or Trino. • Strong understanding of data modeling, data contracts, schema management, and data quality principles in large-scale distributed environments. • Experience designing and maintaining reliable, scalable data pipelines and infrastructure capable of processing high volumes of information. • A product-oriented mindset, with the ability to understand how data products are used and how they contribute to measurable business outcomes. • Strong collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Demonstrated ability to translate ambiguous business requirements into clearly defined technical solutions and well-structured engineering projects. • An AI-forward mindset, including practical experience using AI-powered development tools and a genuine interest in building data infrastructure that supports AI agents and intelligent applications. • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, combined with sound technical judgment and attention to detail. • Ability to take ownership of projects, manage competing priorities, and deliver reliable solutions in a fast-moving technology environment. • Proficiency in Go (Golang) is considered a strong advantage. • Experience collaborating with privacy, legal, or compliance teams on data governance matters, including consent management, data licensing, and clean room environments, is beneficial. • Previous experience building and launching AI-powered products or features, including LLM applications, AI agents, or recommendation systems, is an asset. • Familiarity with data infrastructure for machine learning and AI systems, including feature stores, embeddings, vector databases, retrieval systems, or MCP servers, is highly desirable. • Experience in advertising technology (AdTech) or marketing technology (MarTech) is an advantage. • Experience developing data catalogs, data marketplaces, or semantic layers is considered a plus. Benefits: • Competitive annual base salary ranging from CAD $170,400 to CAD $234,300 for candidates based in Canada. • Market-informed compensation structure based on technical expertise, professional experience, qualifications, and geographic location. • Potential eligibility for additional compensation, including annual bonuses and equity awards, depending on the role. • Global retirement savings and pension programs, including locally applicable retirement benefits. • Competitive paid time off packages, including a day off for your birthday. • Comprehensive health benefits starting on the first day of employment. • Access to dedicated mental health and wellness support programs. • Work-from-home reimbursement to support your remote working environment. • Optional global WeWork membership for employees seeking flexible coworking spaces. • Access to office hubs in London and Toronto. • Comprehensive onboarding and training programs to support professional integration and development. • Financial support for personal development initiatives, including conferences, professional courses, and books. • Access to specialized programmatic advertising courses and certifications. • Enhanced parental leave program. • Friendly, inclusive, and supportive working culture that encourages collaboration and innovation. • Opportunities to participate in social activities, team gatherings, and company events. • Commitment to diversity, accessibility, and equal employment opportunities. • Benefits and perks may vary depending on the country of employment and applicable local employment arrangements.
