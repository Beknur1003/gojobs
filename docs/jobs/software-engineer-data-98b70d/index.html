As a Software Engineer, Data, you'll be a key contributor helping run and improve FloQast's core data platform. You will build and maintain the ingestion, orchestration, storage, and access components that power data across all product and analytics systems. Your work will help teams across engineering, product, and business operations build on a reliable, scalable, and secure data foundation. You've spent a few years building data or backend systems in production. You write clean, tested Python or Go and SQL, you've worked with Airflow and with streaming or CDC pipelines, and you can debug a failing job from the logs. You take ownership of the work you ship, from design to production support, and you learn quickly from review and feedback. At this level, you don't just finish tickets. You grow into the engineer who can own a platform component. At FloQast, you'll work across our lakehouse stack. FloLake runs on Iceberg over S3, orchestrated through MWAA, cataloged in AWS Glue, and queried via Trino and Athena. Kafka on MSK moves data through the hot path. You'll work with senior engineers in the US and India on the platform that 3,000+ tenants depend on. FloQast is headquartered in Los Angeles & San Jose, CA and we are seeking US-based Hybrid Engineers. What You'll Do • Build and maintain core data platform components: ingestion, orchestration, storage and access • Work on our lakehouse stack (Iceberg on S3, Glue, Trino, Spark on EMR, Airflow on MWAA, Kafka on MSK) • Help improve the reliability, observability and cost of the platform • Build and improve tooling that other teams use • Take part in on-call and incident reviews after ramp-up, and fix root causes • Write tested code, review code and share what you learn What You'll Bring • 3+ years in data platform, infrastructure or backend engineering • Strong Python or Go, and SQL • Hands-on experience with Airflow • Experience with AWS (S3, EMR, Glue, ECS or Lambda) and Git-based CI/CD • Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform) • Experience with Kafka or streaming • Experience with monitoring and debugging production issues • Clear communicator who owns problems end to end #LI-JR1 This job posting is for our Software Engineer level, the base pay range for this position is $123,000 - $200,000.
