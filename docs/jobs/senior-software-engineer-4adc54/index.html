Yext (NYSE: YEXT) is the enterprise agentic marketing platform. Built on the world's most comprehensive structured data platform for local businesses, Yext gives brands and their partners the visibility intelligence to win every moment of discovery – across AI and traditional search. Yext's API-first architecture connects structured data to APIs, MCP servers, and generative interfaces, so partners and developers can build purpose-built experiences on the same infrastructure powering Yext's own products. Thousands of brands and digital marketing partners in financial services, healthcare, retail, hospitality, and food rely on Yext to manage, measure, and optimize visibility at scale. For more information, visit yext.com. At Yext, Product Engineering builds and evolves the technology behind our products and services. We’re looking for senior software engineers who want to solve challenging technical problems, lead the development of systems at scale, and help shape what we build next. We work in an agile environment with two-week sprints and regular demos that keep teams aligned and give engineers clear visibility into the impact of their work. As a senior engineer, you’ll remain deeply involved in designing and building software while providing technical leadership to the engineers around you. You’ll partner closely with Product and Engineering leaders to turn broader priorities into sound technical plans, guide projects through delivery, and help less-experienced engineers continue to grow. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join as an early member of our paid media engineering team. We work with multi-location brands to drive meaningful engagement and conversion with local, personalized content while preserving brand integrity across thousands of locations. We do this by launching brand playbooks into localized, cross-channel campaigns at every location, launched in one click and on brand by default. We serve many of the biggest brands and franchises on the planet and deliver data-backed advertising results to their locations around the world. This team builds the platform behind every campaign: the ads infrastructure, the integrations with Meta, Google, TikTok, Yelp, and Apple Maps, and the AI agents that connect a marketing team's tools. We value building beautiful, easy-to-use products within a culture focused on speed, sustainability, and engineering without ego. This role is based in Covington, KY, just across the river from Cincinnati, where in-person collaboration plays an important part in how our teams work, learn, and build relationships. We’re looking for people who genuinely value that environment and are comfortable coming into the office at least three days per week. At the same time, we don’t prescribe specific in-office days and want employees to have the flexibility to handle personal needs without disrupting their work or team collaboration.
