Ready to join a mission that matters? The future of cybersecurity starts with you. The Managed Services product group develops tools and customer facing interfaces to support Falcon Complete. This role will be managing the engineers that develop APIs, UIs and cloud services to support the customer experience for Falcon Complete. As the Manager of the UK Team, you’ll be managing a cross discipline team of Front End and Cloud engineers building new components, supporting product development and ensuring a focus on quality and reliability. You’ll collaborate closely with other product groups, Falcon complete analysts and peers who develop the internal tools that support Falcon Complete. This role is Hybrid with the expectation to go to the London Office 1-2 times a week. Although this is flexible, you must be within 30 miles of London to be considered for this position.
