Platform Engineer Golang Developer with Kubernetes At FIS, our technology and our people are always moving forward. We advance the way the world pays, banks and invests, and we believe that diverse perspectives help us build better solutions. Join us to grow your career while helping shape the future of financial technology. About the role As a Senior/Staff Platform Engineer focused on Developer Experience and Kubernetes Operators, youll build Go-based controllers and operators that automate platform capabilities and make Kubernetes easier for engineering teams to use. Youll design custom resources and reconciliation logic, create internal developer tooling, and improve the reliability and usability of cloud-native platforms. Your key responsibilities • Design, develop, test, and maintain Kubernetes operators and controllers using Go. • Build custom resource definitions (CRDs), reconciliation loops, and platform automation. • Develop internal tooling that simplifies Kubernetes workflows and improves developer experience. • Troubleshoot controller behavior and Kubernetes API interactions, and build reliable recovery and failure-handling patterns. • Contribute to reusable engineering practices, code quality, documentation, and platform reliability. What you bring Required qualifications • 3 to 10 years of Strong hands-on software development skills in Go (Golang). • Hands-on experience building Kubernetes operators or controllers, rather than only deploying or using existing operators. • Experience designing and implementing Kubernetes CRDs and reconciliation workflows. • Experience with controller-runtime, client-go, Kubebuilder, Operator SDK, or comparable Kubernetes controller frameworks. • Strong understanding of Kubernetes APIs, resources, lifecycle management, and cloud-native engineering practices. Preferred qualifications • Experience building internal developer platforms, self-service tooling, or developer experience solutions. • Experience operating Kubernetes across cloud or hybrid environments. • Familiarity with GitOps, infrastructure automation, observability, or platform reliability engineering. The impact you’ll make You’ll help create platform capabilities that turn complex Kubernetes operations into reliable, reusable automation. Your work will enable engineering teams to move faster while giving them consistent tools and safer ways to build and operate cloud-native services. At FIS, you can grow your career as far as you want to take it.
