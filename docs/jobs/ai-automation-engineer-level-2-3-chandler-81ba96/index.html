This role sits at the intersection of AI, automation, CI/CD, and infrastructure, giving you the opportunity to: • Build and integrate AI-powered capabilities into engineering workflows • Design and operate robust CI/CD DevOps pipelines • Support containerized, cloud and on-premise environments at scale Join us and be part of a team driving innovative solutions at the forefront of space systems and advanced software. Your Role and Impact In this role, you will: • Design, develop, document, test, and debug AI and ML capabilities to automate engineering and infrastructure workflows. • Automate processes by building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and AI-enabled tooling within a DevOps environment. • Build and maintain agent orchestration frameworks that integrate with LLM services, system services, and engineering tools. • Develop, deploy, and maintain containerized applications (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) to ensure portability, scalability, and reliability. • Implement and support RAG pipelines and vector database integrations to enable intelligent retrieval and decision-making. • Provide infrastructure support by troubleshooting issues, optimizing performance, and maintaining high availability and observability of systems. • Collaborate closely with engineering, DevOps, and infrastructure teams to identify automation opportunities and bring them to production. • Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders, both in writing and verbally. • Perform other related duties as assigned.
