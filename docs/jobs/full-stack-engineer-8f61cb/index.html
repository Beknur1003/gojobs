We are seeking a Senior Full Stack Engineer who is passionate about building high-performance, scalable, and maintainable applications across multiple client projects. The role will support both new feature delivery and steady-state engineering ownership, ensuring system reliability, strong engineering practices, and effective collaboration across stakeholders. The ideal candidate is hands-on with Golang, SvelteKit (or similar), and GraphQL, and is experienced in microservices-based architectures. Primary Responsibilities Application Engineering Delivery & Continuity • Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications using Golang (backend) and SvelteKit (frontend). • Build, optimize, and maintain GraphQL APIs for data-driven applications. • Implement and evolve microservices for modularity, scalability, and resilience. • Support steady-state engineering operations: bug fixes, incident support, root cause analysis, and production stability improvements. Architecture, Quality & Engineering Practices • Contribute to technical architecture discussions and drive engineering best practices across client projects. • Ensure code quality through peer reviews, automated testing, and CI/CD practices. • Support performance tuning and system optimization (application-level and database-level). • Maintain clear technical documentation (design notes, API contracts, runbooks where applicable). Collaboration & Stakeholder Management • Collaborate cross-functionally with product, design, and operations teams to deliver high-quality software. • Provide continuity across multiple stakeholders by tracking technical dependencies, delivery risks, and follow-through items to closure. • Mentor engineers and contribute to team capability uplift through guidance and knowledge sharing. Key Requirements • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science / Software Engineering or related discipline. • 7+ years of professional experience as a full-stack or backend engineer. • Strong proficiency in Golang for backend development. • Proficiency in SvelteKit or similar modern frontend frameworks (e.g., React, Vue). • Experience building and maintaining GraphQL APIs. • Deep understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems concepts. • Strong SQL skills with experience in PostgreSQL and MS SQL (schema design, query tuning). • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, testing practices, and version control (e.g., Git). • Strong problem-solving, debugging, and communication skills. Good to Have • Experience delivering solutions on AWS (e.g., Lambda, ECS, RDS, S3, API Gateway). • Familiarity with Temporal Workflow (or similar orchestration frameworks). • Experience with Node.js for backend/API development. • Experience with Docker and/or Kubernetes. • Background in performance tuning, observability, and system optimization. • Experience mentoring or leading small engineering teams. Soft Skills • Strong ownership mindset with disciplined follow-through across multiple client deliverables. • Clear written and verbal communication with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Strong coordination and collaboration skills across cross-functional teams. • Demonstrated quality delivery and consistent incremental improvement. Advantage • Familiar with AI-assisted development and the use of AI tools to improve productivity and code quality.
