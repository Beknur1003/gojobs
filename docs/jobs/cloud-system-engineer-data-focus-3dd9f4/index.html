What your job looks like • Assist developers with database setups and structure to maintain the integrity, performance, and efficiency of the systems; • Troubleshoot database performance issues, and define both short and long term actions to fix structural issues • Setup and maintain a new data platform based on databricks • Automating deployment, scaling, and management of the containerized applications in a public cloud environment (e.g., setting up new infrastructure components with terraform and automated pipelines) • Implementation/configuration of security standards (e.g., NIST, OWASP); • Manage problems and (security) incidents (e.g. increased error rates in the itsme components, etc.): • Identifying the root causes of problems (i.e. root cause analysis at every level of the solution (application, network, infrastructure, storage) • Increase database/datalake knowledge in the team in general to make sure everybody in the team has the basic skills needed to troubleshoot database related incidents • Automate repetitive tasks that are performed manually. • You take ownership of your projects, reach out to your colleagues and can act as a go-to person; • You have an open and flexible mindset; You can easily adapt to changing contexts; • You have an automation mindset and are used to managing infrastructure as code. • You are a critical thinker and think about what the best solution is when you are asked to perform a task. • You are a team player who shares his/her knowledge and likes to celebrate joint achievements; • You are service- and customer-oriented; • You have strong SQL skills (query optimization, schema design, troubleshooting) • You have experience with MySQL administration specific on replication setups • You have a working knowledge of apache spark and pyspark for distributed data processing • Experience with azure synapse • Experience with databricks (workspace administration, cluster management, job orchestration) • Familiarity with noSQL DB's like MongoDB • Knowledge of Redis or alternatives • Experience with Kubernetes based cloud environments (compute, networking, iam, storage) • Knowledge of infrastructure-as-code software (e.g. terraform) • Plus: Knowledge of scripting languages: python, golang, ... • Plus: experience with Grafana observability stack • Plus: experience with github actions pipelines • You are fluent in English, and in Dutch or French.
