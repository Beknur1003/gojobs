THE OPPORTUNITY We are hiring a Senior Backend Software Engineer to our Identity Foundations (IDF) team in Brazil! This is a Remote – Brazil position, meaning you can work from anywhere within the country. Please note that this role is only open to candidates in Brazil. We’re looking for an autonomous and resourceful engineer to join our international Identity Foundations (IDF) team, which owns critical services at the heart of our product—including our login engine, session management, and single sign-on (SSO). You’ll build features that sit at the spine of the platform, powering experiences used by millions of users while tackling complex engineering challenges around performance, reliability, and multi-region scalability. You’ll also play a key role in driving our AI transformation, leveraging AI-assisted development to raise engineering standards, accelerate delivery, and build seamless, reliable identity foundations. YOUR IMPACT • Own end-to-end projects from technical design through implementation, rollout, and production operation—impacting critical red-route authentication workflows for users, clients, and partners through heavy testing, organization, and strategic execution. • Build and evolve maintainable, reliable, and scalable backend services that operate at massive scale across different regions, continuously identifying ways to improve production systems. • Drive our AI transformation by leveraging LLMs and AI-assisted development tools to raise engineering standards, accelerate delivery, and elevate problem-solving workflows. • Author technical documentation, such as RFCs, to articulate architectural ideas, share knowledge, and actively engage in reviewing peers' proposals. • Deliver committed initiatives across tactical cycles to help the company achieve its core strategic goals. • Champion a "you build it, you run it" engineering culture by monitoring rollouts, guaranteeing quality through comprehensive testing, and responding effectively to bugs and production incidents. • Collaborate within an agile, people-first environment by exchanging constructive feedback on growth, successes, and mistakes to help your team develop professionally and foster a great place to work. • Live the mission: inspire and empower others by genuinely caring for your own wellbeing and your colleagues. Bring wellbeing to the forefront of work, and create a supportive environment where everyone feels comfortable taking care of themselves, taking time off, and finding work-life wellness. WHO YOU ARE • An experienced backend engineer with years of experience building and operating production backend systems, with expertise in Kotlin/Spring and/or Golang highly desirable. • A distributed systems specialist with strong experience designing and scaling APIs, data-intensive applications, cloud-native architectures on platforms like AWS, and scalable databases such as PostgreSQL. • A cloud-native practitioner with hands-on experience deploying, scaling, and operating services in production using Kubernetes or similar container orchestration platforms, alongside Infrastructure as Code (IaC) to provision components like databases, caches, lambdas, and CDN configurations. • An infrastructure and reliability-minded builder experienced in asynchronous processing using message brokers (such as Kafka, SQS/SNS, or RabbitMQ) and production observability tools (such as Datadog, Grafana, or Prometheus). • An AI-fluent technologist who thoughtfully leverages modern LLMs and AI-assisted development tools to enhance engineering workflows and solve complex technical problems. • An autonomous, end-to-end owner who makes thoughtful technical decisions, embraces a "you build it, you run it" mindset, and independently drives projects from design to production operation. • A clear and confident communicator who thrives in collaborative, agile environments that prioritize people over process—comfortable influencing technical decisions, sharing ideas across teams, and giving and receiving constructive feedback.
