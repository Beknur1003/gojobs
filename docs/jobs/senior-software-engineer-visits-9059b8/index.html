Senior Software Engineer (Visits) About the Role Hinge Health uses software, including AI, to deliver care for joint and muscle health, and has expanded into pelvic health, migraine, and digestive health. We partner with employers and health plans to provide this care at scale, improving members' quality of life and reducing healthcare costs. The Visits team owns scheduling for all of Hinge Health, along with the visit experience in our member app and in the tools our care team uses. That covers HingeSelect (our network of specialists, in-person providers, and surgeons) and visits in every Hinge Health program, from physical therapy to migraine and GI care. Next, we want every visit at Hinge Health to feel like the best doctor's office you've been to, minus the clipboards and hold music. Members should be able to find the right visit, book it in a few taps, show up prepared, and afterward see what happened and what to do next, whether the visit was by video or in person. Providers should be able to act on a visit without leaving our tools, from referrals to labs to prescriptions. Each visit leaves a structured record that shapes the next one, from the follow-up booking to AI experiences that help members act on their care. As a Senior Software Engineer on Visits, you'll own projects across that experience, from technical design through launch, while visit volume grows many times over. Your strength might be mobile, web, or the services behind them. There's central work here for each, and nobody is expected to be an expert in all three. It's 0-to-1 work at a startup's pace, backed by the resources and data of a market leader. What You'll Accomplish • Lead projects from ambiguity to launch: Partner with product, design, and clinical teams to turn loosely defined goals into sequenced plans that several engineers can build against, then ship them at a high, steady pace. • Extend the scheduling platform: Grow the system every Hinge Health visit is booked through to support more specialties, more providers, and real-time booking with outside provider networks, while keeping every booking correct as volume climbs. • Close the loop after each visit: Build encounters, our record of what happened at each visit, along with the visit history and after-visit summaries that members see in the app, providers work from, and our AI experiences build on. • Bring new specialties onto one platform: Launch specialties like migraine and GI care on shared visit infrastructure, so adding the next one is mostly configuration rather than a rebuild. • Run what you ship: Define SLOs for the systems you own, roll out launches and migrations safely, lead incident response through to root-cause fixes, and protect member health data in every design and code review. • Raise the engineering bar: Write design docs for key decisions, give high-signal code reviews, and mentor earlier-career engineers. Use AI tools to multiply your own output and help teammates do the same. Basic Qualifications • 5+ years of non-internship, full-time professional software engineering experience. • Professional experience with at least one of the languages or frameworks we use: TypeScript, Go, React Native, or React. • Experience leading a production feature or system from technical design through launch, working closely with product and design partners. • Professional experience using AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Claude Code) in your day-to-day engineering work. Preferred Qualifications • Experience building products 0-to-1 in a rapid-growth environment, using data and experiments to decide what to build next. • Experience shipping product features powered by LLMs or agentic workflows to production. • Experience building scheduling, booking, or marketplace systems at scale. • Experience with clinical data and healthcare integrations, such as FHIR or HL7 data, EHRs, claims, labs, or pharmacies. • Familiarity with our core stack: React Native, React, TypeScript, Node.js, NestJS, GraphQL, Go, gRPC, Postgres, Temporal, Kafka, Docker, and AWS. • Experience in healthcare, insurance tech, or other high-compliance industries (HIPAA, SOC 2).
