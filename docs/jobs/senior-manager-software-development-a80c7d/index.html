CVS Health | MinuteClinic Product Engineering Primary Job Duties & Responsibilities Lead the MinuteClinic Product Engineering team in building software that helps people access care at CVS MinuteClinic, part of a household name in U.S. healthcare. Your team’s work shapes patient access, provider efficiency, and reliable care experiences across patient-facing kiosks, internal applications, APIs, and provider tools. Take ownership of the next phase of an engineering transformation already delivering results. We have modernized critical systems and shipped AI features that providers use. This role offers the opportunity to develop a strong engineering team, shape the platform’s technical direction, and expand cloud-native and AI capabilities with direct patient and provider impact. This is a highly hands-on engineering leadership role. Stay technically engaged through architecture, code reviews, focused prototyping, and complex production investigations. Contribute code where your involvement removes a critical obstacle, and use that work to strengthen the team’s ability to deliver independently. Technical depth, sound judgment, and a sustained commitment to continuous learning are central expectations. Own engineering priorities and outcomes. Own the team’s engineering roadmap, technical priorities, and delivery commitments in partnership with product and technical leads. Translate business goals into a focused plan, make tradeoffs explicit, and balance product delivery with reliability, technical debt, and team development. Define measurable outcomes for each initiative, including patient or provider value, production performance, and operational readiness. Establish clear ownership and enable independent execution. Build a team that practices single-threaded ownership, with individual engineers accountable for defined services, features, or workflows from problem definition through production. Give engineers decision-making authority appropriate to their scope, clarify expectations, remove blockers, and coach them to resolve dependencies and deliver independently. Remain accountable for team outcomes while developing engineers’ judgment and ownership. Set technical direction through deep distributed-systems expertise. Bring hands-on experience designing, building, and operating critical systems under real production traffic and failure conditions. Partner with Staff Engineers and technical leads to make sound decisions about service boundaries, consistency, asynchronous processing, fault isolation, recovery, and scale. Apply architectural discipline informed by practices such as the AWS Well-Architected Framework, and validate decisions through testing and production evidence. Drive cloud-native modernization. Build on progress across patient-facing kiosks, internal web applications, and APIs. Guide the evolution of the current C#, .NET, and Microsoft environment toward cloud-native architectures and open-source technologies, including Python, Java, Kotlin, Kubernetes, and Kafka. Choose technologies based on the problem, evaluate migration risks, and deliver incremental improvements while preserving dependable patient and provider experiences Advance practical AI and agentic capabilities. Expand on AI features already used by providers. Partner with product and clinical stakeholders to identify valuable applications, define success measures, and deliver AI systems and agents that interact with enterprise data and approved tools. Personally experiment with models, tools, and orchestration approaches to understand their capabilities and limitations. Establish evaluation and operational practices that address quality, privacy, security, human oversight, reliability, latency, and cost. Make production reliability measurable. Define service-level indicators and objectives that reflect patient and provider needs. Use operational data and error budgets to guide priorities, and validate resilience through load testing, failure testing, and recovery exercises. Strengthen observability, automated testing, CI/CD, safe deployments, and incident response. Work alongside engineers on complex failures and ensure corrective actions lead to sustained improvements. Build productive cross-functional partnerships. Work with product managers, clinical stakeholders, security, infrastructure, and other engineering teams to align priorities and manage dependencies. Communicate decisions, progress, tradeoffs, and risks clearly. Resolve conflicts and keep teams focused on shared patient and provider outcomes. Hire, coach, and develop strong engineers. Set clear performance expectations, provide actionable feedback, conduct performance reviews, and support career growth. Develop engineers who can independently own increasingly complex work. Build a collaborative environment where people take initiative, raise concerns early, challenge assumptions constructively, and share accountability for quality. Continuously learn and lead by example. Keep developing your own technical skills through experimentation, feedback, and direct engagement with engineering problems. Apply new knowledge to production work as distributed systems, cloud-native engineering, and AI evolve. Create opportunities for the team to learn, share findings, and put new skills into practice. Treat stronger engineering judgment, broader ownership, and timely escalation of risks as evidence of the team’s growth. Minimum Qualifications: • 5+ years if relevant experience, including 2+ years experience of leading projects or managing directs • Proficiency with at least one of the following programming languages: C++, C#, Java, Golang, Javascript, Typescript, Python or similar. • Strong experience with distributed systems design, implementation and operation at scale. • Hands-on experience building AI tools, agents, or frameworks through professional, personal, or open-source projects Preferred Qualifications: • Adept at execution and delivery (planning, delivering, and supporting) skills • Adept at business intelligence • Adept at collaboration and teamwork • Mastery of problem solving and decision making skills • Mastery of growth mindset (agility and developing yourself and others) skills Education: • Bachelor's degree preferred/specialized training/relevant professional qualification.
