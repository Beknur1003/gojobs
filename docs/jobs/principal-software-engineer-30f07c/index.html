Citizens Financial Group, Inc. (CFG) seeks a Principal Software Engineer for its Phoenix, Arizona location. Responsibilities: • Develop, create, modify, and implement IT solutions and computer applications software to support our banking operations. • Build modern, architecturally sound components, tools, and applications to meet mission-driven strategic business goals. • Ensure implementation of quality of service characteristics including security, scalability, manageability, and maintainability of a distributed service-based framework to create or expand business and technical capabilities. • Participate in adopting Agile methodology and adhering to best practices and collaborating with stakeholders. • Collaborate and contribute insight to solution design ideation, ensuring the success of the product and adherence to enterprise architecture principles. • Design, modify, and implement software solutions. • Apply industry best practices to evaluate, correct, and prevent vulnerabilities during the software development process. • Continuously monitor and enhance application performance, ensuring minimal latency and high availability for critical banking operations. • Guide junior engineers and contribute to team development through code reviews, technical workshops, and best practice advocacy. • Identify opportunities for process improvements and champion initiatives that enhance productivity and customer experience. • Maintain comprehensive technical documentation and contribute to knowledge-sharing initiatives across teams. Qualifications: Requires a Bachelor’s degree, or foreign equivalent, in Computer Science, Electronic Engineering, or related field and 6 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience as a Software Engineer, Computer Programmer, or related position involving software development for the financial services industry. Must have 6 years of experience with the following skills: • Querying, analyzing, and managing big data; • Utilizing Python3, Java, JavaScript, Golang, and Angular, React, and front end frameworks; • Applying knowledge of data structure concepts including linked lists, dictionaries, arrays, and custom object creation; • Managing sensitive assets with AWS and Azure; • CI/CD pipeline using Jenkins; • Utilizing Bash and Linux; and • Utilizing React. May telecommute 1 day per week. Worksite: 645 E Missouri Avneue, Phoenix, AZ 85012 United States, 40 hrs/wk. EO employer. To apply visit our website at jobs.citizensbank.com and apply online (Job ID: 49683) or email resume and cover letter to Recruiting@Citizensbank.com with subject line: 49683. Direct applicants only.
