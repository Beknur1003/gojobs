organizational leadership ✔ Excited to partner with founders and executives to drive product and business impact Typical Roles & Responsibilities • Build and scale high-performing engineering teams, delivery • Establish engineering processes, CI/CD workflows, and DevOps best practices • Collaborate with founders, product, and business teams to shape company-wide strategic direction • Drive
