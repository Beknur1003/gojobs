Senior DevOps Engineer

Гибрид Senior Контракт / B2B
KubernetesDockerTerraformAWSAzurePythonAI/LLMFinTech
Salary: Competitive Our Federal Government client is currently seeking an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to support critical infrastructure and project initiatives on a 12-month contract, with the possibility of a 12-month extension. As the selected candidates, you will: • Lead the design, development, deployment, and support of the Agency’s Cloud & Data Infrastructure. • Prioritise and manage work queues to ensure efficient delivery of key initiatives. • Ensure solutions adhere to best practices for integration and are well documented. • Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand requirements and deliver effective outcomes. • Play a leadership role within project teams using an agile development framework. • Engage with internal and external stakeholders to define project requirements and negotiate solutions. • Support and maintain automation pipelines and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using tools like Terraform. • Develop and maintain solutions across cloud environments, leveraging AWS and/or Azure. • Contribute to the continuous improvement of DevOps practices and tools. • Support containerisation, including Docker and Kubernetes, for scalable application delivery. To be successful in this role, you should have: • Extensive hands-on experience with AWS and/or Azure cloud infrastructure and services. • Proven ability to design, implement, and manage scalable cloud solutions. • Strong expertise in DevOps tools and practices, including continuous delivery pipelines with GitLab, Artifactory, and Jira. • Deep understanding of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) methodologies, particularly with Terraform. • Proficiency with version control systems such as Git, combined with agile development practices. • Capability to write, maintain, and troubleshoot code in Python, JavaScript, and Golang. • Experience with containerisation and orchestration technologies like Docker and Kubernetes. • Ability to work independently with high autonomy and deliver strategic solutions. It is desirable if you have: • Proficiency in managing multi-cloud capabilities across AWS and Azure. • Knowledge of HashiCorp Vault for securing sensitive data and secrets. • Experience with designing and implementing automation solutions incorporating AI agents for tasks such as auto-remediation, documentation, or testing. Successful candidates will need to be Australian citizens and be able to obtain Negative Vetting Level 1 security clearance. Peoplebank and Leaders IT are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone belongs. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds, identities, and experiences. If you need adjustments to the recruitment process due to your circumstances, please let us know—we’re here to support you.
