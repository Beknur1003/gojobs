About the Role: CrowdStrike is hiring an Engineering Manager for the Infrastructure team to lead and grow our global team of engineers focused on developing tools to deploy, observe, and maintain a first-class, highly available cloud platform at massive scale. This is a technical leadership role where candidates with strong engineering backgrounds in Golang and proven people management experience will excel. What You'll Do: The Infrastructure Engineering Dev team is responsible for building and operating CrowdStrike's internal cloud management platform. As the Engineering Manager, you'll lead a small, remote-first team of full stack engineers building APIs and UIs in Go and TypeScript. You'll be responsible for: • Managing and mentoring a team of 3-6 full stack engineers • Providing technical guidance and code review oversight • Collaborating with senior leadership to execute on roadmaps and priorities • Ensuring delivery of high-quality software solutions on schedule • Supporting team culture and individual professional development • Participating in hiring and onboarding new team members • Contributing to technical decisions and architectural discussions What You'll Need: • US Citizenship or Green card Holder • 3+ years of people management experience leading software engineering teams • Extensive hands-on experience in Golang with ability to provide technical guidance • Strong experience in TypeScript/JavaScript and modern web technologies • Experience with gRPC and RESTful API development • Experience with modern SPA frameworks and development practices • Understanding of Linux environments and cloud-native infrastructure • Experience operating within cloud platforms, preferably AWS • Knowledge of CI/CD concepts and DevOps practices • Experience with team leadership, mentoring, and basic hiring processes • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
