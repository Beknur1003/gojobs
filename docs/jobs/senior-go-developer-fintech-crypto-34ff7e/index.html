EMCD is a global crypto ecosystem building products across mining, wallets, payments, custody, and digital asset infrastructure. We are looking for a Senior Go Developer to join the CHW Services team and work on backend services connected to real money flows: holds, debits, credits, provider integrations, reconciliation, and transaction lifecycle management. We are considering candidates from fintech or crypto backgrounds who have built and supported money operations in production. Responsibilities • Build new backend services in Go and improve existing CHW Services products • Integrate external providers via REST and internal services via gRPC and Kafka • Refactor legacy services to reduce support cost, simplify architecture, remove technical debt, and improve test coverage • Improve monitoring, logging, alerting, and security for financial flows • Support production systems, investigate incidents, and track operation lifecycle end to end • Provide technical mentorship within the team Requirements • Production experience with money operations in fintech or crypto: holds, debits, credits, balances, transaction lifecycle, and reconciliation • Strong understanding of how to store and move money between systems safely: decimal precision, operation keys, idempotency, consistency, and auditability • Strong production experience with Go, PostgreSQL, gRPC / REST, and Kafka • Real experience applying microservice patterns to financial systems: Saga, compensation, distributed transactions, outbox, idempotent consumers, and reconciliation loops • Experience integrating external providers via REST, including timeouts, provider-side statuses, delayed reversals, and “our side / their side” reconciliation • Strong PostgreSQL beyond CRUD: transactions, isolation levels, unique constraints, locks, indexes, and EXPLAIN • Strong Kafka knowledge: partitions, consumer groups, offsets, rebalancing, delivery guarantees, ordering, and safe horizontal scaling • Active use of AI agents in development, with a practical understanding of spec-driven workflows and harness building What We Offer • Fully remote work from anywhere in the world • Flexible working hours focused on results • 20 paid vacation days + 12 additional bonus days • 100% paid sick leave • Paid English lessons • Support for courses, certifications, and training programs • Real ownership in fintech and crypto infrastructure projects Originally posted on Himalayas
