Senior System Engineer - Devops We are seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Senior Site Reliability/Infrastructure/ Devops Engineer to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will work across AWS, Azure, and GCP cloud platforms, ensuring the reliability, scalability, and performance of our systems. This role involves collaborating with development and operations teams to implement DevOps practices, automate processes, manage Kubernetes environments, and maintain infrastructure that supports our applications and services. Senior Systems Engineer Rapidly growing startup in the health sector and great founding team to work with. We are building the best healthcare startup in the ecosystem to provide affordable medicine by optimizing supply chain and logistics that are based on highly personalized experience. We are funded by best-in-class VCs. Must be fluent at coding in at least one programming language (Preferably python or golang) • Strong hands-on experience with one of the cloud platforms like AWS, GCP, Azure, etc. • Experience in managing applications on cloud at production scale. Must be familiar with Compute, Load Balancing, Auto Scaling, Databases, Networking and DNS services • Should have experience in setting up and administering at least one CI / CD systems like Jenkins, Gitlab CI, Travis • Proficiency with Kubernetes concepts such as Pods, Services, Deployments, Statefulsets, volume claims, Helm Charts.
