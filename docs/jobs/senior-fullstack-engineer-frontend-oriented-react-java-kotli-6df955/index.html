Senior Fullstack Engineer (Frontend oriented: React + Java/Kotlin/Golang) Who is Parser? Technology alone does not create impact-the right teams do. Founded in 2018, Parser is a boutique technology services and consulting firm helping global organisations solve complex business challenges through digital transformation, product development and Al enablement. We are a fast-growing team of 340+ engineers and consultants across Europe (UK, Spain, Portugal), the Americas (US, Argentina, Uruguay, Colombia), and the Middle East. We combine global reach with a mindset focused on agility, senior expertise, and close collaboration. We work as an extension of our clients' teams, helping them define the right problems, shape solutions, and deliver technology-driven outcomes that create measurable business value. Our expertise spans software engineering, Al & data, product development, and customer experience, delivered by teams that combine strong technical depth with a consulting mindset. Why Join Us? If you are looking for a place where you can think beyond execution, take true ownership of outcomes, influence decisions, and continuously learn alongside top-tier specialists in a truly global environment, we'd love to meet you. How will you impact? We're looking for a Senior Fullstack Engineer to help expand our team working on existing applications. The work will focus on refactoring and improving those applications while shaping sound technical direction across frontend and backend systems and teams. This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who can move between implementation and broader technical leadership: taking ownership of ambiguous, important problems, making and explaining trade-offs, and helping other engineers deliver effectively. Your key responsibilities: • Refactor and improve existing applications built with React and Next.js • Lead technical design for complex fullstack initiatives, considering how components, services, APIs, and teams fit together • Turn business and product needs into clear, scalable technical approaches • Make, document, and communicate technical trade-offs to engineering and cross-functional stakeholders • Work through ambiguity, establish direction, and help teams move forward when the solution is not yet clear • Collaborate across teams and domains to support integrations and consistent technical approaches • Raise engineering standards through design guidance, code reviews, and mentorship • Stay hands-on with implementation while providing technical leadership across the stack What you'll bring to the role: • Extensive professional software engineering experience; 8+ years is a typical benchmark, but demonstrated scope, impact, and technical leadership matter more than years alone • Strong experience with React and Next.js • Proficiency in backend technologies: Java, Kotlin, or Golang • A track record of owning complex engineering work beyond a single, narrowly defined feature or project • Experience making architectural decisions and explaining their impact across applications, services, or teams • Ability to translate ambiguous business problems into technical direction and practical solutions • Evidence of leading significant technical initiatives and influencing engineering practices beyond immediate individual tasks • Strong communication skills with Product, Architecture, Engineering, and non-engineering stakeholders, paired with a hands-on approach to development and the ability to guide other engineers Location: • Argentina: Buenos Aires o Córdoba You will receive: • The chance to join an organization with triple-digit growth that is changing the paradigm on how software products are built. • The opportunity to be part of an amazing, multicultural community of tech experts. • A highly competitive compensation package. • A flexible and hybrid working environment.
