Tech with a Human Touch ANIMARUM is looking for a Senior Software Developer who wants to create technology with a human perspective. We focus on purposeful innovation and see technology as something that empowers both individuals and organizations—not merely as a tool. As a Senior Software Developer, you will: • Design and develop robust, scalable software solutions for our clients, with a strong focus on quality, security, and high performance. • Work closely with clients and internal cross-functional teams to understand needs and goals and develop solutions that create real value. Transparency, open communication, and collaboration are at the core of how we work. • Have the opportunity to contribute throughout the entire delivery lifecycle—from the initial meeting and strategy through implementation, deployment, and testing. • Take responsibility for technical quality, system architecture, and reliability in solutions that are continuously improved and developed. • Share knowledge, coach colleagues, and help strengthen the team’s development practices through feedback, mentoring, and shared learning. We’re Looking for Someone Who Has • At least 7 years of professional software development experience in one or more of the following: Java, Node.js, .NET, Kotlin, Golang, C++, React, or Python. • A deep understanding of cloud services (AWS, Azure, and/or GCP), as well as experience with Infrastructure as Code. • Knowledge of performance, security, and reliability. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills. • The ability to take end-to-end responsibility for technical deliveries. • A desire to contribute to a creative and inclusive work environment. Why ANIMARUM At ANIMARUM, it’s not just about the job—it’s about being part of a team where everyone supports one another. We believe in: A Human-Centered Approach We are a team that genuinely cares about one another and strives to create a workplace where everyone can grow and succeed. Opportunities to Grow Whether you’re just starting your career or are an experienced expert, we offer opportunities to continue learning and developing. Benefits That Make Life Easier At ANIMARUM, we want our employees to thrive—not only at work, but in life. That’s why we offer a range of benefits designed to make everyday life a little easier: Wellness Allowance Support your well-being with our annual wellness allowance of SEK 5,000. Whether you use it for a gym membership, massage, or something else that helps you feel your best—it’s yours to use. Company Car or Bicycle Travel in style with the option of a company car or company bicycle—perfect for your daily commute or weekend adventures. Gross Salary Deductions We offer gross salary deduction arrangements, allowing you to make smart financial choices for your future. Competitive Salaries and Stock Option Programs We know that our greatest asset is our amazing team, and we believe that should be reflected in your compensation. That’s why we offer several flexible and competitive compensation packages designed to suit different needs. Whichever package you choose, we are committed to ensuring that you are fairly rewarded and have the flexibility to shape your own compensation.
