Engineering Manager - Fraud & Financial Crime Technology Every fraudulent transaction prevented, every synthetic identity detected, and every scam disrupted starts with the technology decisions made by this team. Sitting at the intersection of software engineering, data and risk, this role provides the opportunity to shape the platforms that identify and stop financial crime in real time. You'll lead engineers building sophisticated fraud decisioning and risk-scoring capabilities that protect customers, reduce losses and keep pace with an increasingly complex threat landscape. What you'll be responsible for • Leading a team of Software Engineers, Data Engineers and Technical Leads. • Building and enhancing real-time fraud decisioning and risk scoring engines. • Delivering scalable, cloud-native platforms that process high-volume transactions with low latency. • Working closely with Fraud Operations, Financial Crime, Risk and Product teams to translate emerging fraud threats into technical solutions. • Developing fraud controls across credit card fraud, application fraud, identity fraud and account takeover use cases. • Driving engineering excellence across architecture, reliability, security and platform performance. We're interested in people who have • Experience leading engineering teams in banking, payments, fintech or financial crime technology environments. • A strong background in software engineering using technologies such as Java, Python, .NET, Scala or Golang. • Experience building transaction processing, risk, fraud, AML or decisioning platforms. • Exposure to real-time analytics, event streaming, machine learning or fraud detection systems. • Experience working with cloud technologies such as AWS, Azure or GCP. A tremendous opportunity awaits a Financial Crime Principal level Engineer to collaborate a highly respected team. You will join a high?impact engineering group responsible for building and evolving the bank's risk and fraud detection engines. As the organisation scales rapidly, this team is central to protecting customers, strengthening digital trust, and enabling safe growth across payments, digital channels and lending.
