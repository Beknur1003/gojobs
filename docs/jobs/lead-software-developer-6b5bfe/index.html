Job Description Summary You’ll join the Users Squad, a critical engineering team responsible for some of the most important capabilities on the ABC Glofox platform. The team owns core user, account, authentication, permissions, and member-management experiences that are fundamental to how studios and their staff interact with the platform. The Users Squad works on capabilities including authentication, MFA, permissions, member transfers, family accounts, global search, and restricted member profiles. These are high-impact platform services used across the product, requiring strong attention to security, reliability, scalability, and user experience. As a Lead Software Developer, you’ll help set technical direction for the Users domain, lead complex backend initiatives, mentor engineers, and partner closely with Product and other engineering squads. You’ll also contribute to cross-team initiatives such as splitting the Core API and API Gateway. WHAT YOU’LL DO • Provide technical leadership and guidance across the Users Squad. • Mentor engineers through design reviews, pairing, thoughtful feedback, and technical coaching. • Design and develop reliable backend services using Golang and other appropriate technologies. • Own complex technical problems from discovery and design through delivery, operation, and continuous improvement. • Help define and evolve the technical direction for user, account, authentication, and permissions capabilities. • Contribute to authentication and security-related features, including login, MFA, and the permissions service across frontend and backend. • Work on account capabilities including: Leads and client management; Staff and user details; Family accounts. • Build and improve global features including: Categories; Global search; User-related settings; Restricted member profiles; • Contribute to orchestration capabilities such as member transfers. • Work with an energised, fun, friendly, and customer-focused cross-functional team. • Collaborate with designers, developers, product managers, testers, and partner squads to transform ideas into unique, human experiences for fitness entrepreneurs. • Contribute to cross-team platform initiatives, including the separation of the Core API and API Gateway. • Use modern Agile and Lean ways of working underpinned by DevOps and CI/CD. • Use AI coding tools as part of normal delivery while maintaining ownership of design decisions, code quality, testing, and what reaches production. • Reduce repeated manual work through automation and AI-assisted tooling, applying appropriate review and test standards to anything those tools produce. WHAT YOU’LL NEED • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a relevant field preferred. • 6+ years of experience with a general-purpose programming language. • Proven experience owning complex backend features, services, or platform capabilities. • Strong experience designing and building production software and APIs. • Experience leading technical direction and influencing engineering decisions across a team or domain. • Hands-on experience using AI coding assistants or agents to deliver production software, including reviewing and testing generated output. • Strong understanding of practical Agile, Lean, and Continuous Delivery methods. • Knowledge of modern quality and testing principles. • Strong written and verbal communication skills. • Comfortable mentoring engineers and partnering closely with Product and other technical teams. • Comfortable learning new technologies and systems. • Experience working on systems where security, permissions, authentication, and reliability are important considerations.
