About the Role : We're looking for a Golang Developer to build and maintain reliable backend services across cloud and edge environments. The role involves working on distributed services, networking, concurrency, real-time communication, and device connectivity, with a focus on strong engineering fundamentals, problem-solving, understanding complex systems, and building reliable backend solutions. What You'll Do : - Design and develop production services in Go. - Build APIs and services that communicate across cloud and edge environments. - Work with long-lived connections, concurrency, and real-time communication. - Handle unreliable networks, connection failures, retries, and reconnections. - Improve service reliability, performance, and resource utilisation. - Debug production issues using logs, metrics, and profiling. - Contribute to system design and architectural discussions. - Write maintainable, well-tested, and idiomatic Go. - Participate in code reviews and improve engineering practices. What We're Looking For : - 2+ years of professional software engineering experience. - Production experience with Go, or strong backend experience with the ability to quickly become productive in Go. - Good understanding of Go concurrency - goroutines, channels, mutexes, and context. - Good networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, HTTP/HTTPS, TCP vs. UDP, DNS, TLS, timeouts, retries, and connection management, along with a basic understanding of NAT, latency, and packet loss. - Understanding of backend and distributed systems concepts. - Experience building and debugging production services. - Good understanding of software design and modular architecture. - Experience with Linux, Git, and CI/CD. - Familiarity with databases and APIs. Good to Have : - Experience with WebSockets or other real-time communication technologies. - Familiarity with NATS, RabbitMQ, or similar messaging systems. - Experience with Docker/Kubernetes. - Familiarity with Prometheus, Grafana, or similar monitoring tools. - Exposure to edge or IoT systems. - Experience working on networking-heavy applications. - Exposure to video streaming or media systems. - Familiarity with RTSP, WebRTC, HLS, or RTMP. - Experience with FFmpeg, GStreamer, or MediaMTX. How We Work : - We use Microsoft Teams for daily communication, including standups and team meetings. - Our culture values open discussion, ownership, and a founder's mindset. - We prioritize exceptional design, UI/UX, thorough documentation, clear to-do lists, and data-driven decision-making. - Team bonding is encouraged through bi-monthly townhalls, destressing sessions with a certified healer, and biannual company retreats. - We offer a fully remote workplace, comprehensive health insurance, attractive equity options for top performers, mental health consultations, company-sponsored upskilling courses, designated growth hours, 40 hours annually for community causes, and access to a financial advisor. - Wobot is an Equal Opportunity Employer.
