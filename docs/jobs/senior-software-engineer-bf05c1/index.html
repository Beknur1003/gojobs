Passionate about building a scalable cloud platform that powers enterprise-grade observability? Are you a fast learner who enjoys working at the intersection of infrastructure, automation, and reliability? Join one of our Cloud Observability teams The team develops and supports a reliable monitoring system tailored for Linode Enterprise customers. It provides insights through a robust observability platform for telemetry, metrics, and logs. Additionally, the team manages Armada, a foundational platform enabling Cloud Observability services. Armada now plays a pivotal role in Kubernetes infrastructure orchestration, support delivery of one of the company's current top priority - the development of Akamai Inference Cloud. Partner with the best You will contribute to building and evolving a platform that is critical to enterprise customers and strategic internal initiatives. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will be responsible for: • Supporting the development and maintenance of Armada as a core Cloud Observability platform (primarily in a DevOps capacity) • Designing and implementing scalable, reliable Kubernetes-based infrastructure solutions • Building and maintaining infrastructure as code using Terraform and Helm • Automating operational workflows using Python and Bash • Ensuring system stability, performance, and security across observability services • Collaborating with cross-functional teams to enable new services and infrastructure orchestration use cases Do what you love To be successful in this role you will: • Have relevant professional experience and Bachelor's degree in Computer Science-related field or equivalent education • Gain practical experience with a primary cloud platform (AWS, GCP, Azure), understanding external cloud practices like cost optimization and cluster management. • Have strong experience with Python, Bash, Linux, Terraform, and Helm, be proficient with Kubernetes (CKAD level or equivalent experience) • Have a solid understanding of networking fundamentals • Demonstrate experience with GitHub Actions; familiarity with Golang is appreciated but not essential.
