Software Engineer - II, Kafka Infrastructure Summary of role Kafka is the backbone of how data moves through Sumo Logic, and every team that builds on it needs clusters that are predictable, reliable and easy to manage. As a Software Engineer within the Kafka Infrastructure team, you will build and maintain the tools and frameworks that manage the full lifecycle of Kafka clusters at Sumo Logic. The team acts as the platform and center of excellence for Kafka, with the long-term goal of unifying Kafka operations under a single, consistent usage pattern. You will contribute to the automation that lets teams spin up, tear down, migrate and manage Kafka clusters and topics themselves, as part of Sumo Logic's internal Platform-as-a-Service. Responsibilities • Build and maintain automation and tooling for the Kafka cluster lifecycle: provisioning, upgrades, scaling, failover and migration • Develop and own the self-service interfaces teams use to consume Kafka (Terraform modules, CLI tools, APIs, dashboards), with predictable, versioned behavior • Maintain and evolve the team's core components, including cluster balancing, consumer-lag monitoring, schema management, cluster-to-cluster data migration, Kafka-to-S3 backup and hardened machine images • Improve observability and operational practices for Kafka across the organization, and drive standardization of tooling • Provide second-level support for Kafka clusters, and participate in the on-call rotation (with an eventual move into the PaaS global on-call rotation) • Educate and collaborate with application teams through onboarding sessions, documentation and migration guides, and consult on partitioning, consumer-group and schema best practices • Evaluate and test new Kafka technologies and designs, and make recommendations • Write detailed design documents and documentation on system design and implementation Required Qualifications and Skills • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or another scientific or technical discipline. • Ability to influence: Understand people's values and motivations and influence them towards making good architectural choices. • Collaborative working style: You can work with other engineers to come up with good decisions. • Bias towards action: You need to make things happen. It is essential you don't become an inhibitor of progress, but an enabler. • Flexibility: You are willing to learn and change. Admit past approaches might not be the right ones now. • Technical skills: • 2+ years of experience in software development in Java/Scala/Golang or similar. • 1+ years of hands-on experience with Apache Kafka, including brokers, topics, partitions, replication and consumer groups. • Good understanding of Kafka internals and operations: partition reassignment and rebalancing, leader election and in-sync replicas, retention and compaction, and broker and consumer performance tuning. • Experience with cloud platforms like AWS/Azure/GCP (AWS preferred). • Skilled in Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools, specifically Terraform. • Proficient in scripting languages such as Python/Bash. • Comfortable with Linux administration and troubleshooting distributed systems in production. • Familiarity with monitoring tools like Prometheus, Grafana, or similar, including consumer-lag monitoring. • Working knowledge of CI/CD and version control workflows. • Knowledgeable in the security aspects of Kafka (TLS, authentication and authorization). Nice to have: • Experience with Cruise Control, Schema Registry, Kafka Connect or MirrorMaker. • Experience building hardened machine images (for example with Packer). • Experience with Kubernetes and GitOps. • Experience building CLI tools or internal developer platforms.
