The candidate will have responsibilities across the following functions: Backend Engineering: • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services using Golang. • Build and maintain reliable microservices and REST/GraphQL APIs. • Design and implement event-driven systems and asynchronous processing using queues/messaging systems. • Build backend systems capable of handling high-volume and transaction-heavy workloads. • Work on performance, scalability, reliability, security, and availability of backend services. • Diagnose and resolve production issues and performance bottlenecks. System Design: • Own technical solutions from design through implementation and production. • Design scalable distributed systems and define appropriate service boundaries. • Make sound decisions around databases, APIs, caching, messaging, and system architecture. • Participate in architecture and design discussions and contribute to technical documentation. Databases: • Work extensively with relational and NoSQL databases. • Design efficient schemas, queries, indexes, and data access patterns. • Understand database scalability, performance optimisation, consistency, and reliability. Engineering and Collaboration: • Write clean, maintainable, production-quality code. • Develop unit, integration, and performance tests. • Participate in code reviews and technical discussions. • Collaborate with frontend/mobile engineers and infrastructure teams to deliver end-to-end product functionality. • Contribute to CI/CD, observability, monitoring, and engineering best practices. Requirements: • 6+ years of backend software development experience. • Strong hands-on experience with Golang. • Minimum 1+ year of practical Golang experience is mandatory. • Strong experience with system design and distributed systems. • Strong understanding of relational databases and NoSQL databases. • Experience building REST and/or GraphQL APIs. • Experience with microservices and event-driven architecture. • Experience with queues, messaging systems, or asynchronous processing. • Good understanding of scalability, performance, reliability, and fault tolerance. • Familiarity with at least one cloud platform: AWS, GCP, or Azure. • Strong communication and collaboration skills. • Strong Java backend engineers can also be considered only if they have at least 1 year of hands-on Golang experience. • Candidates with less than 1 year of Go experience will not be considered. • The selected candidate should be comfortable working primarily with Golang. Nice to Have: • Experience in fintech, payments, banking, or transaction-heavy systems. • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes. • Experience with observability tools such as Datadog, Prometheus, or Grafana. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated quality gates. • Experience building highly available and fault-tolerant systems. • Experience working with financial transactions, payment workflows, or money movement systems.
