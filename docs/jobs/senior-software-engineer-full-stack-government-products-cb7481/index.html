As a Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack, you will serve as a key technical contributor for this 0-to-1 initiative. What You'll Do • Design, build, and ship features across Go backend services and React-based front-end experiences for a brand-new, standalone product optimized for security, reliability, and scale. • Contribute to the architectural strategy and technical decisions of a 0-to-1 platform, setting foundational engineering patterns built to last. • Partner closely with Product, Design, and external stakeholders to translate complex requirements into secure, well-tested, and maintainable systems. • Move fluidly between backend and front-end work as priorities shift, helping the team stay flexible and deliver quickly. • Mentor engineers through design discussions and code reviews, fostering technical excellence and raising the engineering bar for the team. What You Bring • 5+ years experience building and maintaining production backend services, ideally in Go or Ruby, with a solid grasp of API design, domain modeling, and reliable data flows. • Strong experience building front-end applications with JavaScript frameworks such as React, with comfort working in Node and backend-for-frontend architectures. • Demonstrable execution in a zero-to-one product space, navigating technical ambiguity, making sound technical decisions, and taking features from design through production. • A track record of guiding the work of other engineers through design reviews, code reviews, and mentorship. • Excellent communication skills with the ability to partner closely with Product, Design, and external institutional partners. • Experience with financial products, ledgering, reporting, or investment technology is a plus.
