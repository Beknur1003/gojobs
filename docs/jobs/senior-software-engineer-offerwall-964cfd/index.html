The opportunity We are looking for an experienced, dedicated, and energetic senior developer to join the Offerwall team! Our mission is to build maintainable and resilient systems to identify users by respecting their privacy settings, to create financial transactions for accounting, and deliver virtual rewards to the user. We aim to do so efficiently and effectively at scales in the range of tens of billions of events per hour. Our team and key decision makers are spread across the globe. Come help us ensure users can be identified accurately and rewarded promptly for their activities! What you'll be doing • Design and build new product features, and refactor and optimize existing ones as conditions change • Build and maintain resilient backend services that process events at very large scale • Take ownership of a key part of a large system, building your domain knowledge with support from the team • Take part in code reviews, design discussions and knowledge-sharing sessions that keep the team collaborating and improving • Work with product managers and related teams to deliver results that reach users • Use AI-assisted tools across coding, testing, debugging and documentation, and explore AI approaches that improve the reliability and efficiency of our distributed systems • Apply our engineering principles and bring your own practices to the team What we're looking for • Hands-on backend development experience in Golang or Java • Experience developing and maintaining distributed web services at scale • Experience with event-driven systems, such as Kafka or other message queues • Experience producing and analyzing web service event audit trails • Effective use of AI-assisted development tools while maintaining high standards for code quality, security and reliability • Effective written/verbal communication, with the habit of asking questions early and working closely with teammates You might also have • Experience in ad tech or fintech, or with Ruby on Rails • Experience with SQL and data platforms such as BigQuery and BigTable • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes, and with observability tools such as Grafana or OpenTelemetry Additional Details Base Salary Range: We determine the base salary range for this role based on your primary work location: Zone A: $146,500 - $190,400 Zone B: $130,400 - $169,500 Zone C: $115,400 - $149,900 This range reflects the anticipated base salary for this position.
