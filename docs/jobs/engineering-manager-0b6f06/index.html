FairMoney is a pioneering mobile banking institution specializing in extending credit to emerging markets. Established in 2017, the company currently operates primarily within Nigeria, and it has secured nearly €50 million in funding from renowned global investors, including Tiger Global, DST, and Flourish Ventures. FairMoney maintains a strong international presence, with offices in several countries, including France, Nigeria, Germany, Latvia, the UK, Türkiye, and India. In alignment with its vision, FairMoney is actively constructing the foremost mobile banking platform and point-of-sale (POS) solution tailored for emerging markets. The journey began with the introduction of a digital microcredit application exclusively available on Android and iOS devices. Today, FairMoney has significantly expanded its range of services, encompassing a comprehensive suite of financial products, such as current accounts, savings accounts, debit cards, and state-of-the-art POS solutions designed to meet the needs of both merchants and agents. About the Role We are looking for an experienced Senior Engineering Manager to join our Technology Division, which comprises over 75 professionals across Lagos, Abuja, Bangalore, Paris, Türkiye, and other remote locations globally. Reporting directly to the Head of Engineering within a business unit, you will provide technical leadership and strategic direction across globally distributed engineering teams. The ideal candidate will have a strong foundation in backend software engineering, ideally having progressed from a hands-on backend engineering career into engineering leadership. This role offers an opportunity to shape engineering practices, strengthen technical execution, and drive the delivery of scalable, high-quality software solutions within a fast-growing fintech environment. The position is primarily remote, with occasional office visits. As FairMoney continues to expand its engineering presence across multiple regions, we are building centres of excellence and seeking talented, driven engineering leaders who can contribute to our next phase of growth. Key Responsibilities Engineering Leadership & Team Management • Lead, develop, and scale globally distributed software engineering teams across Backend, Mobile, and Quality Assurance (QA). • Provide technical direction, mentorship, and leadership to engineering teams, fostering a culture of ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement. • Establish clear team objectives, priorities, and accountability to ensure alignment with broader business and technology goals. Engineering Delivery & Operational Excellence • Establish and continuously improve engineering processes, standards, and practices to ensure high-quality and timely delivery of engineering objectives. • Drive effective planning, execution, and delivery across multiple engineering teams while maintaining a strong focus on efficiency and velocity. • Identify and address delivery bottlenecks, ensuring teams have the structure and support needed to perform effectively. Technical Strategy & Architecture • Contribute to the design and development of scalable, reliable, and efficient engineering solutions. • Provide technical oversight across systems and infrastructure, ensuring architectural decisions support business growth and long-term scalability. • Champion engineering best practices, maintain high standards of code quality, and proactively manage and reduce technical debt. • Evaluate engineering solutions through the lens of efficiency, quality, scalability, and delivery velocity. Cross-functional Collaboration • Work closely with product and other business stakeholders to translate business requirements into effective technical solutions. • Drive collaboration across Backend, Mobile, and QA teams to ensure seamless integration and coordinated execution. • Ensure engineering priorities remain aligned with product objectives and the broader business strategy. Our Technology Stack • Cloud Providers: Azure, AWS • Backend: Primarily Golang, with some Ruby on Rails • Architecture: Containerised microservices • Mobile Applications: React Native • Web Applications: ReactJS • Configuration Management & Infrastructure: Terraform, Kubernetes, App Service • CI/CD: Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions Requirements • 7–12 years of experience in backend software development, with a strong understanding of backend engineering principles and practices. • Significant experience managing and leading software engineering teams, ideally across Backend, Mobile, and QA functions. • Strong backend engineering background, with proficiency in at least one backend language such as C#, JavaScript, Python, or Java. • Good understanding of cloud infrastructure and services, particularly Azure or AWS, as well as software architecture and system design. • Demonstrated ability to lead, mentor, and develop engineering teams while driving accountability, performance, and continuous improvement. • Experience establishing engineering processes, improving delivery efficiency, maintaining code quality, and managing technical debt. • Strong problem-solving, decision-making, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to lead distributed, multicultural teams. • Degree or diploma in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field; self-taught professionals with at least five years of experience in computer engineering are also welcome. Benefits • Competitive pay. • Paid annual leave. • A network of talented colleagues. Recruitment Process • Screening call with a member of the recruitment team for 30 minutes. • Technical interview with members of the Engineering team for 45-60 minutes.
