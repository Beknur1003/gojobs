Kubernetes Engineer

InterSystems Dublin опубликовано 17 августа 2026

KubernetesTerraformPrometheusGrafanaLinuxCI/CDAI/LLM
Overview We are seeking a Kubernetes Developer to join our global infrastructure and platform engineering team, helping build software-driven automation for InterSystems Kubernetes-based managed services and SaaS platforms. You will work with software engineers, Kubernetes architects, DevOps, SRE, and security teams to develop reliable, secure, production-ready platform capabilities. The role combines Go development with hands-on Kubernetes experience, with a focus on turning repeatable operational processes into maintainable platform software. Key Responsibilities • Develop and maintain Kubernetes-native software in Go, including controllers, Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs), reconciliation logic, and automation components. • Build automation for provisioning, configuration, upgrades, scaling, recovery, and other managed service workflows. • Write maintainable Go code and develop unit, integration, and cluster-based tests. • Support CI/CD pipelines for building, testing, packaging, and releasing Kubernetes platform software. • Integrate with Kubernetes networking, storage, ingress, certificates, scheduling, security, and cluster lifecycle capabilities. • Contribute to automation using Helm, Terraform, GitOps, Argo CD, Flux, and related tooling. • Implement secure defaults, least-privilege RBAC, secrets handling, workload isolation, and operational guardrails. • Build observable and supportable software using logs, metrics, events, health checks, and status conditions. • Troubleshoot Kubernetes workloads, controller behaviour, failed reconciliations, deployments, and automation issues. • Collaborate with engineering, SRE, security, and application teams, and contribute to documentation, runbooks, and knowledge sharing. Experience & Qualifications • 3+ years of experience in software engineering, platform engineering, DevOps, infrastructure automation, cloud engineering, or SRE. • Experience developing in Go, or strong software engineering experience with the ability to work in Go. • Practical experience with Kubernetes, containers, Linux, and cloud-native deployment patterns. • Familiarity with Kubernetes APIs, CRDs, controllers, reconciliation loops, RBAC, events, status conditions, and resource lifecycle. • Experience with Git, CI/CD, automated testing, and code review. • Working knowledge of Helm, Terraform, GitOps, Argo CD, Flux, or similar tooling. • Understanding of Kubernetes security concepts including RBAC, secrets, namespaces, network policies, admission controls, and workload isolation. • Strong troubleshooting, communication, and collaboration skills. Nice to Have • Experience with Kubebuilder, Operator SDK, controller-runtime, client-go, or similar frameworks. • Experience developing Kubernetes Operators, controllers, admission webhooks, or platform automation. • Experience with EKS, AKS, GKE, Rancher, Spectro Cloud, or similar platforms. • Familiarity with Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Fluent Bit, ELK/EFK, Istio, Linkerd, Cilium, Velero, Kasten, or Stash. • Open-source contributions to Kubernetes, Go, cloud-native, or platform engineering projects. • Relevant certifications such as CKAD, CKA, CKS, Terraform Associate, or LFCS. About InterSystems InterSystems, a creative data technology provider, delivers a unified foundation for next-generation applications for healthcare, finance, manufacturing, and supply chain customers in more than 80 countries.
