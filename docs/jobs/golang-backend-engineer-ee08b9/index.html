About the Company Blitz Electric Mobility is the largest electric vehicle fleet and last-mile delivery platform in Indonesia. We have completed over 20 million deliveries, saved an estimated 25 million tonnes of CO₂ emissions, and operate across the entire Java–Bali corridor. We are an NVIDIA Inception partner and a Grab Ventures portfolio company. Our investors include Balaji Srinivasan (former CTO of Coinbase, General Partner at a16z), Sequoia Capital, and several leading technology-focused venture funds. Our founder has been named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 list. Job Summary We are looking for a Backend Engineer with strong Golang experience to develop and maintain core backend services in a microservices environment. This role focuses on building high-throughput, low-latency services, contributing to system reliability, and working closely with a distributed engineering team. Key Responsibilities • Develop and maintain backend microservices written in Go, including API endpoints, business logic, and data access layers. • Design and implement service-to-service communication using gRPC and REST, with proper error handling, retries, and timeouts. • Write and optimise database queries (MySQL) for performance, including indexing strategies and query plan analysis. • Contribute to system reliability: structured logging, metrics instrumentation, alerting, and on-call production support during Jakarta business hours. • Participate in the design and implementation of new features across order processing, batch management, driver management, and assignment services. • Write and maintain unit tests, integration tests, and participate in load/performance testing for critical services. • Conduct code reviews and contribute to improving backend engineering standards, documentation, and best practices. • Collaborate with the product, QA, and operations teams on requirements, testing, and release planning. • Support production incident response: triage, root cause analysis, hotfixes, and postmortem documentation. Required Qualifications • 3–5 years of professional backend engineering experience, with at least 2 years writing production Go. • Strong understanding of microservices architecture, including service discovery, inter-service communication (gRPC, REST), and fault tolerance patterns. • Solid RDBMS skills (MySQL or PostgreSQL): query optimisation, indexing, schema design, and migration management. • Experience with message queues or event-driven systems (SQS, Kafka, RabbitMQ, NATS, or equivalent). • Familiarity with containerised deployments (Docker) and cloud platforms (AWS preferred). • Ability to diagnose and resolve production issues using log aggregation, tracing, and monitoring tools. • Strong written and verbal communication in Bahasa Indonesia and English. • Based in Jakarta. This is a work-from-office position, five days per week. Nice to Have • Experience with Redis for caching, distributed locking, or rate limiting. • Familiarity with Elasticsearch or similar search/indexing systems. • Experience with observability tooling (Signoz, Datadog, Prometheus/Grafana, or equivalent). • Exposure to CI/CD pipelines, automated testing in Go, and deployment automation. • Experience writing technical documentation, RCAs, or design proposals. Employment Terms • Employment type: Full-time • Work arrangement: On-site, Jakarta office, Monday to Friday • Probation period: 3 months per Indonesian labour standards
