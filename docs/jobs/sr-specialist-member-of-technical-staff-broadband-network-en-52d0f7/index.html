At AT&T, we’re building the next generation of broadband connectivity. As demand for AT&T Fiber and Multi-Gig services continues to expand, we’re looking for an early-career engineer who is excited to help design, develop, test, and deploy the technologies powering tomorrow’s broadband networks. This is an opportunity to build deep technical expertise across fiber broadband, Ethernet networking, PON, optical networking, network security, and software-enabled network technologies while partnering with architects, product teams, security organizations, and leading technology suppliers. What You'll Do: • Design and develop broadband network architectures and solutions supporting Fiber and Multi-Gig services across consumer and business markets. • Work with technologies including Ethernet L2/L3, Passive Optical Networks (PON), optical networking/DWDM, network security, and industry networking standards. • Collaborate with Solution Architects, product owners, engineering teams, technology suppliers, and product marketing organizations to translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions. • Partner with AT&T Security teams to ensure architectures, designs, and features meet established network and information security requirements. • Help drive the development and delivery of hardware and software capabilities with equipment and technology suppliers. • Perform proof-of-concept testing, design validation, vendor-product configuration, and hands-on lab testing. • Use programming and scripting to support data analysis, automation, testing, and prototype development. • Translate technical requirements for suppliers and validate that delivered solutions meet AT&T's technical, operational, quality, and business expectations. • Document complex engineering designs and clearly communicate technical concepts to engineering teams, stakeholders, and leadership. What You'll Bring: • A passion for broadband, fiber, networking, and emerging technologies, with a strong desire to develop deep technical expertise. • Working knowledge of Ethernet networking and L2/L3 networking concepts. • Exposure to areas such as PON/FTTx, network security, optical networking, DWDM, data science, or broadband access technologies. • Programming or scripting experience with one or more languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, Node.js, Go/Golang, C, or C++. • A hands-on engineering mindset and interest in configuring, testing, troubleshooting, and validating network technologies in a lab environment. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to approach complex technical challenges creatively. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document technical designs and explain complex concepts clearly. • Strong collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively across engineering teams, product organizations, security teams, and external suppliers. • Curiosity, initiative, and a commitment to continuous learning in a rapidly evolving technology environment.
