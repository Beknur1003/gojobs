Overview: FinteqHub is hiring a Senior DevOps / SRE Engineer to strengthen the reliability, scalability, and observability of our payment infrastructure. We're looking for an experienced engineer who is comfortable working with production-critical systems, takes ownership of technical challenges, and enjoys improving infrastructure and engineering workflows. About Product: Finteqhub А PCI DSS certified payment gateway for online businesses, providing integration with payment systems via a single software platform. Learn more Purpose of the role: You will help maintain and improve our hybrid infrastructure, combining company-managed bare-metal environments with cloud services. Your focus will be on infrastructure reliability, Kubernetes, CI/CD automation, monitoring, and observability. Working closely with Engineering and Security teams, you'll contribute to shared infrastructure initiatives, support production operations, and help engineering teams take greater ownership of their tools and workflows. This is a hands-on technical role with opportunities to propose improvements, influence engineering practices, and contribute directly to the stability and performance of our platform. Key responsibilities: • Maintain, operate, and continuously improve reliable, scalable, and secure production infrastructure across bare-metal and cloud environments. • Manage and optimize Kubernetes environments, containerized workloads, and related infrastructure components. • Develop and improve CI/CD pipelines, GitOps workflows, and infrastructure automation using tools such as GitHub Actions, Argo CD, Terraform, Terragrunt, and Ansible. • Strengthen monitoring, logging, alerting, and distributed tracing to improve system visibility and incident response. • Define, implement, and continuously improve Service Level Indicators (SLIs) and Service Level Objectives (SLOs). • Support production operations, troubleshoot infrastructure issues, and collaborate with engineering teams on deployments and incident resolution. • Contribute to infrastructure security, vulnerability remediation, system hardening, and annual PCI DSS certification readiness. • Work with engineering teams to improve operational processes, deployment reliability, and ownership of infrastructure workflows. • Participate in daytime operational support rotation, responding to engineering requests and production-related issues. • Identify opportunities for automation, performance optimization, and improvements to infrastructure reliability. Required Experience: • 4+ years of hands-on experience with CI/CD tools and workflows, such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Argo CD, or Argo Workflows. • 4+ years of practical experience with containerization and Kubernetes in production environments. • 4+ years of experience with Infrastructure as Code and configuration management tools, such as Terraform, Terragrunt, Ansible, Pulumi, or CloudFormation. • 4+ years of experience with Git and modern version-control workflows. • 4+ years of scripting or programming experience using Python, Golang, Bash, or similar languages. • Practical experience with monitoring, logging, observability, and alerting tools. • Proven experience defining and implementing SLIs and SLOs to improve service reliability. • Experience operating and troubleshooting high-load, production-critical systems, ideally within financial services. • Familiarity with production data infrastructure, including relational databases, distributed data systems, caching, and messaging technologies. • Strong troubleshooting skills and the ability to investigate complex infrastructure and production issues. • Ability to collaborate across engineering teams, communicate technical challenges clearly, and take ownership of improvements. • Russian language proficiency and at least B1-level English. Nice to have: • Experience working with hybrid infrastructure combining on-premises or bare-metal environments with cloud platforms such as Oracle Cloud, AWS, or GCP. • Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL, ClickHouse, Redis, Kafka-compatible platforms, or similar data technologies. • Familiarity with observability tools such as ELK, VictoriaMetrics, OpenTelemetry, Jaeger, Sentry. • Knowledge of enterprise security practices, infrastructure hardening, access management, and vulnerability remediation. • Experience with PCI DSS or other security and compliance frameworks. • Familiarity with virtualization, networking infrastructure, and secure connectivity. • Understanding of the software development lifecycle and experience supporting enterprise-grade software. • Experience using AI-assisted engineering tools to improve development and operational workflows. Our Benefits: • Full-time remote work opportunities and flexible working hours • Private insurance • Additional 1 Day Off per calendar year • Sports program compensation • Comprehensive Mental Health Program • Free online English lessons with a native speaker • Generous referral program • Training, internal workshops, and participation in international professional conferences and corporate events.
