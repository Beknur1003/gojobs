We see blockchain, smart contracts as a meta-technology with a huge potential and real applicability. Based on our experience in Banking, Regulation, Product management and Software development we will try to shape the future. To be able to reach our goals, building the right team is the base for success. A good venture will be the consequence of having the best performing team. We are creating an environment where you can get your maximum potential, where people matter and where transparency is on the DNA. We are searching for good people that first of all will fit our culture and mindset, and second, will add their experience and expertise to our team. ´The future is here. It's just not evenly distributed yet´, William Gibson. What we offer: • Challenging projects and a great environment. • Innovation and building new business with your own hands. • Flat organization and short decision paths. • Flexible working hours. • Remote working (Based on the team needs). • Remote Work Allowance. • Gross Salary: 30-50k. • Social benefits: Flexible Payment Plan, medical insurance and others. • 25 vacation days. • 2 free days for conference/courses + Individual Training Budget. • The birthday day off. What we’re looking for: We’re looking for a backend engineer who has designed systems with Domain-Driven Design, Hexagonal Architecture and event-driven patterns, to join a high-performing technical team working on licensable B2B products. You’ll be involved in the design, development, and evolution of solutions built on modern, scalable, and maintainable architectures. Your architecture experience matters more than your current language. Our stack is Go, and if you’ve designed these systems in Java, Kotlin, Scala or a similar language, we’ll help you become productive in Go. Blockchain experience is a plus, but not a requirement. You’ll have real impact: taking part in technical decision-making, shaping architecture, and setting engineering best practices. Our stack is Go (and how we’ll help you learn it): • Your architecture experience carries straight over: DDD, ports and adapters, CQRS and messaging work the same way in Go. Only the language changes. • Go is simple and explicit, which keeps the domain model clean and visible. • Structured onboarding: pairing with senior Go engineers, a dedicated learning budget, and reviews focused on idiomatic Go. What you’ll do: • Design, develop, and maintain backend services using Golang • Define bounded contexts and model domains together with Product • Apply Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters) and Domain-Driven Design (DDD) • Design and implement event-driven architectures (async messaging, CQRS, eventual consistency) • Build scalable and resilient solutions for licensable B2B software • Define and promote quality standards, testing, and observability • Review code and mentor other developers on design, testing and architecture • Collaborate closely with Product, Architecture, and DevOps teams • Continuously improve the tech stack and development processes What we’re looking for: • 2+ years of production backend development (Go, Java, Kotlin, Groovy or similar) • Proven experience designing systems with: • Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters) or Clean Architecture • Domain-Driven Design (DDD), including strategic design (bounded contexts, context mapping) • Event-driven systems: async messaging, eventual consistency, CQRS • Able to explain and defend architecture decisions and their trade-offs • Motivation to work in Go (no prior Go experience strictly required) • Experience designing APIs (REST, gRPC) • Strong testing practices (unit and integration) • Experience with distributed systems • Knowledge of relational and/or NoSQL databases • Experience with cloud environments and containers (Docker, Kubernetes is a plus) • Good backend security and performance practices Nice to have: • Any Go experience (side projects count) • Event sourcing in production (e.g. Axon, EventStoreDB) • Experience with blockchain (Ethereum, Hedera, Solana, etc.) • Smart contracts or blockchain integration experience • Background in SaaS / licensable B2B products • Messaging & streaming platforms (Kafka, RabbitMQ, NATS, etc.) • CI/CD knowledge • High-availability environments experience We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity in our company.
