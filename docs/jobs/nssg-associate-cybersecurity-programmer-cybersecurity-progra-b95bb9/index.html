Netpoleon Solutions is a well-established IT Network Security solutions provider and the Asia arm subsidiary of Macnica Inc – a Global Company with trillion-yen revenue. Netpoleon Solutions has offices in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Myanmar, India, Australia and New Zealand, with its parent Company based in Japan. We are now inviting individuals to join the Singapore office. Candidates should have a strong analytical mind and possess excellent cybersecurity skills and knowledge. If you have these qualities, you will embark on an exciting career together with us to pursue our strategic expansion. Job Responsibilities: Security Automation & Development • Design, develop, and maintain cybersecurity automation scripts and applications • Build integrations between security products, ITSM tools, SIEM platforms, and cloud services • Develop custom dashboards, reporting tools, and data visualization modules • Automate repetitive security operations and incident response workflows • Develop API-based integrations for cybersecurity platforms • Enhance existing internal engineering tools and portals Security Platform Integration • Integrate and support cybersecurity solutions such as: o SIEM o SOAR o EDR/XDR o IAM/PAM o DLP o CSPM o Vulnerability Management o Threat Intelligence Platforms • Work with REST APIs, JSON, XML, and webhooks • Perform troubleshooting for integrations and automation workflows DevSecOps & Infrastructure • Support CI/CD pipeline security integration • Develop secure coding and deployment practices • Support containerized deployments using Docker or Kubernetes • Work with Linux and Windows environments • Assist in infrastructure hardening and automation Job Requirements: • Positive and professional working attitude. • Ability to work both independently and as part of a team. • Capable of handling stress, meeting tight deadlines, and thriving in a fast-paced environment. • Diploma or Degree in Cyber Security, Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or other related technical disciplines. • Candidates with 2–5 years of experience in programming or cybersecurity engineering are preferred, while fresh graduates with strong technical foundations in programming and cybersecurity are also encouraged to apply. • Strong hands-on experience in one or more programming or scripting languages such as Python, PowerShell, Bash/Shell Scripting, JavaScript, Java, C#, or GoLang ( Advantageous) is required. • Strong understanding of REST APIs, JSON/XML, OAuth and authentication methods, SIEM concepts, and automation workflows is required. • Good understanding of Linux and Windows Server environments, networking fundamentals, TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, firewalls, and proxies is essential for this
