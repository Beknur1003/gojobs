You will be required to: • develop and maintain REST API services for integration with other company products; • develop micro-services of the platform and modules on Golang from scratch; • participate in the discussion of architectural solutions; • conduct interviews; • solve problems to reduce the risk of falling services (preventing services from falling); • review the code of other team members; - write autotests; • write and update product documentation; • support code quality; • communicate with colleagues in English. Required skills: • English at B2 level or higher (confident spoken English required for daily communication with an international team); • at least 4 years of commercial development experience in Go; • experience working with Kafka or other message brokers; • experience working on highload projects. More from our stack: Docker, Kubernetes, Kafka, PostgreSQL, Google Cloud Platform, Google Cloud Storage, Google Cloud Log Explorer. Benefits: • Good compensation • Health insurance • Gym discount • Corporate English lessons • Modern comfortable offices in Moscow, Saint-Petersburg, Vladimir • Monthly compensation for electricity and the Internet • Multinational team with experts around the world • Participating in professional events and corporate events Please note: while the role is fully remote, it is intended for candidates working from within Russia.
