Senior Loyalty Marketing Lead

The role & impact Accountants and bookkeepers are an important part of the Xero ecosystem, and this role shapes how we recognise and reward them at every stage of their journey with us. You’ll look after our Salesforce Loyalty platform, work with the Journey Managers who care for each stage of the partner experience to build the right incentives into their plans, and run incentives to help partners to take their next step. This isn’t a campaign role. You’ll own a live, global platform and be able to draw a straight line from your work to partner engagement, tier progression and extra revenue. Product-led loyalty is new for Xero, so you’ll Go Bold by shaping it rather than inheriting it, and share what you learn with senior leaders along the way. The team & how they connect You’ll join the Accountant and Bookkeeper (AB) Journey team, which looks after how accountants and bookkeepers experience Xero, from first onboarding through to growth and expansion. You’ll work closely with four Journey Managers, each responsible for a stage of that journey, as well as regional marketing, finance and go-to-market teams. The team is curious and loves to test and learn, with a monthly readout where wins and lessons are shared openly. Initially, the role will focus on • Getting to know our Salesforce partner loyalty platform: what is live today, how data flows, and where improvements will help most. • Building working relationships with Journey Managers and regional marketers, and agreeing one or two loyalty opportunities for each stage. • Setting a starting point for engagement, tiers and points-led actions, so the impact of every change is easy to see. • Launching a first test-and-learn points promotion tied to a clear milestone, then building towards a global promotions calendar with regional versions. Where and how you can work This role is based in the US, and you'll work with colleagues across regions and time zones. We welcome candidates based near one of our offices, and we would also consider a remote US team member for this opportunity. Here are some of the things we are looking for • You bring hands-on experience with a loyalty or incentive platform, ideally Salesforce, from rule configuration through to testing and troubleshooting. • A track record of shaping loyalty, reseller or partner programmes that lifted adoption, engagement, revenue or tier progression. • Comfort with data, so you can define success measures, set up A/B tests and holdouts, and explain the results in plain language. • You Go Fast by testing ideas early and making good calls without waiting for perfect data, which suits new ground like product-led loyalty. • Skill in adapting a global programme for different regions and time zones while keeping the whole thing coherent. • You Go Together with Journey Managers, regional teams and senior leaders, using clear writing to build trust and make the case for change. Apply even if your experience isn’t a perfect match! At Xero, we hire based on your skills, passion, and the unique perspective you can bring to enhance our culture and team. Compensation & Total Rewards At Xero, we value the impact and skills you bring to the team. We don’t just hire for a role; we invest in people. We believe in a Total Package philosophy - which means your value isn't just a single number on a paycheck. While base salary is a core component, we look at your compensation through a holistic lens that includes equity, performance incentives, and world-class benefits.
