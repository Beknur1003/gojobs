About NCR VOYIX NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide. Experience: 10–15 years Job Title: Principal Software Engineer Location: Hyderabad / Chennai About Us NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global technology company delivering technology solutions for the retail and hospitality industries across global markets. Role Overview Join our Edge Platform engineering team as a Principal Software Engineer, providing hands-on technical leadership in the design and evolution of a distributed platform supporting multiple applications, customers, and deployment environments. You will work with engineers, architects, Product Management, Security, SRE, and application teams across globally distributed engineering teams to turn complex requirements into scalable, reliable, secure, and operationally sustainable platform capabilities. You will help teams make strong technical decisions earlier, validate architectures through prototypes and engineering evidence, and reduce unnecessary dependencies and design iterations. Key Responsibilities · Lead and contribute hands-on to the design and development of complex platform capabilities serving multiple applications, customers, and deployment environments. • Partner with architects and engineering teams across locations to develop, validate, and evolve common technical designs and engineering practices, helping teams make sound architectural decisions. • Own and drive resolution of complex engineering problems spanning applications, distributed systems, containers, operating systems, networking, cloud infrastructure, security, observability, and external integrations. • Lead and actively participate in RFCs, technical investigations, POCs, architecture reviews, and production-readiness assessments, while developing similar capability across the engineering team. • Define and reinforce non-functional requirements including scalability, reliability, availability, performance, security, resilience, backward compatibility, supportability, observability, and cost efficiency. • Develop reusable platform capabilities, automation, tooling, and engineering patterns that simplify development, deployment, upgrades, testing, troubleshooting, and operations. • Collaborate across Platform, Security, SRE, application, integration/testing, and Product teams globally to promote reusable solutions, consistent engineering practices, and effective knowledge sharing across sites. • Provide technical leadership for critical production problems, driving root-cause analysis and converting operational learnings into sustainable engineering improvements. • Mentor engineers, facilitate technical knowledge sharing, and help build technical capability across globally distributed teams, reducing dependency on individual engineers, specialists, or locations. • Evaluate emerging technologies and engineering approaches, including AI-assisted development, where they provide measurable product or engineering value. Core Skills · Software & Distributed Systems Engineering: Deep experience designing and building production-grade distributed systems, APIs, services, or platforms, with strong understanding of concurrency, failure handling, scalability, resilience, and maintainability. · Cloud-Native Platforms: Strong experience designing, operating, scaling, and troubleshooting containerized workloads and orchestration platforms; Kubernetes experience is strongly preferred. · Cloud Engineering: Strong experience with at least one major public cloud such as GCP, AWS, or Azure; GCP/GKE experience is preferred. · Systems & Networking: Strong understanding of operating systems, networking, storage, identity, security, and production troubleshooting across system layers; Linux experience is preferred. · Programming: Strong hands-on software development experience in one or more modern languages; Go experience is strongly preferred. · Architecture & Technical Design: Demonstrated ability to translate ambiguous requirements into architecture, RFCs, prototypes, implementation strategies, and production-ready solutions. · Reliability & Observability: Strong understanding of observability, resilience, performance, incident analysis, capacity/scalability engineering, and production operations. · Automation & Delivery: Experience with CI/CD, infrastructure and configuration automation, Git-based development, automated testing, and repeatable deployment/release practices. · Technical Leadership: Demonstrated ability to influence technical direction, make sound engineering trade-offs, mentor engineers, and build alignment across globally distributed teams without relying on formal reporting authority. · Communication: Strong written and verbal communication with the ability to clearly explain technical decisions, alternatives, risks, and trade-offs to engineering and non-engineering stakeholders. Basic Qualifications · Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent practical experience. • 10–15 years of relevant software engineering experience. • Significant experience designing and delivering production-grade platforms or distributed systems used by multiple applications, teams, or customers. • Demonstrated ability to solve complex engineering problems spanning software, infrastructure, cloud, networking, security, and operational concerns. • Experience designing systems for scale, robustness, reliability, resilience, performance, security, maintainability, and operational supportability. • Experience working across cloud, on-premises, hybrid, or distributed environments and integrating with dependent applications and external systems. • Demonstrated technical leadership across multiple engineers, components, or teams. • Strong hands-on engineering background with the ability to remain technically engaged while providing broader architectural leadership. Desirable Experience · Deep Kubernetes experience across cluster lifecycle, workloads, networking, storage, resource management, scaling, and troubleshooting. • Experience with GCP/GKE or equivalent services on AWS/Azure, including identity, networking, storage, messaging, secrets, artifact management, and observability. • Strong Go development experience. • Experience with OpenTelemetry, Grafana, distributed tracing, metrics, structured logging, and alerting. • Platform engineering, developer platforms, control planes, or infrastructure-as-a-product experience. • Experience with hybrid/cloud-to-edge platforms and intermittently connected environments. • Security architecture experience including workload identity, certificates, secrets, authentication, and authorization. • Performance, scalability, and capacity engineering experience. • Experience with service mesh, distributed databases, configuration management, and infrastructure automation. • Experience providing technical leadership across geographically distributed engineering teams, including cross-site design collaboration, mentoring, and knowledge sharing. • Familiarity with AI-assisted engineering and emerging AI technologies relevant to platform capability or developer productivity. • Experience with retail, POS/SCO, or distributed store technology is beneficial.
