Full Stack Lead/Architect - Golang/React.js/Next.js (India) Company: Kiash Soluitons Job Location: India Employment Type: Full-time Experience Required: 8 - 10 years Role Overview: We are seeking a seasoned Full Stack Lead/Architect to spearhead the design and development of our next-generation digital platforms. In this role, you will bridge the gap between high-level architectural strategy and hands-on implementation, working closely with product managers, design teams, and cross-functional engineering squads to deliver robust, scalable solutions. You will be responsible for defining the technical roadmap, ensuring code excellence, and mentoring a high-performing team to build seamless user experiences. Your contributions will directly influence our products performance, reliability, and ability to scale, ultimately driving significant value for our global user base in a fully remote setting. Key Responsibilities: Architect and oversee the development of high-concurrency backend systems using Golang to ensure optimal performance and system stability for our end users. Lead the end-to-end development of responsive, performant frontend interfaces using React.js and Next.js to provide a superior and intuitive user experience. Establish and enforce engineering best practices, including code reviews, automated testing, and CI/CD pipelines, to maintain high standards of code quality across the development lifecycle. Collaborate with stakeholders to translate complex business requirements into technical specifications, ensuring that the product roadmap aligns with long-term business goals. Mentor junior and mid-level developers by providing technical guidance and fostering a culture of continuous learning and innovation within the engineering team. Required Skillset: Demonstrated expertise in building scalable backend services with Golang and crafting modern, server-side rendered web applications using React.js and Next.js. Proven ability to design complex system architectures that are modular, maintainable, and capable of handling high traffic volumes. Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders and lead collaborative discussions across distributed teams. Exceptional problem-solving capabilities and a proactive mindset, with the ability to thrive in a fully remote work environment while managing multiple project priorities. A solid foundation in computer science fundamentals, typically evidenced by a degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, combined with 8 - 10 years of professional software development experience.
