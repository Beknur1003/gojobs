At a Glance: We are looking for a hands-on Engineering Manager to lead our China Digital Tech team (~6–7 engineers) in Shanghai, focusing primarily on our WeChat Mini Program and China digital commerce ecosystem. This role is designed for a strong technical leader who combines practical people management with deep, hands-on engineering craft—someone who loves guiding engineers through architecture, system design, code standards, testing, and AI-native engineering practices while delivering exceptional digital experiences for our customers in China. Based in Shanghai, you will partner closely with our Product Manager—who drives product scoping, prioritization, and business stakeholder alignment—so you can focus on technical strategy, engineering execution, system quality, and growing a high-performing engineering team. You will also work closely with the Global Tech Commercial Extended Leadership team as part of our China Tech Hub, ensuring our local digital solutions are built with global engineering standards, clean architecture, and modern engineering practices. Your Mission As an Engineering Manager for China Digital Tech, you will lead and coach a team of 6–7 engineers building and scaling On’s China digital touchpoints, centered around our WeChat Mini Program ecosystem. You will balance hands-on technical guidance, architecture and delivery ownership, and people leadership to help the team ship reliable, high-craft digital products at speed. You will: Lead and Grow a High-Performing Engineering Team • Lead, coach, and support a team of 6–7 software engineers delivering across the China Digital Tech domain. • Foster a culture of high ownership, psychological safety, continuous learning, and engineering craftsmanship. • Provide regular 1:1s, actionable feedback, career development guidance, and performance management. • Balance hands-on technical involvement with empowering engineers to own designs, decisions, and delivery end-to-end. • Build a healthy, sustainable delivery rhythm through clear sprint goals, effective agile ceremonies, and continuous improvement. Stay Hands-On with Code, Architecture, and Engineering Craft • Guide the team with best practices across code standards, clean architecture, system design, automated testing, and CI/CD. • Stay close to the codebase and system details through architecture and design reviews, code reviews, prototyping, debugging, and critical technical decisions. • Ensure our WeChat Mini Program and supporting backend/BFF services are modular, performant, secure, observable, and maintainable. • Set clear expectations around automated testing (unit, integration, and end-to-end testing), release quality, and production readiness. • Help engineers make pragmatic technical trade-offs that balance delivery speed with long-term system health. This is a role for a hands-on manager who leads from the front on engineering quality rather than managing purely from spreadsheets. Drive China Digital & WeChat Mini Program Excellence • Own the technical evolution and delivery of On’s WeChat Mini Program and wider China digital consumer experiences. • Design and evolve clean integrations between China front-end touchpoints (WeChat Mini Program, WeCom, local payment/social/CRM ecosystems) and On’s regional and global commerce, order, inventory, and customer platforms. • Drive high standards for frontend and backend performance,用户体验 (UX) responsiveness, peak-traffic readiness (campaigns, drops, key shopping festivals), and operational stability. • Establish strong production operations, including monitoring, alerting, SLOs, incident response, and post-incident learning. • Translate local digital innovation into clean, reusable architectural patterns rather than brittle one-off workarounds. Partner Closely with Product Management • Work hand-in-hand with the Product Manager—who leads scoping, prioritization, and stakeholder management—to turn product vision and priorities into clear technical plans and reliable delivery. • Co-shape solutions early by bringing engineering feasibility, architectural options, and effort/risk trade-offs into product discovery and scoping discussions. • Challenge assumptions constructively using data, technical insight, and customer context so the team solves the right problems in the simplest, most effective way. • Provide transparent delivery forecasting, surface technical risks early, and align on realistic milestones and trade-offs. • Connect technical investments (refactoring, testing, observability, performance) to tangible product and business outcomes. You should partner with Product so seamlessly that scoping, prioritization, and technical execution reinforce each other. We are not looking for AI hype. We are looking for a leader who actively coaches engineers to use AI thoughtfully in everyday software delivery. Collaborate Across the China Tech Hub and Global Tech • Collaborate with platform, architecture, security, and engineering teams across Shanghai and global hubs to align on shared APIs, service boundaries, and engineering standards. • Ensure China Digital Tech solutions integrate cleanly with global platforms while respecting local ecosystem realities. • Contribute to hiring, onboarding, and raising the overall engineering bar of our growing Shanghai Tech Hub. Your Story You are a hands-on engineering leader who cares deeply about software craftsmanship, system design, and helping engineers do the best work of their careers. You have solid experience leading an engineering team (you do not need a decade of management experience, but you have moved past being a first-time manager and know how to run and coach a team effectively) while staying deeply connected to the technology. You bring: • 2+ years of hands-on Engineering Management or Tech Lead Manager experience leading agile software engineering teams (ideally 5–8+ engineers), on top of a strong foundation as a senior software engineer. • Deep technical experience in China’s digital ecosystem, particularly building, scaling, and operating WeChat Mini Programs and consumer-facing digital commerce or retail applications. • Strong full-stack or backend/BFF architectural foundation, with hands-on proficiency in modern web/mini-program technologies (TypeScript / JavaScript, WeChat Mini Program frameworks) and backend services (Node.js / NestJS, Golang, or Java). • Proven track record of guiding engineers on engineering best practices, including clean code standards, modular architecture, API and system design, automated testing strategies, code reviews, and CI/CD pipelines. • Practical experience with AI-assisted software engineering, actively using and coaching teams on AI coding assistants, automated test generation, and AI-driven developer productivity workflows. • Strong product collaboration mindset, with demonstrated success partnering closely with Product Managers on technical scoping, effort estimation, trade-off decisions, and iterative delivery. • Solid understanding of distributed systems and production operations, including API design, caching, performance optimization, high-concurrency campaign readiness, observability, and incident management. • Clear, empathetic leadership style—able to coach junior-to-senior engineers, give constructive feedback, and build an accountable, collaborative team culture. • Excellent communication skills in both English and Mandarin, with the ability to collaborate effectively across local product/business partners and global engineering teams.
