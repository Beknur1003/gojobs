We are looking for the best About Us 42dot is a mobility AI company committed to solving mobility challenges with software and AI. As the Global Software Center of Hyundai Motor Group, 42dot pioneers the future of mobility by advancing the development of software-defined vehicles. We develop safety-first, user-centric software-defined vehicle technologies that deliver the latest performance through continuous updates like smartphones. By advancing software and AI technology, 42dot envisions a world where everything is connected and moves autonomously through a self-managing urban transportation operating system. About the Role Develop driving and physical AI foundation models. Support Aria VLA model development. Responsibilities • Data Curation & Pretraining: Design and manage massive, multi-terabyte datasets, including data scraping, filtering, deduplication, tokenization, and synthetic data generation. • Distributed Model Training: Scale training across massive GPU/TPU clusters using parallel processing, custom data loaders, and framework optimization (PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow). • Post-Training & Alignment: Implement instruction tuning, Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), or direct preference optimization to align models with safety and behavior goals. • Inference & Optimization: Compress and optimize models (quantization, pruning, speculative decoding) to run at low latency on cloud infrastructure or edge hardware like smartphones and robotics. • Evaluation & Guardrails: Build benchmarking harnesses, hallucination checkers, and red-teaming pipelines to test model accuracy and safety. Qualifications • Languages: Python, C++, and Golang (for high-throughput systems). • Frameworks: PyTorch, JAX, Hugging Face ecosystem, vLLM. • Infrastructure: Kubernetes, Docker, multi-node distributed training (Megatron-LM, DeepSpeed), and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud). • Math & Theory: Linear algebra, calculus, probability, statistics, and deep learning architectures (Transformers, diffusion models).
