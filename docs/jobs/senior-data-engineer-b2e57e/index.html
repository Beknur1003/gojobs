Role Overview: We are looking for a strong, hands-on, highly autonomous Senior Data Engineer to design, build, and operate our enterprise People Function Capability Layer. You will be instrumental in delivering a secure, reliable, scalable, and cost-efficient service layer that integrates multiple systems across the People ecosystem. You will play a critical role in ensuring operational excellence, data integrity, and business continuity in a cloud-native, event-driven environment. We are seeking someone with deep expertise in data engineering, data modelling, and distributed system design, with a focus on Golang, Python, AWS Cloud, and Kafka. The candidate will contribute to the design, development, deployment, and ongoing evolution of our integration and data platforms. Key responsibilities: • Build and lead the development of high-performance, event-driven integration pipelines, applying strong debugging and performance optimization skills to ensure resilient data flows. • Define and enforce technical standards, design patterns, and engineering best practices across development, security and data governance. • Design and enforce canonical data models and schemas to standardize complex organizational data domains across systems. • Build and manage CI/CD pipelines to enable reliable, repeatable, and automated software delivery. • Drive observability and monitoring using tools such as Datadog and Grafana to ensure system reliability and performance. • Collaborate with distributed engineering teams and cross-functional stakeholders to translate business requirements into scalable data solutions delivered within agreed timelines. • Design, provision, and manage AWS infrastructure to ensure high availability, scalability, and low-latency processing for data workloads. Must Have Skills: • Strong hands-on experience in Golang and Python for backend and data engineering use cases. • Strong hands-on experience with AWS cloud services – AWS Lambda, SQS, SNS, EC2, S3, RDS, API Gateway, VPC, EKS, ECS, IAM, CloudWatch and cloud-native architecture patterns. • Strong experience with relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MS SQL) and NoSQL stores (e.g., DynamoDB). • Strong hands-on experience with message brokers and event-driven architectures (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ). • Hands-on experience designing and implementing RESTful APIs and data services. • Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI). • Experience building highly available, distributed systems on AWS, including containerization using Docker. • Hands on experience building highly available distributed systems in AWS and containerization using Docker. • Experience with observability platforms such as Datadog, Prometheus, or equivalent. • Strong understanding of cloud security best practices including IAM, encryption, secrets management, vulnerability scanning, and compliance controls. Qualifications: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 5+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 3+ years focused on data engineering or distributed data platforms. • 3+ years of experience working with Golang, Python, AWS, and Kafka in production environments.
