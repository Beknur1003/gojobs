This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Backend Developer (Golang) | Payment Systems based in Brazil. We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Developer to take technical ownership of payment systems and drive the development of scalable, reliable backend solutions. In this role, you will work primarily with Go, building and evolving payment services, integrating gateways, acquirers, and payment service providers (PSPs). You will play a key role in designing payment orchestration solutions, ensuring transaction reliability, consistency, and traceability. Working closely with engineering and business teams, you will contribute to architectural decisions and translate business needs into effective technical solutions. You will also help establish engineering best practices through code reviews, documentation, and mentoring. This is an opportunity to make a significant technical impact in a fast-paced, performance-driven, and globally oriented environment. Accountabilities: • Develop, maintain, and scale backend services in Go, focusing on payment processing systems. • Design, build, and maintain integrations with payment gateways, acquirers, and PSPs. • Ensure transaction reliability, consistency, and traceability through idempotency, failure handling, event processing, and transaction reprocessing. • Develop and maintain services and integrations using Node.js and TypeScript. • Work with AWS services to build and operate scalable, reliable backend infrastructure. • Contribute to technical design and architectural decisions, particularly around payment orchestration and system scalability. • Participate in in-depth code reviews, document technical decisions, and mentor junior developers. • Translate business requirements into robust technical solutions, communicating trade-offs and recommendations clearly. • Take ownership of technical initiatives, proactively identifying challenges and driving solutions from conception to delivery. • Within the first 30 days, understand the existing architecture, payment services, and provider integrations while contributing to the payment orchestrator. • Within 60 days, independently deliver key improvements to the payment orchestrator and actively participate in architectural decisions. • Within 90 days, become a technical reference for payment systems within the team, delivering measurable improvements in reliability, traceability, and code quality. Requirements: • More than 3 years of professional backend development experience, with strong hands-on experience using Go in production environments. • Solid experience with Node.js and TypeScript. • Proven experience developing and maintaining payment processing systems. • Strong understanding of the complete payment transaction lifecycle, including authorization, capture, void, refunds, chargebacks, and settlement. • Good knowledge of the payment ecosystem, including gateways, acquirers, PSPs, sub-acquirers, card networks, issuers, BINs, and MIDs. • Experience handling soft and hard declines, payment retries, and routing transactions between multiple providers. • Familiarity with 3DS/3DS2, card tokenization, and PCI DSS requirements. • Experience with recurring payments, subscription billing, and financial reconciliation. • Strong understanding of event-driven architectures, including queues, idempotency, at-least-once delivery, and event reprocessing. • Experience designing and integrating REST APIs and webhooks. • Practical knowledge of AWS services, particularly ECS, DynamoDB, SQS, EventBridge, Secrets Manager, and CloudWatch, as well as basic IAM concepts. • Hands-on experience with Docker in development and production environments. • Good understanding of automated testing practices and observability, including logs, metrics, and distributed tracing. • Intermediate to advanced English proficiency. • Strong ownership mindset, autonomy, and a proactive approach to identifying problems and delivering solutions. • Clear written communication skills, particularly when documenting technical decisions, explaining trade-offs, and contributing to code reviews. • Ability and willingness to mentor teammates, share knowledge, and contribute to raising the team's technical standards. Nice to have: • Experience with payment orchestration platforms and integrations involving multiple payment providers. • Experience implementing card vault solutions and third-party tokenization services. • Knowledge of chargeback prevention strategies and fraud detection systems. • Experience with international payment processing and multi-currency transactions. Benefits: • Work Model: 100% remote, offering flexibility to work from anywhere in Brazil. • Contract Type: Independent Contractor (PJ). • Compensation: To be discussed based on experience and qualifications. • Working Arrangement: Full-time engagement. • Professional Growth: Opportunity to take technical ownership of critical payment infrastructure and influence architectural decisions. • Technical Impact: Work on complex payment systems, integrations, and orchestration solutions supporting global operations.
