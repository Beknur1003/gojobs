We are looking for an experienced SDE 3 - Backend Engineer to join our product engineering team. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in Java or Golang, hands-on experience with AWS or GCP, and excellent knowledge of Data Structures and Algorithms. As an SDE 3 you will take ownership of designing and developing scalable backend systems, contribute to architecture and system design decisions, and solve complex technical problems. Responsibilities: • Design and develop scalable, highly available, and reliable backend services. • Take ownership of critical product modules and deliver high-quality, production-ready solutions. • Work extensively with Java or Golang for backend development. • Design and implement solutions on AWS or GCP. • Apply strong knowledge of Data Structures and Algorithms to solve complex engineering problems. • Participate in System Design and Architecture discussions. • Drive technical decisions and contribute to engineering best practices. • Review code and provide technical guidance to other engineers. • Identify and resolve performance, scalability, and reliability issues. • Collaborate with Product, QA, DevOps, and other engineering teams. • Take ownership of production issues, debugging, monitoring, and continuous system improvements. Requirements: • 5-9 years of software development experience. • Strong hands-on experience with Java OR Golang. • Hands-on experience with AWS OR GCP. • Strong understanding of Data Structures and Algorithms. • Strong knowledge of Object-Oriented Programming and design principles. • Experience building scalable and distributed systems. • Strong understanding of System Design. • Experience developing and consuming REST APIs / backend services. • Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills. • Experience working in a product-based technology environment.
