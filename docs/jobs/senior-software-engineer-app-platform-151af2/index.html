Applied Intuition, Inc. is powering the future of physical AI. Founded in 2017 and now valued at $15 billion, the Silicon Valley company is creating the digital infrastructure needed to bring intelligence to every moving machine on the planet. Applied Intuition services the automotive, defense, trucking, construction, mining and agriculture industries in three core areas: tools and infrastructure, operating systems, and autonomy. Eighteen of the top 20 global automakers, as well as the United States military and its allies, trust the company’s solutions to deliver physical intelligence. Applied Intuition is headquartered in Sunnyvale, California, with offices in Washington, D.C.; San Diego; Ft. Walton Beach, Florida; Ann Arbor, Michigan; London; Stuttgart; Munich; Stockholm; Bangalore; Seoul; and Tokyo. Learn more at . We are an in-office company, and our expectation is that full-time employees primarily work from their Applied Intuition office 5 days a week. However, we also recognize the importance of flexibility and trust our employees to manage their schedules responsibly. This may include occasional remote work, starting the day with morning meetings from home before heading to the office, or leaving earlier when needed to accommodate family commitments. This in-office expectation does not apply to contractor positions About the role • Design, develop, and maintain secure and scalable backend platform infrastructure, abstractions, and tooling that supports application deployment, authentication, and API services. • Build and optimize distributed systems that enable rapid application development and deployment across the organization. • Architect and implement plugin systems and extensibility frameworks to build upon the platform. • Create and maintain cross-platform desktop application infrastructure and deployment pipelines. • Design and implement robust authentication and authorization systems and automated testing strategies, and evaluate, integrate, and maintain authentication solutions. • Engage with external customers to gather feedback on platform capabilities and identify improvement opportunities. • Document platform features, APIs, and best practices for internal and external users. • Implement comprehensive observability solutions including logging, metrics, and distributed tracing. • Monitor platform performance and reliability, identifying and resolving bottlenecks and failures. • Participate in on-call rotations, conduct post-incident reviews, and implement preventive measures to improve system resilience, maintain platform availability, and respond to incidents. We're looking for someone who has: • Must have at least a Master’s Degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or Mechanical Engineering, • At least 1 year of experience in a software engineer role with a technology company • At least 12 months of experience using Python, Golang, TypeScript/JavaScript, SQL, Flask, Node.js/Express.js, PostgreSQL/MySQL, React.js, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS and GCP cloud services, GitHub, OAuth 2.0, and Load Balancing. #LI-DNP Don’t meet every single requirement?
