Senior Backend Engineer

Volumez Tel Aviv District, Israel опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

Гибрид Senior
LinuxPythonJavaC++AI/LLM
About the Role We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer with strong systems programming skills, deep Linux expertise, and a passion for solving complex distributed systems challenges. You'll work across the technology stack, from low-level storage and networking to backend services, high availability, and production infrastructure. You'll take end-to-end ownership of complex technical challenges, leveraging AI coding tools to accelerate development while maintaining high engineering standards. What You'll Do • Storage & Networking: Develop and optimize core storage components and protocols, including NFS, SMB, S3, SPDK, and RDMA, primarily using C and Go. • AI & GPU Infrastructure: Build high-performance data paths for AI workloads, including GPUDirect Storage (GDS), KV-cache offloading, and inference optimization. • Backend & Distributed Systems: Develop backend services using Python, Django, and Celery, and build high-availability and cluster management capabilities. • Infrastructure & Automation: Build and maintain deployment automation across bare-metal and cloud environments using tools such as Ansible and Podman. • Performance & Troubleshooting: Investigate complex production issues, optimize system performance, and improve reliability across Linux, storage, and networking layers. • AI-Assisted Development: Use AI coding assistants as part of your daily development workflow while maintaining code quality and reliability. Requirements • 5+ years of experience in Backend Engineering, Systems Programming, or a similar role. • Solid proficiency in C/C++/Java/Golang/Python • Deep understanding of Linux internals, including memory management, networking, and system-level debugging. • Strong knowledge of distributed systems, concurrency, fault tolerance, and high availability. • Experience working with large, complex codebases and debugging tools such as GDB, strace, and perf. • Experience with infrastructure automation, containerized environments, and troubleshooting complex production issues. • Experience using AI coding assistants in daily development, with strong problem-solving skills and end-to-end ownership. Work Model Hybrid: 4 days from the office, 1 day from home
