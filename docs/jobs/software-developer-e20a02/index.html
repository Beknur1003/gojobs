ARS is seeking an experienced Software Developer to support a secure cloud platform delivery. This role is focused on developing platform-integrated tooling, automation, and user-facing capabilities across multiple classification tiers, enabling mission teams to onboard and operate effectively within Government-furnished cloud enclaves. This is a hands-on development role requiring demonstrated experience in cloud environments, the ability to operate independently from day one, and the discipline to produce thorough documentation in support of formal platform delivery. Must be able to obtain a Secret clearance. Why Work with us? Responsibilities include: Platform Development & Integration • Build automation tooling and scripted workflows supporting dynamic user provisioning, environment configuration, and platform onboarding. • Develop and maintain APIs, interfaces, or internal portal capabilities that enable mission teams to interact with platform services. • Implement software solutions in alignment with enclave-specific security requirements, approved software baselines, and applicable STIGs. • Ensure all developed code is tested, documented, and delivered in accordance with classified environment standards and Government approval processes. • Collaborate with DevOps and ISSE personnel to ensure software integrations are architecturally sound and security-compliant across all enclave boundaries. Documentation & Delivery • Produce engineering-quality development documentation including design specifications, integration guides, and delivery artifacts required for platform acceptance. • Support formal delivery processes including configuration-controlled code submissions and handoff documentation across classification tiers. • Coordinate with DevOps and ISSE team members on issue resolution, integration testing support, and cross-discipline knowledge transfer. • Other duties as assigned. Qualifications/ Technical Experience Requirements: • US citizenship required. • Able to obtain a Secret clearance • Active Security+ certification (DoD 8140 compliant). • 5+ years of demonstrated hands-on software development experience in classified or similarly restrictive environments. • Proficiency in one or more languages, including Golang, Python, Bash, PowerShell, and/or JavaScript/Node. • Experience developing automation, scripting, or tooling in support of infrastructure or platform delivery (not solely application development). • Familiarity with containerization and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes, Podman, etc.). • Experience working with relational databases (e.g. PostgreSQL) in disconnected or controlled environments, including schema design, query optimization, and secure data handling. • Ability to produce clear, structured technical documentation suitable for classified delivery and Government review. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field OR equivalent experience demonstrating the required technical competencies.
