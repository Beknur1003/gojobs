The Principal AI Architect is a senior executive technical authority position responsible for defining, establishing, and governing the enterprise architectural vision for autonomous agentic systems and multi-agent frameworks across Citigroup. The overall objective of this role is to serve as Citi's chief technical authority for autonomous systems and multi-agent frameworks, co-owning enterprise blueprints, review gates, playbooks, and the Product/Platform Loop bridging field implementations with core internal platforms (Arc Platform, R2D2 Gateway, Agentflow, and Helix). Responsibilities: • Serves as the chief technical authority for autonomous systems, multi-agent frameworks, and hierarchical sub-agent architectures across Citi's global technology footprint. • Authors and maintains enterprise reference architectures and blueprints for single-agent, hierarchical sub-agent, and Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) workflows, ensuring strict adherence across all delivery pods. • Acts as the primary technical bridge in the Product/Platform Feedback Loop with the Arc Core, R2D2 Gateway, and Agentflow platform teams—translating field friction from LOB deployments into core platform features. • Chairs the Agent Architecture Review Board (AARB) to conduct mandatory technical, architectural, and security reviews for all high-risk AI agents prior to UAT and Production promotion. • Defines enterprise standards for Model Context Protocol (MCP) server integration, zero-trust boundary enforcement via Tiramisu proxies, and COIN M2M identity token delegation. • Establishes horizontal governance, code quality, testability, and automated evaluation standards across all Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) pods. • Advises senior business and technology leadership on emerging AI paradigms, non-deterministic system validation, and foundation model capabilities. • Appropriately assess risk when business decisions are made, demonstrating particular consideration for the firm's reputation and safeguarding Citigroup, its clients and assets, by driving compliance with applicable laws, rules and regulations, adhering to Policy, applying sound ethical judgment regarding personal behavior, conduct and business practices, and escalating, managing and reporting control issues with transparency, as well as effectively supervise the activity of others and create accountability with those who fail to maintain these standards. Qualifications: • 10+ years of progressive software architecture and engineering experience in enterprise, distributed systems, or financial technology environments. • 3+ years of hands-on experience architecting and deploying production LLM-powered applications, multi-agent systems, and autonomous agentic workflows (e.g., LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, Agent Development Kit / ADK). • Mastery of Python and/or Golang, OpenShift/Kubernetes, distributed microservices, OAuth2/JWT token delegation, API gateway management, and enterprise telemetry (OpenTelemetry, Splunk). • Deep understanding of financial services compliance, Model Risk Management (MRM) standards, and deterministic validation methodologies for non-deterministic AI outputs. • Demonstrated leadership and track record of chairing technical governance bodies and influencing enterprise-level architectural direction. • Consistently demonstrates clear, concise, and persuasive executive-level written and verbal communication.
