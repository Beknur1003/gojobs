The Role This is an exciting opportunity for experienced mid-level Backend Software Engineers to join ABC Digital Product and work on the services and APIs underpinning our digital platforms. We have 2x mid-level Software Engineer opportunities, working primarily with Go / Golang to design and develop high-performance APIs, services and reusable backend components across complex ABC digital environments. The roles also involve cloud infrastructure and maintaining and enhancing existing PHP-based systems. Key Accountabilities • Develop and maintain high-performance, secure and accessible APIs and services across ABC platforms • Contribute extensively to the analysis, design and development of complex, reusable backend components and functionality • Build enterprise-level server-side applications and services using Go / Golang • Design and develop highly available services using cloud infrastructure • Maintain and enhance existing PHP-based systems while contributing to newer backend services • Apply modern frameworks, build tooling and automated testing practices across backend development • Contribute to strong version control, branching, merging and software versioning practices • Maintain detailed technical and project documentation for ongoing reference • Keep up to date with emerging server-side technologies and best practice, particularly around performance and security • Deliver high-quality software within agreed timeframes and work collaboratively across engineering and product teams About You You’ll bring: • Strong Go / Golang experience and solid server-side software engineering capability • Experience developing complex, secure and high-performance backend software • Experience designing and developing APIs and backend services • Experience working with cloud-based infrastructure and highly available systems • Strong knowledge of modern frameworks and development tooling • Experience with automated and unit testing • Experience with version control, branching, merging and versioning strategies • Capability and willingness to work with PHP to maintain and enhance existing services • Strong communication, collaboration and problem-solving skills • Good organisational skills and the ability to deliver work within agreed timeframes • Knowledge of publishing, media delivery or content workflows would be highly regarded • Relevant tertiary qualifications or equivalent professional experience What’s in it for you? Come and join the vibrant Digital Product department in the ABC. Our mission is to enable ABC’s digital future, where ABC transitions from a traditional broadcaster to Australia’s most trusted and valued digital media organisation. Through harnessing our talents in digital, collaborating with our partners and delivering beyond expectations, we ensure the ABC not just remains relevant but thrives. As a group of product and platform teams, we develop and maintain the ABC's audience-facing digital products across mobile, connected TV, desktop and smart speakers. Whether it is staying informed of the latest breaking news, bringing some great TV on ABC iview, streaming the Hottest 100 on the triple j app and website or entertaining the kids with our ABC Kids app, the ABC's Digital Product offerings are some of Australia's most loved and most used platforms. You’ll work on backend technology supporting digital services at genuine scale while collaborating with experienced engineers and multidisciplinary product teams. You can get a sneak peek into our product engineering world through our blog: ABC Digital Product For further information on these positions please contact Jamie Nedved, Digital Talent Acquisition Lead via email nedved.jamie@abc.net.au We respectfully request that Recruitment Agencies do not submit applications for these positions. In line with our focus on diversity, applications are strongly encouraged from Indigenous Australians, people from a range of cultural and linguistic backgrounds, people with disability and LGBTIQA+ individuals.
