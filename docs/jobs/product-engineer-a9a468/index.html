Product Engineer

MainStory Daycare & HomeCare Pintar Jakarta опубликовано 11 ноября 2026

Гибрид Полная занятость
Node.jsAI/LLM
Salary: Rp 10.000.000 – Rp 15.000.000 per month We’re hiring a Product Engineer to build the technology behind childcare in Indonesia. You’ll take products from rough idea to shipped feature, working across product, design, and engineering to create experiences that are intuitive, useful, and impactful. What you’ll do: • Build and ship end-to-end features, backend and frontend, from ideation to production. • Take ambiguous problems and translate them into clear product solutions and technical implementations, including designing the APIs, data models, and system architecture underneath them. • Create simple, intuitive user experiences with strong UX fundamentals, and collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to bring ideas to life. • You’ll make thoughtful trade-offs between speed, quality, and scalability, use AI tools (Lovable, Copilot, etc.) to prototype and build faster, and keep iterating based on feedback, data, and product insights. What we’re looking for: • 2–5 years of experience in software engineering, product engineering, or a similar role. • Strong fullstack skills (Node.js, Golang, or a similar backend, plus modern frontend frameworks) and a solid grasp of API design, system architecture, and database modeling. • You think beyond implementation to user experience and product impact. • You’re comfortable with ambiguity and making decisions independently, and familiar with AI-assisted development workflows (prompting, iteration, tooling). • Collaborative, curious, and proactive. Bonus points: • You’ve built 0 → 1 products. • You have a background in product design or strong UX intuition. • You’ve worked hands-on with AI-native tools like Lovable or Cursor. • You’ve thrived in a fast-paced startup environment. Why join us: • High ownership, so you shape both what we build and how we build it. • Real product problems end-to-end, on a team that embraces AI-native product development. • A fast-paced, collaborative culture with strong learning growth, plus a flexible work setup, competitive compensation, and meaningful impact. About MainStory: MainStory Indonesia is a Daycare & HomeCare Pintar provider building the technology infrastructure for the future of childcare in Indonesia. We believe early childhood development is one of the most important and most underserved problems in this country, and technology is the highest-leverage way to solve it
