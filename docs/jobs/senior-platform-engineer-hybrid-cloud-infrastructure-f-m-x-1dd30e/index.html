Job Description: About the job Deutsche Bank Technology in Berlin DB Technology is a global team of tech specialists, spread across multiple trading hubs and tech centres. We have a strong focus on promoting technical excellence – our engineers work at the forefront of financial services innovation using cutting-edge technologies. Our Berlin location is our most recent addition to our global network of tech centres and growing strongly. We are committed to building a diverse workforce and to creating excellent opportunities for talented engineers and technologists. Our tech teams and business units use agile ways of working to create #GlobalHausbank solutions from our home market. In the Hybrid Cloud Infrastructure organization, we build and operate the platforms, services, and tooling that serve as the gateway to cloud for engineering teams across the enterprise. Our mission is to deliver a secure, scalable, and self-service developer platform that enables teams to build, deploy, and operate applications with speed and confidence. By focusing on developer productivity, developer experience, automation, and AI-enabled capabilities, we empower engineers to spend less time managing infrastructure and more time delivering business value. Through standardized cloud services, platform engineering practices, and seamless access to public and private cloud environments, we accelerate the adoption of cloud technologies at scale. As a Senior Platform Engineer (f/m/x), you will design, build, and operate scalable platform services, cloud-native applications, and developer platform capabilities that enable engineering teams to deliver software efficiently, securely, and at scale. Leveraging your expertise in Golang, distributed systems, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Kubernetes, and modern AI-driven engineering practices, you will help shape the next generation of platform solutions and developer experiences across the organization. -> You love this job but feel you cannot tick 100% of the boxes? Send us your CV anyway! Your key responsibilities • Design, develop, and operate cloud-native platform services and distributed systems using Go/Golang, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), ensuring scalability, reliability, and operational excellence. • Build and enhance platform automation, self-service workflows, APIs, and developer platform capabilities to streamline software delivery and improve developer productivity. • Design and implement observability, telemetry, and reliability solutions, leveraging monitoring, logging, tracing, and SRE practices to ensure platform health and performance. • Drive adoption of modern cloud-native technologies, Kubernetes patterns, GitOps practices, and software engineering best practices across the organization. • Mentor engineers through technical leadership, code reviews, design reviews, and knowledge sharing, fostering a culture of engineering excellence and continuous improvement. Your skills and experiences • Strong software engineering experience with proficiency in a modern programming language. Professional experience with Golang is preferred • Proven experience designing and operating distributed systems at scale. • Strong hands-on experience with Public Cloud (preferably GCP), • Experience with cloud-native architectures, microservices, APIs, and observability. • Strong engineering mindset with a focus on quality, automation, and operational excellence. • Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs.
