SSH Communications Security is a global cybersecurity company specializing in securing access and communications between systems, applications, and people. Trusted by more than 5,000 organizations worldwide, including many Fortune 500 companies, we help businesses protect their critical digital assets and prepare for the security challenges of tomorrow. We're looking for a motivated and collaborative Software Engineer to join our PrivX team, developing our privileged access management (PAM) solution. You'll work with colleagues across engineering, product management and quality assurance to build reliable software that helps organisations manage and secure privileged access. In this role, you'll contribute throughout the software development lifecycle, from exploring requirements and designing solutions to implementation, testing and ongoing improvement. What you'll be doing • Design, develop and maintain software features and components for PrivX. • Write clear, efficient and maintainable Go code, following agreed engineering standards and secure coding practices. • Contribute to technical design discussions and help turn product requirements into practical software solutions. • Test and debug software, investigate issues and improve product reliability and performance. • Develop and support build, integration and automated testing workflows as part of CI/CD. • Review code and share knowledge with colleagues to support high-quality engineering across the team. • Work with product management, quality assurance and architects to deliver product improvements. • Monitor and update software dependencies, document technical changes and help improve development tools and practices. What we're looking for • Experience developing software professionally, or equivalent practical experience in software engineering. • Proficiency in Go (Golang) and experience writing maintainable, well-tested code. • A solid understanding of continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD), including automated builds and testing. • Understanding of software design principles, APIs, testing and version control with Git. • Ability to investigate technical problems, identify practical solutions and communicate your reasoning clearly. • Understanding of secure coding practices and an interest in cybersecurity and privileged access management. • A collaborative approach and the ability to work effectively with colleagues across different disciplines. • Attention to detail and a commitment to producing reliable, high-quality software. • Good written and spoken English. Experience with Linux, containerised or virtualised environments, cloud platforms or security software would be an advantage. Interested? Apply now and help us build software that protects privileged access.
