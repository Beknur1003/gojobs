Preferred qualifications • Experience working on backend/platform systems at scale. • Exposure to Python, SQL, data pipelines, workflow systems, or automation platforms. • Prior experience in high-growth product companies or startup-like environments. • Interest in AI platform infrastructure, agentic workflows, eval frameworks, or model-quality systems. • Exposure to enterprise SaaS, fintech, HR tech, payroll, benefits, compliance, IT, or workflow-heavy platforms.
