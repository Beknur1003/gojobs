SOFTWARE ENGINEER STACKIT CLOUD (BACKUP AND RESTORE) (m/f/d)

Schwarz Group опубликовано 26 сентября 2026

gRPCKubernetesDockerPythonRustC++AI/LLM
Schwarz Digits creates the technological foundation for digital sovereignty in Europe. As the IT and digital division of the Schwarz Group, we develop and manage the IT infrastructures for the retail divisions Lidl and Kaufland, as well as Schwarz Production and PreZero. At the same time, we operate as an independent provider in the external market to support companies across Europe in their digital transformation. We bundle our core services in the areas of Cloud, Cyber Security, Data & AI, Communication, and Workspace. Join us and contribute to digital sovereignty in Europe. With us, you will work at the intersection of agility and security: You will benefit from fast decision-making processes, enjoy genuine creative freedom in your projects, and be able to build upon the stable foundation of the Schwarz Group. The impact You will create: • You drive the design and development of the Backup and Restore service for our STACKIT Distributed Cloud offering. • You work on a digital-sovereign European cloud provider, following the highest security, scalability and reliability standards. • You help deliver a scalable, secure, and reliable product to our customers. • You continuously automate and optimize all technical processes. • You conduct problem and error analyses and identify sustainable solutions. • Being part of NeoNephos organization: the largest EU open source community for cloud computing, you actively use and participate in the open source project that shape the EU digital sovereignty Experience and skills You will need: • You are an experienced developer with expertise in Golang or similar such as Rust, C/C++, Python, etc. • You have proficiency in developing and deploying software with Kubernetes, Docker, and Helm • You have an understanding of distributed application development • You possess knowledge of the Cloud Native landscape, specifically components within the CNCF ecosystem. • You are skilled in interface and communication technologies such as REST, gRPC, etc. • You are a hands-on problem solver with a solid understanding of software design and architectural patterns. • Experience in developing Kubernetes operators is considered a plus • Knowledge of Ceph, and familiarity with Talos, Cilium, and CoreDNS are considered a plus.
