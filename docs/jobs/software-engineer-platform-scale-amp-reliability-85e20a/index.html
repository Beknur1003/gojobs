Join a team of fintech heroes! 🚀 Performativ is changing the way investment advisors and financial institutions operate. We are building a modern platform for portfolio management, reporting, compliance, trading, and client engagement, replacing fragmented systems, manual workflows, and technology built for another era. Our customers include wealth managers, asset managers, family offices, and financial institutions across Europe and beyond. As we serve larger enterprise customers, the expectations placed on our platform increase significantly. Performance, reliability, security, observability, and predictable operation become essential parts of the product. You can read more about our product at . As our new Software Engineer, Platform Scale & Reliability 📈 You will help strengthen the technical foundations that allow Performativ to serve more customers, process more data, and support increasingly demanding enterprise use cases. This is an all-round engineering role rather than a narrowly defined infrastructure position. You will work across services, data flows, APIs, background processing, databases, cloud infrastructure, and operational tooling wherever improvements are needed. • You will identify and resolve performance, scalability, and reliability limitations across the platform, using production evidence to focus effort where it has the greatest impact. • You will improve observability through useful metrics, logs, traces, dashboards, and alerts that help the team understand system behaviour and diagnose problems quickly. • You will strengthen services, data processing, deployment workflows, and operational safeguards so failures are detected early, contained, and permanently resolved. • You will contribute across our technology landscape, including Python, Golang, PHP, TypeScript, MySQL, Redis, AWS, containers, and CI/CD tooling, without being limited to one language or system. • You will direct AI agents to produce much of the implementation, critically review their decisions and output, and establish the tests and operational evidence needed for safe deployment. You should bring broad software-engineering experience and a practical understanding of how production systems behave under real usage. Experience with distributed systems, performance analysis, database optimisation, cloud infrastructure, observability, or high-volume data processing would be valuable. Expertise in our exact technology stack is less important than your ability to understand systems, investigate evidence, and deliver sound solutions. We value your passion 🔥 At Performativ, we are builders first. We have ambitious goals and value the time, judgment, and effort people put into their work. You will be trusted to make your mark and help shape how we solve difficult problems. We do not divide the platform into personal territories. People develop deeper context in different areas, but we solve problems as a team. We expect people to lead when appropriate, follow through on what they take on, contribute to the work of others, and help teammates succeed. AI increases our delivery speed, but it does not reduce our engineering standard or our accountability for the result. You must be able to recognise weak designs, incorrect assumptions, missing failure modes, and unsafe production behaviour. This role is remote from the Philippines and requires working hours that substantially overlap with the team’s working day in Copenhagen. What we can offer you 🌱 • The opportunity to influence the foundations of a financial platform as it moves into enterprise scale. • Broad engineering challenges across applications, data, infrastructure, reliability, and developer tooling. • An AI-native development environment where engineering judgment matters more than manually producing code. • An ambitious, collaborative team with colleagues in Copenhagen and the Philippines. • Strong opportunities to develop your technical breadth and contribute across the platform.
