How You’ll Make an Impact: We are looking for a highly motivated and experienced Senior Fullstack Software Developer to join our development team at HCSS. In this role, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and implementing scalable and robust Cloud-Native software solutions, primarily using Golang, to meet the complex requirements of our projects. What Will You Be Doing? Design, develop, and deploy Golang-based microservices on Cloud providers, following best practices for scalability, performance, and security. Develop responsive and user-friendly frontend applications using the React framework, ensuring a seamless and engaging user experience. Set up and maintain Azure DevOps (ADO) pipelines for automated build, test, and deployment processes, including infrastructure as code. Integrate third-party APIs and services to enhance the functionality of our applications. Mentor junior developers and provide technical guidance and support to ensure the successful delivery of projects. Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies and industry trends, and incorporate them into our development processes as needed. Requirements: Advanced English skills, both written and verbal, to effectively communicate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders. Proven experience as a Senior Fullstack Developer, with strong expertise in Golang microservices and React or Angular frontend development. Strong proficiency in the Golang programming language and experience with frameworks or libraries for building microservices. Hands-on experience with the React framework and proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript/TypeScript. In-depth knowledge of Cloud services, particularly Azure, and experience with deploying and managing applications on cloud infrastructure. Solid understanding of microservices architecture, RESTful APIs, and asynchronous messaging patterns. Strong experience with Azure DevOps (ADO) for setting up build pipelines and infrastructure as code. Experience with containerization technologies such as Docker and container orchestration platforms like Kubernetes. Familiarity with DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines. Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues. Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively in a cross-functional team environment. Bonus: Experience with Ruby would be a plus!
