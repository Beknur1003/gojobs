Platform Engineer Join us and build a streaming platform used by millions. At Sky Czech Republic, we’re building the tech backbone that powers some of the world’s biggest streaming services. Ever heard of Peacock in the U.S., or Sky Showtime in the CzechRepublic? They all run on our global streaming platform—a kind of technological skeleton where each service plugs in its own content and branding. Our platform serves millions of users worldwide. Just to giveyou an idea—Peacock alone has 40 million users in the U.S. Thousands of engineers globally are shaping this platform, and our Prague tech hub is a key part of that effort. But we don’t just keep the engine running—we push the tech boundaries of what’s possible, alongside teams from Lisbon, London, and New York. Here in Prague, we have teams specializing in frontend development (including mobile, TV, and web), backend development (Java), DevOps & PlatformEngineering, AWS, and data science. What is the plot? We are looking for a Platform Engineer to join the Developer Platform Engineering- Edge Delivery Team and help drive improvements and new features for our multi-CDN services, supporting the Web and API traffic for our Global Streaming Platform. Our department provides the platform infrastructure and capabilities for our development teams. By fostering innovation and maintaining stability, we ensure that our teams can efficiently build and support cutting-edge solutions that meet our customers' needs. You'll be supporting an exciting roadmap of enhancements that span across multiple teams. What role will you play? As a Platform Engineer you’ll be joining our Edge Delivery team which consists of team members based in Prague and London. You will be working with various developers and engineers from other teams located across Prague, the United Kingdom, Lisbon, and the Unites States of America. This role requires a strong technical background in software development in the area of cloud and network infrastructure. Your daily tasks: • Platform Engineering: Architect and develop internal developer platforms, self-service workflows, and developer CLI tools to boost team productivity. • Multi-CDN Management: Design, implement, and manage multi-CDN configurations to ensure optimal content delivery and redundancy. • Automation: Develop automation and scripting using Bash and Python • Leverage modern AI workflows (Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and custom AI tooling) to accelerate problem-solving, delivery, and documentation. • Observability: Configuring monitoring and alerting using Prometheus, Grafana, and Datadog • Cloud & Infrastructure: Develop and automate cloud infrastructure using AWS, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code with Terraform and Pulumi. • CI/CD: Build and improve automated testing, deployment, and delivery of pipelines using GitHub Actions. • Collaboration & Support: Work with developers and SREs and participate in the team’s support and on-call rota. • Security and Compliance: Implement security measures and ensure compliance with industry standards and best practices for CDN and edge services. What skills do you need to play your role well? • Strong practical experience developing production-quality software, test driven development, and the whole software development lifecycle process. Golang and Typscript would be an advantage. • Experience and enthusiasm for using AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor, GitHub Copilot, coding agents, or similar tools. • Understanding of containerization technologies; containers, Kubernetes. • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines with automated testing, ideally using GitHub Actions. • Understanding of infrastructure-as-code tools like Terraform or Pulumi. • Basic understanding of HTTP, DNS, caching, load balancing, and CDN in network intensive applications • Calm and methodical troubleshooting. You can follow a problem wherever it goes through code, infrastructure and distributed systems. How do you land the role? We like to keep our recruitment process simple, transparent, and respectful: • First touch: An open chat with one of our recruiters about your experience, goals, and motivation. • First interview: A conversation with your future manager or teammates about the role and team. • Technical interview: A chance to demonstrate your skills on real-world problems, no trick questions. What can you expect in return? • Global Impact: Work in an international environment on cutting-edge technology that scales globally. • People-First Culture: We care about our people just as much as we care about the stability of our platform. • Performance Bonuses: Earn an annual bonus based on your performance. • Hybrid Work: Enjoy the best of both worlds with a mix of office and home working. • Work-Life Balance: Flexible working hours to help you balance work and life. • 25 days of holidays. • 5 days of on-demand leave (sick days). • 2 days of paid community volunteering leave. • 1 day of paid leave for moving house. • Wellbeing Allowance: 18,000 CZK per year to invest in your personal wellbeing. • Fitness Perks: Get a fully covered Multisport card or a 950 CZK monthly contribution to a Benefit Card. • Meal Allowance: 225 CZK per day to keep you fueled. • Premium Life Insurance: Enjoy peace of mind with our premium life insurance scheme. • Fun Perks: Free tickets to Universal Theme Parks.
