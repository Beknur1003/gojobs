About us GoTyme is a multi-country digital banking group with global ambitions, building simple and trusted money tools that help more people access financial services. We serve 23M+ customers through 2,000+ employees across seven locations: South Africa, the Philippines, Singapore, Hong Kong, Indonesia, India, and Vietnam. GoTymeX is GoTyme’s Technology and Product Development Hub, building the engine behind the business. Our engineers, data scientists, product managers, and architects create and scale the platforms, products, and experiences that power GoTyme across markets. Flexible working arrangements are available for discussion.
