Applications Software Engineer

Electric Era Technologies Seattle, WA опубликовано 20 ноября 2026

CI/CDPythonC++TypeScriptFinTech
Electric Era was founded to actualize the transition to a 100% renewable energy powered world. We are a high tech, high intensity company founded and operated by engineers on a mission to revolutionize the world's electric vehicle charging infrastructure. The Job & Role: We do things differently at Electric Era. If you’ve ever wanted to really own software engineering design decisions and take control of your designs all the way through to implementation we have that here. Everyone, from our junior engineers to our CEO, has a strong ownership mentality. We have a small, lean, hard-charging team; we can’t afford not to have people obsessed with owning engineering outcomes. If you want your stuff to see the light of day fast, we have that here. No miles of red tape or bureaucracy. If it’s important, it happens. If it’s easily messed up, it’s simplified, then given a process. If the process is onerous, it’s deleted. We don’t have time to sit around, so you can guarantee that things move fast. The best idea wins; everyone can and is expected to contribute to good system design, to challenge bad requirements and decision decisions, and always move the puck forward. Electric Era is looking for strong applications software engineers that want everything described above. You will be part of a world-class team that owns the complete product life cycle deliverable from ideation, development, implementation, testing and troubleshooting real problems in the field. The Responsibilities: • Developing application-level software for our charging stations and the software platform we build around them • Leading frontend development of our charger user interfaces, designing intuitive, reliable experiences for both EV drivers and our enterprise customers • Designing, building, and supporting applications across our stack, including but not limited to development in: • Charger user interface (HMI) software that guides drivers through every charging session • Cloud-hosted backend services and APIs that support our charging network • Web applications and dashboards for customers and operators to monitor and manage our charging stations • Integrations between charger applications, backend services, and partner platforms • Telemetry systems to enable remote observability • Communicate directly with customers and partners to learn their requirements, and turn them into well-designed product features and integrations • Develop integrations with customer and third-party systems, such as payment, fleet, and energy management platforms • Maintain and support systems with third-party integrations, including troubleshooting issues alongside customers and partners • Build internal tools that support factory test, deployment, and field operations • Write automated tests and contribute to build systems and test infrastructure for unit and integration testing of application software • Participate in formal code review, test, and acceptance criteria for application software • Manage software deliverables through production and into the field, participating in on-call rotations and supporting your product in a real-world environment Preferred Qualifications & Skills: • 3 to 5+ years of experience in software development, preferably in enterprise application development and management • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field • Demonstrated track record of shipping software products to production and supporting them through their lifecycle • Development experience in C, C++, Python, Golang, or TypeScript • Strong frontend development experience with modern web or UI frameworks, ideally for embedded, kiosk, or touchscreen interfaces • Solid understanding of UX principles for interfaces for enterprise customers and everyday end users such as EV drivers • Experience interfacing and managing third-party APIs, data lake connections, and associated enterprise-grade security • Experience with supporting in-field products, including remote diagnosis of issues in production • Experience with automated testing and CI/CD practices • Capability to support operations on-call rotations as needed • Experience working directly with customers or external partners to gather requirements and deliver integrations • Strong verbal and written communication skills, including with customers Compensation Salary Range (level-dependent): • Mid-career: $135k to $175k + equity • Senior: $160k to $200k + equity
