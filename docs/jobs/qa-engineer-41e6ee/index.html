Mantle positions itself as the gateway for institutions and TradFi to move into RWAs on-chain. With $4B+ in community-owned assets, Mantle combines credibility, liquidity and scale with institutional infrastructure to support large-scale adoption. The ecosystem is anchored by $MNT in the Bybit network, and built out through subsidiaries mETH, fBTC, MI4 and MantleX.
