SRE Engineer Location: Mendrisio, Switzerland An opportunity has arisen for an experienced Site Reliability Engineer to join a growing international technology business, helping build and maintain the infrastructure behind a key product. You'll join an established Infrastructure team, working closely with software engineers to design, implement and maintain scalable, highly available infrastructure. The role has a strong focus on automation, cloud technologies and infrastructure as code, with the opportunity to contribute to new initiatives around AI architecture and data infrastructure. The Role • Design, implement and maintain scalable, reliable and high-performing infrastructure. • Work closely with software engineering teams to support the infrastructure behind the company's products. • Contribute to infrastructure architecture and the delivery of new technical initiatives. • Manage and support Azure infrastructure, particularly around observability, monitoring, alerting and log management. • Manage AWS infrastructure to support day-to-day operations. • Develop and maintain infrastructure as code, primarily using Terraform. • Support highly available, scalable and secure cloud architecture. • Work with relational and non-relational databases, including PostgreSQL, Redis and MySQL. • Contribute to automation, scripting and continuous improvement across the infrastructure estate. • Participate in a weekly on-call rotation, with on-call duties compensated in line with company policy. What we're looking for • Advanced hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure. • Experience managing AWS infrastructure. • Strong understanding of cloud architecture, scalability, availability and security. • Practical experience with infrastructure as code, particularly Terraform. CloudFormation would also be beneficial. • Experience with Azure observability, including monitoring, alerting and log management. • Scripting experience with languages such as Python, Node.js, Golang or Bash. • Experience managing databases such as PostgreSQL, Redis and MySQL. • Good knowledge of Git and distributed version control. • Experience with PagerDuty or similar on-call management tools. • Strong communication skills and a good level of written and spoken English. • A proactive approach, with the ability to work independently and collaboratively. The opportunity • Based at the company's Swiss headquarters in Mendrisio, within easy commuting distance of Milan, Como, Varese and Lugano. • Four weeks' paid holiday, increasing to five weeks after two years. • Two additional days per year for volunteering. • Train subscription. • Access to a benefits platform offering discounts and savings. • Opportunity to work alongside highly skilled engineers, with scope for professional growth and development. The position offers the chance to work within an international engineering team, contribute to evolving cloud and AI infrastructure initiatives, and have a meaningful impact on the reliability and scalability of the platform. If this sounds like a good fit for you, or you know someone who would be a good fit, please get in touch either via Ashley.willicott@maxwellbond.co.uk, or call on 07401 087324 for a confidential chat
