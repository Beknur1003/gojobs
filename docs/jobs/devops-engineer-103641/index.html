AWS DevOps Engineer Hybrid - Newcastle / Edinburgh About us: We are a software Consultancy, who provide highly skilled consultants to support client business needs. Test Driven Solutions have had a successful six years of operating, with our consultants working across green/brown field projects using the latest cutting-edge technologies. We work within private and public sectors, including banking, government, healthcare and renewable energy organisations. We are continuously growing as a business, with new client wins and an internal project on-going. Benefits: • Competitive salary • 25 days holiday + Bank Holidays • Remote work and flexible working hours • Contributory pension scheme • Development opportunities/Paid certificates • Discretionary Bonus Scheme • BUPA Healthcare • Perkbox benefits/discount scheme • Social company events • Company away weekend What it’s like to work for TDS: We have clear values and a family ethos. We are multidisciplinary and multimedia, with regular meetups online and at in-person events. We maintain a strong connection across our teams and there's a great sense of community in the way we work. Located in the city centres of both Newcastle and Edinburgh, we have a hybrid offices to provide collaborative spaces surrounded by plenty of great spots to socialise. What you will be involved in • Working in cross-functional teams with our clients, supporting the delivery of their initiatives. • Contributing to design, development and the operations of internal applications. • Communicating effectively, internally and externally. • Promoting the TDS company values: Passion, Effectiveness, Transparency, Commitment. • Advocating best practices in software development, including XP (TDD), agility, DevOps and CI/CD. • Growing the company by contributing to our ongoing recruitment efforts. • Being a curious technologist with an appetite to develop new skills. • Mentoring TDS staff, growing their technical and consultancy skills. • Actively participating in internal product and systems development. Requirements In addition to the roles and responsibilities outlined above, a successful candidate will have at least 5 years of professional experience working in a cloud technology environment using AWS, with experience building and operating applications within this space. A candidate will have worked with the following technologies: • Containerisation and orchestration technologies, such as Docker, Kubernetes and Helm • An infrastructure as code tool, such as: Terraform, CloudFormation • A Configuration as Code tool, such as : Ansible • A DevOps-friendly programming language, such as Python, Golang or JavaScript (Node) • An understanding of a cloud provider’s IAM architecture and its security implications • A monitoring and logging stack, such as CloudWatch, Splunk, Grafana and Prometheus • Secrets Management tooling, such as Vault, AWS Secrets Manager • Continuous Delivery pipelines: GitHub Actions, GitLab, Jenkins, TeamCity Apply today for immediate consideration.
