About the Role: CrowdStrike is hiring a Sr. Software Engineer on the Infrastructure team to help grow our global team of engineers focused on developing tools to build, deploy, observe, and maintain a first class, highly available cloud platform at massive scale. This is a software development role where candidates with a strong background in full stack development, DevOps, and Golang will shine. What You'll Do: The Infrastructure team is responsible for building and operating CrowdStrike's internal cloud management platform. We build and operate the tools that enable other CrowdStrikers to confidently ship code. We're a small, remote-first team of full stack engineers building APIs and UIs in Go and TypeScript. The right candidate will have extensive experience in both Go and TypeScript, as well as a deep understanding of cloud native infrastructure and CI/CD. • Continually enhance existing CI/CD systems and processes to improve efficiency and speed in the software delivery cycle. • Develop, maintain, and operate mission critical pipelines built on top of Temporal and Argo Workflows. • Research and introduce innovative tooling solutions aimed at boosting developer productivity. • Design and implement integration strategies for seamlessly incorporating new tools into the existing development environment. • Work closely with development teams to understand their needs, identify bottlenecks, and gather requirements for system enhancements. • Ensure our development processes adhere to best practices for sustainability and security, with a clear understanding of SOC2 compliance impacts on CI environments. • Engage in collaborative initiatives with development, infrastructure, security, and SRE teams to drive improvements in the SDLC and CI/CD practices. • Participate in an on-call rotation to maintain uptime and operations of the CI/CD system. What You'll Need: • Must be a US Citizen or Green Card holder • 5+ years of overall experience as a DevOps, SRE, or software engineer. • Extensive experience in Golang • Strong experience in TypeScript/JavaScript • Deep understanding of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and CI/CD best practices. • Proven track record of effectively supporting and collaborating with large development teams. • Hands-on experience configuring and operating CI/CD tools such as Argo Workflows, Jenkins, Temporal, or similar. • Proficient with Linux operating systems and cloud computing services. • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes. Preferred Qualifications: • Experience creating development tools is preferred. • Experience with modern software deployment technologies like Kubernetes and Docker. • Experience building artifacts using tools like GoReleaser, and familiarity with packaging formats including Docker, Debian, and PyPI. • Familiarity with Configuration Management and Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform or Pulumi. • Experience with GitOps tools such as Helm, Kustomize, and Argo CD. • Proven ability to work effectively with both local and remote teams. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with an emphasis on clear, correct, complete, concise, and compassionate interactions. • Proficient in using monitoring and alerting tools such as Prometheus, Alertmanager, and Grafana to manage software operations effectively. • Author clear technical documentation, guides and knowledge base articles for developers. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Information Systems, Engineering or a related field. #LI-LY1 #LI-Remote Benefits of Working at CrowdStrike: • Market leader in compensation and equity awards • Comprehensive physical and mental wellness programs • Competitive vacation and holidays for recharge • Paid parental and adoption leaves • Professional development opportunities for all employees regardless of level or role • Employee Networks, geographic neighborhood groups, and volunteer opportunities to build connections • Vibrant office culture with world class amenities • Great Place to Work Certified™ across the globe CrowdStrike is proud to be an equal opportunity employer.
