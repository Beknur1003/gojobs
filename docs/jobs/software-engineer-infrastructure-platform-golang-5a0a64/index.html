About GeoComply We’re GeoComply! We are at the forefront of geolocation, cybersecurity, and anti-fraud innovation, developing and delivering cutting-edge technologies to help ensure regulatory compliance, combat bad online actors, alleviate user friction, and protect businesses from fraud. Achieving significant business and revenue growth over the past three years and dubbed a tech “Unicorn,” GeoComply has been trusted by leading global brands and regulators for over ten years. Our compliance-grade geolocation technology solutions are installed on over 400 million devices and analyze over 12 billion transactions a year. At the heart of it all is the people, united by a deep commitment to problem-solving and revolutionizing how people and businesses use the internet to instill confidence in every online interaction. With teams across five countries, three continents, and a global customer base, we have no plans to slow down. The Service Platform & Infrastructure team owns the path from code to production: Go deployment tooling, GKE/GCP infrastructure, observability, and production maintenance. Our goal is to help teams take a new service from a standard template to a running deployment on our existing platform in minutes, with CI/CD, configuration, and baseline monitoring built in. The role You’ll build the Go tooling and agent integrations behind that path. With guidance from senior engineers, you’ll own well-scoped components from design through production, with team support during design reviews, rollouts, and incidents. The team works across deployment automation, agentic observability, and production maintenance. Your initial projects will focus on a defined area, with opportunities to contribute across all three as you grow. You don’t need prior expertise in every area below. What you’ll do Service deployment and infrastructure automation • Extend our Go deployment tooling so teams can launch services with CI/CD, configuration, and baseline monitoring built in. • Build Go CLIs, services, and Kubernetes controllers that automate tasks such as certificate rotation, environment provisioning, access changes, and capacity adjustments. • Turn recurring DevOps requests into self-service or automated workflows, and measure the reduction in tickets and manual work. Agentic observability • Expose metrics, logs, and traces through well-defined tools, such as MCP servers, so agents can query system state with scoped permissions. • Instrument the services you build with structured logs, correlated traces, and alerts that help engineers diagnose failures and respond effectively. • Work with the team to identify the signals agents need to investigate a service, and close gaps in instrumentation and context. Auto-debugging and production maintenance • Build agents that triage alerts, correlate signals across services, and produce ranked root-cause hypotheses with supporting evidence and clearly stated uncertainty. • Build guardrails into incident-response agents: read-only access by default, explicit human approval before any production remediation an agent executes, and a full audit trail of what it saw and did. • Evaluate agent accuracy against past incidents, track incorrect or unsupported conclusions, and improve the system with each miss. • Automate approved production maintenance through reviewed workflows, including Kubernetes and base-image upgrades, dependency patching, and safe rollbacks. Across all three • Write design docs for the components you own, bring them to review, and incorporate feedback from teammates. • Join the on-call rotation for the tooling you build, with team support, and help resolve incidents and follow through on improvements Reliability and performance Our customers depend on GeoComply in real time, with availability and latency commitments backed by SLAs. Set reliability targets appropriate to each platform component’s purpose and its impact on customer-facing services. • Help define SLIs and SLOs for the services and tools you build, and use error budgets to guide reliability work. • Use load and performance testing to catch availability and latency regressions before production. • Design for failure from the first version with timeouts, bounded retries, graceful degradation, and safe rollbacks. • Make sensible SLOs, dashboards, and actionable alerts the default for services launched through our deployment tooling. What we’re looking for • Typically 1–3 years building and operating production software, with Go as a primary language. • Hands-on experience deploying, debugging, and operating services on Kubernetes. GKE experience is a plus. • Working knowledge of a major cloud provider, preferably GCP, and infrastructure as code such as Terraform. • Experience instrumenting services and using metrics, logs, and traces to investigate failures. • Familiarity with SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, and error budgets, and how they guide availability and latency decisions. • You use AI coding agents daily, provide clear context and constraints, and take responsibility for reviewing generated changes for correctness, security, edge cases, and performance. • You investigate underlying causes, distinguish immediate mitigation from a durable fix, and communicate mistakes and uncertainty promptly during incidents. • You write clear design docs, give structured feedback supported by evidence, and seek review for security-sensitive decisions. You consider cost and security tradeoffs before committing to an approach. Nice to have • Built a Kubernetes operator or controller using controller-runtime or kubebuilder. • Built an LLM agent or tool integration for production systems, such as an MCP server, tool-calling agent, or evaluation harness. • Worked with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, or GCP Cloud Monitoring and Logging at scale. • Experience in a regulated environment, such as gaming, payments, fintech, or systems subject to FedRAMP requirements.
