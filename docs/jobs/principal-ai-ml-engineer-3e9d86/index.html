This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Principal AI/ML Engineer based in British Columbia, Canada. The Principal AI/ML Engineer will provide senior technical leadership in the design and development of sophisticated cloud-based products, platforms, and software systems. This role combines deep engineering expertise with architectural vision, helping teams build scalable, reliable, and efficient cloud-native solutions. You will lead complex technical projects from solution design and coding through testing and integration, while ensuring high standards of quality and performance. As a recognized subject matter expert, you will influence technical direction, introduce innovative technologies, and help solve challenging engineering problems. You will also mentor fellow engineers and collaborate across functional teams in a primarily remote working environment. Accountabilities: Lead the technical design and development of complex products and cloud platforms, ensuring that engineering solutions meet performance, scalability, reliability, and quality standards. Your responsibilities will include: • Lead engineering project teams through the design, analysis, development, coding, testing, and integration of complex products and platforms. • Design and build efficient, scalable, and robust cloud subsystems that support advanced software solutions. • Review technical designs, test plans, and source code to ensure compliance with cloud architecture principles, development guidelines, and engineering standards. • Provide actionable feedback to improve product quality, strengthen engineering practices, and mitigate technical risks. • Represent the engineering team in technical forums, contributing expertise and guidance to cross-functional initiatives. • Provide technical leadership, coaching, and mentorship to less-experienced engineers, supporting their professional development. • Drive innovation by evaluating and integrating new technologies into software systems and engineering projects. • Analyze complex science, engineering, business, application, system administration, and network-related challenges. • Develop and implement effective solutions to complex software and infrastructure problems. • Exercise independent judgment when selecting technical approaches and determining the best methods to achieve project objectives. • Collaborate with technical and functional teams to align engineering solutions with product requirements and broader organizational goals. Requirements: You are an experienced software engineering professional with strong technical judgment, advanced programming capabilities, and a track record of delivering complex cloud-based solutions. You can communicate architectural decisions effectively, guide engineering teams, and translate challenging technical requirements into robust implementations. The ideal candidate will bring: • A bachelor's or master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a closely related quantitative discipline. • Typically 7–10 years of relevant professional experience in software engineering or a related technical field. • Strong programming skills in one or more of the following languages: Python, Java, Golang, or JavaScript. • Solid understanding of microservices architecture and the design of containerized applications in Kubernetes-managed environments. • Experience designing and integrating software systems across multiple platforms within broader technical architectures. • Strong knowledge of software testing methodologies, including test planning, test execution, debugging, and the development of testing scripts and tools. • Ability to review technical designs and code, identify potential issues, and ensure adherence to engineering standards. • Experience developing scalable, reliable, and efficient cloud-native applications and subsystems. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to address complex application, infrastructure, and system-related challenges. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present software architectures and technical proposals to senior management. • Proven ability to work independently, exercise sound technical judgment, and contribute to cross-functional engineering initiatives. • Leadership and mentoring capabilities, with the ability to guide technical teams and support the development of other engineers. Benefits: • Annual base salary ranging from C$141,500 to C$283,500, depending on geographic location, experience, education, training, and skill level. • Potential variable incentives in addition to base compensation. • Comprehensive health and wellbeing benefits designed to support physical, financial, and emotional wellness. • Benefits information for Canadian employees is available in the employee benefits brochure. • Primarily remote working arrangement, allowing you to work from home. • Personal and professional development programs, with opportunities to pursue career goals and expand expertise. • Inclusive working environment that values diverse backgrounds, perspectives, and individual contributions. • Accessibility accommodations available throughout the application and interview process.
