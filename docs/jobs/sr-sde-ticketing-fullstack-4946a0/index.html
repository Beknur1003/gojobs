Learn more about us on our YouTube Channel or Blog Posts About the role We're building HighLevel's Ticketing module, the help desk that lets businesses manage support conversations across email and other channels: tickets, SLAs, assignments, automations and real-time inboxes. You'll own features end to end, from Go services and workers to the Vue.js UI, and run them in production on GCP at scale. Team & System Overview: The Conversations organization powers multi-channel communication across SMS, Email, WhatsApp, and DMs — handling over 2B messages each month. The Threads & Composer team owns the inbox and composer surfaces, and the backend services that deliver and store those interactions.You’ll work across Node.js microservices, GCP-based infra (GKE, Pub/Sub, Cloud Tasks), and MongoDB/Firestore storage layers, ensuring every message and UI state stays consistent, fast, and recoverable. Responsibilities: • • Design, build and ship ticketing features across backend (Go) and frontend (Vue.js). - Build event-driven services and workers on Pub/Sub, Cloud Tasks and Redis, such as SLA timers, email ingestion, assignments and real-time updates. - Own the reliability of your services: dashboards, alerts, on-call debugging and fixing performance problems. - Design data models and APIs that hold up at multi-tenant scale, and review designs and code across the team. - Mentor engineers and work with Product, QA and partner teams (Email, IAM, Platform) to ship on time. Requirements: • • 5+ years of software engineering experience, with strong production experience in Golang. - Solid frontend experience with Vue.js (Vue 3, Composition API, TypeScript). - Redis, used in depth: caching, pub/sub fan-out, locks, rate limiting and data-structure design. - Experience with GCP: GKE/Kubernetes, Pub/Sub, Firestore or other NoSQL, Cloud Storage. - Strong distributed-systems fundamentals: idempotency, retries, queues, concurrency and consistency trade-offs. - Track record of owning features from design to production and debugging live issues. What we look for Strong ownership, clear written communication, pragmatic engineering judgment, and a habit of measuring before optimizing.
