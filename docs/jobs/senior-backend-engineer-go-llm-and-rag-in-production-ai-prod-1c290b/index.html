We are New Wave Devs, a senior engineering team. We are hiring a Senior Go engineer to embed long-term with our client, a Netherlands-based AI-native creative-tools company building the backend of their AI Studio product. It is a fast-paced product team: new frontier AI models are pushed into production within a day or two of release. You will own real pieces of the backend and work directly with their engineers. What you will do: • Design and scale Go microservices that manage the lifecycle of AI generations (image, video, audio, 3D, vector) • Build and run RAG pipelines and agent orchestration around production models • Handle long-running, resource-intensive inference tasks and high data throughput: queues, backpressure, retries • Work with AWS (EKS, S3, Lambda), Kafka and SQS for async processing, ConnectRPC / gRPC / GraphQL • Bridge AI model outputs into user-facing creative workflows You should have: • 5+ years backend, Go-first • Hands-on LLM or RAG work that reached production, not API calls from a prototype: LangChain, LangGraph, pgvector or similar • AWS (EKS/K8s), Kafka or SQS, gRPC/ConnectRPC, microservices at scale • Ownership mindset, comfortable when specs are not perfect, able to switch between shipping fast and building for scale • Real interest in the product itself. The client asks about it directly. • Good English, daily work with the client team We offer: long-term full-time contract through New Wave Devs, competitive rate, fully remote, fast start, days not weeks. Two technical interviews with the client's engineers, screen share and real code.
