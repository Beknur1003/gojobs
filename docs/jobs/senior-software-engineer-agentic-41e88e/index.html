Title: Senior Software Engineer, Agentic & Reusable Identity Department: EPD Reports To: Senior Manager, Engineering FLSA Status: Exempt Location: United States, Remote Job Summary The internet today lacks a consistent way to bind "real-world identity" with "cryptographic authority." As AI agents begin to transact on behalf of humans, this broken architecture is no longer sustainable. This Senior/Staff Engineer will join the team building the foundational trust layer that unifies authentication, authorization, identity, and consent for the next generation of the internet: the Agentic Economy. What you’ll do • Build trust infrastructure. Design and ship the systems that establish identity and trust, from signing services to key and certificate lifecycle management. • Make digital credentials work in the real world. Build issuance, presentation, and verification capabilities that developers and end users can rely on. • Enable agents to act with authority. Help create the foundations for secure authentication and delegation between people, organizations, and agents. • Turn standards into products. Build APIs, SDKs, and integrations, and contribute to pragmatic decision making on emerging specifications and tools. • Own delivery end to end. Take work from design through production, collaborating closely with partners and teammates. Required qualifications • Strong production software engineering experience. Typically 5+ years building backend services, distributed systems, or developer platforms, with demonstrated ownership of substantial capabilities from design through deployment and operation. • Strong programming and systems fundamentals. Proficiency in Go, TypeScript, Rust, or a comparable language, with a practical understanding of API design, data modeling, service boundaries, and distributed-system failure modes. Go and TypeScript experience is particularly useful. • Hands-on identity or security infrastructure experience. Experience building authentication, authorization, credential, key-management, or other security-critical capabilities. A practical understanding of modern authentication and authorization, including OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, and token-based access. • Sound security and operational judgment. Ability to reason about trust boundaries, public-key signatures, credential validation, least privilege, and sensitive data. Experience building reliable services with automated testing, observability, tenant isolation, and deployment controls in AWS or a comparable cloud environment. • Independent execution in an evolving environment. Ability to turn incomplete requirements into a clear plan and working software, and learn unfamiliar technical concepts or specifications as needed. You can distinguish a prototype from production software, test the highest-risk assumptions, and adjust direction as evidence changes. • Clear communication and effective engineering practices. You explain tradeoffs, seek review on consequential decisions, and work well with product, security, and partner engineers. You use automation and AI-assisted development tools thoughtfully, while remaining accountable for correctness, security, and maintainability. • Cryptographic services and trust infrastructure. Experience with PKI, X.509 certificates, HSM/KMS-backed signing, trust registries, public-key discovery, or workload identity. Familiarity with key rotation, revocation, recovery, and compromise handling. Preferred Qualifications • Experience in one or more of these areas is valuable. Related experience counts; expertise across every area is not expected. • Identity, credentials, and privacy-aware systems. Experience with identity verification, federation, credential services, or sharing identity information across organizations. Familiarity with decentralized identity or selective disclosure is welcome. • Delegated authorization and non-human identity. Experience securing services or workloads, implementing fine-grained authorization, or supporting user-approved access across systems with clear limits and lifecycle controls. • Cross-system integrations and transaction workflows. Experience with tool integrations, workflow automation, commerce, payments, or other systems that preserve user context and permissions across services. • Authentication and approval experiences. Experience building sign-in, account-linking, or approval flows across backend and user-facing systems. Exposure to passkeys, digital wallets, or credential management is useful. • Developer platforms and early-stage product development. Experience building APIs, SDKs, reusable libraries, or partner integrations and taking new capabilities from prototype to production. Open-source contributions and interoperability testing are welcome. • We operate in a fast-paced environment with a small team, evolving standards, and new product opportunities.
