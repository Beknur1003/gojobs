Job Title : Staff Software Engineer (Backend) Location : Bengaluru, IN About the Role : We are seeking a highly experienced Staff Software Engineer to lead the design and evolution of our platform and framework ecosystem. This role is ideal for a hands-on technical leader who thrives on building scalable frameworks, driving engineering excellence, and influencing teams across the organization. You will play a critical role in shaping the technical direction of our platform, enabling teams to build high-quality, reliable, and scalable services efficiently. What will you own : • Lead the design and development of scalable and resilient Frameworks hat empower scalable customer-facing APIs. • Influence architecture and design decisions across multiple teams; act as a technical advisor and escalation point for complex challenges. • Align engineering initiatives with business goals and organizational priorities. • Champion best practices in software engineering, including code quality, testing, observability, and maintainability across teams. • Advocate for developer productivity by improving tooling, documentation, and onboarding experiences. Act as a developer evangelist for platform capabilities. • Mentor senior engineers and contribute to raising the technical bar across the organization through reviews, design discussions, and knowledge sharing. • Leverage generative AI and AI coding tools to accelerate developer productivity, engineering workflows, and automation of repetitive tasks What we're looking for : • 10+ years of professional experience in software engineering, including at least 2 years in a staff-level role. • Strong proficiency in Java or Golang • Experience building platforms, frameworks, libraries, or SDKs at scale • Strong understanding of software engineering best practices (code quality, testing, CI/CD, observability) • Experience with cloud-native architectures, containerization (Docker), and orchestration (Kubernetes) • Solid experience with databases (SQL and NoSQL) and caching strategies • Proven ability to lead technical initiatives and influence without direct authority • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and modern DevOps practices. • Understanding of AI-native software development practices, including automated code generation, AI-driven testing, and human-in-the-loop review systems • Proven ability to adopt and drive modern engineering paradigms, particularly integrating AI into development workflows to improve productivity, quality, and delivery speed • Strong communication skills with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders. • Proven leadership experience, including mentoring engineers and leading technical initiatives. Good to have: • Experience with Python and Django • Experience building scalable APIs (external or partner-facing preferred) • Experience acting as a technical evangelist (talks, documentation, internal advocacy) • Familiarity with API gateways, authentication/authorization patterns, and rate limiting Success In this role would look like : • This is a high-impact role at the intersection of platform engineering and developer experience. • You will define how engineers build, scale, and operate services—shaping both the technical architecture and the engineering culture of the organization.
