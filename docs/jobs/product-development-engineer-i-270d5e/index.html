What's In It For You • Help shape the future of DIRECTV for Business products, platforms, and cloud services. • Work with modern cloud, platform engineering, automation, and advertising technologies that directly impact customers and business growth. • Gain hands-on experience building scalable cloud-native services, APIs, automation solutions, and modern engineering platforms. • Collaborate with experienced engineers while expanding your expertise across software development, cloud computing, DevOps, product development, and platform engineering. • Enjoy a hybrid work environment with access to more than 28,000 square feet of advanced networking, video, cloud, and technology lab facilities in Clarksburg, Maryland.
