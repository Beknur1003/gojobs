Who We Are: H LAB is a young, energetic, and tech-savvy company focused on designing and developing efficient healthcare systems for public and private settings. Our team's expertise in healthcare systems engineering dates back to our roots at the Center for Health Systems Analytics. We tackle projects ranging from isolated units in hospitals to interconnected healthcare nodes and offer consulting services as well as systems innovation developments. Role Overview: We’re looking for a skilled Software Engineer to develop features and enhancements for the Cortex Product in a secure, performant, and well-tested environment. You'll work closely with cross-functional teams, ship impactful features with autonomy, and uphold code quality through collaborative reviews. Role Description: • Advocate for improvements to product quality, security, and performance that have particular impact across your team. • Participate in designing code architecture and database on your team responsible domain. • Solve technical problems of high scope and complexity. • Help to define and improve our internal standards for style, maintainability, and best practices for a high-scale web environment. Maintain and advocate for these standards through code review. • Provide mentorship for Junior and Intermediate Engineers in your section to help them grow in their technical responsibilities and remove blockers. • Consistently ship moderately sized features and improvements with minimal guidance and support from other team members. Collaborate with the team on larger projects. • Collaborate with SRE to develop continuous integration for unit test, integration test, e2e test, performance test and security test. Qualification • 1-5 years of experienced in the role of a software engineer​ • Strong understanding of multiple software design tools and languages​ • Able to perform development, testing and debugging work​ • Proven technical aptitude in one or more application programming domains. • Ability to work in an agile development environment​ Our Tech Stack: • Frontend: TypeScript, React 18+ • Backend:TypeScript + NestJS, GoLang, OpenTelemetry, Temporal • Testing: K6, Playwright • AI: Claude Code, Codex • Infra & DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, ArgoCD • Database: OpenTelemetry, Postgres, Elasticsearch • Bonus Skills (nice-to-have): Flutter, Python, Angular, Svelte, Tauri Why You’ll Love Working Here: • Be part of a mission-driven company solving real health problems • Collaborate with talented teammates in a modern tech environment • Take ownership of your work and see your impact on real users • Mentorship and growth pathways as we scale
