About the Role: Our Fusion product is seeking a Senior-to-Principal (Level 7) backend software Engineer to build out a platform product which provides core orchestration and workflow capabilities to all users of Crowdstrike’s Flagship offering, the Falcon Platform, while also offering plug-and-play access to the building block components of the lion's share of our codebase, as well as key feature components among our partner products. Fusion – a core ‘tier one’ offering in our Flagship Platform, Falcon – is used by customers to automate 80% of breach remediation actions with the Falcon Platform. It also serves as a runtime engine of Foundry, a sibling product which gives the Falcon Platform users access to our whole suite of components as well as exposed APIs for a plethora of our security partners to instantly create custom-purpose 'Frankenstein' security apps by configuring selections in just a handful of dropdown menus. Fusion is among the most heavily used products in Crowdstrike's Falcon product suite. Presently, we're integrating Fusion with agentic workflow development to push our Next gen SIEM functionality into the agentic AI-integrated future. This role is for an experienced backend microservices developer adept with cloud engineering principles, user-facing feature design and development ('or' Fine Grained Auth Expertise), and experience with plenty of custom API development as well as high-scale events processing on a msg queue like Kafka. Your passion and meticulous attention to performance, reliability and scalability will be key ingredients to your success here at CrowdStrike. *Be advised, this role is a hybrid work setup involving 2-3 days in-office at our Sunnyvale, CA location.
