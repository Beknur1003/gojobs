Location: This position is remote within the United States or Canada Role Summary Censys is seeking a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to join our Infrastructure and Operations Platform team. Our engineers ship Go services from a single monorepo onto Kubernetes in Google Cloud, and the speed and safety of the path from commit to production sets the pace for the whole company. We're looking for an engineer who can support that path end to end, treat our development teams as internal customers, and build the self-service platform that lets them create, deploy, and run their services with confidence. What You'll Do • Own the developer path to production, from monorepo build tooling and CI in GitHub Actions through GitOps deployments with ArgoCD, Helm, and Crossplane, and drive it toward true continuous deployment • Design and build self-service platform capabilities, including service templates, a service catalog, repository tooling, and documentation, so development teams can stand up and operate new services without filing a ticket • Make observability a default rather than an afterthought, standardizing how our services capture the four golden signals (latency, traffic, errors, and saturation) with Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry • Build, secure, and maintain the container images and tooling that run our applications on Kubernetes and GCP managed services such as Cloud SQL, Pub/Sub, and Memorystore • Shift security left by building dependency scanning, static analysis, and image scanning into CI, working with our Security Operations team to keep it fast and actionable for developers • Lead troubleshooting of complex production issues alongside development teams, and participate in our shared on-call rotation, where SRE owns infrastructure and primary site uptime and development teams own their services end to end • Mentor engineers on the SRE team and across development teams on Kubernetes, CI/CD, and reliability practices, and contribute to the golden-path standards and shared libraries they build on What You'll Bring • A positive attitude biased towards action and iterative problem solving, learning as we grow every step of the way • 5+ years of experience in site reliability, platform, or infrastructure engineering • Hands-on experience deploying, operating, and debugging applications on Kubernetes, including Helm-based deployments and building and securing container images • Experience running workloads on public cloud managed services (GCP preferred), such as managed databases, messaging, and caching • Fluency with infrastructure-as-code and GitOps workflows (Terraform, Crossplane, ArgoCD, or similar) • Experience with CI/CD in a monorepo, trunk-based development model (GitHub Actions or similar), and a strong desire to get to continuous deployment • Practical experience instrumenting and monitoring services with Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, or similar • Experience building automation and internal tooling in a language like Go or Python, not just writing configuration • Strong communication and empathy for developers. You seek out feedback from the teams you support, and explaining a BCDR tradeoff to product management should be as comfortable as walking an engineer through a failed deploy What Sets You Apart • Experience supporting a gRPC microservice architecture, including a service mesh such as Istio for observability, multi-cluster routing, and traffic management • Comfort working in application code to introduce golden paths, shared libraries, and better test and integration environments. Most of our services are written in Go, with some Python and Scala. • Working knowledge of infrastructure networking (load balancers, ingress, DNS, and VPC design).
