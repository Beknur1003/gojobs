Cloud Software Engineer - Managed Kubernetes The Container Services team is responsible for delivering Leaseweb Managed Kubernetes a platform that empowers customers to deploy, manage, and update their own Kubernetes clusters while simplifying the complexity that comes with running them at scale. We are looking for a motivated Cloud Software Engineer to help build and evolve this product. You will be part of a multidisciplinary team of experienced professionals (software engineers, system engineers, scrum master, product owner and product management) that strive to make our product better. **Product Engineering at Leaseweb** Our team of approximately 145 engineers works in small scrum teams, each with end-to-end responsibility for a specific product or part of our architecture. We work on a remote-first basis, coming together in person at our Amsterdam headquarters twice a year. Our organizational structure is flat, placing a high value on independence and entrepreneurship. The atmosphere is informal and relaxed, creating a highly motivating work environment where you will work alongside some of the most inspiring colleagues in the industry. **What is the role about?*** In your role as a Cloud Software Engineer in the Container Services team, you are responsible for designing, developing and maintaining a platform that automates the delivery and management of Kubernetes Cluster for our customers. Your main activity will be working together with the team to design, implement and maintain the components that powers our product. The components you will work on are responsible for deploying Virtual machines, configuring network, volumes, firewall rules, load-balancing and a lot of cloud-related concepts, making Kubernetes deployment work with Leaseweb Public Cloud.
