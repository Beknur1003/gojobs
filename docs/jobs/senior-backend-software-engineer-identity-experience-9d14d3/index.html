This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Backend Software Engineer | Identity Experience based in Brazil. As a Senior Backend Software Engineer, you will help build and evolve the core identity services that power essential product experiences for millions of users worldwide. You will take ownership of critical systems supporting account creation, account management, permissions, and account recovery. Working in an international engineering environment, you will design and operate scalable, resilient backend architectures where performance, reliability, and security are essential. You will also contribute to the team's AI transformation by integrating modern AI-assisted development practices into engineering workflows. This is an opportunity to tackle complex technical challenges, influence architectural decisions, and deliver impactful solutions with a high degree of autonomy. The position is fully remote within Brazil. Accountabilities: You will lead the development and operation of critical identity services, ensuring they remain reliable, secure, and scalable as the platform grows. In close collaboration with product, UX, and engineering teams, you will deliver high-impact features, improve system performance, and help establish modern development practices. • Own and evolve core identity services, including account creation, account management, permissions management, and account recovery. • Architect, develop, and maintain scalable, resilient backend systems that support global user growth. • Optimize the performance, reliability, and infrastructure efficiency of core microservices. • Design and deliver high-impact products and features, taking ownership from technical planning through implementation and production operation. • Drive the adoption of AI-assisted development practices, using modern tools to improve engineering standards, productivity, and problem-solving. • Collaborate with international cross-functional teams, UX specialists, and product leaders to create seamless identity experiences. • Contribute to technical discussions, share expertise, and influence architectural and engineering decisions. • Continuously improve production systems, code quality, maintainability, and operational practices. • Promote a collaborative and supportive team culture that values wellbeing, balance, and shared ownership. Requirements: The ideal candidate is an experienced backend engineer with a strong background in building and operating distributed systems at scale. You should be comfortable making independent technical decisions, managing projects end to end, and collaborating across teams. A thoughtful approach to system design, clear communication, and strong familiarity with AI-assisted engineering tools are also essential. • Demonstrated experience building and operating distributed backend systems at scale in production environments. This is a mandatory requirement. • Strong experience with distributed systems, APIs, data-intensive applications, and large-scale cloud-native architectures. • Proficiency in backend development; experience with Golang is highly desirable. • Experience designing and scaling databases, particularly PostgreSQL. • Hands-on experience with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud Platform (GCP) or AWS. • Practical experience with Kubernetes or similar container orchestration platforms, including deploying, scaling, and operating production services. • Ability to independently own projects from technical design through implementation and ongoing operation. • Strong focus on code quality, maintainability, reliability, and sound technical decision-making. • Excellent communication skills, with the confidence to lead technical discussions, influence decisions, and exchange ideas across teams. • High level of AI fluency, including practical experience using large language models (LLMs) and AI-assisted development tools to improve engineering workflows. • Ability to work effectively in an international, cross-functional environment.
