Software Engineer (Services & Infrastructure)

Audiomob Abu Dhabi, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates опубликовано 8 октября 2026

PostgreSQLRedisKubernetesTerraformGCPTypeScriptNode.jsAI/LLM
Audiomob was founded on the core principle that there is a better way to monetise apps. Audiomob is the industry leader in mobile in-app audio advertising. We push the boundaries of technology to create non-intrusive and highly scalable advertising solutions. At Audiomob, we believe in combining the power of audio, technology, and AI to create seamless and engaging ad experiences for users and effective monetization strategies for game developers and app publishers. We thrive on creativity, collaboration, and a commitment to excellence in everything we do. About the Role We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Software Engineer with extensive experience delivering high-scale, reliable software systems. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in Node.js and TypeScript, with bonus points for Go and prior experience with advertising technology. You will work across a modern engineering stack that includes Kubernetes, Google Cloud Platform, PostgreSQL, Redis, Terraform, SvelteKit 5, and event-driven architectures. This role is critical in the development, scaling, and deployment of our cutting-edge technologies. Key Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain high-scale, performant, and reliable software solutions. • Build and operate services using Node.js, TypeScript, and related technologies across a cloud-native environment. • Contribute to infrastructure, deployment, and operational excellence using tools such as Kubernetes, Google Cloud Platform, and Terraform. • Lead by example, demonstrate what good looks like, and foster a high-performance engineering environment. • Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical issues across application, infrastructure, and data layers. • Actively participate in coding, code reviews, and architecture discussions to maintain technical excellence. • Identify and resolve technical challenges, ensuring timely and high-quality delivery. • Excel in a dynamic environment with the ability to adapt to changing priorities and demands. Qualifications: • 5+ years of professional software development experience. • Demonstrable expertise in Node.js and TypeScript. • Experience delivering high-scale, high-performance software systems. • Strong understanding of cloud-native engineering principles, distributed systems, and production-grade service operation. • Hands-on experience with some of the following: Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, Redis, Google Cloud Platform, Terraform. • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. • Ability to maintain composure and effectiveness under pressure. • Experience with agentic-first engineering workflows. Nice-to-have: • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science. • Proficiency in Go is highly desirable. • In-depth understanding of the advertising technology industry is a plus. We’re building a diverse, inclusive team: Diversity and representation matters to us all. While the forward journey of representation in the game and tech sectors is improving, we recognise that there is still work to be done. We know that the future we want to see should be reflected in our own team and values. As such, we encourage applicants from all backgrounds to apply, and welcome those that believe diversity in all its forms is a key driver of success.
