We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer (Golang) to design and build scalable, reliable, and secure backend systems. The role involves developing distributed services, designing robust APIs, implementing event-driven architectures, and ensuring high standards of performance, maintainability, and system reliability. While the primary focus is backend development, occasional contributions to React-based frontend functionality may be required. Responsibilities • Design, implement, and maintain scalable backend services in Golang, including RESTful APIs and stable data contracts that evolve safely over time. • Implement and manage asynchronous, event-driven communication, with strong guarantees around message reliability, idempotency, error handling, and backward compatibility. • Design and develop distributed systems and microservices with a focus on scalability, resilience, and maintainability. • Optimize application performance, reliability, and resource utilization in production environments. • Participate in architecture discussions and contribute to technical decision-making. • Conduct code reviews and support knowledge sharing across the engineering team. • Design code with testability as a core principle, writing and maintaining unit and integration tests for APIs, business logic, and integrations. • Design and maintain secure APIs and service integrations, applying best practices in authentication, authorization, and identity management. • Build integrations with enterprise platforms such as Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, ServiceNow, Genesys Cloud, and other third-party systems. • Design and maintain reliable integrations between internal services and external enterprise platforms. • 6+ years of experience as a Software Engineer. • Strong experience building backend applications and services using Golang. • Solid understanding of concurrency in Golang and asynchronous workflows. • Hands-on experience with event-driven architectures and message handling (e.g., Kafka, NATS, RabbitMQ, or similar technologies). • Experience with AWS and cloud-native architectures. • Proven experience designing APIs and event schemas with long-term evolution and backward compatibility in mind. • Strong understanding of authentication, authorization, and secure API design. • Experience with microservice architectures and distributed systems. • Experience with relational and/or NoSQL databases. • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes. • Strong commitment to automated testing, including unit and integration testing. Nice to Have • Experience with React and modern frontend development. • Experience with TypeScript, Protobuf. • Experience with cloud platforms Azure, or GCP. • Experience with monitoring and observability tools (e.g., Grafana, Prometheus) • Experience with identity and access management solutions (Keycloak, Auth0, Okta, or similar).
