Software Development Engineer - I (Integrations)

Botsync Bengaluru, Karnataka, India опубликовано 19 сентября 2026

PythonC++
Asia's leading robotics solutions provider, automating enterprise supply chains since 2017. We build syncOS — the industry-first no-code platform that lets manufacturers and warehouse operators integrate, reprogram, and scale automation systems without halting production or calling in specialists. Our autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) run 24/7 for global brands like Ford, Coca-Cola, and Kimberly-Clark — transforming production lines in hours, not weeks. We started as roboticists ourselves. We know the pain of fragmented, proprietary systems that refuse to talk to each other. syncOS is our answer — and we believe it's what the industry has been waiting for. We are a young, passionate team chasing one big mission: #automateasia. If you love robots, hate inefficiency, and want to build solutions that run on real factory floors — read on. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: • Develop, test, and maintain backend services, APIs, and data components for syncOS's integration platform. • Implement integration modules in Python and C++ using REST APIs, Modbus, OPC UA, MQTT, Websockets, CAN, and TCP/IP. • Contribute to sync templates and integration frameworks for machine-to-machine and machine-to-software integrations. • Write clean, testable, and maintainable code; participate in code reviews and follow established engineering standards. Build and extend integrations for AMRs, AGVs, fleet managers, Warehouse Management Systems, and sensor OEM platforms. • Diagnose and resolve backend, API, protocol, and data-flow issues during development and deployment. • Add automated tests, logging, monitoring, and technical documentation to improve system reliability. • Work with senior engineers, QA, Customer Success, and deployment teams to execute clearly scoped deliverables. • Support deployment validation and customer-site troubleshooting, and close issues through root-cause follow-up. Requirements Essentials: • 1-3 years of backend software engineering experience, with strong hands-on proficiency in Python and working knowledge of C++. • Experience building, testing, and debugging backend services, REST APIs, or data-driven applications. • Hands-on experience or strong familiarity with Modbus, OPC UA, MQTT, Websockets, CAN, and TCP/IP. • Good understanding of backend fundamentals, API design, networking, data handling, automated testing, and debugging. • Exposure to integrating robotics or automation equipment such as AMRs or AGVs with fleet-management or orchestration software. Highly Desirable: • Experience integrating Warehouse Management Systems or sensor OEM platforms. • Working proficiency in Golang alongside Python and C++. Benefits • Competitive salary • Comprehensive health benefits package • Hustle of a young startup, always something to do and something to improve • Opportunities for professional development and growth into management roles • Collaborative and inclusive work environment
