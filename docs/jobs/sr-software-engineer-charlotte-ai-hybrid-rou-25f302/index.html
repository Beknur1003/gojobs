About the Role: CrowdStrike is looking for a Senior AI Cloud Engineer to join our growing Charlotte AI Engineering team in Bucharest. You will provide hands-on technical leadership while building globally distributed, fault-tolerant and highly scalable cloud services and agentic AI systems. The role spans Charlotte AI and CrowdStrike’s broader Agentic SOC & SOAR products portfolio. You will help turn AI research and platform capabilities into production products that coordinate agents, data, automation and human expertise to accelerate security detection, investigation and response. This is a deeply hands-on role. Demonstrated experience building agentic systems and agentic AI products, and deploying and operating them in production, is required. Prior experience with Go is helpful but not required; strong distributed-systems fundamentals and the ability to learn new technologies are essential. CrowdStrike is a cybersecurity company, but we do not require candidates for this role to have prior security industry experience. We will mentor and train in security topics as needed. We do expect a strong interest in CrowdStrike's mission and a willingness to engage with the needs of our product teams and customers. This is a hybrid position based in Bucharest, Romania. Candidates should be comfortable working from the Bucharest office twice a week. About the Product: CrowdStrike has pioneered the use of artificial intelligence since we first introduced AI-powered protection to replace signature-based antivirus over 10 years ago, and we’ve continued to deeply integrate AI across our platform. Using recent advances in Large Language Model technologies, CrowdStrike introduced Charlotte AI, a generative AI security analyst. It uses high-fidelity security data and is seamlessly integrated with CrowdStrike’s industry-leading threat hunters, managed detection and response operators, and incident response experts. The Charlotte AI engine is a first-of-its-kind offering that empowers users of all skill levels to leverage the data available in the Falcon platform while reducing security operations complexity and cost. Charlotte AI also helps power CrowdStrike’s Agentic SOC vision, which combines unified Falcon data, specialized agents, automation and human-in-command workflows to accelerate security operations. This role will contribute to product and platform capabilities across Charlotte AI and the broader Agentic SOC. Learn more about Charlotte AI, Agentic SOC Transformation and the public announcement of Charlotte Agentic SOAR.
