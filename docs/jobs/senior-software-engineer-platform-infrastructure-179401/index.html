Join over 5,000 people across the globe who think that’s work worth doing. 12Senior Software Engineer - Platform Infrastructure ​ Why We Have This Role The Platform Infrastructure team builds and operates foundational software that engineering teams use to run their services. This role will focus on making workloads on the platform more cost-effective, including through tooling and frameworks that help teams monitor and optimize CPU and memory use. The team runs EKS and supports engineering teams across Qualtrics, with PGF currently an exception until it is rolled in. The role also includes building services in Golang and working on Kubernetes orchestration as the platform's needs evolve. Engineering teams are the platform's customers, and the team works across service discovery and orchestration. How You'll Find Success • Deep knowledge in open source tooling/technologies (Linux systems etc) • Establishing productive working relationships with other platform teams, AWS counterparts, security, and compliance teams • Designing with an eye towards scalability, both in technical measures and organizational processes • Possessing a customer-obsessed attitude when working with application teams within the company How You'll Grow • Develop broader expertise in Container Orchestration as you build the runtime foundation for the entirety of Qualtrics engineering. • Build experience in driving efficiency at scale across the platform, both for cost optimization and platform performance. • Build services that support engineering teams across the entirety of Qualtrics. Things You'll Do • Influence the way that engineers at Qualtrics running applications through best-in-class tooling, and services • You will create, deploy, and operate services that ensure our overall platform exceeds goals for availability, scalability, and performance • You will help define how Qualtrics engineers operate containerized workloads • You will architect the services and design the best practices for service discovery for all of Qualtrics engineering • Participate in periodic on-call responsibilities as part of a global team What We're Looking For On Your Resume • 5+ years of experience in developing and operating large scale software systems. • Experience with professional software engineering practices (e.g. unit testing, code reviews, design documents) • Experience in high-level languages like Go and scripting languages like Bash • Experience building software or systems that can serve multiple teams or an entire company. • Experience with Kubernetes (ideally EKS), including familiarity with workload and resource management, nodepool management, and policy enforcement. • Skilled at debugging applications within a Linux environment. • Systems engineering backgrounds are welcome when paired with production software development experience; a background focused entirely on Linux or system administration is not the target profile. What You Should Know About This Team • We work closely with AWS, aligning with our AWS TAMs to understand their internal roadmap of enterprise-focused container orchestration features. • We work at tremendous scale; our services must scale to support the entire landscape of Qualtrics engineering. Thousands of hosts in regions across the globe. • This team sits alongside all of the other internal platforms, infrastructure, and developer experience teams that power Qualtrics.
