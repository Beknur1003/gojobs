Senior Software Engineer We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Universal Asset Insights team, located in Tacoma, WA, reporting to the Senior Director of Engineering. In this role, you will work on Infoblox Universal Asset Insights, which automates the discovery and analysis of both IP-based and non-IP-based assets across public clouds, on-premises networks, IoT/OT devices, and third-party applications. Integrated with Infoblox's critical network services, including DNS, DHCP, and IPAM, it enhances network visibility and IPAM accuracy. You will work closely with Product management & engineering leadership to execute product roadmap & also engineering goals of building scalable, reliable & maintainable software. You are the ideal candidate if you are a great collaborator, strategic thinker, operate independently, and are deadline and results-driven. What you’ll do: • Design and develop cloud hosted Data ingestion, transformation, normalization pipeline built to scale, perform and reliable • Design and build extensible framework that enables low-code/no-code integrations, allowing customers and partners to build new asset sources • Lead cross functional & global engineering teams to collaborate on building re-usable software components that can be delivered as a platform • Drive productivity & innovation through adoption of Agentic/AI Platforms in SDLC including Design, Development, QA, Automation, Deployment, Observability, Maintenance & troubleshooting • Drive Product innovation through adoption of Agentic infrastructure into Product core capabilities including building & productizing MCP Servers, Building Agents, sub-agents, skills etc. • Provide technical leadership to steer product architecture and software design, ensuring the delivery of a scalable, extensible, and reliable system • Collaborate cross-functionally with Product Management and Marketing on roadmap, documentation, and other collateral • Stay up to date on key trends in asset insights, no-code/low-code approaches for remediation, data transformation, ETL, agentic AI, etc. What you’ll bring: • 8+ years of experience in software development using Golang/Go, Java, C++, or C, leveraging microservices-based Docker, Kubernetes, Kafka • 8+ years of experience building large scale SaaS-based Network Management, ETL, asset management, data pipelines with ingestion, transformation & persistence, projection, API & presentation layers • Excellent working knowledge of Data Modeling, APIs, System Architecture & design • Excellent understanding of relational, time-series, NoSQL, Graph databases • Strong hands-on expertise applying agentic and AI‑driven frameworks to elevate engineering productivity, optimize performance, and strengthen system reliability including building Agents, Sub-agents, MCP Servers, Skills etc. • Strong hands-on experience with performance engineering in large scale pipelines and active participation in resolving production incidents • Good understanding of networking like switching, Routing, Security and preferably DNS, DHCP, IPAM, CTEM, CAASM, ITSM • Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams across different time zones • Bachelor’s degree in computer science, computer engineering, or electrical engineering required; master’s degree preferred Be Successful — Your Path First 90 Days: Immerse in our culture, connect with mentors
