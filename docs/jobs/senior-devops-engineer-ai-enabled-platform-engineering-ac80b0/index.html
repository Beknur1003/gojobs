Senior DevOps Engineer AI-Enabled Platform Engineering Focus About EverOps EverOps is the partner engineering teams wish they had but didn't know existed. We embed elite operators directly in our customers' teams to help them ship faster, reduce risk, and optimize cloud spend. We call them TechPods: forward-deployed, outcome-driven, backed by proven playbooks and AI-native workflows. We sit in the sweet spot between strategy-only firms and staff-aug vendors. Not Big 4. Not bodies for hire. Operators who think big and deliver fast. Since 2012, companies like Coinbase, Snowflake, and Peloton have trusted us to scale platforms, cut waste, and modernize delivery. We are an AWS Advanced Tier co-sell partner, and our go-to-market is increasingly AWS-led. Our thesis is sharp: make AI actually run in production on AWS, not in a slide deck. That is the work this role delivers. EverOps has always been 100% remote. Our culture was built for a distributed team to thrive, and this role can be based anywhere in the United States. The Challenge EverOps is looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer who brings deep platform engineering expertise and an AI-native mindset to enterprise cloud infrastructure. This role lives at the intersection of traditional DevOps and the emerging AI platform layer—you’ll build and operate cloud-native platforms where AI/ML workloads are first-class citizens, integrate agentic tooling into delivery workflows, and help our clients operationalize AI capabilities within their existing infrastructure. You’ll manage projects autonomously, leverage strong communication and collaboration, and focus on client satisfaction and high availability. The Mission As a Senior DevOps Engineer at EverOps you will join our U.S.-Based Virtual Operating Center (your home office), working with a team of dynamic engineers to manage and evolve multiple customers’ production cloud environments. You’ll bring AI-native thinking to infrastructure automation—using AI-assisted development tools as standard delivery accelerators, designing platform patterns that support AI workloads alongside traditional services, and contributing to EverOps’ growing AI practice. Our existing team of engineers has a deep understanding of our customer environments and are eager to empower, ramp up, and mentor each new hire so that success is achieved.
