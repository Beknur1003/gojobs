Software Engineer - Backend Developement

HyrMe India опубликовано 30 октября 2026

Go — основной язык

Role Overview : We are looking for a Backend Engineer with 2 - 4 years of experience in backend development and platform engineering. The ideal candidate should have strong programming expertise in Golang (Go), Kafka, Redis, and MySQL, along with hands-on experience in AWS and Kubernetes. The role involves developing scalable backend systems, managing cloud infrastructure, optimizing platform performance, and contributing to reliable deployment and traffic management solutions. Key Responsibilities : - Design, develop, and maintain backend services and automation solutions using Go. - Build and manage event-driven systems using Kafka. - Work with Redis and MySQL for caching, data management, and database optimization. - Maintain and scale cloud infrastructure on AWS and Kubernetes. - Manage traffic routing, deployment strategies, and infrastructure integration. - Improve platform reliability, scalability, performance, and availability. - Collaborate with platform engineering teams on service scaling and infrastructure enhancements. - Contribute to networking, monitoring, and Application Performance Monitoring (APM) initiatives. Required Skills & Experience : - 2 - 4 years of relevant experience in backend development or platform engineering. - Strong hands-on programming experience in Golang (Go). - Good working knowledge of Kafka, Redis, and MySQL. - Practical experience with AWS cloud services and Kubernetes. - Understanding of cloud networking concepts, particularly AWS networking. - Experience in backend-heavy development environments. - Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting abilities.
