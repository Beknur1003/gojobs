Senior Backend Engineer (Anti-Scam 2B Team)

Gogolook_走著瞧股份有限公司 Daan District, Taipei City, Taiwan опубликовано 27 октября 2026

Go — основной язык Senior Полная занятость
AWSAI/LLM
Role Overview: As a Senior Backend Engineer in the Anti-scam 2B team, you will be the primary technical driver across two product lines: 1. Brand Protection & Threat Detection Platform: Delivering real-time scam monitoring, alerting, and risk intelligence capabilities to enterprise clients 2. Scam Intelligence API Platform: Enabling enterprise partners and OEM clients to embed Gogolook's detection capabilities into their own products at scale This is not a maintenance role. You'll co-own architecture decisions, lead feature delivery end-to-end, and work directly with Product and cross-functional partners to shape how enterprise clients interact with Gogolook's fraud prevention ecosystem. What you'll work on: In the next 6 months, you'll be tackling: scaling crawling and data extraction pipelines to support new detection scenarios, redesigning high-throughput API services for greater scalability, and exploring innovative applications of AI/LLM technologies — including agent-based architectures — within our risk detection workflows to improve overall detection effectiveness. Responsibilities 1. Own the backend design, development, and maintenance of enterprise solution products, ensuring system reliability and an outstanding client experience. 2. Design, build, and maintain production-grade web crawling and scraping systems, including anti-detection mechanisms, scheduling, rate limiting, and data normalization pipelines to support brand protection intelligence and fraud ad detection pipelines. 3. Integrate backend services, APIs, and data ETL pipelines to power real-time fraud detection, alerting, and threat analysis. 4. Collaborate directly with PM and design to define and build enterprise console: including workflows, dashboards, and reporting surfaces. 5. Drive performance optimization across services: latency, throughput, and resource efficiency. 6. Leverage AI coding agents: including Claude Code and OpenAI Codex, as core development tools to accelerate engineering velocity and build intelligent, AI-augmented product features. 7. Proactively drive technical debt reduction, architecture improvements, and cross-team knowledge sharing as a natural part of how you work. Minimum Qualifications 1. 4-8 years of backend development experience, with deep proficiency in Golang. 2. Strong system design and architecture skills, with demonstrated ability to build scalable, production-grade services. 3. Proven experience building and maintaining production web crawlers or scraping systems, including handling anti-bot countermeasures, dynamic page rendering (e.g., headless browsers), and large-scale URL processing pipelines. 4. Solid understanding of SQL and NoSQL databases; ability to design and optimize schemas for performance. 5. Hands-on experience with AWS cloud services. 6. Hands-on experience using AI coding agents (e.g., Claude Code, Codex) as part of daily development workflow 7. Practical experience integrating or building features with LLMs. 8. Strong ownership mindset — you raise issues proactively, drive solutions to completion, and communicate blockers clearly. 9. Effective collaborator in cross-functional, hybrid environments. 10. Proficient with Git and modern code review workflows.
