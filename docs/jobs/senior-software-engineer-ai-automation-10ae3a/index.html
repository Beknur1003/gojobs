About NCR VOYIX NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. NCR Voyix is headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia, and serves customers in more than 35 countries worldwide. Senior Software Engineer, AI & Automation Onsite Position: Atlanta, GA We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer with 5+ years of software development experience to join a team building and enhancing products in the payments and commerce space. The ideal candidate brings experience from organizations focused on point-of-sale (POS), payments, fintech, or transaction processing systems, along with deep expertise in modern cloud-native development practices and CI/CD automation. This individual will serve as a technical leader, driving software quality, engineering best practices, and scalable architecture while mentoring team members and contributing to the evolution of our development lifecycle. What You'll Do Technical Leadership & Software Delivery • Lead the translation of business and customer requirements into scalable, maintainable technical solutions. • Serve as a subject matter expert for the team on product functionality, customer needs, and strategic initiatives. • Design, develop, and maintain high-quality software with a focus on reliability, performance, security, and supportability. • Identify opportunities to reuse existing components and services to accelerate delivery and improve consistency across products. • Contribute to architectural decisions and influence product direction through technical leadership and innovation. • Drive the implementation of complex solutions, ensuring appropriate validation, testing, and documentation. Quality Engineering & Automation • Champion automation testing strategies and continuous integration practices across the engineering organization. • Coach and mentor developers on automated testing, code quality, and engineering best practices. • Define and drive quality standards, metrics, and processes to ensure a consistent level of excellence. • Monitor and optimize automated test suites and CI/CD pipelines to maintain efficiency and reliability. • Review build and deployment metrics to proactively identify issues and implement corrective actions. • Lead root cause analysis efforts for production and customer issues and drive long-term improvements to development processes. • Ensure test strategies, test cases, and product documentation evolve alongside product enhancements. Development Lifecycle & Continuous Improvement • Advocate for modern software engineering standards and a disciplined software development lifecycle. • Identify recurring issues, recommend process improvements, and implement corrective actions based on lessons learned. • Guide teams in designing observability, monitoring, logging, and diagnostics capabilities into applications. • Leverage metrics and operational insights to identify performance trends and opportunities for improvement. • Stay current on emerging technologies, tools, and engineering practices, including AI-assisted development capabilities. Collaboration & Customer Focus • Partner closely with Product Management, Customer Support, Professional Services, and other stakeholders to understand customer needs and improve product quality. • Incorporate customer feedback into technical decisions to ensure solutions deliver measurable business value. • Act as a trusted technical resource for resolving complex customer issues and driving enhancements back into the product roadmap. • Mentor and develop engineers through technical coaching, knowledge sharing, and leadership by example. Qualifications Required • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience. • Strong experience building and supporting cloud-native applications and services. • Deep expertise with CI/CD pipelines, automated build systems, and containerized application deployment. • Experience leading technical initiatives and mentoring engineers. • Strong understanding of software architecture, design patterns, and engineering best practices. • Hands-on experience with quality engineering, test automation, and observability. Preferred • Experience working within payments, fintech, transaction processing, or point-of-sale (POS) environments. • Experience building highly available, customer-facing software products. • Experience serving as a technical lead or architect on enterprise-scale applications.
