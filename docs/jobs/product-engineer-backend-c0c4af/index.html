Job Description • Design, develop, customize, and optimize NetSuite solutions, ensuring scalability, maintainability, and performance across business-critical processes. • Collaborate with business stakeholders, product managers, engineers, and system owners to translate operational and business requirements into effective NetSuite solutions. • Take ownership of NetSuite enhancements, customizations, workflows, integrations, and system improvements from planning through deployment while adhering to development best practices. • Develop and maintain integrations between NetSuite and internal or third-party systems, ensuring reliable data exchange and system interoperability. • Continuously improve system performance, automation capabilities, and user experience by identifying opportunities for optimization and innovation. • Support troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and resolution of system issues to ensure business continuity and operational efficiency. • Contribute to the design and development of internal business applications and backend services that interact with NetSuite when required. • Stay updated with NetSuite platform enhancements, ERP best practices, integration technologies, and emerging software development trends to improve business systems capabilities.
