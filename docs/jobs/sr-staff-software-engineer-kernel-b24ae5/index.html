Sr. Staff Software Engineer - Kernel

Nutanix San Jose, United States опубликовано 19 ноября 2026

Гибрид SeniorLead
LinuxPythonRustBlockchainFinTech
Hungry, Humble, Honest, with Heart. The Opportunity Are you a skilled developer with a passion for open source technologies and a deep understanding of kernel level systems? If so, joining our team at Nutanix as a Member of Technical Staff - AHV offers the exciting opportunity to contribute to the development of an enterprise-grade hypervisor, working with cutting-edge technologies and making a significant impact on the advancement of our software solution. About the Team At Nutanix, you will be a part of the Acropolis Hypervisor (AHV) team, a group dispersed across the US and APAC regions. AHV is an enterprise-grade hypervisor designed for Nutanix’s software solution, offering reliability, performance, and scalability ideal for demanding enterprise and private cloud workloads. Your focus will be on Virtual Machine Security and Lifecycle Management, ensuring the design and implementation of consistent and highly scalable distributed services. You will report to the Senior Manager, Engineering. The work setup at Nutanix is hybrid, where team members are expected to come into the office 3 days a week, fostering collaboration and face-to-face interactions. We emphasize minimizing meetings and encourage document-based communication for a more flexible schedule and transparent knowledge sharing. Additionally, we have Nutanix lunch days twice a week, providing a great opportunity for in-person connections with your team and other colleagues. There are no travel requirements for this position at Nutanix, ensuring you can focus on your responsibilities and contribute effectively within the team dynamic. Your Role • Contribute to the development of Nutanix's Acropolis Hypervisor (AHV), design and develop new AHV security features • Maintain and fix issues in AHV security, push for improved reliability, performance, and security of AHV • Interact with open source communities, lead the development of features from concept to market • Collaborate with kernel developers, platform engineers, and the ProdSec team to drive strategic security initiatives. What You Will Bring • Have extensive knowledge of OS internals, particularly UNIX/Linux kernel, and low-level software development experience • Strong knowledge of Linux security models, including discretionary and mandatory access control, capabilities, namespaces, and privilege separation. • Experience with vulnerability analysis, exploit mitigation, security auditing, and security compliance certifications. • Strong proficiency in Python and at least one of C/Golang/Rust, with experience debugging complex system-level issues. Preferred qualifications • Contributions to Linux kernel security, upstream open-source projects, or security-related patches. • Familiarity with vulnerability management and security standards such as CVE, CWE, CVSS, and relevant industry security frameworks • Knowledge of virtualization security, hypervisor isolation, and cloud infrastructure protection. • Understanding of cryptography, secure boot, trusted execution environments, and hardware-assisted security.
