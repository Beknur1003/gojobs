As the IT and digital division of the Schwarz Group, we develop and manage the IT infrastructures for the retail divisions Lidl and Kaufland, as well as Schwarz Production and PreZero. We bundle our core services in the areas of Cloud, Cyber Security, Data & AI, Communication, and Workspace. Join us and contribute to digital sovereignty in Europe. With us, you will work at the intersection of agility and security: You will benefit from fast decision-making processes, enjoy genuine creative freedom in your projects, and be able to build upon the stable foundation of the Schwarz Group. The impact you will create: • You drive the design and development of our Next-Gen STACKIT Private Cloud offering STACKIT Distributed Cloud • You work on a digital-sovereign European cloud provider, following the highest security, scalability and reliability standards. • You help deliver a scalable, secure, and reliable product to our customers. • You continuously automate and optimize all technical processes. • You conduct problem and error analyses and identify sustainable solutions. • Being part of NeoNephos organization: the largest EU open source community for cloud computing, you actively use and participate in the open source project that shape the EU digital sovereignty Experience and skills you will need: • You are an experienced developer with 7+ years of development experience with Golang or similar languages such as Rust, C/C++, Python, etc. • You have proficiency in cloud-native application development and deployment using Kubernetes, Docker, and Helm. • You have an understanding of distributed application development • You possess deep knowledge of the Cloud Native landscape, specifically components within the CNCF ecosystem. • You are skilled in communication frameworks and protocols such as REST, gRPC, etc. • You are a hands-on problem solver with a solid understanding of software design and architectural patterns. • Understanding of cloud network protocols and API is considered a plus - CNI, eBPF, BGP, DNS • Experience in developing Kubernetes operators is considered a plus • Familiarity with Talos Linux, Cilium, CoreDNS, Ceph, and OpenBao/Vault is considered a plus.
