We are seeking a self-driven Platform Engineer II to maintain and improve our cloud infrastructure while supporting environments built to meet government certification requirements. You will own small-to-medium infrastructure projects, collaborate across engineering teams, and use infrastructure as code and automation to support reliable, secure deployments. What You’ll Do • Develop and maintain infrastructure using Terraform, Helm, and Argo CD. • Operate AWS EKS, including autoscaling, resource management, and troubleshooting. • Manage IAM, cross-account access, and secrets using Vault and AWS Secrets Manager. • Automate operational tasks using Ansible, Python, and Bash. • Build production-ready containers and support CI/CD pipelines. • Improve system health through Grafana, CloudWatch, Kibana, dashboards, and alerts. • Support application teams, participate in code reviews, and join the on-call rotation. • Use AI tools to accelerate infrastructure work while validating security and accuracy. What You’ll Need • 2–4 years of experience supporting production cloud infrastructure or CI/CD systems. • Hands-on AWS experience and familiarity with Kubernetes/EKS. • Experience with Terraform, Helm, GitOps, Docker, and CI/CD tools. • Experience with Python or Bash. • Familiarity with IAM, secrets management, databases, and monitoring tools. • Azure, Google Cloud, Golang, and Argo CD experience are a plus. Eligibility Requirements • Must be a U.S. citizen eligible to obtain and maintain a government security clearance or favorable Public Trust determination. • Must pass a federal background investigation and meet applicable residency or onsite requirements. Growth Opportunities • Advanced AWS architecture, networking, and Terraform design. • Stateful services, secrets management, and certificate automation. • FedRAMP High compliance automation and enterprise FinOps.
