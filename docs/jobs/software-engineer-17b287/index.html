The Chrysos commitment and mission: Here at Chrysos we combine science and software to create technology solutions that are changing the global mining industry; both from a productivity and environmental impact perspective. Just like atoms and machines, we believe that everyone is different and how individuals come together is what makes us successful. Headquartered in Adelaide and with team members based in Australia, New Zealand, Europe, Africa, Canada, Suriname, Chile and North America, Chrysos Corporation’s diverse team of scientists, engineers and industry specialists are coming together to build technologies to provide rapid, accurate and safe ore analysis for the global mining industry. The company’s flagship technology, PhotonAssay™, steps around centuries-old chemistry-based assay methods to deliver true ‘atom-counting’ analysis for the measurement of gold and other high-value metals. With successful global installations, Chrysos is taking PhotonAssay™ to customers around the world – Chrysos could take you to places that you might not have even imagined yet. There’s opportunity around every corner and the support to help you achieve your goals. About the role: Reporting to the Software Development Team Leader, the Software Engineer will be responsible for on-going development of the software that runs Chrysos’ PhotonAssay™ minerals analysis systems. This software manages storage of and access to large data sets, provides interfaces for users and engineers, and facilitates local and remote control and fault-finding. The successful candidate will also contribute to the development, testing and implementation of software for new products. The candidate should enjoy working in a fast-paced environment and be enthusiastic about learning new technologies and building high-quality products. What you will do: • Design and implement high-quality, clean and testable software providing user access and control of PhotonAssay™ systems, including the design, development, and maintenance of the underlying infrastructure. • Provide practical software engineering input to the Software Development Team Leader and other technical staff to develop conceptual designs for new products. • Maintain documentation, code review, testing, quality assurance and source code management procedures in line with industry best-practice. • Provide practical assistance in the set-up and commissioning of new products. • Provide ongoing support for production units. • Contribute to a culture that values collaboration, technical excellence and on-going staff learning and development. Who you are: Essential • A tertiary qualification in a software engineering subject, or equivalent experience. • At least 3 years’ experience in a software engineering role, including design and implementation of complex technical applications. • Demonstrated experience developing with full-stack applications. • Strong knowledge and understanding of modern software design and management practices, including Git, Devops (CI/CID) and Agile. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including preparation of technical documentation and manuals. • Experience with writing and maintaining tests. • Experience working within a Linux environment. • Experience working with Docker. Desirable • Experience with PostgreSQL. • Experience with Python and Django. • Experience with frontend frameworks like VueJS. • Experience with Golang. • Experience with cloud based platforms like AWS or Azure. • Attention to detail and highly organised. • Ability to work autonomously for extended periods and to take initiative to achieve goals. • Inventive and creative approach to problem solving; comfortable with ambiguity and open-ended questions. • Enthusiastic and enjoy working in a fast-paced environment. • Be comfortable working in a hybrid environment.
