We are looking for a self-directed Lead Platform Engineer who owns complex infrastructure problems and improves the developer experience. In this role, you will own and operate cloud-native infrastructure and internal developer tooling used by application and service developers. You will work with Infrastructure-as-Code, Immutable Infrastructure, containers, and AWS, and partner with platform and application engineers to reduce friction, strengthen reliability, and raise the bar on how the organization builds and ships software. This role suits engineers who can take ambiguous, high-stakes infrastructure problems and drive them to completion with minimal oversight. Location: Mexico (Remote) This position is open exclusively to candidates who are currently residing in Mexico. Before applying, we encourage you to review our open positions and make sure the location requirements match your current location. Candidates who are not currently based in Mexico will not be contacted for this opportunity. Requirements: • 5+ years of software development and infrastructure experience, with a track record of significant contributions. • Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field. • Hands-on, production-level experience with Kubernetes orchestration and AWS. • Experience operating systems at scale, with a deep understanding of failure modes beyond happy-path design. • Ability to independently scope, break down, and execute complex technical problems with minimal direction. • Strong developer empathy and a genuine passion for improving the developer experience. Nice to have: • Experience with Kubernetes (EKS). • Experience with Terraform and/or Pulumi. • Experience with CircleCI. • Proficiency in Ruby, Golang, or Python. Responsibilities: • Own and evolve significant components of the internal platform-as-a-service, from design through production operation. • Lead complex, ambiguous infrastructure initiatives with minimal oversight, in partnership with platform and application engineers. • Build and maintain tooling that improves developer workflows, deployment reliability, and day-to-day productivity. • Apply and advance best practices for Infrastructure-as-Code, containerized workloads, and CI/CD pipelines. • Use AI-assisted development tools to boost productivity, and explore and integrate LLM-powered solutions that automate internal support and operational workflows. • Drive operational improvements and mentor junior engineers, contributing to both technical execution and team growth.
