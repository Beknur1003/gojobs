The Auth0 platform secures more than 100 million logins each day for customers all around the world. The Platform Infrastructure team enables Auth0 engineers to move faster by giving them tools to easily deploy and manage their services on AWS and Azure. This is a role within a team that has a huge impact. You will get to work with engineers throughout the organization and what you build will be a cutting-edge, foundational piece of the infrastructure that allows Auth0 to scale for years to come. We are looking for Engineers who are passionate about distributed systems, performance and scaling, and delivering customer value. Because we build and support the overall Auth0 platform, the ideal candidate is someone who is passionate about infrastructure, operations, and not intimidated by cross-organization coordination and collaboration. You will: • Develop and maintain a scalable platform runtime using infrastructure-as-code, enabling teams to seamlessly deploy and manage services across Kubernetes, AWS, and Azure. • Tackle highly ambiguous requirements to architect scalable, resilient infrastructure solutions for cross-cutting concerns (e.g., service discovery, routing), while setting platform best practices. • Proactively identify performance bottlenecks and advocate for new technologies to optimize platform resiliency, scale, and cost. • Use observability tools like DataDog and Prometheus to design SLIs and enforce platform SLOs. • Design secure-by-default infrastructure and collaborate with security teams to ensure compliance. • Drive and lead complex, cross-functional initiatives end-to-end, aligning multiple teams around technical strategies and ensuring successful delivery. • Act as a technical leader within the team, actively mentoring and elevating the skills of junior and mid-level engineers. • Use company-approved AI tools for software engineering, operations, research, and analysis. • Participate in the team's on-call rotation. You might be a good fit if you: • 5-8 years of software development experience. • Possess deep technical expertise in Golang, Terraform, Kubernetes, and major cloud providers (AWS/Azure) to build complex, modular infrastructure. • Demonstrable experience architecting distributed systems and making complex technical tradeoffs (e.g., consistency vs. availability, build vs. buy). • Deliver work incrementally, and bring strong operational experience leading incident response, driving RCAs, and implementing preventative measures. • Experiment with AI models, exploring the capabilities of different effort levels and context. • Are comfortable working on a fully-distributed (remote) team.
