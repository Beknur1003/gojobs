About the role As a Software Engineer on the Ads Experience (AdEx) team, you will build and evolve core features, services, and workflows that power Moloco’s web-based campaign management portal, Moloco Ads Manager (our Ads Manager UI). Your work will directly shape how advertisers and internal operators create, configure, monitor, troubleshoot, and understand campaigns, helping them operate more efficiently and confidently on Moloco Ads while driving meaningful user and business impact. The Opportunity • Design, implement, and operate backend services and APIs that support key advertiser and operator workflows in Moloco Ads Manager, including campaign setup, configuration, health monitoring, reporting, alerting, and diagnostics. • Own features or projects end-to-end, from problem framing and requirements review through technical design, implementation, testing, launch, and post-launch iteration. • Partner closely with Product, UX, Data, and ML to turn complex user problems into simple, reliable, and scalable product experiences in the Ads Manager UI and via APIs. • Contribute to analytics and reporting capabilities by integrating with data pipelines and helping deliver clear, actionable views of campaign performance and operational health. • Improve the reliability, performance, and observability of the systems you build through strong engineering practices, including testing, metrics, logging, monitoring, and incident response. • Contribute to raising the team’s engineering bar and support a collaborative culture of technical excellence and continuous improvement. Basic Qualifications (2 Titles) Software Engineer II - Ads Experience • BS/MS in Computer Science or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. • 4+ years of software engineering experience in backend or full‑stack development. • Proficiency in at least one modern programming language such as Java, Go, Kotlin, C++, or Python. • Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and system design, with hands‑on experience building or maintaining web services / APIs. • Experience working with relational or NoSQL databases (e.g., BigQuery, Bigtable, MySQL, PostgreSQL). • Experience owning features or components end‑to‑end under guidance from more senior engineers. • Ability to collaborate effectively in a cross‑functional environment with Product, UX, Data, and ML partners. • Fluent verbal and written communication skills in English; ability to discuss designs, trade‑offs, and timelines with global stakeholders. Senior Software Engineer - Ads Experience • BS/MS in Computer Science or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. • 8+ years of software engineering experience, including significant backend or platform work on user‑facing products. • Proficiency in one or more modern programming languages such as Java, Go, Kotlin, C++, or Python, and experience building large‑scale distributed systems or high‑throughput services. • Strong system design skills, with a track record of leading the design and implementation of complex features or services across multiple components or teams. • Deep experience designing and operating web services / APIs and working with relational or NoSQL databases (e.g., BigQuery, Bigtable, MySQL, PostgreSQL). • Proven experience acting as a project tech lead: breaking down ambiguous problems, aligning stakeholders, and delivering projects on time with high quality. • Demonstrated ability to work effectively with Product, UX, Data, and ML partners, influencing product scope and technical trade‑offs. • Fluent in English, capable of driving technical discussions and decisions with global, cross‑functional stakeholders. Preferred Qualifications • Experience building advertiser-, operator-, or analyst-facing tools such as campaign managers, analytics dashboards, troubleshooting or alerting tools, billing or capacity management systems, or internal operations tools. • Strong exposure to or deep understanding of digital advertising / AdTech, including campaign performance metrics, attribution, reporting dimensions, billing concepts, and advertiser needs. • Experience contributing to analytics and reporting capabilities, including working with data pipelines, schemas, data contracts, SLIs/SLOs, backfill strategies, and latency versus cost trade-offs. • Familiarity with or hands-on experience in cloud platforms and big-data technologies such as GCP, BigQuery, Dataflow, Kafka, and Beam/Spark, especially when integrating them into user-facing products. • Experience working closely with Product, Engineering, UX, Data, or ML teams and incorporating user feedback into product and workflow improvements. • Interest in or demonstrated ability to improve reliability, observability, testing, and overall engineering quality in production environments. • Interest in UX for data-heavy products and in presenting complex operational or performance information in a clear, actionable way. • Prior experience in global or multi-region environments where advertiser needs and regulations differ by market is a plus. • For more senior candidates, experience mentoring engineers and positively influencing team culture and engineering practices is a strong plus.
