About Ubiquiti At Ubiquiti Inc., we create technology platforms for Businesses, Smart Homes, and Internet Service Providers, driven by our goal to connect everyone, everywhere. To date, Ubiquiti has shipped over 100 million devices worldwide, from ISP networking products to next generation of IT solutions. Our growth is made possible by the dedicated team of hundreds behind the scenes. From software developers and product managers to designers and strategists, Team UI is driven to achieve our common goal: Rethinking IT. At Ubiquiti, you’ll heighten your potential and broaden your horizons - all while shaping the future of connectivity. Role Summary As part of the Cloud Services team, you’ll work in a fast-paced, innovation-driven environment. We believe in challenging assumptions, breaking limitations, and creating elegant solutions to complex problems. We’re not just building products—we’re building what’s next. Ready to bring bold ideas to life? Join us and help redefine how the world connects. Responsibilities (What he/she will do after joining UI) • Design, develop, and maintain robust, scalable, and secure open APIs for enterprise applications. • Ensure APIs are well-documented, easy to use, and integrate seamlessly with our UniFi solutions. • Develop and maintain a professional and user-friendly website for external developers. • Implement responsive web design principles to ensure the site is accessible on various devices. • Collaborate with UX/UI designers to create an intuitive and aesthetically pleasing user interface. • Work closely with product management, marketing, and other engineering teams to gather requirements and provide technical solutions. • Coordinate with various teams to track progress and ensure timely delivery of projects. • Take ownership of the entire development lifecycle, from conception to deployment and maintenance. • Continuously monitor and improve the API and website performance, ensuring high availability and reliability. • Follow up on the progress of each stakeholder, ensuring milestones are met and addressing any blockers. Requirement (Must-Haves) • Strong proficiency in Golang and React. • Experience with RESTful API design and development. • Solid understanding of frontend technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frameworks/libraries. • Familiarity with database technologies such as SQL and NoSQL. • Knowledge of AWS services and deployment. • Proven experience in developing and maintaining open APIs for enterprise applications. • Demonstrated experience in fullstack development, with a strong portfolio of web applications. • Experience working in an Agile/Scrum environment. • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills. • Strong problem-solving abilities and attention to detail. • Ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment. • Self-motivated and able to drive projects to completion with minimal supervision. • Proficiency in AI development environments (e.g., Cursor, Claude Code) and expertise in AI development techniques, such as Spec-Driven Development, are required. Preferred Qualifications • Leadership in cross-functional or large-scale projects. • Strong ownership, growth mindset, and ability to thrive in ambiguity.
