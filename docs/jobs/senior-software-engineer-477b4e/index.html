Que Technology Group, Inc., is seeking an experienced Senior Software Developer to take ownership of a Kubernetes-native application that orchestrates and deploys workloads within the cluster it runs in. This role advances a platform that brings together a modern web-based UI/UX, containerized microservices, and cloud-native components. The ideal candidate has strong expertise in Go, TypeScript, Python, and Kubernetes, and is comfortable working with containerization, orchestration, and event-driven systems. Job responsibilities include, but are not limited to: • Own the design, development, and continued advancement of a Kubernetes-native application that orchestrates and deploys workloads within its host cluster • Develop and maintain production backend services in Go, along with supporting components in Python • Build and enhance web-based frontends using TypeScript, HTML, and CSS • Design and implement containerized microservices, RESTful APIs, and event-driven components • Deploy and manage applications using Kubernetes, Helm, and GitOps workflows (ArgoCD) • Support hardware and software integration, infrastructure provisioning, and configuration management with Ansible • Collaborate with multiple teams to ensure operational excellence • Maintain and improve documentation while adhering to internal development standards Required Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related discipline with at least twenty (20) years of software development experience, with a strong focus on backend and cloud-native applications. • An additional four (4) years of Software Engineering experience may be substituted for the degree. • Experience with hardware and software integration, infrastructure, provisioning, Ansible configuration management, Kubernetes, cloud technologies, Rancher, and Containers as a Service (CaaS). • Proficiency in Go (Golang), with practical experience developing production services. • Experience building frontends using TypeScript, HTML, and CSS. • Solid experience with Kubernetes, Helm, and GitOps workflows (ArgoCD). • Familiarity with event-driven architectures and message brokers such as Kafka. • Hands-on experience with container technologies such as Docker, and orchestration via RKE2 or similar. • Strong understanding of system design, RESTful APIs, and microservices. • Familiarity with SQL databases and their integration in containerized environments. • Strong debugging, problem-solving, and communication skills. Preferred Qualifications: • Familiarity with the Atlassian Tool Suite (Jira, Bamboo, Fisheye). • Experience with Agile development. • Experience with Continuous Integration and CI/CD pipelines using Git-based workflows. • Linux or UNIX administration and maintenance experience. • Knowledge of SIGINT systems and dataflows. • Experience with Kafka, NiFi, MetalLB, and MinIO in production environments. • Familiarity with infrastructure-as-code and automation using cloud-init or Terraform. • Background in observability tools such as Prometheus or Grafana. • Experience with the Rancher Suite (Harvester, Longhorn, KubeVirt). • Prior work with AWS (EKS, S3, Lambda, RDS) or other cloud platforms. • Ability to perform well in a team environment. Security Clearance: • Active TS/SCI with Polygraph required. BENEFITS: • Competitive salary • Company Medical/Dental/Vision plans – Company paid • Short-term Disability, Long-term disability and Life Insurance – Company paid • Business/ First Class travel upgrade for 7 hour or longer flights & company card will be provided for expenses • Vacation / Personal days granted at 25 days per year • Paid Federal Holidays – 11 days • $5,000 Annual Professional Development Fund plus 40 paid hours if in class • 401K with 6% company match; all contributions are immediately vested by Employee • Employee will be paid a bonus of $10,000 per employee hired based on their referral • Up to 3 paid Code Red days due to customer closure Que Technology Group, Inc., is an equal opportunity employer, and all qualified applicants will receive consideration for employment without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, pregnancy, age, national origin, disability status, genetic information, protected veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.
