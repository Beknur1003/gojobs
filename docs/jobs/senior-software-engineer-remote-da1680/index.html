More on Cyware: () Built and designed by SecOps practitioners and cybersecurity leaders, Cyware offers multiple technologies within its Cyber Fusion platform, including advanced threat intelligence solutions (TIP) for large and small security teams, vendor-agnostic security automation (SOAR), and purpose-built security case management. About you: • You are driven, inquisitive, proactive, and energetic • You have a growth mindset and are committed to delivering results • You thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment • You understand that building high-quality products means adhering to a high bar where security, auditability, and reliability are not afterthoughts • You have experience with AI-based debugging and development tools and practices Why We’re Hiring We are looking for a passionate back-end developer to build and harden Cyware's Cyber Fusion platform across both commercial enterprise and public sector environments.
