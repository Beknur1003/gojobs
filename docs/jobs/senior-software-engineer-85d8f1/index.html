This team owns the core platform responsible for enabling secure, scalable, and reliable access to Autodesk products and services across millions of users and thousands of enterprise customers. In this role, you will design and develop backend applications orchestrates critical Product Access workflows, including user onboarding, entitlement provisioning, product assignment, authorization, subscription lifecycle automation, and policy-driven access management. The platform serves as foundational infrastructure consumed by Autodesk product teams to deliver secure, reliable, and intelligent access experiences.
