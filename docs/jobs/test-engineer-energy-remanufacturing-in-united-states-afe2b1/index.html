They look forward to learning an incredible amount on the job and is excited to be a part of a small team of EngineersRequirementsBachelor's Degree in Electrical, Mechanical, or Software Engineering, or equivalent experience2+ years of hands-on experience as a Manufacturing Test Software EngineerProficient in test data analysis and automation using tools such as JMP, C/C++, and Perl/Python in both Windows and Linux environmentsFluent in Golang, Python, and JavaScript, with a strong grasp of modern software development practicesExperienced with communication protocols including CAN, EtherCAT, and Unified Diagnostic Services (UDS)Skilled in working with Linux-based systems, Docker containers, and version control tools such as Git, GitHub, or SVNFamiliar with data infrastructure and visualization platforms such as Telegraf, Kafka, and GrafanaExperienced in commissioning test equipment and identifying root causes across tester and product failuresKnowledgeable in debugging and working with communication protocols including CAN, Ethernet, and GPIB
