Job Title: Software Development Engineer - II Department, Level: Viewer Experiences, X21 Location: Onsite, Bangalore Work Type: Full time Job Summary: As an SDE-II, you’ll build backend systems that power the most critical moments in a user’s journey — from opening the app, to experiencing personalized, dynamic content, to experiencing. Your primary focus will be on scaling our platform infrastructure, architecting a state-of-the-art compositor engine, and evolving a multi-widget, server-driven layout system that constructs rich, adaptive pages across all devices. You’ll help ensure that hundreds of millions of users enjoy fast, seamless, and always-on experiences, no matter the time, traffic, or device. About the team: At JioHotstar, the Viewer Experience (VX) org is at the heart of how millions discover, engage with, and fall in love with our platform. We own the end-to-end user journey—from first app launch to daily habit loops—across Search, Personalization, Watch Experience, Interactivity, and more. We blend world-class engineering, ML, design, and data to deliver a seamless, personalized, and engaging OTT experience at massive scale. If you're passionate about building immersive, intelligent, and performant user experiences that delight a billion users, join us in shaping the future of streaming. Reporting To: Engineering Manager Key responsibilities: ● Own scale-critical paths, ensuring the system can handle peak concurrency, multi-region delivery, and freshness guarantees. ● Build the infrastructure & capabilities that serves as the orchestration layer between downstream services and client apps — enabling faster experimentation, seamless rollouts, that works for every device & platform out there. ● Develop the Compositor Engine that fetches and ranks content blocks (widgets/cards), merges metadata, and serves highly contextualized page definitions to clients in milliseconds. ● Collaborate with Product, Data Science, and Frontend teams on use cases like personalized feeds, layouts, and dynamic page composition. ● Improve latency, throughput, and observability across all surfaces where UI is driven from the server — including Homepage, Watch pages, and Detail views. ● Contribute to platform primitives that are used by every other team — including layout DSLs, contract validation, fallback mechanisms, and preview tooling. ● Participate in design discussions, code reviews, and on-call rotations, maintaining high service reliability even during peak traffic. ● Continuously evaluate relevant technologies and influence architecture and design discussions ● Guides and mentors juniors on functional attributes and domain knowledge Skills and attributes for success: ● Strong coding skills (Golang) ● Experience building or working with architectures for server driven & guided frameworks and Orchestrator platforms ● Strong understanding of database and storage fundamentals ● Performance tuning skills — optimizing for low p90 latency, high cache hit ratios, and multi-region delivery. ● Experience with AWS stack Preferred education and experience: Bachelors/Masters in Computer Science or a related field with 2-4 years of experience in backend development. About Us: Perched firmly at the nucleus of spellbinding content and innovative technology, JioStar is a leading global media & entertainment company that is reimagining the way audiences consume entertainment and sports. Its television network and streaming service together reach more than 750 million viewers every week, igniting the dreams and aspirations of hundreds of million people across geographies.
