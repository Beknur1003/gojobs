The role / impact In this role, you will help shape the technical foundation for money movement capabilities while unlocking new product lines globally. You will contribute to the design and development of reliable, scalable, performant, and secure services for the platform, ensuring operational excellence and direct business outcomes. This is a rare chance to join an early-stage initiative, contribute to foundational architectural decisions, and have a visible impact on the future of small businesses. By writing production code and developing proof-of-concepts, you will help solve complex financial technology challenges at a massive scale. The team / how they connect The Payments Platform team is the engine powering money movement across the entire ecosystem, collaborating closely with product, security, risk, and operations. We are a highly collaborative collective that values technical excellence, inclusion, and continuous innovation. Together, we work cross-functionally to translate complex business needs into resilient engineering solutions that support millions of subscribers. The team is currently working on / Initially, you will focus on • Designing and building payment platform capabilities, such as orchestration, risk and fraud prevention, and multi-provider integrations. • Building out a global bill payment product to streamline how small businesses manage their cash flow. • Developing core high-throughput platform services using modern backend stacks alongside AWS. • Integrating and leveraging artificial intelligence capabilities to accelerate development workflows and enhance engineering productivity. Where and how you can work We support a hybrid working model that blends the flexibility of working from home with the collaboration of being in the office. You will spend time connecting with your team in our modern office spaces on designated boost days, balancing focused independent work with in-person connection. Here are some of the things we are looking for • A proven track record of delivering highly available, distributed backend systems and high-throughput services under real-world constraints. • Deep understanding of system architecture, API design, and both SQL and NoSQL databases. • Proficiency in modern backend stacks such as C#, Java, Golang, or C++, alongside cloud-native platforms like AWS. • Passion for staying hands-on by contributing directly to production code, proof-of-concepts, and technical deep dives. • Strong collaborative skills with a desire to mentor fellow team members, share technical knowledge, and foster an inclusive culture. • Demonstrated ability in breaking down complex problems into clear, actionable solutions while driving agile execution to deliver results.
