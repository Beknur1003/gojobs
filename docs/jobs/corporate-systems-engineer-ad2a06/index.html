Job Description: The Corporate Systems Engineer designs and develops innovative, scalable, and robust cloud-based solutions for mission-critical applications. This role leads complex software engineering initiatives across solution design, coding, testing, integration, deployment, monitoring, and support. The position applies advanced technical expertise to solve complex business challenges, drives innovation across cloud platforms and applications, and provides technical guidance and mentoring to project teams and less-experienced engineers. Key Responsibilities • Lead the design and development of complex cloud products, platforms, and subsystems, including solution analysis, coding, testing, integration, and deployment. • Design efficient, scalable, and robust cloud-native solutions using modern architectures, including microservices and containerized, Kubernetes-managed environments. • Review and evaluate software designs, test plans, code, and implementation approaches for compliance with cloud development guidelines and engineering standards. • Analyze complex application, system administration, network, scientific, engineering, and business data-processing problems and implement effective solutions. • Collaborate with project managers and development partners to ensure efficient delivery, deployment, operation, monitoring, and support of cloud engagements. • Drive the integration of new technologies into software systems design and development activities and contribute to innovative HPE Cloud Services offerings. • Represent the engineering team in technical forums, provide expert guidance, and mentor less-experienced team members. Requirements • Bachelor’s or master’s degree in computer science, engineering, information systems, or a closely related quantitative discipline or equivalent experience. • Typically, 7+ years of relevant software development or cloud engineering experience. • Strong programming skills in one or more of the following: Python, Java, Golang, or JavaScript. • Strong understanding of microservice architecture and cloud-native application development. • Experience building and operating applications in containerized, Kubernetes-managed environments. • Experience designing and integrating software systems across multiple platforms into an overall solution architecture. • Experience with software testing methodologies, including writing and executing test plans, developing test scripts, debugging, and evaluating test results. • Demonstrated ability to apply advanced subject matter expertise, exercise independent judgment, solve complex technical problems, and provide technical leadership. Knowledge and Skills • Cloud solution design and end-to-end software development. • Microservices, containerization, Kubernetes, and cloud-native architectures. • Software engineering best practices, coding standards, integration, testing, and quality assurance. • System architecture across multiple platforms and applications. • Technical analysis, problem-solving, risk mitigation, and product quality improvement. • Strong collaboration, communication, technical leadership, and mentoring skills. • Ability to drive innovation and integrate emerging technologies into complex engineering projects.
