Get to Know the Team Safety team at Grab is committed to ensuring the safety of our users across all services. By focusing on consumer safety, we directly impact the lives of millions, preventing incidents and building trust within our ecosystem. Our team develops and implements crucial safety features such as AudioProtect, rider verification, live tracking, and more, creating a safer environment for everyone. Get to Know the Role We are looking for a Senior Fullstack Engineer to join the Safety team under the Integrity Tech Family. You will design and build scalable backend systems and web applications that power essential safety features, from user-facing flows to internal operations tools. You will also focus on processing real-time signals, optimizing distributed systems, and ensuring high reliability to support Grab's mission of creating a safe environment for all users. You will report to the Engineering Manager of the Safety team and collaborate with teams to deliver impactful solutions. The Critical Tasks You Will Perform • Design and implement scalable backend services using Golang to improve availability, latency, and efficiency for Grab's safety features. • Build responsive, accessible web applications using React and TypeScript for safety products and internal operations tools. • Design and own APIs end to end, from backend services to frontend integration. • Collaborate with Product Designers and Product Managers to turn safety requirements into great user experiences. • Develop systems that process real-time signals and orchestrate overlapping safety actions. • Optimize distributed systems for high throughput and reliability across multiple regions. • Debug and resolve production incidents across the stack during on-call shifts. • Conduct code and design reviews, and mentor junior engineers. • Platformize existing safety products for long-term reuse across teams. What Essential Skills You Will Need • A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or related fields. • 4+ years of experience in fullstack software engineering, with strong backend experience in distributed systems environments. • Proficiency in Golang, including concurrency and application optimization. • Proficiency in React, TypeScript, and JavaScript, including component design, state management and web performance. • Strong understanding of algorithms, data structures, and system design principles for building scalable and reliable systems. • Experience with relational (MySQL) and NoSQL (DynamoDB, Redis) databases. • Operational excellence in monitoring, capacity planning and incident response. • Excellent communication and analytical skills. • Adaptability to AI tools and frameworks to enhance development processes and integrate intelligent solutions across the stack.
