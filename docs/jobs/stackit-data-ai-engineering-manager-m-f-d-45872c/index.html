Schwarz Digits creates the technological foundation for digital sovereignty in Europe. As the IT and digital division of the Schwarz Group, we develop and manage the IT infrastructures for the retail divisions Lidl and Kaufland, as well as Schwarz Production and PreZero. At the same time, we operate as an independent provider in the external market to support companies across Europe in their digital transformation. We bundle our core services in the areas of Cloud, Cyber Security, Data & AI, Communication, and Workspace. Join us and contribute to digital sovereignty in Europe. With us, you will work at the intersection of agility and security: You will benefit from fast decision-making processes, enjoy genuine creative freedom in your projects, and be able to build upon the stable foundation of the Schwarz Group. Your Tasks • You encourage the individual strengths of your employees and actively support them in achieving their career goals. • You search and find the right candidates to successfully enable the growth of STACKIT. • You are responsible for the design, development, provision, and operation of our Data & AI products and are significantly involved in the creation and validation of system designs and the operational performance of the services. • You conduct code reviews, thereby ensuring that the code meets our requirements regarding scalability, maintainability, and correctness. • You have a comprehensive understanding of your customers' needs, are responsible for the technical vision of the product, and are significantly involved in the roadmap. Your Profile • You are a Servant Leader with a passion for guiding people on their path and supporting them according to their individual needs. • You have the necessary skills to always maintain an overview, even in a fast-paced, complex environment. Even with various, competing priorities, you keep an eye on the big picture and manage to deliver the best possible results. • You have several years of experience in the development and implementation of software products with Golang and Kubernetes. • You possess strong problem-solving and communication skills. • You ideally have experience in the development, architecture, and operation of Cloud Services. • A strong team spirit and the ability to communicate effectively across functions are natural for you. • You are business fluent in English (German is a strong plus). We look forward to receiving your application. Paula Romero Molina · Reference no. 48013 Stiftsbergstraße 1 · 74172 Neckarsulm, Germany
