The Streaming Foundations team builds services and operates data pipeline infrastructure to support event streaming, messaging, and analytics use cases. We are looking for a Software Engineer who is passionate about distributed systems, platform engineering, and solving data-intensive problems at scale. In this high-impact role, you will get to work with engineers throughout the organization to build foundational infrastructure that allows Auth0 to scale for years to come. What you’ll be doing • Contribute to the technical direction of the team and provide input on the engineering roadmap for the Platform's streaming capabilities. • Design and implement high-impact systems, owning features end-to-end from design through delivery. • Participate in architectural discussions that address technical challenges and improve platform scalability. • Collaborate effectively within and across teams on cross-functional projects for successful delivery. • Improve the operational posture of our systems by applying best practices in observability, reliability, and scalability, and sharing that knowledge with teammates. What you’ll bring to our teams • 3+ years of full-time, software development experience in a fast-paced, agile environment • Experience working with Golang or Java is preferred • Hands-on experience developing and tuning highly-scalable, event-driven systems • Solid understanding of database fundamentals and experience with event streaming technologies such as Kafka • A passion and interest to work on systems that are highly reliable, maintainable, scalable and secure.
