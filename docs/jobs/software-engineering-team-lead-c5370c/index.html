About the Role We are looking for an experienced and technically strong Software Engineering Team Lead to lead our engineering team in designing, developing, and delivering scalable, reliable, and high-quality software solutions. This is a technical leadership role that requires a strong combination of hands-on software engineering expertise, software architecture capabilities, and team leadership experience. You will be responsible for driving technical direction, making architectural decisions, establishing engineering standards, and guiding the team in delivering quality software solutions. You should be someone who leads by example, is comfortable diving into complex technical challenges, and can balance technical excellence with business priorities and delivery objectives. Key Responsibilities 1. Technical Leadership & Architecture • Lead the technical direction, architecture, and design of software applications and systems. • Define and establish scalable, maintainable, secure, and reliable software architectures. • Drive architectural decisions and evaluate technologies, frameworks, and tools to meet business and technical requirements. • Define and guide cloud architecture strategies, infrastructure design, and technology choices to ensure scalability, availability, security, and cost efficiency. • Provide technical guidance on system design, coding standards, performance optimization, security, and scalability. • Identify architectural gaps, technical debt, and opportunities for continuous improvement. • Establish and promote engineering best practices, design patterns, and development standards. 2. Software Development & Engineering • Oversee the design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of software applications. • Remain technically hands-on and contribute to critical development tasks, complex problem-solving, and technical reviews when necessary. • Guide the development of web applications, microservices, APIs, database solutions, and backend services. • Conduct and oversee code reviews to ensure code quality, maintainability, performance, and adherence to engineering standards. • Troubleshoot complex technical issues and drive effective, long-term solutions. • Ensure software solutions are designed and implemented with scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability in mind. 3. Team Leadership & Development • Lead, mentor, and develop software engineers across different experience levels. • Provide technical direction and guidance to help the team overcome engineering challenges. • Establish a culture of technical excellence, accountability, collaboration, and continuous learning. • Conduct technical discussions, knowledge-sharing sessions, and design reviews. • Provide constructive feedback and support team members' technical and professional development. • Foster an environment where engineers take ownership of their work and continuously improve their technical capabilities. 4. Engineering Quality & Delivery Ownership • Take ownership of overall engineering quality and ensure solutions meet established technical standards. • Establish and enforce best practices across software development, testing, code reviews, deployment, and maintenance. • Drive improvements in development workflows, engineering productivity, system reliability, and operational efficiency. • Oversee cloud infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and DevOps practices to ensure reliable, secure, and efficient software delivery. • Ensure appropriate technical documentation, system design documentation, and knowledge transfer practices are maintained. • Identify and manage technical risks, dependencies, bottlenecks, and technical debt. • Work closely with project managers, product owners, and stakeholders to ensure technical execution aligns with business objectives and delivery commitments. Requirements & Qualifications • At least 8 years of experience in software engineering, software development, and software architecture. • At least 3 years of experience in a technical leadership role, such as Software Architect, Solutions Architect, Technical Lead, or Engineering Team Lead. • Strong software engineering fundamentals with proven hands-on technical capabilities. • Solid experience designing, developing, and maintaining complex software systems, particularly involving: • Web applications and backend systems. • Microservices and distributed system architectures. • RESTful APIs and system integrations. • Database design, optimization, and management. • Scripting, automation, and backend processing. • Strong understanding of software architecture, design patterns, system integration, and engineering best practices. • Proven ability to make sound architectural and technical decisions and guide engineering teams in implementing them. • Experience conducting code reviews, enforcing coding standards, and driving improvements in software quality. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and troubleshooting skills. • Good communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to engage both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Technical Skills & Technology Stack Experience with some or most of the following technologies is highly desirable: • Programming Languages: Ruby, PHP, Golang, Python, JavaScript. • Frameworks & Platforms: Ruby on Rails, Sinatra, Drupal, React, Node.js. • Databases & Caching: MySQL, PostgreSQL, Oracle, Redis, Memcached, etc. • Cloud Infrastructure & Services: AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Microsoft Azure, etc. • Containerization & Orchestration: Docker, Podman, Kubernetes, etc. • DevOps & Infrastructure Automation: CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), deployment automation, infrastructure monitoring, and configuration management. • Software Architecture: Microservices, RESTful APIs, distributed systems, cloud-native architectures, and system integrations. Candidates are not expected to be experts in every technology listed. However, they should demonstrate strong engineering fundamentals, the ability to understand and work across different technology stacks, and the capability to make sound technical decisions and guide implementation across multiple platforms. What We Are Looking For We are looking for someone who: • Combines strong technical depth with effective leadership capabilities. • Leads by example and is willing to get hands-on with technical challenges when needed. • Can think strategically about software architecture while understanding the practical realities of software implementation. • Takes ownership of engineering quality, technical decisions, and overall system reliability. • Can provide clear technical direction and earn the respect of engineers through technical competence. • Enjoys mentoring and developing engineers while fostering a culture of technical excellence. • Is proactive, adaptable, and passionate about building high-quality software solutions. • Can balance technical excellence, engineering efficiency, and business priorities.
