Are you looking for a place where your work truly matters? At CloudFerro, we build technologies used every day by Europe’s leading scientific and space organisations, including ESA, EUMETSAT, ECMWF, Mercator Ocean International, DLR, and EGI. Working closely with the Earth Observation sector, you will directly contribute to better understanding and analysis of our planet. If you are passionate about modern technologies, large-scale data environments, and want to grow your skills in a place where your work has real impact - we are the right team for you. For more information, visit: Your responsibilities will include: • Ongoing collaboration with developers to automate the software deployment process. • Improving and optimizing CI/CD processes. • Conducting integration tests and performance benchmarks. • Updating system documentation with topics related to application installation and monitoring. • Ensuring proper operation and continuous development of development, testing, and production environments, including application monitoring. • Developing standards for development teams related to effective logging, integrations with monitoring systems, and containerization Team technology stack: Kubernetes, Linux, Terraform, Docker, Ansible, Git, OpenStack, PostgreSQL, Nginx, Prometheus, ELK, AgroCD. What we are looking for: • Basic knowledge of Python. • Experience working with Linux systems. • Interest in automation. • Experience with technologies such as Kubernetes and Ansible. • Ability to communicate comfortably in English. • Ability to work in a hybrid model (one day per week from our office in Warsaw). An additional advantage will be: Knowledge of Ceph, MySQL, Grafana, Redis, as well as programming languages such as C, C++, and Golang, and familiarity with IP networking. What do we offer? • Developing interesting projects based on space exploration. • Working with the best specialists in your field. • Challenging work with new technologies with a full IT background. • Opportunity to implement your own ideas and projects. • Informal and friendly working atmosphere in the office by the Vistula River. • Attractive remuneration based on stable forms of employment. • Employee benefits such as medical care, multisport, life insurance.
