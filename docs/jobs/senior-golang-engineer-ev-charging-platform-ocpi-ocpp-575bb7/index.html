Senior Golang Backend Engineer – EV Charging Platform Role Overview We are looking for a hands-on Senior Golang Backend Engineer to develop, enhance, and support a cloud-native EV Charging platform. The role focuses on building scalable backend services, distributed systems, and event-driven workflows while ensuring high availability and production stability. You will work closely with technical leads, product, infrastructure, and DevOps teams to deliver reliable backend solutions and integrations. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain backend services, RESTful APIs, and microservices using Golang. • Build scalable event-driven and asynchronous workflows for distributed systems. • Improve application performance, scalability, reliability, and availability. • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues, performing root cause analysis where required. • Work with PostgreSQL, including SQL queries, indexing, and performance optimisation. • Develop and maintain integrations with external platforms and third-party systems. • Collaborate with DevOps and infrastructure teams on cloud deployments, monitoring, and operational readiness. • Participate in code reviews and ensure code quality, maintainability, and engineering best practices. • Take ownership of assigned features and technical deliverables from development through production. Must-Have Requirements • Strong hands-on experience developing production applications using Golang (Go). • Experience designing and developing RESTful APIs and microservices. • Good understanding of concurrency, asynchronous processing, and distributed systems. • Strong SQL skills with experience in PostgreSQL or similar relational databases. • Experience with event-streaming technologies such as Kafka. • Hands-on experience troubleshooting and supporting production systems. • Experience with cloud-native environments using AWS or Azure. • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes. • Experience working with distributed, real-time, or high-traffic systems. Good to Have • Beginner to intermediate knowledge of Java. • Experience with observability and monitoring tools such as Datadog or Sentry. • Exposure to Terraform or other Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools. • Experience within EV charging, mobility, IoT, or device-integrated platforms. • Knowledge of EV charging protocols such as OCPI and OCPP. • Experience developing real-time and device-integrated systems. Key Technologies Golang | REST APIs | Microservices | PostgreSQL | Kafka | AWS/Azure | Docker | Kubernetes | Terraform | Datadog | Sentry | Java | OCPI | OCPP
