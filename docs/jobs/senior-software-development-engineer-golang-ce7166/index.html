Go (Golang) Developer - SDE 3 We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Software Development Engineer specializing in Go (Golang) to join our engineering team. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in backend development, with a deep understanding of building scalable, high-performance systems. As an Sr. Golang developer, you will lead projects, mentor junior developers, and collaborate with cross-functional teams to design and develop advanced solutions. Key Responsibilities : - Architect, design, and develop high-performance, scalable, and reliable backend systems using Go (Golang). - Collaborate with product managers, designers, and other engineers to deliver high-quality features and improvements. - Lead technical discussions and design reviews, ensuring high-quality code and adherence to industry best practices. - Optimize existing Go-based applications for scalability, performance, and security. - Implement RESTful APIs and integrate third-party services, ensuring system robustness and responsiveness. - Drive best practices in code quality, testing, CI/CD, and deployment automation. - Troubleshoot and resolve complex issues related to performance, reliability, and scalability in production systems. - Mentor junior and mid-level engineers, providing technical guidance and code reviews. - Stay current with the latest trends and technologies in Golang, microservices, and cloud infrastructure. - Ensure a secure development process by implementing security best practices in coding and system architecture. Required Skills and Qualifications : - 7 - 12 years of hands-on experience in software development, with at least 3 years of professional experience using Go (Golang). - Strong understanding of Go's concurrency model, data structures, and algorithms. - Experience designing and developing RESTful APIs and microservices. - Solid experience with relational (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL databases (MongoDB, Redis). - Strong understanding of distributed systems, networking, and cloud-native architecture. - Experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration tools (Kubernetes). - Experience with continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) pipelines and tools (Jenkins, GitLab, etc.). - Proficient in version control systems like Git. - Deep understanding of software engineering best practices, such as TDD, SOLID principles, and clean code. - Experience with profiling and optimizing Go applications for performance and efficiency. - Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, or Azure. Preferred Skills : - Experience with message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ) and event-driven architecture. - Experience with distributed caching mechanisms like Redis or Memcached. - Familiarity with GraphQL and gRPC. - Experience working in an Agile development environment. - Excellent problem-solving, debugging, and troubleshooting skills. Educational Requirements : - Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
