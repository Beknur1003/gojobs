Software Engineer, Financial Systems (IC3) Madhive is hiring a Software Engineer to join our Financial Systems Engineering team. We’re building TallyIQ, Madhive’s internal finance application, giving Finance, Accounting, and Revenue Operations one place to manage invoicing, adjustments, approvals, reconciliation, and financial reporting. The application also includes financial analytics and decision-support tools, with opportunities to develop AI-powered predictive and prescriptive analytics. The work spans user interfaces, APIs, backend services, and financial business logic, supported by integrations with our proprietary advertising platform, NetSuite, Salesforce, and BigQuery. You’ll report to the Senior Software Engineering Manager for Financial Systems Engineering and work closely with Finance, Revenue Operations, and Product. You’ll help determine what we build next. Working alongside your manager and Product, you’ll meet with Finance and Accounting stakeholders, including the CFO, to understand their challenges, identify opportunities, and turn those needs into application features. The roadmap will continue to evolve as we learn where better software can make the biggest difference. This is a hands-on engineering role with room to grow. You’ll ship features and take increasing ownership with support from your team. Accounting or finance knowledge is strongly preferred, along with curiosity, sound engineering judgment, and an interest in understanding the business behind the software. This role is based out of our New York City headquarters at 225 Broadway. We operate on a hybrid model, and the ideal candidate will work from the NYC office 3 days per week as needed for team collaboration, close cycles, and planning sessions.
