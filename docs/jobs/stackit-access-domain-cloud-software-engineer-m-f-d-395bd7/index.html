Schwarz Digits creates the technological foundation for digital sovereignty in Europe. As the IT and digital division of the Schwarz Group, we develop and manage the IT infrastructures for the retail divisions Lidl and Kaufland, as well as Schwarz Production and PreZero. At the same time, we operate as an independent provider in the external market to support companies across Europe in their digital transformation. We bundle our core services in the areas of Cloud, Cyber Security, Data & AI, Communication, and Workspace. Join us and contribute to digital sovereignty in Europe. With us, you will work at the intersection of agility and security: You will benefit from fast decision-making processes, enjoy genuine creative freedom in your projects, and be able to build upon the stable foundation of the Schwarz Group. Your tasks • End-to-End Ownership: You take full responsibility for designing, developing, improving, and operating a suite of microservices and Kubernetes operators that power our cloud control planes. • Architecture & System Design: You design highly available, scalable, and performant distributed systems. Applying software design patterns and architectural best practices is at the core of everything we build. • Customer-centric APIs: You design and implement robust, scalable, and intuitive REST APIs for our products. • Automation: You develop software to fully automate the provisioning, configuration, and management of our services. • Quality & Maintenance: You champion code quality through code reviews, thorough unit and integration testing, and documentation. • Operations & Reliability: You support your self-developed services in production, proactively analyzing and resolving complex software issues to ensure smooth operations. • Collaboration: You work closely within an agile environment where sharing knowledge is second nature. We tackle challenges together as a unit. Your profile • Culture: We are a group of dedicated team players, not solo superstars. It is highly important to us that you fit our current positive, collaborative team mood and share our expectations and ambitions. We want someone who is kind, supportive, and loves growing alongside their peers. • Programming Mastery: You are an expert developer with strong experience in Golang, as it is one of the most important parts of our tech stack. • Cloud-Native & Kubernetes: You have hands-on experience with containers, Kubernetes, and microservices architectures. If you also have experience developing Kubernetes Operators, we would be thrilled. • System Design Foundation: You have a deep, mandatory understanding of distributed systems and industry best practices and design patterns. • Networking Knowledge: You don't need to be a network engineering guru, but a solid foundation is required. • DevOps Mindset (Recommended): Experience with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring/observability tools is a fantastic bonus. • Education: You have a degree related with Tech, or you are a talented career changer with relevant professional experience. • Languages: English is our default language. If you also speak German or Spanish, that is warmly welcomed. We look forward to receiving your application.
