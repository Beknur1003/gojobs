Senior Software Engineer - Reporting, Insights & Data-In

The role and its impact Reporting, Insights and Data-In builds reporting experiences and shared platform capabilities that help small businesses and their advisors understand how their business is performing and answer meaningful financial questions. The team is moving beyond static reports to more dynamic experiences where customers can explore their numbers, ask new questions and trace results back to source transactions, all while building reusable capabilities used by other product areas such as Payroll, Tax and Inventory. With more than 160 million reports run each month, the work combines customer impact with engineering challenges in performance, financial correctness and scale. As a Senior Engineer, you’ll own reporting initiatives end to end, from design through to production. You’ll build flexible reporting experiences and the shared capabilities behind them, making trade-offs across data-workload performance, calculation correctness and traceability to source transactions. You’ll stay hands-on, partner with Product and engineering teams, and help evolve a reliable reporting platform as the Vancouver team grows. Technical Challenges: • Reporting customers can shape around their needs: Build and improve flexible reporting experiences that help small businesses and their advisors answer practical questions about their finances. • Fast, correct and explainable reporting: Evolve systems to produce timely, accurate results and help customers trace reported figures back to the underlying transactions and financial activity. • Shared reporting foundations: Build reusable reporting services and data capabilities that help product teams deliver consistent experiences. • Modernisation alongside delivery: Improve architecture and developer experience incrementally while continuing to deliver customer value. • AI-assisted reporting and engineering: Explore useful AI-enabled experiences and ways of working, applying sound judgement and protecting customer trust. Here are some of the things we are looking for • Proven track record of delivering highly available, distributed systems and high-throughput services on cloud-native platforms such as AWS, Azure or GCP. • Demonstrated end-to-end ownership of significant work, from design through to rollout and production validation, including the technical decisions that come with it. • Strong systems thinking and engineering judgement, including the ability to make sound architectural trade-offs across APIs, data stores, performance and long-term maintainability. • Proficiency in a modern backend stack, such as Java, Kotlin, Go, Python, Ruby or C++, and you are keen to learn C# and .NET, where existing experience is a bonus. • Passion for staying hands-on, contributing to production code and technical deep dives, and using AI tools with good judgement to move faster and raise the quality bar. • A commitment to Go Together by mentoring peers, sharing technical knowledge and helping teammates grow through code reviews, pairing and documentation. Where and how you can work This role is based in our Vancouver office, where we embrace a hybrid working model. You will have the flexibility to work from home while joining the team in the office for "boost days" (1-2 days a week) to collaborate, socialize, and solve complex problems in person. Our Hiring Process • TX phone screen: a conversation with your Talent Specialist about your background, motivations and the role. • Technical assessment: a coding assessment and systems design assessment, conducted virtually with real engineers, and based on real-world scenarios. • Technical deep dive: walk through a past project you owned end-to-end, covering how you thought about it, executed it and led through its complexity. • Values / Future Proof Xero: how you show up day to day, aligned with Xero's values and capabilities such as adaptability, ownership, customer thinking and AI fluency. Compensation & Total Rewards At Xero, we value the impact and skills you bring to the team. We believe in a Total Package philosophy - ensuring your value is recognized through a holistic lens that includes base salary, performance incentives, and world-class benefits. The expected base salary range for this role is $155,000 - $205,000 CAD. Individual pay is determined by various factors, including geography, level of experience, and the specific skills you bring to the role. In addition to base pay, Xero’s roles are eligible for the following forms of variable compensation: • Variable Pay: Permanent employees are eligible to participate in our annual bonus and equity (RSU) programs. You may also be eligible for performance-based cash or equity (RSUs) incentives depending on your role level, and company performance. • Comprehensive Benefits: World-class health, wellness, and retirement programs.
