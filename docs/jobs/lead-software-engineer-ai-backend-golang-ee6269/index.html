In this role, you will design, build, and scale high-performance backend systems and cloud-native platforms using Golang and modern distributed systems technologies. You will work on engineering complex, data-intensive products that directly create measurable value for enterprise customers. Your work will involve building scalable APIs, distributed services, event-driven systems, reliable data pipelines, and high-throughput backend components that support AI-native SaaS products. You will also contribute to architectural decisions, system design, performance optimization, and engineering best practices across the platform. Responsibilities: • Provide technical leadership for backend engineering initiatives and projects. • Own the architecture and design of large-scale distributed systems. • Create and review High-Level Design (HLD) and Low-Level Design (LLD) documents. • Drive architecture decisions balancing scalability, reliability, security, and cost. • Lead design reviews, architecture reviews, and technical governance processes. • Mentor and coach engineers across multiple experience levels. • Establish engineering best practices, coding standards, and development guidelines. • Perform thorough code reviews and ensure code quality across teams. • Partner with product managers, architects, QA, DevOps, and stakeholders to deliver business outcomes. • Drive technical roadmap planning and technology adoption decisions. • Lead performance optimization, reliability improvements, and production readiness reviews. • Own incident management, root cause analysis, and preventive action planning. • Champion observability, monitoring, logging, tracing, and operational excellence. • Guide capacity planning, resiliency strategies, and disaster recovery planning. • Support hiring, technical interviews, onboarding, and team development initiatives. Requirements: • Expert-level proficiency in Go (Golang). • Strong expertise in data structures, algorithms, and system design. • Hands-on experience creating High-Level Designs (HLD) and Low-Level Designs (LLD). • Deep understanding of distributed systems, microservices, and event-driven architectures. • Advanced knowledge of concurrency, scalability, and fault-tolerant system design. • Strong expertise in API design (REST, gRPC) and integration patterns. • Experience with cloud-native architectures, Kubernetes, containers, and CI/CD pipelines. • Strong knowledge of observability, monitoring, logging, and distributed tracing. • Excellent mentoring, stakeholder management, and technical leadership skills. • Ability to influence architectural decisions and drive engineering excellence across teams.
