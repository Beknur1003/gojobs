About the Role Movate is looking for an AI Engineer with strong expertise in Python and familiarity with the Golang stack to design, develop and deploy production-grade Generative AI and Agentic AI solutions. Candidates with hands-on experience building and deploying Agentic AI applications will be preferred. The ideal candidate should have strong software engineering fundamentals, practical experience integrating Large Language Models into applications, and the ability to build scalable, reliable and secure AI-powered solutions. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop and deploy production-grade Generative AI and Agentic AI applications. • Build AI agents capable of planning, reasoning, executing tasks and interacting with external tools and APIs. • Develop backend services and AI applications primarily using Python, with familiarity in Golang. • Integrate Large Language Models through APIs and frameworks to build intelligent applications. • Implement agent orchestration, tool calling, workflow automation and multi-step reasoning. • Develop Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipelines using embeddings, vector databases and enterprise knowledge sources. • Build and integrate REST APIs and microservices to support AI-powered applications. • Implement prompt engineering, context management, output validation and guardrails. • Optimize AI solutions for latency, scalability, reliability, security and cost efficiency. • Develop automated testing, monitoring, logging and evaluation mechanisms for production AI systems. • Collaborate with product managers, data engineers and software development teams to translate business requirements into AI solutions. • Follow best practices for code quality, version control, CI/CD and production deployment. Document experiments and present technical outcomes to stakeholders. Required Technical Skills • Strong programming expertise in Python. • Familiarity with the Golang (Go) stack. • At least 1+ year of hands-on experience developing production-grade GenAI and/or Agentic AI solutions. • Strong understanding of LLMs, prompt engineering, model integration and inference APIs. • Experience with one or more AI frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex or similar. • Understanding of AI-agent concepts, tool calling, orchestration and workflow management. • Experience developing APIs and backend services using frameworks such as FastAPI or Flask. • Familiarity with Git, Docker, CI/CD and application deployment practices. • Strong debugging, problem-solving and software engineering skills. Preferred Skills • Hands-on experience building and deploying Agentic AI solutions. • Experience with multi-agent systems and agent orchestration frameworks. • Knowledge of RAG, embeddings, vector databases and semantic search. • Familiarity with Model Context Protocol (MCP) and tool integrations. • Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure or GCP. • Knowledge of AI evaluation, observability, guardrails and responsible AI practices. • Exposure to Kubernetes, distributed systems and microservices architecture. Qualifications • Bachelors degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. • Demonstrated ability to develop, deploy and maintain production-grade AI applications. • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
