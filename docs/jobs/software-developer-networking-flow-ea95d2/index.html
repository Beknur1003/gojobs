Hungry, Humble, Honest, with Heart The Opportunity Nutanix engineers are creating a revolutionary technology, building the software-defined data center. We’re using our love of programming and outstanding backgrounds to change the way companies virtualize their data centers. Nutanix, a leader in enterprise cloud, is looking for an outstanding Software Developer to join our Software Defined Security product team, Nutanix Flow. This is an opportunity to join a small yet impactful team where you will tackle seemingly impossible challenges and help build a business from the ground up. You will work on software-defined networking and security solutions that govern environments independent of physical infrastructure, providing granular control and visibility for enterprise applications. About the Team The Nutanix Flow team is a high-impact group focused on delivering advanced networking, security services, and compliance insights for the enterprise. We value big thinkers who are unafraid to take on seemingly impossible challenges and are interested in learning how to build a business along the way. Our team culture is built on collaboration and the open exploration of ideas to deliver granular control for enterprise applications Flow Team’s Tech Stack Development is in a Linux based environment. The backend software is written in Python and Golang. Tools like git, open source libraries etc are used for regular product and test infrastructure development. We work on problems covering aspects of Software defined Networking, Networking security, Offloading AI workloads and enterprise scale. Nutanix Vancouver Nutanix is expanding its engineering presence in Vancouver as part of a long-term commitment to building strong infrastructure and platform teams. This hub brings together engineers working on distributed systems, storage, compute, networking, and platform services. For engineers joining early, this is an opportunity to contribute to meaningful systems while influencing how the team develops. What You Will Bring - BE/BTech/ME/MTech or equivalent in Computer Science. - Strong technical and interpersonal communication (written and spoken). - 2-4 years of software development experience with a focus on Distributed Systems Architecture. - Advanced level programming proficiency in Python, Golang, Java, C, or C++. - Strong fundamentals in Operating Systems, Multi-threaded/Multi-Process Applications, IPC, and RPC. - Desirable to have Networking or Network Security knowledge.
