Senior Data Engineer - PostgreSQL development

Adjust Berlin опубликовано 24 августа 2026

Senior
PostgreSQLPythonAI/LLM
Adjust, an AppLovin (NASDAQ: APP) company, is trusted by marketers around the world to measure and grow their apps across platforms, from mobile to CTV and beyond. Adjust works with companies at every stage of the app marketing journey, from fast-growing digital brands to brick-and-mortar companies launching their first apps. Adjust’s powerful measurement and analytics solutions provide visibility, insights and essential tools that drive better results. Make an Impact/ The role: As a Senior Data Engineer (PostgreSQL development), you will be developing and maintaining databases, aggregation pipelines and extensions for public API directly used by our clients and our internal products. This is a highly scalable product! The API is querying and serving ~5m/req/day from databases distributed over 182 servers. You will be working in a cross-functional team focusing on offering a fast and customer focused experience to our clients. Empower Your Potential/ Responsibilities: • Improve and optimize our architecture and suggest improvements to current processes and structures • Willingness to research and propose cutting edge solution to provide better customer experience • Maintain and expand our database management tools • PostgreSQL maintenance on a very high transaction rate • PostgreSQL custom extensions building and maintaining (C, SQL, PL/pgsql) • Maintain and develop data pipelines (C, Go, Ruby, SQL, PL/pgsql) • Database query development and optimization Bring Your Knowledge/ Experience & Qualifications: • Proven track record as a Data Engineer for high transactional PostgreSQL databases • Knowledge or willingness to learn Go/Python • Knowledge of any Infra management tools (preferably Ansible) • Knowledge of version control system (git) • Knowledge of observability tools • Familiarity with C is a plus • Solid research skills More details about our company culture and perks can be found on our career page.
