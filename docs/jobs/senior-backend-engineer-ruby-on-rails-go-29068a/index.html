Senior Backend Engineer（Ruby on Rails / Go）

Senior English B2
PostgreSQLgRPCKubernetesDockerMicroservicesCI/CDAI/LLM
Avenga is an international engineering firm helping businesses operate with AI at the core. With 6,000+ experts worldwide, we combine engineering expertise with AI-native thinking to turn ambitious ideas into real-world impact across industries, technologies, and markets. At the core of the role We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join a platform modernization project in the sports entertainment industry. The project focuses on transforming an existing Ruby on Rails monolith into a modern, scalable microservices architecture. The goal is to improve system performance, reliability, and maintainability while ensuring a smooth transition without disrupting existing operations. You will work on backend services and internal tools that support real-time operational decisions, including projection management, risk monitoring, alerting, and member operations. The role involves hands-on development, architectural improvements, and close collaboration with cross-functional teams. Core skills you’ll bring • 5–8 years of professional software engineering experience, with strong production experience in Ruby on Rails. • Deep understanding of Rails, ability to write idiomatic Ruby, and knowledge of Rails conventions and best practices. • Proven experience delivering complex, multi-component features in a team environment. • Experience with monolith-to-microservices migrations, service extraction, backward compatibility, and distributed systems. • Solid PostgreSQL knowledge, including schema design, query optimization, and performance tuning. • Experience designing REST APIs and defining service boundaries. • Experience with asynchronous processing, background jobs (Sidekiq or similar), message queues, or event streaming. • Experience with Docker and Kubernetes. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation. • Understanding of observability practices, including logging, metrics, and tracing. • Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to make and communicate technical decisions. • English level Upper-Intermediate or higher Nice-to-have • Experience developing production applications and microservices using Go (Golang). • Experience with event-driven architectures and distributed system design. • Knowledge of gRPC. • Experience with dual-write patterns during system migrations. • Experience using AI-assisted engineering tools. The impact you'll create • Design, develop, and maintain backend services and APIs using Ruby on Rails, with opportunities to contribute to Go-based microservices. • Support the migration from a Rails monolith to independently deployable microservices, contributing to service decomposition and architectural decisions. • Build and maintain internal APIs supporting projection management, risk dashboards, alerting, and member operations. • Own the delivery of complex backend features, from technical design and implementation to deployment and monitoring. • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks, optimize database queries, and improve system reliability. • Contribute to architectural decisions, evaluate technical trade-offs, and communicate recommendations to the team. • Mentor other engineers through code reviews, pair programming, and technical guidance. • Improve engineering practices related to code quality, testing, observability, and deployment automation. • Collaborate with Product Managers, QA Engineers, and other development teams to deliver reliable solutions. • Participate in on-call rotations, troubleshoot production incidents, and contribute to root cause analysis and long-term improvements.
