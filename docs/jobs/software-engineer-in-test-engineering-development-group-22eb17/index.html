Software Engineer in Test - Engineering Development Group

• Deadline: Ongoing • Salary: Competitive • Location: Cambridge • Degree required: Bachelor's • Disciplines: Software, Computer Science, Systems, Web Development, IT Are you interested in making an impact and creating high-quality software? The Engineering Development Group (EDG) is a proven, excellent technical and leadership development programme for early career talent at MathWorks. The Software Engineers in Test within the EDG programme have the flexibility to identify and explore areas of interest within Quality Engineering at MathWorks. While contributing to stimulating work in a fun and collaborative environment, you will gain experience with our products and user workflows through direct interactions with customers. Responsibilities You will receive extensive training, mentoring, and coaching that enables you to transition into teams within the Quality Engineering department aligned with your skillset, interests, and MathWorks business needs. More specifically, you will: • Contribute to high-impact technical projects with teams in the Quality Engineering department that match your background and professional interests. • Build a range of Software Engineering in Test skills, including automated testing, designing and developing test strategies, procedures, and testing frameworks. • Gain an understanding of customer workflows and deep product knowledge by solving complex technical issues for our customers. Work directly with top engineers and scientists that are making an impact with our tools in the world. • Have opportunities to initiate and guide process improvements, and coach and mentor others. • Contribute to developing the highest quality software for MathWorks customers. We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone feels supported. Apply today if you like the idea of working in a team that values your voice, your journey, and your potential! Minimum Qualifications A Bachelor's degree and 3 years of professional work experience (or a Master's degree, or equivalent experience) is required. Additional Qualifications • Master's/Ph.D. in Engineering, Computer Science, or another STEM subject with a consistent, well-rounded academic track record. • Effective problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills. • Curiosity, learning agility, and a willingness to explore new engineering tools and technologies, including AI-assisted workflows. • Experience or interest in software testing and building testing frameworks. • Solid foundational programming skills in at least one language (e.g., C++, JavaScript, Python, Golang, and/or MATLAB). • Experience with MathWorks products, system design and/or object-oriented programming is a plus. Why MathWorks? It's the chance to collaborate with bright, passionate people. It's the opportunity to accelerate the pace of discovery, innovation, and learning in engineering and science. And it’s a commitment to doing the right thing - for each individual, our customers, and the local community.
