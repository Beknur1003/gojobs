We are seeking a highly motivated Senior Engineering Manager to lead our payments engineering team. You will manage a high-performing, agile team of engineers dedicated to building and scaling our payments ecosystem. You will be responsible for the end-to-end architecture, development, and maintenance of our financial technology platform, processing millions of high-volume financial transactions, subscriptions, and payouts for families and caregivers securely and reliably. • Lead a focused, agile team of engineers responsible for the end-to-end management of our payments platform. • Lead the ongoing optimization and advancement of our Stripe-based payment infrastructure to ensure high performance and scalability, following the successful migration from our legacy Vantiv systems. • Design, build, and maintain high-volume, fault-tolerant, and secure payment processing services capable of handling massive transactional throughput. • Oversee the integration and optimization of diverse payment methods, including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and P2P payment solutions, ensuring scalability and strict regulatory compliance. • Partner with Product, Finance, and Compliance stakeholders to translate complex business requirements into technical solutions that ensure regulatory alignment and financial integrity. • Partner with architects to decompose legacy platform components and transition core payment features into a clean, event-driven architecture. • Champion engineering best practices—including observability, testing, performance, scalability, and security across the payments domain. • Mentor and guide the professional and technical development of your team, fostering a culture of high-quality engineering, collaborative problem-solving, and continuous improvement. • Oversee on-call practices, SLAs, and incident management to maintain platform resilience and availability. • 8+ years of professional engineering experience. • 3+ years of experience leading a team (Engineering Manager or Team Lead). • Deep experience building and scaling payments platforms (Stripe, PayPal, ApplePay, ACH), gateway integrations (specifically Stripe) and a strong understanding of navigating legacy payment system migrations (e.g., Vantiv) to support our current architecture. • Solid understanding of payment security, compliance (PCI-DSS), and high-volume transaction architectures. • Experience managing a small, high-performing team and driving technical delivery. • Experience with AWS and Microservices architecture is a plus. • Experience with Java or Golang. • BS or MS in Computer Science or a similar engineering discipline. • Proven success delivering large-scale, consumer-facing systems in a fast-paced environment. • A demonstrated ability to recruit, inspire, and grow engineering talent with a passion for building trustworthy, secure, and frictionless user experiences.
