Role Overview: We’re looking for a hands-on IT Systems Engineer to join our team and own the day-to-day operation and continuous improvement of our corporate IT stack. This is a generalist IC role spanning endpoint management, identity and access management, cloud infrastructure, automation and compliance. You’ll work across a modern, security-first environment and have a direct impact on how the company operates and scales. Key Responsibilities: Endpoint Management • Administer macOS and Windows endpoints using MDM platforms (Jamf Pro, Intune) • Manage device enrollment, configuration profiles, OS patching, and compliance policies • Own the device lifecycle from provisioning through offboarding and retrieval Identity & Access Management • Administer Okta (or equivalent IdP) including SSO, MFA, lifecycle automation, and group policies • Manage provisioning and deprovisioning workflows integrated with HRIS and downstream SaaS apps • Enforce least-privilege access controls across the environment Cloud & Network Infrastructure • Support GCP (or AWS/Azure) infrastructure for corporate IT workloads • Manage VPN, network segmentation, and secure remote access tooling • Write and maintain infrastructure-as-code using Terraform or equivalent Compliance & Security • Support SOC 2, FedRAMP, or CMMC compliance initiatives in partnership with Security • Maintain evidence, controls, and documentation in compliance platforms (e.g. Vanta) • Respond to audit requests and implement remediation for identified gaps Automation & Documentation • Build and maintain automation for IT workflows using Python, Cloud Functions, or workflow tools • Utilize agentic AI for common tasks • Write clear SOPs and runbooks to enable consistent, repeatable operations • Collaborate with Engineering, Security, and HR on cross-functional IT initiatives Required Qualifications: • 3+ years of experience in a corporate IT, IT engineering, or sysadmin role • Hands-on experience with at least one major MDM platform (Jamf Pro, Intune, or equivalent) • Proficiency with a cloud IdP such as Okta, Azure AD, or JumpCloud • Familiarity with at least one public cloud platform (GCP, AWS, or Azure) • Comfort writing scripts or lightweight automation in Python, Bash, or PowerShell • Strong documentation habits and a process-oriented mindset Preferred Qualifications: • Experience supporting compliance frameworks such as SOC 2, FedRAMP, or CMMC • Terraform or other IaC experience for managing cloud resources • Experience with scripting languages such as Python, Shell, and Golang • Exposure to HRIS-driven provisioning integrations (Rippling, Workday, BambooHR, etc.) • Background in a security-conscious or regulated industry (defense, gov-tech, fintech) • Relevant certifications: Google Professional Cloud Engineer, Jamf 200/300, Okta Certified Administrator, CompTIA Security+ • Experience with PAM tooling (Teleport, CyberArk, or similar) • Working knowledge of zero trust networking (Tailscale, Cloudflare Access, etc.) • Contributions to open-source tooling or personal homelab/infrastructure projects What We Offer: • Innovative Environment: Work at a cutting-edge company shaping the future of aerospace communications. • Impactful Work: Directly contribute to critical national security programs and initiatives. • Growth Opportunities: Expand your career with opportunities for professional development and advancement. • Inclusive Culture: Be part of a collaborative, supportive, and inclusive workplace where your contributions matter. • Flexibility: Flexible working arrangements including hybrid remote/in-office schedules. • Compensation and Equity: Competitive salary, comprehensive benefits (401(k), dental, vision, health, life insurance), paid time off, and equity options.
