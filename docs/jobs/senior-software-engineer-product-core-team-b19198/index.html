About the role • What you'll do and for whom. You build and run the core backend service our product teams rely on, from its APIs and persistence layer to email, user provisioning, and cross-tenant capabilities for large enterprises. Through those teams, your work reaches the 20'000+ people who use our Purchase-to-Pay and Order-to-Cash agents every day. • Where you'll work. You join the Product Core Team, about 12 engineers. You work closely with the senior backend engineer who owns these areas today, in a large production Go codebase with well-established patterns, and with the frontend engineers who build the UIs on top of your APIs. • What success looks like. In your first year, you own one core area end-to-end, for example, user provisioning, outbound email, cross-tenant capabilities, analytics, or workflow configuration, from the Go service to the customer using it. What you'll do • Build the service that product teams build on. Design, build, and evolve the core APIs behind document processing, approval workflows, administration, and analytics, with clear contracts so product teams can ship on top of them without workarounds. • Own the persistence layer. Each tenant has its own isolated MySQL database. You evolve the abstraction product teams build on (a tenant-scoped unit of work, typed repositories, and shared type-safe querying) so that isolation, transactions, and permissions are right by default. • Scale BLP for large enterprises. Extend our cross-tenant capabilities so customers with many subsidiaries and business units can manage users, permissions, and documents across all of them from one place, correctly, securely, and fast. • Own email, in and out. Own our email document ingestion and outbound email services, including their audit trail. • Make setup self-service. Turn manual SSO, SCIM, and email setups into migration tools and the APIs behind our admin UIs, so customers and project managers can run them on their own. • Work with customers. Join onboarding calls with customer IT teams and project managers, get their setups running, and turn what you learn into better tooling. • Own it end-to-end. From design through the Go service to production, including tracing, metrics, and incident analysis after it ships. Tech stack: Go, MySQL, Redis, Temporal, Kubernetes on GCP and AWS, GitHub CI/CD, and OpenTelemetry with Grafana, Prometheus, Tempo, and Parca for observability. We're excited about you because • You have 4+ years of experience as a backend developer, building and operating systems in production. • You have 2+ years of professional Go experience, writing services that run in production, not side projects. • You've designed a backend service or API that other teams depended on, and kept it reliable and evolvable as usage grew. • You work well across teams. You align with product, frontend, integration, and infrastructure teams on contracts, priorities, and rollouts, and you can explain technical trade-offs to people who aren't engineers. • You improve existing production systems step by step, and you know when a rewrite isn't worth it. • You have strong fundamentals in type systems, composition, testing, and clean architecture, and you're comfortable working in a codebase built on functional patterns. • You think in products and take ownership. You translate business problems into precise specifications, weigh cost against benefit, work directly with customers when a setup needs it, and stay responsible for what you ship after it goes live. Strong plus • Experience with distributed systems, Kubernetes, Temporal, or cloud infrastructure. • Experience with user provisioning (SCIM) and SSO (OIDC). • Experience running multi-tenant systems. • Domain knowledge in ERP, finance, procurement, or supply chain processes, or German language skills. What you'll get • Ownership of visible outcomes. The areas you own decide how fast enterprise customers go live and how well BLP scales with them. • Impact at scale. Every improvement you ship to the product core multiplies across all product teams and the 20'000+ people who use BLP daily. • Senior scope from day one.
