As a Staff Software Engineer in Container Product Engineering you will design and ship the features, services, and build systems behind Chainguard Containers, and you will lead the small sub-teams of two to four engineers that own them.</p> <p>Chainguard is growing explosively. You find a way, you do not wait to be unblocked, and you unblock others.</li> <li>Judgment on trade-offs at the system level. You have made calls that moved a roadmap, and you can show what you gave up and why.</li> <li>A systems thinker who designs for the whole path: build pipeline, registry, distribution, and the customer's runtime, and who sees second-order effects before they land.</li> <li>AI-native in how you build and lead.
