Technical Responsibilities : - Lead, mentor, and grow a high-performing engineering team by providing technical guidance, architectural direction, and continuous support throughout the product development lifecycle. - Work collaboratively with a core team of architects, DevOps, business teams, and developers spread across different locations to assist with solutions to the requirements from a technical perspective. - Define and drive the long-term technical architecture, ensuring the platform is scalable, resilient, secure, and aligned with future business requirements. - Establish and enforce engineering standards, development processes, coding best practices, and quality assurance frameworks to deliver consistent, maintainable, and high-quality software. - Evaluate emerging technologies, conduct technical research, and develop Proofs of Concept (POCs) to validate new ideas, frameworks, and architectural approaches. - Conduct regular code reviews, architecture reviews, and performance evaluations, providing constructive feedback and coaching to foster technical excellence and professional growth within the engineering team. - Contribute hands-on to the design, development, optimization, and delivery of key product features, technical improvements, and strategic roadmap initiatives. - Drive continuous improvement in system performance, observability, automation, and engineering productivity through proactive optimization and adoption of modern development practices. Skills : - API Development : Building scalable and performant APIs with Python / Golang / NodeJS. - Familiarity with messaging systems (e.g., Kafka, Pub/Sub, or Redis Streams). - Databases : Solid understanding of SQL and NoSQL data stores and schema design. Experience with PostgreSQL (Must), Redis & MongoDB (Optional). - Experience with distributed data processing tools (e.g., Spark, Trino/Presto, or similar). - Experience with in memory data processing frameworks - Pandas, Numpy, DuckDB, Polars, etc. - Data Warehouses : Experience with either Google BigQuery, Clickhouse, Databricks, or Snowflake etc. - Understanding of data modelling, partitioning, indexing, and query optimisation. - Data Pipelines : Develop and manage ETL/ELT pipelines, data models, schemas, and data transformation logic for analytics and operational use. - Implement integrations with third-party APIs and internal microservices. - Collaborate with frontend, DevOps, and data engineering teams to ship end-to-end features. - Experience implementing Data Monitoring tools like Sentry.io, Grafana, Datadog, or similar. - Contribute to CI/CD workflows and infrastructure automation using tools like UV, Poetry, PM2, Docker, and Jenkins. - Experience with containerised environments (Docker) and Serverless architectures. - Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP) and services like S3, Lambda, or Cloud Functions. Requirements : - Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field. - 8+ years of experience with a proven track record of successfully leading cross-functional engineering teams in the development and delivery of complex products. - Cloud Certification in AWS/GCP/Databricks/Snowflake. - Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build rapport and trust with team members and stakeholders. - Proven ability to identify and resolve impediments or conflicts that may arise during the development process. - Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and adapt quickly to changing priorities. - Continuous growth and learner mindset with a passion for Agile principles and practices, and a commitment to ongoing professional development. - Ability to take ownership of complex tasks and deliver while mentoring team members.
