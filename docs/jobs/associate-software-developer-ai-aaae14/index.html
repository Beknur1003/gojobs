At Precisely, we're not just building software — we're shaping the future of data integrity. As a global leader in data quality, data enrichment, and location intelligence, Precisely helps thousands of the world's most trusted brands make confident decisions with data they can rely on. We're an AI-first organization, which means artificial intelligence isn't a buzzword here — it's woven into how we build products, how we work, and how we think about solving complex problems for our customers. When you join Precisely, you join a team of curious, driven innovators who believe that better data makes the world run better. If you're ready to do meaningful work at the intersection of AI and data — and help define what's possible — we'd love for you to apply! Overview: We are seeking a passionate Associate Software Developer (AI) to join our engineering team building scalable applications. This role is ideal for a candidate who is eager to learn, contribute, and grow in a collaborative, fast-paced environment. You will work under the guidance of experienced developers, participate in real projects, and build your skills with modern languages, frameworks, and practices. What you will do: • Assist in designing, developing, and maintaining AI-enabled applications using modern programming languages such as Python, TypeScript, Java, C#, or Go. • Work with team members to understand requirements and implement features. • Learn to use Pydantic for defining and validating data models. • Write clean, well-documented, object-oriented code with guidance. • Participate in code reviews and incorporate feedback. • Help troubleshoot and resolve basic technical issues. • Support testing and CI/CD processes. What we are looking for: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent experience. • At least 1 year of experience building enterprise-level software. • Solid proficiency in at least one modern, general-purpose language, such as Python, TypeScript/JavaScript, Java, C#, or Go. • Working knowledge of object-oriented programming principles and design patterns. • Experience building or consuming REST APIs. • Familiarity with popular frameworks and libraries in your language of choice, such as FastAPI, Django, Flask, Node.js/Express, NestJS, Spring Boot, .NET, or React. • Eagerness to learn new languages, frameworks, and tools as our stack evolves. • Strong problem-solving and communication skills. • Willingness to take initiative and accept feedback. • Ability to work effectively in a team and follow instructions. AI Skills/Knowledge: • Knowledge of large language models (LLMs) and experience with AI coding assistants such as Claude Code or GitHub Copilot.
