Summary Data has never been more valuable and vulnerable. As cybercriminals become more sophisticated and regulations more strict, organizations struggle to answer one key question: “Is my data safe?“ At Varonis, we see the world of cybersecurity differently. Instead of chasing threats, we believe the most practical approach is protecting data from the inside out. We’ve built the industry’s first fully autonomous Data Security Platform to help our customers dramatically reduce risk with minimal human effort. At Varonis, we move fast. We’re an ultra-collaborative company with brilliant people who care deeply about the details. Together, we’re solving interesting and complex puzzles to keep the world’s data safe. We work in a flexible, hybrid model, so you can choose the home-office balance that works best for you. We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer with strong design, coding, and debugging skills to join our Endpoint Management team. You’ll be part of a fast-moving Innovation group, building a new Endpoint Security product in the DLP and AI Security space. Responsibilities • Take end-to-end ownership of complex, high-impact features, from requirements and design through production. • Design, implement, test, deploy, and monitor scalable solutions serving millions of endpoints, while collaborating with teams across the organization. • Maintain a healthy and reliable production environment as the product scales, troubleshooting issues quickly and adapting to evolving priorities and requirements. Requirements • At least 5 years of experience in backend software development. • Experience designing and building complex, large-scale backend systems. • Strong experience with microservices architecture and distributed systems. • Experience with Golang is a significant advantage. • Experience working with cloud platforms; Azure is an advantage. • Strong engineering and architectural understanding, including system design. • Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to collaborate effectively within a team. • Experience in the cybersecurity industry is an advantage.
