Join Axon and be a Force for Good. At Axon, we’re on a mission to Protect Life. We’re explorers, pursuing society’s most critical safety and justice issues with our ecosystem of devices and cloud software. Like our products, we work better together. We connect with candor and care, seeking out diverse perspectives from our customers, communities and each other. Life at Axon is fast-paced, challenging and meaningful. Your Impact As a Sr. Firmware Engineer I on the Platform Firmware team, you are a project technical leader. You lead the technical execution of career-defining programs — from Board Support Package (BSP) development to next-generation Robotics and Camera-based products — setting quality standards, guiding peers, and managing dependencies across Firmware, Electrical Engineering, and other embedded teams. Our firmware environments primarily use Embedded Linux on SoCs/SoMs, with Embedded Android and RTOS-based microcontrollers used across some product lines. Depending on the program, you may work with C, C++, Rust, Golang, or Python. You own full projects or major portions of a multi-engineer project, exercising independent technical judgment across the entire project lifecycle. You build deep expertise in your domain, begin influencing roadmap and architectural decisions, and navigate ambiguity with minimal supervision — coordinating effectively across contributors and stakeholders to get complex, cross-team work done. Where You'll Work Work Location: This role is based out of our Ho Chi Minh City office and follows a hybrid schedule: onsite Tuesday through Friday and remote on Mondays, unless an approved accommodation applies. We place a high value on in-person collaboration and building a strong in-office culture. Travel: Firmware engineering requires hands-on collaboration with Hardware Engineering. While the role is remote near a Local Axon Hub, travel obligations for board bring-up, hardware validation, and manufacturing support are required during critical build milestones. We forecast a modest amount of travel over an 18-month period; the remainder of time is discretionary between home and office. What You'll Do Reports to: Engineering Manager, Platform Firmware Direct Reports: None • Lead the technical execution of firmware projects for current and next-generation Axon devices, setting quality standards and guiding peers. • Own full projects or major portions of multi-engineer projects end-to-end, from design through validation and production support. • Develop firmware for Embedded Linux or Embedded Android on SoCs and SoMs, and for microcontrollers and RTOS-based systems where required. • Drive architecture and design decisions for connectivity technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth/BLE, Sub-GHz, and Cellular LTE/5G. • Set direction for battery, power, and thermal-management subsystems with a critical eye toward safety and reliability. • Lead board bring-up, hardware integration, and debugging efforts in close partnership with Electrical Engineering. • Set testing strategy and maintain advanced unit, integration, and automated tests for scalability, resiliency, and safety-critical behaviors. • Manage cross-team dependencies across Firmware, Embedded Product, Electrical Engineering, Manufacturing, Compliance, and external vendors. • Author and review Software Design Reviews, Pre-Mortems, and After Action Reviews for your projects. • Lead incident resolution and drive improvements in operational readiness for systems in your scope. • Conduct interviews (Engineering with AI and/or system design), primarily for L8/L9 candidates, producing clear, calibrated feedback. • Mentor L7 engineers toward promotion readiness, and proactively share feedback, practices, and context with the team. • Use agentic AI across design, implementation, testing, debugging, and documentation, holding results to architectural, performance, and maintainability standards. • Coordinate multiple AI agents effectively while managing coordination overhead, cost, and reviewability; coach others on validation practices and pilot shared standards for AI-assisted development. What You Bring • B.S. in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent practical experience. • 5++ years of real-world industry firmware or embedded experience developing on IoT products. • Strong programming ability in one or more of C, C++, Rust, Golang, or Python, with the judgment to guide language and tooling choices for a project. • Extensive hands-on experience owning firmware or embedded software projects end-to-end for IoT, consumer electronics, robotics, automotive, networking, or similar products. • Strong experience with both kernel and user-space Embedded Linux, including exposure to Yocto, OpenWRT, or other embedded distros for Board Support Packaging (BSP). • Strong understanding of computer architecture, peripherals, operating systems, concurrency, and networking concepts, with the ability to make sound trade-offs across these domains. • Strong ability to read datasheets and schematics and independently debug issues that span hardware and software. • Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and object-oriented design patterns. • Proven track record setting technical direction, coordinating cross-team dependencies, and navigating ambiguity with minimal supervision. • Experience mentoring engineers and contributing to interview processes. • Working experience using agentic AI tools across design, implementation, testing, debugging, and documentation, holding output to architectural, performance, and maintainability standards. • Experience coordinating multiple AI agents effectively while managing coordination overhead, cost, and reviewability. • Track record of raising team AI effectiveness — coaching others on validation and piloting shared standards for AI-assisted development. Nice to Have • Experience with Embedded Android/AOSP. • Experience with RTOS and microcontroller firmware. • Experience with Cellular LTE, 5G modems. • Experience with battery, power management, safety, and thermal subsystems. • Experience with Robotics and/or 9-Axis Orientation Algorithms. • Experience developing manufacturing, provisioning, or calibration software. • Experience building or extending automated hardware/firmware test systems. • Prior experience leading cross-functional programs or driving architecture/design reviews.
