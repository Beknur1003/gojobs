Meet the Team The Webex Engineering Analytics team is responsible for developing and operating pipelines for Big Data Analytics built on state-of-the-art big data technologies that both solve complex data driven development problems for Webex engineering and enable critical business insights for Webex product. We are looking for a hands-on Software Engineer to join a high-impact data engineering and analytics team with a focus on developing data products and features by leveraging state-of-the-art big data technologies. Key Responsibilities • Build and operate Kafka-based streaming applications, including ingestion, filtering, enrichment, and replication use cases. • Develop data processing jobs using Apache Spark and/or Apache Flink, following established patterns and platform guidelines. • Build high-performant, highly scalable, complex, and distributed systems – end to end. • Share technical solutions and product ideas with the broader team through design review, code review, proof-of-concepts, and show-and-tell sessions. • Participate and contribute ideas to our technology, algorithms, and products. • Work closely with development, test, and various cross-technology teams to design development strategies and plans, followed by designing and writing code reflecting product requirements and functionality. Education • M.Tech/M.E. in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field; or B.Tech/B.E. in a relevant discipline with at least 2 years of relevant professional experience. Required Technical Skills • Solid foundation in data structures, algorithms and object-oriented design • Adept in using AI tools like Copilot, Cursor, Codex, etc., in design/development and testing. • Strong experience in Java, Scala, Golang or other modern programming languages/technologies. • Strong SQL skills and understanding of data modeling for analytical workloads Preferred Skills (Nice to have) • Experience in building large, asynchronous task systems, scalable distributed systems with a good understanding of microservices architecture. • Experience working with Kafka and scaling for high volume workloads. • Practical experience with Apache Spark and Apache Flink. • Practical experience with query engines like Trino/Presto. • Hands-on experience with analytics stores like Apache Pinot/Iceberg for low-latency analytical queries. • Experience with Docker or other configuration management tools. • Experience with cloud stacks like AWS etc Why Cisco?
