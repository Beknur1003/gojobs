As a Senior Software Engineer on the Studio team, you will develop and refactor solutions for Studio SaaS, Bluebeam’s core collaboration product, and Studio Prime, its partner integration platform. Your work will strengthen the stability and reliability of these two platforms, both of which are critical to Bluebeam’s business. Responsibilities: • Performs all task types (architecture, coding, research, and decision) on known and unknown technologies and projects, features, platforms in a way that quickly, efficiently, and effectively arrives at a solution which correctly balances optimal results with timely results according to the needs of the project. • Mentors and supervises the work of less senior developers working on his/her project to effectively arrive at solutions that correctly balance optimal results with timely results according to the project's needs. • Ramps up on new complex problems and code bases to the point of significant project contribution very fast. • Becomes a go-to expert for complex areas of our systems. • Has a deep understanding of the technology used to develop our products • Bachelor’s degree or higher - Computer Science or equivalent field. • 8+ years building high performing/data intensive applications running in the Cloud. • Recent development experience in C# (.NET Framework and/or .NET Core) is an absolute requirement. • Strong experience architecting and building cloud-native solutions leveraging the AWS platform is required. • Must possess a solid understanding of relational SQL databases, preferably Postgres or equivalent. • Solid understanding of the intricacies Entity Framework. • Must possess a solid understanding of NoSQL databases, such as AWS DynamoDB or MongoDB. • Experience working in a fully automated CI/CD environment such GitLab, GitHub, Jenkins. Extra: • Preference given to candidates with experience with GoLang. • Preference given to candidates with AWS Developer Associate certification or above. • Knowledge of IaaS code technology such as Terraform preferrable. • Solid knowledge of microservice architecture, containerization and container orchestration. #Bluebeam #LI-Remote ##LI-TN1 All your information will be kept confidential according to EEO guidelines.
