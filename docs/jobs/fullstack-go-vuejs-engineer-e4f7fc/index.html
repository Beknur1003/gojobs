Job Description We are looking for a mid-level Full-stack Go + Vue Engineerto build backend services and event-driven integrations as part of a team, paired with a senior engineer. You’ll implement and maintain event schemas and data models across services, review AI-generated and peer code for correctness and maintainability, and take part in design discussions, root-cause analysis and production issue resolution. You’ll work closely with product and engineering peers to clarify requirements, switch between languages and codebases as priorities shift, and contribute to the internal tools and integrations. Responsibilities • Build backend services and event-driven integrations as part of a team, paired with a senior engineer • Implement and maintain event schemas and data models across services • Review AI-generated and peer code for correctness, maintainability and data accuracy • Work with product and engineering peers to clarify requirements before and during implementation • Take part in design discussions, root-cause analysis and production issue resolution • Switch between languages and codebases as priorities shift • Contribute to internal tools and integrations that support Viper's provisioning, installation and monitoring Key Requirements • 4+ years of professional software development experience building production backend services • 3+ years of professional backend development experience in Go. Plus hands-on experience with at least one of the following: .NET or Node.js. • Frontend experience with Vue.js • Production experience with AWS services: Lambda, S3, EKS and CloudFront. • Professional experience with Kafka or equivalent event streaming platforms, including topic design and consumer scaling. • Hands-on experience building and consuming GraphQL APIs with Apollo or Hasura. • Strong relational data modeling with working experience in both relational (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL databases. • Experience implementing observability and monitoring with Datadog or equivalent APM tooling. • Experience building and maintaining Jenkins CI/CD pipelines. • Practical use of AI coding tools such as Claude Code to improve development speed. • Strong professional English and cross-functional communication skills.
