Job Summary We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer (Full Stack) to design, develop and scale secure, high-performance digital platforms supporting critical digital infrastructure initiatives. The role involves driving end-to-end solution development, contributing to system architecture, and building scalable, reliable software solutions while promoting engineering best practices and collaborating with cross-functional teams. Mandatory Skill-set • Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, Computer or Electronics Engineering, Information Technology or related field; • Must have min. 5 years of experience in full-stack software development, building, and maintaining production-grade applications; • Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript, React, Node.js and/or Golang; • Solid knowledge of SQL and relational database design; • Hands-on experience managing cloud infrastructure across AWS, GCP or Azure; • Familiarity with Infrastructure-as-Code tools such as Terraform; • Experience developing automated tests, including unit, integration and end-to-end testing; • Proven ability to lead technical initiatives, drive system design and deliver complex engineering solutions; • Strong problem-solving, analytical and communication skills. Desired Skill-set • Experience building AI-native products or developing LLM-powered features; • Familiarity with OLAP or large-scale analytical data stores; • Experience with message queues and event-streaming technologies such as Kafka; • Exposure to distributed systems and high-throughput transactional platforms. Responsibilities • Design and develop scalable, high-performance software services with a focus on business value and usability; • Own the end-to-end design and implementation of complex systems, including technical architecture and documentation; • Drive system design decisions and provide technical direction to the engineering team; • Conduct code reviews, mentor team members and promote engineering best practices; • Work closely with business, product, design and engineering stakeholders to translate requirements into effective solutions; • Champion Agile methodologies and support iterative software delivery; • Leverage AI tools to improve development efficiency while maintaining code quality; • Ensure production services are reliable through effective monitoring, observability, troubleshooting and issue resolution.
