Overview Azure Arc extends Azure services and management capabilities across on-premises, multi-cloud, and edge environments, enabling customers to manage servers, Kubernetes clusters, applications, and infrastructure through a consistent control plane. The platform helps organizations simplify governance, improve operational efficiency, and accelerate innovation regardless of where their workloads run. As a Senior Software Engineer in Azure Arc within Azure Core, you will help build the Kubernetes-based platform that enables Azure services to run beyond traditional cloud boundaries. You will design and develop scalable platform capabilities that allow customers to manage resources consistently across public clouds, datacenters, and edge environments. Working closely with partner teams across Microsoft, including database, application platform, and hybrid infrastructure services, you will help bring Azure experiences to customers wherever their workloads reside. This role offers the opportunity to solve complex distributed systems challenges using technologies such as Go, Python, Kubernetes, Linux, and leading open-source projects. You will play a key role in redefining virtualization for the cloud-native era by building and operating KubeVirt and KVM-based virtualization platforms on Kubernetes, bringing virtual machines and containers together on a unified platform at hyperscale. You'll influence technical direction, collaborate with the open-source community, contribute to highly scalable hybrid cloud infrastructure, and help shape the future of cloud-native management experiences for customers around the world.
