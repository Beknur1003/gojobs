Latam · Remote · Full Time · Technical Engineering Leader · +5 Years of Experience Who We Are At Yuno, we are building the payment infrastructure that enables all companies to participate in the global market. Founded by a team of seasoned experts in the payments and IT industries, Yuno provides a high-performance payment orchestrator. Our technology offers companies access to leading payment capabilities, allowing them to engage customers confidently and maintain global business operations with seamless payment integrations worldwide. About The Role We are orchestrating the best high-performing team! If you are an Engineering Manager passionate about building and scaling world-class teams, joining Yuno will allow you to transform that passion into real high-impact solutions, contributing to our global expansion. As an Engineering Manager, you will lead our Integrations team — a group of engineers responsible for connecting Yuno's platform to PSPs, acquirers, and payment gateways worldwide. You will own the technical direction and delivery of a critical piece of our infrastructure, while growing and developing your team in a fast-moving, global environment. Your Contribution Will Be • Lead the technical evolution of Yuno's merchant experience, focused on Core platform, APIs, and payment integrations • Own system and integration architecture connecting backend services and payment flows • Contribute hands-on to development, architecture decisions, and code reviews • Ensure scalable, maintainable, and well-documented solutions for enterprise merchants • Collaborate with Product, Backend, and Payments teams to align platform and merchant needs • Drive engineering best practices in API design, testing, and performance • Support and mentor engineers through technical guidance and architectural direction Skills You Need Minimum Qualifications • Proven experience managing and growing engineering teams, including hiring, coaching, and performance management • Strong ability to drive technical decision-making and manage competing priorities in a fast-moving environment • Experience in payments companies , payments-related teams or with PSP integrations • Deep experience in payments infrastructure, including network tokens, routing, and transaction optimization • Excellent communication skills to engage with both technical and non-technical stakeholders • Solid hands-on backend engineering experience, with proficiency in Java, Golang, Kotlin or similar languages • Strong understanding of microservices and event-driven architectures, APIs, and integration patterns • Experience with SQL and relational databases • Knowledge of secure software development practices and OWASP principles • Champion of AI-first development — experience setting standards for AI-assisted coding, automated testing, and code generation using LLMs and tools like Claude Code or similar • Experience delivering in agile environments, adapting processes to what actually works for the team • Advanced English; conversational Spanish is a plus What We Offer at Yuno • Competitive Compensation • Remote Work – You can work from everywhere!
