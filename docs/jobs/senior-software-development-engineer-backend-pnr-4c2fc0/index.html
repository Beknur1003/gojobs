JOB DESCRIPTION Senior Software Development Engineer – Backend (PNR API Platform) OVERVIEW OF THE COMPANY Fox Corporation is home to industry-leading brands including FOX News Media, FOX Sports, FOX Entertainment, FOX Television Stations, Tubi Media Group, and FOX One, our recently launched direct-to-consumer (DTC) platform. Across our portfolio, we build and operate large-scale digital platforms that serve millions of users globally. Our teams combine innovative technology, deep data insights, and world-class content to deliver highly personalized, reliable, and scalable consumer experiences across web, mobile, and connected TV platforms. ABOUT THE ROLE As a Senior Software Development Engineer on the Personalization & Recommendations (PNR) API Platform team, you will build and operate the backend systems that power personalized content discovery and recommendation experiences across FOX's digital products. The PNR API platform serves real-time personalized recommendations at scale, combining user behavioral signals, content metadata, vector similarity search, and ML model inference to deliver individualized experiences to millions of users. You will work at the intersection of backend engineering and ML infrastructure - building recommendation engines, reranking services, user profile systems, and model serving pipelines. This role requires engineers who take end-to-end ownership - from understanding the problem and designing systems to implementing, deploying, and operating them in production.
