Andersen is hiring a Go Developer for a project developing a central integration platform and delivering scalable backend solutions for the insurance industry. Our customer is an international organization operating in the financial and protection services domain. The company provides a diverse portfolio of customer-oriented solutions across multiple business areas and markets. With a strong focus on digital capabilities, operational excellence, and long-term development, the organization continuously invests in technology, process improvement, and service innovation to support evolving customer and business needs. The project is focused on developing and maintaining a central integration platform that connects insurance products with multiple business systems and applications. It enables seamless integrations, single sign-on (SSO), and a unified user experience across interconnected services. Responsibilities: • Building and evolving a real-time cross-selling platform, working with real-time data flows and business logic for personalized offers within an insurance context. • Owning the backend powering multiple user-facing apps, advancing our gRPC-based microservice architecture. • Designing and maintaining REST/gRPC APIs for integration with core insurance platforms and third-party services (risk scoring, KYC, payments). • Working with PostgreSQL to model and manage transactional data such as policies, claims, and customer records, including audit trails. • Implementing event-driven workflows using RabbitMQ or Kafka to handle claims, policy updates, and notifications reliably. • Writing and maintaining unit and integration tests, with particular attention to logic involving monetary calculations and claims/premium accuracy. • Instrumenting services with Grafana/Prometheus to monitor critical flows and proactively catch issues before they impact users. • Actively participating in code reviews to ensure the platform and codebase meet the highest quality standards. • Taking a proactive role in all aspects of platform design and development, with a strong emphasis on scalability, availability, and resilience. • Collaborating closely with Business Analysts, QA, and DevOps within an Agile/Scrum framework, translating evolving business and regulatory requirements into technical solutions. • Contributing to architectural decisions that balance flexibility for multiple consuming apps with long-term maintainability and fault tolerance. Must-haves: • Commercial development experience for 5+ years, including 3+ years working with Go in production environments. • Higher education in Computer Science (software development, etc.). • Extensive experience working with AI and the process of implementing/configuring it within a team. • Experience working in fintech, banking domains, and working with complex integrations. • Strong hands-on experience with Golang, including concurrency, for handling high-volume insurance transactions (policy issuance, claims, premium calculations). • Practical experience with Docker for containerized deployments in regulated/enterprise environments. • Solid PostgreSQL skills modeling policy/claims data, handling audit trails and historical records. • Experience with RabbitMQ or Kafka for event-driven workflows (claims events, policy updates, notifications) with reliable delivery guarantees. • Hands-on experience with gRPC/REST APIs, including integration with core insurance platforms and third-party services (KYC, risk scoring, payments). • Strong backend testing discipline, especially for logic involving monetary calculations and claims/premium accuracy. • Practical experience with Grafana/Prometheus for monitoring SLA-critical flows (claims, payments). • Solid understanding of Agile/Scrum. • Proven experience building resilient, fault-tolerant Go applications. • Level of English from Upper-Intermediate+ and above. Nice to have • Experience in insurance domain. • Hands-on experience with Java.
