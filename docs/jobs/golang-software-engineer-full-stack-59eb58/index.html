Salary: $5,000 – $7,000 per month Job Overview • Full Stack Engineer (Golang) • Located at Ang Mo Kio • Mon – Fri (8.00AM to 5.30PM) • Up to $7,000 • Bonuses We are looking for Full Stack Engineers to join an established technology organization in Singapore. You will work on scalable, data-driven applications, with a strong focus on Golang backend development, modern frontend technologies, microservices, and distributed systems. Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications using Golang for backend development and SvelteKit or similar modern frontend frameworks. • Build and optimize GraphQL APIs, microservices, and PostgreSQL databases, including schema design and query performance. • Collaborate with product, design, and operations teams to deliver reliable, scalable, and high-quality software solutions. • Maintain engineering quality through peer reviews, automated testing, CI/CD practices, performance tuning, and architecture improvements. Skills / Experience Required • Minimum 2 years of professional full-stack or backend engineering experience, with strong hands-on Golang development skills. • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline. • Proficiency with SvelteKit or comparable frontend frameworks such as React or Vue, along with JavaScript knowledge. • Strong understanding of microservices, distributed systems, SQL, PostgreSQL and MS SQL, with good problem-solving and debugging abilities. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, testing, and version control; experience with AWS, GraphQL, Node.js, Docker/Kubernetes, Temporal Workflow, C#, observability, or performance optimization is advantageous. Applicants who possess relevant experience for the above responsibilities are most welcome to apply. If you do not possess the above experience, your application will still be considered on individual merits and you may be contacted for other opportunities.
