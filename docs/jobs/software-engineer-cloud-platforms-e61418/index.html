Software Engineer, Cloud Platforms

NVIDIA Israel, Yokneam опубликовано 8 сентября 2026

NVIDIA is looking for an excellent Software Developer to work on open-source cloud platforms such as Kubernetes. We are seeking an engineer who is deeply technical, hands-on, and has a wide system view. You will design, build and deploy high-performance and scalable clouds based on NVIDIA's superior ConnectX NICs and Bluefield DPUs. We are looking to grow our teams with the smartest people in the world. If you're creative and autonomous, we want to hear from you!
