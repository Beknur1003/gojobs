What You’ll Do • Architect a Greenfield System: Lead the greenfield development, owning the architecture and technical vision for the V1 Trust Accounting product. • Build Production-Grade Financial Infrastructure: Design and implement the core backend services for a secure, scalable, and auditable trust accounting subsystem with the highest quality standards. • Drive Quality and Reliability: Establish quality gates, robust testing, and CI/CD integration to ensure every feature ships with measurable confidence and correctness. • Champion Engineering Best Practices: Drive best practices for system design, observability, and security across the organization, with a focus on quality, resilience, and financial accuracy. • Collaborate and Influence: Work closely with product, design, finance, and legal teams to deliver a seamless product. You understand the operational realities of reliability, observability, and failure modes. • Strong Architectural Instincts: You can design systems that start simple and evolve gracefully, making pragmatic trade-offs between speed and extensibility. • Cross-Team Influence: You lead through clarity and credibility, not authority, and have successfully driven the adoption of platforms, patterns, or standards. • Infrastructure Fluency: Comfortable with AWS, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, and operating services in production. • Polyglot Comfort: You have the curiosity and adaptability to integrate with code across these technologies. Nice to have: • Experience with Accounting or Ledger Products: Familiarity with financial systems, accounting principles, or ledger products is a significant plus. • Backend expertise: hands on production experience with Golang. What Success Looks Like • The Trust Accounting subsystem is delivered reliably, is trusted by customers and regulators, and integrates seamlessly into the Hostaway platform. • The system is high-throughput and low-error, providing accurate, auditable data to customers. • Financial correctness and compliance are central to the product's design and operation. • You become a go-to technical authority for financial systems and backend architecture at Hostaway. • The platform scales to meet growing demand without requiring a fundamental re-architecture.
