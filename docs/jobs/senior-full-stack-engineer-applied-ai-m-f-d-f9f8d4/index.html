Senior Full Stack Engineer (Applied AI) (m/f/d) At home24, we aren't just selling furniture; we are engineering the future of how home and living data is handled across Europe. Together with the GIGA purchasing group, we are building GIGA ONE: a new European platform for the entire journey of a product from supplier to retailer. Thousands of suppliers, millions of products and several large retailers work on one platform. The platform goes live in 2027. Our goal is to automate this end-to-end process as far as possible with AI, from simple checks to agents that act on their own, for example: • Onboarding assistants guide suppliers through data entry and follow up when something is missing • Upload agents understand catalogs, price lists and data sheets in any format • Automated checks verify product data, images and certificates • AI creates assembly instructions and translations for all languages • Sourcing agents support buyers with image search, supplier suggestions and offer comparison You will join our central AI engineering team at home24 and work for GIGA ONE, closely integrated with the GIGA ONE team. You build the AI services that execute these use cases, in Go (Golang), the language of our platform, or in Python where it fits best. Your Team You don't need to be an expert in furniture or product data. You are part of the central AI engineering team at home24, closely integrated with the GIGA ONE team. There, you work in a small, high-impact team directly with product managers, business experts and process owners. They know the processes in detail, maintain the test data and sit at the same table with you. No distant stakeholders, no long alignment loops: we decide fast and turn ideas into working software together. Your Responsibilities • Build AI services: You design and implement the services behind our AI features, for example our AI data consistency check, document checks and an upload agent that turns supplier files into structured data. • Own the AI foundation: You set up the shared building blocks for our AI use cases across our platforms, in GIGA ONE as well as on Google Vertex AI: model access, prompt management, vector search and monitoring of quality, cost and latency. • Make quality measurable: You build test data sets and evaluations together with the business team and decide based on data what goes live. • Full-Lifecycle Ownership: You take end-to-end responsibility for your work, from idea to production, rollout and monitoring. You decide, ship a first version fast and improve it. • End-to-end delivery: You build AI features from the service to the screen where suppliers and the central team see the results, together with our front-end engineers. • Modern Tooling: As a proficient AI user, you work with tools like Claude Code or Codex every day. AI writes a large part of the code, and you focus your time on the hard problems: design, quality and decisions. • Quality & Mentorship: You write clean, testable code and share your knowledge with the engineering team through code reviews and knowledge-sharing sessions. Your Profile • Experienced Engineer: You have 8+ years of professional software development, with a focus on backend services and APIs. • Go Specialist: You have 4+ years of professional experience with Go (Golang) in production backend systems within the last five years. • AI in Production: You have built at least one AI-based solution with LLMs or agents and run it in production, and you can show what it achieved. • Python: You use Python confidently alongside Go, for example for AI pipelines, evaluations or document processing. • Cloud AI Platforms: You have hands-on experience with a cloud AI platform, ideally Google Vertex AI (Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform), or a comparable one such as Microsoft Foundry or AWS Bedrock. • Complex System Builder: You have built and operated scalable services and APIs for complex applications. • Quality Mindset: You write clean, testable code and want to make quality measurable, also for AI results. • Infrastructure Savvy: You are comfortable with cloud-based infrastructure (Azure and Google Cloud) and IaC. • Pragmatic: You use a simple rule where it does the job and AI where it adds value, and you explain trade-offs to non-engineers. • Self-driven team player: You take ownership, move fast and like to try new things. You are not afraid to test an idea, be wrong and learn from it, and you share what you learn with the team. • Fluent English • A strong plus: TypeScript and front-end skills to take features end to end, or experience with product data, PIM systems or e-commerce. What we offer you Your impact • Build the AI foundation of GIGA ONE in a small team of engineers who focus on AI • Real impact from day one: your services run for an entire European purchasing group • Freedom to try the newest models and tools, and to decide quickly what works • A small team with short paths and fast decisions Your growth • Extensive learning opportunities from our home24 academy and external partners • Room for growth and development within your role through regular feedback rounds Your path • Hybrid working model: you have the flexibility to work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays in our standard working mode. We are in the office together Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays • The possibility to work up to two months per year remotely within Germany or from an EU country • The opportunity to take up to 30 days paid vacation each year to unwind and recharge • City mobility with a subsidized public transportation ticket and our eco-friendly bike leasing program #ridinghome Your second home • International campus in the heart of Berlin featuring drive-in bicycle garage, outdoor space and lounge areas, fitness facilities, parent and multi-faith rooms close to the Berlin S-Bahn train station Landsberger Allee • Company benefits including a pension scheme, 30% off the home24 assortment, and employee discounts at partner organizations • You want to learn more about our home24 company culture and what it is like to be a Homie? Follow @teamhome24 on Instagram or check out our LinkedIn page Our process is simple: after reviewing your profile and your answers to a few short questions, we invite you to a short phone interview with our Talent Acquisition team. If both sides agree, we send you a practical test task (approx. 1 to 3 hours). You then discuss your solution with our engineering team in a technical interview. If you want to become part of a thrilling success story, we are looking forward to your application. Please send us your application together with your salary expectations and the earliest possible starting date via our career site.
