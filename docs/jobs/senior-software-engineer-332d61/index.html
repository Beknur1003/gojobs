About Us Visa is a world leader in payments technology, facilitating transactions between consumers, merchants, financial institutions and government entities across more than 200 countries and territories, dedicated to uplifting everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid. At Visa, you'll have the opportunity to create impact at scale — tackling meaningful challenges, growing your skills and seeing your contributions impact lives around the world. Join Visa and do work that matters – to you, to your community, and to the world. Progress starts with you. Job Description Visa’s Observability Engineering team builds and operates enterprise-scale monitoring, logging, and tracing platforms that help power Visa’s global payments ecosystem. Our platforms support mission-critical applications and infrastructure, with an emphasis on reliability, security, resiliency, performance, and operational excellence. The Opportunity We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer who can apply software engineering experience and a platform mindset to build and operate large-scale distributed systems. In this hands-on role, you will design and deliver scalable observability solutions, develop automation, and partner with application and infrastructure teams to improve reliability, performance, and operational readiness across Visa’s technology environment. You will leverage development experience and familiarity with cloud-native platforms, observability technologies, and highly available production environments to contribute to secure, always-on digital payments. We welcome candidates with transferable platform engineering experience who are motivated to deepen their observability expertise. Key Responsibilities: Develop, and maintain scalable solutions for monitoring, logging, and tracing across Visa’s hybrid and cloud environments. Build platform services, automation, APIs, and self-service capabilities using languages such as Python, Golang, or Java. Contribute to observability architecture, engineering standards, and practical patterns that improve reliability and developer productivity. Support large-scale deployments, platform modernization, and continuous improvement across enterprise observability platforms. Partner with application and infrastructure teams to advance instrumentation, telemetry adoption, and production readiness for critical Visa services. Apply Infrastructure-as-Code, CI/CD, and GitOps practices to improve platform reliability, repeatability, and efficiency. Participate in root cause analysis, performance optimization, capacity planning, and resiliency improvements for production platforms. Collaborate through design discussions, code reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentorship within observability and platform engineering initiatives. Visa requires at least 3 days in office, expectations of these days will be confirmed by your Hiring Manager.
