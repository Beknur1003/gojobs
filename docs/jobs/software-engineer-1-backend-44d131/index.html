Software Engineer - 1 (Backend)

Parspec Bangalore опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

Полная занятость
MySQLElasticsearchDockerAWSGCPMicroservicesCI/CDPythonAI/LLM
We are seeking a dynamic and experienced Software Engineer to join Parspec's talented engineering team. In this pivotal role, you will blend backend system design expertise, coding proficiency, project leadership, cross-functional collaboration, and mentoring. As a Software Engineer, you will drive the delivery of innovative features while ensuring robustness, scalability, and seamless integration with Parspec's AI-powered platform. You will collaborate closely with Product, Frontend, and AI teams to transform feature ideas into reliable solutions, actively participating in the product lifecycle from estimation to production deployment and ongoing support. What You Will Achieve and Key Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain backend systems (including APIs and messaging solutions) to support new features while ensuring modular code design and maintainability. • Implement design patterns to create systems that deliver high performance with sub-second latency. • Build backend systems capable of scaling efficiently to meet increasing user demands. • Partner with the AI team to deploy, scale, and optimize model inference services in production, balancing latency, throughput, and cost across CPU and GPU workloads. • Lead feature/project lifecycles from estimation to delivery by collaborating with cross-functional teams. • Architect and optimize distributed backend systems for low latency, high availability, and maintainability. • Act as the key point of coordination between Frontend, AI, and Backend teams to deliver cohesive features. • Maintain high standards of code quality through reviews, modular design, and adherence to best practices. • Deploy, maintain, and optimize backend services and infrastructure on AWS/GCP platforms. • Ensure thorough testing of features while tackling debugging challenges in production systems. Why This Matters: Your contributions will be instrumental in building scalable backend systems that power Parspec's AI-driven platform. By delivering high-quality solutions and optimizing infrastructure, you will drive innovation in construction technology while enabling seamless user experiences for a rapidly growing market. Who You Are: You are a skilled backend engineer with 1+ years of experience who thrives in fast-paced environments and has experience working in a start-up. You have a proven track record of leading technical projects while collaborating across teams to deliver impactful results. Minimum Qualifications: • Proficiency in backend software development using Python, Golang, or similar object-oriented languages. • Strong understanding of RESTful APIs, service-oriented architecture (SOA), and microservice architectures. • Hands-on experience with MySQL databases (schema design and query optimization). • Experience deploying and managing services on AWS/GCP platforms. • Familiarity with containerization tools like Docker and CI/CD pipelines. • Skilled in performance optimization and debugging within distributed systems. • Proven ability to lead technical projects and mentor junior team members effectively. Preferred Qualifications: • Exposure to AI/ML pipelines and Elasticsearch integration. • Experience serving ML/LLM models in production using inference frameworks such as NVIDIA Triton, vLLM, TorchServe, or ONNX Runtime, including request batching, autoscaling, and latency/throughput tuning. • Working knowledge of GPU hardware (e.g. NVIDIA T4, L4, A10G, A100/H100): How memory, compute, and precision (FP16/INT8) trade off against cost per inference, and familiarity with optimizations like TensorRT, quantization, and GPU utilization monitoring.
