Summary Data has never been more valuable and vulnerable. As cybercriminals become more sophisticated and regulations more strict, organizations struggle to answer one key question: “Is my data safe?" At Varonis, we see the world of cybersecurity differently. Instead of chasing threats, we believe the most practical approach is protecting data from the inside out. We’ve built the industry’s first fully autonomous Data Security Platform to help our customers dramatically reduce risk with minimal human effort. At Varonis, we move fast. We’re an ultra-collaborative company with brilliant people who care deeply about the details. Together, we’re solving interesting and complex puzzles to keep the world’s data safe. We work in a flexible, hybrid model, so you can choose the home-office balance that works best for you. We are seeking a Senior Backend Engineer to join our team and help build cutting-edge cybersecurity products from the ground up. If you have strong programming skills and a passion for creating robust backend systems, we’d love to meet you. Requirements • 5+ years of experience in building complex backend systems. • Experience in Python / Golang / Java/ C#. • Excellent problem-solving skills and ability to work well in a team. • Experience in the cybersecurity space is an advantage.
