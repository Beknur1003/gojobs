Job Title Software Development Engineering - Advisor II About your role: As a Software Development Engineer, you will design, build, and enhance software for the Finxact Core as a Service platform that supports banking and financial institution services. You will work with business analysts, engineers, and implementation partners to deliver scalable application capabilities, improve software quality, and support cloud-based Software as a Service (SaaS) solutions. Your work helps drive reliable, secure, and effective financial services technology. What you'll do: • Design, develop, test, and enhance application features for the Finxact Core as a Service platform using modern software engineering practices. • Partner with business analysts, engineers, and system users to gather requirements, translate business needs into technical solutions, and support implementation outcomes. • Build and support application programming interfaces (APIs) and service integrations using Representational State Transfer (REST) services, Swagger, JavaScript Object Notation (JSON), and Git-based development workflows. • Improve software quality by increasing automated test coverage, resolving defects, and strengthening application reliability and maintainability. • Provide technical guidance to developers by reviewing designs, supporting solution discussions, and promoting financial services software engineering best practices. • Work with external partners to understand implementation approaches and apply knowledge of cloud-based Software as a Service (SaaS) technologies to solution delivery. • Execute against time-sensitive deliverables by managing multiple development activities, analyzing technical and business information, and adapting to changing priorities. • Responsibilities listed are not intended to be all-inclusive and may be modified as necessary. Experience you'll need to have: • 6+ years of experience in software application design and development within financial services, core banking, loan servicing, deposit operations, card processing, customer relationship management (CRM), or related enterprise platforms. • 6+ years of experience developing and integrating APIs and distributed services using RESTful services, Swagger or similar API documentation tools, JSON, and Git. • 6+ years of experience analyzing business and technical requirements and translating them into application design, development, and implementation outcomes. • Experience leading technical design discussions, reviewing solution approaches, and guiding other developers without direct people management responsibility. • Experience using artificial intelligence (AI)-driven development practices to improve engineering efficiency, code quality, or delivery outcomes. • Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or related field or equivalent combination of education, related experience and/or military experience. Experience that would be great to have: • Experience developing software for cloud-based Software as a Service (SaaS) platforms. • Experience working with core banking platforms, including Finxact. • Experience with Golang or a similar object-oriented or service-oriented programming language. • Experience supporting partner implementations in regulated financial services environments. How you'll work: • This role is on-site Monday through Friday. Fiserv considers in-person collaboration to be an essential part of this role as in-person office experiences help you with your overall onboarding experience and leads to stronger productivity. • This role requires use of a computer and audio equipment.
