SEB Tech Trainee Programme 2027-28: Junior Cloud Platform Engineer | SEB, Stockholm

SEB Stockholm, Stockholm County, Sweden опубликовано 19 ноября 2026

Do you want to question, change, and steer our company into the future? Are you passionate about technology and ready to make a difference? Then you will thrive at SEB. At SEB, we believe in fresh thinking, new working methods, and innovation to create a fairer and more sustainable world. Since SEB was founded in 1856, our focus on customers and long-term relationships has been at the heart of our success, while we place a strong emphasis on sustainability and digitalization as we shape the future of SEB. With the Tech Trainee programme and as an employee, we'll help you develop new skills and take on more responsibility to create a positive impact. You will become a crucial key player in shaping the future of the bank. You tech trainee position and your team Are you a recent graduate passionate about cloud technologies, containers, and modern software development? Do you want to work with cutting-edge technologies such as Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), Kubernetes, Docker, and Go (Golang)? We are looking for a Junior Cloud Platform Engineer to join our team and help build, automate, and operate scalable cloud platforms that support business-critical applications. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who wants to develop deep expertise in cloud infrastructure, container platforms, DevOps practices, and cloud-native development. What You Will Be Doing As a Junior Cloud Platform Engineer, you will: • Learn and work with Google Cloud Platform services • Help manage and develop Kubernetes platforms running on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) • Deploy and operate containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes • Participate in automation and Infrastructure as Code initiatives • Support CI/CD pipelines and application deployment processes • Monitor platform health, performance, and availability • Collaborate with developers, architects, and senior platform engineers • Contribute to internal tools and automation, potentially using Go (Golang) • Continuously learn and explore new cloud-native technologies Simultaneously by working in the role you are being recruited to, you will participate in SEB's Tech Trainee Programme for six months. It will give you the opportunity to develop an extensive network within the bank, gain a deep understanding of the financial industry and our global tech organization, and be professionally and personally challenged. As part of the programme, you can expect to: contribute to meaningful projects, work on tech tracks, gain communication and leadership skills, and participate in a hackathon and a business challenge. Key traits to thrive in this role and programme We’re looking for young talents who are curious, motivated, and ready to question the status quo. To thrive in this role and in the Tech Trainee programme, you need to have: • Graduated within the past two years, or will graduate before the programme starts on 1st September 2027 – from a relevant academic degree or a two-year vocational education in IT or tech. Maybe you have studied computer science, software engineering, information technology, computer engineering or similar. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and the ability to take ownership • Great communication skills and to be able to work in English • Basic understanding of Linux operating systems • Knowledge of networking concepts (TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS) • Familiarity with software development principles We also believe you have: Previous experience (part-time work, internships, personal projects, etc.) and involvement during your studies are advantageous, and may have provided opportunities to work with cloud technologies, Kubernetes, containerization, Go, Python or Bash, Git, CI/CD practices, and Infrastructure as Code tools like Terraform. However, what matters most is your potential and passion. In our recruitment, SEB behaviours are something we consider when choosing whom to hire. These behaviours include creating value, acting long-term, and building positive relationships. What We Offer • A six-month trainee programme, designed for professional and personal development • A full-time employment in the role you have been hired for, after completing the programme successfully • Agile and modern ways of working • A flat hierarchy and openness to share ideas, opinions and points of views • Networking and collaboration opportunities • A real business challenge to solve with other participants in the Tech Trainee programme, where you can create value and make a difference for SEB • Extensive training and learning opportunities Do you want to be a part of SEB? It is our fundamental belief that inclusion and diversity are crucial for our future success. We strive to have an inclusive, value-driven culture where employees feel valued, respected, and involved irrespective of who they are, what they believe or where they come from. The application period for the Tech Trainee programme is open until 10th January 2027 23:59 CET. Please write your CV in English. The Tech Trainee programme will start on 1st September 2027. Our recruitment process We want to get to know you, and for you to get to know us. The recruitment process includes: • Psychometric tests • Interviews – digitally and on-site • A case exercise and presentation • SEB Day – an event to learn more about SEB and the programme, with insights from former trainees Please be aware that our final candidates undergo background checks, a process that includes for example identity control, verification of qualifications, credit checks, company engagements and history of crime. In some cases, we also apply random drug checks. SEB Sweden has a redeployment responsibility, which is why this position might be covered by internal redeployment.
