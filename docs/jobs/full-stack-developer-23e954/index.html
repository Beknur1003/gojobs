Full Stack Developer (FTE) Location: Calgary (Hybrid) Client: Fintech company About the Role Our client, a fintech company supporting global e-commerce, is seeking an experienced Developer to join its Engine team. The Engine powers services across payments, foreign exchange, accounting, remittance, fraud, third-party integrations, and internal tools. You will primarily work with the Engine codebase in Go (Golang), Python, and PL/pgSQL, building and improving the systems behind these services. The ideal candidate is curious, adaptable, and proactive, with a strong sense of ownership and attention to detail. Our client is looking for a collaborative team player who welcomes feedback, enjoys learning, and brings a data-driven approach to solving problems. Responsibilities • Contribute as a collaborative member of a high-performing software development team. • Build, maintain, and enhance the core platform, including APIs, third-party integrations, and databases. • Improve platform quality, maintainability, and performance. • Participate in all phases of the software development lifecycle. • Contribute to the Postman automated testing suite and improve testability and coverage. • Work with stakeholders to understand business problems and deliver practical technical solutions. Technology Stack Go (Golang), C++, Python, PostgreSQL, PL/pgSQL, Vue.js, AWS Lambda, ECS, Docker, and Confluent Kafka. Requirements • At least 3 years of software development experience. • Hands-on experience developing applications in Go (Golang). • Experience with Python, PostgreSQL, and Kafka. • Strong computer science fundamentals through a degree or equivalent experience. • Experience developing and working with REST APIs. • Ability to understand and make changes within an established codebase. • High standards for quality and attention to detail. • Ability to translate stakeholder needs into technical solutions. • Commitment to continuous improvement of systems, processes, and your own skills. • Curiosity, an eagerness to learn, and openness to feedback. Culture and Benefits Our client values practical problem-solving, sound decision-making, initiative, teamwork, and work/life balance. The team focuses on understanding underlying problems and choosing the right technology to solve them. Benefits include: • Competitive salary. • Generous paid time off. • Medical insurance and group benefits. • RRSP matching. • Annual professional development allotment. • A supportive multinational team and collaborative working culture.
