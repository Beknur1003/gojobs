At IBM Software, we transform client challenges into solutions. Building the world's leading AI-powered, cloud-native products that shape the future of business and society. Our legacy of innovation creates endless opportunities for IBMers to learn, grow, and make an impact on a global scale. Working in Software means joining a team fueled by curiosity and collaboration. You'll work with diverse technologies, partners, and industries to design, develop, and deliver solutions that power digital transformation. With a culture that values innovation, growth, and continuous learning, IBM Software places you at the heart of IBM's product and technology landscape. Here, you'l have the tools and opportunities to advance your career while creating software that changes the world. A career in IBM Z is built on shaping critical technology that underpins the global economy. Over 70 percent of global transactional workloads and leading financial, healthcare, and public sector organizations rely on IBM Z and LinuxONE for unmatched security, resilience, and operational scale. As a Senior Software Developer in IBM Z and LinuxONE Security and Automation, you will be a lead technical contributor within a dedicated engineering squad developing next-generation security, identity observability, and runtime governance software for IBM Z. In this senior engineering role, you will bring deep technical expertise in modern open programming languages, including Golang, Python, and Java, and lead the implementation of high-throughput adapters, collectors, and microservices across Linux on s390x and native z/OS environments. You will partner closely with the Lead Architect, drive technical design decisions, mentor development peers, and help bridge open-source observability ecosystems with mission-critical mainframe platforms. Your primary responsibilities will include: - Lead Core Software Development: Architect and implement core modules, high-performance telemetry collectors, and microservices using Golang, Python, and Java, optimizing for concurrent execution, minimal memory overhead, and sub-millisecond latency. - Drive Complex Mainframe Subsystem Integrations: Design and build integration layers interfacing with z/OS UNIX System Services (USS), RACF and SAF security domains, SMF audit streams, IBM MQ, and z/OS Connect. - Implement Zero-Trust Security Patterns: Build secure integration interfaces with enterprise credential systems like Vault on Z, identity access graph engines, and OpenTelemetry pipelines while adhering to strict cryptographic and CVE remediation standards. - Champion Technical Excellence and Code Quality: Lead peer code reviews, establish coding standards, guide architectural refactoring, and implement comprehensive automated unit and integration test suites. - Collaborate Across Technical Boundaries: Work closely with upstream engineering teams, the Lead Architect, and z/OS System Programmers to diagnose complex cross-platform compilation, runtime, and performance bottlenecks across s390x and z/OS. - Mentor and Grow Engineering Talent: Provide technical guidance and mentorship to development squad members, fostering strong software craftsmanship and deep curiosity for IBM Z systems internals. '- Senior Software Engineering Experience: 9+ years of hands-on software development experience building high-scale, resilient enterprise software, microservices, or systems-level software. - Advanced Modern Language Mastery: Deep hands-on proficiency and architectural knowledge in one or more core languages, including Golang, Python, or Java (or C/C++), with expertise in concurrency, memory optimization, and asynchronous design. - Distributed Systems and API Architecture: Extensive experience designing and implementing robust RESTful APIs, gRPC services, and event-driven architectures with high throughput and low latency. - Data Systems and Stream Processing: Practical experience with relational databases (RDBMS), NoSQL datastores, message queues, and high-volume data stream processing. - Linux and Systems Programming Depth: Deep comfort with Linux operating system internals (POSIX standards, socket programming, memory management, multithreading) and container runtimes. - DevOps and Engineering Discipline: Advanced experience with Git workflows, automated CI/CD pipelines, containerization (Docker/Podman/OCI), and test automation. - Passion for Mainframe Systems: A strong enthusiasm and readiness to apply modern systems engineering practices to IBM Z hardware, z/OS operating concepts, and s390x architecture. '- IBM Z and s390x Architecture: Prior hands-on experience with or exposure to Linux on s390x, z/OS, or z/OS UNIX System Services (USS). - Enterprise Security and Credential Management: In-depth understanding of IAM, zero-trust architectures, mutual TLS, PKI, or secret management (e.g., HashiCorp Vault). - Mainframe Security Subsystems: Familiarity with RACF, SAF, or parsing mainframe audit records (SMF record types). - Observability and Event Pipelines: Practical experience building integrations with OpenTelemetry (OTel), Apache Kafka, Prometheus, or distributed tracing engines. - Container Orchestration: Experience building and deploying workloads on Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform or Kubernetes.
