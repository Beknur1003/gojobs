Why Cast AI? Cast AI is an automation platform that operates cloud-native and AI infrastructure at scale. By embedding autonomous decision-making directly into Kubernetes and cloud environments, Cast AI continuously optimizes performance, reliability, and efficiency in production. The old way doesn't work. As Kubernetes and AI environments grow, manual decisions don’t. Cast AI replaces tickets, alerts, and manual tuning with continuous automation that adapts infrastructure as conditions change. Efficiency and cost savings follow naturally from that automation. Over 2,100 companies already rely on Cast AI, including Akamai, BMW, Cisco, FICO, HuggingFace, NielsenIQ, Swisscom, and TGS. Global team, diverse perspectives We're headquartered in Miami, but our impact is international. We take a global and intentional approach to diversity. Today, Cast AI operates across 34 countries spanning Europe, North America, Latin America, and APAC, bringing a wide range of perspectives into how we build and lead. Unicorn momentum In January 2026, we achieved unicorn status with a strategic investment from Pacific Alliance Ventures, the corporate venture arm of Shinsegae Group (a $50+ billion Korean conglomerate). Our valuation now exceeds $1 billion, and we're just getting started. Join us as we build the future of autonomous infrastructure. This is a location-specific opportunity. We are currently accepting applications from candidates residing in the following European countries: Bulgaria, Croatia, Estonia, Greece, Hungary, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Ukraine. What you'll be solving Every dollar Cast AI saves a customer has to be provable, every action taken on their infrastructure has to be attributable to someone authorized to take it, and every price used to make that case has to be current across every cloud, region, and availability zone. This is the trust and accounting layer underneath the whole platform: get it wrong, and customers can't log in, can't trust the savings number, or get billed incorrectly - any one of which ends the relationship. This posting covers multiple teams working on different pieces of the same trust-and-accounting problem: authentication, authorization, and SSO for every user and workload. Billing, audit trails, notifications, and feature flags that keep the platform transparent for every customer. Real-time cloud pricing tracking across every region and availability zone, including Spot availability and discounts. And reporting that turns high-volume time-series data into fast, accurate answers about what the automation actually saved. Nice to haves • Multi-tenant identity system design - authentication, authorization, SSO. • Enterprise identity protocols - SAML, OIDC, and SCIM for enterprise account provisioning and sync. • Billing, audit-trail, notification, or feature-flag systems in production. • Experience with cloud provider pricing/billing APIs across AWS, GCP, or Azure, or tracking Spot instance availability. • High-cardinality time-series ingestion and querying at scale. • Understanding of Kubernetes internals. • CI/CD and DevOps practices experience. • Observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry) in production. Responsibilities • Design, build, and operate backend services in Go that other teams and customers depend on being correct. • Own features end-to-end: from design through implementation, testing, and production rollout. • Debug complex production issues involving security-, money-, or trust-critical flows. • Collaborate with product and other engineering teams to solve problems that don't have textbook solutions. • Contribute to product direction and backlog - shape what gets built next, not just how it's built.
