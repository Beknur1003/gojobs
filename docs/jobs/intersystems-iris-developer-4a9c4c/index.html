Job Description : Job Role : InterSystems IRIS Developer Location : Sydney Job Type : Contract Role Overview We are looking for an experienced InterSystems IRIS Developer with strong backend development experience to work on high-performance, transactional applications within a wagering/gaming environment. The role requires strong hands-on expertise in InterSystems IRIS / Caché / ObjectScript, together with experience deploying and supporting applications within AWS cloud environments. The successful candidate will work on backend platforms requiring high availability, low latency and the ability to process significant transaction volumes during peak sporting and racing events. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop and maintain backend services and transactional applications using InterSystems IRIS, ObjectScript and InterSystems SQL. • Develop and maintain IRIS persistent classes, data models and APIs. • Deploy and manage applications within AWS, including services such as ECS/EKS, EC2 and Lambda. • Containerise applications using Docker and support cloud-based deployment processes. • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, automated testing and production deployment processes. • Optimise database and application performance to support high-volume concurrent transactions. • Develop secure, low-latency REST/GraphQL APIs connecting backend systems with digital platforms and third-party data feeds. • Support integrations with racing/sports data feeds and regulatory systems. • Contribute to architecture and technical design reviews. • Maintain strong code review, engineering and technical governance practices. • Work closely with DevOps, Product and Integration teams. Mandatory Skills & Experience Candidates should have: • Strong commercial development experience with InterSystems IRIS, Caché or Ensemble. • Hands-on experience with ObjectScript and InterSystems SQL. • Strong backend application and transactional database development experience. • Experience deploying and operating applications in AWS. • Exposure to AWS services such as ECS/EKS, EC2, Lambda, IAM, VPC and cloud storage. • Experience with Docker, Git and CI/CD pipelines. • Strong understanding of concurrency, database locking, asynchronous processing and high-volume transactional systems. • Experience developing REST APIs and/or GraphQL APIs. • Strong understanding of highly available and low-latency application architectures. • Good stakeholder communication skills and experience working with DevOps, Product and Engineering teams. Highly Desirable • Previous experience within wagering, sports betting, online gaming, casino gaming or high-frequency fintech environments. • Australian wagering industry experience. • Exposure to Golang, Java or Node.js. • Understanding of Australian wagering compliance, Responsible Gambling frameworks or regulatory reporting. • AWS certification such as AWS Certified Developer or AWS Certified Solutions Architect.
