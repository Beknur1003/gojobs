Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Computer Science or AI-related major is a plus • Hands-on project experience (personal projects, coursework, internships, or open-source contributions all count) • Foundation in at least one backend language: Java or Golang • Familiarity with Node.js and modern frontend frameworks • Experience building end-to-end projects — personal, academic, or internship projects all welcome • Basic understanding of database design and core backend concepts • Familiarity with mainstream AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code, or similar) • Ability to clearly articulate requirements and constraints to AI tools • Self-driven and boundaryless — eager to learn, willing to go beyond assigned scope • Product mindset — curious about the "why" behind what you build • Strong logical thinking and communication skills;
