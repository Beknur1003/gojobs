As a B2B company, we power the retail technology behind digital receipts and secure, legally compliant transactions. And with our paperless receipt solution, we offer consumers a modern alternative while helping merchants streamline their operations. You will join a high-performing squad responsible for our API-first SaaS solutions. Your job is not just to write lines of code; it is to deliver value. We expect you to use every tool in the modern arsenal—including AI coding assistants and automation—to bypass the repetitive parts of software development. This allows you to focus 100% of your brainpower on complex distributed system problems, architecture, and reliability.
