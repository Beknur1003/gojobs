Senior Engineering Manager (Core Services) Full-time · Remote (US) · $200K–$260K + Competitive Equity About this role We're looking for a strong Senior Engineering Manager to lead a backend/platform-focused engineering team responsible for powering our core products. This team owns the backend systems, external APIs, and services that power these experiences, as well as the technical foundation that ensures the product is scalable, reliable, and secure. You will lead a small but growing team of engineers responsible for building API-driven systems, evolving our platform architecture, and enabling product teams to move quickly with confidence. This role sits at the intersection of backend platform development, technical architecture, and people leadership. You'll partner closely with product, data science, infrastructure, and front-end engineering teams to deliver high-quality systems in a fast-moving environment. We are looking for a manager who is fundamentally a strong backend engineer and enjoys staying close to technical work, contributing to architecture, guiding design decisions, and unblocking complex systems challenges. This is a remote, US-based position. Tech stack: React, Golang, Python, PostgreSQL (RDS), Docker, OpenSearch, AWS (Lambda, Cognito, and other standard services) Responsibilities: • Lead and grow a team of backend/platform engineers building the systems that power our core applications • Drive execution across planning, prioritization, and delivery with a focus on reliability and scalability • Own the technical direction of backend services and APIs, ensuring systems are secure, performant, and maintainable • Partner with product and front-end teams to translate product requirements into robust backend architectures • Collaborate with data science to productionize models and integrate decisioning systems into APIs and workflows • Work closely with infrastructure to improve system reliability, observability, and developer experience • Contribute to architectural decisions and guide the long-term evolution of our platform • Maintain high standards for code quality, testing, and operational excellence • Support incident response and ensure systems meet SLA expectations • Hire, mentor, and develop engineers while maintaining a high hiring bar • Foster a culture of ownership, accountability, and continuous improvement • Contribute to code and technical problem-solving as needed
