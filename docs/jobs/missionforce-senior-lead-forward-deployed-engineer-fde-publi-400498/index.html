About the Role As a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) on our Public Sector team, you'll be embedded with U.S. Department of Defense, Intelligence Community, and Aerospace clients — designing and deploying bespoke AI solutions that drive mission-critical outcomes. Additionally you'll architect the backbone of a planet-scale platform that ships hundreds of features daily with rock-solid reliability and security. This role demand a builder's mindset, an AI-first approach, and the technical depth to take complex challenges from concept to production. Mission Responsibilities • Serve as the technical lead on high-profile customer engagements—designing, building, and deploying tailored Salesforce solutions for defense, intelligence, and aerospace missions. • Engineer distributed systems running across thousands of compute nodes on AWS, GCP, Azure, or Alibaba Cloud • Develop Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform), microservices (Kubernetes, Docker), and data pipelines • Build and ship high-quality, production-grade software using modern engineering practices, with AI as a core part of your development workflow by pushing the boundaries of AI development tools to deliver secure, optimized, and high-quality code. • Design and orchestrate complex systems where AI agents integrate seamlessly into human workflows, driving efficiency and innovation at scale. • Contribute to building and maintaining the shared system context, an explicit repository of system designs, constraints, and standards that enables AI to operate accurately and reliably. • Critically evaluate code (human or AI-generated) for correctness, quality, security, and performance. • Partner directly with customers and mission stakeholders to translate complex needs into technical solutions • Lead rapid prototyping, root cause analysis, and best-practice evangelism across teams • Mentor junior engineers and serve as a technical role model What We're Looking For We’re looking for a builder, a problem solver, and a mission-first Leader with a passion for public service.
