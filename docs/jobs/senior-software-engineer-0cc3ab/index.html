About VNT Technologies VNT Technologies is a quantitative research and technology firm focused on developing algorithmic trading infrastructure. We combine mathematical modelling, software engineering, and low-latency technologies to build systematic trading architecture for financial markets. If you’re excited about solving hard technical problems, working with real-time systems, and seeing your code impact markets, you belong with us. Why Join Us • Great minds think alike: a small, exceptional team with opportunities to learn from talented peers. • Real ownership from day one: your work has visible impact on the business. • Hard, interesting problems: a fascinating business domain with no shortage of deep challenges in quantitative research and low-latency engineering. • High performance - high rewards: We set ambitious goals, high standards and recognize strong results with highly competitive base salary and bonus. About the Position We are looking for a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer responsible for developing tools and infrastructure used by Quantitative Researchers. In this role, you will be working with others to provide cutting-edge technology solutions that improve our existing trading architecture. You will have an opportunity to work on high-impact projects that influence the entire organization. How You Will Make an Impact • Develop and maintain algorithmic trading infrastructure • Improve performance and latency characteristics of critical software components • Build and maintain tools for data processing, model evaluation, and system monitoring • Contribute to architectural decisions and long-term technical direction • Collaborate with researchers and engineers on implementation details • Debug and resolve issues in production-grade systems What You Bring to the Team • Deep understanding of one of the following languages: C#, Rust, C++, C, Java, Golang, Assembly. If you use a feature, we expect you to understand how it works and why it exists. • You can explain where performance comes from and why some code is faster or slower — whether for technical or algorithmic reasons. We Would Consider as an Advantage • Familiarity with AWS and Linux • Experience in low-level development • Experience and knowledge in quantitative finance or algorithmic trading What Does Not Matter • Years of experience — mastery takes time, but we don’t put arbitrary numbers on it. • Your previous industry — finance or trading experience is not required. The bar is high, but don’t be intimidated — different people shine in different ways. If you’re curious, driven, and enjoy solving meaningful problems, you’ll fit right in. Life at VNT Technologies • Diverse paths, shared passion. Some of our team are Cambridge and Oxford graduates, International Olympiad medallists and PhDs. Others built exceptional expertise through hands-on experience and deep curiosity. What unites everyone is a passion for solving hard problems. • Work-life balance matters. We expect a significant contribution from every team member, and we don't believe overworked or burned-out people can give us any edge. • A friendly, collaborative and flat culture where good ideas can come from anyone. What We Offer • Base salary range: €5,000 - €10,000 gross per month based on your qualifications, competencies, and professional experience. • Year-end performance-based bonus. • Small but important perks — parking, private health insurance, high-end equipment, tooling of your choice, and more. • Regular team buildings to strengthen our teamwork. • Real ownership and real impact from day one. • A friendly, collaborative culture where good ideas can come from anyone. • Office at the heart of the business district - “Europa” Business Center, Vilnius, Lithuania. We look forward to hearing from you.
