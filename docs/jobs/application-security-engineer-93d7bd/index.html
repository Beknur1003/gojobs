About Us At LangChain, our mission is to enable every company to own its intelligence. We believe agents will transform how companies operate, and we're building the foundation to make that future possible. Our open-source harnesses and LangSmith platform give developers and enterprises the infrastructure to run, improve, and govern agents at scale. We've raised $460M to date, with our most recent funding round valuing the company at $3B, backed by leading investors including Sequoia, Benchmark, IVP, and CapitalG. As we continue to expand our platform and tackle some of the hardest challenges in agent engineering, every team member has the opportunity to make a meaningful impact and help shape both our technology and the future of the industry. More than 7,000 organizations rely on LangSmith, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises, including NVIDIA, Bridgewater, LinkedIn, Workday, and Rippling. We're just getting started on making high-performing agents easier to build and manage at scale. About the Team The Security team ensures that while AI moves at breakneck speed, everyone driving the racecar is wearing a seatbelt. We secure LangChain's core platform and protect AI agents from emerging threats. We work across the stack so developers can confidently ship from prototype to production without compromising on safety or privacy. About the role You'll be a hands-on security engineer working with product teams to secure agentic workloads across our SDKs, LangSmith and LangGraph services, and customer integrations. You'll ship secure designs and code, improve authentication and tenant isolation, and help teams address vulnerabilities and infrastructure risks. You’ll work on security problems that don’t have an established playbook. The scope depends on what you bring. You might: • Own a high-risk surface like authentication or tenant isolation and fix it end to end. • Set direction for a larger security initiative across our SDKs, LangSmith, and LangGraph. • Define how we design and secure agentic products across the company. What you'll bring • 5+ years in security engineering with strong software skills (Python or Go; TypeScript a plus). • Depth in cloud/Kubernetes security (e.g., GCP/AWS IAM, workload identity, admission controls, network policies). • Hands‑on AppSec: code review, threat modeling, secure design, secrets & key management, authn/z patterns, multi‑tenant isolation. • Experience building detection & response and running incident management. • Familiarity with supply‑chain security (SBOM, sigstore/cosign, SLSA‑style controls) and dependency risk management. • Clear, pragmatic communication with engineers and customers. Nice to have • Security for SaaS + self‑hosted offerings, including air‑gapped deployments. • Exposure to SOC 2 / ISO 27001 programs and evidence automation. • Experience with Go services and Infra as Code (Terraform/Helm), plus policy‑as‑code (OPA/Gatekeeper/Kyverno). • Knowledge of privacy patterns (data minimization, retention, masking, workspace scoping).. Compensation & Benefits • Annual salary range: $220,000-$350,000. Equity and benefits are separate.
