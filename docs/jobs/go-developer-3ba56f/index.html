Go Developer

опубликовано 23 ноября 2026

Go — основной язык English B2
PostgreSQLKubernetesDockerTerraformMicroservicesHighloadCI/CDPythonJavaPHPFinTech
You build the core of our platform: the services that accept a merchant's payment request, choose a provider, call it, handle the response and keep every transaction's state correct. These services handle thousands of requests per second at peak, and a lost or duplicated payment costs real money, so correctness, idempotency and observability matter as much as speed. What you will do • Build and maintain Go microservices for payment processing, smart routing, cascading and retries. • Integrate new acquirers, PSPs and APMs: implement their REST, SOAP or ISO 8583 APIs behind our unified interface, including 3-D Secure, tokenisation, refunds and payouts. • Design reliable transaction state machines with idempotency keys, outbox patterns and exactly-once handling of provider webhooks and callbacks. • Work on settlement, reconciliation and fee calculation pipelines. • Keep systems secure and PCI DSS compliant: handle card data only in scoped services, encrypt secrets, follow secure coding practice. • Add metrics, logs and traces; take part in on-call rotation and incident reviews. • Review code, write tests and help shape the architecture with the team. What we expect • 3+ years of commercial backend development, with at least 2 years in Go. • Strong understanding of concurrency in Go: goroutines, channels, context, sync primitives, race conditions. • Experience designing high-load, fault-tolerant distributed systems: retries, timeouts, circuit breakers, idempotency. • Solid PostgreSQL: schema design, transactions and isolation levels, indexes, query tuning. • Experience with a message broker and event-driven architecture. • Experience writing unit and integration tests, and working with Docker and CI/CD. • Upper-Intermediate English or higher for reading provider documentation and talking to partners. Nice to have • Experience in fintech: payment gateways, PSPs, acquiring, banking or e-wallets. • Knowledge of card payment flows, 3-D Secure 2, tokenisation or ISO 8583. • Experience working inside a PCI DSS scope. • Kubernetes in production, Helm, Terraform. • Background in another language such as PHP, Java or Python, which helps when migrating legacy integrations.
