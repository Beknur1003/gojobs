Ford is seeking an experienced and passionate Senior Software Engineer to join our team in developing, enhancing, and expanding our suite of chaos engineering and observability generation tooling. You'll blend software and systems engineering to ensure the uptime, scalability, and maintainability of our critical cloud services. You'll be at the intersection of SRE and Software Development, building and driving the adoption of our global monitoring capabilities. If you're passionate about using your IT expertise and analytical skills to shape the future of transportation, this is your opportunity to make a real impact. Join us and be part of a team that's building the future of mobility!
