Job Title: DevOps Engineer - Cloud Infrastructure (APAC) ﻿Location: Singapore (on-site) ﻿MUST HAVE: Chinese with English Speaking Skills Unlock the Future of Cloud Infrastructure in APAC with Us! Are you ready to play a crucial role in optimizing and securing our cloud infrastructure across APAC, including China? Join our dynamic team as a DevOps Engineer, where you will be the go-to expert for all DevOps matters in the region. Key Responsibilities: Cloud Infrastructure Management: • Maintain and optimize cloud infrastructure for APAC, with a focus on China. • Assist in migrating on-premises servers to the Cloud. • Collaborate with the APAC Cloud Infrastructure Manager to ensure security by design in all projects. • Automate and optimize OPEX costs on the Cloud. Cybersecurity and Compliance: • Maintain guidelines and standard operating procedures for infrastructure in APAC. • Collaborate on the definition and execution of the IT infrastructure strategy. • Address cybersecurity, privacy, and data protection incidents with the helpdesk and global operation teams. • Design and implement SecDevOps processes and automation solutions. • Ensure compliance with industry trends, introducing innovative solutions to optimize or design infrastructure. Technical & Management Skills: • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field. • 2+ years of hands-on DevOps experience in the Public Cloud. • Development experience in Python, Java, Golang, JavaScript, C#, Bash. • Understanding of server fundamentals, virtualization, storage, and network protocols. • Experience with CI/CD tools and pipelines (Jenkins, GitOps). • Proficiency in Infrastructure-as-Code tools (Terraform). • Familiarity with containerization and orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm). • Exposure to Public Cloud platforms (Azure, GCP, Alibaba Cloud). • Fluent in English and Mandarin for effective communication with China vendors. Bonus Qualifications: • Certifications from any Public Cloud and DevOps-related certifications. • Experience in IT organizations supporting a mobile or "disconnected" workforce. • 2+ years of experience in a global organization. • Familiarity with enterprise IT architecture.
