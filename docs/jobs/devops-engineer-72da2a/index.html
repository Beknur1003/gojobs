Role Summary As a DevOps Engineer, you'll join an international platform engineering team responsible for developing and operating a centralized automation platform used across ING. You will design and implement automation solutions that enable teams to independently manage infrastructure and application environments while maintaining high standards for security, reliability, and compliance. This is a growth opportunity suited for professionals with mid-level to senior-level experience who thrive in collaborative environments and enjoy building solutions that create impact at enterprise scale. What You'll Be Doing • Develop and operate a centralized automation platform built on Ansible Automation Platform and Azure DevOps • Build and maintain automation solutions that improve consistency, reliability, and operational efficiency • Create and maintain Ansible playbooks for RHEL-based systems and middleware platforms • Automate installation, configuration, deployment, and lifecycle management processes • Support and enhance CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps • Collaborate with developers, architects, and platform teams across multiple countries and time zones • Contribute to engineering best practices, platform standards, and continuous improvement initiatives About the Team You'll become part of a large international Platform Engineering organization supporting ING's global technology landscape. The team brings together engineers from diverse backgrounds who share a common goal: enabling secure, reliable, and automated technology solutions at scale. At ING Hubs Philippines, you'll work in an environment that values collaboration, continuous learning, and innovation. We empower our people to challenge the status quo, share ideas openly, and make a meaningful impact on the future of banking. Required Skills • Strong hands-on experience with Ansible and Terraform for Infrastructure-as-Code, configuration management, and deployment automation • Experience building and supporting CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps • Scripting experience using Bash, Shell, PowerShell, and/or Python • Development experience using Go (Golang) and/or Java (Spring Boot) • Knowledge of Kubernetes, including deployments, scaling, and cluster operations • Experience working with Linux/RHEL environments • Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. • Ability to collaborate effectively with global stakeholders and cross-functional teams Nice to Have • Cloud platform experience. • Observability and monitoring tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, ELK, or similar • Infrastructure-as-Code and DevOps/SRE practices • Linux systems administration and networking fundamentals • Experience with Agile ways of working • Experience with Automic Workload Automation (AWA) What We Offer • The opportunity to work with a modern technology stack and enterprise-scale platforms. • Exposure to cloud, automation, DevOps, and platform engineering practices. • Collaboration with talented engineers across ING's global network. • Continuous learning and professional growth opportunities. • The ability to contribute to meaningful transformation initiatives that impact teams worldwide. • An inclusive culture where diverse perspectives are valued and encouraged.
