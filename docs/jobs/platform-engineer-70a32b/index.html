Platform Engineering | DevOps | Site Reliability Engineering IBM SiXworks Location: Farnborough, Hampshire - Hybrid Positions: Multiple Security Clearance: Applicants MUST currently hold OR be eligible for SC or DV clearance. Build. Automate. Engineer. Improve. At IBM SiXworks, we’re continuing to develop and strengthen our Platform Engineering capability, and we’re looking to connect with engineers who want to work on some of the most technically challenging programmes across Defence and National Security. As a specialist technology consultancy, we deliver secure, innovative engineering solutions across the full technical lifecycle — from experimentation and proof of concept through to design, build, deployment, operation and continuous improvement. Our engineers work across complex, security-sensitive environments where reliability, automation, resilience and scalability are fundamental to successful delivery. We’re interested in speaking with Platform Engineers, DevOps Engineers and Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) at various stages of their careers. Whether you’re an experienced Platform Engineer, a DevOps professional looking to develop further into platform engineering, or an earlier-career engineer with a strong technical foundation, we’d welcome a conversation. The technical landscape Depending on your experience and the requirements of individual programmes, we’re particularly interested in expertise across: • Cloud & Infrastructure: Microsoft Azure, cloud architecture, hybrid environments and Infrastructure as Code • Containers & Orchestration: Kubernetes, containerisation and cloud-native infrastructure • Automation & Delivery: Terraform, GitOps, CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation • Software Engineering: Go (Golang), Python and platform tooling • Reliability & Operations: Observability, monitoring, incident management, resilience and SRE principles • Platform Engineering: Internal developer platforms, developer experience, self-service infrastructure and repeatable engineering solutions • Security: Secure platform design, secrets management, policy as code and DevSecOps practices We don’t expect everyone to have experience across every area. We’re looking for engineers with relevant technical strengths, an appetite for solving complex problems and the ability to contribute to high-quality engineering delivery. What makes IBM SiXworks different? Working within our specialist consultancy means contributing to meaningful engineering challenges across Defence and National Security. Depending on your role and programme, you could be involved in technical experimentation, developing secure cloud platforms, improving engineering automation or supporting and evolving established mission-critical environments. You’ll have the opportunity to collaborate with experienced engineers and technical specialists, broaden your expertise and contribute to the ongoing development of our Platform Engineering capability. Security clearance: Applicants must hold, or be eligible to obtain, SC or DV clearance, depending on the programme. You don’t need to tick every technical box or be actively searching for your next role. If you’re passionate about Platform Engineering, DevOps or SRE and interested in understanding what a career at IBM SiXworks could offer, we’d love to hear from you.
