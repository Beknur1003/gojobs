Engineering Manager (Infra) Company: iRage Location: Mumbai About iRage iRage is a leading high-frequency trading firm based in Mumbai, founded in 2009 by quantitative trading professionals to build cutting-edge algorithmic and high-frequency trading systems and strategies. About the Role We're looking for an Engineering Manager (Infra) to lead a core team building and running the infrastructure backbone of our trading systems - exchange connectivity, market data infrastructure, cloud/network architecture, and DevOps - across both traditional and crypto trading, with a focus on Indian exchanges. This is a hands-on leadership role: you'll be coding alongside your team while also owning delivery, architecture decisions, and team growth from day one as we build this function out. What You Will Do • Lead and mentor a team of 4-5 software engineers building and maintaining exchange connectivity, market data infrastructure, cloud/network architecture, and DevOps for high-frequency trading systems • Stay hands-on and write core code alongside the team, not just manage delivery • Manage the full software development lifecycle - requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment • Drive continuous improvement initiatives to optimize trading infrastructure for efficiency and scalability • Collaborate closely with quantitative researchers, traders, and other stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical infra solutions • Stay current on developments in financial markets, trading technologies, and regulatory requirements relevant to Indian exchanges • Foster a culture of innovation, collaboration, and excellence within the engineering team What We're Looking For • 7-10 years of software engineering experience, with at least 3 years in a leadership/management role • Hands-on expertise in C++, Python, and Golang in a high-performance, low-latency systems context (mandatory) • Prior experience in the trading or crypto industry is a must • Experience with real-time data processing, messaging protocols, and high-frequency trading infrastructure (e.g., FIX protocol, market data feeds, order execution) • Working familiarity with AWS and containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or Engineering from a top-tier institute • Proven track record of delivering complex software projects on time and within budget • Excellent communication skills with the ability to work cross-functionally with traders and quant researchers
