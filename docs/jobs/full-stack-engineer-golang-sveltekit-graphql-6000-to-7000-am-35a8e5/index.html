Summary We are looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join our engineering team and contribute to the design, development, and enhancement of high-performance, scalable, and maintainable applications. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on experience in Golang, SvelteKit, GraphQL, and microservices architecture. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable software solutions while contributing to system architecture, performance optimisation, and engineering best practices. Job Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain full-stack applications using Golang for backend development and SvelteKit for frontend development • Design, develop, optimise, and maintain GraphQL APIs for data-driven applications • Build and maintain microservices-based architectures that are scalable, modular, and resilient • Design and optimise databases using PostgreSQL and MS SQL • Develop efficient SQL queries and contribute to database performance tuning • Collaborate with Product, Design, and Operations teams to deliver high-quality software solutions • Participate in code reviews and ensure high standards of code quality, testing, and maintainability • Work with CI/CD pipelines, version control, and automated testing • Contribute to system architecture and technical design discussions • Troubleshoot and optimise application performance and system reliability • Support and mentor junior engineers where required Job Requirements: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field • Minimum 2 years of professional experience as a Full Stack Engineer, Software Engineer, or Backend Engineer • Strong hands-on experience in Golang, SvelteKit or modern frontend frameworks (React/Vue.js), microservices and distributed systems, with strong SQL skills in PostgreSQL and/or MS SQL • Familiarity with JavaScript, GraphQL APIs, CI/CD, automated testing, and Git/version control • Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team Good to Have • Experience with AWS, Temporal Workflow, Node.js, Docker, Kubernetes, performance tuning, observability, system optimisation, team leadership/mentoring, C#, and cloud-native distributed application environments If you are interested, please submit your application here.
