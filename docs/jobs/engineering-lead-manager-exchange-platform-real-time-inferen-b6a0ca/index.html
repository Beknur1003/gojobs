Engineering Lead Manager, Exchange Platform (Real-Time Inference)

index-exchange Toronto, CAN опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

Гибрид Lead
RustJavaC++AI/LLM
About the role We are seeking an Engineering Lead Manager to lead a newly formed Exchange Platform team responsible for real-time, online inference on the exchange. Every day, our exchange receives far more supply than we can send to our DSP partners. The systems this team owns decide, in milliseconds, which bid requests are worth sending and which aren’t. That makes it one of the most direct drivers of exchange efficiency, partner performance, and revenue, and it helps us use our infrastructure resources wisely. This is a hands-on engineering leadership role, not a traditional ML role. You’ll own a critical piece of the exchange’s hot path, where latency, correctness, and safe operation at scale matter above all. Our ML teams will be one of many intelligence providers on the exchange. As we open the platform so partners can bring their own intelligence to it, your team will build the infrastructure that makes this possible. That includes ML models, statistical models, and rule-based decisioning, running in real time across every stage of the hot path. We’re looking for a strong technologist first: someone with deep systems expertise, sound engineering instincts, and a defensive mindset toward the systems they own. You’ll lead a small, distributed team of strong engineers and work closely with the offline ML teams, who are your closest partners, and with other Exchange Platform teams. You’ll help define how the team grows, how it operates, and where it goes over the next year and beyond. Here’s what you’ll be doing • Technical Leadership: Own the technical direction and design of our real-time inference systems on the exchange. Set the bar for system quality, performance, and safe operation, with a defensive approach to reliability and a view of the wider landscape the system runs in. • Team Leadership: Build, lead, and grow a high-performing team of engineers across multiple locations. Provide mentorship, guidance, and professional development, and foster a culture of ownership, collaboration, and accountability. • Exchange Hotpath Ownership: Lead the evolution of how model inference is performed on the exchange’s hot path, including the redesign of key parts of our services. Drive toward millisecond-level inference at every stage. • Platform Enablement: Help design and build the interfaces and guardrails that let internal ML teams, and eventually partners, deploy and run their own models and decisioning logic on the exchange safely and independently. • Software Engineering Excellence: Partner with the team to apply sound practices across the software development life cycle, including designing scalable, resilient, and observable systems, with pragmatic code and appropriate rigour in testing, release, and operations. • Cross-Team Partnership: Work closely with offline ML and data science teams, MLOps, and other Exchange Platform teams to deliver features that span training, inference, and the exchange itself. • Reporting and Metrics: Develop and maintain meaningful performance metrics and reporting mechanisms that track the health, latency, and reliability of our systems. Use data-driven insights to guide decisions and triage. • Hiring and Growth: Help grow the team as the scope of the platform expands. Here’s what you need • Strong software engineering skills, with significant experience in Golang (or a comparable language such as Java, C#, C++, or Rust) and the ability to be hands-on in the codebase • Demonstrable expertise in complex, high-throughput, low-latency back-end systems, including designing, building, testing, and operating high quality services at scale • A strong understanding of the fundamentals of real-time systems: latency, concurrency, performance, reliability, and failure modes • A working understanding of machine learning fundamentals, such as how training and inference work and how models are deployed and served. Deep data science expertise isn’t required, but real-time ML exposure is a strong asset • A track record of strong system ownership, with the instincts to operate defensively, anticipate risk, and keep critical production systems safe • Excellent leadership and team-building skills, with at least 2 years of experience leading engineering teams or serving as a technical lead • Excellent problem-solving skills, the ability to think differently, and an understanding that compromise, negotiation, and practicality are important qualities • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams and time zones Equal employment opportunity At Index Exchange, we believe that successful products are built by teams just as diverse as the audience who uses them.
