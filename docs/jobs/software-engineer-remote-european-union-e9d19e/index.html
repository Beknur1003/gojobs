As technology continues to transform healthcare, we are investing in modern software solutions, cloud infrastructure, and intelligent automation. We are seeking talented software engineers across the European Union who are passionate about building reliable, scalable, and impactful technology solutions. Position Summary We are looking for an experienced Software Engineer to join our team remotely from the European Union. In this role, you will design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain scalable software applications and cloud-based services. You will work with technologies including Python, Golang, Kafka, Kubernetes, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Terraform. The ideal candidate has strong software engineering fundamentals, experience with cloud-native architectures, and the ability to collaborate effectively with a distributed international team. Primary Responsibilities • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable software applications using Python, Golang, and modern development technologies. • Build and optimize backend services, RESTful APIs, microservices, and distributed systems. • Develop and manage cloud infrastructure using GCP, Kubernetes, Terraform, and Apache Kafka. • Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code following engineering best practices. • Build and improve CI/CD pipelines to automate software testing and deployment. • Troubleshoot technical issues, optimize performance, and maintain application security and reliability. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams through code reviews, technical discussions, and Agile development processes. • Explore AI-powered solutions and automation technologies to improve operational efficiency. Required Qualifications • 4+ years of professional software engineering experience using Python or Golang. • 3+ years of hands-on experience with Kafka, Kubernetes, GCP, and Terraform. • 2+ years of experience in software design, development, systems integration, and Agile methodologies. • Experience developing RESTful APIs, microservices, and cloud-native applications. • Proficiency with Git, CI/CD pipelines, and modern software development practices. • Strong debugging, analytical, and problem-solving skills. • Excellent English communication skills and the ability to work independently in a remote environment. Preferred Qualifications • Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field. • Experience developing enterprise applications, distributed systems, or cloud-based platforms. • Familiarity with AI-powered applications, machine learning integrations, and workflow automation. • Experience with Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or similar tools. • Knowledge of application security, data privacy, and performance optimization. • Experience working with healthcare technology or other regulated industries. Why Join Us? At Augusta ENT, we value innovation, collaboration, and professional growth.
