This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Release Engineering Technical Leader based in India . We are looking for an experienced Release Engineering Technical Leader to oversee and optimize the end-to-end release lifecycle of a modern cloud-native enterprise platform. In this role, you will coordinate engineering teams, streamline deployment processes, and ensure reliable, timely software releases that deliver a seamless customer experience. You will work across release management, automation, and platform engineering, using technologies such as GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, and Golang. The position combines technical leadership with hands-on development and troubleshooting in a fast-moving software environment. You will help strengthen CI/CD practices, improve installation workflows, and enhance the reliability and efficiency of enterprise platform delivery. This is an opportunity to contribute to networking, security, and observability solutions for modern cloud-native infrastructure. Accountabilities • Own and manage the end-to-end release process, ensuring consistent, reliable releases and a unified customer experience. • Coordinate cross-functional teams to plan, organize, and accelerate enterprise platform deliverables while maintaining release schedules. • Collaborate closely with software development teams to build, maintain, and deliver reliable software. • Use GitHub for source control, version management, and engineering collaboration throughout the release lifecycle. • Design and implement automation strategies to improve release efficiency, consistency, and reliability. • Develop, support, and enhance platform capabilities using Golang and Kubernetes. • Improve deployment and installation processes to simplify platform adoption and strengthen customer satisfaction. • Support release troubleshooting, identify delivery risks, and work with relevant teams to resolve technical and operational issues. • Promote effective CI/CD and DevOps practices to streamline software delivery and improve release quality. Requirements: • 11+ years of experience in release management or a similar technical role within a software development environment. • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience. • Hands-on experience with GitHub Actions and container orchestration platforms such as Kubernetes or OpenShift. • Proficiency in at least one programming or scripting language, preferably Golang or Python. • Strong understanding of release management processes and software delivery lifecycles. • Golang expertise is strongly preferred, including the ability to read, understand, troubleshoot, and debug Golang code. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and DevOps methodologies is preferred. • Experience managing software releases for cloud service providers is highly desirable. • Kubernetes, project management, or other relevant technical certifications are advantageous. • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to coordinate effectively across diverse, cross-functional teams. • Strong organizational and problem-solving abilities, with a focus on release reliability, automation, and continuous improvement. Benefits: • Remote Work: The role is advertised as fully remote. • Technical Growth: Opportunities to work with modern cloud-native technologies, including Kubernetes, Golang, and enterprise platform engineering. • Global Collaboration: Work with distributed engineering teams on technology solutions serving organizations worldwide. • Professional Development: Exposure to advanced networking, security, and observability technologies, with opportunities to deepen technical expertise. • Innovation-Focused Environment: Contribute to open-source technologies and enterprise solutions supporting modern cloud infrastructure.
