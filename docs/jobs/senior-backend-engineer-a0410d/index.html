About the Role We are seeking a Junior Backend Engineer who will help design, build, and maintain our backend systems. You will work closely with product managers, frontend engineers, and other backend engineers to deliver scalable and reliable services that power our credit card platform. Key Responsibilities • Develop, test, and maintain backend services and APIs using Golang • Work with databases (SQL/NoSQL) and caching systems to ensure performance and scalability • Collaborate with product and engineering teams to translate requirements into technical solutions • Contribute to code reviews, technical discussions, and knowledge sharing within the team • Help improve system monitoring, logging, and alerting to ensure high system availability • Continuously learn and grow as part of a supportive engineering culture What We’re Looking For • 3-10 years of experience in backend development (internships or personal projects also count) • Strong interest or experience in Golang • Understanding of RESTful APIs and microservices concepts • Familiarity with relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and/or NoSQL databases • Basic understanding of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or similar) is a plus • Problem-solving mindset, willingness to learn, and strong teamwork skills • Ability to communicate in English (our team is international) Nice to Have • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, or CI/CD pipelines • Knowledge of message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, etc.) • Exposure to fintech or payment systems Why Join Us We work with some of the best operators and builders in the region.
