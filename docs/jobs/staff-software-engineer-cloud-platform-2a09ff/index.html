Summary Are you a result-oriented, polyglot programmer using 12-factor app methodology to craft your Cloud products? If so, we would like to meet you! Guidewire provides outstanding software for the second-largest financial services industry in the world: insurance. We deliver the core applications that Property and Casualty (P/C) insurers use to build products, sell policies, settle claims, and bill customers. We deliver SaaS solutions via Guidewire Cloud that enable our customers to rapidly innovate and drive measurable value. Guidewire Cloud is powered by the highly resilient Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud infrastructure, with SLAs backed by experienced Guidewire teams and SaaS-certified partners spanning application management, system administration, security and compliance, upgrades, QA testing, health checks, and other services. At Guidewire, we foster a culture of curiosity, innovation, and responsible use of AI, empowering our teams to continuously leverage emerging technologies and data-driven insights to enhance productivity and outcomes. Job Description What you get to do • Lead the development of Guidewire’s multi-tenant cloud platform that supports all Guidewire applications and services. Blog: medium.com/guidewire-engineering-blog/guidewire-cloud-why… • Drive the design and development of ATMOS, our infrastructure platform as a product - built using Kubernetes (EKS), Terraform, and in the CNCF ecosystem, including platform observability (logs, metrics, traces). • Design and build core platform services that support developer experience, authentication, authorization, and platform lifecycle automation using Java, Spring Boot, Golang, or equivalent. • Engineer quality, scalability, availability, and security into every aspect of the platform • Employ TDD and automated testing practices to ensure reliability and repeatability. • Deploy containerized applications to AWS using modern CI/CD pipelines. • Work closely with highly skilled engineers, product managers, architects, and operations teams in a pair programming culture to deliver infrastructure and software that matters. • Mentor engineers, influence architectural direction, and drive continuous improvement across the organization. • Apply AI and data-driven insights to enhance engineering workflows, productivity, and platform capabilities. What you bring to Guidewire • 12+ years of experience in SaaS/PaaS environments supporting large-scale enterprise systems. • Strong programming skills in Java/Spring Boot ecosystem, Golang or equivalent. • Strong design, architecture and hands-on experience with Platform Engineering including • Container technologies (Docker or equivalent) in a large-scale cloud environment. • Strong proficiency with Kubernetes eco-system on Public cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure or equivalent) and IaC tools such as Terraform, Ansible or equivalent • A team-first mindset, strong problem-solving skills, and a passion for learning. • Demonstrated ability to embrace AI and apply it to your engineering workflow. • Familiarity with security best practices in cloud environments. • Comfort working in an agile, fast-paced, collaborative, pairing-oriented environment.
