Fanatics Commerce is the global leader in licensed sports merchandise, operating a vertically integrated platform that designs, manufactures, and delivers officially licensed apparel, jerseys, headwear, and collectibles for major leagues, teams, and events worldwide. With more than 900 e-commerce sites and a global omnichannel presence across digital, in-venue, and retail, Fanatics Commerce reaches fans in over 180 countries and powers official fan experiences for many of the world's most iconic sports properties. At Fanatics, we bring our BOLD Leadership Principles to life every day - building championship teams, obsessing over fans, acting with entrepreneurial speed, and delivering with a determined and relentless mindset. As we continue to scale our global platform, the Software Engineer III will play a critical role in designing and delivering scalable warehouse management and fulfillment systems across Fanatics' global fulfillment and wholesale network. ROLE OVERVIEW The Software Engineer III is a senior individual contributor embedded across multiple concurrent fulfillment and supply chain workstreams, partnering closely with engineering, product, and operations teams to solve complex warehouse execution challenges. This role owns end-to-end technical delivery — from system design and configuration through to production-grade reliability — across Fanatics' warehouse management platforms, including hands-on expertise with Tier 1 WMS solutions such as Manhattan Active. The Software Engineer III delivers business and fan impact through BOLD leadership and execution excellence, leveraging data, automation, and AI-enabled insights. HOW WILL YOU DRIVE IMPACT Success is measured by the ability to deliver results through BOLD capabilities and measurable outcomes. Team & Leadership Impact (Build Championship Teams) • Partner cross-functionally with operations, product, and engineering teams to translate complex supply chain business needs into well-architected technical solutions. • Actively raise the technical bar across the team by championing software design best practices, contributing to architecture decisions, and mentoring peers through code review and shared standards. • Serve as a reliable delivery partner across concurrent workstreams, aligning with stakeholders to maintain clarity on priorities, progress, and risk. Ownership & Execution (Determined & Relentless Mindset) • Take end-to-end ownership of software design, configuration, and delivery across multiple warehouse management and fulfillment workstreams, ensuring quality and reliability at every stage. • Operate with a bias toward action in Agile/Scrum environments, maintaining delivery momentum across complex, multi-team projects. • Proactively identify technical debt, architectural gaps, and process inefficiencies — and drive resolution without waiting to be asked. • Hold a high bar for production-grade quality, applying disciplined testing, monitoring, and incident response practices across all owned systems. AI & DIGITAL CAPABILITY We are building a future-ready organization. Good to Have: • Experience with Manhattan Open Systems. • Familiarity with Temporal or similar workflow and orchestration platforms. • Experience with warehouse automation systems, including WES, WCS, or WMS integrations.
