We are considering candidates based in the US and Canada only, within the Eastern or Central time zones. The Opportunity: We are building Mobile Session Replay: a way for teams to see what users actually experienced in their iOS, Android, Flutter and React Native apps, inside Grafana Cloud. Session Replay connects frontend signals to session-level evidence, so engineers can investigate production issues faster and with more confidence. Web replay is already available publicly. Mobile replay extends the same story to native apps as part of our Digital Experience Monitoring (DEM) strategy. We are starting with Android. A working prototype already exists, and you will help turn it into a product customers can trust. The plan is to push ahead from there: iOS next, then our Flutter, React Native and OpenTelemetry mobile SDKs, so customers get a consistent replay experience across platforms. As a company we are remote-first and global, we embrace people of different experiences and backgrounds to build diverse teams where every person brings a new perspective to the software. We are looking for Staff Mobile Engineers who are passionate about working with data and providing seamless experiences for our customers to join our growing team! What you'll be doing As a Staff Mobile Engineer you will be the technical lead for mobile capture and replay, working across the device, the ingest path and the player. Specifically, you will: • Own the on-device replay library end to end, starting with Android and extending to iOS. • Make privacy a default, designing masking and consent controls so sensitive data never leaves the device. • Keep the overhead low and measure it on real devices, protecting startup time, UI responsiveness, battery, memory, network use and disk, and profiling the impact of capture and masking while the app is actively being used. Give customers controls to trade fidelity against cost. • Build the integration layer so customers do not copy demo glue code: adapters that tie replay to the active RUM session, sampling decisions and screen or navigation changes. • Work across the stack with Frontend and Mobile Observability teams on the capture, upload and storage pipeline, session inactivity handling, the timeline player and the mobile sessions and replay experience in Frontend Observability. Evaluate the format and API tradeoffs rather than assuming the current prototype approach is final. • Define quality and compatibility: automated tests, CI, device and OS coverage, and backwards-compatible upgrade paths for an SDK that customers cannot easily update. • Engage directly with customers: join calls, gather structured feedback, and help teams instrument and adopt Mobile Session Replay • Mentor engineers and help grow technical leadership in the team We invest heavily in developer productivity. You can use modern AI coding assistants as part of your daily workflow (your choice of tools, within security guidelines), backed by a company-funded usage budget so you can iterate quickly without unnecessary friction. We encourage pragmatic AI-assisted development: faster prototyping, test generation, refactors, documentation, and incident follow-ups—always with strong code review and quality standards. You'll also have access to the latest frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. You can break large problems into increments and work autonomously • You take a user-centered approach, and you build understanding of customers' context and goals so you build the right thing • You are thoughtful about privacy and security on user devices, including handling of sensitive data • You use AI tools as a natural part of your engineering workflow, for exploration, code review or reasoning through design tradeoffs, and you are thoughtful about where they help and where they don't Bonus points for • Prior work on mobile session replay, screen recording, or sampling for mobile telemetry • Experience with video encoding (H.264, MediaCodec, AVFoundation) or on-device masking and redaction • Familiarity with rrweb or web session replay concepts • Experience with OpenTelemetry, crash reporting libraries or other observability open source • Experience with cross-platform frameworks (React Native, Flutter) or building native bindings and bridges • Experience with observability or debugging tools (Grafana, Datadog, New Relic, Sentry, etc.) • Familiarity with mobile privacy, consent and platform policy requirements • Experience with Go or TypeScript, since the work crosses into our ingest and player In US, the OTE (On-Target Earnings)/Base compensation range for this role is $189,374 - $227,249 USD.
