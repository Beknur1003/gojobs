#### Company Description #### Job Description When a replacement car is needed fast, someone has to make sure the right vehicle is found, offered and delivered without delay. In our Car Replacement technology team in Pullach close to Munich, you will build the Go services and AI agents behind that. These are production systems that decide, act and automate at scale across SIXT and our partner network, running on real data with real business impact. You will work closely with Product, Data Science and business experts, and your work will show up in measurable results. **YOURS TO OWN** - You build and run Go microservices in a cloud-native, event-driven architecture that powers Car Replacement across internal systems and partner integrations - You design, ship and operate AI agents in production, with multi-step orchestration, RAG and context management. You also own how they are evaluated and guarded, and your patterns become the team’s standards - You own features end to end, in an experienced team with short paths to Product and the business. You challenge unclear requirements, define acceptance criteria, then implement, deploy, monitor and validate in production, without waiting for a detailed spec - You build the TypeScript and React interfaces that make agent behavior transparent and usable for business users (roughly 30% of your time, with the focus on backend) - You build quality in from the start through automated testing, observability, code reviews and agent evaluation, and you help shape the team’s technical direction - You use AI engineering tools across the full software lifecycle, and you stay accountable for the correctness of what you ship **YOUR SKILLS MATTER** - **Microservices in Go** You have built and run distributed services in production. You are strong in Go, REST/gRPC API design, event-driven systems, containers and Kubernetes, CI/CD and observability - **Agentic applications** You have built and shipped AI agents in production, not just used AI tools to write code. You know multi-step orchestration, RAG, context management, and agent evaluation and guardrails. You can explain what worked, what failed and how you measured it - **Product mindset** You start with the problem, ask how success will be measured, and take responsibility for the outcome, not only the code - **Engineering quality** You value automated testing, observability, maintainability and systematic root-cause analysis as core parts of your craft - **Collaboration** You communicate clearly with engineers, Product, Data Science and business stakeholders, and you give and receive constructive feedback - **TypeScript & React** You have experience with TypeScript applications, ideally React **WHAT WE OFFER** - **Extra benefits** Enjoy discounts on SIXT rent, share, ride and SIXT+, attractive vehicle leasing offers, and exclusive deals with partners for travel, tech, fashion and more - **Mobility boost** We support you with a monthly mobility allowance of €20 per month for even more freedom - **Future security** We contribute to your retirement plan and support you with capital-forming benefits to ensure you are well covered - **Feel-good atmosphere** Stay active with our modern SIXT gym, various leisure activities like the gaming area or the SIXT choir, and enjoy our high-quality employee restaurant - **Flexibility** Enjoy 30 days of vacation and a hybrid working model with flexible hours - **Giving back** Take one day each year to volunteer at a charitable organization dedicated to supporting children #### Additional Information **About us:** We are a globally leading mobility service provider with a revenue of €4.3 billion and around 9,000 employees worldwide. Our mobility platform ONE combines our products SIXT rent (car rental), SIXT share (car sharing), SIXT ride (taxi, ride, and chauffeur services), and SIXT+ (car subscription), giving our customers access to our fleet of 360,000 vehicles, the services of 4,000 cooperation partners, and around 5 million drivers worldwide. Together with our franchise partners, we are present in more than 100 countries at 2,000 rental stations. At SIXT, top-tier customer experience and outstanding customer service are our highest priorities. We believe in true entrepreneurship and long-term stability and align our corporate strategy with foresight. Get started with us and apply now!
