You are a Senior Software Engineer who will be joining the team of world class DevOps Engineers. The team maintains, supports and grows Wise’s Kubernetes based Compute Platform. Your mission will be to lead, design and implement automation around Kubernetes installation, maintenance and monitoring. That includes building microservices, API-s, workflows and documentation. You should be able to mentor your colleagues, lead projects and take ownership on initiatives. Responsibilities: • Drive Automation and Efficiency: Innovate and implement cutting-edge automation solutions at both the infrastructure (AWS) and application levels, reducing manual overhead, accelerating deployment cycles, and increasing system resilience. • Architect and Enhance Our Platform: You will take ownership of significant system components, designing and implementing scalable solutions that handle complex operational loads with high reliability and security. • Active Collaboration and Leadership: While you will be deeply involved in hands-on technical work, your role also entails leading projects and collaborating with cross-functional teams to share knowledge, mentor peers, and refine our engineering practices. • Embrace and Extend Cloud-Native Practices: With ongoing investments in AWS and Kubernetes, you will have the opportunity to lead initiatives that expand our capabilities in the cloud-native landscape, from developing Kubernetes operators to enhancing our service mesh infrastructure. Must have: • Full proficiency(minimum 6 years of experience) in AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Golang • Deep knowledge on designing and implementing Microservices and API-s • Strong background on building mission-critical Cloud Native Back-End Systems • Strong experience with LLM assisted coding practices and tools (e.g. Codex, Cursor) • Test and Quality Automation Expertise Nice to have: • Kubernetes administration skills • Mentoring skills • Designing and building workflows • Temporal • Full stack software engineering experience • Team or project management experience For everyone, everywhere.
