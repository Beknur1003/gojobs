For the latest news from Aurora, visit or follow us on LinkedIn. What we are looking for We're searching for Summer 2027 Data & ML Platform Engineering Intern. You'll join one of the teams building the infrastructure that makes Aurora's ML work at scale — data pipelines, ML platforms, labeling and data-engine systems, distributed compute, and the cloud tooling that turns raw sensor and behavioral data into model-ready datasets. In this role you will • Build and maintain data pipelines and workflow automation that turn raw fleet and sensor data into model-ready datasets • Design or extend ML platform tooling (labeling systems, data engines, distributed compute) used across autonomy teams • Use cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) to scale data ingestion, storage, and processing workloads • Partner with ML engineers to understand downstream model requirements and translate them into pipeline/platform design decisions • Instrument systems for observability and help harden a prototype into something that can run in production Required qualifications • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related field • Strong programming skills in Python and/or Golang • Working knowledge of data pipeline/ETL concepts and basic distributed systems tradeoffs (e.g. CAP theorem) • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP) • Some applied exposure to ML, robotics, maps/geospatial systems, or a comparable domain • Hands-on experience building or extending a backend system or pipeline with real scale-up/scale-out requirements Desired qualifications • Experience with parallel execution / distributed processing frameworks (e.g. Spark, Ray, Dask) • Fluency with AI/agentic coding tooling • Prior internship or industry experience with production data infrastructure Compensation The hourly pay rate for this internship in Mountain View or San Francisco is: • $52.00 per hour for interns pursuing a bachelor’s degree; and • $58.00 per hour for interns pursuing a master’s degree The hourly pay rate for this internship in Pittsburgh is: • $47.00 per hour for interns pursuing a bachelor’s degree; and • $52.00 per hour for interns pursuing a master’s degree Working at Aurora At Aurora, we bring together extraordinarily talented and experienced people united by the strength of our values.
