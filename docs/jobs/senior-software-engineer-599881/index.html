Job Summary We are looking for a Senior Software Developer to contribute to our Veeam Kasten data management platform, ensuring top-tier security and excellence. Responsibilities • Owning (designing, developing in Go, and testing) significant features in the Veeam Kasten product related to backup and recovery of Kubernetes applications, databases, and storage systems. • Integrating Veeam Kasten with other Veeam products and expanding its capabilities. • Collaborating with distributed teams across different time zones and regions. • Working with and contributing to the cloud-native open-source community. • Participating in technical design discussions, reviewing code, and providing feedback to other engineers. What we expect from you • 8+ years of experience developing enterprise solutions in a high-level programming language such as C++, Java, or C#, and a willingness to code in Go. • Experience with distributed systems concepts and client-server architectures. • Expertise in developing, testing, and debugging production-quality, scalable, multi-threaded, and concurrent systems. • Strong knowledge of computer science fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, and concurrency). • Strong sense of ownership, delivering work end-to-end, aligning with stakeholders, maintaining high level of quality and following up with the delivered solutions in production. • A university degree in a technical field or equivalent industry experience. Will be an advantage • Experience developing in Go. • Experience developing containers, container schedulers, and orchestration platforms. • Experience developing complex systems with AI coding agent tools at scale. • Active contribution to OSS projects in the Kubernetes/Cloud-Native ecosystem. • Participation in CNCF Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) or Kubernetes Special Interest Groups (SIGs). Technologies we work with Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, Golang, Bash, Git, OpenShift, Rancher, Google GKE, AWS EKS, Azure AKS, VMware TKG(s), etc. Our team is passionate about code quality, extensibility, coding standards, testing, and automation.
