Join Apple, and help us leave the world better than we found it. This team is more than a group of engineers -- it's a group passionate about Apple products, services, and engineering culture. The team is seeking an experienced Software Engineering Manager to focus on building the next generation of our production-facing ML platforms, and improving the reliability of existing ones using AIOps. Our work impacts over a billion users and is essential to the success of Apple's growth in AIML. Our team is growing, and you will have the unique opportunity to help us shape our culture and processes. As part of this role, you will: Collaborate to solve complex ML, AIOps, SRE and production-infrastructure problems with the opportunity to invent innovative solutions Evolve the design and architecture to support the continued growth of our AI and LLM-powered Search platform Develop the next generation of services that power Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud OS.
