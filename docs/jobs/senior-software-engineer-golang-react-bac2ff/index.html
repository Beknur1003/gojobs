JOB ID- QT-SUL-11-480 Senior Software Engineer 7+ Hybrid(HYDERABAD) Job Description We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer to design, build, operate, and continuously improve the applications that power our wholesale and retail commerce experience, including purchase order and transfer order workflows, inventory and warehouses, vendor order processing, data warehouse, and the internal tools our operations teams rely on every day. This is a hands-on backend role with end-to-end ownership spanning architecture and design, application development, APIs and integrations, data processing, cloud infrastructure, production support, incident resolution, security remediation, and operational readiness. You will act as a trusted technical advisor to the team, raising the technical bar through design leadership and mentoring. HOW WILL YOU DRIVE IMPACT • Lead technical discovery and design for complex, cross-domain initiatives, and communicate design trade-offs clearly to engineers, product partners, and senior stakeholders. • Partner closely with Product, Business Operations, Platform, Security, Data, and Support teams to align on technical decisions and ship reliable solutions collaboratively. • Write complete, actionable stories and break down complex initiatives into executable work streams. • Design and build scalable APIs, microservices, and integrations using Golang, and user-facing internal applications using React/TypeScript, that keep end-to-end business workflows reliable and extensible. • Develop scalable data-processing solutions for orders, inventory, shipments, and receipts, including batch and asynchronous, event-driven pipelines built on AWS services such as SQS/SNS, EventBridge, and Lambda, with data stored in PostgreSQL. • Define and drive migration strategies, schema migrations, version management, and backward-compatibility approaches as part of application modernization and platform refactoring. • Establish and promote best practices, design patterns, and coding standards, and contribute to architectural decisions across the team. • Mentor and support other engineers through design reviews, code reviews, and knowledge sharing. • Take end-to-end ownership of ambiguous problems through design, implementation, testing, release, and ongoing operational support across the full commerce application stack. • Anticipate technical issues, design for failure recovery, and triage complex system-wide performance problems. • Troubleshoot production issues using structured logs, metrics, distributed traces, and data analysis; drive root-cause analysis and implement durable fixes rather than patches. • Build observability, monitoring, alerting, deployment health checks, and release validation so that customer-facing systems perform consistently and at scale. • Adopt infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD practices via Terraform and GitHub Actions, using safe deployment strategies such as blue-green, canary, and feature flags with reliable rollback. • Address application security and software supply-chain findings, including dependency vulnerabilities, authorization gaps, CI/CD hardening, and least-privilege access, with rigor and urgency. • Participate in production support rotations and serve as a dependable technical resource for time-sensitive ordering escalations and cross-functional issue resolution. • Reduce recurring production issues over time by improving monitoring, operational documentation, and the underlying reliability of data and integration workflows. AI & DIGITAL CAPABILITY We are building a future-ready organization. This role is expected to: Page 2 • Apply AI and technology to improve efficiency, quality, and outcomes, including AI-assisted development, testing, and code review • Use data and digital tools to inform decisions and enhance performance • Demonstrate curiosity and adaptability in adopting new technologies and ways of working • Contribute to a culture of innovation, engineering excellence, and continuous improvement CAPABILITIES & EXPERIENCE YOU BRING Required Qualifications: • 7+ years of professional software engineering experience with a strong track record delivering production software as an individual contributor, including ownership across backend, APIs, integrations, data, and operational concerns. • Strong hands-on experience in Golang and React for building internal web applications. Experience in other languages is not required. • Hands-on experience with PostgreSQL or a similar relational database (e.g., MySQL, AWS RDS/Aurora), including schema design, query optimization, partitioning, replication, and migrations. • Good hands-on production experience with AWS technologies, including compute (ECS/EKS, Lambda), storage (S3), managed databases (RDS), messaging services (SQS/SNS, EventBridge), load balancing, and CDN. • Solid knowledge of distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture, domain-driven design, and cloud-native design patterns. • Experience designing RESTful APIs, service contracts, and versioning strategies, and building batch and asynchronous processing pipelines and system integrations in production. • Experience building high-performance, low-latency applications, including caching strategies (e.g., Redis), rate limiting, and concurrency. • Experience with observability and reliability practices: monitoring, alerting, distributed tracing, SLAs/SLOs, and disaster recovery. • Strong production troubleshooting and root-cause analysis skills using logs, metrics, traces, and data, with the ability to implement durable fixes rather than short-term patches. • Solid understanding of secure coding practices, dependency management, authorization models, secrets handling, and CI/CD security hardening. • Proficiency with AI-powered development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT) and an understanding of LLM integration patterns. • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated ability to explain design trade-offs and work effectively alongside product, business, platform, security, and support partners on ambiguous, end-to-end problems.
