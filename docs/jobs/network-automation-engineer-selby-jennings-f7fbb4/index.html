Network Automation Engineer (Observability, Automation & Trading Infrastructure) Our client is a renowned hedge fund seeking a Network Automation Engineer to join one of the most sophisticated infrastructure engineering environments in financial services. Technology sits at the core of the business, and the firm continues to invest heavily in network automation, observability and engineering excellence to support its global trading platform. This is not a traditional network support position. The successful candidate will help drive the evolution of a highly available global network through software engineering, network automation and telemetry. The role offers the opportunity to build platforms, develop self-service capabilities and influence how infrastructure is managed at scale across a complex, business-critical environment. The ideal candidate is passionate about eliminating manual processes and leveraging network automation to improve reliability, scalability and operational efficiency. You'll join a highly collaborative engineering culture where ownership, innovation and continuous improvement are actively encouraged. This role is ideal for an engineer who wants to be at the forefront of network automation, observability and infrastructure engineering within a world-class trading environment. Key Responsibilities • Design, build and enhance network automation platforms that enable the network to operate efficiently at scale. • Develop self-service tooling, automation workflows and infrastructure-as-code capabilities. • Drive the firm's observability strategy through telemetry, monitoring, alerting and analytics platforms. • Design and support a highly available global multi-vendor network underpinning critical trading infrastructure. • Build and maintain Source-of-Truth systems, network automation pipelines and configuration management frameworks. • Partner with engineering and business teams to deliver scalable infrastructure solutions. • Define and champion engineering standards, network automation best practices and operational excellence. • Troubleshoot complex issues spanning networking, cloud infrastructure, automation and observability platforms. • Identify opportunities to reduce operational risk through network automation and intelligent tooling. • Contribute to a culture of continuous improvement, innovation and technical excellence. Key Skills & Experience • Strong background in network automation, infrastructure engineering and observability. • Advanced Python development experience, ideally alongside exposure to Golang. • Hands-on experience with automation technologies including NetBox, Ansible, Jinja, Git, Terraform, NETCONF and RESTCONF. • Proven experience building network automation frameworks, deployment pipelines and self-service infrastructure platforms. • Strong understanding of telemetry and observability tools including Prometheus, Grafana, Telegraf, Cisco Telemetry, gNMI and YANG. • Exposure to traditional monitoring technologies including SNMP, Zabbix and LibreNMS. • Solid networking fundamentals, including BGP, OSPF, PIM and IGMP. • Experience supporting large-scale, highly available enterprise, cloud or trading network environments. • Strong AWS networking and cloud infrastructure experience. • Ability to diagnose and resolve complex infrastructure challenges across multiple technology domains. • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. • A proactive, engineering-led mindset with a passion for automation, reliability and continuous improvement.
