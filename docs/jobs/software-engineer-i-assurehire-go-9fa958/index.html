Job Overview AssureHire, Mitratech's employment screening platform, is looking for a Software Engineer I to join our engineering team in a support-engineering-focused role. You'll sit at the intersection of customer-facing problem solving and hands-on development: triaging and resolving technical escalations, communicating clearly with stakeholders, and shipping code fixes for issues you can resolve end-to-end. This is a great fit for an early-career engineer who wants real production experience across two modern backend stacks, Ruby on Rails and Go, while building strong debugging, communication, and AI-augmented development skills. Essential Duties & Responsibilities: • Own the resolution of technical escalation tickets: investigate, reproduce, and diagnose issues reported by customers and internal teams. • Communicate with stakeholders (support, product, customer success, and occasionally customers) to gather context, set expectations, and report on resolution status. • Identify tickets that can be resolved as self-service engineering work, then write the code, open the pull request, and see the fix through review and deployment. • Use AI-assisted tooling to accelerate triage — determining whether an issue is a product bug, a configuration problem, or expected behavior — and to draft and review candidate code solutions, applying your own judgment before anything ships. • Contribute to root-cause analysis and help reduce recurring escalations by improving code, documentation, and runbooks. • Follow secure development practices: least-privilege access, secrets management, and careful handling of sensitive candidate and customer data. Requirements & Skills: • 0–2 years of professional software development experience (internships, co-ops, and substantial personal/academic projects count). • Working proficiency in Go — Python is a plus • Familiarity with relational databases and the ability to read and write SQL (PostgreSQL preferred). • Strong debugging instincts: you enjoy digging into logs, reproducing issues, and tracing problems across systems. • Clear written and verbal communication — you can explain a technical issue to a non-technical stakeholder and keep people informed without being asked. • Comfort using AI tools as part of your workflow, paired with the judgment to critically review AI-generated analysis and code before relying on it. Ie . Claude. • Ownership mindset: you follow tickets through to resolution rather than handing them off. • Unix shell scripting.
