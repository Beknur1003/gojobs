AdTech company Salary of up to 7,500 Euros gross a month based on skills and experience Our client is an international AdTech company in Limassol is looking for a Senior Back-End Engineer. Responsibilities: Build and maintain scalable back-end services and APIs using Golang. Translate product requirements into reliable, maintainable technical solutions. Develop services that query, process, and serve large volumes of data efficiently. Work closely with Front-End, Data, and DevOps Engineers to deliver a seamless end-to-end experience. Optimize application performance, database queries, and resource usage. Write testable code, participate in code reviews, and troubleshoot production issues. Implement secure access controls and maintain data isolation across customers. Requirements: 5–8+ years of professional experience building production back-end systems. Strong proficiency in Golang and experience designing RESTful APIs. Solid understanding of concurrency, distributed systems, and service-to-service communication. Strong SQL skills and experience with relational databases such as PostgreSQL. Experience diagnosing performance bottlenecks and optimizing data access. Familiarity with automated testing, Git, and collaborative development workflows. Ability to take ownership of features from technical design through deployment and support. Ability to balance delivery speed with reliability, performance, and maintainability. Experience with geospatial data, location-based queries, or GIS applications is a plus. Familiarity with Docker, AWS, and CI/CD pipelines is a plus. Exposure to Spark, Iceberg, or large-scale data processing architectures is a plus. Experience with Kafka or similar event-streaming platforms is a plus. Familiarity with Elasticsearch, StarRocks, or other analytical data stores is a plus. Knowledge of AdTech systems and real-time data workflows is a plus. Located in Cyprus The working days are Monday – Friday, 9am-6pm with an hour off (onsite job for people located in Limassol, for candidates located in Paphos, Nicosia, etc. hybrid working can be discussed).
