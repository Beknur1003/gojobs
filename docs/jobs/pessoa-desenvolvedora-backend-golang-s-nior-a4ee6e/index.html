Preferred qualifications: • Previous experience developing or supporting financial systems, payment solutions, or core banking platforms. • Strong knowledge of event-driven architectures and messaging technologies such as Kafka, AWS SQS, and SNS. • Advanced understanding of API security practices, including OAuth2, rate limiting, and advanced request validation. • Experience implementing and maintaining highly available, resilient, and fault-tolerant AWS environments. • Participation in technical communities or contributions to open-source projects related to Go, AWS, or associated technologies.
