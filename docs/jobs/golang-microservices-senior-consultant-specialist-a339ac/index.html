Ready to build modern Go microservices for HSBC Finance Technology platforms? Why HSBC HSBC is one of the largest banking and financial services organisations in the world. Guided by our purpose of opening up a world of opportunity, our ambition is to become the world’s most trusted bank globally, putting customers at the heart of everything we do. We are currently seeking an experienced professional to join our team in the role of Senior Consultant Specialist Join Group Functions IT in Finance Technology to design, build and support Golang microservices that power critical finance platforms. This role is hands-on - you’ll ship code into production, support the architecture and take ownership of reliability once services are live. You’ll contribute to modernising legacy capabilities by migrating from mainframe and on-prem finance platforms to containerised services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP). You’ll also help enable artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities by building AI agents that support business activities. Success means delivering robust services, strengthening integration patterns and improving production readiness across the platform. What you'll be doing • Design, build, deploy and support Golang microservices for finance platforms • Deliver production code and contribute to the architecture and system design • Build artificial intelligence (AI) agents to support business activities • Migrate legacy mainframe and on-prem capabilities to containerised services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) • Develop REST application programming interfaces (APIs), gRPC services and event-driven integrations • Implement data extraction, transformation, reconciliation and migration across structured datasets • Create automated testing across unit, integration, performance, contract, regression and migration scenarios • Support production readiness including incident investigation and technical documentation What we're looking for • Bring substantial hands-on Golang (Go) development experience particularly in microservices • Apply working knowledge of Unix and a practical understanding of databases from a consumer point of view • Build and consume HTTP application programming interfaces (APIs) including REST and gRPC patterns • Implement event-driven integrations plus batch or file-based processing using Structured Query Language (SQL) • Use concurrency patterns including goroutines and channels with a focus on performance tuning • Create validation logic, business rules and data-integrity controls within services and pipelines • Demonstrate API versioning, schema evolution and backward compatibility practices • Implement secure configuration and secrets management in modern delivery environments Modernising Finance Platforms If you want to build services that run at scale and stay accountable for how they perform in production this role offers real scope to make an impact. You’ll help modernise legacy finance capabilities onto GCP while strengthening API and event-driven integration patterns across the platform. You’ll also contribute to enabling AI capabilities through practical engineering work not slideware. Bring your Go expertise and build what Finance Technology depends on. You’ll achieve more when you join HSBC. HSBC is committed to building a culture where all employees are valued, respected and opinions count. We take pride in providing a workplace that fosters continuous professional development, flexible working and opportunities to grow within an inclusive and diverse environment.
