metrics-first mindset • Help define and improve how the team applies AI coding tools to embedded firmware work - including code review, ATS authoring, design doc drafting, and pair-programming on complex subsystems • Mentor engineers on effective AI-assisted development for firmware: where it accelerates, where it misleads, and how to keep correctness and safety standards intact • Be a Team Player, Mentor, Strong Communicator and be ready and willing to support the Team when needed What You Bring • B.S. in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or equivalent • 8-14 years of real-world Industry Firmware or Embedded experience developing on IoT products • Experience in programming with C, C++, Rust, Golang and/or Python • Experience with both Kernel and User-space Embedded Linux with exposure to Yocto, OpenWRT or other Embedded Distros for Board Support Packaging (BSP) • Experience with Embedded Android Kernel and User-space (AOSP) • Experience with RTOS on Microcontrollers • Basic ability to read Datasheets and Schematics • Experience with concise Technical Writing and Architecture Diagramming • Hands-on experience using AI coding assistants
