This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Mid-Level Software Engineer – Golang (Banking/Investments) based in Brazil. We are looking for a skilled Software Engineer to join a team responsible for building and maintaining critical financial systems that power fixed-income investment products. In this role, you will contribute to the entire investment lifecycle, from product issuance and financial settlement to accurate interest calculations and maturity payments. You will develop and improve high-performance, mission-critical applications within a large-scale financial technology environment. Working with modern architectures and complex integrations, you will help ensure system reliability, security, scalability, and data integrity. You will collaborate closely with other engineers to deliver robust solutions, improve technical practices, and continuously evolve the platform. This is an excellent opportunity to work on challenging projects with a direct impact on the financial ecosystem. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, maintain, and evolve software solutions using Go (Golang), contributing to new features and improvements across mission-critical financial systems. • Build and optimize scalable, reliable, and high-performance applications that support fixed-income investment products and their financial operations. • Contribute to the entire investment lifecycle, including investment issuance, financial settlement, interest calculations, and maturity payments. • Ensure production systems meet demanding non-functional requirements, including performance, scalability, security, availability, resilience, and data integrity. • Monitor application performance and behavior after deployment, identifying potential issues and implementing improvements to ensure operational stability. • Develop and maintain microservices-based architectures, ensuring efficient communication and integration across the technology ecosystem. • Implement automated testing strategies and promote continuous code quality through best practices and code inspections. • Participate actively in code reviews, sharing technical knowledge and contributing to the continuous improvement of engineering standards. • Maintain clear and up-to-date technical documentation to ensure traceability of software changes and architectural decisions. • Identify gaps in existing processes, systems, and architecture, proactively proposing innovative and effective solutions. • Collaborate with engineering colleagues to improve system reliability, technical efficiency, and the overall evolution of the platform. Requirements: • Solid professional experience in software development using Go (Golang). • Strong commitment to software quality, including experience implementing automated tests and performing continuous code inspections. • Practical experience designing, developing, and maintaining microservices architectures. • Strong knowledge of relational databases, such as PostgreSQL and MySQL. • Understanding of software engineering principles, scalable system design, and application performance optimization. • Ability to identify technical and architectural challenges, question existing approaches, and proactively recommend improvements. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work on complex systems and technical challenges. • Adaptability and enthusiasm for learning new technologies, tools, and engineering practices. • Collaborative mindset and effective communication skills, with a willingness to participate in code reviews and share technical knowledge. Nice to have: • Knowledge of or interest in the financial services industry, particularly fixed-income investments and financial settlement processes. • Experience working with public or private cloud platforms, such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, or OpenStack. • Familiarity with messaging systems and event-driven architectures, including Kafka, RabbitMQ, ZeroMQ, or IBM MQ. • Knowledge of advanced architectural patterns and approaches, such as Event Sourcing, CQRS, Hexagonal Architecture, and Lambda Architecture. • Experience with non-relational databases (NoSQL) and data modeling. • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and automation tools, preferably Azure DevOps.
