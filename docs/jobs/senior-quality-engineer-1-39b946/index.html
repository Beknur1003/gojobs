About the Position: Tech Stack: React JS, Golang, Python, AWS Position Overview and Objective The Senior Quality Engineer 1 leads quality efforts for assigned projects by owning test strategy execution and ensuring end-to-end quality coverage. This role applies advanced technical expertise to validate complex systems, collaborates closely with cross-functional partners, and raises risks early to support timely and reliable software releases. Primary Responsibilities and Duties • Own and execute test plans to ensure appropriate fuctional and non-functional coverage. • Design and execute end-to-end test cases for complex features or applications. • Ensure all planned testing activities are completed prior to release. • Collaborate closely with development teams to identify risks and ensure test readiness. • Act as a point of contact for quality concerns on assigned projects. • Track testing progress using metrics and provide regular status updates. • Participate in or lead bug triage discussions to prioritize and resolve defects. • Partner with product management to validate success criteria and acceptance standards. • Mentor Quality Engineers by providing guidance on testing approaches and tools. Key Skills 5+ Yrs of experience • Advanced knowledge of software testing and quality engineering practices • Strong technical ability to understand system architecture and data flows • Experience with cloud-based applications and test automation • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills Key Competencies Technical leadership • Cross-functional collaboration • Risk assessment • Quality advocacy Minimum Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent combination of education and relevant experience • Significant experience in software quality engineering or testing • Experience executing and owning test plans for complex systems Preferred Qualifications • Experience with cloud platforms, APIs, and automated test infrastructure • Exposure to exploratory testing techniques and metrics-driven quality analysis We are looking for Only Bangalore Based Candidates.
