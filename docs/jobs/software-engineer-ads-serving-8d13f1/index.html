SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. SpaceXSI is building Super Intelligence to help humanity understand the universe. Our teams are building the full Super Intelligence stack: from our Colossus supercomputers, to Grok models, to our suite of applications, including Grok Bot, Grok Build, Grok Imagine, Grok Voice, and X. We're creating a powerful flywheel where more compute trains better models, better models power more useful products, and what we learn from people using those products shapes what we build next. SOFTWARE ENGINEER, ADS SERVING X Ads is one of the highest-leverage product areas at X. You'll work on the backend and infrastructure that powers advertising at massive scale, directly affects the business, and is being rebuilt with modern infrastructure and AI at the center. This is an opportunity to join a small, fast-moving team where engineers can take on real ownership, learn from strong teammates, and ship systems that matter. We're looking for high-slope backend engineers who want to help rebuild and transform the X Ads Platform using SpaceXSI's infrastructure and AI stack. This role is a great fit for engineers who learn quickly, ship fast, and want to work on low-latency, high-QPS distributed systems that operate at massive scale. You do not need to have deep ads experience already, but you should be excited to learn how large-scale advertising systems work — from serving and auctions to event pipelines, attribution, billing, and reliability. You'll work on the core backend services and infrastructure that directly impact the majority of X's revenue. RESPONSIBILITIES: • Design, build, and operate backend services and infrastructure across the X Ads Platform • Work on systems related to ad serving, real-time bidding and auctions, pacing and budgeting, and data pipelines for ads events, attribution, and billing • Build and scale low-latency, high-QPS distributed services, including storage, reliability, and observability • Use SpaceXSI's infrastructure and AI capabilities to improve the performance and reliability of advertising systems • Collaborate closely with engineers, product leads, and cross-functional partners to deliver production-ready systems • Learn quickly, take ownership, and operate in a fast-moving environment where high-quality execution matters • Debug, maintain, and improve production systems that serve real traffic and drive significant business impact BASIC QUALIFICATIONS: • 2+ years of experience building software through internships, projects, research, open-source work, startups, or full-time roles PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE: • Strong software engineering fundamentals • Ability to write clean, maintainable code and learn new codebases quickly • Strong problem-solving skills and comfort working through ambiguity • Experience building backend services, distributed systems, or data pipelines • A bias toward action: you like shipping, operating systems in production, and improving quickly • Strong ownership mindset and willingness to take responsibility for meaningful parts of the stack • Interest in low-latency systems, high-throughput services, reliability, or observability • Experience with ads, real-time bidding, auctions, serving, pacing, recommendations, search, or ranking systems • Experience working with large-scale backend systems or data processing pipelines • Experience with Golang, Python, Rust, C++, Java, Scala • Experience with storage systems, streaming/event pipelines, or production reliability and observability • Prior startup, research, internship, or high-impact project experience COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS: $140,000 - $225,000 USD Base salary is just one part of our total rewards package at SpaceXSI, which also includes equity,
