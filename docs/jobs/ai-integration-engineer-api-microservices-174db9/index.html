Job Description: AI Integration Engineer Location: Pune (Hybrid) Experience: 5+ Years Shift Timing: 2:00 PM 11:00 PM IST (Flexible) Overview: We are seeking a skilled AI Integration Engineer with a strong background in backend development and hands-on experience integrating AI/ML solutions into production systems. The ideal candidate will play a key role in connecting AI capabilities with real-world applications, ensuring scalability, performance, and seamless system interoperability. Key Responsibilities: - Design, develop, and implement AI/ML integrations into existing and new applications. - Collaborate with data scientists, ML engineers, and product teams to deploy AI models into production. - Build and maintain scalable APIs and microservices to support AI-driven functionalities. - Integrate third-party AI services (LLMs, NLP, Computer Vision APIs, etc.) into business workflows. - Optimize system performance, latency, and reliability of AI-powered applications. - Ensure secure and efficient data pipelines for AI model consumption. - Work with cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP) for deployment and scaling. - Troubleshoot, debug, and enhance AI integration pipelines. Required Skills & Qualifications: - 5+ years of experience in backend or full-stack development. - Strong programming expertise in one or more of the following: Node.js, Python, Java, or Go (Golang). - Proven experience in integrating AI/ML models or AI-powered APIs into applications. - Solid understanding of RESTful APIs, microservices architecture, and system design. - Experience working with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP. - Familiarity with databases (SQL/NoSQL) and data handling for AI applications. - Understanding of AI/ML concepts (LLMs, NLP, embeddings, model deployment, etc). - Experience with containerization tools like Docker and CI/CD pipelines. Preferred Qualifications: - Experience working with LLM frameworks (OpenAI APIs, LangChain, etc.) - Exposure to vector databases (Pinecone, FAISS, Weaviate, etc.) - Knowledge of event-driven architectures and message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ). - Prior experience in building AI-powered products or platforms.
