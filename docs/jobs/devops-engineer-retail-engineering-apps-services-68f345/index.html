The Retail Engineering Apps & Services(REAS) team is responsible for delivering the e- commerce systems and solutions that enable and support the buying experience on apple.com. We build one of the slickest e-commerce site on planet while keeping Apple brand at the forefront in every aspect of the engineering. Our solutions are designed for global reach while keeping the regional nuances in mind. We use the latest in open source technologies in addition to building our own frameworks. The breadth of our engineering spans front-end technologies, middleware services, application servers, Cloud infrastructure (AWS along with other leading cloud providers) and various type of relational, newSQL and noSQL databases and sure to cater to anyone looking for a significant career in a highly transparent and rewarding environment. We are seeking a skilled DevOps Engineer who is passionate about operational excellence through automation and engineering processes. As a DevOps Engineer, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the seamless integration of development and operations processes, to deliver best in class and highly available systems.
