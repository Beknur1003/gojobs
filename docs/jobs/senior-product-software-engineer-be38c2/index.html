Senior Product Software Engineer

Group 1001 Zionsville, Indiana опубликовано 16 ноября 2026

Group 1001 is a consumer-centric, technology-driven family of insurance companies on a mission to deliver outstanding value and operational performance by combining financial strength and stability with deep insurance expertise and a can-do culture. Group1001’s culture emphasizes the importance of collaboration, communication, core business focus, risk management, and striving for outcomes. This goal extends to how we hire and onboard our most valuable assets – our employees. Why This Role Matters: As a Product Software Engineer at Group 1001, you own the distance between what our AI-assisted development capabilities can do and what teams actually get out of them. Our agentic ecosystem includes both the platform that engineering teams build on, and the managed development environments with integrated AI coding assistants that let engineers and business technologists alike build and deploy applications in a fast, safe, and consistent way. This is a forward-deployed role where your customers are colleagues. You will frequently engage with application teams, platform and infrastructure teams, and business technologists adopting the agentic platforms we are consistently evolving. If and when you help these partners write code to better leverage these platforms, you then generalize and upstream the capability into our platform/ecosystem. You remain accountable to the platform product rather than becoming staff augmentation for whichever team asks loudest. Adoption of our work and our offerings is always earned. Overselling costs more here than it would with an external customer; our internal relationship has no end date. Your customers can read your code and your backlog, and they will have opinions about both; this is a feature, not a bug. The ideal candidate is a strong software engineer who would rather spend a week finding out why a team abandoned a tool than a week adding a feature nobody asked for. This is a senior individual-contributor role with product ownership. How You'll Contribute: • Deeply engage with teams to learn how they actually work, not how some guide says they work, then build something real that will truly work for them. The deliverable is their workflow running, not a recommendation that it could.Fix it locally, then upstream the pattern. • Enable teams with local fixes to get people going; but leaving a local fix in place is an anti-pattern. • Generalize bespoke fixes and features, then land them in our platform; delete the bespoke version. If something cannot be generalized, say so and articulate why. • Translate between three audiences who describe the same problem differently - application engineers, central platform teams, and business technologists. Each has a vocabulary, a set of constraints, and a threshold for what counts as "working," and they rarely match. • Build the golden paths and the reference implementations. Most adoption failures are not capability gaps; they are the absence of a worked example close enough to someone's real problem to start from. • Carry actionable signal back to the roadmap. Anecdotes from one frustrated team vs. a pattern across six teams are different inputs and deserve different responses. Know which one you are holding and actioning on. • Earn trust. Show up consistently, learn the domain, fix something small early, and be direct about what the tooling can and cannot do. • Say no to work that belongs somewhere else. Some of what you are handed is a backlog item, another team's responsibility, or a problem the platform should not solve. Routing it correctly is more useful than absorbing it. • Write the documentation you wished existed when you got there. What released, what is coming, how to use it, and what it will not do for you. What We’re Looking For: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience. • 7+ years building and shipping production software, including end-to-end ownership of something real; design, delivery, operation, and the respective consequences. • Proficiency in one or more modern programming languages (e.g. Python, Golang, JavaScript/TypeScript) at production quality. You will be writing code other teams depend on, not prototypes they rewrite. • Hands-on experience building and/or working with agentic LLM applications and supporting infrastructure: tool-calling, MCP or comparable integration patterns, context engineering, evaluation design, and working with non-deterministic behavior in systems that need to be dependable. • CI/CD and infrastructure-as-code fluency: pipeline design, containers, Terraform or equivalent. Backend engineering fundamentals: service and API design, contracts, and integration with enterprise systems and data pipelines you did not build and cannot change. • Product judgment: deciding what is not worth building and distinguishing one team's preference from a genuine platform gap. • Track record working directly with people who are not engineers, turning an ambiguous workflow into something a system can actually execute. • Strong written communication. This role will be expected to produce documented analysis, plans, and designs that both senior leaders and peers will act on. • Mindset for security as a first-class feature. Just getting something functional is not enough; product safety and durability are imperative. Compensation: Our compensation reflects the cost of labor across several U.S. geographic markets. The total compensation for this position ranges from $160,000/year in our lowest geographic market up to $240,000/year in our highest geographic market. Pay is based on a number of factors including market location and may vary depending on job-related knowledge, skills, and experience. Benefits Highlights: Employees who meet benefit eligibility guidelines and work 30 hours or more weekly, have the ability to enroll in Group 1001’s benefits package. Employees (and their families) are eligible to participate in the Company’s comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance plan options. Employees are also eligible for Basic and Supplemental Life Insurance, Short and Long-Term Disability. All employees (regardless of hours worked) have immediate access to the Company’s Employee Assistance Program and wellness programs—no enrollment is required. Employees may also participate in the Company’s 401K plan, with matching contributions by the Company. Group 1001, and its affiliated companies, is strongly committed to providing a supportive work environment where employee differences are valued. Diversity is an essential ingredient in making Group 1001 a welcoming place to work and is fundamental in building a high-performance team. Diversity embodies all the differences that make us unique individuals. All employees share the responsibility for maintaining a workplace culture of dignity, respect, understanding and appreciation of individual and group differences. #LI-AS1 #LI-REMOTE
