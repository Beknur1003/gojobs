Software Architect Job Description We are looking for an experienced Software Architect to design and deliver scalable, secure and high-performance software solutions. The successful candidate will work closely with Engineering, Product and DevOps teams to translate business requirements into robust technical architectures and reliable software solutions. Key Responsibilities • Design and develop scalable distributed systems using Java, Golang and Node.js. • Architect and implement microservices-based and cloud-native applications on AWS. • Design high-performance backend systems using concurrency, caching and event-driven architecture. • Lead the architecture and development of enterprise and financial technology applications. • Design, optimise and maintain SQL and NoSQL databases, including MySQL, Redis and MongoDB. • Apply appropriate database strategies including indexing, sharding and replication to support scalability and performance. • Develop and improve CI/CD pipelines and containerised deployments using Docker and Kubernetes. • Conduct system profiling, load testing and performance optimisation to improve system reliability, scalability and response time. • Design secure and highly available systems with appropriate authentication, encryption and industry security practices. • Collaborate with Engineering, Product and DevOps teams to establish technical standards and deliver business solutions. • Provide technical guidance and mentorship on software architecture, distributed systems and application performance. Job Requirements • Diploma or Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related discipline. • At least 10 years of relevant experience in software development and software architecture. • Strong hands-on experience in Java, Spring Boot and distributed systems. • Strong experience with AWS, Docker, Kubernetes and CI/CD. • Good knowledge of microservices architecture, concurrency, networking and distributed systems. • Strong experience with SQL and NoSQL databases, including MySQL, Redis and MongoDB. • Good understanding of software design principles, data structures and algorithms. • Experience in financial technology, trading platforms or other high-performance systems will be an advantage. • Strong analytical, problem-solving and communication skills. • Able to work effectively with cross-functional teams and provide technical leadership.
