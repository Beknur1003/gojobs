Yahoo serves as a trusted guide for hundreds of millions of people globally, helping them achieve their goals online through our portfolio of iconic products. A Little About Us: The Enterprise IT team at Yahoo is seeking a Senior Software Engineer to lead the development of AI-powered productivity tools and modern web applications that revolutionize how our employees work. In this role, you will lead the design and implementation of enterprise-grade software solutions that leverage the latest advancements in AI and cloud technologies to accelerate business workflows, increasing efficiency and productivity across the organization. As a key member of the IT - Productivity, Support, and Shared Engineering team, you will contribute to the development of enterprise-grade software solutions that integrate cutting-edge AI technologies to streamline business processes and significantly boost employee productivity. Responsibilities: • Design, architect, and deliver scalable AI-enabled software solutions that address complex business challenges. • Collaborate with product managers and engineers across different time zones to understand requirements and develop technical solutions. • Lead technical initiatives and mentor junior engineers across the team. • Evaluate and integrate emerging AI technologies and frameworks, including generative AI and large language models. • Architect RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) patterns and AI-enabled workflows that replace manual data processing with automated, intelligent systems. • Drive adoption of cloud-native architectures using AWS or GCP services to ensure scalability, reliability, and security. • Collaborate with cross-functional partners to define requirements, set technical direction, and deliver impactful solutions. • Continuously monitor industry trends in AI productivity and quickly translate innovations into actionable solutions for employees. Basic Qualifications: • Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field. • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience for full stack development, DevOps, and AI/ML development, including recent experience in a senior developer role. • Strong programming skills in languages such as Python, Golang or TypeScript. • Experience integrating AI/ML technologies and services (e.g., LLMs, natural language processing, machine learning APIs). • Expertise in structured interaction with AI tools (prompt engineering) and the ability to exercise judgment in AI-augmented vs. manual development approaches. • Excellent communication skills and a track record of cross-functional collaboration. • Strong understanding of system design, architecture patterns, and security best practices. Preferred Qualifications: • Strong understanding of data structures, algorithms, and software design principles. • Familiarity with cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) and containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes). • Experience in AI platforms, GenAI, and machine learning application development. • Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills, with strong attention to detail. • A proactive attitude towards evolving skill sets and staying current with technology trends. • Capable of leading in a collaborative environment involving developing solutions, presenting those solutions for review by peers, and training co-workers on the solutions. • Team player with good interpersonal skills and the ability to work independently. • Background in leading agile development teams and delivering high-impact software solutions at scale. Why You Should Join Us: As a Senior Engineer on this team, you’ll have the opportunity to shape the future of AI-driven productivity at the enterprise level. You’ll work at the intersection of emerging AI technologies and real business needs—creating tools that empower Yahoo employees to work smarter and faster.
