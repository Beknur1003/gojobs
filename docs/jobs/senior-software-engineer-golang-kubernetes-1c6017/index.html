We are looking for a highly experienced and driven Senior Software Engineer to join our AI and container cloud infrastructure delivery team. In this role, you will design, develop, and maintain code that extends and customizes CNCF container platform components — including Kubernetes and its ecosystem — to run AI workloads on cutting-edge NVIDIA-certified hardware from leading vendors. You will work hand in hand with our Senior Site Reliability Engineers on the same customer projects. While the SRE deploys and operates the platform, you will own the software changes it requires: Kubernetes operators and controllers, custom resources, scheduler extensions, device plugins, admission webhooks, CNI/CSI integrations, and patches to upstream projects. Together you will turn architecture and implementation designs into reliable, secure, and performant production systems. As a senior engineer, you will work closely with stakeholders to shape technical solutions, solve complex engineering challenges, and mentor team members and Mirantis customers. This is an excellent opportunity to make a significant impact on open source infrastructure for AI in a rapidly evolving cloud ecosystem. Main Responsibilities • Design, develop, test, and maintain software that extends and modifies Kubernetes and other CNCF components for AI workloads (operators, controllers, CRDs, schedulers, device plugins, admission webhooks, CNI/CSI drivers). • Partner closely with Senior SREs on shared projects, turning operational requirements and field findings into robust code changes. • Integrate GPU and high-performance networking and storage capabilities into the container platform, working with NVIDIA software stacks such as the GPU Operator and Network Operator. • Fork, patch, and maintain open source components where customer needs require it, and contribute fixes and features back upstream. • Build automation, tooling, and CI/CD pipelines for building, testing, and releasing platform components. • Collaborate with stakeholders to gather and refine technical requirements and translate them into software designs. • Optimize the performance, reliability, scalability, and security of platform code. • Troubleshoot, debug, and resolve complex issues across the software, Kubernetes, Linux, and hardware layers. • Write and participate in code reviews to maintain high quality standards. • Work with geographically distributed international teams on technical challenges and process improvements. • Apply AI-assisted development practices across the software lifecycle, including code generation, testing, and maintenance. • Produce clear technical documentation and facilitate knowledge transfer to customers during delivery. • Stay up to date with industry trends and best practices in cloud-native and AI infrastructure development. Required Skills/Abilities • 5+ years of professional software development experience, with a strong focus on cloud and infrastructure software. • Strong proficiency in Golang, with working knowledge of other programming languages (Python, Bash, and ideally Rust or C). • Hands-on experience extending Kubernetes: writing controllers or operators (client-go, controller-runtime, Kubebuilder or Operator SDK), CRDs, and webhooks. • Solid understanding of Kubernetes internals (API server, scheduler, kubelet, container runtimes) and how its extension points work. • Strong knowledge of distributed systems, microservices architecture, API design, and CI/CD pipelines. • Good understanding of Linux systems, networking, and storage as they relate to containerized workloads. • Solid software engineering practices: automated testing (unit, integration, e2e), version control with Git, code review, and release management. • Exceptional problem-solving and debugging skills, with attention to performance optimization and security. • Demonstrated ability to lead technical tasks and collaborate effectively with diverse teams, including operations and SRE colleagues. • Comfortable making independent judgment calls when working directly with customers, often with limited day-to-day oversight. • Excellent written and spoken English. • Excellent customer-facing communication skills. • Ability to travel up to 25% if needed, including internationally. Nice to have • Upstream contributions to Kubernetes or other CNCF projects (e.g., Cluster API, Kueue, Volcano, KubeVirt, Cilium, Istio, Helm). • Experience with GPU and high-performance computing software: Kubernetes device plugins, Dynamic Resource Allocation (DRA), GPU scheduling, MIG/vGPU, NVIDIA GPU Operator and Network Operator, DCGM, or NVIDIA AI Enterprise. • Experience with RDMA/RoCE or InfiniBand fabrics, NVLink, SR-IOV, or Multus networking in Kubernetes. • Experience with AI/ML platform components such as Kubeflow, KServe, Ray, or LLM inference servers (vLLM, Triton). • Experience building CNI or CSI plugins, or deep knowledge of network and/or storage architecture. • Familiarity with GitOps tooling (Flux, Argo CD) and policy engines (OPA/Gatekeeper, Kyverno). • Experience with OpenStack development or operations. • Prior experience with commercial container and virtual compute platforms such as Rancher, OpenShift, and VMware. • CKAD/CKA/CKS certification will be a plus. We offer: • Operate some of the most advanced AI infrastructure environments in production today. • Work with the latest NVIDIA GPU technologies, Kubernetes platforms, and high-performance networking environments. • Help define operational standards and reliability practices for next-generation AI infrastructure services. • Influence the adoption of AI-powered operational capabilities through k0rdent AI. • Work alongside highly skilled engineers solving complex infrastructure and platform challenges at scale. • Join a growing organisation investing heavily in AI infrastructure, platform services, and operational innovation. #Remote It is understood that Mirantis, Inc. may use automated decision-making technology (ADMT) for specific employment-related decisions.
