Top 3 Reasons To Join Us Advance your career in the gaming industry Take ownership and grow with global talents Build games played by millions worldwide The Job About GDS GDS is the data backbone of VNGGames. We centralize data from every game studio into one platform and turn it into analytics products used daily by producers, game operators, marketers and analysts: Analytics Hub, Dashboard Builder / Live Artifacts, Monitoring Hub, game performance dashboards, and AI-assisted data exploration tools. About the role As a Product Engineer, you don’t just build what’s in the ticket. You own problems end to end: you understand how our users work with data, shape the solution with Product and Design, build it, ship it, and measure whether it actually made their work better. You’ll join a small, fast-moving team where engineers have real influence on what we build next. What you’ll do • Own features end to end, from discovery and scoping through design, build and release, then keep iterating after launch. • Talk directly with internal users (game studios, LiveOps, marketing, analysts) to understand their pain points and turn feedback into product improvements. • Partner with PM and Design to define problems, propose solutions, and make smart trade-offs between scope, quality and speed. • Design, build and maintain backend services and APIs (primarily in Go) that power our analytics products, with high standards for availability, latency and security. • Work across the stack when needed (frontend, data pipelines, integrations) to get features into users’ hands. • Build AI-powered experiences such as natural-language data exploration and LLM-assisted insights, and use AI tools daily to ship faster. • Instrument features, define success metrics, run experiments or A/B tests, and use data to decide what to build next. • Raise the engineering bar through code reviews, performance tuning, security improvements, and pragmatic CI/CD and team processes. Your Skills and Experience Must-have • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 4+ years of software engineering experience with strong backend skills in Go. Strong Java (Spring Boot) or Node.js engineers willing to work in Go are also welcome. • A track record of shipping user-facing products or features end to end and improving them based on user feedback or usage data. • A product mindset: you care about the “why” and about user outcomes, you’re comfortable with ambiguity, and you can turn vague problems into clear scope. • Solid experience with SQL and NoSQL databases (MySQL, MongoDB, Redis) and with API design (REST; gRPC is a plus). • Experience with event-driven architecture and message brokers such as Kafka. • Hands-on use of AI coding tools (e.g., Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) to accelerate delivery. • Clear communication with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Nice-to-have • Frontend or full-stack experience (React, TypeScript). • Experience building data or analytics products: dashboards, BI tools, query layers, or OLAP databases (e.g., ClickHouse, Druid, BigQuery). • Experience integrating LLMs into products (prompting, RAG, tool calling, agents). • Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and cloud platforms. • Experience with A/B testing, product analytics, unit testing and clean code practices. • Background in gaming, B2B SaaS, or internal tools/platform products. • Experience working in Agile/Scrum teams. Why You'll Love Working Here • Great working environment & large scale payment-Projects. • Fast adoption with new & trending technology. • Opportunity to rotate in products lines. • Attractive package & long-term career path.
