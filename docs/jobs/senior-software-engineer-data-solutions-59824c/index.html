This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Software Engineer, Data Solutions based in the United States. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will play a key role in building next-generation data products that power advanced marketing technologies, analytics, and AI-driven applications. You will help transform massive volumes of proprietary, licensed, and third-party data into reliable, reusable assets that support critical business functions. Working with modern technologies such as Flink, Spark, Kafka, Iceberg, Go, and Kubernetes, you will design scalable data infrastructure and intelligent data services. You will collaborate closely with Product, Data Science, and Engineering teams to turn complex data challenges into impactful solutions. This role offers the opportunity to shape the foundations of an AI-forward technology ecosystem while influencing products used by customers and internal teams worldwide. Accountabilities: • Design, develop, deploy, and maintain end-to-end data products, including audience segments, signals, data enrichments, APIs, and supporting services. • Build foundational data infrastructure that provides AI assistants and agents with reliable contextual information about audiences, campaigns, inventory, and performance. • Develop and maintain scalable batch and real-time data pipelines using technologies such as Apache Flink, Spark, and Kafka, publishing data into an Iceberg-based architecture. • Establish clear data contracts, including schema standards, freshness expectations, ownership, and quality requirements. • Create reusable frameworks and processes for integrating third-party and partner datasets efficiently across multiple products. • Transform raw platform data into actionable insights, including audience characteristics, inventory signals, and performance benchmarks that improve targeting and recommendations. • Collaborate with Product, Data Science, Engineering, and external partners to identify business needs and define technical solutions. • Lead projects throughout their entire lifecycle, from technical architecture and implementation to production deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement. • Integrate AI-powered tools and agents into everyday development workflows while contributing to best practices for AI-assisted engineering. • Mentor fellow engineers, contribute to technical design discussions, and strengthen engineering standards through code and architecture reviews. Requirements • At least 5 years of professional experience in backend, data, or platform software engineering, with strong programming skills in Java, Scala, Python, or Go. • Demonstrated experience building and delivering reliable data products or platforms used by other engineering teams or external customers. • Hands-on experience with distributed data processing, streaming technologies, and modern lakehouse or data warehouse platforms such as Flink, Spark, Kafka, Iceberg, Redshift, ClickHouse, or Trino. • Strong understanding of data modeling, data contracts, data governance, and maintaining data quality at scale. • Product-oriented mindset, with the ability to understand how data solutions serve users and contribute to measurable business outcomes. • Ability to work effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders, translating ambiguous requirements into clear, scalable technical solutions. • Familiarity with AI-powered development tools and a strong interest in building reliable data foundations for AI agents and intelligent applications. • Strong problem-solving, communication, collaboration, and technical decision-making skills. Additional assets: • Proficiency in Golang. • Experience collaborating with privacy, compliance, or legal teams on data usage, consent management, licensing, or clean-room environments. • Experience developing AI-powered products, including LLM applications, autonomous agents, or recommendation systems. • Familiarity with data infrastructure for machine learning and AI, including features, embeddings, vector databases, retrieval systems, or MCP servers. • Previous experience in advertising technology (AdTech), marketing technology (MarTech), or related industries. • Experience building data catalogs, data marketplaces, or semantic layers. Benefits • Competitive annual base salary ranging from $170,400 to $234,300 USD, subject to location, experience, technical expertise, and other relevant factors. • Potential eligibility for additional compensation, including annual bonuses and equity awards, depending on the role. • Comprehensive health benefits starting on the first day of employment. • Global retirement savings programs, including 401(k) and pension plans, depending on location. • Competitive paid time off, including an additional day off for your birthday. • Access to comprehensive mental health support and wellness resources. • Work-from-home reimbursements to support your remote working environment. • Optional global WeWork membership for access to flexible workspaces. • Generous parental leave programs. • Structured onboarding, training, and continuous professional development opportunities. • Financial support for conferences, courses, books, and other learning initiatives. • Access to specialized industry training and professional certifications. • A welcoming, collaborative, and inclusive workplace culture. • Opportunities to participate in social activities and team events.
