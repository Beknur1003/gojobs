Software Development Engineer III (Fullstack)

The core responsibilities for the job include the following: Feature and Module Ownership: • Translate business goals into working, deployable systems. • Propose architecture, data models, workflows, and implementation plans for your modules, then refine them with tech leads and engineering managers before you build. • Own modules end-to-end: design, release, post-launch stability, and ongoing improvements. • Design clean, versioned, well-documented APIs as a core part of every feature, so the platform can be integrated wherever it's needed. Rapid Prototyping and Iteration: • Turn ambiguous ideas into working demos quickly, using AI to build and iterate fast. • Validate through experiments and real user feedback, then iterate to production quality. • Drive feature attribution and metrics visibility so every release is measurable. Scale, Performance, and Reliability: • Build and maintain the email ecosystem, designing for scalability, reliability, and data integrity at 40M+ emails/day. • Evolve our sending infrastructure, including IP pool management, dedicated IPs and domains, and shared domain pools, to protect sender reputation and maximize inbox placement. • Build robust error handling: idempotent processing, retries with backoff, and graceful degradation. • Optimize frontend rendering, component architecture, lazy loading, and state management. Tune backend queries, caching (Redis/CDN), and latency at scale. • Design data models and analytics on ClickHouse, MongoDB, and Firestore. • Reason through token consumption, inference cost, and infrastructure tradeoffs when integrating AI and LLMs. Engineering Quality and Security: • Practice test-driven development and hold a high bar for automated test coverage. • Build securely by default: authentication, secrets management, and safe handling of customer data and PII. Operational Excellence: • Instrument systems with monitoring and telemetry. • Build evaluation loops for continuous quality improvement. • Automate workflows that increase engineering velocity. • Keep documentation clear and repeatable. Requirements: • 4+ years building and running production systems, including owning them when they broke: the on-call, the incident, and the fix. • Node.js (TypeScript) at scale: You've shipped and operated high-throughput backends (queues, workers, retries, millions of events a day) and built real product UIs in Vue.js (or React). • Systems that grew: You've designed or re-architected a system for 10x growth and can explain the trade-offs you made in queues, caching, partitioning, and storage. • Data you can trust: You've built pipelines where losing or duplicating a record wasn't acceptable, using idempotency, deduplication, reconciliation, and error handling that fails loudly, not quietly. • Data modelling and analytics: You've modelled data for transactional and analytical workloads and made queries fast over billions of rows in ClickHouse (or BigQuery, Snowflake, or similar). • Cloud in production: You've run services on GCP or AWS (Kubernetes, Pub/Sub or SQS, managed databases, IAM). • Quality and security by habit: You write tests first or alongside code (TDD) and treat auth, secrets, PII, and abuse prevention as part of the design, not a review step. • AI in the product: You've shipped at least one AI or LLM-powered feature to real users and can walk through the model choice, prompts, evals, cost, and latency. • AI in your workflow: You use AI coding tools every day, go from idea to working prototype in hours or days, and still own and stand behind every line that ships. • Problem solving and ownership: You take vague problems, break them down, and get to a working answer without waiting for a ticket. Bonus Points: • Experience with Go (Golang). • Experience in the email domain: email rendering, templates, SMTP/ESPs, or deliverability (IP warm-up, sender reputation, SPF/DKIM/DMARC). • Experience with data pipelines, analytics, or telemetry. • Strong UX and front-end sensibility. • A portfolio or GitHub showing deployed systems.
