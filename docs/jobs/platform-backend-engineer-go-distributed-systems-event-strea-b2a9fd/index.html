Job title Platform Backend Engineer (Go, Distributed Systems & Event Streaming) Job Summary We're hiring Platform Backend Engineers to build and operate the distributed systems powering event processing, workflow execution, and shared services across Kaseya's platform. This is a hands-on engineering role focused on Go, Kubernetes, event-driven architecture, and production reliability. You'll work on high-throughput event pipelines, reliable messaging, and backend services that support automation and AI-enabled workflows across multiple products. Required Qualifications • Experience building and operating production backend services using Go • Experience designing event-driven or streaming systems using Kafka, Numaflow, Flink, or similar technologies • Experience developing distributed systems that handle retries, idempotency, ordering, backpressure, and failure recovery • Experience building or operating containerized services using Kubernetes in production • Experience troubleshooting production distributed systems, including performance, observability, and reliability issues Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Numaflow, Apache Flink, Kafka Streams, or other distributed stream-processing platforms • Experience building Kubernetes-native platforms, operators, or custom controllers • Experience with Temporal or another durable workflow orchestration platform • Experience with multi-tenant SaaS platforms, identity, authorization, or secrets management • Experience with event ingestion, CDC, data pipelines, or real-time processing infrastructure • Experience with GitOps, Terraform, CI/CD, or cloud-native infrastructure automation • Experience with ClickHouse, analytical data stores, or event-driven audit systems #ID525 Additional information Kaseya provides equal employment opportunity to all employees and applicants without regard to race, religion, age, ancestry, gender, sex, sexual orientation, national origin, citizenship status, physical or mental disability, veteran status, marital status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.
