Shaping the Future of Foodtech Scalability, efficiency, and seamless integrations – that’s what drives our platform. You'll play a key role in shaping our backend infrastructure, developing high-performance APIs, and optimizing our internal tools to ensure smooth operations and continuous innovation. Your Mission at Foodji • Develop and maintain cutting-edge REST APIs to enhance platform efficiency and scalability • Seamlessly integrate external APIs (e.g., payment providers, ERP systems) for smooth data flow • Improve internal tools to streamline machine management, product handling, orders, and customer interactions • Continuously evolve our tech stack to keep Foodji at the forefront of foodtech innovation • Take ownership of projects, implement technical improvements, and shape the future of our platform Our Tech Stack • Go, AWS (Lambda, Fargate, API Gateway), DynamoDB, MongoDB • Docker, GitHub Actions, CloudFormation • Angular + Ionic for internal tools and apps • Python for ML and forecasting Requirements YOUR PROFILE • Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field • At least 4 years professional experience in Golang and MongoDB • First-hand exposure to AWS Lambda, Fargate, and Docker • A proactive mindset with a passion for problem-solving and ownership of projects • Ability to work independently while thriving in a fast-paced startup environment • Enthusiasm for innovation and a drive to continuously improve and optimize our systems • Strong English communication skills Any Questions? We’d love to hear from you and are happy to help! people@foodji.com WHY US? • Fully Remote: Work from wherever you do your best work. • Amazing Team: You will join a small backend team working mainly with Go, building the cloud platform behind our Foodji machines. The team collaborates closely with our Machine Learning Engineers working on demand prediction and with frontend developers building internal tools and apps. As part of a small and fast-moving team, you will have significant ownership over features and technical improvements. • Cutting-Edge Tech: Our platform is powered by the AWS Serverless Stack, GoLang, Python ML models, and more – allowing us to deploy and scale fast without the classic DevOps hassle. • Continuous Learning: We empower our teams by sharing knowledge in our Engineering Meetings and Engineering Talks. Your growth is important to us – we schedule regular feedback talks and training, as well as offering exciting opportunities for career progression. • Team Events: We are one team – whether it's our culture week, or office party, we celebrate together! ABOUT US Hi, glad you're here! We are Foodji - a passionate team of Food-Lovers & Tech-Nerds. Our mission is to make fresh, healthy and, above all, delicious food available wherever people spend most of their day: in offices, whether in business parks or city centers; during long shifts in hospitals; or deep inside production halls where people work around the clock. Wherever and whenever you need it, we offer wholesome meals, snacks and drinks right there. Our proprietary technology, self-learning algorithms and efficient supply chains help us solve the challenge of making fresh food available sustainably. With our own technology platform, we optimize planning and fight food waste. We want to make a positive difference — for the environment, our customers’ everyday lives and our communities. That’s why we donate leftover meals to Foodsharing and other local social initiatives. As the market leader, we already serve thousands of people across Germany every day. Our customers include Munich Airport, Klarna, Enpal and Wingcopter. And we’re just getting started: in Germany alone, there are more than 100,000 locations that could be relevant to us. If you're ready to make a difference, join our team and make your contribution towards our mission.
