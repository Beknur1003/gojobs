Position Summary... The Staff Software Engineer will provide technical leadership for Walmart's next-generation UGC platform, driving the design and evolution of cloud-native, event-driven distributed systems that power customer ratings, reviews, and seller feedback across Walmart's global eCommerce ecosystem. The role is responsible for defining technical strategy, leading architecture decisions, mentoring engineers, and delivering highly scalable backend services built on Golang, Kafka, Kubernetes, Cosmos DB, and BigQuery. As a technical leader, the Staff Engineer will influence cross-functional teams, modernize the platform through engineering best practices and automation, and ensure high availability, low latency, and operational excellence. By enabling a reliable and scalable customer-generated content platform, this role directly strengthens customer trust, product discoverability, and conversion while accelerating Walmart's digital transformation and long-term business growth. What you'll do... About the Team The UGC – Ratings & Reviews team at Walmart Global Tech builds and operates the next-generation UGC 2.0platform that powers customer ratings, written reviews, image reviews, video reviews, and seller reviews across Walmart's web and mobile experiences. UGC is a cloud-native platform built on an Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) using Golang microservices, Kafka, Cosmos DB, BigQuery, Kubernetes, and Azure/GCP. The platform enables real-time ingestion, moderation, storage, and delivery of customer-generated content at Walmart scale while providing high availability, low latency, and operational resilience. Customer-generated content is a key driver of customer trust, product discoverability, and purchase conversion. As Walmart continues to modernize its eCommerce platform, UGC serves as the strategic foundation for delivering scalable, reliable, and innovative customer experiences.
