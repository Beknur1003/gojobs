Product Architect - Distributed Systems

Taglynk Careers опубликовано 3 июля 2026

Lead
AWSGCPAzureMicroservicesPythonRustJavaAI/LLM
Impact Analytics is building next-generation AI-native SaaS products used by global enterprises across retail, supply chain, forecasting, and decision intelligence. We are looking for a seasoned Product Architect to drive the architecture strategy for highly scalable, cloud-native platforms that handle massive volumes of data and transactions. Responsibilities : - Define and drive architecture for large-scale distributed systems. - Design highly available, low-latency, event-driven microservices. - Lead technical decisions across cloud infrastructure, backend platforms, data systems, and platform engineering. - Architect systems around messaging, caching, distributed queues, and high-throughput processing. - Drive engineering excellence, scalability, observability, resiliency, and security best practices. - Partner with engineering leaders to shape long-term technology strategy. - Evaluate emerging technologies and lead architecture modernization initiatives. Requirements : - 12+ years of software engineering experience. - 4+ years in Architect, Principal Engineer, or Staff Engineer roles. - Strong expertise in distributed systems, microservices, and cloud-native architectures. - Hands-on experience with Python, Java, Golang, Rust, or similar backend technologies. - Deep understanding of event-driven architecture, REST APIs, message brokers, caching, concurrency, and system design. - Experience building products at scale, serving millions of users or processing large data volumes. - Strong exposure to AWS, GCP, or Azure environments. - Experience mentoring engineering teams and driving architecture decisions across organizations. Preferred Background: - Experience building scale products from scratch. - Exposure to Big Data, Streaming, ML Platforms, or Forecasting systems is a plus.
