Summary : We are seeking a Technical Lead for the WMS and Ledger Accounting team. This Lead will drive the end-to-end agentic workflow implementation of WMS processes and products that ease the day-to-day ground operations of all warehouses. If you enjoy being an observer and have a knack for converting pain points into technology and product, this role is for you. Responsibilities : - Lead: Product and Systems thinking to design architecture and APIs for large scale. - Build: Work with product, design, and other stakeholders to understand requirements and turn them into architectural and design plans with an implementation roadmap. - Champion "Agentic" Engineering: Drive an Agentic-first approach to solution design, utilizing AI-driven development (AIDD) to solve complex engineering hurdles. - Optimize Daily: Identify bottlenecks in queries, execution plans, and overall system design to ensure a low-latency end-user experience. - Collaborate and Mentor: Work across functional teams to fine-tune system health and guide developers in writing efficient, high-performance code. - Ensure High Availability: Own the delivery of I/O optimized low-latency APIs and DB Schema that supports 24/7 uptime and exponential scalability. - Security: Implement Security Principles and PII Compliance within the application schema and access policies. - System Design: Develop and sustain large-scale distributed platform services. - Data Driven: Keep a focus on the usage/metrics of the owned services and be customer-focused. - Mentorship: Provide hands-on technical leadership, including software design, coding, debugging, prototyping, and code reviews. Tech Stack : - Core Tech: Golang, Python, Kafka, NSQD, Redis, Elasticsearch, MongoDB, Unit Test, HTTP/TCP, Nginx, MCP, AIDD, AIRD, Mobile First APIs. - AI Tooling: Advanced expertise in Cursor and Windsurf for AI-driven development. - Architecture: Micro-service scalable architecture and asset hosting for edge performance. - Infrastructure: AWS/Azure ecosystems, Linux Kernels, and automated backup/DR strategies. Required Qualifications : - 5-7 years of experience in distributed system design, performance tuning, and cloud-based best practices. - Hands-on experience building high-scale D2C, B2B, attribution applications, order management systems, and product catalogs. - Deep expertise in DSA, LLD, HLD, ACID, caching, distributed system design, DBMS, Garbage Collector, microservices, VM, K8S, data pipelines, Kafka, and NSQD. - Proven ability to navigate the challenges of early-stage product journeys. - Mastery of Git-based workflows and performance profiling tools.
