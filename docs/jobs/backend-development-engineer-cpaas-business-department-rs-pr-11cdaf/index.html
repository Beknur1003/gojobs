Job Description: About Organization We are building the next generation of our CPaaS platform, a robust ecosystem powering multi-channel communication services. Our platform supports global traffic across SMS, Email, Voice, WhatsApp, LINE, RCS, Passkey, and advanced Authentication services. We are looking for a highly skilled Backend Engineer to drive the scalability, reliability, and evolution of these services within a high-autonomy, fast-paced R&D environment. Job Duties • Backend Service Development: Design, implement, and optimize microservices powering multi-channel communication workloads. • Feature Engineering: Build and enhance backend capabilities supporting messaging, identity, verification, and communication APIs. • Distributed Systems Engineering: Develop high-throughput, low-latency services for global CPaaS traffic. • Database Engineering: Design schemas, optimize queries, and ensure peak performance across databases. • Cloud-Native Architecture: Deploy and operate services on Kubernetes, ensuring resilience, scalability, and cost-efficiency. • API-First Engineering: Build clean, scalable REST APIs and contribute to API-first product architecture. • CI/CD & GitOps: Manage version-controlled pipelines for build validation, automated testing, and safe deployments. • AI-Enhanced Development: Utilize LLM-based tools (e.g., Claude, Codex) to improve code quality, automate documentation, and accelerate development workflows. • Observability: Ensure system visibility using Grafana, Prometheus, and structured logging. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Product Managers, QA, DevOps/SRE, and UI/UX teams to deliver high-quality features. • Engineering Excellence: Uphold coding standards, conduct code reviews, and drive continuous improvement of engineering practices. Minimum Qualifications • 5+ years of hands-on backend development experience. • Strong proficiency in Java or Golang. • Expertise with Postgres and MySQL. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and containerization. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions preferred). • Experience with Microservices and API-first system design. • AI-Assisted Development: Proficient in leveraging LLM-based tools for coding, automation, and workflow acceleration. • Advanced English proficiency (strong communication, documentation, and collaboration skills). Preferred Qualifications • Domain experience with communication protocols (SMPP, SIP, REST APIs, WebRTC). • Experience across multiple CPaaS domains (SMS, Email, Voice, Passkey, Auth Services, WhatsApp, LINE, RCS, etc.). • Proven ability to work effectively in fast-moving, high-autonomy environments. Key Interfaces • Backend Engineering Team • QA & Automation Teams • DevOps / SRE • Product Managers • UI/UX Designers • Project Managers • R&D Leadership / CTO Languages: English (Overall - 3 - Advanced)
