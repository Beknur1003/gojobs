Warby Parker is seeking a Staff Software Engineer to lead the design and development of our scalable backend systems. You'll architect and build the platforms that power our core business, working at the intersection of large-scale data processing, API design, and distributed systems. Reporting to the Senior Director of Engineering, you'll collaborate with Product Managers and cross-functional teams to design and deliver high-performance backend services that power customer experiences across our retail and e-commerce channels. Your work will directly impact system scalability and resilience, requiring technical excellence and strategic thinking to balance business objectives with robust engineering practices.
