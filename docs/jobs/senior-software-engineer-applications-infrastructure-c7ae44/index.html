Senior Software Engineer (Applications & Infrastructure)

ACHERON PTE. LTD. Singapore опубликовано 11 августа 2026

Senior
PostgreSQLRedisgRPCKubernetesDockerTerraformAWSGCPMicroservicesCI/CDPythonRustFinTech
Acheron is a market maker holding a CASP license issued by the AFM in the Netherlands under MiCAr and with passporting in all EU countries. Acheron provides liquidity services to digital asset issuers from their most embryonic stages and throughout their adoption cycle. It is our belief that market inefficiency is one of the factors preventing digital assets from achieving mass adoption. We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer to lead our core Applications vertical, deputize for Infrastructure, and front engineering risk governance under our regulatory frameworks (MAS TRM / MiCA). Key Responsibilities 1. Applications Domain Leadership (Primary Scope) • Client & Commercial Touchpoints: Direct the architecture and delivery of institutional client dashboards, merchant portals, balance-tracking interfaces, and external customer API gateways. • Internal Operations & Back-Office Tooling: Oversee the development of internal admin portals, automated trade reconciliation workflows, settlement tracking interfaces, and application-level incident triage tools. • Financial Engine & Ledger Systems: Own the application logic for multi-asset ledgers, parallel accounting books (finance vs. regulatory compliance), and automated fiat/stablecoin payment and payout pipelines. • Identity & Access Governance: Enforce application security standards across all client and internal interfaces, including API key management, OAuth/JWT token rotation, MFA, and programmatic "4-eye" approval controls for sensitive operations. 2. Infrastructure Deputization & Engineering Hygiene (Secondary Scope) • Deputy for Infrastructure: Partner closely with the Senior Infrastructure Engineer to co-own platform architecture, system observability, cloud environments, and automated fallback/disaster recovery protocols. • Environment Provisioning: Direct the implementation of isolated, firewalled UAT/Staging environments, test data seeding, and mock API integrations to ensure zero unverified code touches production. • Release Pipeline Enforcement: Establish automated CI/CD gating and static analysis rules, instituting formal release discipline and production access controls across the department. Candidate Requirements: 1.Experience & Leadership • 6+ years in software engineering with 2+ years in a Engineering Leadership role. • Proven track record of guiding software engineering teams in high-velocity startup or scaling environments without needing to be an individual coding contributor. • Demonstrated experience establishing engineering processes from scratch (UAT environments, mandatory PR reviews, release governance). 2.Technical & Domain Architecture • Core Language Stack: Deep architectural familiarity and hands-on exposure to Go (Golang), Python, and Rust across microservices, payment/payout pipelines, data processing, and performance-critical backend architectures. • Distributed Systems: Deep architectural knowledge of modern distributed microservices, data layers (PostgreSQL, Redis), API design (REST, WebSockets, gRPC), and cloud-native infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform). Good to Have: • Regulated Domain Expertise: Direct experience working in a regulated fintech, payments, digital asset, or trading platform environment. • Regulatory Frameworks: Direct familiarity with MAS Technology Risk Management (TRM) guidelines, ISO 27001, or equivalent financial security/governance standards. Our Recruitment Philosophy: Leadership, trustworthiness, and reputation are at the core of Acheron Trading’s DNA. We seek individuals who are: • Analytical thinkers capable of assessing and optimizing execution strategies • Self-starters with the confidence to make informed trading decisions • Trustworthy and accountable, ensuring reliability for colleagues and clients • Committed to excellence, going above and beyond to uphold Acheron Trading’s reputation Our Offer: • Competitive salary • Placement in the Employee Revenue Share Bonus Pool (paid semiannually) • The opportunity to work with one of the most innovative and fastest-growing trading firms in the industry How to apply: Send your resume together with a brief introduction on yourself to sean.lim@acherontrading.com Note: we do not work with middle-men recruiters, any hire should be direct.
