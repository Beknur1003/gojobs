Overview of the Role: As a Director of Software Engineering, you will lead a mission-critical segment of Salesforce’s Engineering organization. You will be responsible for the architectural integrity, scalability, and reliability of distributed services that power millions of customer interactions every second. Responsibilities: • Strategic Technical Leadership: Define and execute a long-term roadmap for backend services, focusing on high availability, low latency, and massive scalability. • Navigate the Salesforce Matrix: Drive alignment across cross-functional "Clouds" (Sales, Service, Marketing, etc.). You will collaborate with dotted-line stakeholders and Architecture Councils to ensure your team's output integrates seamlessly into the broader ecosystem. • Team Scaling & Mentorship: Hire, develop, and retain a world-class org of 15–40+ engineers (including Managers and ICs). You will foster a culture of "Ownership" where engineers feel empowered to innovate. • Architectural Diplomacy: Act as a bridge between executive leadership and deep-tech engineering. You must be able to translate complex backend constraints into strategic business risks and opportunities. • Operational Excellence: Own the full software development lifecycle (SDLC). You will champion CI/CD, automated testing, and "Security-by-Design" to ensure our B2C-scale services are rock-solid. • Hands-on Technical Acumen: While you aren't coding daily, you are expected to participate in high-level design reviews and troubleshoot architectural bottlenecks in Java, Golang, or Python environments. Required Qualifications: • Engineering Pedigree: 10+ years of experience in software engineering, with at least 5 years in a significant leadership role (Manager of Managers preferred for Sr. Director). • Distributed Systems Mastery: Proven track record of building and operating large-scale distributed systems (Microservices, Service Mesh, Event-driven architectures). • Operational experience with Kubernetes, Observability, and other cloud infrastructure. • Critical eye for product design and well-versed in good design principles. • Ability to adapt and manage multiple competing priorities in a fast-paced environment. • Excellent communication and presentation skills, effective with both technical and non-technical audiences Preferred Qualifications: • The "Matrix" Skillset: Demonstrated ability to influence without direct authority and navigate complex organizational structures to get "Buy-in" for large technical migrations. • Active, hands-on proficiency with AI development tools; full utilization is the baseline expectation, along with deep technical expertise sufficient to evaluate architectural integrity and make informed decisions about AI-generated output. • Experience assessing candidates on their ability to formulate effective prompts, evaluate AI outputs, and orchestrate multi-agent workflows in addition to foundational technical skills. • Degree in Computer Science or a related technical field.
