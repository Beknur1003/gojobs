Job Title: Senior Staff Engineer Cloud Infrastructure Compute Location: Remote Duration: 12 Months (Possible Extension) Job Overview We are seeking a highly experienced Senior Staff Engineer Cloud Infrastructure Compute to provide technical leadership in the design, development, and lifecycle management of a large-scale Kubernetes compute platform. This role will focus on building a reliable, scalable, secure, and cost-efficient cloud infrastructure platform that supports engineering teams across security, networking, developer experience, and cloud operations. The ideal candidate will have deep hands-on Kubernetes engineering expertise, strong Go (Golang) proficiency, and experience developing Kubernetes controllers and admission webhooks in production environments. Key Responsibilities • Provide technical leadership for high-impact cloud infrastructure and compute platform initiatives. • Design, develop, test, deploy, maintain, and enhance Kubernetes-based infrastructure platforms. • Develop and improve Kubernetes controllers, operators, and admission webhooks. • Drive platform reliability, scalability, security, availability, and cost optimization. • Lead complex engineering projects spanning multiple teams and distributed engineering organizations. • Establish technical direction, define engineering standards, and mentor engineers. • Improve developer experience and enable product teams to deliver features efficiently. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to establish project goals, priorities, timelines, and deliverables. • Contribute to the long-term technical strategy and evolution of the cloud platform. • Support consistent infrastructure delivery across multiple public cloud providers. Required Qualifications • Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 12+ years of relevant infrastructure engineering experience. • At least 4 years of hands-on experience with public cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), or Microsoft Azure. • Advanced, hands-on Kubernetes engineering experience in production environments. • Experience developing and maintaining Kubernetes controllers. • Experience building and working with Kubernetes admission webhooks, including validating or mutating webhooks. • Strong software engineering skills, with a preference for proficiency in Go (Golang). • Experience defining technical direction and successfully delivering large-scale projects across multiple teams. • Strong understanding of infrastructure reliability, scalability, availability, security, and performance optimization. • Excellent technical leadership, collaboration, and communication skills. Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Istio, Envoy, or service mesh technologies. • Experience developing cloud-native infrastructure platforms at scale. • Experience with Kubernetes operators, custom resources (CRDs), and controller-runtime. • Background in cloud-native, DevOps, platform engineering, or infrastructure-focused organizations.
