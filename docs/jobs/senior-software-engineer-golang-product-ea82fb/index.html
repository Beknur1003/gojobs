Job Summary We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to build reliable control-plane and infrastructure automation software. You will design and develop backend services, APIs, CLI tools, controllers, agents, and distributed workflows that integrate with Kubernetes and infrastructure platforms. This role combines strong software engineering with infrastructure, distributed systems, and automation. Job Description • Design, develop, test, deploy, and operate Golang backend services, APIs, CLI tools, agents, and controllers. • Build integrations with Kubernetes, networking, storage, identity, cloud/private-cloud, and automation platforms. • Develop control-plane capabilities for provisioning, configuration, policy enforcement, resource lifecycle management, and infrastructure automation. • Design robust REST/gRPC APIs with authentication, authorization, validation, versioning, auditability, and idempotency. • Build event-driven and asynchronous workflows using queues, streams, schedulers, and workers. • Design for retries, timeouts, concurrency, duplicate delivery, partial failures, eventual consistency, and safe recovery. • Ensure backward compatibility and safe rollout/migration of APIs, schemas, resources, and long-running workflows. • Improve reliability and performance through testing, profiling, observability, and production troubleshooting. • Collaborate with product, infrastructure, security, and SRE teams on requirement analysis, system design and operational readiness. • Contribute to technical design, code reviews, engineering standards, and mentoring. • Own services throughout their lifecycle, from design and implementation to production operation and continuous improvement. Required Skills & Experience • 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, including substantial production experience developing backend services in at least one modern programming language such as Go, C#, Java, Python, or equivalent. • Strong programming fundamentals, including concurrency, asynchronous programming, synchronization, cancellation, error handling, testing, and profiling. • Experience designing and operating backend services, distributed systems, REST/gRPC APIs, and asynchronous architectures. • Strong understanding of reliability patterns such as idempotency, retries, backoff, deduplication, rate limiting, backpressure, timeouts, and failure isolation. • Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL), Redis or equivalent, and messaging/streaming systems such as NATS, Kafka, or RabbitMQ. • Familiarity with cloud computing, containers, networking & infrastructure fundamentals. • Experience with production troubleshooting, observability, logging, metrics, and performance analysis. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to learn new technologies and work across software and infrastructure domains. • Ability to work in English, specifically in reading and writing.
