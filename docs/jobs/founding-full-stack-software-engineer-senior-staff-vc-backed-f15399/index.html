Join SignalFire’s Talent Network for Founding Full Stack Software Engineer Roles at VC-Backed Startups 🛑 This is not an application for a specific job. Instead, this is a way to get on the radar of VC-backed startups that are actively hiring Engineering talent. If you have any questions, please direct inquiries to talentnetwork@signalfire.com. At SignalFire, we partner with top early-stage startups that are shaping the future of technology. Our portfolio spans 200+ innovative companies across AI, cybersecurity, healthtech, fintech, developer tools, and enterprise SaaS. We’re looking to connect with exceptional Full Stack Engineers who are excited about joining high-growth startups as founding team members. By joining SignalFire’s Talent Network, your profile will be shared with our portfolio companies, giving you visibility into exclusive early-stage opportunities that may not be publicly listed. Who Should Join? We’re looking for engineers who are: ✔ Passionate about building and scaling software products from the ground up ✔ Excited about joining early-stage startups and working directly with founders ✔ Interested in taking ownership of full stack development, technical strategy, and architecture Typical Roles & Responsibilities • Design, build, and maintain full stack applications, from intuitive front-end interfaces to scalable back-end systems • Develop APIs, microservices, and cloud-based architectures to power innovative products • Collaborate closely with founders to define technical strategy and align it with business goals • Contribute to the technical roadmap, ensuring scalability, reliability, and performance • Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code across the stack • Help shape the engineering culture, establishing best practices for software development Common Qualifications While each startup has its own hiring criteria, many founding software engineering roles in our network look for: • 3+ years of experience as a full stack or back-end engineer, with a track record of shipping high-quality products • Proficiency with modern front-end frameworks (e.g., React, Angular, Vue.js) and back-end frameworks (e.g., Node.js, Django, Flask, Ruby on Rails) • Strong understanding of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerization tools (Docker, Kubernetes) • Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and NoSQL databases (MongoDB, DynamoDB) • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code tools • Proficiency in common programming languages such as JavaScript, Python, Java, Ruby, Go, or TypeScript • Experience in early-stage startups or lean teams is a plus 💡 Technologies You Might Work With: • Languages & Frameworks: JavaScript, TypeScript, Python, Ruby, Golang, Java, React, Angular, Vue.js, Svelte, Node.js, Django, Flask, Ruby on Rails, FastAPI • Databases: PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, DynamoDB, Redis • Cloud & DevOps: AWS (Lambda, EC2, S3, RDS), GCP (App Engine, Firestore), Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, Jenkins, GitHub Actions • Other Tools: GraphQL, REST APIs, WebSockets, Elasticsearch What Happens Next? • Submit your application to join SignalFire’s Talent Ecosystem. • We review applications on an ongoing basis to identify strong candidates. • If there’s a match, a SignalFire talent partner or a leader from one of our startups may reach out directly. • No match yet? We’ll keep your profile on file for future full stack software engineering roles in our portfolio.
