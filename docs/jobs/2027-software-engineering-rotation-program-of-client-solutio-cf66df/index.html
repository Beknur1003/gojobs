2027 Software Engineering Rotation Program of Client Solutions Group The Software Engineering team delivers next-generation application enhancements and new products for a changing world. Working at the cutting edge, we design and develop software for platforms, peripherals, applications and diagnostics — all with the most advanced technologies, tools, software engineering methodologies and the collaboration of internal and external partners. Join us as a Software Engineer as part of our Client Solutions Group (CSG) Engineering Rotation Program in Taipei to do the best work of your career and make a profound social impact. What you’ll achieve The Engineering Rotation Program provides you a fast-paced, dynamic organization in a supportive environment that encourages learning and growth. The program consists of 3 rotations, each 6-months long, to offer you the ability to learn across different projects, solutions, and technical deliverables needed to bring our products to market. Built into the program are key opportunities for networking, knowledge sharing, and career progression. As a Software Engineer, you will analyze the customer’s business goals, needs and general business environment to build technical system answers and create sophisticated software solutions regarding our desktop/ laptop/ peripheral products. You will: • Be supported by technical subject matter experts, mentors and peer advisors that will provide day-to-day support, grow your Dell network, and assimilate you into the Dell and CSG culture • Complete the rotation program with a deeper understanding of the CSG Engineering portfolio, accelerated technical expertise, and the ability to align your passions to your future career at Dell • Participate in the preparation, review and evaluation of Software/Firmware requirements and specifications • Prepare design, functional, technical and/or user documentation, as needed • Use defined software lifecycle methodologies Take the first step towards your dream career Every Dell Technologies team member brings something unique to the table. Here’s what we are looking for with this role: Essential Requirements • Master's degree in Engineering, Technology or Computer Science related fields and expect to graduate between February 2026 to August 2027 • Strong interpersonal and communication skills that will make you a great collaborator in a global team environment. • Understanding of Computer Science Foundations and basic knowledge of the Software Development Lifecycle as well as experience in one or more of the following languages: Python, Rust, C, C++, Java, or Golang • Ability to code/debug moderately complex programs using either written or verbal design specifications Desirable Requirements • Internship experience in a technology related field • Participation in roles outside of the classroom
