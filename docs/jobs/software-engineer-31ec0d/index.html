That's why we're reinventing the mortgage experience from the ground up with intelligent technology, automation, and exceptional customer experiences. Since launching, Pine has become one of Canada's fastest-growing fintech companies, managing billions in mortgage volume while building products that make home financing dramatically simpler, faster, and more transparent. We're proud to have been recognized as one of LinkedIn's Top Startups in Canada for the past two consecutive years, a reflection of both our rapid growth and the incredible team that's building the future of homeownership.
