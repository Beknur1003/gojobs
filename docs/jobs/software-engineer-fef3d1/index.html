﻿The Job **Contact: Vas Fasoulas <vas@sliide.com>** **Salary: circa £90000 ** **JOB DESCRIPTION** Company Overview Our client a London based mobile technology company that solves some of the most pressing challenges for mobile network operators and handset manufacturers globally. Their digital products are used and loved by millions of people every month and drive billions of impressions to content producers and advertisers around the world. They are on a mission to help companies get more from the mobile economy. Their suite of mobile apps empower millions of users to unlock their essential device, and enable mobile carriers, handset manufacturers and app developers to engage, retain and maximise the value of their users. They have more than tripled in size in the last 12 months and have no intention of slowing down. Investing significantly in existing product suite and taking new and exciting products to market. **The Job** You will be responsible for the continued development of our back-end platform. We are looking for someone who is passionate and creative, who will be able to drive innovation and ensure we have the best solution possible. **Our challenges include:** Developing the best platforms we can, using the latest technologies, providing the best experience and reaching as many of our customers as possible. We’re looking for someone that knows what a well designed API looks like, and can help to shape this by working closely with our android and back-end teams. Working with legacy code and improving it, whilst making the architecture more scalable. Interacting with non-technical team members and occasional outsourced development/support partners. **Requirements** Golang (preferred) Version control (Git) Integration of RESTful web service APIs Understand how to balance immediate product needs with long-term project health and maintainability Practical knowledge of common design and architectural patterns and the ability to apply them effectively Ability to write compact and performance-conscious yet readable code Ability to express your ideas clearly and effectively Curiosity and drive to understand and solve problems beyond the surface level Ability to document requirements and designs Familiarity with agile software development methodology and release process Ability to adapt communication for technical and non-technical people **Bonus points for:** Docker Kubernetes gRPC Experience in microservice oriented environments Familiarity with AWS Unit Tests and Automation Tests Personality profile. You are: Team player: you make your team more efficient and enjoyable Keen on diversity (international company) Pro-active, look for pragmatic solutions, not winning arguments Passionately curious about new trends and approaches Keen contributor on open source projects **Benefits** Competitive salary + EMI share options 25 days of holiday + bank holidays up to 8% company pension contribution Flexible working Buzzing London office with a vibrant culture and well-stocked snack cupboards and drinks fridge Working with a passionate team who love to learn and implement new technologies Flat structure with plenty of exposure to the senior leadership team Bike to Work Scheme Paid gym membership Private Health Insurance (with 24/7 Digital GP) Budget for learning resources, courses and conferences Regular all-company socials Free haircuts in the office **Equal opportunity** We are hugely committed to equality of opportunity.
