About Taglynk : At Taglynk, we are a leading talent acquisition and management consulting firm. We partner with fast-growing startups, SMEs, and industry pioneers to build dream teams that scale businesses from 0 to 1 and beyond. Our focus is on precision and excellence, ensuring we connect world-class leaders with outstanding opportunities. We are currently representing a high-growth client that serves as a pioneer in the specialized field of integrated identity platforms, fraud detection, and privacy compliance. Their products process millions of interactions daily for some of the biggest names in banking, e-commerce, and logistics. Role Overview : As a Director and Technology Leader, you will act as a hands-on Engineering Leader. You will head an exceptional team to champion the technical architecture, push for zero-defect software, and orchestrate the development of powerful microservices and complex cloud native applications. You must be highly proficient across modern tech stacks and fully capable of maintaining meaningful client/customer engagements. What Your Day Will Look Like : - Team Leadership : Directing a high-performing engineering team with a continuous, hands-on approach. - Execution & Oversight : Running in-depth design and code reviews to ensure top-tier standards. - Architecture : Crafting design artifacts and developing sophisticated, cloud-native applications on GCP or AWS. - Development : Writing zero-defect software alongside test cases and actively building out APIs, business components, and microservices. - Client & Product Synergy : Collaborating heavily with product and external business teams to dictate priorities and resolve support challenges at their root. - Culture Enabler : Cultivating an environment of extreme ownership, experimentation, and proactive innovation within your pod. We are a PERFECT match if you have : - Core Competence : Strong expertise in System Design, Architecture, Functional or OO programming, and adherence to SOLID principles. - Design Prowess : Rich understanding of common design patterns, microservices, OOAD, data models, and API data contracts. - Backend Expertise : Proficiency in two or more of the following : Elixir, Java, Python, Golang, Node.js, or Ruby (with an open mindset to upskill in others as required). - Databases : Solid database design and heavy SQL performance tuning skills working with RDBMS (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, etc.). - Methodologies & Cloud : Extensive practice with Agile methodologies alongside Docker, Kubernetes, and major Cloud platforms. - Front End : Exposure to UI development leveraging modern web frameworks such as React. - Soft Skills : A solution-oriented mindset, fantastic communication/articulation skills, and previous experience managing customer interactions.
