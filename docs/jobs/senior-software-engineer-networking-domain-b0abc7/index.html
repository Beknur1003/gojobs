Senior Software Engineer - Networking Domain

Barracuda Networks Bangalore опубликовано 28 октября 2026

Senior
PostgreSQLMySQLLinuxCI/CDPythonC++AI/LLM
We at Barracuda are at the forefront of protecting our customers from email-borne threats and data leaks. We are a passionate team of engineers building the next generation of innovative cloud connected email security appliances. We are looking for a highly skilled and passionate Senior Software Engineer to join our cross functional Agile team. As a Senior Software Engineer you will have the opportunity to work on cutting-edge on-premises appliance based and cloud-based email security products. You will be working closely with product managers, customers, and developers to design, develop, test, and deliver new and innovative features in a continuous delivery model. So, if you've got what it takes to excel in this role, we would like to talk to you! Tech Stack : - Programming Languages : PERL, C, C++, Golang - Databases : PostgreSQL, MySQL - Tools : CI/CD pipelines, GitHub, GDB, Strace - Operating Systems : Linux - Protocols : SMTP, DNS, TLS - Scripting Languages : Perl, Python, Bash What You'll Be Working On : - Designing, developing, and optimizing robust backend systems using C/C++/PERL and other languages, focusing on scalability, maintainability, and performance. - Guiding and mentoring junior developers, enforcing best practices, and promoting technical excellence within the team. - Collaborating with product managers, customers, and cross-functional teams to translate business requirements into cutting-edge solutions. - Conducting thorough code reviews, troubleshooting issues, and ensuring adherence to high coding standards. - Staying ahead of industry trends and technologies, influencing architectural decisions and technology roadmaps. - Contributing to a supportive and inclusive team culture that values diverse perspectives and fosters creativity. - Integrating Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning capabilities into our systems to drive automation, intelligent threat detection, and advanced product features. What You Bring to the Role : - Experience : 6 to 8+ years in software development in PERL/C/C++ - Database Proficiency : Strong knowledge of SQL databases, particularly PostgreSQL and MySQL, including optimization techniques. - DevOps and CI/CD : Experience with continuous integration and deployment pipelines, and version control systems like GitHub. - Debugging Skills : Proficiency in debugging and troubleshooting using tools such as GDB, and Strace. - System Knowledge : Familiarity with Linux operating systems and networking protocols (e.g., SMTP, DNS, TLS). - Development Practices : Understanding of Agile methodologies and the software development lifecycle. Additional Skills : 1. Knowledge of front-end technologies (JavaScript, CSS, HTML, AJAX) is advantageous. 2. Experience in email security, cloud-connected appliances, or network security is beneficial. 3. Familiarity with scripting languages like Perl, Python, or Bash. 4. Familiarity with Golang is a plus - AI Integration : Proven experience integrating AI/ML technologies into production environments, including working with LLMs, crafting effective prompts, or utilizing AI APIs to solve complex engineering problems. What you'll get from us : A team where you can voice your opinion, make an impact, and where you and your experience are valued. Internal mobility - there are opportunities for cross training and the ability to attain your next career step within Barracuda. In addition, you will receive equity, in the form of non-qualifying options
