About the role We are seeking a Senior Golang Developer to join our client, a Web3 Solution Provider in a full-time capacity. . As a Senior Golang Developer, you will play a pivotal role in architecting and implementing robust backend systems that power next-generation blockchain and decentralised applications. This position reflects the strategic importance we place on technical excellence and innovation within a growing development team. Key responsibilities • Design, develop and maintain high-performance backend systems and APIs using Golang for Web3 applications and blockchain platforms • Lead the architecture and implementation of scalable microservices that support distributed systems and decentralised protocols • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product, design and DevOps to deliver robust Web3 solutions • Conduct thorough code reviews and provide mentorship to junior developers, ensuring code quality and best practices • Optimise system performance, security and reliability across production environments • Participate in technical design discussions and contribute to technology decisions that shape our platform • Investigate and resolve complex technical issues, ensuring minimal downtime and maximum system availability • Stay current with Web3 technologies, blockchain frameworks and emerging best practices in the industry What we're looking for • 6+ years of professional software development experience, with at least 3 years specializing in Golang development • Proven expertise in building scalable, high-performance backend systems and microservices architectures • Strong understanding of blockchain technology, cryptocurrencies and Web3 ecosystems • Experience with distributed systems, concurrency patterns and system design at scale • Proficiency in RESTful API design and experience with message queues or event-driven architectures • Solid knowledge of databases (both relational and NoSQL) and data management principles • Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to work independently and as part of a collaborative team • Strong communication skills and experience mentoring or leading junior developers • Familiarity with containerisation technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes is advantageous • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and automated testing frameworks Apply now If you are a talented Senior Golang Developer passionate about Web3 and ready to make an impact, we would love to hear from you. Please submit your CV, a cover letter highlighting your relevant experience, and links to your portfolio or GitHub repositories. Join our client in building the future of decentralised technology.
