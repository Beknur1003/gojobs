DevOps Engineer

As a DevOps Engineer of our Engineering Group you will dive head-first into creating innovative solutions that advance businesses and careers. You’ll join an inspiring and curious team of technologists dedicated to improving the design, code, and implementation that goes into creating high quality infrastructure and platform. You’ll be tasked with keeping the team up to speed on the progress of what’s being developed. Coming in with an understanding of the importance of end-to-end automation. And best of all, you’ll be working with and sharing ideas, information and innovation with our team of technologists from all over the world.This role requires a wide variety of strengths and capabilities, including: • Understanding of application, networking, and infrastructure architecture disciplines. • Knowledge of architecture and design across all systems. • Proficiency in multiple modern Programming/Scripting languages (Golang, Python, Groovy, Shell, Bash, or related). • Awareness of industry-wide technology trends and best practices. • Keen understanding of financial control and budget management. • Passionate about building an innovative culture. • Ability to collaborate with high-performing teams and individuals throughout the firm to accomplish common goals. • Keen understanding of Version control system (Github, GitLab, and Bitbucket). • Familiarity with Linux/Unix environment. • Experience working with public cloud services (AWS/AZURE/GCP). • Monitor, troubleshoot, maintain, and continuously improve building, packaging, and deployment processes. Add-Ons: • Experience implementing and managing Kubernetes clusters. • Ability to write deployment pipelines for Kubernetes using tools such as Helm/Kustomize. • Familiarity with CI/CD tools and their implementations. If you're passionate about technology and eager to make a positive impact, we want to hear from you! Join us on our journey to deliver high-quality software solutions.
