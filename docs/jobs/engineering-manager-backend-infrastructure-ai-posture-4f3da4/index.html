Job Summary About the Role As an Engineering Manager on the AI Posture team, you will lead a high-performing backend team of 8–10 engineers operating in one of our most complex and cross-functional domains. This role requires a blend of empathetic people leadership, operational excellence, and deep backend systems expertise—specifically around Go microservices, high-efficiency runtime environments, and lower-level infrastructure abstraction. You will lead with empathy to support, empower, and grow your engineers while staying on the cutting edge by being an AI-savvy leader—embracing AI-driven development practices, modern engineering tools, and intelligent automation to boost team productivity and runtime performance. Key Responsibilities • Empathetic Team Leadership: Build, mentor, and lead a backend engineering team using an empathy-first approach. Foster an inclusive culture of psychological safety, trust, empowerment, and continuous growth. • AI-Savvy Engineering Culture: Drive the adoption of modern AI tooling, modern engineering practice innovations, and intelligent automation across the software development life cycle to accelerate development and improve code quality. • Backend & Microservices Architectures: Lead the design, execution, and optimization of high-throughput, low-latency microservices built predominantly in Go. • Infrastructure & Platform Interaction: Partner directly with Infrastructure and Platform teams to ensure services interact seamlessly with cloud infrastructure, optimizing runtime efficiency, memory usage, compute density, and hardware/cloud resources. • Roadmap & Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner closely with Product Management, Security, and SRE teams to align priorities, translate technical strategy into execution, and deliver on multi-quarter roadmaps with predictability and speed. • Operational Excellence & Observability: Drive production readiness by enforcing rigorous standards for deployment pipelines, telemetry, tracing, performance tuning, and incident response across critical backend services. • Technical Communication: Clearly communicate technical trade-offs, systems architecture, risks, and strategies to executive leadership and strategic cross-functional partners. Qualifications Required Qualifications • 5–7 years of experience in software engineering management leading distributed backend systems teams. • 8+ years of hands-on software engineering experience with a strong systems-level foundation, with primary expertise in Go (Golang) and concurrent programming patterns. • Proven track record of designing, building, and operating high-concurrency microservices architectures at scale. • Deep understanding of infrastructure layers, container orchestration (e.g., Kubernetes, Docker), and cloud platforms (GCP, AWS, or Azure), with a focus on optimizing cost, latency, and resource utilization. • Empathetic, people-first leadership style with demonstrated success in mentoring engineers and building cohesive, high-trust team environments. • Forward-thinking approach to software engineering, with practical experience leveraging AI tools (e.g., AI coding assistants, automated testing/profiling) to elevate team productivity. Qualifications Preferred Qualifications • Hands-on experience with network discovery, traffic analysis, packet processing, or network protocol engineering.. • Experience with cloud-native message brokers, event streams, and databases (e.g., Kafka, gRPC, BigQuery, Redis, MongoDB). • Experience in cybersecurity, threat intelligence, cloud security posture management (CSPM), or AI risk vectors.
