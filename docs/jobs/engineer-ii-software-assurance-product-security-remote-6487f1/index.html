About the Role CrowdStrike's Product Security team breaks the mold of traditional internal security and focuses on active threats to CrowdStrike's products. Help us protect CrowdStrike and its customers from the most advanced threats by assessing, designing, and implementing security controls and systems associated with the outgoing aspects of the software supply chain. As a Security Engineer II, you will focus on securing our product releases and downstream customers. You will perform technical security assessments of GitHub organizations, hardening of CICD build, sign, and release pipelines, monitor for artifact integrity throughout pipelines, create tooling for known gaps, and create automations to make operations more efficient. What You'll Do • Assess risk and provide security guidance to engineers across the company on CrowdStrike’s open-source repository configurations. • Implement and maintain controls and processes to secure public and private GitHub organizations, including repository guardrails and secret scanning. • Monitor product build, sign, and release pipelines for integrity. • Monitor and review internal networking configurations related to build system security • Advocate for secure coding and compliance practices to the wider engineering organization. • Contribute to the delivery of security initiatives aimed at hardening CrowdStrike’s software supply chain and artifact distribution paths. What You'll Need • Experience working in an engineering role implementing, supporting, and monitoring software security systems. • Strong working experience with GitHub, including repository administration, and access management. • Experience working with cryptography, especially signing certificates or keys • Strong working knowledge of the different types of access models and authentication tokens • Experience identifying and mitigating open-source risks (SCA, dependency management, licensing risks). • Proficiency in one or more common scripting languages, such as Python, Golang, Shell, or JavaScript. • Domain knowledge of the software development lifecycle (SDLC), secure coding practices, code reviews, and source control security. • Collaborative mindset with experience contributing to cross-team security projects and initiatives. • Efficient communicator with strong writing skills, comfortable working in a remote environment. • Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes. Bonus Points • Experience with common monitoring or log aggregation tools such as LogScale, Splunk, DataDog, Prometheus, etc. • Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and associated tools (Jenkins, Argo CD) from an integration and ingestion perspective. • Experience working in and securing configurations of large-scale cloud platforms. • Self-motivated to identify security problems and engage with development teams to find pragmatic solutions.. #LI-Remote #LI-AM6 Benefits of Working at CrowdStrike: • Market leader in compensation and equity awards • Comprehensive physical and mental wellness programs • Competitive vacation and holidays for recharge • Paid parental and adoption leaves • Professional development opportunities for all employees regardless of level or role • Employee Networks, geographic neighborhood groups, and volunteer opportunities to build connections • Vibrant office culture with world class amenities • Great Place to Work Certified™ across the globe CrowdStrike is proud to be an equal opportunity employer.
