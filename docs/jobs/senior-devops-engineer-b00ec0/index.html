Your Role As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will join a strategic cloud transformation program focused on building and operating next-generation sovereign cloud platforms. You will work at the intersection of software engineering, cloud infrastructure, platform operations, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), contributing to large-scale cloud-native services used across multiple business domains. In this role you will: • Design, develop, and maintain cloud-native software components, Kubernetes operators, controllers, APIs, and platform automation tools using Go. • Build and operate enterprise-scale Kubernetes environments, ensuring reliability, scalability, security, and operational excellence. • Contribute to the development of modern platform engineering capabilities that accelerate service delivery and improve developer experience. • Implement Infrastructure-as-Code and GitOps methodologies to create reproducible, automated, and auditable environments. • Drive automation initiatives across infrastructure provisioning, deployment pipelines, configuration management, and operational processes. • Define and maintain observability solutions, including monitoring, logging, alerting, and distributed tracing capabilities. • Collaborate with architects, Product Owners, platform engineers, and security teams to ensure alignment with business, operational, and compliance requirements. • Participate in incident management, root cause analysis, post-mortems, and continuous service improvement activities. • Apply SRE principles to improve service reliability, availability, and operational performance. • Improve engineering standards through code reviews, technical documentation, mentoring, and knowledge sharing. • Leverage AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity, software quality, and operational efficiency. Your Profile • 5+ years of experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Engineering, SRE, or Software Engineering roles. • Strong hands-on experience with Kubernetes administration, operations, and troubleshooting. • Experience with Go (Golang) development. • Experience developing Kubernetes Operators, Controllers, Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs), or related cloud-native automation components. • Strong understanding of container technologies and cloud-native architectures. • Proven experience implementing Infrastructure-as-Code solutions using Terraform or OpenTofu. • Hands-on knowledge of GitOps practices and tools such as ArgoCD or Flux. • Experience working with CI/CD ecosystems and deployment automation. • Solid understanding of Linux systems, networking concepts, and security best practices. • Experience implementing observability solutions including metrics, logging, tracing, and alerting • Excellent communication skills in English. Proficiency in French is a plus.
