Senior Software Engineer – AI-Native Platform Austin, TX (Hybrid 3 days per week in office) Context & Impact Lansweeper is evolving its engineering organization toward an AI-native development model. We are hiring a Senior Software Engineer to build reliable, scalable systems while operating in an AI-augmented development environment. Team Info In the near term, you will contribute to the Discovery Team's work on Traffic Sensor evolutionary efforts, with AI capabilities at the center of everything we build. The team is responsible for ensuring system quality, architectural clarity, and scalable design across multiple parallel AI-assisted development streams. Longer term, you will help shape how AI-native engineering practices scale across the platform. AI Fluency At Lansweeper, we are building an AI-native engineering culture. We expect all engineers to actively use AI tools to enhance productivity and quality across the software development lifecycle, including coding, testing, documentation, and system design. Candidates should be comfortable working with AI-assisted workflows and continuously evolving their approach as tools and practices advance. Challenge The main challenges you'll face are: • Maintaining engineering rigor and system quality in an AI-assisted development environment • Scaling systems while balancing performance, reliability, and architectural clarity • Coordinating parallel AI-driven development workflows without losing technical coherence Key Responsibilities • Design and build maintainable, scalable, observable, and well-tested software systems • Operate in an AI-augmented development workflow, decomposing problems and validating AI-generated outputs • Contribute to architecture discussions, long-term system evolution, and technical direction • Apply strong system thinking across data models, APIs, distributed systems, and service boundaries • Produce clear technical documentation, structured prompts, and design proposals • Mentor peers and collaborate with Product, UX, and Engineering teams Key Requirements Hard Skills • 10+ years of relevant software engineering experience • Strong foundation in software engineering fundamentals including testing, version control, CI/CD, and code review • Proven experience in C, C++, Golang or Rust • Experience building backend systems or distributed systems • Solid understanding of data modeling, APIs, and service-oriented architectures • Experience designing scalable and reliable systems • Practical experience working in an AI-assisted development environment • Experience with SQL • Knowledge of networks and underlying hardware infrastructure Soft Skills • Strong systems thinking and problem-solving ability • Curious and adaptable with the ability to learn new tools quickly • Strong ownership mindset and commitment to engineering quality AI Specific Skills Lansweeper is an AI-native company. In this role, you are expected to actively use AI-assisted development tools such as Claude, code assistants, and agent-based workflows as part of your daily engineering practice. You should be comfortable prompting, validating, and refining AI-generated outputs, and using AI to improve development speed while maintaining production-quality standards. Team Info You'll join the Discovery Team, working closely with Product, UX, and Engineering teams. Team size: 10 engineers, distributed across North America.
