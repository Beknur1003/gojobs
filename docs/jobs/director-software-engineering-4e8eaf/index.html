Director, Software Engineering Why Birdi? At Birdi, we're transforming the pharmacy and healthcare experience through technology, innovation, and patient-centered solutions. As we continue to evolve our technology ecosystem, we're investing in modern platforms and AI-enabled tools that can help us build smarter, more scalable healthcare experiences. This is an opportunity to lead from the front. You'll help build a new AI-powered care platform from the ground up while shaping the engineering practices, architecture, tools, and team behind it. If you're an engineering leader who still loves technology, enjoys building great teams, and is excited about the possibilities of AI in healthcare, this could be the opportunity for you. About the Role The Director, Software Engineering will lead Birdi's software engineering team while driving the architecture and development of a greenfield, AI-powered care platform. This role combines people leadership, technical strategy, architecture, and hands-on engineering expertise. You'll lead and develop software engineers, architect scalable backend systems using Golang (Go), guide database strategy across SQL Server and PostgreSQL, and establish engineering standards around testing, code quality, and deployment. You'll also play a key role in defining how Birdi incorporates AI-assisted and agentic development tools into the software development lifecycle. You'll evaluate emerging AI technologies, including tools such as Claude Code and Codex, and guide their thoughtful adoption across the engineering team. Working closely with Product, Design, and organizational leadership, you'll help translate business goals into technical strategy while ensuring Birdi's solutions meet the reliability, scalability, security, and compliance expectations of healthcare technology.
