🌍 Remote Salmon is a technology-driven financial company building a banking and lending platform across Southeast Asia, starting in the Philippines. We combine global fintech expertise with deep local market knowledge to make financial services simple, accessible, and useful for millions of people across the region. 7M+ app downloads. 2M+ monthly active users. 7,000+ partner stores. US$310M+ raised from leading global investors. Manila-based, globally distributed, and hybrid-first — our team spans 45+ countries. If you want to solve complex problems at scale and impact how millions of people access and manage money, come build with us. Southeast Asia's fintech moment starts here. About the role You'll build and scale the AWS infrastructure behind Salmon's banking and lending platform, and help keep deployment and production systems fast and reliable. What you'll do • Craft, develop, and manage scalable AWS infrastructure supporting Salmon's products • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines that keep application deployment fast and reliable • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ship and support production systems end to end What you'll own • Own the design and management of AWS infrastructure across EC2, S3, RDS, CloudFront, VPC, Lambda, and more • Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines using GitLab, GitLab-CI, and FluxCD v2 • Monitor application and infrastructure health, and resolve issues quickly to protect production availability • Drive automation of infrastructure and deployment processes using IaC tools including Terraform, AWS CDK, and Ansible • Ensure infrastructure and deployments follow security policies and industry best practices • Ensure the resilience and availability of production systems What makes you a strong fit • 5+ years of professional experience in Cloud, Infrastructure, or Software Engineering • Deep, hands-on expertise with core AWS services (EC2, S3, RDS, CloudFront, VPC, Lambda, and more) • Strong command of Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, AWS CDK, Ansible) and containerization/orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes — EKS/ECS) • Proficient in Python, Bash, or Golang, with hands-on CI/CD experience (GitLab, GitLab-CI, FluxCD v2) • Solid grasp of networking principles and protocols, plus experience with Istio service mesh • Familiarity with monitoring tools such as Prometheus, VictoriaMetrics, AlertManager, and Grafana What we offer Ownership and flexibility • Fully remote work with core collaboration hours from 12:00 to 6:00 PM Manila time (UTC+8) • Company-provided tools and equipment Health and time off • Medical insurance support for you and your family through co-funding or reimbursement, depending on your location and subject to policy limits • Access to an internal mental health support specialist • 22 vacation days, Philippine public holidays, and 15 sick days Growth and team experience • Opportunities to learn and share your expertise through internal expert meetups, external conferences, speaking opportunities, and industry publications • Company-sponsored trips to Manila to meet and work with your team in person • High-performing teams can earn a dedicated beach house week in Southeast Asia We believe strong teams are built by people with different backgrounds, experiences, and points of view.
