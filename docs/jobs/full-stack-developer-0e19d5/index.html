About the role Domino's Pizza Indonesia is looking for a Full Stack Developer Associate to join our Digital & Technology team. In this role, you will be responsible for developing, testing, and maintaining web applications, building front end and back end systems, managing databases, integrating APIs, and maintaining technical documentation to support reliable, scalable, and maintainable digital platforms that meet our business and operational needs. Job Description : • Develop, test, and maintain web applications to meet business and operational requirements. • Build and manage both front-end and back-end systems to ensure application functionality operates as expected. • Design, develop, and manage databases to support application performance and data integrity. • Develop and integrate RESTful APIs with internal and external systems. • Build scalable and maintainable systems using clean architecture and appropriate design patterns. • Conduct code reviews and maintain technical documentation to ensure code quality and knowledge sharing. • Identify and resolve technical issues, troubleshoot application problems, and continuously improve system performance and development efficiency. • Collaborate with the Digital & Technology team, relevant departments, internal users, and external vendors to deliver effective digital solutions. Job Requirement : • Bachelor's Degree in Informatics Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field. • 1–2 years of work experience in web based software development. • Strong proficiency in Golang and PHP (Laravel). • Experience in building RESTful APIs and microservices. • Proficiency in JavaScript and modern front-end frameworks such as React.js, Vue.js, or Angular. • Experience with MySQL and PostgreSQL databases. • Good understanding of clean architecture, design patterns, and scalable system development. • Strong technical problem solving skills, analytical thinking, and attention to detail. • Good collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross functional teams. • Adaptability and a commitment to continuous learning and improvement.
