About NCR VOYIX NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global platform-powered leader in unified commerce for shopping and dining. Combining a flexible, intelligent platform with end-to-end payments capabilities and services developed through its deep industry experience, NCR Voyix empowers retailers and restaurants to accelerate new possibilities for their operations, experiences and business outcomes. Experience: 5 – 9years Title: Senior Software Engineer - Edge Platfrom Location: Chennai About Us NCR Voyix Corporation (NYSE: VYX) is a global technology company leading how the world connects, interacts, and transacts with business. Our assisted and self-service solutions and comprehensive support services address the needs of hospitality and retail sectors in more than 40+ countries. Role Overview Join our market-leading, innovative Edge platform team at NCR Voyix. As a Senior Software Engineer, you will be an integral part of the Edge team, designing and developing complex distributed platform capabilities primarily using Go and Kubernetes. You will own technical problems end-to-end, contribute significantly to architecture and design, perform code and design reviews, troubleshoot complex platform issues, and collaborate with QA, SRE, Architecture, and other engineering teams to deliver scalable, reliable, and production-ready solutions. Key Responsibilities · Own complex features and technical problems from design through development, testing, release, and production readiness. · Design and develop scalable, reliable, and maintainable platform services using Go. · Design, troubleshoot, and optimize applications and platform services running on Kubernetes. · Drive or significantly contribute to technical design and architecture discussions. · Perform high-quality code and design reviews and help maintain engineering standards. · Troubleshoot complex issues across application, Kubernetes, Linux, networking, and cloud layers. · Collaborate with QA, SRE, Architecture, and internal stakeholders on quality, scalability, reliability, and release readiness. · Mentor engineers and contribute to knowledge sharing and continuous improvement across the team. Core Skills · Proficiency in Go: Very strong hands-on production development experience with Go, including concurrency, goroutines, channels, interfaces, error handling, testing, performance optimization, and service/API design. Ability to design efficient, scalable, reliable, and maintainable distributed services. · Kubernetes & Containerization: Strong hands-on expertise with Kubernetes and containers, including workloads, Services, DaemonSets, configuration, storage, networking, RBAC, scheduling, resource management, cluster lifecycle, upgrades, scaling, and complex troubleshooting. Strong understanding of Kubernetes architecture and cloud-native design principles. · Strong development and design skills: Strong understanding of software architecture, clean code, SOLID principles, automated testing, debugging, performance, scalability, reliability, and backward compatibility. · Git / CI/CD: Strong experience with Git, GitHub, GitHub Actions, and automated build, test, and release workflows. · Linux OS: Strong working knowledge of Linux, including Bash scripting, processes, filesystems, networking, system services, installation, and troubleshooting. Deeper system/kernel knowledge is beneficial. · Cloud computing: Hands-on experience with cloud platforms, preferably Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including familiarity with GKE, IAM, networking, Cloud Storage, Pub/Sub, and Cloud Logging/Monitoring. · Technical Leadership: Experience mentoring engineers, leading technical discussions, performing design reviews, and driving solutions across multiple teams. · Additional technologies: Familiarity with Service Mesh/Linkerd, CouchDB, OpenTelemetry (OTEL), Grafana, Helm, GCP Cloud Logging, KCC, kubeadm, or GraphQL is beneficial. Basic Qualifications · Degree in a numerate, Computer Science, Engineering, or related technical discipline. · 5–9 years of relevant software engineering experience. · Excellent programming skills with substantial hands-on Go development experience. · Strong hands-on expertise designing and troubleshooting Kubernetes-based applications and services. · Strong grounding in Computer Science and distributed-system fundamentals. · Demonstrated experience owning complex technical problems and contributing to software/system design.
