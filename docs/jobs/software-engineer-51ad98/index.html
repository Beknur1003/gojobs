About the job ProxyScrape empowers businesses with seamless, scalable, and ethical access to publicly available web data. Our proxy and data-access infrastructure supports thousands of companies across eCommerce, cybersecurity, research, and advertising — delivering reliability, performance, and global scale. We are a fast-growing, bootstrapped, and profitable tech company built around speed, automation, and impact. We move quickly, ship often, and value people who take ownership and push things forward. We are now looking for a Software Engineer to join our Dev/Product team — a hands-on engineer with real experience in proxy and scraping systems who enjoys building, shipping, and owning production infrastructure. You’ll work on the core of our products, from backend services and data pipelines to frontend and infrastructure. Your Mission You will build, scale, and improve the systems behind ProxyScrape’s core products, turning technical challenges into reliable, fast, and cost-efficient solutions. • Design and implement features and services across core products and internal systems • Contribute to architecture discussions and technical decisions • Identify repetitive, manual, or error-prone processes and automate them, using AI-assisted tools where it makes sense • Help the team ship faster through better tooling and systems • Work closely with product and engineering to turn business needs into working software Key Responsibilities • Build and maintain backend services, APIs, and data pipelines with Go and PHP • Develop frontend features with React and Next.js • Work on scraping and proxy infrastructure: improve success rates, latency, reliability, and cost • (Optional) Work on anti-bot systems, including fingerprinting and protection bypass strategies, to make the platform more resilient • Take part in deployments, CI/CD, and monitoring alongside the team • Write clean, tested, well-documented code and take part in code reviews What You Bring Proxy & Scraping Experience •
