The opportunity ahead Spotnana is seeking an experienced Sr. Staff Security Engineer to join our global Security team. As a Sr. Staff Security Engineer you will be responsible for developing security tooling that enhances Spotnana’s ability to secure our corporate and platform system. You will be deeply engaged across the business advocating for and implementing security best practices and guardrails that enable the business to move quickly while maintaining our enterprise security. The ideal candidate is a hands-on leader who lives in the details of security engineering and can thrive in a fast-paced environment where both individual drive and team collaboration are the keys to success. You’ll be working to identify problems, establish a vision for how to address those problems, and unite the relevant owners within the business on achieving that vision. How you'll help us build • Build in-house applications to improve security posture across the entire product lifecycle and codify secure by design principles • Build integrations between various security products • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines with shift-left security practices • Build and own security orchestration (no code, low code, functions, etc) • Design and evaluate security solutions for an API driven, multi-region SaaS platform • Create prototypes and/or proof of concepts to demonstrate solution viability • Research the industry landscape and technology developments • Partner with leaders and engineers to influence and define execution plans • Mentor junior engineers Experience you bring • 10+ years of security engineering experience • Ability to influence without authority, innovate to tackle tough problems, and communicate clearly to all levels of the organization • Fluency in building and deploying software using modern engineering practices • Expert in at least one programming language • Java, Golang and Python a strong plus • Familiarity with the application of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in a security context, including its capabilities and limitations • Familiarity with data anonymization and data privacy concerns • Experience leading other engineers a plus • Experience with delivering large-scale SaaS applications a plus • Experience delivering results in an enterprise environment, ideally from a position of little or no direct authority, ideally in efforts that span teams or organizations What to expect next Selected candidates will participate in cross-functional virtual interviews exploring their relevant experience, approach to problem-solving, critical thinking, and alignment with Spotnana’s core values. Interviews will include face-to-face conversations as well as coding exercises to demonstrate your ability. Work Authorization: This position is not eligible for employment-based visa sponsorship. Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States and must not require current or future employment sponsorship.
