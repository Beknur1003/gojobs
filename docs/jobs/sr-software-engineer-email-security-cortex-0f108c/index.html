Job Summary Job Summary As a Software engineer on our Email Security team, you will play a key role in shaping and delivering a cutting-edge email security solution within the Cortex platform. You will design and build a highly available, reliable, and secure solution that adheres to the highest standards. A can-do approach, a passion for excellence and a commitment to continuous improvement are essential qualities for success in this position.
