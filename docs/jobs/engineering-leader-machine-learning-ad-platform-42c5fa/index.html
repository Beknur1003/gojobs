The Opportunity As Engineering Leader for Machine Learning & Ad Platform at Moxxi, you'll be a hands-on technical leader who can architect production systems, build and ship the machine learning models that drive our revenue, and lead the teams that do both. You'll own the intelligence at the core of our performance marketing engine — the ranking, prediction, targeting, and optimization models that decide which offer is shown, where, and to whom — along with the low-latency platform that serves those decisions on every impression and click. You'll personally design, prototype, and productionize data science and ML solutions, set the technical direction for our ad platform, and lead one to two engineering teams (spanning platform engineering, ML, and data) and the products they own. You'll partner directly with the CTO, Product, and our Ad Ops and Account Management teams to turn revenue goals into models, systems, and measurable results. If you want to be the person who writes the model, designs the system around it, proves its lift with a clean experiment, and builds the team that scales it, this role is for you. Responsibilities Machine Learning & Data Science (Hands-On) • Design, build, and ship production ML models for ad ranking and decisioning, including click, conversion, and revenue prediction (pCTR, pCVR, EPC, and RPI) that determine which offers and placements are served. • Develop optimization algorithms for bidding, budget pacing, and traffic and yield allocation that maximize revenue and advertiser outcomes under real-world constraints. • Build audience, lookalike, and targeting models that identify high-value users and improve campaign performance across publishers and placements. • Own the full model lifecycle: problem framing, exploratory analysis, feature engineering, training, offline evaluation, online deployment, monitoring, and retraining, in Python with PyTorch, TensorFlow, or XGBoost/LightGBM. • Design and run rigorous online experiments (A/B tests, multi-armed bandits, holdouts) with sound statistical methodology, and translate results into clear go/no-go decisions. • Build the ML platform foundations on GCP — feature pipelines and feature store, training pipelines, model registry, real-time inference, and model monitoring — using Vertex AI, BigQuery, Pub/Sub, and Dataflow. • Apply LLMs and agentic workflows where they deliver measurable value, such as AI-assisted campaign setup, creative and offer generation, content classification, and operator copilots in Moxxi's ad ops platform. • Partner with Data Engineering to ensure event, tracking, and attribution data in BigQuery is accurate, timely, and fit for both billing and model training. Technical Leadership & Platform Engineering • Own the architecture and technical roadmap for Moxxi's ad platform, including ad serving, real-time decisioning, tracking, and campaign management. • Stay hands-on in the codebase — designing, building, and reviewing production code in Golang (backend services) and Python (ML and data services), and guiding React work on operator-facing applications. • Design low-latency, high-throughput serving systems that run model inference within strict performance budgets for latency, throughput, and cost, using Redis, Docker, and Kubernetes (GKE). • Build and maintain integrations with advertisers, affiliate networks, publishers, and ad tech partners via APIs, postbacks, and real-time event pipelines. • Own system and model reliability end to end — observability, alerting, on-call, incident response, and blameless post-mortems — using tools like Datadog. • Set and uphold engineering and ML standards: code review, testing, CI/CD, reproducible training, model validation, and design reviews. • Make pragmatic build-vs-buy, heuristic-vs-model, scope, and sequencing decisions, balancing speed to market against long-term maintainability. People Leadership & Team Building • Lead one to two engineering teams — spanning software engineers, ML engineers, and data scientists — and the products they own, with clear charters, goals, and success metrics. • Directly manage, mentor, and grow engineers through regular 1:1s, clear expectations, actionable feedback, performance reviews, and career development; develop tech leads as the teams grow. • Own hiring for your teams — define roles and the hiring plan, run technical and ML interviews, close candidates, and onboard new team members. • Run the teams' delivery cadence: planning, estimation, prioritization, and tracking, with predictable, high-quality releases across both software and model launches. • Raise the technical bar through example — coaching engineers on system design, modeling, experimentation, and ownership. Cross-Functional Partnership • Work closely with the CTO, Product, Ad Ops, and Account Management to translate business goals and operator needs into scoped, prioritized technical and ML roadmaps. • Communicate model performance, experiment results, tradeoffs, risks, and timelines clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders, including leadership. • Stay current on ML research and industry changes (privacy regulation, signal loss, partner platform changes) and their impact on Moxxi's models and platform. Basic Qualifications • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience. • 10+ years of professional software engineering experience, including 5+ years building and deploying machine learning systems in production. • 3+ years leading engineering teams as an engineering manager or tech lead manager, including hiring, performance management, and leading multiple engineers or teams. • Hands-on experience building ranking, recommendation, prediction, or optimization models for ads, search, recommendations, or marketplaces — from feature engineering through online deployment. • Production-level fluency in Python and the ML stack (PyTorch, TensorFlow, or gradient-boosted trees), plus strong backend development skills in a compiled language (Go, Java, Scala, or similar). • Strong grounding in statistics, experimentation design, and causal measurement, with a track record of using online experiments to drive product and business decisions. • Proven track record designing and operating low-latency, high-throughput, highly available distributed systems, including real-time model serving. • Experience building ML infrastructure: data and feature pipelines, training pipelines, model deployment, and model monitoring. • Strong data skills — advanced SQL, data modeling, and large-scale data processing on a cloud data warehouse or big data platform. • Experience building and deploying on a major cloud provider (GCP, AWS, or Azure) with containerization and orchestration. • Comfort with ambiguity and a track record of making sound, defensible tradeoffs with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment. • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain models and results to technical and business stakeholders alike. Nice to Have / Bonus Qualifications • Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Statistics, Operations Research, or a related field. • Direct experience in AdTech or performance marketing, especially ads ranking, real-time bidding, budget pacing, lookalike targeting, or conversion and attribution modeling. • Familiarity with programmatic concepts (OpenRTB, DSPs/SSPs, auctions) and performance marketing economics (CPA, CPC, EPC, CTR, revenue per impression, ROAS). • Experience with GCP's ML and data stack, including Vertex AI, BigQuery, Pub/Sub, Dataflow, and GKE. • Experience with deep learning for ranking (e.g., deep cross networks, transformers, sequence models) and distributed training. • Experience with mathematical optimization (linear/integer programming), reinforcement learning, or contextual bandits applied to allocation or bidding problems. • Experience building LLM-powered and agentic product features, including evaluation frameworks, and using AI-assisted development workflows. • Experience with event streaming technologies (Kafka, Pub/Sub, or equivalent) and real-time feature computation. • Hands-on experience with Golang and a modern front-end framework such as React. • Startup experience — moving quickly, wearing multiple hats, and building teams and ML capabilities from an early stage. • Awareness of privacy regulations and compliance relevant to advertising and consumer data (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, TCPA).
