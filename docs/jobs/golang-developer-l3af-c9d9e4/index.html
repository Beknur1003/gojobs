We are looking for a passionate and innovative software engineer to join Walmart's Global Technology Platform group. We build and design systems handling millions of requests per second, play a critical role in scaling Walmart systems under the extremely high-traffic situations, and provide tools to accelerate, secure, and distribute traffic to all the right places. In this role, you will get to design and build high-performance, next-gen config distributed systems at massive scale. Working with teams across E-Commerce, Stores, and everything in between to make Walmart technology the fastest and most resilient in the industry. This critical role will be responsible for leading and participating in medium to large-scale, complex, cross-functional projects. Capable of taking complex problem domains, dissecting them until they're well understood and delivering excellently engineered solutions. Responsibilities: • Lead and participate in medium to large-scale, complex, cross-functional projects, from discovery to production. • Develop innovation strategies, processes, and best practices. • Collaborate within the open-source community to develop and implement cutting-edge technologies in the Linux kernel. • Evangelise new products and features to existing and potential internal and external customers. Requirements: • 10+ years of professional experience in software engineering. • 6 + years of software development experience in SDN or related technologies. • Proficient in the Go (Golang) programming language. • Development experience with network drivers or eBPF/XDP. • Development or Operational expertise with the Linux kernel. • Experience and knowledge with cloud platforms (OpenStack / Public cloud). • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • Familiarity and experience working with large-scale infrastructures, and being able to explain reasons for specific design decisions. • Willingness to support code in production and be a point of escalation to resolve issues. • Strong presentation and communication skills, including giving product/code demonstrations. • Experience with Kubernetes and containers. • History and experience contributing to open-source projects are a huge plus.
