Optum Tech is a global leader in health care innovation. Our teams develop cutting-edge solutions that help people live healthier lives and help make the health system work better for everyone. From advanced data analytics and AI to cybersecurity, we use innovative approaches to solve some of health care's most complex challenges. Your contributions here have the potential to change lives. Ready to build the next breakthrough? Join us to start Caring. Connecting. Growing together. As a Lead Software Engineer on the Public Cloud Platform Team, you will drive the architecture, development, and operationalization of enterprise-grade multi-cloud platforms across Azure, AWS, and GCP. You will lead cutting-edge initiatives focused on cloud governance, networking, security, and automation while designing scalable backend services and Infrastructure as Code frameworks. In this role, you will champion modern software engineering principles, robust DevOps practices, and AI-powered automation tools to streamline workflows, enforce compliance, and deliver high-impact cloud capabilities for the enterprise. You'll enjoy the flexibility to work remotely * from anywhere within the U.S. as you take on some tough challenges.For all hires in the Minneapolis or Washington, D.C. area, you will be required to work in the office a minimum of four days per week. Primary Responsibilities: • Design, architect, and implement multi-cloud infrastructure, networking, and governance frameworks across AWS, Azure, and GCP platforms • Develop and maintain scalable Infrastructure as Code (IaC) templates using Terraform to automate cloud resource provisioning and configuration management • Build enterprise-grade backend microservices and automation tooling utilizing Golang or Python • Collaborate with enterprise security and compliance teams to integrate cloud guardrails, policy enforcement, and automated security controls into cloud platforms • Establish and mature DevOps practices and CI/CD pipelines to ensure automated, secure, and reliable software delivery • Design, develop, and deploy AI-powered solutions and automations to address complex engineering and operational challenges with an emphasis on responsible AI use • Leverage enterprise-approved AI tools to streamline software development workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and drive continuous platform improvement • Evaluate emerging cloud and AI technology trends to inform solution design, technical roadmap development, and strategic innovation You'll be rewarded and recognized for your performance in an environment that will challenge you and give you clear direction on what it takes to succeed in your role as well as provide development for other roles you may be interested in. Required Qualifications: • 8+ years of professional software engineering or cloud engineering experience • 5+ years of hands-on programming experience with Golang or Python • 3+ years of experience architecting, building, and deploying solutions on public cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) • 3+ years of experience authoring and managing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform • 2+ years of experience designing and automating CI/CD pipelines and DevOps workflows • Demonstrated experience integrating security, compliance, and governance tools into cloud architectures at scale Preferred Qualifications: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience) • Experience operating across multi-cloud environments (simultaneous hands-on expertise with AWS, Azure, and GCP) • Experience utilizing modern AI-assisted development tools to accelerate engineering, automation, and testing workflows • Professional certifications in Public Cloud platforms (e.g., AWS Solutions Architect, Azure Solutions Architect, GCP Cloud Architect) or HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate • Knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes *All employees working remotely will be required to adhere to UnitedHealth Group's Telecommuter Policy. Pay is based on several factors including but not limited to local labor markets, education, work experience, certifications, etc. In addition to your salary, we offer benefits such as, a comprehensive benefits package, incentive and recognition programs, equity stock purchase and 401k contribution (all benefits are subject to eligibility requirements). No matter where or when you begin a career with us, you'll find a far-reaching choice of benefits and incentives. The salary for this role will range from $112,700 - $193,200 annually based on full-time employment. We comply with all minimum wage laws as applicable.
