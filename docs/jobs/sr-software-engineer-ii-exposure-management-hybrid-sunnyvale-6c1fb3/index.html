About the Role As part of CrowdStrike’s Falcon Exposure Management team, you’ll design and build the risk intelligence platform that shows customers how risk compounds across their environment. The platform covers attack path analysis and posture scoring across hosts, cloud infrastructure, and identity assets in hybrid environments. You’ll build the backend services behind it: services that ingest high-volume data streams, run risk calculations, and automate remediation. You’ll work mainly in Golang microservices backed by PostgreSQL, Redis, and other data stores. This is an AI-first team, so you’ll also build agentic, AI-powered capabilities into the product and use AI tools throughout your engineering workflow. Leadership & Impact As a technical leader, you'll drive end-to-end product delivery, architect scalable solutions, and collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to define priorities and scope. Your expertise will directly influence the cybersecurity landscape, protecting organizations from sophisticated threats while advancing the state of the art in exposure management. This role is hybrid in Sunnyvale, CA requiring 2-3 days in office. What You'll Do: • Hands-on design and delivery: Design distributed backend services and the systems around them (databases, real-time data pipelines, APIs, and command-line tools), and write the production code yourself. You own the work from design to delivery. • Agentic product features: Design and ship customer-facing agents that surface risks proactively, answer complex security questions, and guide remediation. • AI for team productivity: Own at least one agentic initiative that improves how the team works, turning manual or hours-long workflows into automated, verifiable ones. • Problem decomposition: Break ambiguous, high-stakes problems (risk compounding models, attack path analysis, exploitability scoring) into measurable engineering solutions with clear contracts and failure modes. • Design leadership: Lead architecture reviews, write design docs and ADRs with explicit reasoning, and shape systems so they can absorb future AI advances without a rebuild. • Own what you ship: Set up monitoring and alerting, keep services highly available, take part in incident response and in the [field rotation: describe frequency and scope], and treat questions from sales and support as product signals. • Cross-team influence: Work with PM, UX/UI, Sensors, and Platform teams, with enough domain depth to drive architectural decisions, and keep clear documentation. • Technical Mentorship: Educate peers on AI automation patterns, share agentic approaches across workstreams, and raise the team's overall force-multiplying capability without compromising quality or ownership. What You'll Need: • Experience & Scale: 12+ years building, owning, and operating large-scale distributed systems at high data volume - with a hands-on track record of shipping, not just designing. • System Design Instincts: Strong system design instincts with the ability to make and document well-reasoned architectural trade-offs, including when to build white-box systems (understood internals, human-maintained mental model) vs. black-box systems (AI-built end-to-end, boundary-tested). • Analytical Problem-Solving: Analytical problem-solving depth: comfortable decomposing security and data-intensive problems - graph traversal, probabilistic risk scoring, exploitability modeling - into tractable, verifiable engineering components. • Programming Expertise: Advanced Go or Python expertise with hands-on microservices experience; you write and own production code, not just review it. • AI-First Practice: Demonstrated AI-first practice: you have built or meaningfully improved AI systems - agentic workflows, LLM-powered features, or automation pipelines - with output-level impact you can articulate. • LLM Fundamentals: Working knowledge of LLM fundamentals applied to real systems: inference behavior, hallucination patterns, non-determinism, context management, and agent/tool/memory architecture. • Distributed Systems Depth: Distributed systems depth including Kafka, Cassandra, Elasticsearch, and patterns such as sharding, eventual consistency, and caching - understood well enough to make system design decisions, not just operate them. • Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud infrastructure fluency (AWS preferred) for provisioning, observability, and cost-aware architecture. • Product Ownership: Product ownership mindset: you treat operational signals as product feedback, raise insights to PM and leadership, and iterate - build it, support it, own it. • Core Foundations: Strong foundation in algorithms, data structures, and distributed system design principles. • Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science/Engineering or equivalent professional experience. • Proven experience using AI to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency, and drive business outcomes.
