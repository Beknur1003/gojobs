Blockchain Developer (Golang / Cosmos SDK) Project: BlockMaze Open Positions: 1 Experience: 6 - 9 Years About the Role: We are looking for a skilled Blockchain Developer with strong Golang programming skills and hands-on experience with Cosmos SDK-based blockchain development. You will work on BlockMaze's core blockchain implementation, custom protocol modules, validator and staking functionality, EVM compatibility, and network upgrades. The role requires the ability to understand, develop, and modify blockchain-level source code rather than focusing solely on smart contract deployment. Key Responsibilities: - Develop, maintain, and enhance Cosmos SDK blockchain modules. - Work on existing protocol functionalities such as validator bonuses, reward distribution, vesting, epochs, oracle functionality, and token management. - Implement deterministic blockchain state transitions and transaction handlers. - Develop and maintain validator registration, staking, delegation, rewards, and slashing-related functionalities. - Investigate consensus behavior and application integration with CometBFT. - Support EVM-compatible transaction execution and account integrations. - Maintain blockchain query services using gRPC, REST, and RPC. - Develop comprehensive tests covering transactions, upgrades, state migrations, and protocol behavior. - Participate in blockchain upgrades, genesis configuration, and release preparation. - Investigate chain-level issues, transaction failures, and performance bottlenecks. - Collaborate with backend engineers integrating with blockchain services. - Maintain clear technical documentation for custom protocol features. Required Technical Skills: - Strong programming skills in Golang (Go). - Hands-on experience with Cosmos SDK or significant experience developing blockchain protocol software. - Understanding of blockchain architecture, consensus mechanisms, and distributed systems. - Familiarity with CometBFT and Tendermint. - Understanding of Proof of Stake (PoS), validators, staking, and delegation. - Knowledge of cryptographic signatures, hashing, and blockchain account structures. - Experience with gRPC, Protocol Buffers, Git, and automated testing. - Strong debugging, analytical, and code-review skills. Good to Have: - Experience with Cosmos EVM or Ethereum-compatible blockchain environments. - Knowledge of IBC-Go and cross-chain communication. - Experience developing custom Cosmos SDK modules. - Familiarity with Solidity and EVM smart contracts. - Experience deploying and maintaining validator or RPC nodes. - Understanding of blockchain state migrations and production upgrades. - Familiarity with RWA tokenization and permissioned digital asset infrastructure. Expected Outcomes: - Secure, reliable, and maintainable blockchain modules. - Successful blockchain upgrades and protocol changes. - Comprehensive testing of consensus-sensitive application logic. - Strong technical ownership of the BlockMaze blockchain codebase.
