Senior Software Engineer - AI Inference Platform

Hiring Eye Bangalore опубликовано 27 июля 2026

Senior
gRPCKubernetesMicroservicesPythonAI/LLM
We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer AI Inference to design and develop scalable AI inference platforms for large-scale production environments. The ideal candidate should have strong expertise in distributed systems, cloud-native technologies, and AI inference frameworks. Key Responsibilities Design and build scalable, high-performance AI inference services. Develop and optimize distributed systems for reliability, scalability, and performance. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver production-ready AI infrastructure. Improve system performance through observability, automation, and infrastructure optimization. Mentor team members and contribute to architecture and technical design. Required Skills 5+ years of experience in backend software engineering or distributed systems. Strong programming skills in Go/Golang or Python. Experience with Kubernetes, microservices, and cloud-native architectures. Knowledge of AI inference frameworks such as vLLM, Ray Serve, SGLang, or similar. Understanding of GPU optimization, LLM serving, distributed systems, and Infrastructure as Code (IaC). Familiarity with gRPC, observability tools, and open-source technologies is a plus.
