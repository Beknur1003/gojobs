Software Engineering Manager 5451 Cloud, Mobile & IoT Platforms Location: Hybrid (Galleria Area) Compensation: $150K-$155K Base + 10% Bonus (Total Target Compensation: $165K-$170.5K) Tier4 Group is seeking a Software Engineering Manager to lead a high-performing team responsible for developing and enhancing a cloud-connected platform that combines web, mobile, and IoT technologies. This is a player-coach leadership role ideal for a Lead Engineer looking to move into management or a hands-on Engineering Manager who enjoys staying close to architecture, technology decisions, and delivery execution. We are looking for a leader who can balance technical credibility with strong people leadership. You'll oversee a team of approximately six engineers, drive engineering execution, remove obstacles, mentor developers, and ensure the successful delivery of business-critical initiatives. This role is focused on outcomes, collaboration, and team success rather than managing large organizations or operating solely at a strategic level. What You'll Do Engineering Leadership & Delivery • Lead, mentor, and develop a team of approximately 6 software engineers. • Drive execution across multiple product initiatives and ensure successful delivery against business objectives. • Foster accountability, collaboration, and a high-performance engineering culture. • Partner closely with Product, Operations, and Customer Success teams to align technical priorities with customer and business needs. • Help establish team goals, delivery expectations, and development best practices. Technical Leadership • Remain actively involved in architecture discussions and key technical decisions. • Guide development efforts across backend services, cloud infrastructure, web applications, mobile applications, and connected-device systems. • Conduct design and code reviews to ensure quality, scalability, maintainability, and security. • Champion engineering excellence through CI/CD automation, testing, monitoring, and operational best practices. Platform Growth & Innovation • Support the development and scaling of cloud-connected applications and IoT-enabled solutions. • Ensure system reliability, performance, fault tolerance, and security. • Lead integrations with third-party SaaS applications, identity providers, and external technology platforms. • Coordinate with development partners and vendors to support strategic initiatives and product delivery. Required Qualifications Must-Have Experience • 3+ years of engineering leadership experience managing software development teams. • Strong hands-on software engineering background with the ability to contribute to architectural and technical discussions. • Proven experience delivering and scaling cloud-connected platforms. • Strong experience with GoLang and AWS cloud infrastructure. • Experience leading full-stack development teams across backend, web, and mobile technologies. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, release management, monitoring, and production operations. • Demonstrated success driving delivery, execution, and cross-functional collaboration. Preferred Qualifications The following experience will help candidates stand out: • IoT, connected-device, smart access, access control, or hardware-connected software platforms. • Experience with cloud-connected products that interact with physical devices. • React, TypeScript, and React Native development experience. • Native mobile development using Swift and/or Kotlin. • Third-party integration experience including APIs, OAuth, and SAML. • Security expertise including RBAC, authentication, encryption, TLS, and device provisioning. • Experience managing multidisciplinary teams delivering both software and hardware-connected products. • Experience working with external vendors, consultants, and development partners. Ideal Candidate Profile This role is well suited for: • A Lead Software Engineer ready to step into a broader leadership role. • A Software Engineering Manager from a small or mid-sized organization who remains technically engaged. • A leader who enjoys mentoring engineers while staying connected to product development and architecture. • Someone who is motivated by building teams, improving delivery, and driving measurable business outcomes. Key Partnerships You'll collaborate closely with: • Product Management • Customer Success • Operations • Software Engineering Teams • Mobile Development Teams • Cloud Infrastructure Teams • External Development Partners • Executive Leadership Why This Opportunity? This is an opportunity to make a significant impact in a growing organization where engineering leadership is measured by execution, team development, and product delivery. If you're a hands-on technical leader who enjoys balancing people management with technology leadership, we'd love to hear from you.
