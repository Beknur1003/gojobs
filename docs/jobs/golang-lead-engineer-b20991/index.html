As a senior technical leader, you'll: • Lead the design, build, release and support of complex backend services and shared platform capabilities. • Drive engineering quality through code reviews, technical leadership, reusable patterns and modern software engineering practices. • Build resilient, observable and secure services that perform effectively in production environments. • Partner with Product Owners, Architects, Engineers, SREs and Security specialists to translate requirements into practical technical solutions. • Mentor engineers, support capability growth and contribute to engineering communities and standards. • Champion automation, operational excellence, reliability, secure coding and continuous improvement.
