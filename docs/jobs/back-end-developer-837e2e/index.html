Job title: Senior Backend Engineer (Rust) Job type: Permanent Role Location: Onsite in Paris The company: A scaling consumer tech business based in central Paris. They're building out their backend team and looking for early members who can take ownership of core components as the product grows. Role and responsibilities: • Build backend systems designed to handle heavy traffic and large data volumes, scaling horizontally • Maintain and evolve a shared Rust component used across iOS and Android, covering networking, data synchronisation and data-driven feature logic • Work closely with mobile engineers to keep a unified codebase and a consistent experience across platforms • Contribute to feature design and product discussions • Own your scope, including monitoring analytics, managing system crashes and triaging bugs • Take ownership of central components over time and develop deep expertise in your area Job requirements: • Production experience with Rust, or a strong background in systems-level languages (C++, C, Golang, OCaml) • Strong, hands-on experience with distributed systems in production (Kafka, Redpanda, ScyllaDB, Cassandra or similar) • DevOps/Infra experience, ideally in Kubernetes, deployed on a major cloud (GCP, AWS, Azure) • Strong instincts for what makes consumer social products succeed or fail • Comfortable being versatile in an early-stage team Benefits: • Central Paris office with good transport links • 100% health cover • Maternity, paternity and second parent leave on full salary • 8 to 9 weeks of annual leave, including company-wide shutdowns for two weeks in summer and one in winter • Full relocation support: visa sponsorship, one month of accommodation on arrival, help finding a permanent home, assistance with French admin and French lessons Accessibility Statement: We make an active choice to be inclusive towards everyone every day.
