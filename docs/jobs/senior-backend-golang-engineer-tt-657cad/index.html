We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join an early-stage cloud security startup founded in Berlin in 2026, with an office in Berlin and a team across Europe. The company is building a real-time, agentic cloud-security platform. It acts as an enforcement layer that applies security and compliance policies from commit through production, stopping or fixing non-compliant infrastructure changes before they ship. You'd join a small, foundational engineering team and help build the core platform from the ground up. The environment is fast-moving and sometimes chaotic, so we want someone proactive and highly communicative who enjoys that. Logistics • Location: Germany, hybrid in Berlin. You must already be based in Germany. • Employment: full-time • Level: Senior, around 8 years of experience • Visa: no visa sponsorship or work permit support is available What you'll do • Build and shape the core backend of the platform in Go • Work across multi-cloud environments (AWS, GCP, Azure) • Design systems that enforce policies in real time and handle non-compliant infrastructure changes • Use AI coding tools and agentic development in your daily work, and critically review the output • Challenge ideas, including your own and AI-generated code, rather than accepting them blindly Core stack Go, SQL, AWS / GCP / Azure, Terraform or Pulumi, AI coding tools and agentic development Requirements • Solid professional experience with Go (Golang) • Around 8 years of software engineering experience (at least 5), across at least two or three companies • Hands-on experience configuring and working with at least one major cloud provider (AWS, GCP or Azure), and ideally exposure to several • Based in Germany and able to work hybrid in Berlin, with no need for visa or work permit support • Strong critical thinking and communication, including the ability to push back on a technical proposal Nice to have • Experience with complex, multi-tenant environments or platform integrations (e.g. observability, cloud security) • Terraform or Pulumi • Background in high-bar, fast-paced tech environments • Comfort in a small, early-stage team where you shape the product and the processes Hiring process • Initial screening and meet-and-greet with the Founder's Associate • Technical stage 1: an algorithmic challenge and a discussion about AI usage with a Senior Engineer or the CTO (30 minutes) • Technical stage 2: a system design interview (scaling, caching, communication) with the CTO and a Senior Engineer • Culture-fit interview with the co-founders, ideally in person in Berlin
