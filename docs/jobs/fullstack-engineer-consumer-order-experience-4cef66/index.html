Software Engineer II (Full-Stack) - Consumer Order Experience

Deliveroo London, United Kingdom - Deliveroo опубликовано 26 сентября 2026

Go — основной язык
TypeScriptReactAI/LLM
Our Global Structure Deliveroo is now part of DoorDash, bringing together teams with even greater reach, scale, and ambition. Depending on your role, you may collaborate with teammates, systems, and leaders across DoorDash and Wolt. Together, we’re unlocking new possibilities as one global team. Why Deliveroo? We're building the definitive online food company, transforming the way the world eats by making hyper-local food more convenient and accessible. We're at the forefront of an industry, powered by our market-leading technology and unrivalled network to bring incredible convenience and selection to our customers. Working at Deliveroo is the perfect environment to build a definitive career, motivated by impact on your own development, on Deliveroo as we scale, and on the marketplace and communities we serve. Consumer Ordering Experience owns the core purchase journey: the store page, item page, basket, and checkout flow, across both app and web. It's a global team — the surfaces we build support all of our brands: Deliveroo, Wolt, and Doordash. You'd be the team's first dedicated web/full-stack engineer, helping us build out our web experience from the ground up. What You'll Do • Own and evolve the store, item, basket, and checkout experiences on web, working primarily in React and TypeScript • Contribute to backend services written in Golang — deep Go experience isn't a hard requirement, but you should be comfortable picking up a new statically typed backend language • Improve code structure, shape architecture, and review code across the team • Partner closely with your Product Manager and fellow engineers to scope and ship changes to the ordering funnel • Take part in the hiring process and support the growth of your teammates as the team scales What We're Looking For Must haves: • Strong full-stack experience — comfortable working across web frontend (React/TypeScript) and backend (Go, or similar) to unblock delivery end-to-end • Experience designing, implementing and rolling out A/B experiments (or strong aptitude to quickly learn our experimentation platform) — including guardrail metrics, staged rollouts and reading results • Comfortable operating in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment with shifting priorities and short XP cycles (weeks, not quarters) • Strong ownership mindset — able to take a brief PRD through design, implementation, rollout and production monitoring with minimal hand-holding • Track record of shipping incrementally and iterating on data rather than big-bang launches • Willing to participate in an on-call rotation and respond to incidents Nice to haves: • Experience coordinating engineers across multiple platforms (iOS/Android/Web/BE) on a single initiative • Active use of AI-assisted coding workflows, and interest in building reusable tooling/skills for the team We're looking for someone London-based who can work from our office 2–3 days a week alongside the rest of the team. Why Join Us? At Deliveroo, you’ll do work that matters—solving real-world problems in a three-sided marketplace that’s constantly evolving. We’re food lovers, problem solvers, community organisers, proud parents, and more, brought together by a shared drive to make things better. Working here you can expect to: 🔧 Solve meaningful problems at real scale Work on a complex, always-on marketplace that impacts millions every day. 🌱 See your impact, fast Ship, test and improve ideas quickly in a low-hierarchy, high-ownership environment. 🧠 Grow through challenge and ownership Take on big, ambiguous problems and accelerate your career with strong support. 🌎 A culture built for builders High standards, collaboration, flexible working and continuous learning. 💰 Share in the success you help create Competitive salary and equity options, so you’re rewarded for the impact you make. ➡️ Want a deeper look at how we build?
