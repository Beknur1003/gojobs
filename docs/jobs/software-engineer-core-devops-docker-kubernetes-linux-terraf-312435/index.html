Meet the Team The Cisco IQ and Validation team is building the next generation of intelligent, AI-driven network validation and automation capabilities. We combine cloud-native software engineering, Agentic AI, LLMs, MCP, A2A, and network automation to enable customers to validate and operate complex environments more intelligently. The team brings together architects, senior engineers, software engineers, AI specialists, and domain experts working across a global organization. We are building foundational capabilities that will enable AI agents to reason, interact with enterprise tools, and safely execute validation workflows at scale. Your Impact You will serve as the core DevOps partner for our application and QA teams, directly responsible for accelerating development velocity. Your role involves designing and maintaining build/release pipelines, managing containerized services, and bridging the gap between application requirements and underlying cloud infrastructure. You will evolve from infrastructure-level automation toward high-level microservice development, ensuring our platform remains scalable, secure, and highly performant as we drive Cisco’s transition to cloud-recurring business models. Minimum Qualifications • 8+ years of professional software engineering experience designing and building complex backend, distributed, automation, or platform systems. • 4+ years of experience developing and deploying applications in the cloud. • Deep technical proficiency in Docker, Kubernetes, and Linux systems administration (systemd, networking, and performance tuning). • Advanced competency in Python and shell scripting. • Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible). • Demonstrated experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitHub Actions). • Practical experience with observability tooling, including metrics (Prometheus/Grafana) and centralized logging (ELK, Loki, or Splunk). • Strong understanding of REST, JSON RPC, and secure cloud application hardening practices. Preferred Qualifications • Proficiency in Golang. • Experience deploying OpenShift clusters and managing workloads in OpenStack or AWS. • Expertise in GitOps deployment patterns (ArgoCD, Flux). • Experience synthesizing industry benchmarks and technical feedback to drive architecture decisions. • Background in network consulting or automated infrastructure testing.
