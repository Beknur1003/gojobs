About the Company：BIT (formerly Matrixport) is a global digital asset financial services and infrastructure group. Headquartered in Singapore and founded in 2019, BIT bridges traditional finance and digital assets through governance-driven financial services and technology. The firm manages over US$7 billion in assets and facilitates more than US$7 billion in monthly trading volume. BIT offers services including custody, trading, asset and wealth management, liquidity and financing solutions, and tokenised real-world assets (RWA), serving institutional and professional investors globally. BIT Group entities maintain a licensed and regulated footprint across Singapore, Hong Kong, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, the United States and Bhutan. For more information, visit www.bit.com Director of Software Engineering Singapore · On-site · Employment Pass sponsorship available About the Role We are looking for a Director of Software Engineering to lead a 10–15 person backend engineering team in Singapore. You will own the architecture of our core systems and help the team ship reliably and with high quality — combining strong technical judgment with hands-on engineering leadership and effective people management. What You'll Do Technical Direction • Own the overall architecture and technology choices for the systems under your remit, with final accountability for their reliability, performance, and maintainability. • Lead design and review of critical modules, and drive high-complexity or high-risk initiatives with well-controlled, executable plans. • Define the roadmap for system quality — including technical debt reduction, reusable middleware and libraries, and integration testing infrastructure. Team Leadership • Set goals, run performance reviews, and lead hiring and development across the engineering teams in your department. • Understand needs across business, product, operations, and risk, and allocate engineering resources accordingly. • Establish engineering processes and standards built for the AI era, and lead the team in applying AI pragmatically to raise delivery quality and efficiency. What We're Looking For • 6+ years of backend engineering experience, including 3+ years leading engineering teams. • Expert in Golang or Rust, with hands-on experience building and maintaining large-scale applications. Comfortable with scripting languages such as Python, Bash, and SQL. • Deep understanding of MySQL transaction isolation levels and their common patterns in real-world application development. • Familiarity with standard cloud services, AWS in particular — EC2, EBS, S3, RDS, VPC, ELB, etc. • Extensive experience designing, operating, and evolving distributed systems, with a deep understanding of their complexity across transactional semantics, data consistency, testability, and observability — and proven methods for managing it. • Professional working proficiency in Mandarin for technical discussion and management communication, as the team collaborates primarily in Mandarin; proficient in written English. • Eligible to work in Singapore, or eligible for an Employment Pass. Nice to Have • Experience building financial services such as asset management, trading, or payments, with familiarity in clearing and settlement, risk management, and double-entry bookkeeping. • Familiarity with derivatives, structured products, or DeFi products. • Experience managing cross-regional or remote teams. What We Value • Technical taste — You simplify relentlessly and find reliability in simplicity. You have little tolerance for uncontrolled growth in complexity. • Curiosity — You have the interest and patience to learn new things, the drive to get to the root of a problem, and you build solutions from first principles. • Pragmatism — Your decisions are grounded in thorough research. You respect ROI and smooth migration paths, and you know what not to build.
