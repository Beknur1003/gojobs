About Us The financial advisory industry is a $80T market in the US, but has seen little technological innovation compared to the self-service market (Robinhood, Betterment, Wealthfront, SoFi). Client data management is a universally painful and expensive problem for financial advisors. Today, advisors manually collect, update, and utilize client data across the 7-10 software tools that they use to run their business. Data transcription and translation across systems is time-consuming and error-prone, leading to costly inefficiencies and embarrassing client-facing problems. Managing data access control, audit logs, and compliance is a constant challenge, particularly in larger organizations. We are building the first wealth tech data orchestration company. We sync client data across a financial advisor's tech stack, unlocking the efficiency of a fully-integrated software suite with the choice of tools that best fit their business. We are laser-focused on enabling the best technology experiences for advisors and unlocking the potential of the entire wealth tech software ecosystem. We’re an experienced team of start-up operators with deep roots in both tech and advisory. You can learn more about us on our website. Role Details Role overview: We're looking for a fullstack engineer to build features across our platform. You'll own end-to-end delivery of product workflows, working across frontend interfaces and backend services to help financial advisors manage client data seamlessly across their tech stack. Where you'll make an impact: • Build and ship features end-to-end, working across React, Node.js and Golang. • Develop user-facing workflows including data collection forms, reporting interfaces, and account management experiences that handle complex financial data. • Implement backend APIs and services that power our data syncing and orchestration capabilities, ensuring reliable data flow across systems. • Write well-tested, maintainable code and participate in code reviews, contributing to the team's technical quality and standards. • Collaborate with Product, Design, and other engineers to break down requirements, identify technical approaches, and deliver solutions efficiently. • Debug and resolve production issues across the stack, ensuring system reliability for our customers. Requirements: • 4-6 years of fullstack engineering experience with exposure to both frontend and backend development. • Solid fundamentals in web development, including JavaScript/TypeScript, modern frontend frameworks (React preferred), API design, and working with databases. • Comfortable working across the full stack - you may have a preference but can contribute effectively on both frontend and backend work. • Collaborative mindset and strong communication skills - you work effectively with teammates and ask good questions when you need clarity. • Eager to learn and grow your technical skills - you're curious about new technologies and open to feedback. • Self-directed and capable of owning features with minimal oversight, while knowing when to ask for input or guidance. • Bias toward shipping and iteration - you balance getting things done with building things well. You'll stand out if you have: • Experience building B2B SaaS products or working in fintech/financial services. • Exposure to building integrations, working with third-party APIs, or data transformation pipelines. • Background at startups where you had to wear multiple hats and ship features independently. • Experience with testing frameworks and writing automated tests for both frontend and backend code. • Interest in using AI tools to improve your development workflow and productivity. Our tech stack: React, TypeScript, Node.js, Fastify, Golang, PostgreSQL Why Dispatch? Why work at Dispatch? • We’re a highly experienced team with a track record of successfully building together. We’re well-versed in building for the best tech companies and asset managers. • We’re a well-funded company backed by top-tier investors. • We're laser-focused on customers and believe that a world-class user experience is critical for product and business success. • We move fast, iterate quickly, and have a high bar for what “excellent” looks like.
