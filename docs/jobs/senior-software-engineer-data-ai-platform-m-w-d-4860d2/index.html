Join us and build the future of live entertainment. With over 10 million end users and usage quadrupling annually, our infrastructure now handles over 1 billion requests per month — and counting. Our API-first platform solves complex system challenges at scale — delivering performance, flexibility, and reliability for the world’s leading live entertainment brands. As a Senior Software Engineer - Data & AI Platform (d/f/m) your responsibilities will include: Your Responsibilities: • Build Analytical Query Engines: Connect our transactional backends (MongoDB) with modern analytical event stores (ClickHouse) to support sub-second aggregations across millions of ticket transactions. • Implement AI Retrieval & Reporting Workflows: Build and deploy Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, semantic parsers, and agentic query supervisors that convert natural language prompts into deterministic, accurate SQL and aggregation queries. • Maintain Low-Latency Reporting APIs: Write high-performance production services in Go or TypeScript/Node.js that power real-time reporting dashboards for global organizers without degrading transactional processing. • Centralize Data Flows & Auditing: Partner with platform and security teams to build clean data schemas, strict audit trails, and automated evaluation suites that catch hallucinated data or metric drifts before they reach clients. • Sprint Scoping & Architecture: Collaborate directly in monthly in-person architecture sessions in Frankfurt alongside the VP of Engineering, CTO, and engineering leads to define technical milestones. What you will need to succeed in this role: • 5+ years of hands-on experience building high-throughput, distributed production services using Go, Python, or TypeScript/Node.js. • Analytical Warehousing Experience: Direct, hands-on production experience with ClickHouse (or closely related low-latency analytical engines like DuckDB, Snowflake, or Apache Pinot) for high-volume event streams. • Database Mastery: Solid practical experience with both document/NoSQL stores (MongoDB) and relational engines (PostgreSQL). • Production AI / RAG Engineering: Proven experience deploying production RAG systems, function calling, evaluation frameworks, and supervisor architectures (e.g., LangGraph, LlamaIndex, or custom orchestration layers). • Event-Driven Architectures: Strong understanding of distributed message streaming using Kafka, RabbitMQ, or cloud pub/sub mechanisms. • Pragmatic Mindset: High autonomy and scrappiness—you choose reliable, deterministic validation layers and battle-tested databases over fragile AI hype.
