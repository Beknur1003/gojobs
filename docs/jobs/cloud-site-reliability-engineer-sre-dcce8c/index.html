Cloud Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

Adecco San Francisco Bay Area опубликовано 20 ноября 2026

MySQLRedisAWSAzureLinuxPythonJava
About the Team Our client is a leading global cloud and technology company. Its Network team sits at the core of the company’s cloud infrastructure platform, delivering a broad range of network resources and solutions across the industry. The team supports the parent organization’s internal business needs and helps millions of enterprise customers worldwide succeed in their digital transformation. Responsibilities To strengthen the stability of the company’s cloud networking, the team is hiring experienced network operations engineers who can respond quickly to live production issues and help standardize network operations, keeping customer businesses running reliably. Key responsibilities include: • Own the stability and reliability of cloud networking, ensuring business continuity for users. • Design, build, and maintain automated operations systems for cloud networking to improve operational efficiency and quality. • Manage monitoring, alerting, and troubleshooting for cloud networking, responding to and resolving issues promptly. • Contribute to the architecture design and optimization of cloud networking to improve system performance and scalability. • Stay current on advancements and trends in cloud network operations and stability, and drive technical innovation and improvement. • Participate in the on-duty rotation, responding to customer issues within Service Level Agreement (SLA) timeframes, resolving problems, and improving the customer experience. Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree or higher in a computer-related field, with 3+ years of experience in cloud computing or network operations, and solid familiarity with cloud infrastructure and network technologies. • Strong proficiency in one or more programming languages such as Java, Python, or Golang, with the ability to write efficient, scalable scripts and tools. • Strong command of Linux systems, including common commands, network protocols, and system performance tuning. • Working knowledge of major cloud platforms (for example, AWS, Azure, or other leading providers), with deeper understanding of cloud network products, cluster management, and automated deployment. • Knowledge of databases such as MySQL and Redis, with the ability to quickly locate and resolve database-related issues. • Strong troubleshooting skills, with the ability to rapidly identify and resolve issues in production environments. • Strong teamwork skills, working closely with development, testing, and other team members to maintain system stability and reliability. • Effective communication skills, including the ability to express ideas clearly and write clear, easy-to-understand technical documentation.
