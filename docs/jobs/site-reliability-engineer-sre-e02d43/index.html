Job Details The Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) team at India is the core technical force within the Monitoring and 3rd Level department. We own the availability, performance, and infrastructure health of our global hosting environment. Our team proactively detects, diagnoses, and resolves infrastructure anomalies and system-level outages before they impact our users, and we act as the definitive 3rd-level engineering authority when major service or product disruptions occur. We are expanding our department with a Site Reliability Engineer who specializes in deep infrastructure diagnostics and system-level troubleshooting. In this role, you will be responsible for maintaining and optimizing the uptime of our massive footprint of Shared, Dedicated, and OpenStack-based VPS environments, alongside modern containerized applications. A core focus of this position is utilizing our unified, in-house monitoring application (powered by a Prometheus backend) to proactively track infrastructure health, interpret complex system metrics, and rapidly isolate the root cause of service degradations. As an SRE III, you will own critical incidents, mentor junior SREs (SRE I/II) within the department, and ensure seamless monitoring visibility across legacy systems, Plesk setups, and Kubernetes clusters. Key Responsibilities ● Proactive Infrastructure Monitoring: Utilize our in-house monitoring application to maintain constant visibility over our environments. Analyze metric trends and implement robust, symptom-based alerting thresholds across Shared, Dedicated, VPS, and Kubernetes environments to catch issues before they impact products. ● 3rd Level Customer issues and Infrastructure Outage Resolution: Act as the primary technical responder and diagnostic expert during critical service or product outages, performing deep-system troubleshooting under pressure. ● Diverse Platform Support: Diagnose and maintain maximum uptime and performance for traditional webhosting tech stacks, Plesk control panels, and multi-tenant OpenStack VPS environments. ● Proactive Problem Management: Analyze long-term metric trends using advanced PromQL to identify systemic performance bottlenecks, hardware degradations, or configuration drift, driving deep Root Cause Analysis (RCA). ● Internal Department Mentorship: Coach and guide SRE I and SRE II engineers within the department to elevate the team's collective diagnostic capabilities and troubleshooting workflows using our in-house monitoring application. Core Technical Experience ● Advanced Linux Internals: Deep, low-level understanding of Linux operating systems (CPU scheduling, memory management, I/O bottlenecks, kernel logs, file systems, and network stack troubleshooting). ● Applied Prometheus & PromQL Expertise: Strong proficiency as a power user of Prometheus-backed monitoring tools. Expert-level ability to write highly optimized PromQL queries for complex alerting, dashboarding, and live incident troubleshooting. ● Multi-Tenant Hosting Ecosystems: Extensive hands-on experience diagnosing failures in both traditional Shared/Dedicated Linux hosting architectures and modern Plesk control panel environments. ● OpenStack & Virtualization Monitoring: Practical experience monitoring and troubleshooting Virtual Private Servers (VPS) and hypervisor behavior within an OpenStack cloud environment. ● Kubernetes Workload Monitoring: Proven track record of tracking the health of containerized applications, utilizing tools like Prometheus Operator or Kube-State-Metrics to isolate pod, node, or cluster-level issues. ● Internet Services Stack: Expert knowledge of underlying hosting services: Web (Nginx, Apache, Varnish), DNS (Bind/PowerDNS), Mail (Postfix/Dovecot), and Databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL). Skill Requirements Mandatory Technical Skills ● Advanced Linux Systems Engineering & OS Internals (Kernel, I/O, Networking) ● Applied Prometheus monitoring (Advanced PromQL, Metric Analysis) ● OpenStack VPS infrastructure diagnostics and health management ● Kubernetes workload and container-level monitoring ● Webhosting services stack (Plesk, Nginx, Apache, Postfix, DNS) Mandatory Behavioral & Leadership Skills ● Advanced Systemic Debugging: Exceptional ability to isolate and trace complex bugs across disparate legacy and cloud systems under pressure. ● Analytical Thinking: Relies heavily on deterministic data and metrics to solve architectural and server-level problems. ● Team Mentorship: Strong collaborative mindset with a willingness to mentor SRE I/II peers within the department. ● Clear Synthesis: Translates complex incident realities into structured technical documentation and clear, actionable feedback for platform/development teams. Desirable / Optional Skills ● Automation & Scripting: Ability to write or modify scripts (Python, GoLang, or advanced Bash/Shell) to assist in data gathering or routine tasks is an advantage but not mandatory. ● Experience with configuration management tools (such as Chef or Ansible). Education and Experience Requirements ● Education: B.Tech/B.E. in Computer Science, IT, ECE, or a related technical field (or equivalent practical experience). ● Experience: 4–8 years of experience in IT operations, with a strong focus on high-scale Webhosting, Managed Service Providers (MSP), or infrastructure administration. ● Direct SRE/3rd-Level Experience: Minimum of 2 years strictly in an SRE or 3rd-level infrastructure engineering role dealing with large-scale distributed systems and deep diagnostics.
