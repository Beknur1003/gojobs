The Role The Software Engineer position is a key contributor to a key portfolio within the Cloud and Platform Engineering Group, ensuring business strategies, plans and initiatives are executed / delivered in compliance with governing regulations, internal policies and procedures. This role is accountable for development and operations implementation of technology solutions that support Digital Banking and Cloud Business strategies and objectives as well as ensuring compliance with security and bank regulations. This role will contribute to the overall success of the Cloud and Platform Engineering unit by ensuring the delivery of high-quality technology solutions through effective collaboration with technology professionals and key stakeholders within Digital Banking and across Scotiabank. Is this role right for you?
