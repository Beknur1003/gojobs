Senior Software Engineer

Legrand North America Blumenau, SC, Brazil опубликовано 7 августа 2026

Гибрид Senior
PostgreSQLRabbitMQMicroservicesLinuxCI/CDPythonC++React
Legrand has an exciting opportunity for a Senior Software Engineer to join the Data Center Power and Control ZPE Systems Team in Blumenau, SC, Brazil. This senior position will work on ZPE Cloud project, that is a SaaS solution hosted on a cloud provider and integrated inside Nodegrid OS. This position will work on the platform team, building and operating the cloud microservices that manage a large fleet of distributed network devices. For this role we are looking for a person with strong engineering principles that can help plan, document and then execute with quality as a priority. ZPE Cloud as a product provides a centralized view and management platform for Nodegrid OS, that in its turn is the Linux based OS for our serial console and service routers products. Among the main responsibilities: • Design, build and maintain cloud microservices that manage a large fleet of distributed network devices. • Plan and document technical solutions before implementation, keeping quality, security and reliability as first-class concerns. • Model and operate device-to-cloud communication over MQTT, working with message queues to handle telemetry, configuration and command flows at scale. • Develop and evolve cloud-side microservices backed by PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ, keeping them performant and observable. • Lead code reviews, set and uphold coding standards, and mentor engineers across levels. • Own architectural decisions for your area and contribute to the long-term technical vision of ZPE Cloud. • Participate in incident response and root cause analysis, and drive process improvements that raise team effectiveness. We are looking for a senior engineer with strong ownership who can plan, document and then execute with quality as the priority. You should communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders and raise the bar for the people around you. • Deep expertise in software architecture, scalability and system design. • Proven experience delivering complex projects with minimal oversight. • Ability to reason about distributed systems: message brokers, queues, eventual consistency and fault tolerance. • Comfortable with ambiguity, able to balance immediate delivery with long-term technical health. • Mentors and coaches others and influences technical direction through evidence and consensus. Skills/Knowledge/Abilities: These are the minimum skills we’re looking for in a good candidate: • Strong programming skills in Python and Go (Golang). • Experience building and consuming REST or message-driven APIs in microservice architecture. • Solid knowledge of relational databases, particularly PostgreSQL. • Hands-on experience with messaging and queueing systems such as RabbitMQ. • Practical understanding of security, performance and reliability trade-offs. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, testing discipline and clear technical documentation. • Excellent written and verbal communication. Nice to Have: • Good command of Linux and computer networking concepts (TCP/IP, routing, services). • Programming experience in C++. • Experience with MQTT for messaging. • Front-end development with React. • Experience with cloud platforms and infrastructure as code. • Background in managing or monitoring fleets of distributed network devices.
