ABOUT US Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game. For more information, visit xsolla.com. ABOUT YOU We are looking for a Middle/Senior Backend Engineer who is passionate about building scalable blockchain infrastructure and Web3 services to join our Xsolla ZK team. The best candidate will be someone who thrives in a fast-paced, highly collaborative, and exceptionally dynamic setting and is excited to develop backend systems that power NFT marketplaces, smart wallets, and blockchain-based gaming infrastructure. Strong backend development skills in Go are essential, along with experience in building high-load distributed systems and working with databases. The ability to design resilient microservices architecture and integrate with blockchain networks will be key to your success in this role. If you're passionate about building the infrastructure layer for next-generation gaming and love working on cutting-edge blockchain technology that enables gamers to truly own their digital assets, we would love to hear from you!
