Our momentum is backed by the market: Druva was named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Backup and Data Protection Platforms, a Leader in the IDC MarketScape for Cyber-Recovery, and a Leader & Outperformer in the GigaOm Cloud Data Protection Radar. Team & Mission: Druva Product Platform The Druva Product Platform team builds the shared core SaaS foundation that every Druva product line runs on. We build and operate the multi-tenant control plane, distributed APIs, tenant lifecycle and provisioning pipelines, Identity & Access Management (IAM/RBAC), distributed job orchestration, usage metering, and platform observability. When a new workload or product capability ships at Druva, it ships on top of what this team owns. That makes the work high-leverage and high-blast-radius: our services are multi-tenant, globally distributed, always-on, and measured against strict availability and latency objectives at a scale of thousands of enterprise customers and billions of operations per year. Role Overview As a Senior Software Engineer (Development) on the Druva Product Platform team, you will be a core backend engineer responsible for designing, developing, scaling, and operating critical multi-tenant microservices and APIs. You will own complex technical deliverables end-to-end—from architectural documents and high-performance Go/Python implementations to automated CI/CD deployments and production telemetry. This is a hands-on individual contributor role for an engineer who thrives on distributed systems challenges, concurrency, high data throughput, and building foundational platform services that empower other engineers. Key Skills: • 5–8+ years of hands-on experience in software product development, building high-scale distributed systems and SaaS backends. • Strong proficiency in Go (Golang) and/or Python, with deep understanding of concurrency (goroutines/async I/O), memory management, and data structures. • Solid experience in designing, building, and maintaining microservices and REST/gRPC APIs in high-throughput multi-tenant environments. • Deep hands-on experience with AWS Cloud services (such as EKS, EC2, S3, DynamoDB, RDS, SQS, SNS, Lambda) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes). • Strong database expertise across relational engines (PostgreSQL/MySQL) and distributed NoSQL databases (DynamoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch). • Proven experience with event-driven architectures and message streaming systems (Apache Kafka, AWS SQS/EventBridge). • Demonstrated ownership of services in production: telemetry/instrumentation (Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Datadog), SLI/SLO monitoring, on-call support, and root cause analysis (RCA). • Solid commitment to lean engineering practices: automated unit/integration testing, CI/CD pipelines, and rigorous peer code reviews. Desirable Skills: • Demonstrated experience building multi-tenant platform primitives: tenant isolation, rate limiting/token buckets, quota management, and schema migrations. • Domain expertise in data protection, backup/recovery, storage systems, virtualization, or cyber resilience (RPO/RTO). • Knowledge of enterprise authentication and authorization standards (OAuth 2.0, SAML, OIDC, RBAC/ABAC, mTLS). • Exposure to AI/LLM integration patterns (vector search, prompt engineering pipelines, LLM agent integration). • Experience in high-growth, fast-paced SaaS product environments. Role and Responsibilities: • Architect, implement, and maintain high-performance, resilient microservices in Go/Python that serve as the backbone for Druva's SaaS platform. • Design clean, backward-compatible, secure RESTful and gRPC APIs consumed by internal product engineering teams and enterprise customers. • Author clear technical design documents evaluating architectural trade-offs across latency, consistency, scalability, and cost. • Optimize database queries, caching strategies, and data models across PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, and Redis for sub-millisecond responsiveness. • Drive production operational excellence: set up granular telemetry (metrics, traces, logs), define SLOs, and participate in on-call incident response. • Conduct blameless root cause analyses (RCAs) for production issues and ship permanent software mitigations to eliminate recurring failure modes. • Partner with Product Managers, Architects, and Quality Engineers to break down business requirements into deliverable engineering milestones. • Champion code quality and engineering best practices: perform in-depth code reviews and maintain high automated test coverage (>85%). • Mentor and coach junior engineers, fostering technical excellence, design rigor, and continuous learning across the team. Qualification: • B.Tech / B.E / M.E / M.Tech in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent practical experience.
