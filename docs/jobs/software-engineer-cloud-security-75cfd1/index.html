OUR STORY: 🇪🇺 Join Scaleway and shape the sovereign cloud of tomorrow ! Since 1999, we have been designing secure, sustainable infrastructures aimed at supporting the most ambitious companies. Historically known for our dedicated servers (Dedibox), we made a strategic shift to cloud computing in 2015. Staying true to our principles of simplicity, flexibility, and technical excellence, we have become one of the leading players in Europe in the sector. With the rise of artificial intelligence, we have strengthened our commitment, supported by the Iliad Group, which is investing €3 billion to develop a serious, sovereign AI alternative to American and Asian giants. Every day, thanks to our fast-growing portfolio of cloud and AI products (bare metal, containerization, serverless, AI, etc.), Scaleway proudly serves thousands of customer across the private and public sector, from corporations like France Télévisions or Hachette Livre, to fast-growing startups like Photoroom and Biolevate, to institutions like the City of Copenhagen. 📍 Our offices are located in Paris, Lille, Toulouse, Rennes, Rouen, Bordeaux and Lyon. WHY WE NEED YOU? As Scaleway continues to grow, we are strengthening our Secret Service team to expand our security product portfolio, including Key Manager, Secret Manager and Audit Trail, and support the increasing security requirements of our cloud customers. As a Software Engineer, your mission will be to design, build and maintain critical security services at the heart of Scaleway's cloud infrastructure. You will work on challenging technical topics involving cryptography, Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), key management and secure backend architectures, ensuring the reliability, security and scalability of our services. This is an opportunity to contribute directly to the development of secure, reliable and compliant cloud services, while helping strengthen Europe's sovereign cloud ecosystem. YOUR FUTURE TEAM At Scaleway, we work in a collaborative and international environment where knowledge sharing, technical excellence and diverse perspectives help us build ambitious products at scale. You will join our Secret Service team, composed of 4 people (1 manager and 3 Software Engineers), responsible for developing and operating key security services across Scaleway's cloud platform. The team works at the intersection of backend engineering, cybersecurity and cloud infrastructure, building services that protect sensitive data, manage cryptographic keys and support secure access to cloud resources. You will work in a collaborative environment with a strong focus on ownership, technical quality and continuous improvement, following Kanban practices, backlog refinement and bi-weekly retrospectives. YOUR DAILY ROUTINE • Design, develop and maintain secure backend services powering Key Manager, Secret Manager and Audit Trail. • Build and improve robust, high-performance APIs for cryptographic key management and secure access to sensitive resources. • Work with Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) and integrate cryptographic operations through PKCS#11 interfaces. • Contribute to the architecture and evolution of security-critical infrastructure, with a strong focus on reliability, availability and resilience. • Develop clean, maintainable and scalable software using Go (Golang). • Improve infrastructure automation and deployment processes using Terraform, Docker and GitLab CI/CD. • Implement and maintain observability, monitoring and logging solutions using tools such as Loki. • Work with PostgreSQL to ensure reliable and secure data management. • Contribute to security best practices and compliance requirements, particularly within the SecNumCloud framework. • Collaborate with other Engineering and Infrastructure teams to solve complex security challenges and continuously improve our services. ABOUT YOU HARD SKILLS • Solid experience in Backend Software Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering or DevOps, ideally in environments with strong security or reliability requirements. • Strong development skills in Go (Golang), or proficiency in another backend language like Python with the ability and motivation to quickly become productive in Go. • Good understanding of cryptography, key management and secure backend architectures. • Knowledge of Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) and PKCS#11 interfaces, or a strong interest in developing expertise in these areas. • Hands-on experience designing, developing and maintaining REST APIs and backend services. • Good knowledge of Linux environments, networking fundamentals and security best practices. • Experience with Docker, Terraform, Git and GitLab CI/CD. • Familiarity with PostgreSQL and observability tools such as Loki. • Understanding of cloud infrastructure, distributed systems and production environments. • Familiarity with SecNumCloud or other security and compliance frameworks is a plus. SOFT SKILLS • Ownership & Autonomy: You take responsibility for your work and drive technical topics from design to production. • Problem-solving: You enjoy investigating complex technical challenges and developing pragmatic, reliable solutions. • Security Mindset: You naturally consider security, reliability and maintainability in your engineering decisions. • Collaboration: You enjoy working in a small team, sharing knowledge and collaborating with other engineering teams. • Curiosity & Continuous Learning: You are eager to explore new technologies, particularly around cryptography, cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity. • Communication: You can clearly explain technical decisions, share ideas and contribute to constructive discussions.
