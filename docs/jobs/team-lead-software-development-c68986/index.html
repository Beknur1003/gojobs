We’re looking for a Lead, Software Development to help us build and lead a high-performance team that solves challenging engineering problems as we build the next generation of social products. You will support the Manager, Development to hire, grow, coach, and lead a team of developers to build, scale, monitor, and optimize our Identity infrastructure working on user management, Authentication and Authorization, Member status, Onboarding and provisioning and so much more user impacting domain features.This role is envisaged to be 50% technical and hands-on, 50% people leadership, process, and project management. While based in Canada (in locations where we can legally hire), you will report to the Manager, Software Development. WHAT YOU’LL DO: • Build, support and lead a high performance, cohesive team of developers, in close partnership with the Senior Manager, Development • Facilitate ongoing development conversations with your team to support their learning and career growth • Proactively share information, actively solicit feedback, and facilitate communication, within teams and portfolios • Participate in the hiring process, with an aim of attracting and hiring the best developers • Assess and prioritize the team's work, appropriately delegating to others and encouraging team ownership. • Maintain high standards of security, reliability, scalability, performance, and quality in all delivered projectsHelp shape our technical roadmap as we scale our services and build new products • Design, write, test, and deploy high quality scalable code • Work with the following technologies: Go, Scala, PHP, Docker, Jenkins, Kafka, Redis, MySQL, Domain Driven Design, Event Sourcing, Test Driven Design and Agile, React and Typescript.
