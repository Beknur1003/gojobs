Software Development Engineer II, Global Money Movement

Remitly Poland sp. z o.o. Kraków, Poland опубликовано 6 августа 2026

English B2
PostgreSQLMySQLKubernetesDockerAWSJavaKotlinAI/LLMFinTech
Every Remitly transfer, from any product, in any market, ends the same way: money has to actually move. The Global Money Movement team builds and maintains the platform that customer-facing product lines use to move money between our customers and their recipients across borders, through a broad network of external banking and payment partners. As a Software Development Engineer II on the Money Movement team in Krakow, you will build the services that orchestrate those flows: the APIs that product lines call to move money, the integrations with external partners that execute the movement, and the reporting that keeps our financial systems correct. This is real integration work with external banking and payment partners, the kind engineers rarely get to do at this level. You will work closely with stakeholders across time zones, designing and implementing effective solutions with a strong focus on customer impact. We have no dedicated QA, so you own the quality and operational health of what you ship. You will report directly to the Engineering Manager, based in Poland. You will work on a platform the whole company builds on: decisions you make affect every product line, not just one. You will work alongside experienced technical leadership, with room to learn and real ownership from day one, in a global and diverse environment with cross-timezone collaboration and travel opportunities. And you will do real integrations with external banking and payment partners, exposure that is rare in typical backend roles at this level. Our Connected Work Culture: Driving Innovation, Together At Remitly, we believe that true innovation sparks when we come together. Our Connected Work Culture fosters dynamic in-person collaboration, where ideas ignite and challenging problems find solutions faster. Under the Company's current hybrid-work expectations, employees in this position work onsite Tuesday through Thursday each week. Additional onsite attendance may be required based on business needs, and the Company may modify its hybrid-work expectations from time to time. These intentional in-office moments are vital for deepening relationships, fueling creativity, and ensuring your impact is felt where it matters most. • A BS/MS in Computer Science or equivalent professional experience • 3+ years of professional experience as a software developer • Proficiency in a general purpose language such as Java/Kotlin or Golang • Experience with data structures, algorithms, and software design patterns • Proficiency in an RDBMS like MySQL, PostgreSQL, or SQL Server • Proven experience and understanding of software development practices, system design, software design patterns, and algorithms • Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes and AWS • Comfort using AI tools across the development lifecycle • Excellent verbal and written communication skills • Fluent English on B2 level or higher
