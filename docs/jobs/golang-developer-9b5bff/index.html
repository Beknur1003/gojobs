ABOUT INVENTIFF Inventiff is a software development and digital product agency based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania. We work as a full-service tech partner for companies of different sizes and industries, helping them turn ideas into scalable digital products, from custom software, web platforms and mobile apps to APIs, integrations and cloud infrastructure. Our team covers the full delivery cycle, from discovery and design to development, testing and ongoing support. ABOUT THE ROLE We are looking for a Golang Developer to build fast, reliable backend services for our clients' products. You will design and develop APIs and distributed systems, work on performance-sensitive components, and collaborate closely with frontend, QA and DevOps colleagues to take features from design to production. experience with gRPC / Protocol Buffers is a plus • Experience with relational databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL) and SQL • Understanding of microservices architecture and distributed systems fundamentals • Experience with testing in Go (unit and integration tests) • Familiarity with Git, CI/CD pipelines and Docker • Experience working in Agile / Scrum teams • Good communication skills and English at B2 level or higher NICE TO HAVE • Experience with messaging systems such as Kafka, NATS or RabbitMQ • Experience with NoSQL / caching solutions (Redis, MongoDB) • Experience with Kubernetes and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure or GCP) • Experience with observability tools (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry) • Experience with performance profiling and optimization (pprof) • Knowledge of another backend language (Java, Python, Node.js or Rust) • Awareness of secure coding practices ﻿ WHAT WE OFFER • Fully remote work • Long-term collaboration on a variety of client projects and industries • A small, senior-minded team where your input shapes technical decisions • Direct communication with clients and stakeholders, no unnecessary layers • Room to learn and work with modern tools, including AI-assisted development
