Responsibilities We are seeking a highly experienced, forward-thinking Senior Systems / Solutions Architect to provide architectural leadership, technical direction, and innovation for the Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug System (MARx), a mission-critical system supporting the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).MARx supports complex, high-volume Medicare Advantage and Part D enrollment processing, premium calculations, plan payment operations, and information exchanges with numerous internal CMS systems and external partner organizations, including Medicare Advantage Organizations and Prescription Drug Plans.The successful candidate will be responsible for defining and evolving the system architecture to ensure scalability, reliability, performance, security, maintainability, and operational efficiency, while supporting changing Medicare business requirements, federal regulations, and legislative mandates.The architect will work closely with CMS business owners, the CMS Office of Information Technology (OIT), program management, and cross-functional technical teams spanning business analysis, software design, development, testing, infrastructure, cybersecurity, and production operations.This position requires a unique combination of strong technical architecture expertise, deep understanding of complex business processing systems, strategic thinking, and hands-on technical leadership.Beyond traditional architecture responsibilities, the individual will serve as a trusted technical advisor, thought leader, and mentor—driving innovation, promoting engineering excellence, identifying optimization opportunities, and helping shape the long-term evolution of MARx.________________________________________Key Responsibilities1.
