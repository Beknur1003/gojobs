About Stensul Stensul is the Governed Creation™ Platform for enterprise marketing teams that need to create campaigns quickly, safely, and at scale. We bring creation, collaboration, and control together in one connected platform, combining AI-enabled creation, no-code production, embedded governance, and seamless integrations across the marketing tech stack. The result: faster output, lower costs, reduced risk, and work that stays consistently on brand. At Stensul, we’re just as intentional about how we work as about what we build. We’re a people-first team that values inclusive collaboration, ownership, and continuous learning. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment and want to help define the future of marketing creation, we’d love to meet you. Why This Role Matters We're looking for a Technical Lead to own the technical direction of our email builder team: where enterprise marketers assemble campaign emails and check them against brand, legal, and rendering standards before the email ever goes out for review. Get it right here and the review cycle is short. Get it wrong and the whole campaign stalls in rework. It is Stensul's original product surface and its most heavily used, so anything that breaks here is felt by customers immediately. You would join a long-lived, cross-functional team where Engineering, Product, and Design work together on the same domain, owning it from idea through production and into long-term maintenance. Nobody hands the team a spec and nobody hands the team's output to someone else to run. You would own technical direction as an individual contributor, partnering with the Engineering Manager who owns people, capacity, and delivery. You'll navigate the tension between technical sustainability and business urgency, making architectural decisions that compound over time in a B2B platform serving large, heavily regulated, demanding organizations. If you care as much about how a team works as about what it builds, and you'd rather model good practices than mandate them, this is the kind of role where you'll thrive. What makes this problem interesting Email is one of the last genuinely hostile target environments in software. There is no single rendering standard, the clients that matter most disagree with each other, and the people producing the output are marketers, not engineers. Our job is to let someone with no technical background assemble something complex and have it come out correct every time, in every inbox. Then add the harder constraint: many of our customers are large financial institutions. For them, "on brand" is not a preference and "approved" is not a formality. A campaign that goes out with the wrong disclosure is a regulatory problem, not a design problem. So the guardrails cannot be a review step at the end. They have to be built into the creation experience itself, tight enough to guarantee compliance and flexible enough so people can still do their work. That is the tension you would spend your time on, and it is getting sharper. As AI and agents start producing more of this content, the constraint system has to hold for output no human wrote. Operational Excellence • Own system availability and reliability, taking accountability when critical situations arise and leading the team through them. • Treat quality as the team's responsibility, not a downstream function's. You will partner closely with QA Engineering on test strategy, automation, and prevention, and you will be expected to raise the team's own bar rather than rely on someone else catching things later. • Lead and participate in incident reviews, post-mortems, and continuous improvement efforts. • Proactively identify and communicate risks, blockers, and technical challenges.
