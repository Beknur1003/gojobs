Job Purpose: Lead team of software engineers within the Engineering & E-commerce function. Own the delivery, quality and production reliability of the systems within the assigned domain, such as ordering, menu serving, payments, loyalty, aggregator integration, in-store technology or customer platforms. Turn approved priorities into working, reliable software, delivered on time and at a controlled cost. This enables the company to serve customers and locations across markets and grow digital sales. Key Responsibilities: • Own the assigned domain end to end - Own the architecture, roadmap execution, quality and production reliability of the domain’s systems and APIs. The scope of the domain will be assigned in writing by the line manager. • Deliver the approved roadmap predictably - Define scope, estimate, sequence, manage risks and dependencies, and report status and trade-offs early, in line with the department’s software development lifecycle, so that commitments to the business are met. • Own the system design and architecture of the domain - Propose designs and architecture changes and make technical trade-offs related to performance, resilience, data consistency, security, cost and time to market. Decisions must remain within the architecture principles and engineering standards established by the Director of Engineering & E-commerce and the engineering leadership. • Own the reliability of the domain systems - Define and meet service-level objectives. Run on-call support, incident response and root-cause analysis. Monitor systems using the reliability tools provided by Platform Engineering and keep systems fast and efficient under peak loads. • Use AI by default and advocate its use - Use AI for software development and the automation of other tasks, in line with the department’s AI usage standards. Maintain engineering quality through automated testing, code reviews, continuous integration and deployment, secure coding and controlled releases, including feature flags, progressive rollouts and rollback plans. • Act as custodian of the domain’s spend - Track cloud consumption, licences and contractor time. Identify and remove waste and raise cost risks early with the line manager. Commercial and budget decisions remain with the line manager and must follow company processes. • Build, lead and grow a team of software engineers - Coach team members, provide regular feedback, manage performance and support career development. Contribute to hiring through the company’s recruitment process. Direct the day-to-day work of external developers, contractors or vendors when assigned to the domain. • Align with peers and stakeholders before acting - Agree on interfaces, dependencies and priorities with other Engineering Managers, Product, Operations and business teams. Escalate disagreements to the line manager with clear options. Do not make unilateral decisions that change another team’s work, cost or risk. Leadership Requirements: • Ownership: Takes full accountability for the domain, its production systems and its people. • Hands-on credibility: Designs systems and understands the code sufficiently to make sound technical decisions and coach engineers. • AI-first mindset: Uses AI by default and leads the team to do the same. • Delivery discipline: Plans realistically, commits carefully and keeps commitments. • Calm under pressure: Leads clearly during incidents and peak periods. • Decides through alignment: Works across team boundaries through agreement rather than authority. • Financial responsibility: Treats company money responsibly in every technical decision. • People development: Develops software engineers and builds a culture of engineering excellence. Major Challenges • Deliver new features while maintaining production stability. • Manage dependencies on other teams and vendors that the role does not directly control. • Reduce technical debt and modernize legacy systems while keeping the systems operational. • Maintain delivery quality across a team comprising employees and external developers working in different locations. • Migrate existing systems to the department’s standard programming language, Golang, while continuing ongoing delivery. Qualifications: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field • Cloud or architecture certification is an advantage Experience: • Five or more years of software engineering experience, including at least two years managing engineering teams • Proven experience designing and delivering production-grade, high-availability systems • Experience with consumer digital platforms within quick-service restaurants, retail, e-commerce, marketplaces or logistics is preferred • Experience working with external developers, contractor teams or vendor teams is an advantage Job-Specific Skills: • System design and architecture: Service architecture, API design, data modelling, distributed systems, modern system design and cloud principles • Programming: Golang is preferred. Java, C#, Node.js or PHP are accepted. Willingness to learn Golang and migrate systems to Golang is required • Databases and caches: MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis or Aerospike • Messaging and streaming: Kafka and RabbitMQ • Monitoring and reliability: Logs, metrics, traces, service-level objectives, alerting and incident response • Performance: Profiling, load testing, latency optimisation and cost optimisation • AI for engineering: Daily use of AI tools for development and the automation of other tasks • Engineering quality: Automated testing, code reviews, continuous integration and deployment, and secure coding. Generic Skills: • Clear communication and the ability to translate engineering details into business impact • Stakeholder management • Project management, including planning, estimation, risk management and dependency management • Coaching and performance management Competencies: Behavioral Competencies • Ownership and accountability • Delivery focus • Collaboration and influence • Developing people • Customer focus • Integrity Technical Competencies • System Design and Architecture: Advanced • Backend Engineering: Advanced • Reliability, Monitoring and Performance: Advanced • AI-Assisted Engineering: Advanced • Data Stores and Messaging: Advanced • Cloud Principles: Intermediate • Security and Data Privacy: Intermediate
