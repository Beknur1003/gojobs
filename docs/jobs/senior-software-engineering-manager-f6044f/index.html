Senior Engineering Manager Alicante, Spain · Hybrid (3 days onsite/week) · Full-time Context & Impact: Lansweeper is the AI Cyber Asset Intelligence platform helping IT and Security teams gain full visibility, reduce cyber risk, and scale automation with confidence. We transform raw asset data into a continuously validated, trusted source of truth — so teams can move faster and act with certainty. As a Senior Engineering Manager, you will lead multiple engineering teams responsible for designing, developing, and delivering high-quality software that drives Lansweeper’s IT asset management platform. You’ll work closely with Product, Architecture, and Operations leaders to set technical direction, foster innovation, and ensure our teams deliver features that delight our customers and support the company’s growth. AI Fluency At Lansweeper, we are building an AI-native engineering culture. We expect all engineers to actively use AI tools to enhance productivity and quality across the software development lifecycle, including coding, testing, documentation, and system design. Candidates should be comfortable working with AI-assisted workflows and continuously evolving their approach as tools and practices advance. Key Responsibilities • Grow and manage people so teams stay healthy, aligned and increasingly autonomous. • Support career development by providing continuous feedback, coaching, and development opportunities. • Provide strong technical judgment to guide architecture, quality, and team decisions. • Translate business goals into clear technical objectives, roadmaps, and team priorities. • Partner with Product Management and Architecture to define and execute product and platform strategies. • Drive best practices in software engineering — including system design, quality, automation, and scalability. • Monitor progress against goals, manage risks, and ensure predictable and on-time delivery. • Drive execution with clear plans, predictable delivery, and proactive risk management • Promote diversity, psychological safety, and inclusion within the engineering organization. • Contribute to the continuous improvement of engineering processes and tools. Key Requirements Hard Skills • 8+ years of experience in software engineering, including 3+ years in engineering management or technical leadership roles. • Proven success leading multiple teams in an agile, product-driven environment. • Experience with modern software architectures, cloud technologies, and DevOps practices. • Strong organizational, communication, and leadership skills. • Demonstrated ability to align engineering efforts with strategic business goals. • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience). • Strong expertise in Golang and • AI assisted development tools as part of your daily workflow Soft Skills • Experience in SaaS or enterprise software environments. • Track record of scaling engineering organizations in a fast-growing company. • Experience with Rust AI Specific Skills Lansweeper is an AI-native company. In this role, you are expected to actively use AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code, agents, subagents, and MCP-based workflows as a core part of your engineering workflow. You should be comfortable prompting, validating, and refining AI-generated code, and using AI to improve development speed while maintaining production-quality standards.
