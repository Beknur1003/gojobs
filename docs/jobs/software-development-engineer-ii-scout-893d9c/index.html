Whether it's supporting loved ones back home, growing a business across continents, or pursuing new opportunities abroad, we're not just here to move money— we're here to move our global customers forward. We're looking for builders, reimaginers, and global thinkers who want to work at the intersection of technology, trust, and transformation. About the Role: We're hiring a Software Engineer II to join our Scout team—Remitly's Agentic AI team. We build AI-powered experiences that help customers send money and complete tasks wherever they are- on Remitly's website, mobile app, or third-party platforms like WhatsApp. As a member of the Scout team, you'll help enable money transfer functionality on external platforms and contribute to a robust conversational AI platform used across the business. You'll be involved in prototyping, validating, and scaling new AI-driven solutions, working closely with cross-functional partners including UX, Legal, Compliance, and Marketing. You Will: • Gather and analyze requirements, clarifying feature requirements and technical design. • Design and write high-quality, modular, and maintainable code for APIs, data storage solutions, and integrations with third-party services. Resolve root causes of most software failures, not just symptoms. • Propose trade-offs on technical approach and surface risks early in business expectations and technical design. • Partner with product/program managers, marketing, compliance, and legal specialists to understand requirements and shape solutions. • Provide comprehensive and constructive code reviews and design feedback to peers. • Improve how software is deployed, documented, tested, logged, and monitored. • Be an onboarding resource for new team members. • Report directly to the Engineering Manager in Seattle. You Have: • A BS in Computer Science or equivalent professional experience • 5+ years of professional experience • Experience building and optimizing large-scale web architectures. • Knowledge of backend programming languages such as Java or Golang • Proven experience software development practices, system design, software design patterns, and algorithms • Proficiency in CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitLab, etc.) Compensation Details.
