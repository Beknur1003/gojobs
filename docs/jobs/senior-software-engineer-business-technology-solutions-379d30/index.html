The Business Technology Solutions (BTS) team champions and executes technology-enabled business solutions that have material top-line and bottom-line impact to Apple. We are seeking a Senior Software Engineer responsible for the development and design of web applications and components across both front-end (GUI) and backend. You will design, code, test, and implement websites and applications for internal or external use, and troubleshoot issues around performance, response times, capacity, and system limitations. You’ll collaborate with internal business partners, internal technology resources (database, system, networking), and ensure data quality, integrity, and consistency across the organization. Preferred Qualifications • Testing, CI, and DevOps — working knowledge of integrated testing frameworks (RSpec, MiniTest), CI suites (CircleCI, Jenkins), and DevOps and AWS infrastructure. • GoLang and Java — expert knowledge of GoLang, Java, or a similar compiled, high-concurrency, lower-level language (Erlang, C++, Rust, etc.). • Analytical, Problem Solving, and Communication Skills — excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, including cross-functional collaboration and relationship building.
