Why You’ll Love This Role Lumentum is looking for an Embedded Software Engineer to help shape the next generation of advanced optical communication products. In this role, you’ll join a world-class team and contribute to the design, development, and support of cutting-edge embedded software across a wide range of technologies. What You’ll Be Doing Key responsibilities: • Work with internal teams and customers to diagnose and resolve complex software issues. • Document and review requirements and designs with peers and stake holders. • Document, review and execute designer and unit test cases. • Provide support throughout the software and product verification cycle. • Participate in code inspections. • Collaborate and share technical expertise with other members of the software team. What We’re Looking For Education: • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering or a related field. Qualifications: • 5-10 years of embedded C/C++ development. • Solid understanding of multi-threaded software. • Familiarity with hardware devices such as ADCs/DACs/Clocks/UART/FPGAs. • Knowledge of interfacing to embedded devices via interfaces such as PCIe, I2C, SPI, RS-232. • Team player with strong communication and collaboration skills. • Strong problem solving, troubleshooting and debugging skills. Assets: • Python, GOLANG, Shell Scripting. • Knowledge of SONiC, Redis, Docker, OpenConfig, YANG, Protocol Buffers, gRPC, gNxI. • Linux, SYSBIOS, FreeRTOS or other embedded OS software development.
