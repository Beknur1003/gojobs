About the Team: LinkedIn's members entrust us with their information every day and we take their security seriously. Our core value of putting our members first powers all the decisions we make, including how we manage and protect the data of our members and customers. Information Security at LinkedIn is dedicated to protecting and securing business-critical member data and company assets. Our core mission is to empower LinkedIn to create a secure and thriving platform for every member of the global workforce. The Production & Enterprise Security team strives to proactively safeguard our products, applications, cloud environments, and core engineering infrastructure by identifying, assessing, and mitigating security and privacy risks. We are dedicated to protecting our members by conducting security reviews, threat modeling, establishing secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC) controls, and building scalable automation to minimize potential risks across the company. About the role: As a Senior Staff Security Engineer on the Production & Enterprise Security team, you will drive the overall security posture and technical vision across LinkedIn's core products, cloud infrastructure, AI platforms, and enterprise workflows. You will lead cross-functional architecture initiatives across threat modeling, zero-trust Identity and Access Management (IAM), cloud security guardrails, post-quantum cryptography transitions, handle security incidents, and secure-by-default developer platforms. At LinkedIn, our approach to flexible work is centered on trust and optimized for culture, connection, clarity, and the evolving needs of our business. The work location of this role is hybrid, meaning it will be performed both from home and from a LinkedIn office on select days, as determined by the business needs of the team. Responsibilities: • Own and drive the secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC) and shift-left security controls across enterprise and cloud engineering platforms, including automated SAST/DAST/IAST scanning, software supply chain security, and CI/CD security guardrails. • Architect and build secure-by-default frameworks, paved roads, zero-trust Identity & Access Management (IAM) controls, and guide engineering teams on modern authentication/authorization protocols (OAuth, OIDC, SAML, SPIFFE/SPIRE) across cloud infrastructure and microservices. • Build scalable automation and security tooling for continuous vulnerability detection, automated remediation, and secure telemetry; partner with Detection & Response and Infrastructure teams to bolster SIEM logging, threat intelligence, and incident containment playbooks. • Lead end-to-end threat modeling, architectural design reviews, and risk assessments for distributed cloud environments, enterprise infrastructure, and emerging AI/ML models and pipelines. • Define cloud and enterprise security standards, reference architectures, and cryptographic baselines—including planning and leading post-quantum cryptography (PQC) readiness and migration strategies across core systems. • Drive cross-functional architecture and strategic technical vision across platform engineering, cloud security, AI research, and DevEx to build developer platforms, AI systems, and cloud infrastructure that are secure by design. • Lead end-to-end security incident response lifecycle, managing critical events affecting core products and platform services across global regions through full remediation. Basic Qualifications • BA/BS Degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Information Security, or related technical field, or equivalent technical experience. • Hands-on experience or working knowledge of web/application security, cloud/network security risks, or secure development lifecycle practices and/or cryptography. • 14+ years experience in security engineering with deep expertise in application security, cloud security (AWS, GCP, Azure), identity and access management (IAM), and modern cryptographic primitives/protocols. • Demonstrated track record of leading complex threat modeling, security architectural reviews, and vulnerability assessments and remediations for large-scale distributed systems. • Experience with programming languages such as Java, GoLang or Python. • Experience architecting automated security tooling, security guardrails, and enterprise security platforms. Preferred Qualifications • BS and 10+ years of relevant work experience, MS and 8+ years of relevant work experience, or PhD and 5+ years of relevant work experience. • 5+ years experience with programming languages such as Java, GoLang or Python. • Experience securing cloud-native platforms, hybrid/sovereign cloud environments, containerized workloads (Kubernetes), and modern CI/CD software pipelines. • Deep knowledge of zero-trust architecture, identity federation (OAuth/OIDC/SAML), PKI, key management, and post-quantum cryptography implementation strategies. • Demonstrated ability to ramp up quickly on emerging threat vectors and technology stacks. • In-depth expertise in assessing, architecting, and safeguarding AI/ML platforms, LLM security, model safety, and data governance controls. • Proven track record of challenging existing paradigms and championing continuous enhancement through strategic innovation and novel approaches. Suggested Skills: • Technical Leadership in Product Security • Security Assessment Methodologies • Security Architecture • Identity & Access Management (IAM) & Zero Trust Architecture • AI/ML Security & Model Governance • Post-Quantum Cryptography & Applied Cryptography • Cloud Infrastructure Security & Guardrails • • Ability to work across teams and communicate concisely and clearly to stakeholders. • Experience with security research, bug bounty or CTF competitions.
