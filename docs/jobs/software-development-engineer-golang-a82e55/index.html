Software Development Engineer - Golang

Zealant Consulting Group Bengaluru, Karnataka, India опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

Go — основной язык
AWSGCPPythonTypeScript
Why This Role Exists We ship fast. Our platform takes brands from brief to full research report in minutes — and the engineering behind that has to be tight, reliable, and constantly improving. We work with some of India's biggest consumer companies and fastest-moving D2C brands — Coca-Cola, Britannia, Foxtale — and partner with some of the largest agencies like WPP. We're hiring a Software Engineer who can pick up a ticket and close it. Not someone who needs hand-holding through scope or technology choices — someone who reads the requirement, figures out the right approach, and ships something they're proud of. What You'll Actually Do • Own tickets end-to-end: understand the problem, make the call, ship the feature, and handle the edge cases. • Build across the stack — frontend, backend, or both depending on where you're strongest. • Work with TypeScript, ReactJS/NextJS on the frontend and Python/Go on the backend. • Design and work with SQL and NoSQL databases as part of feature development. • Collaborate with product and design to implement experiences that actually feel good to use. • Debug, optimize, and improve systems that are already in production. • Learn fast — the stack will evolve and you're expected to evolve with it. Who We Think This Is For Experience: 3+ years of engineering experience. Startup experience is preferred — not because corporates are bad, but because you already know what it means to ship without a safety net. You Probably • Have closed real features in production, not just academic or tutorial projects. • Specialize in either frontend or backend but can move across the stack when needed. • Are comfortable in Python or Go on the backend, and TypeScript with ReactJS/NextJS on the frontend. • Know your way around cloud environments — AWS or GCP. • Understand databases well enough to make sensible design decisions, SQL and NoSQL. • Have at least two projects you'd genuinely put in front of someone and say: "I built this, here's why it's good." • Learn new tools by using them, not by waiting until you feel ready. This Role Is Probably Not For You If • You need a detailed spec before you can start. • Your best work lives in a private repo no one has seen. • You're looking for a large team with defined lanes — here, you own what you pick up. Skills: python,nosql,typescript,nextjs,go (golang)
