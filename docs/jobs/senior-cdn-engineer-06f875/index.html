Senior CDN Engineer

Sky CP Ltd Prague опубликовано 7 сентября 2026

Senior Полная занятость
TerraformAWSCI/CDPythonJava
Join us and build a streaming platform used by millions. At Sky Czech Republic, we’re building the tech backbone behind some of the world’s biggest streaming services. Ever heard of Peacock in the US or Sky Showtime across Europe, including the Czech Republic? They all run on our global streaming platform — a shared technological core where each service plugs in its own content and brand. Our platform serves millions of users worldwide — Peacock alone reaches over 40 million viewers in the US. Thousands of engineers across the globe help shape it, and our Prague tech hub plays a key role. We don’t just keep things running; we actively push the boundaries of streaming technology together with teams in Lisbon, London, and New York. In Prague, we work across a wide range of engineering domains: frontend development (mobile, TV, and web), backend (Java), platform engineering, content delivery networks, video playback (Golang), and data engineering (Python). Whatever your focus, you’ll work on technology used at truly global scale. What role will you play? As our Senior CDN Engineer, You’ll join the Content Delivery Engineering team within NBCUniversal’s Global Video Engineering organization. Your mission is to help deliver outstanding VOD, live event, and linear streaming experiences to millions of viewers across our US, UK, EU, and Africa services—including Peacock, SkyShowtime and NOW/WOW. You’ll collaborate daily with colleagues and vendors across the US, UK, and beyond - excellent written and spoken English is essential. Our team specializes in large-scale Multi-Content Delivery Network design and operations, performance telemetry and analytics, selection & interfacing with content origins via multiple workflows, dynamic ad insertion, manifest manipulation and integrating new player technologies. What You'll Do: • Design new and optimize existing content delivery designs to boost performance, improve resilience and expand features for our products and services. • Focus on using log analysis and observability systems to troubleshoot, diagnose issues and drive in-depth analysis for rapid resolutions. • Ensure diligent peer review of changes, contributing to our goal of improving reliability and reducing incidents. • Bring your rigor and insight to the development of our FT and NFT test methods and procedures. • Create and maintain operations runbooks and checklists for our production delivery. • Participate in on-call rotation to resolve operational issues and performance degradation. • Attain a comprehensive, technical understanding of how all NBCUniversal's streaming products and associated technologies operate. What skills do you need to play your role well? • Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, or a relevant field, equivalent professional experience will also be considered. • A background in CDN or web server/proxy configuration management is desirable but not required, as full CDN training will be provided to the right candidate. • Sound understanding and use of TCP/IP, IPv4/v6, DNS, HTTP, TLS and load balancing in production. • Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools, such as Terraform or AWS CloudFormation, including module design and peer review of infrastructure changes. • Hands-on experience with cloud-based networking, firewall, security and data products is a plus. • Skilled with using software repositories, CI/CD pipelines and writing test automation, with the ability to define and champion engineering best practices within the team. • Familiarity with large-scale, distributed performance testing, including Functional/Non-Functional Testing (NFT), is a plus. • Scripting and programming skills (e.g., Python, Bash) applied to operational automation, tooling, and reducing toil. • Well versed, working with SRE teams & technologies to assist safe, consistent technology operations. • Ability to work effectively in a distributed, multi-cultural team, collaborating across time zones. How do you land the role? We like to keep our recruitment process simple, transparent, and respectful: • First touch: An open chat with one of our recruiters about your experience, goals, and motivation. • First interview: A conversation with your future manager or teammates about the role and team. • Technical interview: A chance to demonstrate your skills on real-world problems, no trick questions. • Culture check: discussion with the manager’s manager. What can you expect in return? • Global Impact: Work in an international environment on cutting-edge technology that scales globally. • People-First Culture: We care about our people just as much as we care about the stability of our platform. • Performance Bonuses: Earn an annual bonus based on your performance. • Hybrid Work: Enjoy the best of both worlds with a mix of office and home working. • Work-Life Balance: Flexible working hours to help you balance work and life. • 25 days of holidays. • 5 days of on-demand leave (sick days). • 2 days of paid community volunteering leave. • 1 day of paid leave for moving house. • Wellbeing Allowance: 18,000 CZK per year to invest in your personal wellbeing. • Fitness Perks: Get a fully covered Multisport card or a 950 CZK monthly contribution to a Benefit Card. • Meal Allowance: 225 CZK per day to keep you fuelled. • Premium Life Insurance: Enjoy peace of mind with our premium life insurance scheme. • Fun Perks: Free tickets to Universal Theme Parks.
