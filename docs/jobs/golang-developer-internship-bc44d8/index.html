Overview Faveo Helpdesk is a leading open-source, customizable ticketing system designed to meet the needs of startups, SMEs, and enterprises. As we continue to scale and modernize our product suite, we're offering internship opportunities to passionate and motivated individuals who want to kickstart their careers in backend development using Golang. Role Summary: We’re looking for enthusiastic Golang Developer Interns to support our engineering team in developing backend services and APIs for the Faveo Helpdesk platform. As an intern, you'll gain hands-on experience with real-world projects, work closely with experienced developers, and learn how to build scalable and maintainable backend systems. Key Responsibilities: • Assist in building and maintaining backend components using Golang • Learn to integrate third-party APIs into backend systems • Write clean, testable, and well-documented code under guidance • Understand and follow secure coding practices • Participate in code reviews and team discussions • Help document features and technical processes • Support the team in debugging and fixing issues as they arise Requirements Skills & Requirements: • Basic understanding of Golang (academic or self-learned projects) • Familiarity with concepts like REST APIs, version control (Git), and backend development • Eagerness to learn and apply new technologies • Strong logical thinking and problem-solving skills • Ability to work in a team and take feedback constructively • Good communication and willingness to ask questions Preferred (Not Mandatory): • Exposure to Linux/macOS/Windows development environments • Basic understanding of GitHub, CI/CD pipelines, or open-source contributions • Familiarity with PHP (especially Laravel) is a bonus Why Join Faveo Helpdesk? • Work on a widely adopted and growing open-source product • Be part of a collaborative and innovation-driven environment • Flexible working hours and remote work options • Opportunities to contribute to meaningful product decisions • Learning and development opportunities
