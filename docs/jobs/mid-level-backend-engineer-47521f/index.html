Backend Engineer (Mid-Level) Who is Parser? Technology alone does not create impact—the right teams do. Founded in 2018, Parser is a boutique technology services and consulting firm helping global organisations solve complex business challenges through digital transformation, product development and AI enablement. We are a fast-growing team of 340+ engineers and consultants across Europe (UK, Spain, Portugal), the Americas (US, Argentina, Uruguay, Colombia), and the Middle East. We combine global reach with a mindset focused on agility, senior expertise, and close collaboration. We work as an extension of our clients’ teams, helping them define the right problems, shape solutions, and deliver technology-driven outcomes that create measurable business value. Our expertise spans software engineering, AI & data, product development, and customer experience, delivered by teams that combine strong technical depth with a consulting mindset. Why Join Us? If you are looking for a place where you can think beyond execution, take true ownership of outcomes, influence decisions, and continuously learn alongside top-tier specialists in a truly global environment, we’d love to meet you. How will you impact? As a Mid-Level Backend Engineer, you will directly drive the backend architecture for a cutting-edge conversational property search experience where LLMs orchestrate the user journey. By building scalable microservices, optimising data integrations, and enabling real-time streaming, you will turn complex AI capabilities into seamless, highly reliable consumer interactions. Your key responsibilities: Your responsibilities include, but are not limited to: • Develop, maintain, and enhance backend microservices and APIs (REST/gRPC) primarily using TypeScript (Node.js) and Go. • Implement and optimize event-driven, asynchronous workflows using messaging platforms such as Kafka, SQS, or SNS. • Manage and tune relational, search, and caching data stores including PostgreSQL/MySQL, OpenSearch, and Redis for maximum performance. • Deploy, maintain, and monitor containerised applications across AWS and Kubernetes environments. • Diagnose, debug, and resolve complex backend production issues to ensure system stability and high availability. • Drive code quality standards across the team through automated unit/integration testing, peer code reviews, and technical documentation. • Partner with product managers, designers, and cross-functional engineers to translate user needs into practical, high-impact backend solutions. What you’ll bring to the role: Essential Requirements: • 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a strong focus on backend microservices and API design (REST/gRPC). • Hands-on production experience with both TypeScript (Node.js) and Go (Golang), including developing, maintaining and scaling backend services. • Solid experience working with relational databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL), search engines (OpenSearch/Elasticsearch), and in-memory caches (Redis). • Practical experience with AWS cloud services and asynchronous/event-driven architectures (Kafka, SQS or SNS). • Demonstrated commitment to software quality through unit/integration testing, debugging and production support. Desirable Requirements: • Hands-on experience or exposure to production LLM-powered applications (e.g., function/tool calling, context management, prompt caching). Location & Work Model: Serbia: Fully Remote (Contract) Candidates must be based in Serbia and able to work UAE hours (GST). You will receive: • The chance to join an organization with triple-digit growth that is changing the paradigm on how software products are built. • The opportunity to be part of an amazing, multicultural community of tech experts. • Direct hands-on exposure to modern, production-grade LLM applications and AI orchestration patterns.
