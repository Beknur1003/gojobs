Position Summary: We are looking for an experienced Go (Golang) Developer to join our engineering team and contribute to the design, development, and maintenance of scalable, high-performance backend systems. The ideal candidate has 4–6 years of overall software engineering experience, with substantial hands-on experience building production-grade services in Go. This role requires someone who can work independently, make sound architectural decisions, and collaborate closely with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, well-tested software. You will play a key role in building and scaling backend services that power our core Key Responsibilities : • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable microservices and APIs using Go. • Write clean, efficient, well-documented, and maintainable code following best practices and coding standards. • Participate in architectural discussions and contribute to key technical decisions around system design, scalability, and reliability. • Build and optimize RESTful/gRPC APIs, ensuring high performance and low latency under production load. • Work with relational and/or NoSQL databases (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis) — schema design, query optimization, and data modeling. • Integrate services with message queues/event streaming systems (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ, NATS) where applicable. • Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and help improve build, test and deploy automation Requirements: • 4–6 years of overall software development experience, with at least 2–4 years of hands-on Go development in production environments. • Strong understanding of Go fundamentals: goroutines, channels, interfaces, error handling, and Go's memory model/concurrency patterns. • Solid experience building and consuming RESTful APIs; familiarity with gRPC is a strong plus. • Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL) and writing efficient SQL queries; exposure to NoSQL databases is a plus. • Practical experience with Docker and container orchestration (Kubernetes) in production settings.
