ABOUT US Live Payments is one of Australia's leading payment service providers, servicing thousands of businesses across Australia. We continue to innovate our payment solutions and expand our product offering alongside our industry leading strategic partners including Qantas Loyalty and Prospa. ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES Live Payments is seeking a talented and experienced Full-stack Developer to join our growing/ multi-national team. Reporting directly to our Vietnam based Team Leader, the Full-stack Developer will work on developing and maintain our existing and future products. Core duties include: Frontend Development • Develop and implement user interfaces using React.js, Next.js, and modern front-end technologies • Build responsive, accessible UIs with CSS and Material-UI • Troubleshoot and resolve end-user issues and optimise automated processes Backend Development • Design, develop, and maintain robust backend APIs using Node.js (NestJS)/Golang • Build and maintain high-performance, high-throughput services with strict reliability requirements • Design and maintain scalable microservices capable of handling high transaction volumes • Ensure idempotency, consistency, and fault tolerance across processing flows • Implement robust error handling, retry strategies, and reconciliation processes AI-Augmented Development & Collaboration • Use agentic AI tools (e.g. Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot) daily to accelerate development, automate code reviews, and improve code quality • Design effective prompts, workflows, and context strategies to maximise output from AI coding agents • Identify and implement opportunities to integrate AI agents into development workflows, CI/CD pipelines, and operational processes • Collaborate with stakeholders to clarify project requirements and recommend improvements • Conduct code reviews with a strong focus on performance, security, and reliability • Prepare and maintain comprehensive technical documentation, including SRS and design documents • Work closely with DevOps teams to improve observability, monitoring, and alerting Required skills: • At least 5 years of proven experience in full-stack development. Advanced proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript (ES6+), Node.js, React.js, Next.js, NestJS, or Golang. • Strong expertise designing and integrating RESTful APIs and socket protocols, with sound backend architecture and applied security practices (authentication, authorisation, input validation, secrets handling). • Solid experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server) and transactional data modelling, plus working experience with NoSQL databases such as MongoDB or DynamoDB. • Practical understanding of timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, and idempotency; deep understanding of concurrency, asynchronous programming, and multithreading. • Hands-on experience building and running services on AWS (Lambda and similar), GCP, or Azure, provisioned through Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, or equivalent). • Proven hands-on use of agentic AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor Agent, GitHub Copilot, or equivalent), not limited to chat-based AI use; able to design prompts, workflows, and context strategies that get reliable output from coding agents. • Excellent communication with a strong command of English; demonstrated problem-solving, self-motivation, and effectiveness both independently and in a team. Desirable skills: • Experience with payment gateways, fintech, or financial systems; knowledge of AS2805, ISO 8583, ISO 20022, or equivalent message formats; knowledge of PCI DSS or payment security standards. Not required; we will train the right engineer on the domain. • Strong understanding of Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) and microservices. • Handling peak traffic, failover, and disaster recovery; rate limiting, throttling, and traffic shaping; performance profiling and optimisation.
