New Graduates Welcome YAGEO Group is looking for a motivated Junior Software Engineer to join our global development team. New graduates and early-career engineers are strongly encouraged to apply. We are looking for someone who has a solid foundation in software development, enjoys learning new technologies, and is excited to grow as a full-stack software engineer. You do not need to be familiar with all of the technologies we use—we value your fundamentals, curiosity, and ability to learn. Work of Significance YAGEO has distinguished itself by building a growing portfolio of digital products and engineering tools for customers around the world, including leading global companies. Our team is responsible for developing and supporting the YAGEO Group corporate website, yageogroup.com/, which provides customers with online tools to search and compare electronic components, generate customized specifications, and access quotation and Bill of Materials (BOM) information. We also develop and maintain customer-facing engineering applications such as K-SIM, ksim3.kemet.com/, which provides electrical characteristic curves and simulation data for electronic components. As a member of our team, you will have opportunities to work across the full software stack, including frontend and backend applications, APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure, and deployment. You will work within a global team and report directly to a US-based bilingual manager who communicates in both English and Mandarin. YAGEO offers a collaborative, energetic, and casual work environment. We enjoy exploring new technologies and continuously integrating modern software tools and frameworks into our development workflow. Primary Responsibilities • Develop new software applications and features based on business and customer needs. • Maintain and improve existing applications used by customers and internal teams. • Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code. • Develop and integrate APIs and backend services. • Help improve application deployment and development workflows. • Collaborate with other developers through code reviews, technical discussions, and pair programming. • Learn new technologies and gradually take ownership of features and projects. What We Are Looking For We especially welcome new graduates and junior engineers who are interested in building their software engineering careers. You do not need to meet every requirement below. We are looking for candidates who have strong fundamentals and are willing to learn. • Basic understanding of software development processes, algorithms, and data structures. • Familiarity with object-oriented and/or functional programming concepts. • A willingness and ability to learn new programming languages and technologies. • Interest in full-stack software development, including frontend, backend, APIs, databases, infrastructure, security, gateways, and microservices. • Basic English communication and reading/writing skills. • Ability to understand existing code, investigate problems, and learn how to improve or extend existing systems. • Strong attention to detail. • Good communication and teamwork skills. • A positive, proactive, and self-motivated attitude. • Curiosity and willingness to take on new challenges. Experience No professional software development experience is required. New graduates, recent graduates, and junior software engineers are encouraged to apply. Academic projects, personal projects, internships, open-source contributions, or other hands-on programming experience are all valuable. Languages and Technologies Experience with our specific technology stack is not required. We care more about your programming fundamentals, learning ability, and interest in software engineering. Our development environment includes: • Rust • Golang • Docker • Kubernetes • SQL / NoSQL Databases • REST Services / GraphQL • Linux • Microservices You will have opportunities to learn and work with these technologies as part of the team.
