At Chemster we are building and orchestrating an open inclusive platform aimed at undertaking a full digital transformation of the personal care industry. Chemster solves a complex problem by bringing disruptive changes to most of the outdated, analog, nontransparent and time-consuming processes with the ultimate target to reduce time to market. Our platform is a first in this industry and will revolutionize how this industry works worldwide by providing a delightful user experience, regardless of entry point. Chemster has a strict no-jerk policy and we believe in teamwork at all levels. We are currently looking for seasoned engineers that are highly collaborative and driven to contribute to our engineering department. Our primary focus is on how we can deliver the most value to our customers while building the coolest things possible. Engineering at Chemster consists of ReactJS, Salesforce, Microservices (Go, Python & NodeJS), AWS, GitLab all in an agile workflow. What You’ll Be Doing * 7+ years professionally as a software engineer * 5+ years delivering service-oriented architecture solutions (SOA, Microservices) using RESTful APIs or GraphQL * Profound experience in at least two languages (Python, goLang, Javascript, etc) * Participate in and support pair programming, code reviews, automated testing and internal training * Contribute to architecture and design discussions dedicated to the usability of our platform * Professionally experienced with test driven web development and continuous delivery. * Be an owner and a champion for engineering quality, performance and integrity * Excellent communication skills in English * You must be able to live and work in the EU, we are not currently proving immigration support. Bonus Points * Experience with Salesforce ecosystem * Production experience with ReactJS * Production experience with OAuth, Serverless, PostgreSQL * Highly motivated to deliver innovative products * Any level of German
