Senior Software Engineer

GetGo Technologies Pte Ltd Singapore, Singapore опубликовано 3 ноября 2026

Гибрид Senior
KubernetesDockerAWSGCPMicroservicesCI/CD
At GetGo, our vision is to build a world where everyone can move around freely and sustainably. As we continue to expand our geographic reach and champion sustainability, we are looking for those ready to join us on a ride in creating a meaningful future of mobility. The Senior Software Engineer is a critical role responsible for taking ownership of critical backend services that power GetGo's carsharing platform — driving reliability, performance, and engineering excellence across a modern microservices stack on AWS/GCP. Responsibilities • Technical Expertise: Strong proficiency in backend technologies (e.g., Golang, C#) and hands-on experience with microservices architecture and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP). • Problem-Solving: Proven ability to analyze complex technical challenges and implement practical, systemic solutions. • Effective Communication: Ability to articulate complex technical ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. • Operational Excellence: Solid understanding of Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines to ensure seamless feature deployment. • Domain Knowledge: Intermediate to advanced knowledge of software design patterns and agile development methodologies. • Proven Track Record: 3–5 years of professional software engineering experience, with a portfolio of shipped features or services running in production at meaningful scale. • You care about craft and culture — writing clean, maintainable code, supporting your teammates, and continuously pushing the quality bar higher — in line with our GetGo Values: • Driven by Purpose • Stay Curious and Humble • Collaborate with Empathy • Make it Better • Get It Going! Requirements • Own the overall health, stability, and high availability of assigned services with minimal guidance to meet defined quality standards. • Monitor service metrics and identify bottlenecks, implementing optimizations to improve efficiency and scalability. • Regularly identify and propose solutions for improvements within your area of work to enhance system performance and developer experience. • Collaborate with Tech Leads and the SRE team to review changes, ensuring alignment with technical specifications and proactive issue detection. • Troubleshoot complex technical issues and conduct root cause analysis to implement preventive measures and avoid recurrence. • Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers, participating in code reviews to maintain high standards of code quality. • Document service operations and troubleshooting procedures to ensure knowledge sharing across the engineering team.
