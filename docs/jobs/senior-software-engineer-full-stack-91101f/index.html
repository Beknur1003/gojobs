We're looking for a Senior Full-Stack Engineer to help build our marketing and customer engagement platform. In this role, you'll design and develop data-driven, customer-facing features and backend services that power real-time personalization, campaign delivery, and transaction support. Our stack is Ruby on Rails on the backend and Vue.js on the frontend — and we value engineers who lead with strong product instincts, deep technical judgment, and a pragmatic mindset. This is a great opportunity for an experienced engineer who enjoys owning features end-to-end, collaborating closely with cross-functional partners, and mentoring less-experienced teammates, with room to grow into broader technical leadership. We believe the best engineers today are fluent in AI — comfortable using AI-assisted development tools and thoughtful about how AI capabilities can be woven into product features and platform experiences. This is a hybrid role based out of our Broomfield, Colorado office. Responsibilities • Full-Stack Development: Design and build robust, secure, and scalable systems across our Ruby on Rails and Vue.js stack, with ownership from API design and data modeling through to rich front-end experiences. • Technical Contribution: Contribute to architecture and design discussions for key platform initiatives, and help establish and follow sound engineering patterns. • Mentorship: Support the growth of junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, design feedback, and pairing. • Performance & Quality: Identify and drive improvements to backend and frontend performance, and help raise the bar for code quality, testing, security, and observability. • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Product, Design, and Customer Success to translate business problems into sound technical solutions that deliver measurable outcomes. • AI-Augmented Development: Leverage AI-assisted tooling to accelerate delivery, and contribute ideas on where AI capabilities can add genuine value to the product. • Agile Delivery: Work effectively in an iterative, fast-paced environment — shipping frequently and adapting quickly. • Incident Response: Participate in resolving production incidents and contribute to post-incident reviews that prevent recurrence. Requirements • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with meaningful full-stack scope. • Solid experience with Ruby on Rails, including ActiveRecord, background job processing, API design, and service-oriented patterns. • Strong Javascript proficiency, including SPA architecture, component design, state management, and front-end performance. • Track record of owning and delivering complex features or projects end-to-end. • Strong RESTful API design experience, including versioning, authentication, and third-party integrations. • Solid database fundamentals, particularly MySQL or similar relational databases, including schema design and query optimization. • Ability to participate in technical discussions and contribute to architectural decisions. • Experience mentoring junior or mid-level engineers and contributing to a culture of technical excellence. • Familiarity with marketing or customer engagement platforms — campaign management, personalization engines, A/B testing, or equivalent — is a plus. • Good communication skills: able to explain technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences. • Experience with agile development methodologies and working within cross-functional product teams. • Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code) and interest in evaluating where AI/ML capabilities can be integrated into product features. Bonus Points For • Experience with Go (Golang), Vue.js, and/or Node.js. • Experience integrating LLM APIs (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI) or building AI-powered product features. • Familiarity with AI/ML concepts such as embeddings, vector search, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), or fine-tuning. • AWS experience, including deploying and scaling cloud-based applications. • Hands-on experience with OpenSearch for search or analytics. • Use of Redis for caching, session management, or background jobs. • Exposure to Kafka or similar messaging systems in high-throughput environments. • Familiarity with Docker for local development or production deployment. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows. • Exposure to GDPR, CCPA, or similar data privacy regulations. • Background in B2B SaaS products or high-volume transaction systems.
