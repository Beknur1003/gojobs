Tencent Singapore, Singapore опубликовано 18 ноября 2026

Senior
LinuxPythonJava
What The Role Entails • Oversee the operation and maintenance of Tencent's overseas big data platforms to ensure platform and cluster stability. • Assist users in resolving issues related to big data platform usage, including but not limited to platform operations, component errors, and performance optimisation. • Optimise existing service workflows, standards, and pain points, and propose actionable solutions. • Develop front-end/back-end operational efficiency tools, including but not limited to: capacity management platforms, DevOps frameworks, coverage analytics, automation systems, and health monitoring. • Optimise big data cluster costs by implementing new technologies or architectures to improve processing efficiency and reduce operational expenses. Who We Look For • Proficient in ≥2 of these big data technologies: HDFS, YARN, Flink, Spark, Presto, Hive, HBase, StarRocks. • Skilled in ≥1 programming language (Java/Python/Golang, etc.). • Expertise in Linux/Unix OS, system performance tuning, and failure resolution. • ≥3 years of big data operation/development experience, with strong troubleshooting skills, technical sensitivity, and risk identification capabilities. • Knowledge of mainstream big data platforms (e.g., Databricks, Snowflake) will be considered a strong plus.
