Job Description: In the HPE Hybrid Cloud, we lead the innovation agenda and technology roadmap for all of HPE. This includes managing the design, development, and product portfolio of our next-generation cloud platform, Green Lake. Working with customers, we help them reimagine their information technology needs to deliver a simple, consumable solution that helps them drive their business results. Join us redefine what’s next for you. What you’ll do: We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Software Engineer with experience in software development, test automation, Linux-based platforms, and cloud-native technologies. The role involves designing, developing, testing, debugging, and automating solutions across the software development lifecycle. The ideal candidate will have strong programming skills, a quality-first mindset, and hands-on experience leveraging AI-assisted tools throughout the SDLC to improve engineering productivity and software quality. Experience with OpenStack, Kubernetes, OpenShift, storage technologies, and CI/CD practices is desirable. Responsibilities: • Design, develop, maintain, and enhance software components and automation solutions. • Develop automated tests covering unit, integration, system, regression, and performance validation. • Analyze requirements, participate in design discussions, code reviews, and technical problem-solving. • Debug and resolve software defects across development, test, and customer environments. • Build tools, utilities, and test frameworks to improve quality and engineering efficiency. • Collaborate with developers, architects, quality engineers, and support teams to deliver high-quality solutions. • Contribute to CI/CD, automation, and continuous improvement initiatives. • Create and maintain technical documentation, test plans, and deployment guides. What you need to bring: Education and Experience Required: • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field. • 5+ years of experience in software development, test automation, validation, or platform engineering. • Experience working in Agile development environments. Desired Candidate Profile • Strong developer with a quality and automation mindset. • Comfortable working across both development and validation activities. • Demonstrated adoption of AI-assisted engineering practices. • Self-driven, collaborative, and focused on continuous improvement. Knowledge and Skills: • Strong software development and debugging skills. • Proficiency in Python and/or Golang, with solid Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) fundamentals. • Experience with test automation and validation methodologies. • Strong Linux administration and troubleshooting skills. • Understanding of distributed systems, REST APIs, containers, and cloud-native architectures. • Familiarity with Git-based development workflows. • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills. AI in SDLC • Demonstrated experience using AI-assisted engineering tools (e.g., GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, or equivalent) as part of daily software development activities. • Ability to leverage AI tools for: • Requirements analysis and solution design • Code development and review • Test case creation and automation • Debugging and root cause analysis • Documentation generation and maintenance • Productivity improvement and knowledge management • Proven mindset of incorporating AI to accelerate delivery while maintaining software quality and engineering standards. Preferred Skills • CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD, or Jenkins. • OpenStack (Nova, Cinder, Neutron, Manila, Swift) and/or RHOSP. • Kubernetes and OpenShift. • Ansible or other automation frameworks. • Storage technologies and distributed storage systems. • Performance, scalability, and resiliency testing. • Open-source software development experience.
