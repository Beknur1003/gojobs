Why Sony Interactive Entertainment? Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity. Senior Software Engineer Financial Services At the heart of the PlayStation Network is the Commerce Platform, servicing 30+ million customers daily. The platform delivers services including product browsing, purchasing, subscription fulfillment, and payment options. About the Role: We are seeking a Senior level Engineer to help drive the ongoing evolution of PlayStation’s Global Financial Systems, with a strong focus on building event-driven pipelines and intuitive frontends. While your initial focus will be delivering scalable solutions for PlayStation’s finance systems, anticipate growth potential to expand into other areas across the business, driving efficiency and innovation. Responsibilities: • Lead & Innovate: Architect and develop robust, event-driven microservices in Go, designed to manage high-volume, real-time financial transactions and data flows. • Modernize & Refine: Translate legacy ETL and reporting systems into the modern tech stack, moving from NodeJS, Informatica to primarily React and Go • Collaborate & Impact: Work multi-functionally with teams across engineering, product management, finance, and compliance to ensure solutions are resilient, compliant, and strategically aligned. • Leverage AI/ML: Pioneer the integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning to automate manual finance tasks such as reconciliations, reporting, forecasting, and financial journal creation-enhancing accuracy, efficiency, and innovation. • Real-Time Excellence: Design, deploy, and optimize streaming technologies like Kafka to deliver real-time financial insights and ensure rapid response capabilities. • Strategic Integration: Advocate and facilitate seamless integration of microservices across diverse financial applications and services, driving scalability, reliability, and future-proof solutions. Skills and Qualifications: • Education: Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field – or equivalent professional experience • 6+ years of experience in large-scale software engineering, specializing in event-driven architectures and distributed systems. • Expertise in Golang or similar languages, building highly scalable, real-time microservices as well as REST APIs • Proficiency in designing, building and maintaining single-page applications using React or similar frameworks • Capability to automate financial processes such as payment settlements and reconciliation, revenue recognition, and royalties, with consideration of how to extend similar solutions to non-financial systems. • Proficiency in SQL and data modeling with a wide variety of databases. Nice to have experience with additional reporting databases such as Oracle and Snowflake. • Experience working with cloud platforms, including managing hybrid cloud/on-prem environments in AWS. • Proficiency with the following tools (or similar): Issue Tracking (e.g. JIRA, Service Now), Collaboration (e.g. Confluence, Trello), Communication (e.g. Slack, Webex, Skype for Business), Version Control (e.g. Github), Automation (e.g. Jenkins, Ansible, CloudFormation, Terraform) • Extensive experience with Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD). You should be proficient in automating build, test, and deployment processes using modern CI/CD tools and platforms (e.g. Github Actions, Jenkins, etc). Why SIE? This is your opportunity to be at the forefront of designing and implementing pioneering technology that powers a global entertainment leader. You’ll work on exciting, impactful systems that touch millions of users, while also having the flexibility to grow your skills and contribute across various business areas.
