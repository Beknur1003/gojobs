About the Role Our client is looking for an experienced Senior Full Stack Software Engineer to join their growing product engineering team. This is an opportunity to work in a small, agile engineering environment where you will take ownership of end-to-end product development, build scalable applications and contribute to key technical decisions. You will work closely with cross-functional teams, including product, design and engineering colleagues based in Singapore. The team operates in a fast-paced environment, with regular production releases and a strong focus on engineering quality, innovation and continuous improvement. We are looking for someone who enjoys solving complex technical challenges, takes ownership of their work and can operate independently in a fully remote environment. Key Responsibilities • Take end-to-end ownership of software development projects, from technical design and development through to deployment and production support. • Design, develop and maintain scalable, secure and high-performance full-stack applications. • Build and maintain backend services, REST APIs, database structures and front-end applications. • Design distributed systems with a focus on scalability, reliability, fault tolerance and performance. • Develop clean, maintainable and well-tested code while following engineering best practices. • Work with AI-powered development tools, coding assistants and AI agents to improve development efficiency, code quality and testing. • Collaborate with product managers, designers and engineers to translate business requirements into effective technical solutions. • Identify opportunities to improve application performance, system reliability and overall engineering standards. • Participate in technical discussions, architecture decisions and code reviews. • Troubleshoot production issues and implement improvements to prevent recurring problems. • Manage CI/CD pipelines, automated testing and deployment processes. • Monitor applications in production and participate in a light on-call rotation when required. • Maintain technical documentation and communicate effectively with distributed teams. Key Requirements • 5–7 years of professional experience in software engineering, with a strong focus on full-stack development. • Strong hands-on experience with Go (Golang). • Solid experience with React and React Native for front-end and mobile application development. • Previous experience with .NET-based technologies would be an advantage. • Strong understanding of distributed systems, microservices architecture, event-driven architecture, scalability and fault tolerance. • Hands-on experience building, deploying and managing applications on AWS. • Experience with containerisation and orchestration technologies, particularly Docker and Kubernetes. • Good understanding of relational databases, particularly MySQL, including database design, data modelling, ACID principles and performance optimisation. • Experience designing and developing secure, scalable and low-latency REST APIs. • Familiarity with Git, CI/CD pipelines, automated testing and modern software development practices. • Experience working in small, agile engineering teams with the ability to manage projects independently and take ownership of deliverables. • Comfortable working in a remote environment with strong written and verbal communication skills. • Able to collaborate with teams operating in the Singapore time zone.
