Who we are looking for We're looking for an Engineering Manager to lead two teams in our Investor/Owner segment: Owner Growth and Owner Actions. Together, these teams shape how property owners engage with AppFolio, from how they discover and adopt the experience to the actions they take to manage and improve the performance of their real estate. You'll own the success of both teams: their people, their delivery, and their outcomes. You'll also share ownership of strategy and delivery with your Product partners. The right candidate is a technically credible leader who builds high-trust, high-performing teams, keeps a sharp eye on customer value, and helps their teams use the latest AI-assisted techniques to build software for our large and growing audience of property managers and owners. This is a hands-on technical leadership role. That doesn't mean writing code. It means staying close to the technical work day to day, weighing in on architecture and quality decisions, and having real skin in the game on how our systems get built. Key responsibilities • Lead and grow two teams across Owner Growth and Owner Actions, 8–10 experienced engineers in total, all reporting directly to you. You'll own all aspects of people management: hiring, onboarding, goal setting, feedback, performance, and career growth. • Share ownership of strategy and delivery for both teams with Product. Turn Investor/Owner segment plans into team roadmaps and an operating rhythm that delivers results within the year. • Drive execution across both teams: organize work, set clear goals, manage dependencies between the teams, adjust pace to match criticality and risk, and hold the teams accountable for outcomes. • Stay close to the technical work. Take part in architectural discussions, and lead them when needed. Make informed trade-offs on service boundaries, APIs, and modularization that fit the segment's technical strategy. • Own quality: make sure the systems your teams own are maintainable, tested, secure, performant, observable, and ready to scale with our growth. Balance investment in technical health with net-new development. • Use engineering and product metrics, including growth and engagement metrics, to measure delivered value, track team health, and drive continuous improvement. • Work with Product, Design, and Machine Learning to bridge the gap between "what's possible" with AI and "what's valuable" for owners. Stay close to customers through calls, feedback, and usage data. • Build a culture of trust, ownership, and psychological safety. Coach engineers with direct feedback, address underperformance quickly, and give emerging leaders stretch opportunities. • Champion AI-first ways of working. Help your teams evaluate, adopt, and validate AI tooling and new patterns that improve quality and speed up delivery. • Attract top talent: assess candidates rigorously, work closely with Recruiting, and build an inclusive hiring pipeline. You know you're the right fit if... • You have 7+ years of experience building and scaling web applications, preferably in SaaS, including 2+ years directly managing software engineers. Experience leading more than one team is a strong plus. • You've led teams from early ambiguity to a successful launch. Along the way you aligned stakeholders, made tough trade-offs, and protected long-term system health. • You're technically credible. You can go deep on system design and code quality, and you stay close enough to the work to make good technical calls with your teams. • You've worked on growth, engagement, or workflow-heavy product areas and know how to connect engineering work to measurable customer outcomes. • You set clear expectations, give specific and actionable feedback, and handle conflict and underperformance with empathy and accountability. • You delegate to grow others, even when you could do it faster yourself, and you know when to step in during critical moments. • You communicate clearly, adapt your message to your audience, and give your teams clarity when the path isn't obvious. • You understand the business, not just the code. You can explain how your teams' work connects to customer outcomes and company goals. • You've built AI tooling into your own workflow and your teams'. You know how to prompt, iterate, and validate AI-generated output, and you set the bar for doing it well. • You're pragmatic about technology. You adopt new tools where they add value, stick with proven ones when they get the job done, and push for refactoring and clean architecture where it pays off. • You balance intensity and sustainability. You'll put in the extra hour when needed, but you model healthy work-life balance for your teams. Nice to have • Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D. in Computer Science or a related technical discipline. • Experience with technologies in our stack, such as Ruby on Rails, Golang, React, AWS, or service-oriented architectures.
