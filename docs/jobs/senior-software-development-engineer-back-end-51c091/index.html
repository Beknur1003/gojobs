Senior Software Development Engineer - Back end Placement in Alam Sutera, South Tangerang / Surabaya (Onsite) About Super We are a group of business enthusiasts, scientists, communicators, designers, productive mothers and many more who are unified and unduly spirited to work towards one big vision and goal. As a leading social commerce platform in Indonesia, we aim to solve economic inequality distribution across rural areas by building agent networks and hyperlocal supply chains to make goods more affordable. Aplikasi Super goes beyond the passion and creativity to decipher social issues among our surroundings. Key Responsibilities: • Create and implement high-quality features to augment our operations while adding value to our customers. • Write and deploy efficient, usable, pragmatic, and scalable solutions to grow the company. • Implement and upgrade our engineering best practices hence defining novel standards in our system. • Act as the center of expertise and provide technical leadership to solve issues in your respective tribes following the defined SLA. • Partaking in the review process for existing as well as new codes to advance system performance, stability, quality, and availability. • Groom other junior engineers in the organization through regular knowledge-sharing sessions. • Collaborate closely with disparate functions including Product Managers, Frontend Engineers, and Quality Assurance in delivering solutions following the technology roadmap. Role Requirements: • Graduated with Computer Science related background. • Minimum of 3-4 years of working experience using NodeJs, NestJs, or Typescript. Experience with Golang, Java, Ruby on Rails, Python, or Elixir is also welcome. • Excellent understanding of app development tools such as Redis, Kafka, MySQL, and Elasticsearch. • Fluent in software engineering concepts and design patterns. Join Super At Super, we are continuously providing equal, unparalleled opportunities across various business operating pillars from operations, commercial, and technology for all candidates from diverse types of backgrounds and expertise. If you are someone with a high passion for delivering innovative work as part of your extraordinary career, are committed to self-integrity, enjoy working collaboratively, and are ready to work in an agile environment, you are very welcome to apply.
