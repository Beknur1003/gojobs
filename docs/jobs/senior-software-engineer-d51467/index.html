The Test Platform team is a group of software engineers that builds and operates a suite of OpenShift-native tooling and infrastructure supporting Continuous Integration processes for hundreds of GitHub repositories, deploying thousands of OpenShift clusters daily. We are seeking engineers in Brazil with a schedule allowing regular collaboration and maintaining meaningful working-hour overlap with the rest of the team in the US East Coast and EMEA. Our primary focus is on maintaining testing infrastructure, including OpenShift cluster operations, building tools for CI infrastructure, including upstream kubernetes-sigs/prow. We are not analyzing test results. Go is our main programming language. Candidates should be willing to learn technologies mentioned below, comfortable logging into AWS or GCP consoles, and understand basic cloud computing. What you'll do? • Design and build scalable, distributed solutions that enable Red Hat engineers to be productive and efficient • Engage with open source communities to build consensus on goals in shared projects and implement features to achieve those goals • Collaborate with Red Hat engineers to identify gaps in productivity and efficiency to be addressed by tooling • Investigate, communicate, and troubleshoot escalated issues coming from the engineering team • Maintain development, staging, and production environments and their monitoring and alerting systems • Participate in an on-call rotation to support multiple systems during working hours • Constantly learn new things and maintain an overview of current technologies • Perform code review for features and bug fixes • Mentor other engineers, lead them in building excellent services What you'll bring? • An engineering degree or higher, or equivalent in computer science or a related field. • Five years or more experiences in software development • Proficiency with object-oriented and functional programming languages • Good written and verbal communication skills to handle communication in a distributed team • Knowledge of Git, GitHub, JIRA, Red Hat OpenShift, golang, Prow • Familiarity with Cloud-Native system design and development • Experience with AI-driven software development • Solid experience with Linux (i.e., Fedora), cloud computing (AWS, GCP), distributed systems, high availability, and build tools Preferred Skills • Experience in container technologies like Docker, Kubernetes and OpenShift • Experience in CI/CD pipeline and infrastructure • Ability to work in a globally distributed team • Understanding of Continuous Integration process About Red Hat Red Hat is the world’s leading provider of enterprise open source software solutions, using a community-powered approach to deliver high-performing Linux, cloud, container, and Kubernetes technologies. Spread across 40+ countries, our associates work flexibly across work environments, from in-office, to office-flex, to fully remote, depending on the requirements of their role. Red Hatters are encouraged to bring their best ideas, no matter their title or tenure. We're a leader in open source because of our open and inclusive environment. We hire creative, passionate people ready to contribute their ideas, help solve complex problems, and make an impact.
