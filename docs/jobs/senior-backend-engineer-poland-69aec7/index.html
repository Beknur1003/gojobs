Webshare is an affordable and powerful self-serve proxy infrastructure. Built for prosumers, solopreneurs, and growing businesses, it delivers enterprise-grade capabilities without the premium price tag or the sales calls. That same principle shapes the team: lean, flat, and async by default, where clarity and follow-through matter more than hierarchy. Webshare is an enterprise-grade proxy platform delivering fast, self-service access to over 80 million global IPs across 195 countries. Built for extreme reliability with 99.97% network uptime, it enables tens of thousands of businesses worldwide to run large-scale web scraping, market research, and automated data collection through dev-friendly APIs. In this role, you’ll: • Build and maintain high-performance backend APIs that power Webshare’s proxy infrastructure. • Own the design and delivery of services that handle billions of requests daily. • Collaborate in a highly autonomous team to scope, plan, and execute work with minimal meetings. • Investigate and resolve production issues across distributed systems. • Balance speed and quality, by identifying which trade-off is better for a particular situation. • Operate and improve our containerized infrastructure. • Continuously improve system performance, observability, and cost-efficiency. Your skills & experience: • Strong experience in at least one OOP language. • Ability to work with industry standard AI tools (LLMs), while balancing code quality and speed, but never compromising on reliability and architecture. • Ability to work in a programming language agnostic environment, you're eager to learn and excited to grow your backend engineering skills, including working with Python. • Comfortable working in an infrastructure heavy environment. • Proven experience designing and scaling APIs. • Familiarity with distributed systems and async architectures. • Willingness to understand deep networking concepts (BGP, CIDR, ASNs). • Fluent English (international team). • Can handle working with a remote team (we’re worldwide). Nice to have: • Experience with Python. • Experience with very large datasets. • Experience with proxies or other networking-related technologies. • Experience with golang. Salary & Benefits: • Gross salary: 24 000 - 33 400 PLN/month gross + quarterly KPI-based performance bonus. Keep in mind that we are open to discussing a different salary based on your skills and experience. • Growth & Learning: 40+ internal learning options, external conferences, mentorship, and year-round knowledge-sharing. • Health & Well-being: Private health insurance. • Celebration & Community: Team events, an overseas workation and plenty of ways to mark milestones together. Webshare is part of Source, a data ecosystem for a world built on intelligence. One of five independent technology companies connected by shared standards, expertise, and a belief in responsible access to web data. Up for the challenge? Let’s talk!
