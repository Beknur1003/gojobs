Ready to code the future of US dental care? Teero is looking for a Full Stack Software Engineer to join our Austin headquarters. If you ship fast, build with AI, and want to launch products shoulder-to-shoulder with the customers who use them, we want to talk. About Teero The $160B+ dental industry still runs on fax machines and phone tag—we're fixing that. We're an AI-native company building the operating system for modern dental offices, starting with staffing and expanding into revenue cycle management, patient billing, and beyond. Backed by Construct Capital with thousands of offices and hygienists using our platform monthly, we're growing fast across the country—and we're just getting started. About the Role You'll be among our first engineers in Austin, embedded directly with our operations team and customers at our US headquarters. This isn't a remote satellite of an engineering org—you'll be the technical co-pilot for our product expansion, building and iterating on new products in real time with the dental offices who use them. You'll hear a customer problem in the morning and ship a fix by the afternoon. You'll sit next to the team selling and onboarding new products like revenue cycle management and patient billing, and build the AI-powered tools that help dental offices grow their revenue faster. You'll launch entirely new products from zero, with a feedback loop measured in hours, not sprints. We have no product managers: you'll shape what we build and how we build it, working directly with our operations team, founders, and the Amsterdam engineering team. We don't just use AI—it's how we build. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and whatever comes next aren't nice-to-haves, they're core to how we work. We expect our engineers to stay on the bleeding edge of AI-assisted development, constantly adopting new tools and techniques that let a small team ship at a pace that shouldn't be possible. As Teero grows, so will you.
