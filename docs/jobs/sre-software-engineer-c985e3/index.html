This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for an SRE Software Engineer based in India. The SRE Software Engineer will play a key role in developing and enhancing observability and chaos engineering platforms to improve the reliability of mission-critical cloud services. This position combines software engineering, site reliability engineering, and platform engineering to build scalable, resilient, and high-performing systems. The successful candidate will design and develop backend services, APIs, and cloud-native solutions using technologies such as Golang, Rust, Python, and JavaScript. Working closely with engineering and infrastructure teams, you will drive improvements in system availability, performance, monitoring, and operational resilience. You will also lead complex troubleshooting activities, contribute to incident management, and implement strategies to prevent recurring production issues. This is an exciting opportunity to influence enterprise-scale technology platforms while contributing to innovative solutions that support the future of mobility. Accountabilities: • Software Development & Engineering • Design, develop, test, and deploy scalable software solutions using Golang, Rust, Python, JavaScript, and other relevant technologies. • Build and maintain REST and gRPC APIs, system integrations, and high-performance backend services. • Apply engineering best practices, including test-driven development, code reviews, automated testing, and continuous integration. • Develop reliable, maintainable, and efficient software components for distributed systems. • Site Reliability & Platform Engineering • Design, develop, and support distributed cloud-native systems, preferably using Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Kubernetes. • Develop and maintain chaos engineering platforms, reliability tools, and automation capabilities. • Collaborate with engineering teams to improve system availability, scalability, observability, and overall performance. • Define and implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability solutions using OpenTelemetry and other industry-standard technologies. • Conduct capacity planning, performance analysis, and infrastructure optimization to ensure reliable platform operations. • Incident Management & Operational Excellence • Lead troubleshooting, debugging, root-cause analysis, and resolution of complex production incidents. • Participate in on-call rotations and incident response activities to maintain service continuity. • Conduct post-incident reviews and implement preventive measures to minimize recurring failures. • Develop, maintain, and validate disaster recovery and business continuity plans. • Implement security best practices, support security audits, and coordinate vulnerability remediation activities. • Maintain comprehensive technical documentation, including architecture decisions, operational runbooks, playbooks, and support procedures. • Mentor engineering team members and provide technical leadership through design reviews, knowledge sharing, and engineering best practices. Requirements: • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience. • Minimum of 8+ years of professional experience in software engineering, site reliability engineering, or platform engineering. • Strong programming expertise in Golang and Rust, with additional experience in Python, JavaScript, or other programming and scripting languages. • Proven experience designing, developing, and operating distributed systems within cloud environments. • Strong understanding of Linux operating systems, system internals, and infrastructure-level troubleshooting. • Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and cloud platforms, including deployment and management of cloud-native applications. • Experience with observability and monitoring solutions, particularly OpenTelemetry or equivalent technologies. • Strong background in developing APIs, backend services, and system integrations using REST and/or gRPC. • Familiarity with relational and/or document-oriented databases. • Excellent troubleshooting, debugging, root-cause analysis, and performance optimization capabilities. • Understanding of site reliability engineering principles, system availability, scalability, and operational resilience. • Strong knowledge of software development best practices, automation, and continuous integration methodologies. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate across technical teams and stakeholders. • Ability to take ownership of complex technical challenges and work effectively in a collaborative engineering environment. Preferred Qualifications: • Experience working with Google Cloud Platform (GCP). • Knowledge of chaos engineering principles, practices, and related tooling. • Experience with Dynatrace or comparable observability and application performance monitoring platforms. • Knowledge of C++, Rust, or other systems programming languages. • Experience with platform engineering practices and infrastructure automation. • A Master's degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline is an additional advantage. Benefits: • Full-time professional opportunity with a remote working arrangement in India. • Opportunity to work on enterprise-scale technology platforms and mission-critical cloud services. • Exposure to modern technologies, including Golang, Rust, Kubernetes, GCP, and cloud-native architectures. • Opportunity to develop advanced expertise in site reliability engineering, observability, and chaos engineering. • Collaboration with experienced software engineers, platform specialists, and technology professionals. • Opportunity to influence system architecture, reliability strategies, and engineering standards. • Continuous technical learning and professional development opportunities. • Exposure to complex engineering challenges and large-scale distributed systems. • Opportunity to contribute to innovative technology solutions supporting the future of mobility.
