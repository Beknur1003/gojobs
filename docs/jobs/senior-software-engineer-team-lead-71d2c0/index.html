Senior Software Engineer - Team Lead

This hands-on Senior Software Engineer - Team Lead, will lead a talented team of software engineers, with responsibility for the collaboration, coordination and communication for the squad. You’ll be active in maintaining best practice code quality, design and architecture. Contributing to the larger codebase and technology roadmap, you’ll be designing, developing and implementing solutions both on your own and in peer development style. Movio applications require high performance and process lots of data, giving our engineers experience with concurrency, fault-tolerant systems, and distributed cloud architectures. Testability and Continuous Integration are very important to us, and we have great test coverage. You'll need to be able to work well in an autonomous and diverse environment while being able to prioritise effectively. About the role • Motivate and lead the team, encouraging, guiding and directing Engineers in your squad, assisting them to become self-organised and to take ownership • Foster an environment of continuous improvement, sharing ideas, learnings and technologies with the team to help inspire, foster and encourage innovation • Support team members’ learning and growth, and lead by good example • Work with the Engineering Manager to encourage healthy team collaboration and communication processes such as code reviews, test shares, design discussions etc... • Help guide a team of Engineers to deliver high-quality and innovative solutions, acting as a Lead Engineer in the delivery of quality software architecture and code • Assist the Engineering Manager with technical interviews, selection, induction and mentoring of new team members, ensuring they are well integrated, trained and supported • Provide employee reviews, support, team-building, and cross-squad communication, providing coaching and mentoring, and recommending additional training where necessary • Act as a communication point to the team and other stakeholders communicating early when timeframes are at risk • Report team progress to the Engineering Manager, and execute and helping refine the Strategic Objectives Requirements We're looking for: • Well-developed people management skills, with proven leadership experience in a Team Lead or similar role • Ability to provide strong mentorship and establish and maintain key relationships with stakeholders • Proven experience in a senior level back end Software Engineer role Technologies You'll be working with: • Golang • Python • Typescript • PostgreSQL • AWS • Kubernetes About Vista Vista is a world-leading company that makes software for the cinema industry. Started in Auckland, New Zealand, over 25 years ago, we now serve cinemas, film distributors, and moviegoers worldwide. Part of the Vista Group, Movio is the global leader in marketing data analytics and campaign management solutions for the cinema industry. Solutions are designed specifically with the challenges of movie marketing in mind, with an aim to connect all moviegoers to their ideal movie so everyone can experience the magic of cinema. Our love for cinema and film drives the purpose and foundations that generate innovation for our customers. Our HQ is in the City Works Depot in Auckland. We have offices and subsidiary companies in Sydney, Los Angeles, London, Amsterdam, Cape Town, Shanghai, Mexico City, and Kuala Lumpur. We use the latest technologies and offer a fun, agile and collaborative environment. We continue to innovate and build our reputation as one of the best Kiwi tech companies to work for.
