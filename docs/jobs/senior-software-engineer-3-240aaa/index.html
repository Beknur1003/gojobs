The company is backed by **leading venture capital investors** and experienced technology and fintech operators and is in an early stage of rapid growth. The engineering team is building the core technology platform from the ground up, with a strong focus on scalability, security, reliability, and automation. ## Job Summary We are looking for a **Senior Software Engineer (SDE III)** who enjoys solving complex backend problems and taking complete ownership of systems. This role is crucial in designing and building high-performance systems that support our financial technology initiatives. ## Responsibilities - Design, build, and own scalable backend services and APIs using **Java/Spring Boot and/or Golang**. - Build high-performance systems involving **financial workflows, data processing, and third-party integrations**. - Design and implement microservices, REST/gRPC APIs, and event-driven architectures. - Write clean, well-tested, production-grade code and actively participate in code reviews. - Own the reliability, monitoring, and observability of the systems you build. - Troubleshoot and resolve complex production and scalability issues. - Work closely with Product and Engineering leadership on architecture, estimation, prioritization, and delivery. - Mentor junior engineers and contribute to engineering standards and best practices. - Build systems with a strong emphasis on **security, reliability, data integrity, and compliance**. ## Qualifications - **6–10 years** of professional software engineering experience. - Strong hands-on expertise in **Java and/or Golang**. -
