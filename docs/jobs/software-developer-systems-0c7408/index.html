About DevRev At DevRev, we're building the future of work with Computer – your AI teammate. Unlike traditional tools, Computer unifies all your data sources, tools, and workflows into a single AI-ready platform, giving employees real-time insights, proactive suggestions, and powerful agentic actions. It extends your existing software with AI-native apps and agents that work alongside your teams and customers – updating workflows, coordinating across teams, and eliminating repetitive work. We call this Team Intelligence: human-AI collaboration that breaks down silos, brings people back together, and frees you to solve bigger problems. Backed by Khosla Ventures and Mayfield with $150M+ raised, DevRev is trusted by global companies across industries. About the role We are looking for ambitious software developers who can bring a fresh perspective to solving challenging problems and build infrastructure that will support AI/ML applications and a delightful user experience. A good grasp of operating systems, distributed systems, databases, and networking concepts and the ability to design for scale, optimized memory usage, and performance are required to be successful in this role. As a software developer at DevRev, you will have the opportunity to build things that truly matter and to work with a thriving global team.
