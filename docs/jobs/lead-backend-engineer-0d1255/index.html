Job Summary We're looking for a Lead Backend Engineer with deep expertise in Golang to design and build scalable, cloud-native services. Job Description What You'll Do • Lead the design, development, and delivery of distributed backend systems using Golang. • Define architecture, technical standards, and best practices across the team. • Build and operate cloud-native applications on AWS. • Deploy and manage services using Kubernetes and Terraform. • Drive technical planning, estimation, and execution of large-scale initiatives. • Partner with product, architecture, and engineering teams to deliver business outcomes. • Mentor junior engineers through code reviews, design discussions, and technical coaching. • Ensure high standards for reliability, scalability, security, and operational excellence. Required Qualifications • 8+ years of software engineering experience with a strong backend focus. • 4-6+ years of experience leading technical projects and delivering production systems. • Expert-level proficiency in Golang. • Strong experience with: • AWS • Kubernetes • Terraform • Microservices and distributed systems • REST APIs • CI/CD and DevOps practices • SQL/NoSQL databases • Experience designing and operating large-scale distributed systems and microservices. • Strong understanding of concurrency, performance optimization, resiliency patterns, and service reliability. • Proven track record of mentoring and developing engineers. Soft Skills & Collaboration • Strong technical leadership and ownership mindset. • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills. • Ability to influence without authority and drive alignment across teams. • Collaborative approach to problem-solving and decision-making. • Comfortable working in fast-paced, cross-functional environments. • Passion for mentoring, knowledge sharing, and fostering a high-performance engineering culture. Preferred Qualifications • Experience with video processing, media workflows, or video analytics. • Experience building LLM-powered applications, AI agents, or agentic workflows. • Experience owning end-to-end product delivery from design through production operations. • Experience working with high-scale, cloud-native platforms.
