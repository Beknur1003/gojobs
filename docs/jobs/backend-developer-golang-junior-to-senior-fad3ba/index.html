We are looking for Backend Developers across all experience levels (Junior to Senior) to join our engineering team. You will design and build high-performance, distributed backend services that power our real-time platform. This role has a strong emphasis on solution architecture - we want engineers who don't just write code, but who think about how systems fit together and can propose and own end-to-end technical solutions. Responsibilities • Design, develop and maintain backend services and APIs, primarily in Golang • Build and operate event-driven systems using message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ) • Participate in system design discussions and contribute to architectural decisions • Design database schemas and optimize queries for both relational and NoSQL databases • Analyze business requirements and translate them into scalable, maintainable technical solutions • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues; perform root-cause analysis • Collaborate with product, QA, and DevOps teams throughout the delivery lifecycle • (Senior) Lead solution design for new features and systems, mentor junior engineers, and drive technical standards across the team Qualifications • Strong proficiency in Golang or another backend language (Java, Python, Node.js, etc.) with willingness to work in Go • Hands-on experience with message queues such as Kafka or RabbitMQ — producers/consumers, partitioning, delivery guarantees, error handling • Solid knowledge of system design: microservices, caching, load balancing, high availability, and scalability patterns • Good understanding of databases (e.g., MySQL, MongoDB, Redis): data modeling, indexing, transactions, and query optimization • Strong problem-solving skills — able to break down complex problems, debug across service boundaries, and reason about trade-offs • A solution-architect mindset: you think beyond individual tickets, consider the bigger picture, and can propose designs that balance business needs, cost, and technical constraints Preferred Qualifications • Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and cloud platforms (AWS preferred) • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code • Experience building real-time or high-throughput systems (e.g., sports data, gaming, fintech) • Familiarity with gRPC, WebSocket, and RESTful API design • Experience with observability tooling (Prometheus, Grafana, distributed tracing) Benefits • Five-days work • Birthday Leave • Discretionary bonus • Group Medical Insurance • Free Snacks & Drinks
