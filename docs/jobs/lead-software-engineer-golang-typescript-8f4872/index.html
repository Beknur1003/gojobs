If successful, you will: • Take responsibility for the quality of the code and architecture delivered by your team • Influence and coach the team engineers • Align your team on goals, outcomes and timelines • Collaborate with Product and Technology teams to translate our strategic roadmap into actionable tasks; provide visibility on delivery progress outside your team • Design, develop, test, the performance and observability of our services and applications • Improve the quality of life of your team and the business, through automation, process improvement or new technologies About the team: • You will join a technical team of c. 40 technologists with a key purpose of continuously and reliably collecting large amounts of financial data describing cross-border payments, then processing the data, checking the quality and serving it to our users so that they can make the best business decision • A typical team contains around 5 people, including a Developer, a QA and a Product Manager • We follow Agile methodologies and collaborate closely with product and commercial teams, so you will be interacting with various stakeholders across the business in addition to your immediate team You should apply if you can confidently: • Manage a team technically, coaching them to reach your level of expertise • Write Golang services and web app backends, also using SQL • Build beautiful, componentised application frontends in React/TypeScript • Understand clean code principles and the language idioms of TypeScript and Go, experience reviewing code using these principles and establishing team quality standards • Learn and engage with a new business domain and product • Communicate complex topics to technical and non-technical people • Talk about how you learn and improve observability tools (e.g. Prometheus and Grafana) • Containerisation services (e.g. Docker) and/or Kubernetes • Experience utilising AI to increase productivity and efficiency of work and
