Meet the Team About Our Business Group: Cisco Cloud Security is a leading provider of network security services, enabling the world to connect to the Internet with confidence on any device, anywhere, anytime. The team is building the next generation of AI powered cloud security services as highly available distributed systems using cloud engineering practices. We are looking for hardworking engineers to help us launch the next generation of cloud-delivered security services and deliver a stronger offering with outstanding customer experience. Our engineering team is composed of highly skilled and diverse individuals who are comfortable working in a fast-paced and technically challenging environment. Members are involved with all stages of the product development process from solving complex engineering problems to working directly with customers. About Us: We are building the future of cloud-delivered, AI powered, multi-dimensional security. Our team is responsible for the development and operations of the high impact, core platforms and frameworks that enable seamless security application management. We specialize in developing core platforms like APIGW, user interfaces, resilient RESTful APIs, and Enterprise Network integrations that are key market differentiators, while closely following established processes. Our cloud security services are critical to the success of the next wave of innovation in the security industry. About You: You are excited about and take pride in developing AI powered Cloud-native solutions. You have strong programming skills and are passionate about building applications. You are a problem-solver and a trouble-shooter with a growth mindset. You have the ability to think creatively and are self-motivated. You are passionate about AI and like to experiment and learn. Your Impact • Have an impact on Cloud security Products and AI Enablement. • Work in a fast-paced team to build the intuitive user interfaces and scalable SaaS and Enterprise Network integrations for AI powered Cisco cloud security products. • Solve important customer problems and deliver impactful results with attention to detail. • Contribute to all phases of product development, operations, monitoring, and maintenance. • Contribute to a no-blame culture that values learning, transparency, accountability, trust, kindness, and mutual respect. Minimum Qualifications • Must have a bachelor's degree in computer science or in the field of engineering. • Must have an experience working in a team setting with good collaboration and communication skills. • Preliminary experience with Cloud friendly technologies like Docker, Jenkins, GoLang, Terraform, and AWS technologies like S3, DDB, Lambda, ECS, Code-Build. • Preliminary experience with web application technologies including HTTP, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, with an emphasis on React and modern frontend development patterns. • Preliminary experience with testing methodologies and tools such as Jest, React Testing Library, Cypress, and general frontend quality practices. • Good understanding of micro-frontend architectures, shared UI platforms, and integration patterns across multiple applications and teams. • Good understanding of RESTful APIs, client-side data fetching, authentication flows, and application state management. • Knowledge of monitoring and observability practices is a plus. • Knowledge of frontend build and delivery tooling such as Webpack, Babel, Yarn is a plus.
