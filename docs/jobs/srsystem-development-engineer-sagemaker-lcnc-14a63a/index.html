De ion At AWS AI, we want to make it easy for our customers to train their deep learning workloads in the cloud. With Amazon SageMaker, AWS is building customer-facing services to empower data scientists and software engineers in their deep learning endeavors. As our customers rapidly adopt large language models and generative AI for their business, we're building the next-generation AI platform to accelerate their development. We're seeking a dedicated engineering team lead to drive building our next-generation AI compute platform that's optimized for large language models and distributed training. You will be influential within your team and work with peers and senior leaders to define and revise the standards for operational excellence across systems. You will consistently tackle issues that span multiple functional areas and drive your team to push for improvements that can scale across other teams, services, and platforms. You'll work alongside teams of talented engineers, collaborate with applied scientists, and solve problems that span the full AI lifecycle — from efficient resource scheduling across heterogeneous hardware to fault-tolerant distributed training and reinforcement learning. If you're passionate about building reliable, high-performance infrastructure and want to work where cloud systems meet AI, this is your team. Key job responsibilities Design and improve the automation, reliability, and performance of generative AI training compute infrastructure, including resource scheduling and fault-tolerant distributed training across heterogeneous hardware. Lead architecture and code reviews for your team and adjacent teams, identifying bottlenecks and proposing solutions that reduce complexity and eliminate recurring operational issues. Define and instrument key infrastructure metrics such as GPU utilization and training throughput, using data to drive continuous improvement and capacity planning decisions. Mentor and guide other engineers on engineering best practices, decomposing complex problems into deliverable components and enabling parallel execution across the team. Partner with applied scientists and software engineers to evaluate new technologies and deliver infrastructure changes that improve the end-to-end AI development workflow. A day in the life You'll spend your days close to the customer — identifying their pain points and building the tools that solve them. You'll dig into how resources are being used, find opportunities to make them more efficient and secure, then implement those optimizations end to end. You'll also define and instrument the metrics that matter, such as GPU efficiency, so the team can measure what's working and continuously improve. It's a role for someone who enjoys moving between understanding a problem, engineering a solution, and proving its impact with data. About The Team Diverse Experiences AWS values diverse experiences.
