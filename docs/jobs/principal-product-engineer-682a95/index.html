About Xero Our purpose is to make life better for people in small business, their advisors and communities. Our vision is to be the most insightful and trusted small-business platform. Small businesses are the heartbeat of the economy, and we're unlocking products that ease their pressure. The role As a Principal Engineer, you'll be a hands-on, backend-leaning full-stack engineer with a strong product mindset. You'll set the technical direction for a fast-scaling product with mission-critical workloads. It's built on large-scale distributed systems, with event-driven and API architecture and microservices across web, API and data layers, running on AWS with SQL and NoSQL data stores. Staying close to the technical detail, you'll set the example for a portfolio of 40 to 50 engineers. Our three-year company strategy is reaching a turning point, and you'll help shape what comes next. You'll work with Product and Design to define a multi-year technical strategy, with domain-level influence over the next two to three years. You'll Go Bold by setting long-term direction, and Go Together by bringing product, engineering and senior stakeholders with you. You'll also join a global community of Principal Engineers across Xero, driving engineering standards, architecture and best practices. Initially, you will focus on • Defining a multi-year technical strategy for the domain with Product and Design, aligned to Xero's next company strategy. • Architecting large-scale, event-driven and API-first distributed systems on AWS that stay reliable as the product and its traffic grow. • Making key technical decisions for mission-critical applications in production, balancing reliability, performance and cost. • Partnering with Product and Design to understand customer problems and prioritise the highest-impact features. • Defining architectural patterns and guardrails for our agentic features, including orchestration, human-in-the-loop approvals, auditability and evaluation. • Setting standards for AI-assisted delivery (AI-DLC) so that it stays safe, secure and high quality. Where and how you can work You'll be based in Sydney or Melbourne, working a hybrid model that blends time together in our modern offices with the flexibility of working from home. Twice weekly boost days bring teams together to connect and collaborate in person, on your terms. What we're looking for • Deep experience designing and running large-scale distributed systems in production, balancing reliability, performance and cost, and making key technical decisions for mission-critical applications. Payments or fintech experience is a real plus. • Strong backend-leaning full-stack skills across microservices, event-driven and API architecture, in languages such as C#, Java, Go or C++. You'll also need experience with AWS and with SQL and NoSQL data stores. • Evidence of a product mindset. Please bring examples where you shaped the customer outcome, not only the implementation. • A track record of working hand in hand with Product and Design on customer-facing features, influencing the roadmap and building consensus across delivery teams. • Experience setting multi-year technical direction at domain or portfolio level, and bringing senior stakeholders with you. • Clear communication with engineers and executives alike, and a commitment to mentoring and an inclusive culture. • A strong interest in using AI tooling is essential. You'll model how to use these technologies to automate tasks and improve engineering outcomes, and know where human review matters most. Experience with LLM-powered or agentic systems is a plus. Apply even if your experience isn’t a perfect match! At Xero, we hire based on your skills, passion, and the unique perspective you can bring to enhance our culture and team.
