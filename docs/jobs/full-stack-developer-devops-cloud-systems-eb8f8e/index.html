DevOps Developer (AWS) Fully Remote Opportunity About BrightOrder We build technology that keeps fleets safer, smarter, and more profitable. Our platform connects maintenance, telematics, and data to help operators make real-time decisions. We’re scaling fast across North America and we need exceptional builders who code, automate, and optimize systems end-to-end. The Role We’re looking for a Full Stack Developer with strong DevOps and AWS experience to help build, automate, and scale BrightOrder’s cloud-based platform. This role is ideal for someone who can contribute across the stack — building backend services, APIs, integrations, internal tools, and frontend components — while also supporting cloud infrastructure, deployments, monitoring, and automation. You’ll write production-grade code, improve developer workflows, and help keep our systems fast, secure, reliable, and scalable. What You'll Do • Design, develop, and maintain full stack applications, backend services, APIs, and internal tools. • Write clean, maintainable code using Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, and Go (Golang). • Build and enhance frontend or internal tooling using React.js or similar frameworks. • Develop RESTful APIs, integrations, and service-to-service communication. • Build automation scripts and internal tools to improve deployments, monitoring, and troubleshooting. • Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines for reliable releases. • Configure and manage AWS services, including ECS, EKS, Lambda, EC2, RDS, S3, and CloudWatch. • Optimize and maintain databases, including PostgreSQL and Amazon Aurora. • Use RabbitMQ or similar tools for inter-service communication. • Containerize and deploy applications using Docker and Kubernetes/EKS. • Use infrastructure as code tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation. • Build and maintain monitoring, logging, alerts, dashboards, and observability practices using tools such as New Relic, OpenTelemetry, CloudWatch, Prometheus, or Grafana to improve system reliability, performance visibility, and troubleshooting. • Collaborate with Product, QA, DevOps, and development teams to deliver secure, scalable releases. • Use tools such as Jira Service Management/Jira Service Desk to support issue tracking, escalation workflows, production support, and cross-functional communication with QA, Customer Support, and Development teams. • Support production troubleshooting, root cause analysis, and system reliability improvements. • Use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT, GitHub, Copilot, or similar platforms to support development, testing, documentation, and troubleshooting. Requirements Must-Have Experience • 5+ years of professional software development experience in a full stack, backend, DevOps, or cloud systems role. • Strong proficiency with Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, and React.js or another modern frontend framework. • Solid experience designing and building backend services, RESTful APIs, integrations, and service-based systems. • Intermediate hands-on experience with AWS services, including ECS/EKS, Lambda, RDS, EC2, S3, and CloudWatch. • Experience with CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, Docker, Kubernetes, and Git-based workflows. • Strong database experience across relational and non-relational systems, including PostgreSQL, Microsoft SQL Server, Oracle, and MongoDB. • Familiarity with RabbitMQ, Redis, microservices, serverless architectures, GraphQL, real-time messaging, or similar distributed system technologies. • Experience with observability and monitoring tools such as New Relic, OpenTelemetry, CloudWatch, Prometheus, or Grafana. • Understanding of Linux systems, networking fundamentals, application security, cloud security best practices, security reviews, and vulnerability management. • Experience building tools or solutions for developers, operations, QA, customer support, or other internal users, including exposure to Jira Service Management/Jira Service Desk workflows. • Exposure to SaaS, fleet management, logistics, telematics, IoT, or connected device ecosystems. • Demonstrated use of AI-powered development tools for coding, documentation, testing, reviewing, and troubleshooting. What Success Looks Like Success in this role means you can contribute across the technical stack while improving the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of our cloud systems. You write clean, secure, maintainable code, build practical solutions, automate repetitive work, improve internal workflows, and help the team ship better software faster. Attributes We’re Looking For We’re looking for someone who is accountable, resourceful, proactive, collaborative, reliable under pressure, tech savvy, results-oriented, service minded, fast-moving, and committed to continuous learning. Our Values At BrightOrder, how we work matters as much as what we build. We look for people who demonstrate Passion, Growth Mindset, Integrity, Authenticity, and Accountability. Benefits Why BrightOrder • Impact that matters — your work keeps fleets running and industries moving. • Fully remote, high-trust culture built on accountability and autonomy. • Competitive compensation with health, dental, and vision benefits. • Continuous learning through mentorship, new tech, and AI-driven innovation. • A team that values integrity, ownership, and excellence. Ready to build? Join BrightOrder and code the infrastructure that powers a safer, smarter logistics world.
