About the Role Are you an experienced Engineering Manager who genuinely enjoys developing people, building effective teams and creating an environment where engineers can do their best work? We're looking for a Senior Engineering Manager to join our Development & AI organisation, taking a leading role in supporting our engineering people, strengthening management practices and helping our teams operate effectively as we continue to grow. Our engineering organisation brings together a broad range of technical disciplines, each with different challenges, specialisms and development needs. As our teams evolve, we want to ensure our engineers have the leadership, support, career opportunities and working environment they need to succeed. This is a senior, people-focused leadership position. You'll take ownership of day-to-day engineering management, working with both individual contributors and engineering managers to develop capability, improve collaboration and establish consistent, effective ways of working. You'll also play an important role in engineering resource and capacity planning, recruitment, performance management and continuous improvement, helping ensure our teams have the right skills, structures and support to meet current and future priorities. Importantly, this isn't a hands-on coding or technical architecture role. You'll need a strong understanding of software engineering and the challenges engineers face, but your real impact will come through developing people, improving organisational effectiveness and enabling great engineering work to happen. Working closely with our Director of Development & AI, technical leadership and product stakeholders, you'll help shape a positive, collaborative and sustainable engineering environment. If you're passionate about engineering leadership, enjoy solving organisational challenges and believe that great teams are built through trust, accountability and genuine investment in people, we'd love to hear from you. About the team You'll join our Development & AI organisation, working with talented engineers across frontend, backend, full-stack, desktop development, machine learning engineering (MLE) and quality assurance (QA). Our teams work with a diverse technology stack, including: • Backend: Go (Golang), Java and Python • Frontend: React and TypeScript • Desktop: C#/.NET • Machine Learning Engineering: Python • Engineering practices: Cloud-native development, CI/CD, automated testing and modern software delivery We work across a range of interesting technical challenges, from mobile emulation and streaming technologies to sovereign AI platforms and other specialist software solutions. Our engineering structure includes dedicated technical leadership responsible for architecture, engineering standards and technical direction. As Senior Engineering Manager, you'll work alongside these technical specialists, providing complementary leadership focused on people, management capability, engineering culture and team effectiveness. We value technical excellence, collaborative problem-solving, personal ownership and continuous improvement. We're looking for someone who can help us build on those foundations and create an environment where engineers feel supported, challenged and empowered to develop. What You'll Be Doing • Providing visible, approachable and effective leadership to engineers and engineering managers, creating an environment built on trust, accountability and collaboration. • Coaching, mentoring and developing individual contributors, existing managers and emerging engineering leaders. • Establishing consistent people management practices, including regular one-to-ones, constructive feedback, performance reviews and personal development planning. • Supporting engineers with career development, progression opportunities and clear expectations across different engineering disciplines. • Managing performance and addressing people-related challenges fairly, proactively and constructively, working alongside our People team where appropriate. • Taking ownership of engineering resource and capacity planning, ensuring our teams have the right people and capabilities to support business and delivery priorities. • Working with the Director of Development & AI and recruitment teams on hiring priorities, workforce planning, onboarding, retention and future skills requirements. • Identifying organisational bottlenecks, inefficient processes and opportunities to improve how engineering teams collaborate and deliver. • Working closely with product, delivery and technical leadership to improve planning, communication, dependencies and delivery predictability. • Helping establish meaningful measures of team health, engineering effectiveness and continuous improvement. • Supporting positive engineering practices that balance delivery commitments with sustainable workloads, individual wellbeing and long-term development. • Providing clear visibility of engineering capacity, capability gaps, organisational risks and development needs to senior stakeholders. About You More than anything, we're looking for an experienced, approachable and people-focused engineering leader who genuinely enjoys helping others succeed. You'll understand that successful engineering management is about much more than tracking delivery or managing workloads. It's about building trust, developing capability, supporting individuals through challenges and creating the conditions for teams to perform consistently well. You'll be comfortable having difficult conversations, addressing performance concerns and making balanced decisions that combine empathy with accountability. You'll also be someone who can recognise when engineering processes or organisational structures aren't working effectively, bring people together to understand the challenges and introduce practical improvements without adding unnecessary complexity. We'd particularly welcome candidates who have progressed from a software engineering background into management and understand the different challenges faced by engineers working across multiple technical disciplines. You don't need to be the most technically knowledgeable person in the room. What matters is having the credibility to engage with technical specialists, an appreciation of modern engineering practices and the confidence to lead through people rather than technical authority. What Success Looks Like Success in this role isn't simply about managing teams or introducing new processes. It's about building an engineering organisation where people have the leadership and support they need, teams work effectively together and delivery becomes increasingly predictable and sustainable. Within your first three months, you'll have built trusted relationships across the engineering organisation, developed an understanding of our people, strengths and challenges, and identified the opportunities where your leadership can make the biggest difference. Within six months, you'll be helping establish more consistent management and development practices, clearer expectations for engineers and managers, and practical improvements to planning, communication and collaboration across teams. Within twelve months, we want to see an engineering organisation where people feel supported in their careers, managers are confident and effective, teams collaborate well, and leadership has greater visibility of engineering capacity and delivery risks. Ultimately, you'll help us build an environment that can continue to grow and evolve without unnecessary bureaucracy, where engineers feel both supported and challenged to do their best work. Requirements Skills & Experience We Are Looking For You don't need to tick every box. We're interested in candidates who can demonstrate strong engineering management experience, genuine leadership capability and a track record of helping individuals and teams succeed. People Leadership & Development • Proven experience in software engineering management, with significant responsibility for people leadership, development and team effectiveness. • Experience managing individual contributors and engineering managers, or a demonstrable ability to lead through other managers. • A strong track record of coaching, mentoring and developing engineers at different stages of their careers. • Experience establishing effective performance management, feedback and career development practices. • Confidence managing complex people situations, including underperformance, interpersonal challenges and organisational change. • The ability to build positive, inclusive team environments that balance individual wellbeing with accountability and delivery expectations. Engineering Management & Operations • Experience improving engineering working practices, team coordination, resource planning and delivery effectiveness. • Strong understanding of modern software engineering environments and the challenges involved in multidisciplinary software delivery. • Experience working with product and delivery stakeholders to improve prioritisation, planning and delivery predictability. • Ability to identify organisational bottlenecks and introduce proportionate, practical improvements. • Experience developing or implementing career frameworks, competency expectations or structured progression pathways. • Experience managing remote or distributed engineering teams. • Strong organisational awareness, stakeholder management and communication skills. Technical Understanding & Collaboration • A solid understanding of modern software development lifecycles, CI/CD, automated testing and software delivery practices. • Sufficient technical knowledge to understand engineering challenges, engage with specialists and contribute meaningfully to engineering planning discussions. • Ability to work effectively alongside dedicated technical leaders, respecting the distinction between technical direction and people management. • Confidence translating technical and delivery challenges into appropriate people, capability or organisational actions. Expert-level knowledge of our specific programming languages or frameworks isn't required, and this position does not involve routine hands-on development. We're more interested in your ability to understand the challenges facing engineering teams and demonstrate how your leadership has helped people and organisations improve. Nice to Have Experience or exposure to some of the following would be beneficial: • Managing multidisciplinary teams spanning frontend, backend, full-stack, desktop development, machine learning engineering or QA. • Experience within cloud-native, SaaS, desktop software or other modern product-led technology environments. • Supporting engineering organisations through growth, transformation or changes to team structures. • Developing engineering career ladders, management frameworks or leadership development programmes. • Familiarity with Agile and Lean practices, engineering delivery metrics and continuous improvement approaches. • Experience partnering with Staff Engineers, Principal Engineers, Technical Leads or other dedicated technical leadership roles. • Formal management, coaching or leadership development training. A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline may be beneficial, but isn't essential. We value demonstrable engineering management capability, relevant experience and proven leadership outcomes over formal qualifications.
