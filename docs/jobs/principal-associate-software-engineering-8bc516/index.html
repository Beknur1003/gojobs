About The Team: The Card Tech+ organization at Capital One is the massive, diverse and globally distributed technology engine that powers spending for customers across Upmarket, Mainstreet, Business, International and Partnerships segments. With associates spread out globally, we build world-class technology focused on removing friction, unleashing speed, and driving efficiency to create outstanding customer experiences. Our work is structured around five critical towers: • Digital Commerce & Innovation Tech modernizes customer spending on devices and online, focusing on payment innovation, fraud reduction and platforms like Loyalty and Capital One Travel Tech. • Card Intelligent Decisions and Experiences (CIDX) creates real-time, intelligent customer experiences through platforms for Authorizations, Decisioning and Machine Learning. • Core, Payments & Partnerships Tech transforms our foundational Core lending, Inbound Payments and Partnerships platforms, including the Discover Integration Planning. • Servicing, Business, and International paves the way for business growth and global expansion while providing world-class customer servicing, fraud protection and leveraging data and ML. • Encore drives modernization efforts for enhanced customer experiences through cutting edge tools and platforms. We foster a culture designed to maximize Associate Potential, Growth & Impact, offering extensive development opportunities.
