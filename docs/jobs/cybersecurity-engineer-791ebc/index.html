Job description: As a member of our Cybersecurity team, you will be responsible for managing and improving vulnerability management processes, securing infrastructure environments, and driving automation initiatives across hybrid cloud and on-premises environments. You will work closely with infrastructure, cloud, development, security, and compliance teams to strengthen the organization's security posture while ensuring operational efficiency through automation and innovation. Responsibilities: • Create custom internal scripts for task automation, data analysis, and API integrations connecting security platforms and infrastructure tools. • Manage the full vulnerability lifecycle, including identification, assessment, prioritization, remediation tracking, validation, and reporting. • Prepare vulnerability assessment reports, remediation plans, dashboards, operational procedures, KPIs, and security metrics. • Coordinate remediation activities across multiple teams to ensure timely patching, security updates, and configuration changes. • Deploy, administer, secure, and harden Linux, Windows, and VMware ESXi server environments. • Deploy, configure, and maintain network devices including switches, routers, firewalls, and VPN gateways. • Design, orchestrate, and deploy scalable and resilient infrastructure services supporting cyber threat management operations. • Develop and implement automated workflows to reduce manual intervention and prevent recurring incidents. • Collaborate with IT Infrastructure, Cloud Operations, SOC, Development, Compliance, and external vendors to ensure effective remediation and security operations. • Monitor and report on vulnerabilities, risk exposure, high-risk assets, actively exploited threats, ticket trends, and security performance indicators. • Produce and maintain technical documentation for engineers, operators, and end users. • Troubleshoot mission-critical services and continuously improve operational efficiency through automation. Technical skills: • Strong expertise in Linux, Windows Server, and VMware ESXi administration and security hardening. • Advanced scripting and automation skills using Python and Bash. • Experience with API integration and workflow automation between enterprise technologies. • Strong knowledge of TCP/IP networking, firewalls, VPN technologies, and network security principles. • Hands-on experience with AWS and Azure cloud platforms and related security practices. • Experience with vulnerability management tools such as Rapid7 Nexpose and Nessus. • Experience with SIEM platforms, preferably Splunk. • Experience with ticketing and ITSM platforms such as ServiceNow and Jira. • Knowledge of cybersecurity operations, cyber threat intelligence, and vulnerability management best practices. • Familiarity with containerized environments and DevSecOps practices, including Docker and Kubernetes. • Experience with messaging and event-driven platforms such as Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, AWS SQS, or Google Pub/Sub. • Knowledge of emerging threats, attack vectors, and modern hybrid cloud security challenges. • Experience in Java, GoLang, or Rust is considered an asset. Profile: • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Information Technology, or a related field. • 5+ years of experience in cybersecurity, infrastructure engineering, vulnerability management, or a similar role. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to manage complex technical environments. • Ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams and multiple stakeholders. • Excellent communication, reporting, and documentation skills. • Proactive mindset with a strong focus on automation and continuous improvement. • Fluency in English is required; French is considered a strong asset. Why Cronos Group? We'll propose you: • An attractive salary package. • A good work-life balance environment. • The opportunity to work with cutting-edge cybersecurity and cloud technologies. • Challenging projects in a dynamic and innovative environment. • Tailor-made training courses and professional development opportunities. • A friendly and collaborative workplace. If you wish to integrate a dynamic structure on a human scale while working with the latest technologies, don't wait anymore and join Cronos!
