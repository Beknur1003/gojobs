About the Role: We are seeking a talented and motivated Data Infrastructure Engineer Intern who is best in class with a high IQ plus a high EQ. This role presents an exciting opportunity to thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment within a rapidly growing team, with abundant prospects for career advancement. What You’ll Do: • Assist in maintaining and improving our data warehousing systems (AWS DMS, Redshift). • Support the team in monitoring data pipelines, identifying issues, and troubleshooting basic problems. • Write and optimize SQL queries for analytics and data validation. • Collaborate with engineers and analysts to understand data needs and support use cases. • Participate in testing, documentation, and improving reliability of data systems. • Identify and automate repetitive daily manual processes, reducing operational overhead and improving team efficiency. • Learn and explore new tools and technologies in the data ecosystem. What We Look For: • Currently pursuing (or recently completed) a degree in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or a related technical field. • Basic understanding of relational databases (MySQL, Postgres, relational concepts) and no sql databases. • Familiarity with at least one programming language (Python, Go, or similar). • Interest in data infrastructure, distributed systems, or backend engineering. • Strong problem-solving skills and willingness to learn. • Ability to work in a collaborative team environment. • Demonstrated experience working with Claude or equivalent large language model tools is required; candidates must be comfortable leveraging AI to enhance productivity, research, and communication. • Leveraged AI tools such as Claude to accelerate development workflows, troubleshoot complex technical issues, and enhance productivity. Preferred Knowledge and Skills: • Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS preferred). • Basic understanding of data pipelines or ETL concepts. • Awareness of data warehousing concepts. • Interest in big data or streaming technologies (Kafka, Kinesis Streams, Flink etc.) About You: • You’re curious and proactive - you ask questions and seek to understand how things work. • You take ownership of your learning and follow through on tasks. • You’re open to feedback and eager to improve. • You communicate clearly and work well with others. • You’re excited to build real-world skills in data engineering.
