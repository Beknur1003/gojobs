adjoe builds the technologies behind mobile apps growth and monetization. adjoe Ads is our programmatic in-app advertising platform that seamlessly connects advertisers and publishers through direct integration of our Android and iOS SDKs. Every ad decision happens in under 200ms across 10 million daily users, powered by real-time bidding algorithms and user quality prediction models that match the right ad to the right user at the right moment. Engine Behind 120M Processed Auctions Daily The Ad Network team builds and operates the backend services that power adjoe Ads' programmatic advertising engine. The core challenge is straightforward to state and hard to solve: for every incoming ad request from a mobile SDK, decide within milliseconds whether it's a good fit for any active campaign — and at what price to bid. To solve this, the team has built a Real-Time Bidding platform of 20+ Go microservices that orchestrate ad auctions at 1,500 bid requests per second, integrate with external demand partners via OpenRTB, and connect to ML models making 50 billion predictions daily across 600+ features to optimize bid pricing. The stack runs on Kubernetes, with Kafka handling event streaming, DynamoDB and Elasticache powering low-latency data access, and Prometheus and Grafana providing observability across the entire system.
