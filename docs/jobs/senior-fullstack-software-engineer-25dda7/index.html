What We Need to See: • Strong experience with Golang, Unix/Linux • Proven knowledge of web languages typescript, javascript • In-depth understanding of database concepts and object modeling. • Solid understanding of algorithms, computer architecture and computer science theory • Bachelor's degree (or higher) in Computer Science or equivalent experience • 6+ years of software development experience • Flexibility/adaptability for working in a global and dynamic environment with different frameworks and requirements Ways to Stand out from the Crowd: • A love for engineering and passion for what you do • Experience with container technologies like Docker or container orchestration like K8S • Knowledge of Ruby • Ability to read and understand C++, especially Modern C++ #LI-Hybrid Your base salary will be determined based on your location, experience, and the pay of employees in similar positions.
