Job Description: Role Summary HPE is seeking a Cloud Developer to contribute to the design, development, and deployment of cloud solutions that support mission-critical applications. This role supports HPE's Cloud Services portfolio and works closely with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, scalable, and secure cloud-based systems. The ideal candidate has experience with cloud technologies, software development, and containerized environments, along with strong problem-solving and collaboration skills. This position provides opportunities to learn new technologies, contribute to innovative projects, and grow technical expertise in cloud-native development. Key Responsibilities • Design, develop, test, and maintain cloud-based applications and services under the guidance of senior engineers. • Participate in code reviews, testing activities, and design discussions to ensure quality and adherence to engineering standards. • Troubleshoot and resolve software defects, performance issues, and system integration challenges. • Collaborate with team members to implement cloud-native solutions and improve system reliability and scalability. • Contribute to the adoption of development best practices, automation, and modern engineering tools. • Analyze application and system data to identify issues and recommend improvements. • Work with project teams and stakeholders to understand requirements and support the delivery of business objectives. • Document technical designs, implementation details, and operational procedures. Basic Qualifications • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field. • Typically 2+ years of experience in software development, cloud development, or related disciplines. Knowledge and Skills • Proficiency in one or more programming languages such as Python, Java, Golang, or JavaScript. • Experience with cloud platforms and cloud-native application development concepts. • Familiarity with microservices, containerization technologies, and Kubernetes. • Understanding of software development lifecycle practices, testing methodologies, and debugging techniques. • Knowledge of source control, CI/CD pipelines, and automation tools. • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills. • Effective verbal and written communication skills. • Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment and learn from experienced engineers. • Familiarity with enterprise software development practices and agile methodologies.
