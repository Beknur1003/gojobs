Veeam is the Data and AI Trust Company, specializing in helping organizations ensure their data and AI are fully understood, secured, and resilient to enable the acceleration of safe AI at scale. As the market leader in both data resilience and data security posture management, Veeam is built for the convergence of identity, data, security, and AI risk. Headquartered in Seattle with offices in more than 30 countries, Veeam protects over 550,000 customers worldwide, who trust Veeam to keep their businesses running. Join us as we go fearlessly forward together, growing, learning, and making a real impact for some of the world’s biggest brands. About the Role We are the leader in Kubernetes Backup and Disaster Recovery. Veeam Kasten, our data management platform purpose-built for Kubernetes, gives enterprise operations teams an easy-to-use, scalable and secure system for backup and restore, disaster recovery, and application mobility. We are looking for a Software Development Engineer in Test to build and own the infrastructure, frameworks and pipelines that we use to develop and test Veeam Kasten. Our team cares about code quality, extensibility, testing and automation. What You’ll Do • Design, build and own test frameworks and internal tools for Veeam Kasten • Design, operate and improve CI/CD pipelines: test stages (pre-merge, nightly, release gates), pipeline speed and stability, flaky-test control, release automation • Create and manage the full lifecycle of test environments: ephemeral Kubernetes clusters in the cloud and on-premises, environments as code • Take part in performance, scale and security-scanning pipelines together with the software engineering teams • Turn unclear technical problems into working solutions together with the software engineering and platform teams Technologies You’ll Work With Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, Golang, Bash, Git, OpenShift, Rancher, Google GKE, AWS EKS, Azure AKS, VMware TKG(s), etc. What You’ll Bring • 5+ years of related experience in software engineering, SDET or DevOps roles • You built a test framework, a performance-testing process or a large part of a product CI/CD from scratch, and you owned it • Production-level Go or Python. You write tools and services, not only test scripts • Hands-on Kubernetes: you deploy, debug and operate clusters on EKS, GKE or AKS, and on-premises • You designed and operated CI/CD pipelines, not only used them • You manage test environments as code (Terraform or similar) through their full lifecycle • You work independently on unclear problems and agree on solutions with other teams. Good communication skills. Bonus Skills • Experience developing in Golang • Performance or soak testing • CI/CD at scale • Experience with public cloud APIs like AWS What You’ll Get • 26 paid days off annually, plus 4 extra global VeeaMe Days for self-care and 24 paid volunteer hours annually through Veeam Cares • Paid parental, maternity, and paternity leave • Fully covered family medical plan, dental, rehab, and vaccinations • Life, critical illness, and disability insurance • Employer pension contribution via PPK • Monthly Edenred allowance of 450 PLN for meals • MultiSport card fully covered by Veeam, giving access to sports facilities nationwide • Up to 12 free therapy sessions annually, plus legal and financial advice • Opportunities to learn and grow through on-demand libraries (LinkedIn Learning, O’Reilly), mentoring, workshops and learning events like our annual Global Day of Learning Please note: If the applicant is permanently present outside of Poland, Veeam reserves the right to refuse to consider the application for a job.
