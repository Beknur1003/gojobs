About Amartha At Amartha, we empower micro-businesses across Indonesia, enabling growth and equal prosperity. We've supported over 3.5+ million enterpreneurs–mostly women–by disbursing 2 billion USD in funding. As we step into 2026, Amartha is evolving into a technology-driven financial ecosystem, expanding our reach in lending, funding, and payments. Through innovation and digital solutions, we aim to enhance accessibility, streamline processes, and create a seamless user experience. Roles and Responsibilities: • Design, build, and own critical backend services that power underwriting, credit scoring, and loan decisioning workflows. • Develop high-performance Go services for score orchestration, policy evaluation, decision APIs, and related lending platform capabilities. • Integrate credit models, rule engines, third-party data providers, and internal risk systems into reliable production flows. • Ensure decision pipelines are accurate, explainable, auditable, and resilient under high throughput. • Define and evolve service contracts, data models, and event schemas used across underwriting and scoring systems. • Improve reliability, latency, and operability of core backend platforms through monitoring, alerting, and performance optimization. • Partner with ML, risk, product, and data teams to productionize scoring and underwriting capabilities end to end. • Lead technical design discussions, review architecture trade-offs, and raise the engineering bar through mentoring and code review. • Handle production incidents, root-cause analysis, and continuous improvement of system resilience. • Uphold engineering and compliance standards for secure, governed, and business-critical lending systems. Requirements • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with strong production experience in Go (Golang). • Deep understanding of designing and building scalable, reliable microservices and APIs (REST and/or gRPC). • Experience with distributed systems concepts: concurrency, messaging, idempotency, retries, consistency, and fault tolerance. • Strong proficiency with SQL databases, data modeling, indexing, query performance, and transactional integrity. • Familiarity with Kafka or similar event-driven architectures, caching (e.g. Redis), and containerized deployments. • Experience building or integrating underwriting, credit scoring, loan decisioning, or risk-related systems in fintech or digital lending. • Understanding of credit workflows such as application intake, feature/data aggregation, score orchestration, policy rules, decisioning, and audit trails. • Ability to work with ML-serving systems, model APIs, feature stores, or score pipelines in production. • Experience with cloud platforms, CI/CD, observability, and production incident handling. • Strong system design, code quality, and mentorship skills.
