We are hiring a Software Developer to build and ship high-quality Golang services that connect our platform to customer and partner systems. You will work across cloud environments (Azure and/or AWS), own features end-to-end, and use modern AI tooling, especially Claude, as a real development partner to move faster without sacrificing quality. This role fits engineers who like clear ownership, clean service boundaries, and building reliable integrations in a fast-moving cybersecurity product. Responsibilities: • Design, build, and maintain Golang connector services and related APIs. • Deploy and operate services in Azure and/or AWS cloud environments. • Own work from ticket to production: implementation, tests, reviews, and follow-through. • Use Claude (and similar tools) as a force multiplier for design, coding, debugging, and documentation. • Collaborate on an Agile team: standups, sprint delivery, and iterative improvement. • Work with high autonomy in a startup setting: unclear requirements, shifting priorities, and ownership of outcomes. • Partner with product, security, and platform teammates to ship connectors that are secure, observable, and maintainable. Requirements: • 1-3 years of professional software engineering experience. • Hands-on experience writing Golang services in Azure and/or AWS. • Practical experience using Claude as a development partner and productivity multiplier. • Experience delivering software on an Agile team. • Comfort operating autonomously in a fast-growing, dynamic startup environment. • Strong debugging instincts, clear written communication, and ownership mindset. Nice to Have: • Cybersecurity or Identity domain experience (authn/authz, IAM, SSO, directory systems, security tooling). • Experience building integrations/connectors against third-party APIs. • Familiarity with CI/CD, containerised services, observability, and secure coding practices. • Prior work in SaaS or multi-tenant environments.
