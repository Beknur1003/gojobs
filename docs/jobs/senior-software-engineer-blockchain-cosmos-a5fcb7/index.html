We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer with strong expertise in Blockchain, Golang, Cosmos SDK, and DevOps to build and manage scalable blockchain infrastructure and decentralised applications. The ideal candidate should be a hands-on developer with a passion for writing high-quality code, solving complex engineering problems, and building production-grade blockchain solutions. This role is ideal for someone who is technically strong and aspires to grow into a Technical Lead role, taking ownership of projects, mentoring engineers, and driving technical decisions over time. Responsibilities: • Design, develop, and maintain blockchain applications using Golang and Cosmos SDK. • Build and manage blockchain nodes, validators, and network infrastructure. • Develop REST/gRPC APIs and backend services. • Manage Kubernetes-based deployments and CI/CD pipelines. • Automate infrastructure using Terraform, Docker, Helm, and cloud platforms. • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver scalable blockchain solutions. • Troubleshoot production issues and optimise system performance. • Take ownership of technical deliverables and contribute to architecture and design discussions. • Mentor junior engineers and gradually take on technical leadership responsibilities. Requirements: • 4-5 years of software development experience. • Strong proficiency in Golang with a solid software engineering background. • Hands-on experience with Cosmos SDK, Tendermint/CometBFT, and blockchain architecture. • Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, Jenkins/GitHub Actions. • Strong knowledge of Linux, networking, distributed systems, and cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure). • Experience with Git, CI/CD, monitoring (Prometheus/Grafana), and scripting. • Strong problem-solving skills and excellent communication. Preferred Skills: • Experience with Ethereum, EVM, or Hyperledger. • Knowledge of blockchain security and validator operations. • Exposure to smart contracts and Web3 ecosystems. • Experience contributing to open-source blockchain projects. • Prior experience mentoring developers or leading technical initiatives is a plus. Mandatory: • Hands-on production experience with Golang, Cosmos SDK, Blockchain Development, and DevOps. • Strong development mindset with a passion for building scalable products. • Leadership potential with an interest in growing into a Technical Lead role in the future. • Profiles with only academic, training, or proof-of-concept experience will not be considered.
