Senior Software Engineer (Full Stack)

Senior

Job Summary We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer (Full Stack) to design, develop, and maintain scalable, high-performance software applications. The role involves end-to-end system development, architecture design, cloud infrastructure management, database design, automated testing, and production support. The position requires strong technical expertise in modern full-stack technologies, technical leadership, and agile software development practices. Mandatory Skill-set • Minimum 5 years of experience in full-stack software development within production environments; • Strong hands-on experience with TypeScript, React, Node.js, and Golang; • Strong knowledge of SQL and relational database design; • Hands-on experience managing cloud infrastructure using AWS, GCP, or Azure; • Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools such as Terraform; • Experience developing automated unit, integration, and end-to-end tests; • Experience leading technical initiatives and driving end-to-end project outcomes; • Strong understanding of system design, architecture, scalability, and performance optimization; • Experience troubleshooting and supporting production applications; • Strong problem-solving, analytical, and attention-to-detail skills; • Excellent written and verbal communication skills; • Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, Computer/Electronics Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field. Desired Skill-set • Experience building AI-native products or LLM-powered features; • Knowledge of OLAP or large-scale analytical data stores; • Experience with message queues and event-streaming platforms such as Kafka; • Experience developing distributed and high-throughput transactional systems; • Familiarity with Agile software development methodologies; • Experience leveraging AI tools to improve software development productivity and efficiency. Responsibilities • Design, develop, and maintain scalable, high-performance software services and applications; • Own the end-to-end design and implementation of complex software systems; • Lead system architecture and technical design decisions; • Prepare and maintain technical documentation and system architecture specifications; • Conduct code reviews and maintain high engineering and coding standards; • Provide technical guidance, mentoring, and direction to development team members; • Collaborate with business, product, design, and engineering teams to understand requirements and deliver solutions; • Apply Agile principles and practices throughout the software development lifecycle; • Leverage AI tools to accelerate development while maintaining code quality; • Implement observability and monitoring to ensure application reliability; • Provide production support, troubleshoot technical issues, and drive timely resolution; • Continuously improve system performance, scalability, usability, and engineering practices.
