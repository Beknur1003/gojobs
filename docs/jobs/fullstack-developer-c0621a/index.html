Position Overview and Objective The Senior Core Platform Services UI Developer is an essential contributor to the R&D organization by helping bridge the gap between UX Design and Agile Development at Arctic Wolf. They bring engineering knowledge and user context to the Agile team to help make well-informed design decisions that are technically feasible, testable and maintainable. They bring expertise of Front-End technologies and help mentor other developers as a subject matter expert. You will be a key member of the Core Platform Services team responsible for the Access Control Platform, which is critical for Arctic Wolf Networks to deliver world-class security operations at scale to global customers. Responsibilities: As a Senior Developer at Arctic Wolf, you will: • Design, develop, and deliver high-quality software solutions using ReactJS and Golang. • Own complex features from design through implementation, testing, deployment, and support. • Build scalable, maintainable, and reusable software components that support long-term product goals. • Collaborate with product managers, architects, and engineering teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions. • Participate in architecture reviews, technical design discussions, and code reviews to ensure engineering excellence. • Troubleshoot and resolve complex technical challenges across systems and teams. • Contribute to software quality through automated testing, performance optimization, and adherence to coding standards. • Mentor and support engineers through technical guidance, code reviews, and knowledge-sharing. • Drive continuous improvement in development processes, system reliability, and engineering best practices. • Participate in operational support and incident resolution as needed. Experience & Requirements • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. • 5+ years of professional software development experience building and delivering scalable, production-grade applications • Proven experience building and delivering scalable, production-grade software applications. • Strong hands-on experience with: • ReactJS • Golang • Solid understanding of software engineering principles, object-oriented design, and modern development practices. • Experience designing, developing, testing, and supporting cloud-native or distributed systems. • Proficiency in code reviews, debugging, performance optimization, and automated testing. • Experience participating in technical design and architecture discussions. • Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently on complex technical challenges. • Demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively across cross-functional teams. • Excellent verbal and written communication skills. • Experience mentoring engineers and promoting engineering best practices is preferred.
