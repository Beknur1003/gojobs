Meet the Team Splunk Cloud's Fleet Automation team builds robust and resilient automated solutions for managing Splunk's enterprise software at scale. We're fast-paced, high-velocity, and work with state-of-the-art AI technology, tackling automation challenges that span multiple groups across Splunk to keep services running smoothly and expediently for our customers. With a substantial AWS and Kubernetes footprint powering large-scale containerized systems, the team blends deep infrastructure expertise with a builder's mindset. It's a tight-knit group that thrives on solving hard, cross-cutting problems rather than settling for manual workarounds. This is an incredible opportunity to bring your leadership and cloud experience to a team that directly drives the growth and reliability of Splunk Cloud. Your Impact • Lead, coach, and grow a high-performing team of software engineers responsible for deploying updates across our cloud offerings. • Own and evolve team processes to improve operational health, streamline hiring, and remove organizational impediments. • Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to shape and maintain a roadmap that balances technical excellence with business priorities. • Champion automation as a default mindset, replacing manual processes with scalable solutions to reduce operational risk. • Coach the team on incident response and root-cause analysis to ensure platform resilience. • Utilize your interpersonal skills to foster a culture of inclusivity and continuous development, ensuring the team remains a destination for top talent. Minimum Qualifications • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field with 8+ years of applied experience in software or systems engineering, or a Master’s degree with 6+ years of applied experience. • 2+ years of prior experience managing software engineers and/or site reliability engineers. • Applied experience in Golang development within Kubernetes environments, including the use of kubectl and Docker containers. • Applied experience building and scaling secure cloud and container services on a major cloud provider. • Applied experience delivering software systems that meet defined security, performance, and disaster recovery standards. Preferred Qualifications • Applied experience operating in a CI/CD environment and utilizing AI-integrated engineering tooling. • Applied experience establishing telemetry, monitoring, and alerting protocols to surface issues proactively. • Applied experience in full-cycle recruiting and building high-performing engineering teams.
