A career in IBM Z is built on shaping critical technology that underpins the global economy. Over 70 percent of global transactional workloads and leading financial, healthcare, and public sector organizations rely on IBM Z and LinuxONE for unmatched security, resilience, and operational scale. As the Lead Architect in IBM Z and LinuxONE Security and Automation, you will be the chief technical authority driving the end-to-end software architecture for next-generation security and identity observability offerings on IBM Z. In this senior technical leadership role, you will design robust, high-performance integration architectures that connect upstream open-source and distributed platforms with mission-critical z/OS and Linux on s390x systems. You will collaborate across organizational boundaries, partnering with upstream engineering leadership, IBM Z platform teams, security specialists, and client architects to deliver resilient, enterprise-grade on-premises and hybrid software solutions. Your primary responsibilities will include: - Own End-to-End Solution Architecture: Define, document, and govern the technical architecture for IBM Z security and observability products, ensuring seamless integration across distributed services, Linux on s390x container runtimes, and native z/OS subsystems. - Design Mainframe Collectors and Adapters: Lead the architectural design of high-throughput, low-latency telemetry adapters and collectors interfacing with z/OS UNIX System Services (USS), RACF and SAF security domains, SMF data streams, IBM MQ, and z/OS Connect. - Drive Cross-Boundary Technical Alignment: Serve as the primary technical liaison between upstream platform engineering teams and downstream IBM Z development squads, resolving complex cross-platform integration, compilation, and dependency challenges. - Define Security and Zero-Trust Frameworks: Architect robust integration models with enterprise secret management systems such as Vault on Z, identity graph engines, and OpenTelemetry pipelines while enforcing strict zero-trust, cryptographic, and CVE compliance standards. - Govern Scalability, Performance, and Resilience: Establish performance benchmarks, latency limits, memory budgets, and high-availability patterns to ensure software operates efficiently within high-volume mainframe transaction environments. - Mentor and Guide Engineering Squads: Provide technical leadership, architectural oversight, and code reviews for developers, test leads, and system programmers, fostering technical excellence and continuous learning of IBM Z internals. '- Senior Software Architecture Experience: 8 or more years of progressive experience designing and delivering complex, multi-tier enterprise software architectures, microservices, and distributed systems. - Modern Programming and Runtime Mastery: Deep hands-on technical proficiency in languages such as Golang, Java, Python, or C/C++, with strong expertise in API design (REST, gRPC) and asynchronous event-driven architectures. - Cross-Solution Integration Depth: Proven expertise in integrating disparate distributed platforms, data pipelines, and middleware systems into cohesive, high-reliability enterprise solutions. - Cross-Team Leadership and Collaboration: Demonstrated ability to lead technical strategy across diverse engineering organizations, upstream platform providers, and enterprise stakeholders. - Linux and Container Platforms: Strong architectural experience with Linux operating system internals (POSIX, sockets, memory management), container runtimes (OCI/Docker), and enterprise Kubernetes or Red Hat OpenShift platforms. - Aptitude for Mainframe Architecture: Strong foundational understanding of operating systems and system architecture, combined with a clear passion for mastering IBM Z hardware, z/OS operating concepts, and s390x architecture. '- IBM Z and z/OS Subsystems: Hands-on familiarity with Linux on s390x, z/OS UNIX System Services (USS), RACF, SAF, SMF record processing, or zSecure. - Enterprise Security and Secret Management: Architectural experience with HashiCorp Vault, PKI, mutual TLS, OAuth2, OIDC, or zero-trust identity architectures. - Telemetry and Event Streaming: Experience designing architectures incorporating OpenTelemetry, Apache Kafka, Prometheus, or real-time event normalization pipelines. - Multi-Architecture Porting: Prior experience refactoring or validating software workloads across heterogeneous architectures (x86_64, s390x, ARM). - Security Standards and Compliance: Familiarity with enterprise compliance standards such as FIPS 140-2/3, Common Criteria, and enterprise vulnerability management.
